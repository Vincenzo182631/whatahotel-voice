# Anantara X Jim Thompson

Discover an exclusive collaboration between Anantara and the iconic fashion and design house Jim Thompson. Celebrating Thailand's most enduring traditions through a series of beautifully crafted pieces, the Soul of Siam collection embodies the spirit, energy and heritage of this timeless destination. Available only at Anantara hotels and resorts throughout Thailand, selected Jim Thompson stores and online at jimthompson.com.

### The design story

The collection is anchored by the bespoke Soul of Siam print, featuring tropical botanical motifs and classic Thai iconography – a temple, a mythical guardian, a scene from everyday life. Intricate illustrations echo the mystical landscapes that once evoked the spirit of exploration in Jim Thompson himself. Rendered in a deep blue palette, this richly detailed print is an elegant addition to any ensemble.

### Soul of Siam – A timeless collection

Each piece in the collection was created exclusively for Anantara: distinctive, considered, and made to be worn, collected and treasured. The Soul of Siam Wrap Kaftan is the statement piece: a luxurious and versatile silk wrap featuring the signature print. It is complemented by a limited collection of resort accessories: the Soul of Siam Capri Beach Bag in the signature print; the large Lotus Pouch and smaller Cactus Pouch, each in two colourways; the deep-blue Origami Elephant Cap; and a Pocket Square and Silk Scarf, both in the signature Soul of Siam Print.

### Jim Thompson: The legacy of the Thailand's 'Silk King'

A celebrated cultural icon, Jim Thompson is credited with reviving Thailand’s silk industry and introducing Thai craftsmanship to the world. An American architect and former intelligence officer, he settled in Thailand after World War II and set up a silk business that would become a worldwide sensation. Then he disappeared without a trace. Explore the enigma and legacy of Thailand's 'Silk King'.
