Immerse yourself in an unforgettable adventure at Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort, a breathtaking retreat set amid 160 acres of bamboo forest in northern Thailand. Perched on a mountain ridge overlooking the borders of Thailand, Myanmar and Laos, the resort is approximately one hour from Chiang Rai International Airport (CEI) and just a five-minute drive from the historic town of Chiang Saen. Explore more of northern Thailand with Doi Tung Royal Villa approximately 50 minutes away and Chiang Rai’s famed White Temple, Wat Rong Khun, around one hour from the resort.

Let your sense of adventure lead you through ancient ruins reclaimed by nature, journey through the picturesque countryside by Royal Enfield sidecar, or find serenity in an incense-laden temple. Here, hill-tribe traditions and Buddhist culture converge in a landscape where time seems to stand still.

Connect with the gentle giants at our renowned elephant camp and discover meaningful encounters rooted in conservation and care. Spend an extraordinary night immersed in their natural habitat in a transparent Jungle Bubble, gazing at star-filled skies and waking to the sounds of the jungle. Indulge in rejuvenating spa rituals and ancient healing practices inspired by nature, or drift along the Mekong River as morning mist rises from its waters, discovering the cultures and landscapes of three neighbouring countries.

Take dining to enchanting heights at Canopy: Treetop Dining, where intimate meals are served high among the branches with spectacular views over the surrounding wilderness.

### Mekong Explorer Suite

Mekong Explorer Suite pay homage to the intrepid explorers who charted the Mekong River centuries ago, blending the rich traditions of Lanna with elegant colonial influences. Dark wood paneling and intricate elephant motifs evoke the essence of Asian heritage, while vintage travel trunks, adorned with antique brass hardware and leather straps, bring a touch of Western charm. Each suite is thoughtfully named after distinct regions along the Mekong River, with captivating artworks that grace the walls above each headboard, reflecting the unique character of these historical locales.

- 64 - 96 sqm
- One-time SamSarn dining experience
- Explorer-inspired décor paired with refined luxury and classic elegance.

### Mekong Explorer Tent

Discover the Mekong Explorer Tent, a secluded hillside retreat offering an extraordinary glamping experience in Chiang Rai. Surrounded by nature, each private tent features its own swimming pool and terrace, with panoramic views across the landscapes of the Golden Triangle.

Designed for couples seeking privacy and adventure, two individual tents sit adjacent to one another and can also be reserved together for families or groups of friends. Combining the romance of outdoor living with refined Anantara comforts, the Mekong Explorer Tent invites you to slow down, reconnect with nature and experience the spirit of exploration in one of Northern Thailand’s most remarkable settings.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**What are the check-in and check-out times at Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort?**

Check-in from 2.00 pm and check-out is until 12.00 noon.

**How far is Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort to the airport?**

Approximately one hour from Mae Fah Luang International Airport.

**Is Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort an all-inclusive hotel?**

With Golden Triangle Luxury Explorer Package, you can enjoy the immersive experiences at the hotel along with full-board dining, airport transfer and more.

**Does Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort have a gym?**

Yes. Our gym is fully equipped with treadmills, exercise bikes, and weights. Sauna, steam rooms and whirlpool are also available while tennis court can be booked in advance.

**Does Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort have a swimming pool?**

Yes. Our infinity pool is perhaps one of the most stunning pools in Asia.

**What are the nearest famous tourist attractions around Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort?**

Golden Triangle, Phrathat Doi Pu Khao and House of Opium are one of the most visited attractions.

**Can I sponsor elephants at Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort?**

Yes, you can. The Golden Triangle Asian Elephant Foundation (GTAEF) serves as a home for elephants, mahouts and their families - rescued primarily from illegal logging camps, the street, or elephant shows. It takes approximately USD 18,000 to look after a single elephant a year, and your money will be put towards food, general upkeep and medical care. Please contact our Front Desk for more information.
