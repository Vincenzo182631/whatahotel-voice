## Overview

Located on Belle Isle, steps away
from the action on South Beach,
The Standard Spa, Miami Beach is
less a “hotel,” and more an adults-only spa with
guest rooms. Set amidst peaceful
tropical gardens, the breezy,
soothing rooms feature private
terraces, many with outdoor
soaking tubs, as well as an indoor
and outdoor hydrotherapy
playground, complimentary to hotel
guests. The Spa encourages the
communal rituals of ancient
bathing traditions and serves as
a hub for sessions by some of the
world’s top yogis and treatments by
expert practitioners. Lido Bayside Grill offers the perfect
warm-weather, sun-kissed
global-inspired menu.
There’s also a café, gym, boutique,
nail salon and intimate indoor bar.

How do I apply to be a Member?

Want to become a member at The Standard, Miami? Simply contact membership@standardmiami.com to learn more about our Membership program.

How do I book a Spa service?

To book a Spa Service at The Standard, Miami Spa simply go to https://www.standardhotels.com/miami/properties/miami-beach#spa for our service menu and to book an appointment. You can select from hydrotherapy, Turkish-style hammam, aroma steam room, sauna, ice room, Roman waterfall hot tub, arctic plunge, treatment baths, and an infinity pool.

See More

How do I book a Day Pass?

You can reserve Day Passes online, Monday–Thursday, which grant you access to the Pool and our third-floor Spa amenities. Day Passes are based on availability.

How do I contact Marketing?

**Have an idea? We’re listening.**

For brand partnerships, photo and video shoots, or other marketing-related inquiries, get in touch with our Miami marketing team at **marketing@standardmiami.com**.

**A note for creators and influencers:** We love working with you, but please note that we are not accepting paid or value exchange collaborations at this time. We respectfully ask that you do not submit requests for these collaborations, as they will not receive a response.

Do I get access to the Spa?

Our hotel guests enjoy full access to our hydrotherapy playground. Not a guest? When you book any Spa service from Monday to Thursday that is over $125 or from Friday to Sunday that is over $225, we'll give you exclusive access to enjoy our hydrotherapy amenities. This offer is based on availability. You can browse our services here: https://www.standardhotels.com/miami/properties/miami-beach#spa

Can I use the amenities before check in and after check out?

Want to use the Pool, Spa or The Gym at The Standard, Miami before checking in or after checking out? You are welcome to arrive as early or stay as late as you'd like during your stay.
