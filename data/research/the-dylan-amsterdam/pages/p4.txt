### Discover The Dylan Amsterdam

#### A five-star boutique hotel in a unique historical building

Located in the heart of the “9-streets” area on the prestigious “Keizersgracht” canal, The Dylan Amsterdam is the ideal home base as you discover the hidden treasures of Amsterdam’s past and present by foot or bike.

### FAQ

Is The Dylan Amsterdam located near the city center?

Yes the Dylan Amsterdam is located right in the Historical Canal Ring of Amsterdam. 0.0km away!

What kind of breakfast is served at The Dylan Amsterdam?

The Dylan offers breakfast daily from 07:00 – 10:30hrs, and until 11:00hrs in the weekends. A la carte options are served with a wide range of variety in Bar Brasserie OCCO. Full menu can be found here.

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Dylan Amsterdam?

Check-in is at 15:00hrs and Check-out is at 12 noon.

Does The Dylan have a pool?

The Dylan does not have a pool, it does have a well equiped Gym and offers in-room treatments.

Does The Dylan Amsterdam have a nice restaurant on site?

The Dylan has two restaurants on site:

- até; The Dylan’s chef’s table, where three influential cuisines—French, Japanese, and Mexican—come together in perfect harmony.
- Bar Brasserie OCCO; an informal brasserie style eatery
- Restaurant Vinkeles; two-Michelin Star Awarded Fine Dining Restaurant

How do I get to The Dylan Amsterdam from the nearest airport?

From the nearest Airport you can use the following options to get to The Dylan Amsterdam:

- Train: 30km / 20 min
- Taxi: 17km / 20 min

What type of room can I book at The Dylan Amsterdam?

Room options at The Dylan Amsterdam:

- Luxury Single Room
- Double Room
- Duplex Room with staircase in the Room
- Junior Suite overlooking the Canal
- Superior Suite overlooking the Garden
- Luxury Suite overlooking the Keizersgracht Canal
