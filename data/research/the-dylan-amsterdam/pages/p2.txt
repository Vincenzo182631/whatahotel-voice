### Duplex & Luxury Duplex Room

The Duplex Room is located in “the Original building”, with a view over the garden, featuring a bathroom with separate bathtub & shower, Aesop bath amenities, Illy espresso maker and a B&O sound system.

A Luxury Duplex Room, also uniquely known as a staircase room, is arranged over two floors connected by a steep internal staircase, with the bedroom and bathroom located on separate floors.

Available in Loxura or Amber style.
