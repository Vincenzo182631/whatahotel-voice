### Experiences to discover this Spring

Canal mornings and golden hour cruises

Spring light defines Amsterdam. A private boat offers an intimate view of the canals, especially in the early morning or at sunset.

**Museums at a measured pace**

Balance iconic institutions such as the Rijksmuseum or the Van Gogh Museum with smaller canal house museums for a more intimate cultural experience.

**Markets and weekend strolls**

Seasonal markets, local delicacies and gentle walks along the canals capture the essence of the city at this time of year.

### FAQ

Does The Dylan offer outdoor dining in spring?

With the arrival of the season, the terrace at Bar Brasserie OCCO reopens, offering a relaxed setting for lunch, drinks, or a leisurely afternoon in the courtyard once temperatures allow.

Is spring a good time to visit Amsterdam?

Spring is widely considered one of the most beautiful moments to experience the city. The days grow longer, gardens come into bloom, and the atmosphere feels lighter and more spacious before the height of summer.

Can The Dylan arrange cultural or seasonal experiences?

Our concierge team is delighted to curate bespoke itineraries, from museum reservations and private art guides to countryside excursions and dining experiences, ensuring each stay reflects your interests.

Are the new Aesop amenities available in all rooms?

Our Aesop amenities are available in all guest rooms and suites, offering a refined touch of calm and comfort throughout your stay.
