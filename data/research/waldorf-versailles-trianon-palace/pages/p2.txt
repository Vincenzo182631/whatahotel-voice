# Welcome to Trianon Palace

Welcome to Trianon Palace

The Trianon Palace is a 5 star venue featuring majestic neoclassical architecture and opulent décor. Discover our grand gallery filled with daylight, decorated with a contemporary touch, and explore a rich history in the Clemenceau Ballroom. This emblematic place includes 85 guest rooms and 15 suites, providing stunning views over Parc de Versailles and the hotel’s gardens. The restaurants, bar, spa, and swimming pool are all located in this building.

## Palace Rooms

Our spacious Deluxe Rooms offer beautiful views of the gardens or Parc de Versailles. Discover stylish mansard windows, exquisite comfort, and beautiful surroundings.

## Palace Suites

Experience unrivalled luxury in our collection of five-star suites. Relax with a separate living area and views of the Chateau and its Royal Domain.

## Signature Suites

The epitome of chic and sophisticated, our regal signature suites feature stunning views of the Palace of Versailles, luxurious furnishings, and an abundance of space.
