# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Discover a Michelin-starred restaurant and refined cuisine at Waldorf Astoria Versailles. From innovative dishes at Gordon Ramsay au Trianon, to luxury gastronomy at La Véranda brasserie, a world of flavor awaits.

## Le Jardin du Trianon

Summer vibes are in full swing at Le Jardin du Trianon. Enjoy terrace-side freshness, seasonal cuisine, creative cocktails, and a chic street food twist.

**Opening Hours**:

Open daily — no reservation needed

Sunday – Thursday: 12 pm – 12 am

Friday & Saturday: 12 pm – 1 am

Barbecue Specials: Thursday – Saturday, 6 pm – 10 pm

Live Music: Thursday – Saturday evenings

View Le Jardin du Trianon

## Gordon Ramsay au Trianon

Our Michelin-starred restaurant Gordon Ramsay au Trianon provides a feast for the senses. Discover delectable French cuisine in an elegant and intimate setting.

**Opening Hours:**

Tuesday to Saturday: 7:30 pm – 9:30 pm

Sunday & Monday: Closed

View Gordon Ramsay au Trianon

## La Véranda

Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, La Véranda brasserie offers exquisite continental cuisine, complemented by an extensive wine list and contemporary interior design. During the warmer months, ask for a table on the terrace to enjoy the beautiful view of the Palace of Versailles.

**Opening Hours**:

**Breakfast**

Served daily from 7 am – 10:30 am

**Lunch**

Served daily from 12:30 pm – 2:30 pm

**Dinner**

Served daily from 7 pm – 10:30 pm

View La Véranda

## Peacock Alley

Enjoy afternoon tea, a wide selection of wines, plus a warm and relaxing atmosphere at Peacock Alley.

**Opening Hours**:

Daily from 10:30 am – 11 pm

View Peacock Alley

## Room Service

Indulge in delicious cuisine from the comfort of your room. Available 24/7 with a limited menu from 11 p.m. Explore our ‘Little Extras’ services in your room to make your stay even more memorable.
