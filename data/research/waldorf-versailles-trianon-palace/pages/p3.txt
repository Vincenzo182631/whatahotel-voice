# Welcome to Pavillon du Trianon

Welcome to Pavillon du Trianon

The Pavillon is a 4 star building accommodating 99 rooms, most of which overlook the hotel’s stunning gardens. The restaurants, bar, spa, and swimming pool are located in the Palace building, which is easily accessible through a dedicated pathway. Experience genuine French art de vivre in peaceful surroundings right by the Parc de Versailles.

## Pavillon rooms

Our Pavillon rooms offer modern amenities and tranquil surroundings. For a room with a view, opt for one of our Garden View rooms. Pavillon du Trianon also offers accessible rooms for those that require additional mobility requirements.
