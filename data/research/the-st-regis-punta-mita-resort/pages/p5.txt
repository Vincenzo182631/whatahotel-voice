Exclusive Rituals at The St. Regis Spa Punta Mita

### Burnout Relief Therapy

Our neuro-sedative massage targets burnout syndrome, addressing stress, fatigue, anxiety, and insomnia. Using vetiver and lavender, renowned for their deeply relaxing properties, alongside white jade stones, we relieve tension and negative emotions in the face, neck, and shoulders.

### Temazcal

Elevating Huichol tradition with transformative healing; awaits the Temazcal experience of a lifetime. A sanctuary of luxury where ancient traditions and contemporary indulgence converge. A unique sensory experience as you renew your body and spirit in this exclusive haven of rejuvenation at Punta Mita.

### Couples Massage

Let the tranquil sounds of the ocean and the beauty of Punta Mita enhance this journey designed for two. crafted for those seeking a serene escape together. Enjoy the possibility of customizing any of our treatments..

### Spa Treatments and Services

Discover ultimate relaxation and rejuvenation at the premier spa in Punta Mita, offering an unparalleled wellness experience in Riviera Nayarit. Every treatment is thoughtfully designed to restore balance and well-being. Begin your journey in tranquil wet areas, sipping tea while you embrace the serene beauty of this coastal paradise. ## Luxury Suite

Experience our luxurious suite designed for bridal preparations with bridesmaids or for intimate moments with couples. It features a heated pool, outdoor shower, private lounge, and exclusive treatment cabin where you can enjoy massages, facials, or body treatments. For brides and their bridal party, hairstyling and makeup services are available. The Luxury Suite includes a complimentary amenity of champagne with a cheese board featuring fruits and chocolates, ensuring an unforgettable experience.

## Couples' Cabin

Couples' Retreat Cabin: Exclusively designed to provide a serene sanctuary for our esteemed guests, featuring a tranquil heated pool and a private lounge area, perfect for unwinding after enjoying our spa in Punta Mita. Enhancing this experience, guests will indulge in a bottle of fine white wine paired with an exquisite cheese platter.
