The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort
For upscale relaxation, panoramic ocean views, and Mexican culinary excellence

Why book?
For upscale relaxation, panoramic ocean views, and Mexican culinary excellence. A hushed elegance defines the St. Regis Punta Mita, and a late 2023 renovation breathed new life into an aging property. Despite its sprawling footprint, the resort feels discreet and private, and nails the details.
Set the scene
Located on Mexico’s Riviera Nayarit, St. Regis (along with several neighboring luxury hotels and residences in Punta Mita) is on a 1,500-acre lush enclave with more than nine miles of private beach and two golf courses from Jack Nicklaus. You’ll spot A-listers, Silicon Valley founders, and stylish couples here for some serious R&R. Despite the exclusivity, it’s refreshingly convenient—the complex is less than an hour drive from Puerto Vallarta, the closest major city and airport.
The backstory
First opened in 2008, St. Regis received a comprehensive $45 million refresh in 2023, led by Bibiana Huber of B-Huber architecture studio, based in Guadalajara, Jalisco. Updates include a dramatic new ocean-facing entry and bar, an expanded light-filled spa, and a striking dual-level pool. We loved the locally made decor and furniture, like custom leather-and-timber chairs by Casa Bobadilla, a specialized carpentry workshop in Jalisco.
The rooms
The 120 renovated rooms, suites, and villas blend Mexican- and Mediterranean-style architecture with views of the ocean, gardens, or Marietas Islands in the distance. Exposed wood-beam ceilings, terra-cotta floors, and artisan-crafted tapestries give the spaces a hacienda-by-the-sea feeling. Even base-level accommodations include both an indoor
and
outdoor shower, along with an oversized soaking tub and double sinks.
Families and groups traveling together should splurge for an entry-level suite—it’s not only massive, but also features a dining table for six guests. The crown jewel, though, is five new residential-style beachfront villas, ranging from one to three bedrooms.
Food and drink
The resort's five culinary experiences range from feet-in-the-sand casual to buttoned-up fine dining. For a pre-dinner cocktail or mocktail, head to the St. Regis Bar. Besides knockout sunset views, there’s unexpectedly excellent food for a lobby bar. Order the build-your-own tacos de trompo, a preparation of grilled slices of pork and pineapple, with the entire vertical rotating spit and all the fixings presented tableside.
For something more upscale and elegant, head to Carolina, named for New York socialite Caroline Astor. The seasonal 10-course tasting is not to be missed, featuring a culinary tour de force of Mexico, like charcoal-grilled lobster tail and a kampachi and orange tostada.
The spa
Beyond your typical massages and facials, the 10,000-square-foot St. Regis Spa features the ancestral Huichol purification ritual, Temazcal. Water infused with herbs is poured over hot stones to create steam within an enclosed sweat lodge. The heat is intense, but the physical detoxification and spiritual reflection are worth it.
The neighborhood/area
The jungle-shrouded luxury community of Punta Mita is a self-contained ecosystem of hotels and residences. Just beyond the gates, however, local fishing villages add a sense of local flair and authenticity. Further down the Nayarit coast, those who might want to experience a neo-hippy, bohemian surfing town can arrange a tour through the hotel (or call an Uber).
The service
Service leans more relaxed than stuffy, and it’s executed perfectly. At the upper echelon of Marriott’s luxury portfolio, the St. Regis brand is known for its personalized butler service. A lot of hotels offer it, but here, it’s legit. Requests were consistently fulfilled within minutes, whether that was an ice bucket or a morning coffee order. The 2023 revamp of the hotel brought new management, and from what I heard from guest reports, a big improvement in service levels.
For families
The hotel caters well to families, with an expansive children’s club and tiered infinity pool to keep little ones entertained. (A separate adults-only pool is on the other side of the resort.)
Accessibility
Sprawling beach resorts aren’t always easy for people with limited mobility, but the property has gently sloping ramps, and guests can call for a complimentary golf cart service. Some casitas have steps at the entryway, so note if you need step-free access at the time of booking.
Anything left to mention?
If you’re staying more than a few days, consider venturing beyond the gates of Punta Mita, whether that’s scuba diving in the Marietas Islands Marine Reserve or walking around the relaxed beach town of San Pancho.
