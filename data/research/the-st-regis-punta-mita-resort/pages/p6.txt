# Holiday Events at The St. Regis Punta Mita

## Echoes of Celebration 2026

Discover an unforgettable holiday escape on Mexico’s Pacific coast, where signature St. Regis traditions, personalized service, and the beauty of Punta Mita come together. From festive dining experiences inspired by authentic Mexican flavors to holiday rituals that bring family and friends together, every moment is designed to create lasting memories. Celebrate Christmas and New Year’s in effortless...See More luxury, surrounded by ocean views, exceptional experiences, and the signature sophistication that defines The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort. A Thanksgiving Celebration of Gratitude and Elegance at The St. Regis Punta Mita

### Turkey Trot

Begin your Thanksgiving celebration with our festive Turkey Trot. Gather with family and friends for a scenic run along Punta Mita’s beautiful coastal trails. Embrace the spirit of gratitude and community as you race towards a Thanksgiving feast waiting for you at the finish line. Departure from Motor Lobby 8:00 AM Complimentary.

Reserve

### Thanksgiving Dinner

Celebrate Thanksgiving with a special buffet featuring traditional holiday favorites, thoughtfully prepared and served in a warm, welcoming atmosphere. Gather with family and friends to honor the spirit of gratitude, share meaningful moments, and enjoy an evening of memorable dining. Marietas Lawn 5:30 PM – 10:00 PM.

Reserve

### Black Friday Mercadito

Celebrate the spirit of Mexico through handcrafted treasures, artisanal creations, and seasonal culinary offerings that reflect the richness of its culture, flavors, and traditions. Immerse yourself in a Mexican market experience where craftsmanship, local gastronomy, and community come together, offering a meaningful way to gather and celebrate the Thanksgiving weekend.

Reserve

A Magical Christmas at The St. Regis Punta Mita: Celebrate the Season by the Sea

### Christmas Tree Lighting

Embrace the festive spirit and join us at Marietas Lawn as we light our Christmas Tree and welcome the magic of the season. Surrounded by the beauty of Punta Mita, enjoy live music, seasonal cocktails, and an evening filled with holiday cheer.

### Christmas Eve Dinner

Join us for a Christmas Eve celebration featuring live music and a Mexican culinary journey showcasing regional flavors and cherished traditions from across the country. An evening designed to bring loved ones together while honoring the warmth and spirit of the season.

Reserve

### Santa's Arrival

Celebrate the enchanting arrival of Santa Claus at The St. Regis Punta Mita! Delight in this magical event as Santa makes his grand entrance, bringing joy and holiday cheer for children and families alike. Experience the excitement of festive activities, photo opportunities, and special surprises, making this a memorable start to the holiday season.
