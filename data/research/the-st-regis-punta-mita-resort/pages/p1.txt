# Welcome to The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort

## A secluded paradise on the Pacific Ocean.

Inspired by the artistry and architecture of Mexico, The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort redefines beachfront luxury on a serene peninsula in Punta de Mita, Mexico. Set amidst 22 lush acres overlooking the Pacific Ocean, this Punta Mita Resort showcases Mexican art, handcrafted furnishings, and an immersive design experience rooted in the spirit of Riviera Nayarit. Discover 120 guest rooms and suites at our Punta Mita Beach Resort, featuring world-class amenities, breathtaking ocean or garden views, and the signature St. Regis Butler Service. Surrounded by natural beauty, guests can explore outdoor adventures and water experiences along the Pacific coast. As a renowned Punta Mita Luxury Resort, we invite guests to unwind at The St. Regis Spa, play two Jack Nicklaus Signature Golf Courses, and savor distinctive dining experiences. Nestled along one of Mexico's most coveted coastlines, this Luxury Resort in Riviera Nayarit offers moments to remember, from sunrise over the Pacific to starlit evenings by the sea.

## St. Regis Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service has been a treasured hallmark of the St. Regis experience for more than one hundred years.

Service Details

###### Beverage Services

Upon arrival, allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange American coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast.

###### eButler

During your stay, whether you are inside or outside the hotel, St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via email. Simply send your request and allow the Butler Service Desk to assist.

###### Unpacking & Packing Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can simply relax and enjoy. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the Butler Service Desk to dispatch a butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

##### Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This gracious service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day.

Delights Delivered

Elevate the in-room experience.

Send a meaningful amenity gift during or in advance of an upcoming stay through the thoughtfully executed Delights Delivered Program. From a fresh bouquet of flowers to a personalized cake for a special celebration, deliver a memorable amenity to be enjoyed within the comforts of our rooms and suites.

St. Regis Punta Mita Rituals

**Champagne Sabering Ritual**

The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort continues a tradition started by John Jacob Astor IV when he founded the first St. Regis Hotel in New York, circa 1904. Guests gather for a ceremonial champagne sabering to celebrate the transition from the day into night. Experience the St. Regis tradition at sunset everyday at 6:30 PM. Join us to celebrate this memorable event.

**Bloody Mary**

In 1934, Fernand Petiot became a bartender at The St. Regis New York Hotel, bringing with him the “Bloody Mary”, a vodka and tomato juice drink he started making in Paris. It was in the St. Regis King Cole bar that Petiot perfected his original formula, adding salt, pepper, lemon, Tabasco and Worcestershire Sauce to create the drink that is beloved today.

Following Fernand Petiot’s tradition, every St. Regis in the world has created their signature Bloody Mary by adding a special hint, so The St. Regis Punta Mita created the “Mita Mary” prepared with white tequila instead, and other local ingredients.

You will be able to enjoy the Mita Mary as a teaser for breakfast in all our restaurants, as well as a Bloody Mary menu that includes all recipes from St. Regis hotels around the world.

**Catch of the Day Ritual**

Every Friday at 11:30 am at Sea Breeze Beach, we invite you to meet our Chef to choose your Catch of the Day. Local fishermen deliver the bay’s freshest fish and guests may select the day’s lunch or dinner directly with our Executive Chef. This specialty meal may be prepared in any of our restaurants or private dining (in room-dining).

Continuously improving our environmental performance in alignment with Green Key standards.

We are committed to full compliance with all applicable international, federal, state, and local environmental laws and regulations. We also manage, measure, and reduce the environmental impact of our operations through initiatives focused on:

Through these efforts, we reaffirm our commitment to Green Key principles and the continuous improvement of our environmental performance.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort is Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR). PVR is located approximately 41.3 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort have an airport shuttle to Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR)?

Yes, The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort has an airport shuttle to Gustavo Diaz Ordaz International Airport Puerto Vallarta (PVR).

Does The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
