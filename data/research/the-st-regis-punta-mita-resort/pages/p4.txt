## Dining

The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort offers an extraordinary selection of restaurants and bars, each providing ambiance and views as inspiring as their cuisine. Enjoy dining on al fresco terraces that overlook the Pacific Ocean, and delight in our passion for artistic cuisine. Our signature restaurant, Carolina, showcases the very best of modern Mexican cuisine. For more casual dining, visit Sea Breeze...See More Restaurant & Bar, featuring vibrant ceviche and fish tacos. The St. Regis Punta Mita Resort caters to all dietary needs by creating special menu options. We ask that guests inform us of any allergies and dietary concerns prior to arrival.See Less
