### Our Commitment to Sustainability

At InterContinental Genève, sustainability is a core value that guides our daily operations. Through responsible resource management, community engagement, and the IHG Green Engage programme, we continuously reduce our environmental impact while supporting more sustainable hospitality, in line with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals

Learn more

### Delectable Dining

Savour a refined culinary journey at our signature outlets. Enjoy all‑day dining and an indulgent Sunday brunch at Woods, where fresh, locally sourced ingredients take centre stage. Unwind at Les Nations Bar & Lounge, an intimate setting offering refined flavours and classic cocktails, designed to elevate your stay in Geneva.

Learn more

### We inspire incredible

Luxury and functionality unite in our 16 refined meeting spaces and Grand Ballroom, hosting up to 800 guests. From intimate gatherings to grand galas, our 5-star service, 2,000 sqm of meeting space and expert team turn your vision into reality, crafting memorable experiences for all occasions.

Learn more

### Cinq Mondes Spa

Spa Cinq Mondes harmoniously converts beauty rituals into a sensorial escape of relaxation and renewal. Going beyond skincare, we harmonise energies and emotions for cellular balance that exudes happiness. Fusing tradition with innovation, our treatments become transformative journeys towards holistic well-being.

Learn more

### Our Concierge

Our Concierge team is dedicated to making your stay unforgettable. With their "Clefs d’Or" expertise, they unlock the most beautiful places and hidden gems for you. From gourmet dining to historical sites, and from natural wonders to cultural experiences, let us guide you through an extraordinary journey in our city.

## FAQs for InterContinental Geneve

Does InterContinental Geneve have a pool?

InterContinental Geneve has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor seasonal Pool

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Geneve?

At InterContinental Geneve, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 40 CHF and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 40 CHF .

Private indoor parking is conveniently available at a cost of CHF 40 per day for guests staying at the hotel, or CHF 3/hour, CHF 600/month, CHF 6'000/year. For more details about hourly rates, please contact our friendly concierge team.

Parking fee: 40 CHF per day

Parking for registered guests only

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Geneve?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Geneve.

Pet policy description. Pets are welcome to stay at the hotel with unlimited access to all public areas except the swimming pool. Advance notice is required and a fee of CHF 30 is charged per pet per night.
We accept only small dogs in our restaurants.

Pet fee per night: 30 CHF

Pet weight limit: No weight limit per pet

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: Only dogs and cats allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Geneve for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Geneve have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Geneve has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Geneve?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Geneve.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Geneve?

Check-in at InterContinental Geneve is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Geneve offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Geneve offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Geneve from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Geneve is conveniently located near Geneva International Airport (GVA), approximately 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Geneve?

InterContinental Geneve is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Geneva Water Fountain - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Red Cross Museum - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- La Perle du Lac - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)
- Patek Phillippe Museum - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Contemporary and Moderne Art Museum - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Archaeological Site of St Peter's Cathedral - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie - 3.11 miles away (5 kilometers)
- Rue du Rhône - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometers)
- Ariana Museum - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- United Nations Office in Geneva

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Geneve puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
