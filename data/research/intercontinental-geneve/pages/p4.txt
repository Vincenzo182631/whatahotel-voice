### Our Commitment to Sustainability

At InterContinental Genève, sustainability is a core value that guides our daily operations. Through responsible resource management, community engagement, and the IHG Green Engage programme, we continuously reduce our environmental impact while supporting more sustainable hospitality, in line with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals

Learn more

### Cinq Mondes Spa

Spa Cinq Mondes harmoniously converts beauty rituals into a sensorial escape of relaxation and renewal. Going beyond skincare, we harmonise energies and emotions for cellular balance that exudes happiness. Fusing tradition with innovation, our treatments become transformative journeys towards holistic well-being.

Learn more

### Our Concierge

Our Concierge team is dedicated to making your stay unforgettable. With their "Clefs d’Or" expertise, they unlock the most beautiful places and hidden gems for you. From gourmet dining to historical sites, and from natural wonders to cultural experiences, let us guide you through an extraordinary journey in our city.

Learn more

### Cigar Lounge

Experience refined relaxation at our Cigar Lounge, designed by Tony Chi. Open daily from 8am to 12:30am, it welcomes both guests and visitors. Immerse yourself in a warm, inviting atmosphere, perfect for cigar aficionados. Enjoy an intimate setting enriched with unique features and a diverse cigar selection.

### Sustainability

#### Sustainability Certification

- Our hotel has a third-party sustainability certification.

**Certifications: Green Key (FEE)**

Our hotel is certified with Green Key (FEE), a globally recognised eco-label run by the Foundation for Environmental Education that is awarded to tourism and hospitality facilities that demonstrate outstanding commitment to environmental sustainability. Certified hotels must have implemented a range of measures across multiple categories including energy (e.g. energy-saving lightbulbs), water (e.g. water-saving taps, showers, and toilets) and waste (food waste and single-use plastic reduction schemes).
