### Exceptional Hospitality, with Room for Improvement

20 days ago

My stay was a very positive experience overall, largely thanks to the exceptional staff, who were consistently attentive, courteous, warm, and discreet. The level of service was truly outstanding, and the hotel was impeccably clean throughout. I particularly appreciated the excellent security measures, which made me feel completely comfortable and at ease during my stay. Yael, the concierge, deserves special recognition for going above and beyond to accommodate every request with remarkable efficiency and genuine kindness.

To make the experience even better, I believe the rooms and bathrooms would benefit from a design refresh, as they currently feel somewhat dated. The view overlooking a construction site was also disappointing, and the information regarding the possibility of bedbugs entering through the windows was unsettling. These points do not detract from the excellent service provided by the team, but addressing them would help bring the rooms up to the same exceptional standard as the hotel's hospitality. Overall, the staff truly made the difference, and I would like to see the physical environment reflect that same level of excellence.

ProsSleep Quality, Room Cleanliness, Location, recognition for ihg rewards club members

ConsAmenities

Yes, I recommend this hotel.
