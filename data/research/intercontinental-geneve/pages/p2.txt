### Epitome of excellence

Behind grandiose doors as tall as those of Versailles Palace, our Residence Suite, designed by famed New York architect Tony Chi, exudes the elegance of past palaces. Immerse yourself in unparalleled beauty with our most luxurious suite's extraordinary 360-degree view, showcasing all of Geneva, including the iconic Jet d'Eau.

Learn more

### Panoramic Lake View Suite

Immerse yourself in the iconic grandeur of the Mont Blanc, Lake Geneva and the acclaimed Jet d'Eau water fountain. Unique in design and décor, the 100m² Panoramic Lake View Suites offer a private sanctuary to unwind and admire the view. Contact directly for further information and booking.

Learn more

### Presidential Lake View Suite

Surrounded by dramatic panoramic views of the Mont Blanc and Lake Geneva, the 120m² Presidential Lake View Suites offer a contemporary celebration of Luxury and heritage. Contact directly for further information and booking.

Learn more

### Comfort for the whole Family

Designed with families in mind, our Family Suite offers generous space, comfort, and privacy for a truly relaxed stay in Geneva. With separate areas to unwind and thoughtful amenities for all ages, it provides the perfect balance between togetherness and tranquility, ensuring time well spent for every family member.
