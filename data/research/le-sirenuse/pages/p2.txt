# The World in a Room

## Rooms & Suites

View complete Rooms & Suites list

Rooms are arguably the most important part of any hotel. They are, after all, where guests spend most of their time. And yet room descriptions on hotel websites are so often focus on the dull stuff: lists of facilities, floor area, aspect, types of bed, amenities...

You need those things too, of course, to decide which room to book. But they don't answer the question of what awaits when you turn the key in the door.

View

“Each of the 58 rooms is a soothing, spotless realm of light.”

If it's daytime, it's the light that strikes you first. Le Sirenuse's rooms and suites embrace and reflect the luminous shimmer of the Mediterranean. White walls, repainted every winter, white ceilings, many of them in traditional Amalfi Coast cross-vault style, crisp white bedspreads, soft white cotton curtains through which the sunlight gently filters – each of the 58 rooms is a soothing, spotless realm of light.

But there's something else that's more difficult to put into words. It's to do with feeling at home as soon as you settle into an elegantly upholstered sofa, or a vintage cast iron chair on your private terrace with its ridiculously beautiful view. It's to do with feeling at home – but also feeling that you've come to a place owned by people who care, who pay attention to details.

There are stories behind each of those details. Stories about floor tiles designed especially for the hotel and fired in the centuries-old kilns of an ancient Salerno ceramics firm. Stories of the late Franco Sersale's passion for collecting antique furniture, artworks and Suzani rugs, and of his unerring instinct, when he saw a promising item in an auction catalogue, of knowing exactly where it would find a home, in just that corner of just that room.

Stories of a contemporary artist who was asked to create a video work that greets guests as they enter, because most hotel TV welcome messages are just so darned boring. Stories of the bathroom amenities by Eau d'Italie, a fragrance and body care brand born at Le Sirenuse out of the Sersale family's desire to capture the heady scents and emotions of Positano.

“Every room is a little world of Italian seaside simplicity, charm and elegance.”

At Le Sirenuse, no two rooms are alike. All have a history of little improvements and adjustments, of careful, continuous curation.  Each one is the hotel in miniature, a little world of Italian seaside simplicity, charm and elegance.

## FAQ

- ##### Does Le Sirenuse welcome children?

  - To maintain the tranquil and romantic atmosphere our guests expect, Le Sirenuse welcomes children aged 6 years and older. We do not allow children under the age of six.
- ##### Is the hotel suitable for guests with limited mobility?

  - Le Sirenuse is built into the cliffside and is arranged vertically, with many stairways and level changes. While some areas are accessible by elevator, certain routes and spaces may require stairs. If you have mobility considerations, please contact us before booking so we can recommend the most appropriate room options and access routes.
- ##### Are pets allowed?

  - To ensure the comfort of all our guests, pets are not permitted at Le Sirenuse.
- ##### Is the hotel smoke-free?

  - All indoor areas are non-smoking. Smoking may be permitted in designated outdoor areas; please ask our team for guidance on arrival.
- ##### Is there parking available on-site?

  - While the hotel does not have a private garage, we offer a valet parking service for a daily fee (approximately €100.00 per day). Due to the limited space in Positano, we recommend requesting this service in advance.
- ##### What forms of payment do you accept?

  - We accept major credit cards. For specific payment arrangements (wire transfers, invoicing, or corporate details), please contact our reservations team.
- ##### Are all rooms the same within a category?

  - No—each room and suite at Le Sirenuse is unique. Within the same category, there may be differences in layout, size, terrace configuration, view angle, and interior details. This individuality is part of the hotel's character and reflects the historic structure of the building.
- ##### What should I expect from “Sea View” or “Partial Sea View”?

  - Sea views in Positano can vary by room position, elevation, and terrace orientation. “Sea View” generally indicates a meaningful view of the water; “Partial Sea View” indicates the sea is visible but may be framed by buildings, vegetation, or the hillside. If you have a strong preference (sunset exposure, higher floor, wider terrace), please share it—our team will guide you to the best available option.
- ##### Can you guarantee a specific room number or exact room?

  - We are happy to note preferences and will do our best to honor them, but specific room numbers cannot be guaranteed in advance as allocations can shift with operational needs.
- ##### Do all rooms have terraces or balconies?

  - Many rooms do, and terraces are a defining feature of the Le Sirenuse experience. However, not all accommodations have the same type or size of outdoor space. Please refer to the room description, and contact us if you have any questions.
- ##### Do you offer connecting rooms?

  - Connecting rooms may be available in limited configurations. Please contact reservations well in advance so we can advise on options.
- ##### What is the best way to book?

  - Booking directly with the hotel guarantees the lowest price. You also receive the most accurate, up-to-date information on room features, views, and services, and allows our team to tailor the stay to your preferences.
- ##### What is your cancellation policy?

  - Policies may vary by season, rate type, and booking channel. Your specific cancellation terms will be clearly stated at the time of booking and in your confirmation.
- ##### What is included in my rate?

  - All rates at Le Sirenuse include breakfast. Our rates are intentionally generous and designed to allow guests to enjoy the hotel at their own rhythm. In addition to daily breakfast, stays include access to the pool and gym, concierge services, and participation in our seasonal calendar of activities, which may feature guided hikes, wellness sessions, and other curated experiences. The calendar of activities varies throughout the season and is available to guests during their stay. Your booking confirmation will always outline the specific details of your rate.
- ##### What extra costs should I plan for?

  - Common extras can include: dining and beverages (including room service), spa treatments, private transfers, laundry services, excursions, and valet parking. The city tax and a discretionary service charge of 5% are not included and will be added separately. If you would like a tailored estimate based on your plans, our concierge will be happy to help.
- ##### Do you offer laundry and pressing?

  - Yes. Laundry and pressing services are available.
- ##### Is Wi-Fi available throughout the hotel?

  - Yes, Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.
- ##### Can the concierge help with restaurants, beach clubs, and excursions outside the hotel?

  - Yes. We can assist with reservations, private boat charters, day trips (Amalfi, Ravello, Capri, Pompeii), guides, and curated experiences. During peak season, planning in advance is recommended.
- ##### Can you help organize celebrations (honeymoons, anniversaries, proposals)?

  - Yes. From flowers and champagne to bespoke experiences, our team can help create something memorable. Please contact our concierge with your dates and preferences.
