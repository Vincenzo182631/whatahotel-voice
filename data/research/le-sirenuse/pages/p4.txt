## FAQ

- ##### Do I need reservations for La Sponda, the Pool Bar, or Franco's Bar?

  - We recommend reservations whenever possible, especially for dinner at La Sponda. Our concierge can assist with booking and with choosing the best time based on your preferences. Franco's bar is first come first served, but hotel guests go straight to the front of the cue.
- ##### What is the dress code for La Sponda and Franco's Bar?

  - We suggest a "Smart Casual" dress code. For dinner at La Sponda, we ask gentlemen to avoid shorts, tank tops, or baseball caps. While a jacket is not mandatory, many of our guests choose to wear one to match the refined atmosphere. Franco's Bar has no dress code.
- ##### Can you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?

  - Yes. Please inform us in advance of any allergies or dietary requirements so our culinary team can prepare appropriately. On arrival, we are also happy to reconfirm preferences.
- ##### Is Le Sirenuse Mare included for hotel guests?

  - Le Sirenuse Mare is our beach club in Nerano. Access and services are subject to availability and may vary by season and day. Our concierge can explain the current arrangements and help you plan the best experience.
