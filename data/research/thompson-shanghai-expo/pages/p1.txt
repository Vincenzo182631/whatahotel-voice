# Lifestyle Hotel by Hyatt \| Thompson Shanghai Expo

## Experience Timeless Sophistication with a Modern Edge in Downtown Shanghai

Thompson Shanghai Expo is a lifestyle hotel located at Expo Place, a landmark commercial complex in the heart of the Shanghai World Expo area, a lively hub of business and culture, a magnetic stage where cultures collide and ideas ignite. Step into an urban retreat inspired by the area’s rich industrial heritage and global spirit, celebrated through design, art, and gastronomy. Unwind in stylish comfort. Revel in the club-like atmosphere of TOTS on the rooftop. And when the city calls, you’re just steps from a dynamic mix of arts, entertainment, and nature, beckoning you to explore.

## Rooms & Suites

Timeless elegance with a modern industrial edge sets the tone for a restful stay in our 254 rooms and suites. Revel in the warm sophistication of hardwood floors, luxurious residential comforts, and floor-to-ceiling windows that showcase inspiring city and Huangpu River views.

## Take a Look Around

Swimming Pool

Unwind in our 25-meter heated indoor pool, allowing the warm water to soothe and refresh you. Then melt any lingering tension in our sauna and steam rooms, leaving you feeling rejuvenated for whatever the day holds next.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our ongoing commitment to reducing waste, our hotel provides a limited selection of disposable in-room amenities on request. We recommend bringing your own essentials in advance.

Dining & Nightlife

Discover our inviting spaces for dining and socializing. Enjoy a relaxing coffee in the BAOBAO Lounge. Indulge in innovative Chinese cuisine inspired by the bounty of the five renowned coastal and riverine regions, before heading up to TOTS, our club destination on the rooftop, the ideal spot for a nightcap with live music and spectacular skyline views.​

Fitness Center

Our state-of-the-art fitness center offers a comprehensive setup for your exercise needs, from cardio machines to weights and strength training equipment. You can also enjoy the social atmosphere of a group fitness class before completing your warm down in the comfortable members’ lounge. Open 24 hours a day.
