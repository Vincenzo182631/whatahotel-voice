Getting here

Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA)

Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (IATA: SHA) is located in the western part of Shanghai, about 13 kilometers from the city center, serving as one of China's major aviation hubs. The airport has two terminals: T1 handles some domestic and international (regional) flights, while T2 mainly serves domestic flights and is seamlessly connected to Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station. With convenient transportation options, passengers can reach the city and surrounding areas via Metro Lines 2 and 10, high-speed rail, taxis, and buses. The airport offers a range of services, including dining, duty-free shops, VIP lounges, free Wi-Fi, and self-service check-in facilities to enhance travel efficiency. As a key part of Shanghai’s dual-airport system, Hongqiao Airport continues to improve its services and passenger experience.

Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG)

Shanghai Pudong International Airport (IATA code: PVG) is one of China's major international aviation hubs, located in Pudong New Area, about 30 k from downtown Shanghai. Covering approximately 40 sq k, the airport was officially opened on October 1, 1999. It features two terminals (T1 and T2), along with four runways capable of accommodating large aircraft such as the A380. As Shanghai’s primary international gateway, Pudong Airport handles around 60% of the city's international flights. In terms of transportation, the airport offers multiple travel options, including the Maglev train (8 minutes to Longyang Road Station), Metro Line 2, airport buses, and taxi/ride-hailing services, ensuring convenient access to downtown Shanghai and surrounding areas.

Shanghai South Railway Station

Shanghai South Railway Station, located at 289 Humin Road in Xuhui District, is one of Shanghai’s major railway hubs, primarily serving routes to East, Central, and South China. Originally built in 1908 as "Shanghai South Train Station," it was rebuilt and reopened in 2006, covering a total area of approximately 60,000 sq m. The station operates conventional trains along the Beijing-Shanghai Railway, Shanghai-Kunming Railway, and Nanjing-Qidong Railway, connecting destinations such as Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Fujian, Hunan, and Guangdong, while also handling some intercity routes within the Yangtze River Delta. In terms of transportation, the station is directly accessible via Metro Lines 1 and 3, multiple bus routes, taxis, ride-hailing services, and a long-distance bus terminal at the South Square, providing convenient travel options for passengers.

Shanghai Railway Station

Shanghai Railway Station is one of Shanghai’s major railway stations, located in Jing'an District at Huning Road, about 4 kilometers from downtown. Originally built in 1909, the station has undergone multiple expansions and renovations, handling major railway lines such as Beijing-Shanghai, Shanghai-Nanjing, and Shanghai-Hangzhou. The station features modern architecture with a total area of approximately 130,000 square meters, equipped with multiple waiting halls and tracks, ensuring the efficient management of large passenger flows.

Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station

It is one of Shanghai’s key railway hubs, connecting major lines such as the Beijing-Shanghai High-Speed Railway and Shanghai-Nanjing High-Speed Railway. Shanghai Maglev Train Longyang Road Station

Shanghai Maglev Train Longyang Road Station is the starting station of the Shanghai Maglev Train, located on Longyang Road in Pudong New Area, Shanghai, connecting downtown Shanghai with Pudong International Airport. Opened in 2004. The station is equipped with modern facilities and offers convenient transfer services, with easy access to Metro Line 2 and several bus routes, providing passengers with convenient travel to the city center and other areas.

China Art Museum Station (Line 8)

Shanghai China Art Museum Subway Station is located in Pudong New Area, Shanghai, and serves as a station on Metro Line 8. It is close to the China Art Museum, providing easy access to art exhibitions and cultural events. The station is equipped with modern facilities for convenient travel.
