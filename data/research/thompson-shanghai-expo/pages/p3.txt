## Fishermen’s Pavilion

At our all-day restaurant, begin the morning with a buffet breakfast of Chinese and Western dishes. Thereafter, savor a refined dining experience where seafood and the high-quality freshwater fish from the rich waters of the five renowned coastal and riverine regions take center stage. Celebrating the fishermen’s harvest, our experienced chefs have created a menu of Chinese cuisine that puts a creative, contemporary twist on the region’s rich culinary heritage. Feast your eyes on the Huangpu River and Shanghai skyline as you dine. Four private dining rooms seat 12 -20 guests for intimate gatherings.

## TOTS

With an alluring ambience and a great mix of dining and drinking spaces, TOTS, our club destination at rooftop level sets the mood for chilling out in style. Take a seat at the bar or on the outdoor dining terrace, enjoying specialty cocktails complemented by Italian tapas and Chinese snacks as you unwind to a soundtrack of DJ music. Or settle in for an evening in one of the cabanas with tableside service, taking in a spectacular 270-degree view as the sunset gives way to Shanghai’s dazzling city lights.
