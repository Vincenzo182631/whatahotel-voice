# New Year’s Eve   in Zurich at   the Dolder Grand

Celebrate the turn of the year at the Dolder Grand – whether with a festive gala, a stylish dinner or a lively party. On New Year’s Eve, we treat you to exceptional culinary experiences: from a gourmet menu at the Michelin-starred The Restaurant by Chef Fine Dining Heiko Nieder, to the vibrant atmosphere at Restaurant Saltz, and the exclusive flair of our pop-up restaurant.

Look forward to live music, disco vibes and an unforgettable midnight moment: as the clock strikes twelve, raise a glass of champagne and enjoy fine snacks while taking in the breathtaking view of Zurich, its sea of lights and the fireworks on the horizon.
