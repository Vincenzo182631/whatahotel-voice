# Canvas Bar

The redesigned Canvas Bar seamlessly blends into the existing decor at the heart of the Dolder Grand and is a popular meeting place. Enjoy an avant-garde design that creates a warm, inviting atmosphere, while we serve you drinks and snacks, as well as home-made cakes, tarts and coffee.

Sit back and enjoy the atmosphere. In the evening, not only our DJ but also our bartenders mix classic cocktails and our signature drinks, such as “Dolder Negroni” or the “Grand Espresso Martini” – bar feeling guaranteed.

## Grand Espresso Martini

The drink was invented by a London bartender in 1983. The legend says that a young, upcoming supermodel asked the bartender to mix her a cocktail that would waken her up. This popular cocktail icon was reinterpreted by our bar team using a carefully prepared, in-house coffee liqueur and transformed into a gourmet version.

Matthieu Doucet

### HEAD BARTENDER

Matthieu Doucet has been Head Bartender at Canvas Bar & Lounge since January 2022 and his boundless creativity ensures cocktail creations that are unrivalled in terms of taste, texture and aromas. Born in France, he learnt his craft in London, where he began working as a bartender in 2016. This was followed by various stints in cocktail bars and gourmet restaurants. What drives him? Creating unforgettable memories. He succeeds in doing so, and not just with his customers. In 2023, the Canvas Bar was voted the best hotel bar in Switzerland.

## Dolder Torte – the Patisserie Icon

At the Dolder Grand, our in house pastry team brings the art of classic patisserie to life with a modern touch. Each creation is crafted with precision, creativity and a passion for seasonal flavours. From the iconic Dolder Torte to delicate pastries, cakes and macarons, every piece invites you to indulge in a moment of pure pleasure. Discover our sweet creations during your stay or enjoy them at home with our take away selection.
