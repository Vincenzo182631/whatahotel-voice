Enjoy Zurich to the max!

## Junior Suites Grand

Our Junior Suites Grand are located in the Main Building and in the two wings. The suites are between 54 and 64 square metres in size and feature a king-size bed, a spacious seating area with sofa and an area for work. The bathrooms are furnished with a whirlpool bathtub and separate walk-in shower. Every Junior Suite Grand is guaranteed to offer an impressive panoramic view of the city of Zurich, the lake and the Swiss mountains.

## Junior Suites Superior

Both the spacious dimensions of the suites and the elegant, luxurious furnishings ensure a supreme sense of well-being. You can choose whether to enjoy your stay in a modern (with balcony) or classic setting (without balcony). The suites feature an exquisite king-size bed, a spacious seating area with sofa and a desk, along with a luxurious whirlpool bathtub and a separate walk-in shower in the bathroom. Our south-facing Junior Suites Superior offer wonderful views of the Zürichberg forest or the hotel garden.
