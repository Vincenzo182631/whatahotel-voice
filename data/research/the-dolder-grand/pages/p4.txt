# 4,000-sqm oasis for wellness and fitness

Our multi-award-winning spa world offers a unique range of well-being on 4,000 square meters. The Ladies’ Spa and the Gentlemen’s Spa are each equipped with a sauna, steam bath, foot bath, aroma pool, steam pots, plunge pool and solarium.

The Aqua Zone offers a 25 meter long swimming pool, whirlpools, sanarium, snow paradise, steam bath and outdoor sun terrace. You will find meditative calm in our meditation room with its fascinating dome made of mirror mosaic. In addition to the spa area, our spa café and shop await you, as well as fitness facilities and treatment rooms for your next spa treatment.

Children are welcome from the age of twelve.
