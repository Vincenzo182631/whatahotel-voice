### 18-Hole Course

The 6,107-meter, par-72, 18-hole course — with handicaps of 36 for both ladies and gentlemen — is set amid a windswept landscape of sculpted granite formations, natural lakes, and the captivating fragrance of juniper, myrtle, and wild strawberry typical of the Mediterranean maquis. The Mistral—the region’s renowned northwest wind—increases the challenge of each shot, creating a thrilling experience.

### In the Heart of Costa Smeralda

Renowned for its unspoiled natural beauty, exclusive atmosphere, and unique technical design, Pevero Golf Club ranks among the most fascinating, memorable, and challenging courses in the world. Facilities include an expert putting and pitching green, a bunker, and a chipping green, as well as a driving range with 20 outdoor and five indoor bays — ideal for instruction and practice.

The joy of moving with agility meets the confidence that comes with being ready for any sport. A total-body workout designed to enhance strength, coordination, and resilience. A practice of movement and breath that benefits everyone — a mind–body discipline centered on meditation, relaxation, and ancient wisdom. You will develop a better posture, greater flexibility, and a deep sense of mental and physical well-being. By focusing on body awareness and control, you will learn to move fluidly and precisely, in harmony with your breathing rhythm — achieving balance, strength, and lightness. You will improve muscle tone, posture, flexibility, and joint stability.

### Pevero Health Trail

Inspired by a love of nature and a desire to reveal a different side of the Costa Smeralda, the Health Trail paths offer an intense workout for runners or mountain bikers, as well as relaxing family walks. Along the trail, eleven exercise stations equipped with benches and fitness gear bear the names of local plant species. The Pevero Health Trail winds through the Pevero and Romazzino areas, passing by Porto Liccia Bay and Grande Pevero Bay. Stretching for 13 kilometers, the trail features eleven fitness stations — Lavanda (Lavender), Rosmarino (Rosemary), Cisto (Rockrose), Mirto (Myrtle), Lentisco (Mastic Tree), Ginestra (Broom), Corbezzolo (Strawberry Tree), Ginepro (Juniper), Euforbia (Euphorbia), Caprifoglio (Honeysuckle), and Tamarice (Tamarisk). Each station is equipped with benches and exercise apparatus, allowing guests to combine walking or running with outdoor workouts amid the natural beauty of Costa Smeralda.

A variety of paths lead visitors through ancient tracks and traditional dry-stone walls, guiding them to natural and historical landmarks. Perched above Grande Pevero Bay, the “Madonnina dello Speronello” commemorates the flood that struck the Tuscan village of Montalto di Castro. Dating back to the early 20th century, “Lu Rotu” was once used for wheat processing, while the “Stazzo di Monte Zoppu”, with its traditional stone animal enclosures, offers a fascinating glimpse into Sardinia’s rural heritage. At the summit of Monte Zoppu, photography lovers will find a spectacular Instagram Point—the perfect spot to capture the timeless beauty of the Costa Smeralda.

The route is mostly straightforward, except for the stretch leading to the summit of Monte Zoppu, which is better suited for experienced walkers, reaching a maximum elevation of 139 meters above sea level. This trail offers peaceful walks through Mediterranean scrubland, as well as opportunities for jogging or hiking with enchanting sea views. In the Grande Pevero area, part of the path is covered with a fine layer of white sand—formed over centuries by the wind that once shaped the coastal dunes.” Thanks to its terrain, part of the trail is ideal for mountain bikers seeking a fun ride.

The Pevero Health Trail features five entrances, each with a nearby parking area and clearly marked with “START” on the map. You can begin your walk from: the Club House at the Pevero Golf Club car park; Grande Pevero Beach, using the entrance and parking area near Piccolo Pevero; Piccolo Romazzino, Hotel Romazzino, or Cala Liccia, with parking available just beyond the hotel in the Porto Liccia area.

Along the map and the trail itself, you will find arrow-shaped rustic signs and red-and-white painted stones marking the way. Pay attention to the icons that indicate the recommended use of each section of the trail. In addition to points of interest and designated fitness stations, the entire route is divided into its optimal activity types: mountain biking, walking, or expert trekking.
