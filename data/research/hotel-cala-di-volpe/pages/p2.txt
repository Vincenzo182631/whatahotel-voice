## Accommodations

A haven of thoughtful comforts and exquisite local design, each of the 121 guest rooms celebrates the region’s natural beauty and superb Sardinian artistry. Simple whitewashed walls are adorned with whimsical...See More trompe-l’œil paintings, each one unique. Soft pastel accents echo the island’s spring and summer palette, while handcrafted furniture, painted to match each room’s theme, is complemented by vibrant ceramic tilework throughout the floors and bathrooms. Linger on your private terrace in a plush bathrobe and slippers, soaking in the breathtaking panorama of land and sea before retreating to your marble bathroom, where premium bath amenities await. Refresh with bottled water replenished daily, and enjoy the generous selection of locally inspired treats in your refreshment center. High-speed Internet access ensures a seamless connection to life at home, while multilingual satellite channels and a flat-screen LCD TV provide refined in-room entertainment.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda?

The largest occupancy room at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda is the Harrods Suite with a maximum occupancy of 6.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda?

Some rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have fridges?

No, rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
