Pet Policy

There’re no weight limits, but we kindly request only two pets per room. Pets are not allowed inside our restaurants and bars, by law, but you’re free to bring them to the lobby. Our doormen offer waste bags and can point you to Chicago’s most pet-friendly walking paths and parks.

**Fees:** No fees

**Weight Limits:** No weight limit – but please no more than two pets per room. Guests with pets must sign an animal waiver form upon check-in.

\*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

Chicago Athletic Association FAQ

While there’s undoubtedly much to do in and around downtown and near Millennium Park, our landmark hotel on Michigan Avenue is practically a world unto itself. Chicago Athletic Association features seven dining establishments, including our famous rooftop restaurant and bar, Cindy’s, a fitness center and sweeping views of Lake Michigan’s blue horizon. Discover the secrets hidden among the trophy and bookcases and small surprises waiting to be discovered throughout our pet-friendly boutique hotel.

We’ve collected most of the basic Q&A’s below. Please feel free to contact the Concierge Desk if you have additional questions that we haven’t answered yet.

**Transportation and Directions**

**Q: Is parking available at the hotel?** A: Valet parking is available 24/7 for $84 per night, per vehicle with unlimited in and out privileges at 71 East Madison Street.

**Q: Do you offer shuttle service to the hotel?** A: With so many ways to get around Chicagoland, we do not maintain a hotel shuttle service and recommend a taxi, Chicago’s fine public transportation system or the Go Airport Express shuttle service.

**Q: How much is a taxi from the airport and how long should I expect it to take?** A: **From O’Hare:** A taxi to or from O’Hare runs $35–$55, depending on traffic, and can take anywhere from 35 minutes to 1.5 hours.

**From Midway:** A taxi to or from Midway is about $30–$45, depending on traffic, and can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 1 hour.

**Q: How do I get to the hotel from the airports via public transit?** A: **From O’Hare:** Hop on the Blue Line train heading toward Forest Park and hop off at the Washington stop in the Chicago Loop. It’s easy and you’ll get to know Chicago a little more like a local. Back on street level, head east on Washington Street three blocks toward Millennium Park. You’ll pass under the “L” and then make a right on Michigan Avenue across from the park where you’ll see the beautiful steel and glass awning above the hotel entrance just past Madison Street. Welcome!

**From Midway:** Take the Orange Line train towards the Loop and get off at the Washington/Wabash stop. You will proceed one block east on Madison Street toward Michigan Avenue. The hotel’s Madison entrance is on 71 East Madison Street or turn the corner and enter our Michigan Avenue doors at 12 South Michigan Avenue.

**Q: Is a car necessary when staying at the hotel?**

A: Most visitors find a car unnecessary due to the hotel's central location and proximity to public transportation, attractions, restaurants, and entertainment.

**Q: Does the hotel offer complimentary bicycles?**

A: Availability varies seasonally. Please contact the hotel directly for current bicycle offerings and guest amenities.

**General**

**Q: What time is check-in and checkout again?** A: You may check into your room beginning at 4 p.m. and we respectfully ask that you check out by noon to allow time for us to prepare for our next guests.

**Q: Where can I find the pool?** A: Gotcha! It’s true, the original Chicago Athletic Association was once known for its deep roster of swimmers—including a five-time Olympic gold medalist with a couple of aliases, Johnny Weissmuller and Tarzan—but today our focus is on (mostly) fully clothed fun and games.

Originally called “The Tank” by club members, the pool has been transformed into a one-of-a-kind Chicago event space ideal for creative public and private gatherings. In fact, the room behind the hotel’s grand staircase near Shake Shack now sports a mosaic tile floor, complete with swimming lanes and “Shallow” and “Deep” markers. Oh, and if you’re looking for a pool table, proceed immediately to Game Room just off the 2nd floor lobby.

**Pet Requirements**

- Pets can be featherweights or heavyweights—but please no more than two pets per room.
- Guests with pets must sign an animal waiver form upon check-in.
- Service animals are exempt from restrictions.

**Pet Restrictions**

- By law, pets are not allowed inside our restaurants and bars—but your pets are allowed to congregate and entertain in lobby areas and relax on the floor of your guest room.
- By law, pets must be on leash at all times in public spaces—we can’t have our buddies wandering into traffic or getting lost.
- We’re proud of our clean Chicago sidewalks, so please be a good citizen and do not use the sidewalks outside the hotel as dog runs or potty areas. Please see the doorman for a place for your pooch to potty.

**Q: Are the rooms quiet?** A: The hotel is located in a vibrant downtown setting. Interior-facing rooms typically offer the quietest experience, while park-facing rooms provide iconic city views.
