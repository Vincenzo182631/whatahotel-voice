# Historic Chicago Hotel near Millennium Park \| Chicago Athletic Association

## Landmark Hotel near Millennium Park in Downtown Chicago

The Chicago Athletic Association is a landmark hotel located on Michigan Avenue in downtown Chicago, directly across from Millennium Park and the Art Institute. This iconic property features 240 boutique-style rooms and suites that combine contemporary amenities with the building’s athletic heritage. Guests can enjoy a collection of restaurants and bars, along with distinctive event venues and historic ballrooms offering sweeping views of Millennium Park and Lake Michigan. The Chicago Athletic Association is proud to have been recognized with One Key in the 2025 MICHELIN Guide celebrating the world's most authentic and unique travel experiences.

## Accommodations

Experience the best of the city at our downtown Chicago hotel, where historic character meets modern comfort just steps from Millennium Park. Each room and suite at the Chicago Athletic Association showcases the building’s rich past with warm wood details, leather touches and custom finishes. Whether you’re settling into a cozy room or spreading out in a spacious two-bedroom suite, you’ll soak in the energy and skyline views of downtown Chicago throughout your stay.

## Explore Our Hotel

Heritage Bikes

The Chicago Athletic Association hotel offers seasonal bike borrowing to hotel guests, featuring our custom Heritage Bicycles. Explore the city while enjoying the convenience of onsite rentals.

Fitness Center

Our fitness facility is open 24 hours a day and offers access to two Peloton bikes, a rowing machine, elliptical machines, and more!

Cindy’s

Perched atop the 13th floor under a glass atrium, Cindy's welcomes guests with sweeping views of Millennium Park, Lake Michigan, and beyond. With vibrant service, an all-season terrace, and an extensive beverage menu that intertwines naturally with the dishes, every detail of your experience is thoughtfully crafted to make lasting memories.

Game Room

The original billiards room, Game Room, is a refined Chicago bar and social hub, where cocktail culture meets classic brews, elevated comfort fare, and timeless games including billiards, shuffleboard, chess, and checkers.

TopGolf Swing Suite

Located on the hotel’s ground floor level, the Topgolf Swing Suite features an advanced indoor golf and interactive gaming simulator, offering virtual golf experiences and more to hotel guests and locals alike. The bay accommodates up to 8 people for a max of 4 hours, and offers food & beverage service operated by Boka Restaurant Group.

### We Welcome Your Pet

Your furry friends are welcome here. There’re no weight limits, but we kindly request only two pets per room. Pets are not allowed inside our restaurants and bars, by law, but you’re free to bring them to the lobby. Our doormen offer waste bags and can point you to Chicago’s most pet-friendly walking paths and parks. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.
