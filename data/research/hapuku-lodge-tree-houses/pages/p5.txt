# Lodge Suites

#### Luxury accommodation in Kaikōura \| New Zealand

Suites that invite you to linger longer.

A study in comfort, the Lodge accommodation is where spacious, well-appointed living meets the dreamlike backdrop of farm and mountain vistas.

## Lodge Suite

Expansive and elegantly appointed, our two Lodge Suites offer a separate living area with a stone fireplace, providing a cosy retreat with panoramic views of the Kaikōura mountains and deer paddocks.

- Separate living room with stone fireplace
- Custom-made king-size bed (can be configured as twin beds)
- Deep two-person Japanese soaking tub
- Walk-in rain shower
- Private balcony or patio with scenic views
- Sofa in lounge can be converted into a single bed

## Hapuku Room

The Hapuku Room is a spacious, light-filled suite featuring a stone fireplace, offering sweeping views of the lodge’s lap pool, deer paddocks, Kaikōura mountains, and the Pacific Ocean.

- Open-plan layout with seating area and writing desk
- Custom-made king-size bed
- Freestanding infinity soaking tub and walk-in rain shower
- Private balcony with panoramic views
- Private stairway access to the pool area

## Lodge Room

Our Lodge Room offers an intimate space, perfect for couples seeking comfort with stunning views of the Kaikōura mountain range.

- Custom-made king-size bed (can be configured as twin beds)
- Two-person Japanese soaking tub and walk-in rain shower
- Private outdoor terrace with mountain views
