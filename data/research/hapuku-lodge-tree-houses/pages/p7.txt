# Spa Treatments

#### Kawakawa Studio Spa is open to guests and visitors by appointment

Our spa treatments are inspired by the wild beauty of Kaikōura and grounded in traditional healing practices.

From signature massages to tailored facials and wellness packages, each experience is crafted to restore balance throughout your body, mind and spirit.

Whether you’re staying overnight or simply visiting for the day, we welcome you to relax and recharge.

## SIGNATURE TREATMENTS

- Pinda Massage **$280**

Heated cotton pouches filled with sea salt, herbs, and essential oils are used to massage the body, helping relieve tension and promote full-body alignment. (60 mins)
- NZ Glacial Clay & Mānuka Facial **$250**

A nourishing facial with local glacial clay, mānuka oil, and organic botanicals to cleanse, brighten and hydrate. (50 mins)
- Kawakawa Rose Facial **$250**​Soothing and balancing mask treatment with Kawakawa and rose oil. Calming irritated skin and hydrating to promote natural healing. (50 mins)
- Prenatal Ritual **$220**A nurturing treatment designed to provide comfort, relaxation and relief through your pregnancy journey.

## PACKAGES MENU

Combine treatments for complete mind-body renewal

- Relaxation Package **$260 / 1hr 30min**

Increase your sense of calm and wellbeing by combining a 60-min aromatherapy massage with a 20-min herbal bath.
- Detox Package **$510 / 2hr 20min**

Designed to cleanse and recharge, this package blends a 60-minute invigorating massage with a 45-minute facial and a 20-minute herbal soak for deep renewal inside and out.
- Restore Package **$330 + $35 / 1hr 40min + optional bath**

Rebuild your radiance. Choose a 60-minute massage, then add a 30-minute facial – either lymphatic drainage with bee venom or an uplifting facial massage. Optional 20-minute restorative bath with handmade salts.

The ultimate ritual. Our signature Pinda massage flows into 30 minutes of facial massage to leave you glowing and deeply relaxed. Optional 20-minute luxury bath with geranium and rose petals.
