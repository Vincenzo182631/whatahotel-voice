# Kaikōura Activities

#### Discover the best things to do in Kaikōura

Kaikōura is made for exploration. Known as the Marine Serengeti, this unique corner of New Zealand offers rare wildlife encounters, stunning scenery, and a mix of land and sea adventures.

Where Nature Comes to You.

Whether you’re here for the whales, the trails, or the views, there’s an experience waiting for every kind of traveller. Let our team help you choose and book the right experience for your interests. We work closely with trusted local operators to ensure every adventure is safe, memorable and personal.

Contact Us

-

**Whale-Watching Tours**

See the giants of the deep with local tour operators such as Whale Watch Kaikōura and Wings Over Whales. Sperm whales are present year-round, with seasonal visits from humpbacks, orca, and more.

Find out more

-

**Swim or Kayak with Dolphins and Seals**

Get up close to Kaikōura’s marine life with operators such as Dolphin Encounter and Kaikoura Kayaks. You might even spot a super pod of dusky dolphins along the coast.

Find out more

-

**Kaikōura Seal Colony**

Watch fur seals bask on the rocks or play in the waves just south of town. You can also spot them at Mangamaunu beach and Ohau Point near the Lodge.

Find out more

-

**Kaikōura Peninsula Walkway**

A scenic coastal trail with lookouts over cliffs, ocean and wildlife. This three-hour loop combines history, geology and unbeatable views.

Find out more

-

**EcoZip Adventures**

Zipline through native forest and across gullies while learning about local ecology and culture. An opportunity for couples, families and thrill-seekers alike to see Kaikōura from a whole new perspective.

Find out more

-

**Mountain Biking Trails**

Borrow bikes from the Lodge and hit local trails like Middle Hill or the Inland Kaikōura Range. A great way to explore off the beaten path.

Find out more

-

**Scenic Flights**

Take to the skies with a helicopter or plane tour for a unique view of Kaikōura’s mountains, ocean and marine life.

Find out more
