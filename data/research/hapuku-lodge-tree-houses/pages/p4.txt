## The Olive House

Once home to the estate’s olive press, the Olive House has been transformed into a spacious, standalone residence that comfortably accommodates up to six guests. This two-storey home combines rustic charm with modern luxury, offering expansive living areas, custom-crafted furnishings, and sweeping views of the Kaikōura mountains and Pacific Ocean.

**Bedroom 1 (Upper Floor)**

- Extra-large king-size bed
- Private balcony with mountain views
- En-suite bathroom with deep-soaking tub and rain shower

**Bedroom 2 (Upper Floor)**

- A mirror of bedroom 1
- Extra-large king-size bed
- Private balcony overlooking the Pacific Ocean
- En-suite bathroom with deep-soaking tub and rain shower

**Living Space (Upper Floor)**

- Living space with plush seating and large fireplace
- Two balconies with mountain and ocean views
- Kitchenette for snacks, tea and coffee

**Bedroom 3 (Ground Floor)**

- Extra-large king-size bed
- Private entrance via carport and patio with garden views
- En-suite bathroom with walk-in rain shower and spa bath
- Wheelchair accessible

All bedrooms can be configured as twin beds by request.
