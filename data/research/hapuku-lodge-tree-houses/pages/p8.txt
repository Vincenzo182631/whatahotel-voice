# WELLNESS

#### We invite you to pause and unwind for your wellbeing

Indulge in a spa treatment inspired by the wild beauty of Kaikōura, swim in the mineral pool or let the sauna surround you with restorative warmth.

View treatments

**What does wellness look like at Hapuku Lodge + Tree Houses?**

-

**Spa Treatments**

Signature therapies that blend traditional techniques with New Zealand botanicals to relax, restore, and revive body and mind.

-

**Facials**

Deeply restorative facials use beautiful, natural ingredients such as glacial clay, algae and minerals to nourish, cleanse and hydrate.

-

**Massages**

From deeply soothing to energising, each massage is tailored to dissolve stress and encourage full-body wellbeing.

-

**Movement & Outdoors**

Take to the trails with complimentary bikes for adults and children, or stretch your legs along the lodge’s private walking paths and beach tracks.

-

**Sauna**

Step into stillness as warmth surrounds you and your gaze drifts across the deer paddock to the Kaikōura mountains beyond. Our wood-panelled sauna offers deep muscle relief and a moment of solitude with one of the lodge’s most sublime views.

-

**Magnesium Pool**

Sink into calm. The heated magnesium mineral pool eases tension and promotes recovery through naturally therapeutic minerals. Swim year-round, rain or shine, and feel a sense of balance restored as soon as you enter the water.

-

**Wellness Gym**

Whether you’re after a mindful morning stretch or a high-intensity workout, our gym is ready. This private, light-filled space is equipped for all levels of movement, from strength sessions and cardio bursts to slower, grounding routines.

## DINING WITH WELLNESS

Garden-to-table nourishment.

At Hapuku, we believe food is the foundation of wellbeing. Our chefs harvest herbs, greens, and fruit straight from our kitchen garden. We thoughtfully source what we don’t grow, and design menus that celebrate wholefoods, seasonal vegetables, and sustainably caught seafood. From freshly picked produce to nutrient-rich dishes, every meal is created to enhance your stay.
