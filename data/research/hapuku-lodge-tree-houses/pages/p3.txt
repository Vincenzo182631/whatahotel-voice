# TREE HOUSES

#### Unique accommodation in the treetops of Kaikōura

A luxurious version of the perfect childhood retreat.

Perched in the canopy of native trees, including kānuka and pūriri, our Tree Houses bring you closer to nature. Designed for couples or families, they’re where comfort meets adventure.

## One-Bedroom Tree House

Ideal for couples or honeymooners, the One-Bedroom Tree House is a private sanctuary nestled in the treetops. With expansive windows, guests can enjoy uninterrupted views of the surrounding landscape, creating an intimate connection with nature.

- Elevated 10m up in the canopy
- Custom-made king-size bed or twin configuration
- Mountain and ocean views
- Private balcony
- Deep-soaking bath
- Walk-in shower
- Romantic fireplace
- Underfloor heating
- Smart TV and Wi-Fi
- Complimentary minibar

## Two Storey Family Tree House

Designed with space and connection in mind, the Family Tree House consists of two ensuite bedrooms, an Upper and a Lower Branch, connected by an internal staircase. This set-up provides both privacy and togetherness, making it perfect for family getaways.

Upper Branch:

- Custom-made king-size bed
- Mountain and ocean views
- Romantic fireplace
- Private balcony
- Deep-soaking bath
- Walk-in shower

Lower Branch:

- Twin beds
- Ensuite bathroom
- Private balcony
- Cosy space ideal for children

Both rooms include:

- Smart TV and Wi-Fi
- Minibar stocked with local treats
- Configurations available as twin beds or a king bed.
