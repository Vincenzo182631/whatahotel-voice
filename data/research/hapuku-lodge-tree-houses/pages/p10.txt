# Kaikōura Family Holiday

#### Adventure, comfort, and connection for the whole family.

Adventure, comfort, and connection for the whole family.

Bring the family together at Hapuku Lodge + Tree Houses, where space, comfort, and nature combine for an unforgettable New Zealand escape. Our two-bedroom Tree Houses and three-bedroom Olive House offer room for everyone, surrounded by the beauty of Kaikōura’s wild coastline and mountains.

Highlights Of Your Stay

- Two-bedroom Family Tree House with separate sleeping spaces
- Three-bedroom Olive House for larger families or multi-generational stays
- Family-friendly dining spaces with a larger private table available as well

- Kids’ menus available in the restaurant
- Complimentary bikes and helmets
- Colouring books, crayons, and board games

- Deer spotting from your private deck
- Outdoor adventures right on your doorstep, including trails, beaches, and marine life tours
- Access to spa, sauna, and heated mineral pool (great for a splash after a day of exploring)

## Experience Kaikōura

Where family time is effortless and extraordinary.

Let the kids roam free in wide-open spaces, marvel at deer grazing at dusk, and share stories over fresh, locally inspired meals.

Whether it’s kayaking with seals, exploring coastal trails, or simply relaxing by the pool, every day at Hapuku promises memories to treasure.

#### Dine
