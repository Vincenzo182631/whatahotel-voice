# Stay where the mountains meet the sea

#### Luxury accommodation in Kaikōura \| New Zealand

Immerse Yourself in Nature at

Hapuku Lodge + Tree Houses.

Wake up in a Tree House, where birdsong greets the morning. Check in to a spacious lodge suite with a backdrop of farm and mountain vistas. Or gather your friends and family in the heritage ambience of the Olive House.

All stays at Hapuku Lodge + Tree Houses include:

-

Three-course gourmet dinner each evening

-

Chef-cooked breakfast each morning

-

Private deep-soaking bath in every room

-

Access to our spa, sauna and heated mineral pool

-

Complimentary minibar in your room

-

Guided nature walk & stargazing

-

Use of mountain bikes and walking trails

-

Daily, and evening turn-down, services

TREE HOUSES

## TREE HOUSES

Perched in the canopy of native trees, including kānuka and pūriri, our Tree Houses get you closer to the mountains, the ocean, and the birds. A luxurious version of the perfect childhood retreat, with options for couples or families.

Every Tree House stay includes:

- Sumptuous king bed (or twin option)
- Elevated among native trees with panoramic views
- Large windows to watch the sunrise or stargaze
- Deep-soaking baths with mountain or ocean views
- Fireplace and underfloor heating for year-round comfort
- Options for one-bedroom or two-bedroom layouts

The Lodge

## The Lodge

Suites and rooms that invite you to linger longer. A study in comfort, the Lodge accommodation is where spacious, well-appointed living meets the dreamlike backdrop of farm and mountain vistas.

Every Lodge Suite includes:

- Feature fireplace, and underfloor heating
- Deep-soaking bath and walk-in shower
- Private outdoor seating area
- Expansive layout with separate living space
- Farm, deer-paddock and mountain views

Every Lodge Room includes:

- Sumptuous king bed
- Intimate, light-filled space
- Mountain or paddock views
- Soaking bath and private deck

THE OLIVE HOUSE

03/03

## THE OLIVE HOUSE

Gather your family or friends and settle in to the heritage of Hapuku. This three-bedroom house, once home to our olive press, weaves together elegant spaces where memories are meant to be made.

Your Olive House stay includes:

- Three bedrooms and three bathrooms
- Ideal for families or groups
- Kitchenette and spacious living area
- Balconies with both ocean and mountain views
- Indoor fireplace and deep soaking baths
- Exclusive use of a standalone, character-filled residence
