A Culinary Journey Through Mexico
Dining
Indulge in a refined Mexican journey of flavors, artfully crafted with locally sourced ingredients, while taking in sweeping views from above at our rooftop restaurant in Mexico City.
Pasaje
Each evening at 6:00 PM, as sunset casts golden light across the skyline, The Ritz-Carlton unveils its defining moment above Mexico City, set within Carlotta Reforma.
A curated blend of Mexican sounds—prehispanic, mariachi, and echoes of “La Llorona”—sets the tone for Pasaje, a cocktail of tequila, citrus, and warm spices.
An aromatic profile inspired by Día de Muertos and subtle ceremonial elements transform the space into a living moment. A signature experience designed to be felt, remembered, and returned to.

At Our Restaurant You Don’t Look at the Skyline; the Skyline Looks Back at You.
Where the City Becomes Architecture
Reflective surfaces inspired in Art Deco dissolve the boundary between interior and city, allowing Mexico City to enter the space and become part of the experience. The city becomes both spectator and spectacle, revealing its beauty from unexpected angles. Inspired by the tradition of sobremesa and the warmth of large Mexican family gatherings, the modular tables transform the restaurant into a stage for spontaneous encounters, conversation, and connection. Like a contemporary frame around Chapultepec Castle, the architecture creates the feeling that, for a moment, the city belongs exclusively to those gathered around the table.
