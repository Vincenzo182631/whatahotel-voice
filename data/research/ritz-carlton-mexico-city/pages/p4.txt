Spa Treatments Inspired by Indigenous Traditions
Reawaken the senses and find your balance at The Ritz-Carlton Spa. Located inside The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City, this urban sanctuary offers locally sourced ingredients and botanicals combined with the healing and spiritual traditions of Mexico City's Aztec culture transformed into uniquely crafted treatments and experiences. The Ritz-Carlton Meaningful Wellness Journeys nurture the body, mind, and skin by providing experiences and habits to build inherent resilience, strength, and fortitude. For busy schedules, the Timeless Capsule is a private space for express treatments. The Zero Gravity Chair positions the body to induce a sense of weightlessness by elevating the feet to the same level as the heart, leaving you stress-free and energized. Enjoy access to an assortment of luxury spa amenities, including a steam room, sauna, and time capsule treatments. Or simply choose to relax in our Mexico City wellness spa pool while taking in the spectacular views of the city.
Featured Treatments
Time Together Massage
Enjoy the experience of shared relaxation and well-being in our Couples Treatment Room. A customized massage enjoyed side-by-side with your special someone.
Relaxation Massage
Unwind and de-stress with this aromatic bespoke treatment. This soothing aromatherapy massage uses light to moderate pressure and a blend of essential oils to melt away tension.
Deep Muscle Massage
Recharge your body with this powerful massage designed to alleviate deep-seated tension and muscular stress. Using deep movements and stretching techniques concentrated on specific areas of concern, helping to relieve common discomforts and sports-related tensions.
The Men’s Massage
The ultimate male escape. Unwind in style with this rebalancing and intensely relaxing treatment that includes a full body massage using heated stones, a facial massage, and a soothing scalp massage.
Fusion Back Massage
Revive tired and fatigued muscles with this luxurious chair massage using moderate to firm pressure to leave a feeling of rejuvenation.
