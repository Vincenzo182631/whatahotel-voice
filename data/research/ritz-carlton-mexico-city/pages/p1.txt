Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City
Rising 58 stories above Chapultepec Park, The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City, is a luxury hotel in Mexico City, with liberating views of the capital—from historic Chapultepec Forest to vibrant Paseo de la Reforma. Inspired by Mexican traditions and landscapes, this modern hotel in Mexico City invites guests to reconnect and rediscover. Whether you're seeking hotels in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City or unique accommodations in Cuauhtémoc, Mexico City, every detail—from Mexican cuisine to indigenous spa botanicals—ignites imagination and enlightens souls. Discover Mexico City from sunrise to sunset at our luxury hotel. Helipads are available less than a mile away, with courtesy transportation for guests.

Revealing Mexico City Through Art, Memory, and Imagination.
A New Perspective, Written in Architecture
Our property’s architecture is an artistic dialogue between what Mexico City was, is, and is to become.
Smoke-like forms move through our spaces as a symbol of cleansing and renewal. Art, color, texture, and craftsmanship evoke celebration and memory, while elevators become portals between past and future. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame the city not simply as a view, but as a living work of art.
Rising 58 stories above the city, the hotel transforms glass, light, stone, movement, and space into a living expression of Mexican identity. Inspired by surrealism, ancient rituals, folklore, and the energy of contemporary Mexico, every detail invites a different way of seeing.

Art Pieces
New Blue
Multimedia artist Omar Barquet reimagines The Ritz-Carlton’s iconic blue through New Blue, inspired by a story of chance that became a symbol of distinction.
In 1920s Boston, European glass unexpectedly transformed from clear to blue when touched by the city’s air. What began as a serendipitous transformation became a rare and coveted color, later woven into The Ritz-Carlton story.
Today, Barquet gives this legacy new life, turning blue into a meditation on chance, rarity, and the beauty of the unexpected. For the 10 elevator halls, Barquet created a series of site-specific works, each a different expression of this enduring blue.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City, with rates of $7.00 per hour, and $12.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City is Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX). MEX is located approximately 15.1 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Mexico City does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
