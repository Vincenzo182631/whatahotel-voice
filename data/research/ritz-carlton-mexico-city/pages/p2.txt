Art Pieces
The Expansive Universe
Mexican artist Emilio Chapela created original works for selected Rooms and Suites, inspired by the ancient radiation left behind by the Big Bang—an invisible trace of energy traveling through the universe for billions of years.
What cannot be seen or isolated, Chapela transforms into a poetic meditation on cosmic origins, time, and energy.
Look closer, and the cosmos becomes Mexico City: a landscape echoing its skyline, rhythm, movement, and luminous vitality. A quiet dialogue between the infinite and the city below.

The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy, comfort, and exclusivity with the personalized service and attention to detail that are the hallmarks of The Ritz-Carlton. This oasis offers a dedicated Concierge who attends to individual needs in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. Perched on the 12th floor, Club Lounge guests are invited to enjoy stunning views of the majestic Chapultepec Castle in an elegant setting. Toast the sun's descent with a glass of champagne on the private terrace and enjoy light snacks and hors d'oeuvres throughout the day. Welcome to a new level of luxury.
