# Welcome to JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America

## Experience our luxury hotel connected to Mall of America

Treat yourself to contemporary convenience at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America. Our hotel, attached to Mall of America, offers direct access to world‑class shopping, dining, and entertainment. As one of the premier luxury hotels in Bloomington, MN, near Minneapolis, we place you close to top destinations like Grand Casino Arena, U.S. Bank Stadium, and Nickelodeon Universe. Savor local cuisine...See More and craft cocktails at our signature restaurant, Cedar + Stone, Urban Table. Unwind with a swim in our heated indoor pool or recharge in our state‑of‑the‑art fitness center. Hosting an event in Minneapolis? Choose from nearly 20,000 sq. ft. of sophisticated, newly renovated space with expert planning and catering. Relax in elegantly furnished rooms with deluxe bedding, premium amenities, and floor‑to‑ceiling windows. As one of the leading Minnesota luxury hotels, JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America delivers a first‑class stay for business and leisure travelers.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America. Self parking is available for $27.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $37.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America is Minneapolis-St Paul International Airport (MSP). MSP is located approximately 5.7 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
