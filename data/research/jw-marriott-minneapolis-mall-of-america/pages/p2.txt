# Mindful Moments

## Accommodations

Enjoy mindful moments when you experience our luxurious first-class accommodations. Many of our 342 elegantly furnished guest rooms include floor-to-ceiling windows, and they all offer deluxe bedding and a host of premium amenities. Upgrade your stay by choosing one of our elegantly appointed suites.

### Beautifully Appointed Suites

Elevate your stay at JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America by booking a 1 Bedroom or Executive Suite. The luxury suites are appointed with spacious bathrooms clad in Italian marble, featuring stand-alone tubs, separate marble showers, expansive mirrors, terry bathrobes, and world-class toiletries.

The sophisticated charm of our Presidential Suites provides an exclusive setting for private meetings and intimate gatherings with sophisticated décor, natural lighting, and top-tier amenities, including a full parlor with a spacious dining table.

## Concierge Lounge

Watch the planes go by as you indulge in breakfast or a snack when you enjoy our Concierge Lounge. The Lounge is open to Platinum Elite and higher Marriott Bonvoy Members or guests who have reserved packages that include Concierge Lounge access.
Breakfast: Monday-Friday 6:30-10 AM, Saturday-Sunday 6:30-11 AM
Dinner: Sunday-Thursday 5:30-7:30 PM
Dessert: Sunday-Thursday 7:30-10 PM

## Picture Perfect Location

JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America is perfectly located between the twin cities of Minneapolis and St. Paul in an urban location with a suburban feel. Attached to Mall of America, our destination is ideal year-round!

## Your Personal Oasis

Every one of our 342 guest rooms are accommodated with a luxurious and peaceful soaking tub, along with deluxe bedding and a host of other premium amenities.
