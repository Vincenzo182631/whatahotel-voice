### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America?

FlyOver America, Science Museum of Minnesota, Mill City Museum, American Swedish Institute, Minnesota Zoo and more are nearby JW Marriott Minneapolis Mall of America.
