## Jungle Spa

In a land defined by its diverse landscape and evocative old-world traditions, we were inspired to create an unforgettable spa experience. Our signature outdoor Soma Spa provides a variety of ancient rituals designed to relax and rejuvenate your body, mind, and soul. All our treatments include the use of well-renowned products from around the world combined with essential oils for modern holistic treatments, that restore what has been lost and ensure a renewed state of mind.

## Gym Pavilion

We are thrilled to welcome you to our Gym Pavilion and offer our extensive amenities that will encourage you to perform at your peak, with stunning views of the bright blue Cretan sky, exquisite facilities, expert fitness mentoring, and healthy food and beverage options readily available. Whether you're interested in strength training, cardiovascular workouts or even enjoying a refreshing juice after exercising outdoors, we have what you need to achieve aspirational results!

### The Best Place in Greece for Families

Domes Zeen Chania is an exclusive resort experience on Crete's rugged coastline that combines the best of both worlds – close to Chania but hidden enough that you'll be hard-pressed to find any signs of the city’s hustle and bustle. Strengthen your tribe by immersing yourself in local culture and bonding through curated family activities, destined to create collective memories for the future. Every space is an open invitation to make it part of your life story.

## Kids Club

Full of creativity and fun, this one-of-a-kind neverland is a non-competitive environment where children from around the globe are encouraged to express themselves and make new friends. This is not your average playground! This place offers so much in the way of self-expression and endless opportunities for playing outdoors. Always supervised by a Montessori-inspired, highly creative, qualified staff, they will help your child blossom into a cultured, well-traveled young individual. Occasionally, find opportunities for volunteering or educational play with children in the local community.

## KiEpos

KiEpos ‘Kee-pos is a brand new eco-conscious concept brought to you by Domes Resorts, specially curated for our young guests. Planting, decorating and learning all about veggies, insects, and flowers under the guidance of our experts, will cultivate knowledge and awareness regarding the importance of sustainability in our lives. Children will transform into young growers, who will truly understand the significance of eco-responsibility. They will embark on an eco-journey in the Cretan Wonderland.

Domes Zeen Chania is a true beachfront resort where you can relax, reconnect with friends and family through relaxation, sunbathing, and watersports. Our beautiful private beach will host all your unique moments, and complimentary loungers and umbrellas, for moments of unwinding and relaxation.

Bespoke Experiences - Unique Tailor-Made Experiences

### Chania Old Town Tours

With the help of an energetic and knowledgeable tour guide, explore Chania's rich and varied history on a walking tour of the old town. Enrich your knowledge of the town's unique landmarks, such as Etz Hayyim synagogue, which has withstood time to become one of Crete's most treasured attractions. Learn all about the majestic lighthouse standing tall at the port of Chania – a true visual treat day or night!

### Samaria Gorge Hiking tours

The world-renowned Samaria Gorge awaits your visit on this exciting hiking tour, a most unexpected one-on-one with the Island’s extreme natural beauty. Saddle up for an unforgettable hike back in time, through Samaria Gorge, a world’s biosphere reserve and famous European trail with its 450 protected species, waterfalls, and riveting historic landmarks along the way.

### Wine and Olive Oil Tasting

Olive oil can easily be considered Greece’s liquid gold, and wine a proud storyteller of the Greek land’s extraordinary natural wealth. Have a sneak peek behind the rituals of olive oil and winemaking, and enjoy exclusive tasting sessions, for an authentic, flavorful experience.

### Ceramic Workshops

With ceramic art trending in interior décor, you certainly mustn’t miss our carefully curated Ceramic Workshops, with the expert guidance of ceramics professionals trained in this ancient craft, passing on thousands of years of knowledge, in the land where it originated.

### Koumpania Alfresco Live Station

Our chef prepares a very special surprise for all our beloved guests. Those who know, cannot resist tasting it again, and first-comers will be left in awe with this flavorful explosion of taste. Our special treat for you is the extremely fresh koumpania – Greek donut ball with honey; a traditional recipe that dates back ages.

### Private Wine Tasting by an Erudite Sommelier

The chronicles of wine. Cretan land holds the secret to some of the most exquisite wine varieties in Greece. Whether you are a true wine connoisseur, or simply enthusiastic for new experiences, our private wine tasting followed by an amazing tapas-pairing of local flavors (Greek mezedes), will surely leave you speechless, along with the unique setup of ENINO, our wine-themed degustation restaurant.
