BOUTIQUE STYLE AND CLASSIC SOPHISTICATION

# DISCOVER PATHWAYS, NOT HALLWAYS

Escape in style and relax in luxury at Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa in Indian Wells, part of the Greater Palm Springs destination. Spread across 11 acres of fragrant citrus trees and flower gardens, our rooms and suites blend the natural beauty of the Coachella Valley with Tommy Bahama’s relaxed island spirit. Garden or mountain views, thoughtful details, and signature amenities invite you to settle in and make yourself at home.

## It’s All  In The Details

Each room and suite is thoughtfully designed with woven textures, organic tones, and Tommy Bahama's signature prints—a calming sanctuary that will inspire you to relax, whether you're soaking up the perfect view or enjoying a breakfast for two.
