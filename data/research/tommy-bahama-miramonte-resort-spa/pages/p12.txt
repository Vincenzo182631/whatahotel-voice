# Things to Do in Greater Palm Springs

A little adventure. A little pool time. Find your balance at Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa in Indian Wells, your home base for exploring Greater Palm Springs. From golf and tennis to hiking, art, shopping, and local dining, discover the Coachella Valley at your own pace. Whether you’re venturing beyond the resort or settling into a lounge chair beneath the palms, make each day your kind of getaway.

#### Bocce Ball

With sunny weather year-round, our bocce courts keep you entertained when you don’t feel like wandering far.

#### Hand-Picked Citrus

Borrow a fruit picker and a basket to enjoy the fruits of your labor. With over 100 citrus trees resort wide, experience picking your own fresh fruit.

#### 24-Hour Fitness Center

Leave the worries behind and immerse yourself in a state of well-being. Our 24-hour fitness center is complete with cardio machines, strength equipment, and free weights.

#### Pools & Cabana Service

Cool off from the year-round Palm Springs sun with a dip in our sparkling pools. Enjoy poolside service, cabanas, and secluded pools perfect for grown-ups.

Learn More

#### Golf

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa is surrounded by world-class golfing opportunities—both next door and in the surrounding Greater Palm Springs Valley.

Learn More

#### Tommy Bahama Restaurant

The Tommy Bahama award-winning restaurants feature scratch kitches, signature cocktails, and live music—plus, our legendary apparel. Located in Palm Desert.

Learn More

#### Marlin Bar

Fast, fun, casual—this low-key concept offers shareable bites and island-inspired drinks, plus a curated selection of our best apparel. Located in Palm Springs.

Learn More

#### Tommy Bahama Home

Enjoy the casual elegance of the Tommy Bahama lifestyle at home. Visit the Tommy Bahama Home Store, less than three miles away on fashionable El Paseo in Palm Desert, directly across from the Tommy Bahama Restaurant and Store.

Learn More

#### Palm Springs Aerial Tramway

Visit the world’s largest rotating tramcar and travel up to 8,500 ft. over two-and-a-half miles off the Chino Canyon. Soak up the views at the top, and don't forget to visit the restaurants, observation deck, and museum.

#### The Living Desert® Zoo & Gardens

Voted one of the nation’s best zoos, discover California Desert conservation through preservation, education, and appreciation. Spot rhinos, giraffes, jaguars, coyotes, and more.

Learn More

#### Big Wheel Jeep Tours

Whether it’s a tour of the San Andreas fault, venturing into Indian Canyons. or Southern California back-country trips with outstanding views, off-road tours are the ideal way to see the area.

Book Your Tour

#### Golden Getaway Farm

A one-of-a-kind experience to cuddle, connect, and unwind with Golden Retrievers in a private desert setting designed to bring peace, joy, and a whole lot of tail wags.

Learn More

#### El Paseo

Featuring trendy, upscale retailers, the Greater Palm Springs Valley's premier shopping district is a one stop destination for fashion for all ages. Enjoy an assortment of some of the valley's most popular restaurants from fine dining to cafe choices.

Learn More

#### Palm Springs Art Museum & Architecture Design Center

A cultural and educational leader in the greater desert community, experience transformative collections, exhibits, and programs that connect you to art, culture, and architecture.

Learn More

#### BMW Performance Center

Be brought up to speed with a BMW or MINI driving experience like no other. Perfect for group events, you'll get a chance to go off-road or take adventure tours with the experts.

Learn More

#### Rock Climbing

Explore the rugged beauty of the desert valley. Head to Joshua Tree National Park, known for its stunning desert rock formations, for an expert session with Cliffhanger Guides.

Learn More

#### Hiking Trails

Greater Palm Springs Valley has some of the best hiking in the world—from backcountry trails to easily accessible routes. Pick up maps for the best family-friendly hikes at our convention and visitor bureau.

Learn More

#### Windmill Tours

Palm Springs Windmill Tours will take you behind the fences onto the private grounds and go where no other tour will go. Get upclose to an operating modern turbine that is as tall as the Statue of Liberty and understand how renewable energy is quickly becoming part of our energy solution.

Learn More

#### Palm Springs Mid-Century Architecture Tour

Experience Palm Springs world-famous, mid-century modern architecture, with a fascinating exploration of the city's unique modernist homes and buildings. Learn about the architects, celebrity clients, and builders who molded Palm Springs with a fascinating and entertaining tour.

Book Your Tour

#### Palm Springs International Film Festival

Experience one of the largest film festivals in North America, featuring a lineup of new and celebrated international feature-length films and documentaries held annually during the month of January.

Learn More

#### La Quinta Arts Festival

Ranked the #1 Fine Art Festival in the Nation by Greg Lawler, be inspired and educated by the arts with the sunny California desert as your backdrop.

Learn More

#### Hot Air Ballooning

See the desert from the air. Embark on a breakfast flight or a sunset trip with champagne. A variety of operators offer personalized trips and breathtaking views.

Learn More

#### BNP Paribas Tennis Tournament

Every March, witness The World’s Largest ATP World Tour and WTA Combined Professional Tennis Tournament just minutes from our resort.

Learn More
