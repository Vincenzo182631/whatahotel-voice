## Pool Cabanas

Put off your plans for a day and relax. Reserve your own private poolside retreat. Cabana amenities include:

- Up to six day passes
- Private shaded luxury cabana with two lounge chairs
- Complimentary pitcher of margarita or domestic beer
- Complimentary soft drinks, still, and sparkling water
- Complimentary sun care kit from Spa Rosa

##### Hideaway Pool

Our Hideaway Pool is tucked away within the resort to create a serene atmosphere and is equipped with sun decks and a heated whirlpool.

##### Spa Rosa Pool + River Bench

Spa Rosa Pool is our most secluded pool area, nestled within the private courtyard of Spa Rosa. Relax in a cool-water pool, kept at a comfortable 82 degrees year-round or the intimate River Bench boasting private waterfalls and nooks for privacy set at 102 degrees.
