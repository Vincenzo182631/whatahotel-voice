## Resort Information & Getting Here

- Where is Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa located?

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa is located at 45000 Indian Wells Lane in Indian Wells, California, in the heart of Greater Palm Springs. The resort is near Palm Desert, La Quinta, El Paseo, the Indian Wells Tennis Garden and many of the Coachella Valley’s leading golf courses.

- What makes Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa unique?

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa is the first resort from the Tommy Bahama brand. The Indian Wells property pairs the brand’s relaxed approach to living with garden-filled grounds, mountain views, Spa Rosa, resort pools and dining at Grapefruit Basil.

- Which airport is closest to the resort?

Palm Springs International Airport (PSP) is the closest commercial airport. The resort is approximately 15 miles from the airport, with drive times typically around 25 minutes depending on traffic.

- Does the resort provide airport transportation?

The resort does not currently advertise a complimentary airport shuttle. Guests may arrange a taxi, rideshare service, rental car or private transportation between Palm Springs International Airport and the resort.

- How far is the resort from downtown Palm Springs?

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa is in Indian Wells, east of downtown Palm Springs. Travel time varies with traffic and the time of year; guests should allow approximately 30 to 40 minutes by car.

- Is parking available at the resort?

Yes. On-site self-parking is available for overnight resort guests for $25 plus tax per night. Parking may be included or reduced with select resort offers.

- Does the resort have electric-vehicle charging stations?

Yes. Electric-vehicle charging station access is listed among the resort amenities. Availability is limited and on a first-come, first-served basis.

- Who should I contact with general questions?

Contact us by calling 442-305-4500 or emailing reservations@miramonte.com. For room reservations, call 858-281-5972.

## Reservations & Accommodations

- What time are check-in and checkout?

Check-in begins at 4 PM, and checkout is at noon.

- Can I request early check-in or late checkout?

Early check-in and late checkout may be available based on occupancy and cannot be guaranteed in advance. Contact the front desk before your arrival or departure to ask about availability and any applicable fee.

- What is the resort’s cancellation policy?

Cancellation requirements vary by rate plan, offer and booking channel. Review the cancellation terms displayed with your reservation before completing your booking, and retain your confirmation for reference.

- What types of accommodations are available?

The resort offers guestrooms, suites and villa-style accommodations set throughout its garden-filled grounds. Visit the Stay section to compare current room types, features and views.

- Does the resort offer accessible guestrooms?

Yes. Accessible accommodations are available with features that may include roll-in showers and bathroom grab bars. Contact reservations to discuss the specific accessibility features you need before booking.

- Are connecting rooms available?

Connecting or adjoining guestrooms may be requested but are subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed. Contact reservations before arrival if nearby rooms are important to your stay.

- Are cribs or rollaway beds available?

Contact reservations before arrival to request a crib or rollaway bed. Availability, eligible room types and any associated charges should be confirmed directly with the resort.

- Is the resort pet-friendly?

Yes. The resort welcomes well-behaved dogs and currently lists a $100 fee per stay, with a maximum of two dogs per room. Pet amenities include a dog bed, bowls, waste bags and a pet privacy sign. Confirm the current fee and any size or area restrictions before arrival.

- Are service animals permitted?

Yes. Trained service animals are welcome in accordance with applicable law. Contact the resort if you have questions about accessibility or your stay.

- Is smoking permitted at the resort?

No. Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa is a non-smoking property, including its resort pool areas.

- Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes. High-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the resort and is included in the resort fee.

- What is included in the resort fee?

The resort fee currently includes amenities such as Wi-Fi, the 24-hour fitness center, a daily two-hour bicycle rental for up to two guests, in-room coffee and tea, pool access, bocce ball, firepits and additional resort benefits. Inclusions and fees can change, so confirm the current list when booking.

## Resort Amenities, Pools & Cabanas

- How many pools does the resort have?

The resort features the Chiki Palm Pool and Hideaway Pool for registered resort guests, plus a secluded pool within Spa Rosa for eligible spa guests. Access may vary by pool and guest type.

- What are the resort pool hours?

The resort policies page currently lists the Main Pool and Hideaway Pool as open from 6 AM to 11 PM. Hours, food-and-beverage service and seasonal operations may vary.

- Are the pools open to the public?

The resort pools are designated for registered guests. Spa Rosa pool access is tied to the spa experience, and cabana packages may include a limited number of day passes. Confirm eligibility when reserving.

- Are children permitted at the pools?

Yes. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult at the resort pools. Proper pool attire is required, and guests should review all posted safety rules.

- Can guests reserve pool chairs?

Pool chairs cannot be reserved in advance. Seating is available upon arrival, and unattended items may be removed in accordance with the resort’s pool policies.

- Can I bring outside food or drinks to the pool?

No. Outside food and beverages are not permitted at the resort pools. Poolside food and drinks are available through Chiki Palm when operating and Grapefruit Basil bar.

- Are private cabanas available?

Yes. Private cabanas are available by reservation at the Chiki Palm Pool. The current package is designed for up to six guests and includes shaded seating, refreshments and select amenities; inclusions and pricing are subject to change.

- How much does a pool cabana cost?

The website currently lists cabanas starting at $250 Monday through Thursday and $350 Friday through Sunday, plus a per-adult food-and-beverage minimum and 20% gratuity. Confirm current pricing, availability and inclusions before reserving.

- What are the cabana rental hours?

Cabana rental hours are currently listed as 10 AM to 5 PM. Seasonal or event-related changes may apply.

- Does the resort have a fitness center?

Yes. The 24-hour fitness center includes cardio machines, strength equipment and free weights.

- Does the resort have a business center?

The resort does not have a dedicated business center. The front desk can assist with printing and basic online needs.

- What recreational amenities are available on property?

The resort fee currently includes amenities such as Wi-Fi, the 24-hour fitness center, select wellness activities, a daily two-hour bicycle rental for up to two guests, in-room coffee and tea, pool access, bocce ball, firepits and additional resort benefits. Inclusions and fees can change, so confirm the current list when booking.

## Dining

- What restaurants and bars are at the resort?

The resort’s signature restaurant is Grapefruit Basil, serving breakfast and brunch daily, followed by dinner in both an indoor-outdoor and out-door setting. Chiki Palm offers poolside food and drinks during seasonal operating hours.

- Can people who are not staying at the resort dine at Grapefruit Basil?

Yes. Grapefruit Basil welcomes resort guests, local residents and Greater Palm Springs visitors. Reservations are recommended, especially during weekends and peak season.

- What are Grapefruit Basil’s hours?

Grapefruit Basil currently serves breakfast and brunch from 7 AM to 3 PM and dinner from 5 to 9 PM daily. The bar is listed from 11 AM to 10 PM, with Island Hour from 3 to 5 PM. Confirm current hours before visiting.

- How do I make a reservation at Grapefruit Basil?

Reserve online through OpenTable or call Grapefruit Basil at 442-305-4520. Large-party and private-dining inquiries can be submitted through the restaurant’s website.

- Does Grapefruit Basil accommodate dietary restrictions?

The culinary team can assist with many dietary preferences and allergies. Inform the restaurant when making your reservation and speak with your server before ordering, as preparation areas and ingredients may be shared.

- Is outdoor dining available?

Yes. Grapefruit Basil offers patio dining surrounded by citrus and olive trees, subject to weather, availability and restaurant operations.

- Is room service available?

In-room dining availability and hours may vary. Contact the resort before or during your stay for the current service schedule and menu options.

- When is Chiki Palm open?

Chiki Palm’s hours vary seasonally and based on business levels. Check the Chiki Palm webpage or ask the front desk for the current schedule during your stay.

## Spa Rosa

- What is Spa Rosa?

Spa Rosa is the resort’s full-service spa, offering massages, facials, body treatments and restorative experiences in a private courtyard setting. Facilities include eucalyptus steam rooms, a hydrotherapy jacuzzi, saltwater pool, locker rooms and an outdoor spa terrace.

- Do I need to be a resort guest to visit Spa Rosa?

No. Spa Rosa welcomes both resort guests and local day guests with advance reservations, subject to availability and the access included with the selected service.

- How do I book a Spa Rosa treatment?

Reserve through the Spa Rosa webpage or contact the spa directly using the booking information provided online. Advance reservations are strongly recommended for weekends and peak season.

- How early should I arrive for my spa appointment?

Spa Rosa recommends arriving 30 minutes before your appointment so you have time to check in, change and settle into the spa experience.

- What is Spa Rosa’s cancellation policy?

Spa Rosa requires at least 24 hours’ notice for appointment changes or cancellations. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be subject to a charge; confirm the current policy when booking.

- How old must guests be to use Spa Rosa?

Guests must be at least 18 years old to access Spa Rosa and its facilities.

- Is gratuity included in Spa Rosa treatment pricing?

A 20% staff and house charge is applied to Spa Rosa treatments. Please contact the spa if you have questions about treatment pricing or charges.

- What should I bring to Spa Rosa?

Spa Rosa provides a robe, slippers and access to private locker rooms for eligible guests. Bring a swimsuit if you plan to use the pool or hydrotherapy facilities and leave valuables secured in your guestroom or locker.

## Experiences & Things to Do

- What can guests do at the resort?

Guests can enjoy pools, Spa Rosa, dining, bocce ball, bicycles, fitness facilities, citrus picking when available, firepit gathering areas and a rotating calendar of signature experiences and seasonal events.

- Does the resort offer bicycles?

Yes. The resort fee currently includes a daily two-hour bicycle rental for up to two guests. Availability, waivers and riding guidelines may apply.

- Can guests pick citrus at the resort?

The resort has more than 100 citrus trees. When fruit is in season, guests may borrow a fruit picker and basket to enjoy a hands-on citrus experience, subject to availability and resort guidance.

- Is golf available nearby?

Yes. The Indian Wells Golf Resort is nearby, and Greater Palm Springs offers many additional courses. Resort-fee benefits currently reference a golf discount and golf-cart transportation to Indian Wells Golf Resort; confirm current details with the resort.

- Is tennis available nearby?

Yes. The Indian Wells Tennis Garden is just minutes from the resort and hosts the annual BNP Paribas Open. Court access, lessons and event tickets are arranged separately unless included in a specific resort package.

- What attractions are close to the resort?

Nearby attractions include El Paseo, The Living Desert Zoo and Gardens, the Indian Wells Tennis Garden, Palm Springs Art Museum, area hiking trails, golf courses and Greater Palm Springs’ dining and shopping districts.

- Where can I find the resort’s upcoming activities and events?

Visit the resort’s Experiences and Event Calendar pages for current programming. Schedules, pricing, age requirements and advance-reservation policies vary by experience.

## Meetings & Events

- What types of meetings and events can the resort host?

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa hosts corporate meetings, executive retreats, incentive programs, social gatherings, wellness programs, dinners and special events in indoor and outdoor settings.

- How much meeting and event space does the resort offer?

The resort advertises more than 35,000 square feet of flexible indoor and outdoor event space, including ballrooms, meeting suites, terraces, lawns and garden settings.

- What group sizes can the resort accommodate?

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa can accommodate meetings and events for groups of 10 to 600 guests. The best venue and setup will depend on attendance, event format, food-and-beverage plans and indoor or outdoor space needs.

- Are outdoor event venues available?

Yes. Outdoor options include scenic terraces, lawns and garden settings with Santa Rosa Mountain views. Venue availability and weather backup plans should be discussed with the event team.

- Does the resort provide catering for events?

Yes. The resort’s culinary team provides banquet and catering options for meetings and social events. Menus and service formats can be reviewed with the event-planning team.

- Can event menus be customized?

Customization may be available based on the event, dietary needs, season and selected service format. Discuss menu requests and deadlines with the event-planning team.

- How do I request a proposal for a meeting or event?

Submit the Start Planning form, call 442-305-4533 or email sales@tbmiramonte.com. Include your preferred dates, anticipated attendance, guestroom needs, meeting-space requirements and food-and-beverage plans.

## Weddings

- Can I host my wedding at Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa?

Yes. The resort hosts ceremonies, receptions and wedding-weekend gatherings in indoor and outdoor venues framed by gardens and Santa Rosa Mountain views.

- What wedding venues are available?

Wedding settings include garden lawns, mountain-view terraces, ballrooms and additional indoor-outdoor spaces. Review the Weddings and Capacity Charts pages for the current venue collection.

- How many wedding guests can the resort accommodate?

Capacity depends on the venue, room setup, service style and event plan. Consult the current capacity chart and confirm final capacities with the wedding team before selecting a space.

- Does the resort provide wedding catering?

Yes. The resort offers banquet menus and works with couples to plan the food-and-beverage experience for ceremonies, receptions and related events.

- Can the resort accommodate dietary restrictions at a wedding?

The culinary team can plan for many dietary requirements when provided with advance notice. Couples should discuss allergies, dietary needs and menu deadlines with their wedding or catering manager.

- Can we host other wedding-weekend events at the resort?

Yes. Depending on availability, the resort can host welcome gatherings, rehearsal dinners, farewell brunches, poolside time and spa experiences in addition to the ceremony and reception.

- Does the resort offer room blocks for wedding guests?

Wedding room blocks may be available based on dates, availability and anticipated room needs. Ask the Sales team about rates, booking deadlines and contractual requirements.

- How far in advance should we reserve a wedding date?

Popular dates and outdoor venues can book well in advance. Contact the wedding team as soon as you have a preferred season, estimated guest count and general budget.

- How do we begin planning a wedding at the resort?

Submit the wedding Start Planning form, call 442-305-4533 or email sales@tbmiramonte.com. Share your preferred dates, estimated attendance, venue interests and any guestroom needs.
