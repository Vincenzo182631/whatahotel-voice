### Resort Policies

For the enjoyment of all guests, Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa observes the following guidelines.

###### Pools

- Proper pool attire is required
- Outside food and beverage is prohibited at the resort pools
- Guests will be accommodated upon arrival, reserving pool chairs is not permitted
- Items left unattended at the resort pools are subject to removal
- The resort pools are non-smoking areas
- Music players and speakers are prohibited poolside
- The resort pools are designated for registered guests
- Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult at the resort pools
- The Main Pool and Hideaway Pools are open between the hours of 6 AM—1 1PM

###### Pets

- Pets are expected to abide by restaurant guidelines and as stated in pet waivers\*

###### PHOTOGRAPHY

- Any level of professional photography and filming will require pre-approval from the resort and we may ask to cease. Personal and private photography will be granted on an ad hoc basis\*

###### PROMOTIONS

- Promotions of any kind will require prior approval from the resort\*

\*Policy extends resort-wide
