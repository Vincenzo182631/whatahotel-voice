# a flavor-filled journey awaits

Gather, sip, and savor at Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa in Indian Wells, at the heart of Greater Palm Springs. From leisurely brunch and dinner at Grapefruit Basil® to poolside cocktails at Chiki Palm, our dining experiences welcome resort guests and Coachella Valley locals alike. Creative menus inspired by ingredients grown on property and Tommy Bahama’s relaxed island spirit invite you to settle in and stay awhile.

A flavor-filled journey awaits. Grapefruit Basil® is a culinary experience uniquely designed for resort guests and desert locals. Whether you’re popping in for a relaxing brunch or celebrating a special occasion, our creative menu—inspired by fresh ingredients found on the property and Tommy Bahama’s island-inspired sensibility—is a true treat for the senses. Send your taste buds tingling as you savor every bite on the olive grove patio, where seating is woven between mature olive trees. Elevate the evening as the patio comes to life with fire pits, lantern lights, a signature firewall, and bocce ball courts.

A play on the word cheeky, Chiki Palm is the fun new poolside bar at the Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort. Offering poolside service of savory small bites and a paradise of cocktails, it’s the perfect spot to unwind solo or socialize with family and friends, old and new. Pink-and-white striped umbrellas and straws make the perfect accessories (and photo backdrop) for this retreat. Sink into your lounge chair and listen to the palms sway overhead… Sip. Sun. Repeat.

#### FIRESIDE DINING &  LIVE ENTERTAINMENT

Mix and mingle "al fresco"! The Olive Grove is flourishing with mature, sun-kissed olive trees, providing a tranquil space for you to enjoy the comfortable outdoor seating surrounded by the sweeping views of the Santa Rosa Mountains. At night, our twinkling lights and fire pits create a magical place perfect for unwinding and savoring the island life. Immerse yourself in island vibes with live acoustic sets every Thursday through Saturday from 6pm - 9pm.
