A play on the word cheeky, Chiki Palm is the fun new poolside bar at the Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort. Offering poolside service of savory small bites and a paradise of cocktails, it’s the perfect spot to unwind solo or socialize with family and friends, old and new. Pink-and-white striped umbrellas and straws with an ice-cold drink in hand make the perfect accessories (and photo backdrop) for this retreat. Sink into your lounge chair and listen to the palms sway overhead… Sip. Sun. Repeat.
