# Island living meets desert luxury

Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa is an oasis in Indian Wells, where the beauty of the Coachella Valley meets the relaxed spirit of Greater Palm Springs. Warm days, starry nights, and the easy rhythm of island living invite you to slow down and savor the moment. Here, guests escape the everyday and rediscover life’s simple pleasures beneath the palms.
