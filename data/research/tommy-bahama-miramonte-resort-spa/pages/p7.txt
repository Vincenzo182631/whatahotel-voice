Indulge in culinary delights where a flavor-filled journey awaits. Grapefruit Basil® is a first-of-its-kind experience uniquely designed for resort guests and desert locals, exclusively at Tommy Bahama Miramonte. Taste the island life as you sip, savor, and indulge in the allure of paradise. Our signature restaurant and bar, Grapefruit Basil® features island-inspired menus with al fresco dining on the patio. Our creative menus are inspired by local on-property ingredients and paired with Tommy Bahama's island-inspired sensibility that will delight your senses from the flavors to the colorful dishes. Life truly is one long weekend at our exclusive Grapefruit Basil® Restaurant and Bar.
