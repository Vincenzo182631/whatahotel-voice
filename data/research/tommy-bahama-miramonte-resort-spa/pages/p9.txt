# Spa Rosa: A Spa Retreat in Indian WellS

Discover Spa Rosa at Tommy Bahama Miramonte Resort & Spa in Indian Wells, your retreat for restoration in Greater Palm Springs. Inspired by the surrounding Coachella Valley desert and Tommy Bahama’s relaxed island spirit, our massages, facials, and body rituals invite you to slow down and reconnect. From treatments tailored to your needs to outdoor soaking pools and views of the Santa Rosa Mountains, settle into a spa experience that leaves you feeling relaxed, renewed, and restored.
