## A Red Sea escape in AMAALA, Saudi Arabia's visionary coastal destination

Set within dramatic landscapes, Six Senses AMAALA unfolds as a village-inspired resort that blends 100 suites and villas and 25 branded residences into the natural terrain. The striking clifftop Mesa crowns the resort extending a warm welcome framed by beaches, a mangrove-fringed lagoon, and pristine coral reefs. Dedicated to authentic experiences and well-being, we combine holistic healing with longevity science, and create an uplifting community that lives in flow with the serenity of the sea and in tune with you.

## Diverse culinary experiences

Dining at Six Senses AMAALA infuses our Eat With Six Senses philosophy, with fresh, made-from-scratch food prepared with natural ingredients. Our pioneering non-alcoholic beverage program features fermented drinks, botanical infusions, and probiotic tonics. This is sophisticated indulgence designed for celebration and your body and mind will thank you too.

- #### Joud

  - All-day dining with Mediterranean and Middle Eastern influences. Enjoy breakfasts with homemade wellness shots and kombuchas, relaxed lunches, wood-fired pizzas, and vibrant dinners.
- #### Soraya

  - Our signature dinner restaurant perched atop the Mesa with panoramic bay views. Japanese precision meets Latin American passion through the bounty of the Red Sea.
- #### Beach Shack

  - Relaxed beachside dining from noon to sunset, offering a menu without borders. Fresh ceviche and seafood are menu highlights, along with small bites, vibrant salads, pastas, sandwiches, and desserts.
- #### Bunna

  - Our take on a coffee shop and roastery that follows a true bean-to-cup approach. Coffee is roasted on site offering Arabian house blends and cold brews are infused with local botanicals for unique flavors with a story.
- #### Makhbaz

  - Artisan bakery dedicated to naturally leavened breads and simple, time-honored baking. From slow-fermented loaves to freshly baked flatbreads, everything is made by hand.
- #### Nafas

  - This cooking school and meeting space is a vibrant and relaxed home for culinary learning, hands-on masterclasses, and meaningful gatherings that transform into memorable celebrations.
- #### Destination Dining & Chef's Table

  - From immersive chef's tables with live preparation to imaginative private beachside or clifftop dinners, our bespoke dining experiences are designed to delight, so you remember each taste and carry the feeling home.

## Accommodation

Inspired by traditional Saudi coastal villages, our accommodations are built with locally sourced materials that reflect understated elegance. Choose from pool suites or villas with one to four bedrooms, and branded residences that blend into the natural landforms. Interiors feature calming tones, natural wood and layered textiles, reflecting the understated purity and elegance of our sustainable design standards.

## Wellness & Spa

Welcome to a hidden desert oasis sheltered between sloping walls, with lush vegetation, pools, and waterfalls creating a sense of refuge. Treatment rooms open onto private terraces while the Watsu pool and sound dome offer calming sensory experiences. The Longevity Center features cutting-edge recovery and separate male and female thermal areas include saunas, a salt room, and vitality pools. From signature massages, hammam rituals and family treatments to daily yoga and fitness classes and multi-day programs, wellness flows naturally through your stay.

## Destination

AMAALA spans over 1,600 square miles (4,144 square kilometers) of protected coastline on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea. This new destination inspires well-being, discovery and meaningful connection. The Corallium Marine Life Institute offers marine exhibits, while the AMAALA Yacht Club hosts a vibrant sailing community, all powered by renewable energy.
