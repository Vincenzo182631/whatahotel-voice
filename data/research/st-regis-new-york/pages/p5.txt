# Holiday Events at The St. Regis New York

## Festive Season The St. Regis New York

Celebrate the magic of the season at New York City's best address with our enchanting holiday programming. Indulge in a delightful Holiday Afternoon Tea, create lasting memories in our Gingerbread House workshop, and ring in the New Year with an extraordinary celebration.

### Holiday Afternoon Tea

Holiday Afternoon Tea at The St. Regis New York celebrates the season with fine teas, festive pastries, and timeless elegance in La Maisonette.

Curated Holiday Dining Events

### Thanksgiving

Gather over a three course prix fixe menu in the charming La Maisonette, featuring traditional Thanksgiving menu fare prepared to perfection.

### Christmas Day Brunch

Delight in an exquisite brunch and indulge in traditional brunch favorites, decadent desserts and holiday brunch cocktails.

### New Year's Eve

Ring in the New Year at The St. Regis New York with an extraordinary celebration in The Drawing Room. Enjoy live entertainment, dancing, a premium open bar, savory bites and sweet treats throughout the evening, culminating in the signature Champagne Sabering Ceremony at midnight.

### Celebrate New Year's Eve in the Drawing Room

Ring in the New Year lavishly with an extraordinary celebration at The St. Regis New York. Begin your evening with sharable small plates in our newly opened Drawing Room. Enjoy live entertainment and dancing from 9.00 PM to 12:30 AM and a premium open bar, late evening savory small plates and sweet bites, and a celebratory Champagne Sabering Ceremony. Black tie optional. Adults only.

## St. Regis Gingerbread House Workshop

Celebrate the season with our Gingerbread House Workshop, a joyful afternoon of creativity and festive treats. Guided by our acclaimed culinary team, decorate your own gingerbread house while savoring hot cocoa, sweet delicacies, and assorted tea sandwiches. Each young guest will leave with a commemorative apron and chef’s hat, making this holiday experience unforgettable.

## Festive Suite Escapes

Celebrate Thanksgiving and Christmas with the ultimate expression of holiday entertaining in the privacy of the Royal Suite. Designed for intimate gatherings, these exclusive experiences feature private Thanksgiving and Christmas dinners, festive décor, and personalized St. Regis service, creating an unforgettable setting for celebrating the season with family and friends.

## Holiday Dining & Cocktails

Celebrate the season with festive cocktails and seasonal fare at the King Cole Bar and The Drawing Room. Timeless traditions, celebratory moments, and the unmistakable spirit of The St. Regis define the holiday experience.
