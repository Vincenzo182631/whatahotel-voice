## CONTEMPORARY BEACHFRONT LUXURY AT OUR ZIHUATANEJO RESORT HOTEL

Journey to one of Mexico’s best-kept secrets located near Ixtapa, where tranquil waters meet contemporary luxury in Bahía de Zihuatanejo. At what was once a secluded fishing village, our Playa La Ropa beachfront resort hotel feels a world away from bustling Zihuatanejo Centro and fulfills your whims and sense of adventure. From parasailing and jet-skiing to alfresco spa treatments and wedding vows under coconut palms, Thompson Zihuatanejo offers a sophisticated, picturesque beach resort that’s yours to discover.

## Rooms & Suites

Discover 56 one-, two- and three-story beachfront hotel rooms and suites with sweeping ocean views, framed by coconut trees and designed with an elegant mid-century aesthetic honoring materials and textures indigenous to Playa La Ropa. Our Ixtapa luxury hotel features thoughtful touches like deep amber parotta woods, Oaxacan clay light fixtures, brass details and Mexican artwork––a nod to the surrounding tropical Zihuatanejo landscape.

## Take a Look Around

A Workout Experience Like No Other

Elevate your game on one of our luxury beach resort’s two beautifully maintained tennis courts, designed for both casual play and dedicated practice. We provide racquets and balls for your convenience. Please contact the hotel for use. Keep your wellness routine on track in our fully equipped fitness center surrounded by tropical gardens. Enjoy top equipment, free weights, a cardio zone with Peloton Bikes and full air conditioning, all designed to deliver an energizing workout experience.

A Taste Of Thompson Dinner Parties

La Parota, our resort’s unique “Dinner in the Wild,” is a simple and clean offer that seeks to disconnect you from the day to day. Created to reconnect, coexist and blend in with the nature of Zihuatanejo, our fresh products are focused on satisfying you beyond dinner. With an origin and philosophy of less is more, we have created a menu that is a flavorful journey through Latin America and our Chef’s own heritage and travels. Our menu is ever changing, as we respect what the season gives us as well as what our garden is offering, but the base for all dishes is smoke, with nuances of haute cuisine and primitive techniques.

Day Pass at Thompson Zihua

Step into the rhythm of Playa La Ropa with a day designed for indulgence. Enjoy full access to Thompson Zihuatanejo’s pools, loungers, beachside amenities, and signature service. Beachfront Dining

Whether under a palm-thatched palapa, in a poolside cabana or at our resort’s two modern Zihuatanejo restaurants, savor fresh seafood and wood-fired delicacies paired with a hand-mixed cocktail and followed by a decadent dessert.

Luxurious Spa

### In Room Dining

Enhance your stay with the refined in-room dining experience at Thompson Zihuatanejo, where local flavors meet effortless comfort. Enjoy sunrise breakfasts featuring artisanal pastries and cage-free egg dishes, or indulge in elegant all-day options such as salmon, handcrafted pizzas, and fresh seasonal salads. Each dish is thoughtfully prepared to delight your senses. Treat yourself to decadent desserts and sip from a stylish mini bar stocked with premium refreshments, all in the privacy of your room. To enjoy this elevated dining experience, simply dial Ext. 111, and savor the flavors delivered with the signature Thompson flair.
