# Exploring New Delhi

A symbol of the country's rich past and thriving present, the capital city of Delhi is where the ancient blends seamlessly together with the modern. It is a place that invokes a strange passion that is likely to fasten your heartbeat to a frenetic speed as you take in the contrasts of the beautifully still monuments and the hustle and bustle of the crowded streets . Home to millions of dreams, the city takes on unprecedented responsibilities of realizing these dreams, bringing people closer and inspiring their thoughts.

With a history that dates back to many centuries ago, Delhi showcases an ancient culture and a rapidly modernising country. Dotted with monuments that transport you the era in which they were built, there is much to discover here. The seat of many powerful empires in the past, the city’s long history can be traced in its many carefully preserved monuments, ancient forts and tombs.

It is time to celebrate this city that is seemingly a fitting window into the soul of our country. So look into this window and discover a whole new perspective to viewing a city as old as time, featuring numerous stories and experiences.
