# FAQs - The Leela Palace New Delhi

Find answers to your frequently asked questions

### 1\. Why The Leela Palace New Delhi, a 5-star hotel, is the ultimate choice for Travellers?

Nestled right in the heart of the capital’s diplomatic enclave, The Leela Palace New Delhi is like a gem that showcases the stunning architecture and elegance of Lutyens’ Delhi. 5-star palace in New Delhi, it is impressive enough to receive 2 Michelin Keys and featured amongst the World’s 50Best Discovery Hotels. This palace hotel in Delhi matches the high-end luxury vibe while keeping modern tech and top-notch amenities intact.

Guests at The Leela Palace New Delhi receive personalized hospitality services, including a dedicated butler and indulgent experiences that range from local culture and exquisite cuisine to wellness traditions and even art and architecture. Lastly, the rooftop pool is Delhi's only temperature-controlled one on the eleventh floor, offering stunning views of the city skyline.

### 2\. Where is The Leela Palace New Delhi located?

The Leela Palace, New Delhi, stands grandly on Africa Avenue in the Diplomatic Enclave, in Chanakyapuri. It is an upscale neighbourhood in New Delhi, home to many embassies and dignitaries. Be assured to have a lavish stay, whether for business or leisure.

### 3\. How far is The Leela Palace New Delhi from the airport?

The distance from The Leela Palace New Delhi to the Delhi international airport is approximately a 30-minute drive, depending on traffic. The international airport is just a short drive, making it easy to get to the flight on time or explore the city landmarks.

### 4\. What are the room options at The Leela Palace New Delhi?

The Leela Palace New Delhi brings an impressive lineup of 11 different types of lavish rooms with expansive space and extra elegance:

- Grande Deluxe Room – A spacious 552 sq ft room designed for comfort and refined luxury with garden or skyline views.
- Premiere Room – A regal 592 sq ft retreat with vibrant interiors and serene ambience reflecting royal inspiration.
- Royal Premiere Room – Expansive elegant room offering sunlit spaces and tranquil views for a restful, indulgent stay.
- Royal Club Room – Lavishly appointed 592 sq ft room with exclusive privileges including private check-in, lounge access, and butler service.
- Parlour Suite – A refined suite with separate lounge space and elegant decor inspired by classic sophistication.
- Executive Suite – A two-bay suite with a serene living room and bedroom blending tradition with modern comfort.
- Grand Premier Suite\- A contemporary 560 sq.ft two-bay suite, featuring a spacious living room, two bathrooms and an opulent bedroom.
- Luxury Suite– Plush 970 sq ft suite featuring a rich lounge area, separate bedroom, and lavish bathroom for indulgent living.
- Luxury Suite with Balcony \- This spacious 5-star suite comes with a lounge area, separate bedroom, spacious balcony and a luxurious oversized bathroom.
- Grand Suite with Plunge Pool – Regal suite with open-to-sky private plunge pool and timeless architectural elegance.
- Royal Suite with Plunge Pool – Elegant suite with serene garden views and an in-room temperature-controlled plunge pool.
- Maharaja Suite – A grand two-bay suite with rich decor and a private plunge pool overlooking the city skyline.
- Presidential Suite – The most expansive residence with multiple living areas, private gym, formal dining space, and luxurious amenities

### 6\. Does The Leela Palace New Delhi have meeting and event facilities?

Yes, The Leela Palace New Delhi does offers versatile meeting and event facilities. We are well-equipped to host Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions (MICE) events. Whether you are planning a corporate gathering, a lavish event, or something in between, you can count on us to provide a fantastic experience. It is all about creating the right atmosphere and ensuring everything runs smoothly during the meetings and events at The Leela Palace New Delhi.

### 8\. What type of cuisines are served at the Qube at The Leela Palace New Delhi?

The Qube is a world cuisine atelier, where you can find a bit of everything for every taste. Watch the chefs in full zest and zing at the live stations. Admire the making of all sorts of exotic dishes, from those mouth-watering Asian wok-fried specialties to cheesy pizzas and hearty pastas. A breakfast buffet spread that celebrates wellness and seasonality, a continental selection of Mediterranean preparations, and exquisite Thai culinary offerings combine to elevate a fulfilling dining experience at The Qube.

### 9\. What are the dining options available at The Leela Palace New Delhi?

Experience award-winning dining destinations at The Leela Palace Delhi:

-    Megu, the modern Japanese fine-dining restaurant. Also comes with an outdoor experience called Izakaya at Megu.

-    Le Cirque celebrates French and Italian flavours featuring seasonal creations and artful presentations.

-    Jamavar, brings forth the vibrant spirit of royal Indian cuisine from across the country with majestic ambiance and the iconic Maharaja Table

-    The Qube – the World Cuisine Atelier is an elegant all-day dining destination where global flavours, live kitchens, and immersive design come together, offering one of the most refined premium breakfast experience in New Delhi alongside curated lunches, themed brunches, and intimate dinners.

For table reservations, please call +91 (11) 3933 1212 or email us at restaurantreservation.tlpnd@theleela.com

### 10\. What are the nearby attractions to The Leela Palace New Delhi?

The city has plethora of malls to offer like DLF Emporio, Chanakya Mall, Khan Market, DLF Promenade or Ambience Mall. Experience the contrasts between the old and the new. With old Delhi and its ancient tombs, crumbling forts and winding bazaars, Mughal architecture and magnificent monuments set in beautifully landscaped gardens, the city narrates diverse stories of a rich past that it has been witness to. One must also make sure to sample the renowned Delhi street food at Chandni Chowk or thrift shop at the wonderfully affordable Sarojini Market.

### 11\. What are the Ceremonial Rituals at The Leela Palace New Delhi?

A symbol of positivity and hope, the Ceremonial rituals at The Leela Palace New Delhi capture the essence of the rich heritage, traditions and culture of India, with an immersive showcase of classical music and dance at the opulent gardens. During the ritual’s culmination, delectable high tea with local delicacies are served as well.

### 12\. Does the hotel arrange guided tours to Delhi’s historic sites like Red Fort, India Gate, and Qutub Minar?

Yes, the hotel can arrange guided tours to some of Delhi’s most iconic spots like the Red Fort, India Gate, and Qutub Minar. The rich history of the city can be explored in a half-day or full-day tour, depending on what suits you best. Our expert local guides, who knows all the ins and outs of Delhi, will take you on a relaxed journey through the historic streets of Purani Dilli, where you can admire the sights of stunning historic monuments.

To book your experience, please call on +91 9560093828 or email us at concierge.tlpnd@theleela.com.

### 13\. Are there wellness or spiritual experiences available nearby, like yoga or meditation centers?

Yes, The Leela Palace New Delhi offers the best of European and Ayurveda-inspired therapies and holistic treatments at The Spa. Those inclined towards Yoga and meditation sessions can be personalised both in group and private settings. The spiritual experiences are elevated with the serenity of The Spa's terrace.
