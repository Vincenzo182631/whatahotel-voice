## The Leela Palace New Delhi

Echoing the grandeur of Lutyens’ Delhi with a confluence of contemporary styling and magnificent architecture, The Leela Palace New Delhi is a regal sanctuary in the heart of the Diplomatic Enclave. Recognised as a premier luxury hotel in New Delhi, it stands as a testament to timeless sophistication and Indian artistry. Centrally located near historic landmarks and the capital’s business district, this distinguished 5-star hotel in New Delhi offers seamless access wrapped in royal elegance.

Inside, world-class comforts unfold amid vibrant artworks, hand-carved artefacts, and brass accents by renowned Indian artists. Begin your day with a lavish breakfast at The Qube, followed by seamless productivity in our elegantly appointed workspaces. As dusk descends, partake in the serene rituals that capture the timeless essence of India’s culture at the courtyard shrine of our resident goddess.

Unwind with a refreshing swim or indulge in a crafted cocktail at The Library Bar before retreating to the indulgent sanctuary of your suite — where timeless elegance meets unparalleled comfort at this celebrated 5-star hotel in New Delhi.

**Notice**: Owing to a private event, the hotel's facilities and restaurants will remain unavailable to non-resident guests from 11th to 15th October 2026. Operations will resume on 16th October 2026. For queries, please contact +91 95600 93892.
