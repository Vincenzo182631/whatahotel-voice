Elegantly appointed guest rooms and suites invite relaxation and rejuvenation after a day of sightseeing. Savor Mediterranean-inspired cuisine and handcrafted cocktails by Santé’s glowing fireplace, or indulge in Afternoon Tea and weekend Brunch at this refined hotel in Pentagon City near Washington, D.C.

Protecting Our Environment
Sustainable Stays
The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City is committed to reducing the environmental impact of its operations through initiatives focused on conserving water, energy and other resources.
Through our collaboration with BioGreen360 and the Convertus Group, food waste from our kitchen is diverted from landfill and processed into material that can be repurposed into all-natural compost, supporting a more circular approach to food waste management.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City. The daily valet fee is $67.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City is Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA). DCA is located approximately 2.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City does not have an airport shuttle to Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
