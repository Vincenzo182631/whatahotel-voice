Explore DC and Beyond From This Arlington Hotel
Experiences
Steeped in history and buzzing with activity, the DC area attracts families, couples, and culture seekers. Located just minutes from DC’s monuments, attractions, museums, and nightlife, The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City allows visitors to experience everything the nation’s capital has to offer. Explore the city like a local from our hotel near Pentagon City Metro Station, just two stops, or ten minutes, from Downtown DC and Reagan Washington National Airport. Our luxury hotel in National Landing allows guests to easily access Arlington’s historic attractions, like Arlington National Cemetery and the Pentagon, as well as upscale shopping and dining at the Fashion Centre Mall and Pentagon Row.

A Destination That Invites Discovery
Discover Northern Virginia with ease at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City. Located in Arlington, VA just 2.0 miles from Reagan National Airport and offering direct Metro access to Washington, D.C., our hotel is steps from Fashion Centre at Pentagon City and Pentagon Row. From the timeless beauty of Mount Vernon and the charm of Old Town Alexandria to Northern Virginia wineries and the capital’s most iconic landmarks, every experience is within effortless reach, all complemented by the comfort and elegance of our luxury hotel.
