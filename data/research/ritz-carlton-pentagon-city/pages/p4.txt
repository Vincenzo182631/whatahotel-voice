Elegant Dining in Pentagon City
Dining
Enjoy elevated dining in Arlington, VA at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City. Indulge in delights prepared by our expert culinary team, and meet us at Santé to sip and savor the flavors of the Mediterranean. With a variety of delicious seasonal menu offerings, hand-crafted cocktails, and exciting events, Santé welcomes locals and visitors alike to sip in the scene and savor the moment. Enjoy luxurious Afternoon Tea, exciting Weekend Brunch, refined Private Dining, exquisite events, and more in this upscale Arlington restaurant.
Complimentary Valet Parking
All dining experiences at Santé include complimentary valet parking for your convenience. Upon arrival, please proceed to the hotel's main entrance, where our valet team will be pleased to assist you. We look forward to welcoming you!
