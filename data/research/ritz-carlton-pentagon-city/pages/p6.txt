Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City
Experience the Magic of the Season
Celebrate the most wonderful time of the year with friends, family, and loved ones at The Ritz-Carlton, Pentagon City. Enjoy festive experiences including exciting brunches, spirited dinners, a warm breakfast with Santa, delightful holiday teas, and an engaging gingerbread house workshop to create lasting memories.
Gingerbread House Workshop
Create cherished holiday memories as children design their own gingerbread houses, guided by our talented pastry team. Each masterpiece is yours to take home and enjoy throughout the season.
Holiday Tea
A timeless Ritz-Carlton tradition, Holiday Tea invites you to unwind with freshly baked scones, delicate sandwiches, and festive pastries paired with premium loose-leaf teas. Young guests may delight in our Teddy Bear Tea, complete with child-friendly treats, hot chocolate, and personally selected bear to take home.

Festive Experiences
Thanksgiving Brunch
Gather with loved ones for a lavish Thanksgiving Brunch Buffet, featuring traditional holiday favorites prepared by our culinary team. Raise a glass with a sparkling wine toast as you celebrate the season of gratitude.
Thanksgiving Dinner
Savor the warmth of Thanksgiving with an exquisite à la carte dinner, featuring a Chef’s Holiday Special inspired by the flavors of the season.
Christmas Eve Dinner
Celebrate Christmas Eve with an indulgent four-course menu or our signature à la carte offerings. Enhance your evening with expertly curated wine pairings for a truly festive dining experience.
Christmas Day Brunch
Embrace the joy of Christmas with a lavish holiday buffet, where our chefs showcase timeless seasonal dishes and festive favorites. Delight in every moment as you raise a glass with a sparkling wine toast to the holiday season.
Christmas Day Dinner
Conclude your Christmas celebration with an elegant dinner, featuring à la carte offerings and a specially crafted holiday entrée from our culinary team.
New Year's Eve Dinner
Savor an extraordinary New Year’s Eve with a specially curated four-course dinner, enhanced by the option of wine pairings. Continue the evening in Santé with lively DJ entertainment as you welcome the year ahead in style.
