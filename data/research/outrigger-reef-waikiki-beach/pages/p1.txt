How to get to the OUTRIGGER Reef Waikiki Beach Resort

## How do I get to the OUTRIGGER Reef Waikiki Beach Resort from the airport?

From airport arrival terminal exit toward WAIKIKI/HONOLULU.

Go up the ramp and stay to RIGHT going EAST toward WAIKIKI/HONOLULU for 1 mile/1.61 km to H1 Freeway.

Merge onto H1 East.

Get into LEFT lane and continue on H1 EAST for .8 miles/1.29 km going toward HONOLULU.

Take exit 23 to PUNAHOU ST and get into RIGHT lane, turning RIGHT onto PUNAHOU ST.

Go south on PUNAHOU ST for one block (.07 miles/.11 km).

Turn RIGHT onto SOUTH BERETANIA ST and travel for one block (.06 miles/.09 km).

Get into LEFT lane and make LEFT at first traffic signal onto KALAKAUA AVE.

Continue on KALAKAUA AVE then turn RIGHT onto BEACHWALK.

OUTRIGGER Reef Waikiki Beach Resort on the Beach is STRAIGHT ahead where BEACHWALK ends at KALIA RD

## How close is the beach to the OUTRIGGER Reef Waikiki Beach Resort?

The beach is located steps away from the hotel! Please walk towards the backside of the hotel to get to the ocean. If you would like to visit Waikiki beach, please make your way to Kalakaua Avenue and walk towards our sister property, OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort.

Honolulu Surf Club Hawaii is located on our beachfront. They offer beach chaise rentals and surfing lessons.

## What is the address of OUTRIGGER Reef Waikiki Beach Resort?

2169 Kalia Road. Honolulu HI, 96815

## Is there an airport shuttle?

There are various shuttle services that offer door-to-door services between the airport terminals and the resort

## How far in advance should I leave for the airport from the property to safely make it through security?

Two hours if it's a domestic flight, Three hours if it’s international. The drive to the airport can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 1-hour as it depends on the time of day. If you need additional guidance, please feel free to stop by the front desk or bell desk when you are here.
