## Join the new OUTRIGGER DISCOVERY program

Enjoy more recognition, more rewards and more access in more places. In other words: A loyalty program designed for you with rewards from day one. DISCOVERY Dollars (D$), our unique rewards currency, Experiences and Local Offers are available to you instantly, at all membership levels.

Even better? Your rewards and recognition extend beyond OUTRIGGER Resorts & Hotels to more than 40+ independent hotel brands across 100+ countries.

## How DISCOVERY works

###### **Join the program**

Join our one-of-a-kind loyalty program for free, and you’ll unlock a wealth of unexpected benefits like upgrades, exclusive Member Rates, and our rewards currency, D$.

###### **Start earning DISCOVERY Dollars (D$)**

Book direct and save 10% with Member Rates. Earn D$ on purchases across the hotel. There are tons of gratifying ways to earn D$ with us.

###### **Spend DISCOVERY Dollars (D$) on your stay**

Use your D$ toward your next stay, room upgrades, dining, or other indulgences along the way. The choice is yours! Simply put your D$ towards your bill at checkout.

## More recognition

OUTRIGGER DISCOVERY comes with an abundance of benefits available at every level. There are three ways to advance your membership status – by accumulating nights, reaching eligible spend amounts or staying in multiple hotel brands.
