# Our location

Ashford Castle is surrounded by 350-acres of beautiful manicured gardens, verdant forests and woodland rich with birdlife, all overlooking the shores of rippling Lough Corrib in County Mayo, Ireland.

### Boundless beauty

The west of Ireland is a place where the rugged landscapes are a canvas for outdoor adventures, where the warm hospitality of its people welcomes you with open arms, and where every twist and turn reveals a new facet of Ireland's charm. Beyond Ashford Castle, the West of Ireland is a destination that beckons the explorer in you, promising unforgettable experiences in one of the world's most captivating regions.

### Chauffeur service

Chauffeured car journeys are available to all major Irish airports. The prices below are based on a maximum of two passengers.

**Ashford Castle to:**

- Dublin Airport / Dublin City €690, each way

- Shannon Airport €355, each way

- Knock Airport €225, each way

- Galway City Centre €225, each way

We are happy to arrange private chauffeur tours priced at €110 per hour for up to two guests in a standard vehicle, €120 per hour in our Mercedes V-Class accommodating three to six guests, and €140 per hour for six to fifteen guests in a Sprinter. Private chauffeur tours must be booked for a minimum of four hours, and for larger party requests, please contact the hotel directly for further details.
