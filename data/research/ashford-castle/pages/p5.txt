# Experiences & activities

The expansive 350-acre Ashford Estate lends itself perfectly to a range of experiences suitable for all the family, from country classic pastimes such as horse riding, fishing, falconry and shooting, to more modern activities such as golf and cycling. Our dedicated Activity Coordinator, Michael, is on hand to advise on all the action.

Looking for culinary experiences? From estate-led dining to curated food and beverage tastings, explore here.

## A world to discover

With 350-acres of rolling hills and ancient woodlands to explore, our activities and experiences ensure you make the most of your time in this magnificent corner of Ireland.

## Safety information

All the above mentioned activities are provided and organised by independent companies and contractors and are booked at your own risk. Advance bookings are recommended

**Safety information for the gardens and grounds:** There are low walls around the gardens and grounds. Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times. No diving, paddling or swimming is permitted in the lake, river or fountains.

**Safety information for the lake and river:** The waters in the river and lake are deep and hazardous. Children must be accompanied and supervised by an adult at all times.

**Safety information for ramblers:** The terrain across the estate grounds is uneven. All guests must be suitably attired and activities are undertaken at your own risk.
