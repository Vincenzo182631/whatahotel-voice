# What not to miss

Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at Ashford Castle.

Managing Director

### Eat

Our signature dish at Ashford Castle features tender, perfectly grilled lamb seasoned with fresh herbs. Paired with a rich jus and accompanied by seasonal vegetables, this dish offers a sublime blend of flavours, highlighting the best of local Irish produce.

Discover George V Dining Room

### Drink

Indulge in a classic gin and tonic in our Prince of Wales Bar. Savour premium gin, artisanal tonic, and a twist of lime, all enjoyed in a luxurious, historic setting. This classic cocktail offers a refreshing experience you won't want to miss.

Discover The Prince of Wales Bar

### Sleep

The Boathouse offers a secluded, luxurious retreat on the edge of Lough Corrib. Perfect for a romantic escape, it features stunning lake views, a cosy fireplace, and exclusive amenities. This private sanctuary blends elegance and tranquility in an unforgettable setting.

Discover The Boathouse

### Explore

Situated in the West of Ireland in County Galway, Connemara National Park is one of the most beautiful parks in Ireland, covering some 2,000 hectares of scenic mountains, bogs, heaths, grasslands and woodlands for you to explore.

Discover Connemara National Park

### Experience

Meet the people who are at the forefront of Irish artisan food culture in one of our sustainable experiences. We have curated a choice of two itineraries which includes three experiences with our local producers, privately hosted by Ashford Castle’s Experience Ambassador.

Discover our experiences

### Meet

Meet Liam Finnegan, our Executive Head Chef at Ashford Castle. By combining old-school-style-cooking with creative flair, Liam is furthering his creation of innovative and exciting dishes at Ashford Castle for all guests to enjoy.
