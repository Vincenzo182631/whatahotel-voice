# Virtual tour

Before embarking on your adventure to visit us, we are delighted to offer you a rare opportunity to explore Ashford Castle virtually. Our online tour will allow you to survey some of the creativity we poured into each of the castle's spectacular spaces following our five-year restoration project. The centuries-old expertise of local artisans and craftspeople was central to our restoration project, bringing these ancient walls and storied rooms back to glorious life.
