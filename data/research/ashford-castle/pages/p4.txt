# The Spa

The Spa at Ashford Castle earned the title of 'Ireland’s Best Hotel Spa' for its 10th consecutive year in the 2025 World Spa Awards and proudly holds the distinction of being Ireland's first Forbes Five-Star Spa. Additionally, we were honoured to receive the 'Best in Sustainability' award at the 2019 Irish Tatler Awards.

## A restorative journey

The Spa showcases remarkable design features, including three exquisite seashell chandeliers and a mesmerising 'Tree of Life' mural stretching the length of the relaxation pool.

## Spa treatments

We are dedicated to restoring beauty and balance through treatments and spa journeys, personalised for each of our guests by exceptionally skilled therapists. Our commitment to wellbeing focuses on essential ancient wisdom with modern expertise. A luxurious spa experience, our guests leave feeling nurtured, purified, pampered, and relaxed.
