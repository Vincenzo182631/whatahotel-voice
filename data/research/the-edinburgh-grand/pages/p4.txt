## Family Stays

Make yourselves at home with spacious two and three-bedroom suites and connecting rooms designed to give families room to relax together. Step outside and Edinburgh becomes your playground, with castles, museums, hidden closes and family adventures waiting to be discovered.

A Scottish Twist on a British Tradition

### Afternoon Tea

Take time for a quintessentially British tradition with a distinctly Scottish touch. Served in the elegant surroundings of The Register Club, our Afternoon Tea brings together beautifully crafted savouries, freshly baked scones and delicate sweet treats, high above the heart of Edinburgh.

### Night-Time Dram

End the day with a taste of Scotland. Each evening, guests are invited to enjoy a dram of locally selected Scotch whisky, perfectly paired with chocolate – a warming nightcap and a distinctly Scottish way to round off the day.

### A Taste of Our History

Discover the stories behind The Edinburgh Grand on our weekly guided hotel tour. Explore the building’s fascinating past as the former headquarters of the Royal Bank of Scotland, before finishing with a taste of cranachan, one of Scotland’s most celebrated desserts.

### A Brighter Start

Begin your morning with one of our freshly prepared wellness shots at breakfast. Created with combinations of fruit, vegetables, herbs and spices, each vibrant blend offers a refreshing boost to start a day of exploring Edinburgh.

Does The Edinburgh Grand, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Edinburgh have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Edinburgh Grand, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Edinburgh offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio, weights and machines., Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Edinburgh Grand, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Edinburgh have an on-site spa?
