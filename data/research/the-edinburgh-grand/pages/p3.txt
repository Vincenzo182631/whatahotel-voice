A Collection of Distinctive Dining

### The Register Club

Begin the day with breakfast overlooking St Andrew Square, from fresh favourites to energising wellness shots. At the weekend, settle into the elegant surroundings of The Register Club for Afternoon Tea, where British tradition is given a distinctly Scottish character through beautifully crafted savouries, scones and sweet treats.

Discover More

### Lady Libertine

Bold, playful and made for evenings that unfold, Lady Libertine brings together cocktails, wine, food and late-night entertainment across two distinctive levels. Drop in for a drink, stay for dinner or make a night of it with live music, DJs and alternative entertainment throughout the weekend.

Discover More

### Hawksmoor

Set within the magnificent former National Bank of Scotland Banking Hall, Hawksmoor Edinburgh is renowned for exceptional dry-aged steaks, Scottish seafood and expertly made cocktails. Grand proportions, original architectural details and a lively atmosphere make it a memorable place for lunch, dinner or a classic Sunday roast.

Discover More

## Start Your Day the Right Way

Served in The Register Club, The Edinburgh Grand offers a refined start to the day in a beautifully resorted historic setting with extensive breakfast and brunch offerings. The menu combines hearty Scottish favourites such as a full Scottish breakfast with haggis, black pudding and local produce, with modern brunch dishes like smashed avocado and poached eggs.  For lighter options, guests can enjoy freshly baked pastries, buttermilk pancakes, seasonal fruit, Scottish porridge with honey or berries, and granola bowls.

## Afternoon Tea

A quintessentially British tradition and an experience everyone should enjoy at least once, Afternoon Tea is given a distinctly Scottish character at The Edinburgh Grand. Served in the grand surroundings of The Register Club, high above St Andrew Square, our Afternoon Tea celebrates the ritual with a carefully crafted selection of savouries, freshly baked scones and beautifully created sweet treats, infused with flavours and ingredients inspired by Scotland. Settle in, take your time and discover our extensive tea collection, with unlimited tea and coffee throughout. Whether celebrating a special occasion or simply enjoying an afternoon in Edinburgh, Afternoon Tea is one British tradition you simply have to experience during a visit to the UK.
