FAQs

**How do I get to Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar?**

The resort is approximately 2.5 hours by car from Muscat International Airport. As Jabal Akhdar is accessed via a mountain checkpoint, a 4WD vehicle is required. Airport transfers and checkpoint transportation can also be arranged by the resort for added convenience.

**Is Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar a family-friendly resort?**

Yes. Families can choose from spacious rooms and private pool villas, while children can enjoy the Kids' Club and outdoor activities. With experiences for all ages, family dining options and plenty of space to relax together, the resort is well suited for memorable family holidays in Oman.

**Is Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar a good resort for a honeymoon?**

Yes. The resort offers a peaceful mountain setting, spectacular canyon views and romantic experiences that are ideal for couples. From private dining and relaxing spa treatments to unforgettable moments at Diana's Point, it provides the perfect setting for honeymoons, anniversaries and romantic escapes.

**What accommodation options are available at the resort?**

The resort offers Canyon View Rooms, Garden Pool Villas and one-, two- and three-bedroom private pool villas. Premier Canyon View Rooms are typically on the ground floor, while Deluxe rooms offer wider mountain views. Garden Pool Villas feature private pools, while Deluxe villas add private entrances and dedicated parking.

**What are the top things to do near Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar?**

Guests can explore Diana’s Point, scenic hiking trails, traditional mountain villages, terraced farms and Jabal Akhdar’s famous rose gardens during blooming season. These nearby attractions offer opportunities to experience the region’s natural beauty, mountain landscapes, local culture and heritage.

**What dining options are available at the resort?**

The resort offers five distinctive dining venues, ranging from international and regional cuisine to unforgettable dining experiences overlooking the dramatic canyon landscape. Whether you're looking for casual meals, authentic local flavours or a memorable celebration, you'll find a dining experience to suit every occasion.

**What facilities and services are available at the resort?**

Guests can enjoy a range of luxury facilities, including temperature-controlled swimming pools, an award-winning spa, a fitness centre, family-friendly facilities and curated wellness and outdoor experiences. Selected accommodation categories also feature private pools, allowing guests to relax while enjoying the breathtaking mountain surroundings.

**What should I know before arriving at the resort?**

Check-in begins at 3:00 pm, and check-out is by 12:00 noon. Extra beds are available in selected room categories for an additional charge. If you have any special arrival requests or require an extra bed, we recommend contacting the resort before your stay to confirm availability.

**What unique experiences can guests enjoy at the resort?**

Guests can enjoy a range of signature experiences designed to showcase the beauty of the mountains, including sunrise yoga, cliff-edge dining, wellness journeys, stargazing and authentic cultural experiences. These carefully curated activities allow guests to relax, reconnect and create unforgettable memories throughout their stay.

**Why is Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar one of the best luxury mountain resorts in Oman?**

Set high in the Al Hajar Mountains, the resort offers breathtaking canyon views, elegant accommodation, exceptional dining and personalised service. Its spectacular mountain setting and refined hospitality create a distinctive luxury retreat for couples, families and adventure seekers exploring Oman.
