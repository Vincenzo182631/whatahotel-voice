## Dan Accadia, Herzliya

## The Resort, Reimagined.

Dan Accadia Resort has undergone a significant upgrade as part of a large-scale project, including the renovation of the hotel's CHALET wing. The upgrade included an investment in renovating 90 Garden and Terrace Rooms and 5 unique suites.

The innovative design combines the architectural tradition of the 1960s with a modern aesthetic. Every design detail was carefully selected, with the use of luxurious marble, original textiles, and Israeli and international artworks. Guests will enjoy spacious bathrooms with showers and relaxing bathtubs, while Garden Rooms offer a private outdoor wading pool for a tranquil experience under the sky.

**90 renovated rooms, 5 exceptional suites**

- **Garden Rooms** with a private garden facing the sea and pool.
- **Terrace Rooms** with a designed balcony and an open view.

* * *

Overlooking the tranquil Mediterranean Sea, the cliff-top Dan Accadia Resort continues to be a favorite for discerning guests of all ages seeking the ultimate resort experience. The 208 beautifully appointed guestrooms and suites feature a fresh and modern design, sporting colors and décor inspired by the sea. Whether guests arrive for business or for pleasure, the wide selection of leisure-time activities means that you never need to leave the premises. Dining options include the renowned sumptuous breakfast buffet, International, Mediterranean and Middle East cuisine, lounge and bar with all-day menu.

## Dan Accadia Resort – More than any Other Hotel in Herzliya

This sparkling seaside resort is surrounded by landscaped lawns, and an expansive pool area that includes a full-size pool, children’s pool, spacious wooden decks, and a poolside restaurant/bar. Other facilities in Dan Accadia Herzliya include the pampering spa and health club, gym, floodlit tennis courts, sea sports, supervised play areas for children, games room for teenagers, and executive lounge.

Art plays a distinctive role in the hotel. In collaboration with Tel Aviv’s Gordon Gallery, works are displayed by prominent Israeli artists including Castel, Weinstein and Dorchin, as well as Ron Arad’s famous crushed limousine artwork. The garden suites feature the innovative art of Shira Barzilai, and the roof of the pool restaurant is designed by the acclaimed Milwaukee-based international tattoo artist, Mike D Tattoos.

## Ideal Location in Herzliya

Just a few steps separate the hotel from the sandy beach and promenade. The neighboring Herzliya Marina offers a wide choice of water sports, sea trips and windsurfing, as well as inviting restaurants, bars and boutiques. With cosmopolitan Tel Aviv just a twenty-minute drive away, Dan Accadia Resort is the ideal hub for touring around the length and breadth of Israel.

The hotel in Herzliya is one of 4 Dan hotels in Center District Israel, all have a perfect location and offer quality services.

## Hotel on the Herzliya Beachfront

The Dan Accadia Hotel in Herzliya offers its guests the highest level of hospitality. As the hotel is within walking distance of the beach, you can stroll down to the beach at any hour, enjoy a guided fitness session outside, run on the beach or take a pleasant walk while watching the waves. Hotel guests can also enjoy a highly accessorized fitness room while maintaining their fitness routine. Personal fitness sessions can also be booked for individual training.

The hotel’s pool area is large and pampering. During the swimming season guests can enjoy hours overlooking the magnificent ocean view. The pool area is also well lit in the evening hours, and also offers a specially designated area for children. As the Dan Accadia Herzliya hotel is only a two-minute walk from the ocean, guests can enjoy a view of the waves, suntanning on the beach, and during swimming season – a swim in the refreshingly cold waters.

## Luxury Spa in Herzliya

The luxury Spa Accadia Herzliya  invites you to take time out from life. Even before starting the treatment you will enjoy the pleasant scent of lavender and the spectacular ocean view. Considerable thought was given to the spa design, aimed at combining the most modern and advanced facilities with soothing and relaxing treatments. The spa includes four treatment rooms, two for couples' treatments. All rooms have soft lighting and lit candles emanating a pleasant and gentle fragrance. The spa’s house massage combines oil and ocean-sand filled bags, and the intensive King David Massage relaxes the upper torso while nurturing the skin with a unique lotion.
