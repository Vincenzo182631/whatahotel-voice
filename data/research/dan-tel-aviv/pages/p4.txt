## Link hotel & hub Overview

## A young and fresh guest experience at affordable rates

Link Hotel & Hub is located in the vibrant heart of Tel Aviv – on Shaul HaMelech Boulevard – close to major cultural and entertainment hotspots such as the Tel Aviv Museum of Art, the Cameri Theatre, the Israeli Opera, the Midtown complex, Azrieli Malls, Sarona, the Light Rail and more.

**New!** At the hotel’s entrance plaza, you’ll find **“Shaula 39”** – a local specialty coffee shop from the _Cafe Echad Ha’am_ group, where you can enjoy a wide selection of hot and cold coffee drinks, as well as sandwiches, pastries, and cakes from Nordinio (We Like You 2) – a favorite among Tel Avivians.

A distinctive feature of the hotel is how internationally-acclaimed photographer **Daniel Siboni** has curated and displayed Tel Aviv’s Street Art throughout the hotel.

Based on the shared workspace concept found in cities all over the world, the Hub floor is a dedicated space where guests can work, meet, play, relax, eat and drink.There are common worktables, Kitchen & Bar, private meeting rooms of different sizes, a pool table, and board and video games. There is a fully-equipped Precor gym.

The Hub also features a kitchen and bar serving a boutique breakfast buffet with a varied rustic-Italian menu for hotel guests and visitors, private workrooms in various sizes, a pool table, and board and video games.

**The hotel is not under the supervision of the Chief Rabbinate, and there is no Shabbat elevator.**
