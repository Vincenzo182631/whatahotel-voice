ITZANA MARINA

# Embark on your water adventure from our private harbor

Located mere miles from the world's healthiest and second largest reef system, Itz'ana is the perfect launching pad for fishing, diving, snorkeling, and leisurely sailing through some of the most pristine waters in the world. Our full - service marina features a watersports activity center, refueling station, and supplies store. It also services the residences that feature boat docks on the lagoon.

WATERFRONT LIVING WITH THE CARIBBEAN AT YOUR DOORSTEP

## Marina Villas

Stay steps from the water in Itz’ana’s Marina Villas, where private waterfront living meets the ease of resort service. Designed for guests who want space, privacy, and direct access to the marina, each villa offers a private pool, waterfront terrace, fully furnished interiors, indoor and outdoor dining, and access to the resort’s curated experiences.

Wake up to calm lagoon views, spend the day exploring the Belize Barrier Reef, and return to the comfort of your own villa by the marina. With boat docking facilities, access to resort amenities, and the relaxed elegance of Itz’ana, the Marina Villas are ideal for families, couples, and guests arriving by sea.
