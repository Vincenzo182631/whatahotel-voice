# The Foods and Flavors of the Caribbean

Experience a melting pot of cultures, textures, and flavors at our resort restaurants and bars. Celebrating the mosaic of Caribbean, Creole, Mestizo, and Garifuna influences that make up Belizean cuisine, Itz'ana Resort and Residences offers a creative dining and drink scene. After a day of exploring jungles or diving into the barrier reef, experience a culinary adventure.

Indulge in locally-inspired, seasonal foods made from ingredients grown in our garden and caught fresh from the Caribbean Sea. Discover the bold foods and tropical goodness of Placencia, Belize.

## Limilia Restaurant

Limilia celebrates the fresh flavors and culinary spirit of the Caribbean with a nod to the Garifuna, Belize's native people. Seasonal, seafood-forward cuisine highlights locally sourced ingredients, including vegetables and herbs from our organic farm and fresh fish line-caught by our own fisherman. Dine with soothing views of the pool and beach from our dining room and outdoor patio. Savor a unique gastronomy experience that awakens the senses.

## Biblio Bar

Deep within The Great House, Biblio Bar is a quiet sanctuary with leather chairs and overflowing bookcases. Catch your breath and lose yourself in the story of great authors with an aperitif. Hold The Old Man and the Sea in one hand and a Hemingway Daiquiri in the other.

Enjoy an extensive craft cocktail menu, a selection of beer and wine and delicious bar bites. Discover our speakeasy hidden among the walls of the library or have a drink on the breezy balcony with views of the Atlantic Ocean.

A new gathering place has arrived at Itz'ana Resort & Residences.

## Nadu

Experience superior culinary delights at our new pop-up restaurant, "Nadü". Meaning "tree" in Garifuna, Nadü invites you to an exclusive dining experience beneath the trees. Savor sustainable dining with fresh, organic ingredients and a grill-to-table concept.

At Nadü, the ever-changing menu is curated based on the fresh, seasonal produce received daily from local vendors. Be surrounded by panoramic views of the Caribbean Sea, and enjoy exceptional cuisine on our beachfront, only at Nadü.

**This exclusive dining experience is available for groups of 15+ people, reservation required.**
