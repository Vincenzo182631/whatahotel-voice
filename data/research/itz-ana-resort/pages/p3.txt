# BEACH VILLAS

Experience beachfront villa living at Itz’ana Resort & Residences, where private beach villas in Placencia offer space, privacy, and direct access to the Caribbean Sea. Designed for families, couples, and groups, each beach villa blends indoor-outdoor comfort with elegant interiors, tropical surroundings, and the relaxed service of a luxury Belize resort.

Choose from spacious beachfront villas designed for relaxed Caribbean living, with multiple bedrooms, full kitchens, open living and dining areas, private pools, outdoor terraces, and direct access to the beach. Ideal for extended stays, family vacations, romantic escapes, or group getaways, Itz’ana’s beach villas offer the privacy of a coastal residence with the service of a luxury resort in Belize.
