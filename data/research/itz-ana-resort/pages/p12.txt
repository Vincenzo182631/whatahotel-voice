## Explore Belize

The heart of the Maya empire, Belize is a tropical paradise on the eastern coast of Central America. Edging the Caribbean Sea, Belize offers pristine beaches, rainforest, dense jungle, and breathtaking scenery. Hike to towering waterfalls, see ancient Mayan pyramids, and zipline through the treetops. Enjoy diving in the Belize Barrier Reef and cayes with rich marine life. Whether you crave romance, family fun, culture, or adventure, come discover the best things to do in Belize at Itz'ana.
