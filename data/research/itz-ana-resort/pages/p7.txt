Discover the flavors and stories of Garifuna cuisine through a hands-on cooking class at Itz’ana Resort & Residences in Placencia, Belize. This resort experience invites guests to connect with Belize through flavor, heritage, and shared preparation. Learn how Garifuna recipes are shaped by community, coastal ingredients, and generations of knowledge, then enjoy a deeper appreciation for the culture that continues to enrich Belize’s Caribbean identity.
