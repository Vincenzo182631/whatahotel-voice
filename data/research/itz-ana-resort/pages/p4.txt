# Escape to Your Own Private Caye

Just off the coast of Placencia, Coral Caye offers a unique opportunity to experience Belize from a different perspective. Surrounded by turquoise waters and natural beauty, this curated day experience invites guests to enjoy island-inspired dining, ocean adventures, and unforgettable Caribbean scenery. Whether you're seeking relaxation or exploration, Coral Caye is a memorable addition to your Marina Villa stay.

- ## Coral Caye

Coral Caye is a serene two-acre private island escape off the coast of Belize, offering barefoot comfort, ocean-view cottages, a thatched dining pavilion, and a dedicated island team. Spend your days snorkeling, kayaking, lounging on the dock, and ending the night by the bonfire. Off-grid and solar-powered, it is ideal for families or adults seeking privacy, peace, and total disconnection.
