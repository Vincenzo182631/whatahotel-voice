Private Island Experience

## Coral Caye, Just Beyond the Shore

Step from Itz’ana’s beachfront into one of Belize’s most unforgettable experiences: Coral Caye, a private island escape set just off the coast of Placencia. Spend the day snorkeling, kayaking, paddle boarding, swimming in clear Caribbean waters, or simply relaxing in the quiet beauty of your own island setting.

Available for day trips, private island picnics, overnight stays, and curated experiences, Coral Caye brings the spirit of Belize to life through barefoot luxury, open sea, and moments designed entirely around you. From romantic escapes to family adventures and special celebrations, this is Itz’ana’s most exclusive way to experience the Caribbean.

Health & Wellness

## Wellness, Naturally in Belize

Find your own sense of balance at Itz’ana Resort & Residences in Placencia, Belize, where wellness is shaped by the Caribbean Sea, open-air movement, calming spa rituals, and the quiet rhythm of the coast. From yoga and meditation to massage, cycling, running routes, and restorative moments by the water, every experience is designed to help you slow down, reconnect, and feel renewed.

Begin the day with yoga by the sea, explore the Placencia Peninsula by bike, follow scenic running routes, or unwind with a personalized spa treatment in the comfort of your suite or villa. At Itz’ana, wellness is not a schedule to follow, but a natural part of your stay — rooted in movement, rest, nature, and the effortless beauty of Belize.
