# Belize Wellness to Restore the Soul

Find your own sense of balance at Itz’ana, a serene escape designed for rest, renewal, and well-being. Immerse yourself in a collection of wellness experiences that nurture both body and mind — from calming spa rituals and mindful movement to peaceful moments by the sea.

Join the Wellness Journey

**Wellness by the Water’s Edge**

Reconnect body and mind with guided yoga, breathwork, and movement sessions set directly along the Caribbean shoreline. The rhythm of the waves, warm sea breeze, and open horizon create a naturally grounding environment that enhances focus, relaxation, and restoration—far beyond a traditional studio setting.

**Rooted in Nature, Designed for Renewal**

Surrounded by lush coastal landscapes and Garifuna heritage, Itz’ana offers a wellness experience deeply connected to nature. From sunrise beach walks to cycling through local villages and mindful moments beneath swaying palms, every experience invites you to slow down, reconnect, and restore balance naturally.

**Your Wellness, Your Pace**

Whether you seek active rejuvenation or quiet relaxation, Itz’ana’s curated wellness offerings adapt to your rhythm. Enjoy energizing beachside yoga, refreshing swims in the infinity pool, nourishing cuisine, and personalized concierge recommendations—all designed to support your individual path to well-being.

## Run the Peninsula

Discover a different way to experience the Placencia Peninsula through scenic running routes designed for every pace. From light morning jogs along the coast to longer runs toward Maya Beach and Placencia Village, each route offers a unique blend of movement, nature, and tranquility.

Enjoy quiet roads, ocean views, and the freedom to set your own rhythm while exploring one of Belize’s most beautiful coastal stretches.

#### Running Routes

**Short Run (Easy)**

**Distance:** 1–3 km (0.6–1.8 miles)

A relaxed out-and-back route from the resort, perfect for a light jog or a peaceful sunrise run along the coast.

**Mid-Distance Run (Moderate)**

**Distance:** 4–6 km (2.5–3.7 miles)

Head south toward the Maya Beach area for a steady run with quiet roads and tropical surroundings.

**Long Run (Advanced)**

**Distance:** Up to 13 km (8 miles) one way

For experienced runners, continue toward Placencia Village along one of the peninsula’s most scenic coastal stretches.
