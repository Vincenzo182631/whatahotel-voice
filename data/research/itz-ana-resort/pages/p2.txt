# Refined Penthouse Living on the Caribbean Sea

Set above Itz’ana Resort in Placencia, Belize, our two- and three-bedroom penthouses bring together generous indoor and outdoor living, expansive terraces, private plunge pools, and views across the resort and Caribbean Sea.

Choose the two-bedroom Pool View Penthouse for up to four guests or the three-bedroom Seaview Penthouse for up to six. Both feature king beds, full kitchens, open living and dining areas, and generous outdoor space. Ideal for families and friends seeking privacy, comfort, and room to reconnect on Belize’s Caribbean coast.
