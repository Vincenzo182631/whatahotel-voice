### Newly Restored Rooms and Suites

Experience the Willard InterContinental’s iconic elegance reimagined. All 335 guest rooms and 69 suites at Willard InterContinental have been meticulously restored, blending historic charm with modern luxury and honoring legendary figures – from pioneering women to U.S. presidents – through bespoke design, curated art, and refined details.

Learn more

### The Willard Spa

Wellness is not something to be conquered, but to be experienced. Escape to a revitalizing oasis at The Willard Spa and soothe your restless spirit with a custom massage or restorative skin treatment.

Learn more

### Willard Weddings

Celebrate love at the sought-after wedding venue, Willard InterContinental. Located in the heart of Washington, D.C., it offers legendary settings, sumptuous cuisine, and impeccable service for intimate ceremonies, ballroom weddings, and receptions. Plan your unforgettable celebration at this luxurious hotel.

Learn more

### Round Robin Bar

Nicknamed the "Oval Office of Bars" by Condé Nast Traveler and one of the Best Hotel Bars in the US by the USA TODAY 10Best Readers’ Choice travel award, the historic Round Robin Bar in Washington, D.C. has been a lively meeting place for the political and social elite since it was established in 1847. Join us for a quintessential DC experience.

## FAQs for InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.

Does InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. have a pool?

No, InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.?

At InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C., valet parking is available for a daily rate of 78 USD .

Valet parking is available for hotel guests 24 hours a day. Overnight parking is $78.00 per night and includes unrestricted in and out privileges. The garage vehicle height restriction is 6′ 5" and features two electric car charging stations.

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C..

Pet policy description. Canine pets welcome. Must weigh no more than 40 pounds. Deposit is due at time of check in and is nonrefundable. Guide dogs are free of charge. One small pet per room allowed.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C..

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.?

Check-in at InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. from the nearest airport?

InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. is conveniently located near Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA), approximately 3.7 miles away (5.95 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.?

InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Hillwood Estate Museum and Gardens - 3.5 miles away (5.63 kilometers)
- Eastern Market - 1.65 miles away (2.66 kilometers)
- The Phillips Collection - 1.32 miles away (2.12 kilometers)
- Ford's Theatre - 0.4 miles away (0.64 kilometers)
- National Mall
- United States Holocaust Memorial Museum
- Old Town Trolley Tour
- The Spy Museum
- Washington Monument

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C. puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
