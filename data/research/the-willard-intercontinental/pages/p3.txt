Willard InterContinental offers a variety of exceptional and unique culinary experiences, from a French brasserie with coffee shop to the historic Round Robin Bar to the grandeur of Afternoon Tea in Peacock Alley.

### Round Robin Bar

Nicknamed the "Oval Office of Bars" by Condé Nast Traveler and one of the Best Hotel Bars in the US by the USA TODAY 10Best Readers’ Choice travel award, the historic Round Robin Bar in Washington, D.C. has been a lively meeting place for the political and social elite since it was established in 1847. Join us for a quintessential DC experience.

### Café du Parc

This French brasserie in Washington, D.C. offers classic French dishes with a contemporary twist for breakfast, lunch, brunch, and dinner. Cafe du Parc has been recognized as the Best Hotel Restaurant in DC by Modern Luxury and received the Diners Choice Award by OpenTable. The restaurant's seasonal outdoor terrace is perfect for al fresco dining.

### Willard Afternoon Tea

At the newly-reimagined Peacock Alley of Willard InterContinental Washington, D.C., guests are treated to the soothing sounds of the harp while enjoying a delightful selection of teas, savory finger sandwiches, decadent pastries, and Willard's famous freshly-baked scones. Available Friday through Sunday; reservations are required.
