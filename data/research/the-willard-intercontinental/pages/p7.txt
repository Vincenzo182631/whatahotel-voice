### A Wedding To Remember

3 days ago

An absolutely exceptional stay! The Willard is a beautiful hotel filled with history, elegance, and old-world charm. The wedding ceremony and reception were spectacular, with a gorgeous setting, beautiful candlelight, crystal, and floral arrangements, and absolutely delicious food. It was a truly memorable occasion. The wait staff at both the cocktail reception and wedding reception were outstanding—courteous, professional, attentive, and gracious throughout. Their exceptional service helped make the celebration even more special.

The café was also wonderful, with delicious cappuccinos, croissants, and fabulous lunches. My room was well appointed, clean and meticulously maintained. Every staff member, from reception and concierge to the bellmen and housekeeping, was warm, gracious, and incredibly accommodating. The location was ideal for sightseeing, restaurants, and nearby amenities. I would happily stay here again and highly recommend this outstanding hotel. A well-deserved 10 out of 10!
