# InterContinental The Willard Washington D.C.Rooms & suites

Guests experience the welcoming hospitality and comfort of an elegant private residence in our sophisticated and spacious Classic Rooms, ranging from 300 ft²/28 m² to 550 ft²/51 m². These guest rooms feature one king bed or two queen beds, a walk-in marble shower or shower with bathtub an oversized work desk integrated with power outlets and USB charging points. Select Classic Rooms have the ability to connect to additional room types based on availability and at an additional cost.
