## For The Four-legged Friends

We’re not just ‘pet friendly’, we’re ‘pet-welcoming’. We love to see your canine companions in our historic Washington hotel and you’ll even find special amenities to make their stay a paws-itive.

## To Your Health

Our residential fitness center features state-of-the-art cardio equipment including, 2 Pelotons, elliptical machines, treadmills, and a TRX Training Center. All guests have access to the expanded fitness center and pool located at the University Club.

Keep Fit

## First Library Book Program

We are proud to partner with two incredible organizations to give the gift of reading to the youth of Washington, D.C.

For every room booked, we sponsor the purchase of a children’s book for the DC Public Library’s ‘Books from Birth’ program, in collaboration with Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library.

Young readers benefit when you treat yourself to a great stay.
