## Frequently Asked Questions

Planning where to dine should feel as seamless as the experience itself. Below, you will find answers to some of the most frequently asked questions about dining at The Jefferson, DC.

What dining options are available at The Jefferson, DC?

The Jefferson, DC, offers The Greenhouse for seasonal all-day dining and brunch, Quill Bar & Lounge for cocktails and light fare, and in-room dining for a private experience.

Does The Greenhouse serve brunch in Washington, DC?

Yes. The Greenhouse offers one of the most refined brunch experiences in Washington, DC, served in a light-filled setting beneath a skylight ceiling.

Is Quill open for cocktails and evening dining?

Quill Bar & Lounge is open in the evening, offering expertly crafted cocktails, fine wines, and a selection of light dishes in an intimate setting.

Can non-guests dine at The Jefferson, DC?

Yes. Both The Greenhouse and Quill Bar & Lounge welcome non-hotel guests to enjoy dining and cocktails.

Is in-room dining available all day?

In-room dining is available from morning through late evening, offering a full menu designed for comfort and privacy.

Can I make a reservation online?

Yes. Reservations for The Greenhouse and Quill Bar & Lounge can be made online through the website.

Does The Jefferson, DC, offer private dining options?

Yes. The hotel offers private dining experiences and intimate event spaces with personalized menus and dedicated service.
