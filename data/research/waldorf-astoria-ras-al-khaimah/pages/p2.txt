# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah is home to seven restaurants, lounges, and bars, offering dining and entertainment experiences to suit every mood and taste.

## Lexington Grill & Bar

**Signature Restaurant**

Indulge in a remarkable selection of mouthwatering steaks, delectable seafood, and exquisite plant-based dishes at Lexington Grill & Bar. Our extraordinary New York steakhouse-style restaurant boasts an impressive wine cellar and opulent ambiance.

Discover a bespoke dining experience like no other as you witness our extensive dry-age selection, accompanied by the captivating tunes of a live band. Personalize your meal by selecting your knife, salt, and condiments.

Monday to Saturday

Bar: 5 pm – 11:30 pm

Happy Hour: 5 pm – 7 pm

Restaurant: 6 pm – 10:30 pm

## Our Executive Chef

Executive Chef Michael Kreiling brings a wealth of culinary expertise to Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah, shaped by years of experience in some of the most prestigious kitchens across the globe. From his early days perfecting his craft in Michelin-starred restaurants to leading dynamic culinary teams in diverse cultural settings, Chef Kreiling has developed a passion for innovation and excellence. His culinary journey has taken him through various international destinations, where he has overseen multiple food and beverage outlets, catered exclusive events, and honed his leadership skills.

At Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah, Chef Kreiling heads a talented team of chefs who draw inspiration from their collective experiences, ensuring that every dining experience is nothing short of extraordinary. Under his leadership, the hotel’s eight distinctive dining outlets offer guests a blend of creativity, innovation, and refined culinary craftsmanship. Chef Kreiling’s dedication to culinary excellence continues to elevate the dining scene, setting new standards of luxury and sophistication for guests.

## Qasr Al Bahar

### Indulge in a special breakfast or dinner experience with us.

Welcome to Qasr Al Bahar – the ideal spot to kick off your day with stunning views from floor-to-ceiling windows and a spacious outdoor terrace. Indulge in a memorable dining experience with a menu that blends international inspirations, Waldorf Astoria classics, local specialties, and diverse global flavors. Our restaurant even offers a designated children’s area with fun interactive features. Whether you’re enjoying a hearty Waldorf breakfast or a flavorful dinner buffet, we promise only the highest quality cuisine awaits you!

Breakfast – AED 130 per person

## Azure

### Coastal Mediterranean Dining

Set beside the resort’s main pool, Azure brings together the vibrant flavours of the Mediterranean, inspired by the coastlines of Italy, Greece, Turkey, and beyond. From fresh seasonal dishes and family-friendly favourites to dedicated options for younger guests, every meal is designed to be shared and savoured.

Signature spritzes, handcrafted cocktails, and refreshing beverages complement an inviting indoor and outdoor setting, where poolside views and gentle breezes create the perfect backdrop for leisurely afternoons under the Ras Al Khaimah sun.

Monday to Saturday: 12 pm – 6 pm

Sunday: 12 pm – 10 pm

Happy Hour: 5 pm – 7 pm

## Camelia Lounge

### Refined Lounge & Dining

Located in the heart of the resort’s grand lobby, Camelia Lounge is a refined gathering place overlooking the Arabian Gulf. Renowned for its signature afternoon tea, it also offers an international dining menu, handcrafted coffees, premium teas, artisan pastries, and beautifully crafted cakes throughout the day.

From casual meetings and catch-ups with friends to afternoon indulgence and evening drinks, Camelia Lounge provides an elegant yet inviting atmosphere complemented by warm hospitality, and panoramic Gulf views.

Open Daily: 9 am – 11 pm

## Sunset Beach Lounge

### Adults-Only Beachfront Escape

Set along the shores of the Arabian Gulf, Sunset Beach Lounge is an exclusive adults-only retreat where contemporary dining and effortless beachfront living come together. Featuring a selection of international favourites, light bites, and refreshing beverages, it is the ideal setting for leisurely afternoons by the infinity pool and the beach.

Whether unwinding in a private cabana, enjoying a relaxed lunch, or taking in the resort’s iconic sunset views, guests can savour an atmosphere of understated luxury, framed by the beauty of the Arabian Gulf.

Open Daily: 10 am – 6 pm

## Palm Shore Pool Bar

### Relaxed Poolside Retreat

Located beside the resort’s family pool, Palm Shore Pool Bar is a sun-filled escape designed for easygoing days under the Ras Al Khaimah sky. Guests can enjoy a selection of international favourites, freshly prepared light bites, grilled specialties, and refreshing beverages, all served in a relaxed outdoor setting.

Whether taking a break between swims, sharing a casual meal with family, or simply unwinding by the water, Palm Shore offers a laid-back atmosphere where poolside comfort, gentle sea breezes, and resort leisure come together effortlessly.

Open Daily: 9 am – 6 pm

Orders accepted: 11:30 am – 5:30 pm
