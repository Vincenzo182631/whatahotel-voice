# Golf at Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah

Golf at Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah

Challenge yourself to a round of golf on the 18-hole Peter Harradine-designed championship golf course at the Al Hamra Golf Club, located adjacent to the resort. No matter where or how well you play, you’ll enjoy an unforgettable Waldorf Astoria golf experience.

## Al Hamra Golf Course

The award-winning 18-hole championship golf course was designed by the Swiss architect Peter Harradine. Four interconnected open water lagoons merge seamlessly with the water of the Arabian Gulf. As many traditional courses do, the 3,578-yard front nine makes its way out and away from the Clubhouse, while the 3,747-yard back nine returns home around the lagoon to the Clubhouse. The result is a stunning Par 72 championship course with five varying tee options on each hole.

Originally planned to be built on the salt flats bordering the water edge, 5 million cubic meters of sand combined with expert shaping create gentle elevation changes as the terrain moves around the lagoons rising and falling from plus one to plus eight meters.

## Night Golf

With the region’s limited daylight hours, Al Hamra Golf Club utilizes the latest zero-pollution floodlights to offer golfers the ability to play into the night. Using a combination of the front nine and back nine championship courses, a composite 9-hole course is created, providing the perfect option for the businessperson after work or the true golf enthusiast who just can’t get enough. Either way, golf under the stars should be a must-try experience for all. We also offer a newly renovated floodlit driving range, where anyone can enjoy hitting some practice balls.

## Golf Lessons with PGA Pro

Enhance your golfing experience with personalized lessons at Al Hamra Golf Club, led by our PGA professional, Damien Scott. We offer both group and individual lessons, providing a tailored approach to meet your specific needs and skill level.

Whether you’re a beginner looking to learn the fundamentals or an experienced player aiming to refine your technique, our golf lessons offer the perfect opportunity to try something new or elevate your skills during your vacation. Immerse yourself in the joy of golf and unlock your potential with expert guidance in the stunning surroundings of Al Hamra Golf Club.
