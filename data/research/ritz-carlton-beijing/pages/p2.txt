Beijing With a British Touch
Suites & Rooms
Reflecting the elegance of an English country home, our hotel accommodations in Beijing embody luxury in both their design and amenities. Spacious guest rooms feature workspaces and marble bathrooms with soaking tubs, and hotel suites offer residential touches including living rooms and walk-in closets.
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level
The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge is located on the 16th floor where you can enjoy the beautiful scenery of the city center. Also offers a dedicated and knowledgeable concierge from 07:00 to 23:00, who will attend to all of your individual needs in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. Meanwhile, to preserve the ambiance of our Club Lounge, we kindly request our guests be dressed in smart casual at all times. Also, your children are warmly welcomed to the club lounge under your guardianship, thank you in advance for your understanding and cooperation.

The centerpiece of the bathroom features an oversize deep soaking tub with an inset TV. A separate independent shower provides perfection for beginning your day in Beijing.

Ritz-Carlton, Beijing
Indulge yourself in The Ritz-Carlton Club, which includes 61 luxuriously appointed Club rooms and 38 suites. The Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge offers a dedicated and knowledgeable concierge who will attend to all of your individual needs in a well-appointed, relaxed lounge environment. With complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi Internet access and business center service, a comfortable place - for you to conduct business, plan an outing or simply unwind.
The Ritz-Carlton Club Level
The Club Lounge opens 24 hours a day with the club concierge team available from 6:30 a.m. – 11 p.m. We have five complimentary Food & Beverage presentations for you to enjoy and fine selections of coffee and tea will be available whenever needed.
