Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing
The Ritz-Calrton, Beijing at China Central Place in CBD area
The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing, is centrally situated in one of Beijing’s most thriving business area – China Central Place, within the heart of the city’s central business district, the hotel is 30 minutes away from the Beijing International Airport and offers easy access to the city’s historical and cultural landmarks. In a classic Ritz-Carlton style, timeless and elegant, the hotel, designed by HBA design consultants, features 305 well-appointed guest rooms. Two exquisite award-winning restaurants including Aroma serving world cuisine throughout the day and The Lounge serving afternoon tea and western food. This building also includes Italian Restaurant ATTA, and Michelin star restaurant Jing Ji. Other exclusive facilities include Spa, fitness center, indoor pool with balcony, 1,100 square-meter meetings and conference space, 648 square-meter Ballroom and an outdoor garden.
Chinese Culture With a Borrowed Style From the Ritz-Carlton
Luxury Beijing Hotel
In a city where the story spans millennia, The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing shares the past in ways that blend seamlessly with the present. Rooted in the art of the tea ceremony and borrowing classic Ritz-Carlton elements, this Beijing luxury hotel offers a city center location in China Central Place next to shopping at SKP Shopping Centre. Amenities include three restaurants, a spa and several meeting spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing is 2:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing, with rates of ¥12.00 per hour, ¥288.00 per day, and ¥2016.00 per week.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing is Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK). PEK is located approximately 30.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Beijing offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
