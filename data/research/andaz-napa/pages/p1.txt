# Stay Inspired at Our Downtown Napa Valley Boutique Hotel

Be inspired by the charm of Napa Wine Country from our boutique downtown hotel. Experience essential Napa, including Oxbow Public Market and the Napa Valley Wine Train, which are both located within walking distance of our hotel. Explore the arts, wine and culinary culture through exclusive hotel events, a tasting room and world-class dining.

## Rooms & Suites

Retreat to the rustic-chic boutique hotel rooms of Andaz Napa, featuring hardwood floors and deep soaking tubs. Choose a loft accommodation with a sleek fireplace or upgrade to a suite with a furnished terrace overlooking the heart of downtown Napa.

## DINING

Details & Reservations

### Cork & Comfort: In Room Dining

Whether you're enjoying a cozy breakfast in bed or unwinding with a fine bottle of wine and a movie, our in-room dining menu offers a thoughtful selection of beverages, light bites, and seasonal dishes crafted for comfort and convenience. Available for in-room delivery or pick-up upon request.

### Mercantile Social

Mercantile Social offers farm-fresh seasonal foods with local Napa ingredients, from salads and smoothies to grass-fed beef burgers. Enjoy healthy light bites or comfort foods while sipping an artisanal drink or regional wine in this welcoming downtown Napa restaurant.

### Mercantile Terrace

Unwind with outdoor dining and drinks at our signature downtown Napa restaurant, Mercantile Terrace, located on the 2nd floor. Get cozy around fire pits or in a comfortable cabana while enjoying selections from a farm-to-table menu of locally-sourced light bites. Expert mixologists craft refreshing beverages and cocktails with ingredients native to Napa Valley and Northern California.

### Andaz Farmers Table

Start your morning with a farm-to-table breakfast served in a rustic Napa restaurant setting. Gather around the communal dining table for handcrafted breakfast foods that highlight the bounty of the Napa Valley or pick up an invigorating coffee beverage from the Barista Bar.

## Pets Are Welcome

We welcome your traveling canine companions to our pet-friendly hotel in Downtown Napa. Kindly advise our Hotel team at least three days in advance of your arrival if your pet (or pets) will be joining you during your stay. Pets are welcome with a one-time, nonrefundable fee of $150.00 charged to your guestroom. To ensure your furry friend(s) are comfortable at Andaz Napa, we will gladly provide a doggy bed, two bowls, and treats for your pup(s). We respectfully request that dogs not be left unattended and must be secured during servicing of your guest room.
