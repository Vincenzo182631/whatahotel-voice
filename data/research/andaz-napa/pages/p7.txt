Andaz likes to blur the line between home and away, so imagine you’ve come home to Napa, downtown and walkable. Rooms are loft-like with hardwood floors and other beloved contemporary touches. Mercantile Social for cocktails and Farmers Table for heartier fare get high marks as does the smooth service.
