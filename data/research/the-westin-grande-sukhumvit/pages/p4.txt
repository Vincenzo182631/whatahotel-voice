# Enjoy Local Experiences From Our Hotel Near BTS and MRT

## Experiences

At The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok, wellness is thoughtfully woven into every stay. Whether you're visiting for business, a family holiday, a romantic getaway or a weekend escape, our facilities and...See More programmes are provided to help you maintain your routine and feel your best while travelling. Ideally located in the heart of Sukhumvit, just steps from Asok BTS Skytrain Station and Sukhumvit MRT Station, our hotel makes exploring Bangkok effortless. Discover stunning Buddhist temples, magnificent palaces, modern shopping destinations such as Terminal 21 and EmSphere in the EM District, world-class restaurants, scenic parks, and the city’s rich culture. Major business complexes and event venues, including QSNCC and BITEC, are also easily accessible from our Bangkok hotel. Nowhere else can you find such an exotic mix of old and new as in Bangkok.See Less

## Vareena Spa

Restore the body and expand the mind at the 380-square-meter Vareena Spa in our hotel near BTS Asok and MRT Sukhumvit. Named for the Thai word for water, the spa focuses on relaxation, restoration, and personal evolution. A wooden floor, lantern-like lighting and cream-colored wall panels printed with nature-inspired green patterns frame plush armchairs and daybeds, inviting you to recline, unwind and escape the bustle of Bangkok. Native Thai details including wooden screens and potted plants add to the rare atmosphere—as does a welcome ritual comprised of hot lemongrass tea or bale fruit juice, served alongside cold hand towels. This same characteristic ambiance pervades our hotel spa’s seven treatment rooms, which include two couple’s rooms and five single rooms. There is also a hydrotherapy room, a Vichy shower, an aroma steam room, and a VIP day spa suite. Overlooking Bangkok through expansive windows, the VIP suite houses its own whirlpool, Vichy Shower, and more.

## WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio

Fit a workout into your schedule at our 405-square-meter WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio opens 24/7, featuring state-of-the-art LifeFitness® cardio machines, weights and strength machines. Our Run Concierge and other activities help guests to stay active during their stay.

## Outdoor Pool

Enjoy an exhilarating swim in our outdoor pool on the 8th floor. Filled with saltwater, it overlooks Bangkok’s spectacular skyline. Dive right in, or simply enjoy the view from one of the many poolside lounge chairs. For truly inspiring leisure, retreat to the adjacent Pool Bar and order a refreshing cocktail to sip as you watch the stunning Thai sunset. For added convenience, men and women’s locker rooms are available at the fitness center, which is connected to the pool. Please note that children under the age of 16 must be supervised by an adult at all times.

## Sleep Well

At Westin, we believe that quality sleep is essential to well-being, with our signature Heavenly® Beds, calming room ambiance, and thoughtful touches such as the Sleep Well Menu and soothing Lavender Balm, all designed to create a restful sensory experience that allows you to unwind fully and wake up refreshed, recharged and ready for the day ahead.

### Lavender Balm

Our Sleep Well Lavender Balm bedside amenity, infused with calming lavender and chamomile oils, soothes the senses and relieves tension. Rub on your temples or wrists before going to bed to help you fall asleep and rest well.

### Sleep Well Menu

All is set for a bite before bed! Try the Westin Sleep Well Menu, a curated variety of nutrient-rich food served in-room to enhance your sleep.

### Westin Heavenly® Bed

The renowned Westin Heavenly Bed has signified unparalleled comfort and delivered restorative sleep to our guests for over 25 years. The next-generation Heavenly® Bed offers a superior sleep experience, ensuring you rise refreshed every morning.

### Exploring Bangkok

When staying at our hotel in Sukhumvit, you can explore a wide range of amazing local attractions by the BTS Asok skytrain or the Sukhumvit MRT subway station. Our hotel near Terminal 21 is well-positioned for the city's cultural highlights, shopping destinations, dining, and entertainment complexes. Explore the beautiful temples like Wat Arun or the spectacular Grand Palace, which is without a doubt some of Bangkok's most celebrated landmarks.

Get to Know Bangkok

### City & Culture

Discover Bangkok’s cultural charm and modern city beauty with seamless access by BTS and MRT. Just a few steps away from the hotel, enjoy a relaxing stroll or cycling experience at Benjakitti Forest Park. Explore the timeless elegance of Jim Thompson House, or venture by MRT to the historic riverside district near Wat Pho and hop to the Grand Palace for an unforgettable glimpse into Thailand’s rich heritage.

### Shopping Districts

For smaller gatherings, Botan, which means "peony" in Japanese, can host up to 40 guests theater-style or be divided into up to four separate rooms for even more intimate occasions. Connected to Kisso Japanese Restaurant, it allows for working lunches or dinners-and also features a foyer area for coffee breaks.

### Taste of Bangkok

Experience Bangkok’s celebrated culinary scene with renowned dining destinations conveniently connected by metro. Travel by BTS Skytrain to the trendy neighbourhoods of Thonglor and Ekkamai for stylish cafés and rooftop bars, or take the MRT to Wat Mangkon Station for Yaowarat, Bangkok’s legendary Chinatown, famous for its vibrant street food and authentic local delicacies served well into the night.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Strength Training, Cardio, Free Weights, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have an on-site spa?

Yes, Vareena Spa is located on-site at The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok?

EMSPHERE, ONE Bangkok, Emporium and EmQuartier (EM District), CentralwOrld - Bangkok's Largest Shopping Complex, Wat Arun Temple of Dawn and more are nearby The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok.
