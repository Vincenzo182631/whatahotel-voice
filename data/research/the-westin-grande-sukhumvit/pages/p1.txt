# Welcome to The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok

## A Rejuvenated Stay in the Heart of Sukhumvit

The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok offers a rejuvenating stay in the heart of Sukhumvit, where wellness, convenience and vibrant city experiences come together. Located next to Asok BTS Skytrain and...See More Sukhumvit MRT, the hotel connects you to business districts, shopping, cultural attractions and event venues, including Terminal 21, Emsphere, QSNCC and BITEC. Retreat to spacious and thoughtfully designed accommodations featuring the Heavenly® Bed, Lavender Balm and White Tea bath products, ensuring a restorative stay for business and leisure travelers alike. Keep moving at the 24-hour WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, join Run Concierge or swim in the outdoor pool overlooking Bangkok. Enjoy Japanese cuisine at Kisso Japanese Restaurant, international favorites and buffets at Seasonal Tastes, and cocktails and light bites at Zest Bar & Terrace. Whether hosting a meeting, celebrating or exploring the city with family and friends, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok offers everything at the center of it all.See Less

## Spectacular Meeting Spaces

Outlined in elegant crown molding and awash in calming, neutral hues, the Grand Ballroom is ideal for conferences and grand affairs. Altitude, right at the top of the hotel on the 25th floor, is a private rooftop event space that is unique in the Sukhumvit area.

## Spacious Boardrooms

Formal meetings can also be held in the eighth-floor meeting rooms of our Sukhumvit Road hotel, which share a pre-function area, or in our boardroom, which is bathed in natural light.

### Outdoor Pool

Enjoy an exhilarating swim in our 227-square-meter rooftop pool, with a picturesque waterfall. Filled with salt water, it overlooks Bangkok’s spectacular skyline. For truly inspiring leisure, retreat to the adjacent Pool Bar and order a refreshing cocktail to sip as you watch the stunning Thai sunset from our hotel in Sukhumvit, Bangkok.

## Concierge Desk

Take advantage of our attentive Concierge team, available 24 hours a day in our 5 star hotel in Sukhumvit, Bangkok. Dedicated to ensuring you enjoy the best of Bangkok, they are pleased to offer recommendations, reservations, and more. They will also be happy to arrange for you to sample the local cuisine, schedule Thai cooking classes, or engage in local sports, such as Muay Thai boxing or golf.

## Tour Desk

Separate from the Concierge, our tour desk is pleased to arrange full, half-day, and evening tours to one-of-a-kind locations in and around the city from our hotel in Sukhumvit, Bangkok.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok?

The check-in time at The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok.

What are the parking options at The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok?

The closest airport near The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok is Don Mueang International Airport (DMK). DMK is located approximately 22.4 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have an airport shuttle to Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK)?

Yes, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok has an airport shuttle to Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK).

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have an airport shuttle to Don Mueang International Airport (DMK)?

Yes, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok has an airport shuttle to Don Mueang International Airport (DMK).

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok have a Westin Club Privileges lounge?

Yes, The Westin Grande Sukhumvit, Bangkok has Westin Club Privileges lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club Privileges lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
