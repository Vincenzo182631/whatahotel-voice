### Beautiful Hotel and Atmosphere, but Service Could Be More Attentive

We had a wonderful stay overall, and the hotel itself is beautiful. We especially loved the atmosphere and design of the breakfast area—it was warm, elegant, and full of character, which made breakfast a particularly enjoyable experience.

That said, there were a couple of service issues during our stay that were disappointing.

When we checked in, our room was not ready, and we were told that the hotel would call us once it was available. Since we were traveling with our baby, we spent some time at a nearby park while waiting. However, after quite a while, we never received a call. When we returned to the hotel, we found that our room was already ready, and we were told that the staff had simply forgotten to call us.

There was also a welcome amenity that the hotel had thoughtfully prepared for my wife's birthday. We received a voicemail explaining that it had been prepared but had not been delivered to our room. I didn't hear the voicemail until the following morning, so I explained the situation to the front desk before breakfast and asked if it might still be possible to receive it. I was told that the manager would be consulted and that someone would get back to me, but unfortunately, no one followed up.

To be clear, our disappointment was not about the welcome amenity itself. We genuinely appreciated that the hotel had taken the time to prepare something special for my wife's birthday. It was more about the lack of follow-up and the feeling that our concerns were not given much attention.

Despite these issues, we really enjoyed the hotel, the beautiful surroundings, and the overall experience. With a little more attention to communication and follow-through at the front desk, this could have been an even more memorable stay.
