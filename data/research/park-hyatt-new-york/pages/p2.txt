Experience the height of luxury at Park Hyatt New York, where our elegant suites and rooms in Midtown Manhattan offer a refined retreat near Central Park. Among our 211 spacious accommodations, 93 suites seamlessly blend form and function, featuring heated natural-stone flooring, deep marble soaking tubs, and custom furnishings by Yabu Pushelberg. Many of our hotel suites provide stunning partial views of Central Park, while select options, such as the Central Park Balcony Suite, the Royal Suite, and the One-Bedroom Residential Suite, boast floor-to-ceiling windows for an unforgettable cityscape. Or, for a truly unique perspective, you can choose our 3,000 sq ft, luxurious Manhattan suite with views of NYC from your private wraparound balcony.
