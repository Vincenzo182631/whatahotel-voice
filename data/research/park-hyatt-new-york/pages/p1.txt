# Luxury Hotels Near Central Park NYC \| Park Hyatt New York

## Discover Personalized Luxury At A Central Park Hotel

Find an elegantly warm welcome in New York City. Our luxury hotel sits in Midtown Manhattan, across from Carnegie Hall and one block from Central Park, and boasts suites with floor-to-ceiling windows and marble soaking tubs. Enjoy peaceful moments in the spa, salon and saltwater pool, explore our curated art collection or host a Manhattan soiree in one of our striking venues.

## Discover Park Hyatt New York

Family Travel

Enjoy a memorable family escape in our generous rooms and suites, which include several options for connecting rooms and amenities for babies and kids. Explore the best of the Big Apple from our hotel’s convenient Midtown location, close to an array of iconic attractions and leisure activities for the entire family. Our Concierge Team can also assist with planning your family’s stay and arrange reservations at vibrant family-friendly restaurants.

Wellness Amenities

Find serenity at Spa Nalai. Enjoy the finest amenities at our hotel spa, including a eucalyptus steam room, a hot tub and an indoor saltwater pool with underwater speakers that play specially selected music. Adjacent to our pool area, you’ll find a 24-hour fitness center with the latest equipment and views of Central Park and the New York skyline. Treat yourself to rejuvenating massages and facial treatments and leave refreshed.

Specialty Suites

Discover our collection of Specialty Suites, featuring intimate yet lavish spaces that capture the energy and vibrancy of New York City. These luxury hotel suites offer Central Park views and are designed to embody the relaxed sophistication of a Midtown residence.

Art in The Park

Park Hyatt New York proudly unveils RiPpLeS iN TiMe is an introspective ceramic collection that explores life’s rhythms through abstract forms by emerging artist and Park Hyatt New York colleague, Jeffrey Okyere-Agyei. Each sculpture reflects the curves, flows, and irregularities of human existence—echoing moments of ease as well as struggle. By emphasizing movement, light, and texture, the works invite viewers into a sensory dialogue, encouraging reflection on how emotions and experiences shape both ourselves and our environments. Experience it in the Avenue Gallery, now through December 2025.

Martini & Caviar Happy Meal

Elevate your evenings with the newest indulgence at our upscale Central Park restaurant, the Martini & Caviar Happy Meal. This modern twist on a timeless classic features a handcrafted martini, Petrossian caviar and perfectly crisp fries — the ultimate pairing of luxury and whimsy.

### The Living Room Restaurant & Bar

Dine in an upscale restaurant with the ambience of a Central Park residence. Delight in elevated breakfasts, artful lunches and imaginative dinners. At the bar, enjoy a masterfully concocted cocktail, champagne and a distinguished collection of wines.

Reservations are available exclusively for lunch and dinner. Walk-in guests are graciously accommodated on a first-come, first-served basis. Parties of up to six may reserve seamlessly through OpenTable, subject to availability. For breakfast arrangements or for larger groups, please contact us directly at +1 (646) 774 1257. An 18% service charge will be applied to parties of more than six guests.
