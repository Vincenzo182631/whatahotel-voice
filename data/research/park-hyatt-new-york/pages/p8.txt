# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

Please note that there is an additional $15 fee per day for oversized vehicle, defined as a vehicle of 181” or longer with 70” height, or greater than 75” height regardless of length. Our Concierge and Doormen Teams are at your service for your transportation needs.

### Self Parking - Open Daily, 24 Hours

Please note that there is an additional $15 fee per day for oversized vehicle, defined as a vehicle of 181” or longer with 70” height, or greater than 75” height regardless of length. For Self-Parking, please utilize the Icon Parking garage located at 166 West 58th street with direct hotel access. If luggage assistance is needed, please utilize the main entrance to unload your luggage before parking your vehicle. Our Concierge and Doormen Teams are at your service for your transportation needs.
