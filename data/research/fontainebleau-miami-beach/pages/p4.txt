# Cellar 1954

Pairing Dinner Series

**The Cellar 1954 Pairing Dinner Series** offers an elegant evening where exceptional vintages and rare spirits meet culinary artistry. Each dinner is a collaboration between Fontainebleau’s finest chefs and celebrated winemakers, featuring a bespoke multi-course menu paired with exclusive pours and first-look releases. This isn’t just a meal — it’s an experience designed for those who live for the details. Step into a new era of pairing.
