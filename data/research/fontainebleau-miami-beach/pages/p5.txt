Pool Guidelines

A guest room key and wristband are required in order to access the pool deck.

One pool chair per guest is available on a first-come basis and may not be reserved.

Personal items left on unattended chairs for more than 45 minutes will be removed and turned into lost and found.

Outside food and beverage is not permitted. Please enjoy our restaurants and lounges in lieu of bringing your own food and beverage on the pool deck.

Florida health restrictions mandate infants and toddlers wear swimmies at all times in the pool. Complimentary swimmies are available at the towel hut.

Pets are not allowed on pool deck.
