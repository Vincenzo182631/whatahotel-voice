Hakkasan

**MICHELIN-recognized Hakkasan offers a chef’s selection of the most beloved Cantonese fare.** Enjoy a three-course dining experience with your choice of appetizer, entrée, rice or vegetable, and dessert, featuring favorites like Chicken with Mushroom Siu Mai, Sanpei Prawn, Sweet & Sour Chicken, Wok-Fried Shishito Beef, and Mango Custard. Enhance your meal with an optional curated wine flight for the perfect pairing.

**Curated by Executive Chef Michael Paley, Prime 54’s modern take on steakhouse classics**. Complete the evening with an optional curated wine flight for the perfect pairing.
