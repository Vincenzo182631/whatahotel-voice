## An ode to modern Morocco anchored in _timeless_ traditions

Nestled at the confluence of the Bouregreg and the Atlantic Ocean, offering breathtaking views of the region’s storied landmarks, Fairmont La Marina Rabat Salé is designed to mirror a majestic cruise liner dropping anchor on the estuary. Our luxury hotel conveys you on an unparalleled journey in the heart of Rabat-Salé region through a fresh and new perspective.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont La Marina Rabat-Salé located?

Fairmont La Marina Rabat-Salé is located at Fes Avenue, in front of the striking modern Marina of Salé. The hotel is 10 minutes away from Rabat city center, a 20-minute drive from Rabat International Airport, and 1h30m from Casablanca International Airport.

Is parking available at Fairmont La Marina Rabat-Salé?

For a seamless arrival, you'll have at your disposal an underground parking facility including terminals for electrical powered cars, along with a valet parking service.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont La Marina Rabat-Salé?

Yes, our 5-star hotel provides a concierge service. Our head concierge Jaafar is a clés d'or concierge and will be your best guide to discover some of the most hidden gems of Morocco's capital and its fascinating surroundings during your luxury trip at Fairmont.
