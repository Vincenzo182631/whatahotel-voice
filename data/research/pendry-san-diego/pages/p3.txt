# Restaurants in San Diego

## Dine in Downtown San Diego

Pendry San Diego is a true culinary and entertainment hub. With modern coastal cuisine at Lionfish, Southern California-driven seasonal fare at Provisional, light bites at The Pool House, the perfect cocktail at Fifth & Rose and local craft beer at Nason’s Beer Hall, we have everything under one roof.

Fifth & Rose

Cocktail Bar in San Diego’s Gaslamp Quarter

The Pendry San Diego hotel bar, Fifth & Rose, is a classic cocktail bar in an elegant, chic, and comfortable room. A classic setting for a fitting drink, it is a place to engage in conversation, enjoy bar fare, and linger over the perfect cocktail.

- Discover Fifth & Rose

Provisional

Brunch Restaurant in Downtown San Diego

Designed in partnership with Raan and Lindsay Parton from LA’s innovative retail concept, Alchemy Works, Provisional is a restaurant-marketplace hybrid featuring seasonal fare and gourmet takeaway options.

- Discover Provisional

Nason’s Beer Hall

Craft Beer Hall in Downtown San Diego

A new addition to San Diego’s iconic craft beer scene, Nason’s features an expansive selection of local and international beers, globally inspired comfort food, and vintage bar games. Its name pays homage to Nason & Co. Farmer’s Market, which occupied this unique space in the early 1900s.

- Discover Nason’s Beer Hall

Lionfish

Seafood & Sushi Restaurant in San Diego

Driven by seasonal, local ingredients, Lionfish serves modern coastal cuisine with fresh catches and prime cuts from Executive Chef Jose “JoJo” Ruiz, whose talent is on full display with sushi, seafood, and coastal cuisine on the menu in the airy, two-floor dining room.

- Discover Lionfish

The Pool House

Rooftop Pool Bar in San Diego’s Gaslamp Quarter

Boasting some of the best views in the Gaslamp Quarter, The Pool House at Pendry San Diego is a premier rooftop lounge, embodying the essence of the Southern California lifestyle. Light, contemporary, and open-air, the indoor/outdoor space is perched above the Gaslamp’s famed 5th Avenue and plays host to relaxed daytime and evening events featuring a signature bar and cocktail experience for poolside guests.

- Discover The Pool House

In-Room Dining

Room Service at Pendry San Diego

At your service. Day or night. Pendry’s signature in-room dining features a specially crafted menu of small bites, entrées, beverages and sweets, specific to each hotel and its local culinary influences, all carefully delivered to your guestroom to satisfy any craving with convenience and comfort top of mind.

- Discover In-Room Dining Experiences

## Pendry San Diego Dining FAQs

### How many restaurants and bars are located at Pendry San Diego?

Pendry San Diego offers 5 restaurants and bars, including Lionfish, a modern coastal seafood and sushi restaurant with San Diego-inspired flavors and Provisional, a daytime dining destination known for brunch, lunch and café offerings. Guests can also enjoy Nason’s Beer Hall, a lively spot for craft beer and casual bar fare, Fifth & Rose, an intimate cocktail bar and The Pool House, offering rooftop drinks and a classic pool-inspired menu.

### Does Pendry San Diego offer in-room dining?

Yes, Pendry San Diego offers in-room dining, allowing guests to enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner and late-night fare delivered directly to their guestroom or suite at our luxury hotel in downtown San Diego.

### Do any restaurants at Pendry San Diego offer outdoor dining or scenic views?

Yes, The Pool House, Pendry San Diego’s rooftop bar, offers drinks and light fare in an open-air setting overlooking the Gaslamp Quarter. Lionfish, Provisional and Nason’s Beer Hall all feature seasonal outdoor patio seating.

### Does Pendry San Diego offer brunch?

Yes, Pendry San Diego offers brunch at multiple restaurants:

- Provisional Kitchen has a daily brunch menu with housemade classics and brunch cocktails. On weekends, a special chef-created, multi-course menu is also available.
- Nason’s Beer Hall serves weekend brunch in a relaxed setting, with TVs throughout to catch your favorite sports team.

### Does Pendry San Diego have a bar for watching sports and live events?

Yes, Nason’s Beer Hall offers a lively atmosphere for watching sports and live events, featuring multiple TVs. It’s also a great spot to grab a drink before heading to Petco Park, just a 4-minute walk away.
