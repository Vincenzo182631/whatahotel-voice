In a nutshell
: Despite its prime location for Padres fans or first-time visitors looking to be in the mix, the flagship Pendry makes it hard to leave with delicious on-site restaurants and a lounge chair–ringed pool.
Madison Flager
Madison Flager
Rooms
California-cool meets Big Bear coziness here, with light, airy rooms warmed up by charming blue plaid chairs, thick wooden furniture, and floral wallpapers in various coastal shades. I stayed in a one-bedroom suite with a spacious living room and bedroom, both boasting wraparound windows, and a Jack-and-Jill bathroom setup that would be appreciated by families traveling with little ones or duos on separate schedules or working remotely. Standard rooms start at 350 square feet and go up to the two-bedroom Pendry Suite; all are labeled by view, so you can have your pick of skyline, bay, city, or pool overlooks. Throughout, you'll find glass-walled walk-in showers lined with subway tiles and plush robes hung up in the closet alongside cozy slippers.

Madison Flager
Food and drink
There are four main dining experiences on-site, and genuinely, all are worth a visit. If you only have time for one dinner, though, make it at Lionfish, where fresh sushi abounds alongside splashy made-to-be-Instagrammed dishes that are actually worth the hype (the flaming Mariscos al Fuego chief among them). Both Lionfish and Fifth & Rose are serving up creative cocktails, while Fifth & Rose skews a little moodier into the night and would make a romantic date spot. I had a great brunch at Provisional—I’m still thinking about the pistachio latte and chiliquiles. Dozens of beers are on tap at Nason’s Beer Hall, along with all the pub-style dishes you could want: spinach and artichoke dip, wings, nachos, burgers, and even a Wagyu crunch wrap.
Elsewhere, in-room dining is served 24 hours a day, and The Pool House menu fills in any gaps with poke bowls, club sandwiches, and post-late-night-friendly bites like the breakfast burrito.
Amenities and experiences
This is a buzzy place to be—the kind of hotel you can come back to for a nightcap and find a crowded bar welcoming you in. On the weekends, a Mom & Me Boozy Tea is held at Fifth & Rose with tea-based cocktails and small bites like caviar blinis and chocolate madeleines.
In the day, decompress with time by the pool or a trip to the spa, where you can book a facial, massage, sound bath, or IV treatment. I was sufficiently blissed out after my 60-minute Vagus Nerve Balancing Stone Massage, and loved only needing to walk a few steps to find myself on a pool lounger, basking in the late afternoon sun. (I was happy to spot
Osea
products in my treatment room too.)
What to do nearby
Petco Park is a 10-minute walk away, so if your visit coincides with baseball season (or you’re seeing a concert in the off-season), you’re in a prime location to avoid any post-game traffic. You’re also smack dab in the middle of the historic Gaslamp Quarter here, within walking distance of tons of bars and restaurants, shopping, and the convention center.
The backstory
This is the very first Pendry, opened in 2017 as the brand’s flagship location. Launched by father-and-son duo Alan and Michael Fuerstman, it began as a new luxury brand from Montage Hotels & Resorts, rooted in place and catering to a slightly younger audience than Montage without losing the five-star service. The highlights are all in the details here—little things that you might overlook or not think twice about in their absence, but that add up to a lovely, friction-free stay. The valets that buzz open the door without batting an eyelash every time you come and go; the outlets right next to the bed; the nightly chocolates on your pillow.
Insider tip
Swing by Fifth & Rose Monday to Thursday from 4 to 6 p.m. for Bestie Hour—you and a pal can dine on charcuterie, a Caesar salad, pesto rigatoni, and a martini (espresso, dirty, or a cosmo) for just $35 per person. Take that, girl dinner. Also, don't overlook the Moët & Chandon Champagne vending machine hanging out in the Provisional lobby.
