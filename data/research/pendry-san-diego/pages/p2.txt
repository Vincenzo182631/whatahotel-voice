# San Diego Accommodations

## A San Diego Gaslamp hotel, with Pendry’s polished style in every guestroom and suite.

Pendry San Diego is a stylish downtown San Diego city stay with contemporary interiors, a perfect location in the Historic Gaslamp District, 317 guestrooms and hotel suites with city skyline views, five distinct dining, drinks and nightlife destinations, and signature Pendry style, inside and out.

## Rooms

Our hotel’s 317 spacious rooms offer 350+ square feet of luxurious space outfitted in a blend of California ease and the sophistication of grand hotels. Guestrooms offer custom furnishings, luxurious linens and incredible views of the bay and downtown.

View Rooms

## Suites

These spacious and luxurious suites offer the ultimate in the Pendry experience. Views of the San Diego skyline complement the ocean-inspired interiors with warm blue wall coverings, a rich wood-crafted desk, chaise lounge wrapped in a classic tartan fabric, and cabinet filled with a superior mini bar experience.

View Suites

## Accessible

Our Accessible rooms and suites are equipped with accessible features and inspiring views of the city. Accessible accommodations are appointed with an accessible luxury bathroom, featuring grab bars and roll-in shower, and lowered features throughout the room.
