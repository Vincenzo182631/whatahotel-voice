## THE _legendary_ CASTLE IN THE HEART OF BANFF NATIONAL PARK

Step through the doors of Fairmont Banff Springs and you'll instantly feel the grandeur of Canada's "Castle in the Rockies." Located just minutes from the Town of Banff, this historic 5-star resort offers travelers the same warm hospitality it has since 1888. Wildlife, hiking, world-class skiing, and championship golf are all at your fingertips.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closest airport to Fairmont Banff Springs?

The closest airport to Fairmont Banff Springs is Calgary International Airport (YYC) located approximately 80 miles (130 km) east of the resort. Most major airline carriers fly into Calgary.

How can I get to Fairmont Banff Springs from the nearest airport?

Daily airport shuttles run frequent door-to-door service between Calgary International Airport (YYC) and Fairmont Banff Springs. Private taxis, limo services, or rental cars can be reserved in advance with all major car rental agencies represented at Calgary International Airport.

Is parking available at Fairmont Banff Springs?

Yes. The hotel's parking is located beside the Convention Centre. Self-parking is $39 CAD/night, valet parking is $59 CAD/night with unlimited access. Fairmont Banff Springs is pleased to offer complimentary charging for all our guests' electric vehicles.
