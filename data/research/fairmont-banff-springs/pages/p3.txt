Fairmont Banff Springs
Stay here when you want the luxury of a castle stay seamlessly paired with outdoor adventures surrounded by some of North America’s most iconic scenery.

Best for:
Outdoor enthusiasts, families, golf lovers, and those who’ve always wanted to stay in a legendary castle
Location:
Banff, Canada in the heart of Banff National Park
Can’t-miss feature:
The views of the Canadian Rockies from every hotel window—I often found myself stopping in my tracks to take it in.
In a nutshell
: The legendary "Castle in the Rockies" is a destination in its own right, pairing historic grandeur with unparalleled access to Banff National Park. With a dizzying number of food and beverage outlets, a world-class spa, family-friendly excursions, and accessible outdoor activities for every mood, the property seamlessly blends luxury, adventure, and relaxation against one of Canada's most spectacular mountain backdrops.
Lara Kramer
Lara Kramer
Rooms
It’s not a proper castle without ample rooms and Fairmont Banff Springs has 724 of them, ranging from intimate single guest rooms to six-person, two-bedroom suites. Throughout them all, you’ll find earthy browns and beiges with pops of smoky blues and metallics. Because the hotel is surrounded by such incredible scenery, the highlight of almost any room is the view, with options that overlook the Canadian Rockies and the Bow River. I also loved that all the room windows open, letting in fresh mountain air.
If you’re looking to splurge, book a room through the property’s Fairmont Gold program which are located on exclusive floors of the hotel and offer a more contemporary alpine style featuring distinctive artwork, rich fabrics, and upgraded bathrooms outfitted with granite and slate surfaces. All Fairmont Gold bookings come with a private check-in experience and a members-only lounge that serves complimentary breakfast, evening canapés, and a roaring fire.
Food and drink
You will not get bored with the culinary options at Fairmont Banff Springs. With 12 restaurants, pubs, and bars all offering a variety of distinctive cuisine under the leadership of executive chef Atticus Garant, it’ll be a true quest to hit them all. Must-visits include the standout signature 1888 Chop House for an elevated tour of Alberta beef, seafood, and wild game. Dine indoors under an imported olive tree from Italy at Castello Italiana, my personal favorite intimate dining space, where I had the best eggplant parm of my life (don’t tell my Italian mother-in-law). If you’re feeling French classics in a lively setting, the moules frites at The Vermillion Room will satiate any craving.
While you can certainly snag an excellent cocktail at any of the above restaurants, there’s nothing quite like the innovative tipples you can order at Rundle Bar, the buzzy heart of the property. If the library from
Beauty and the Beast
was reimagined as a bar with rich wood finishes and moody backlighting, you’d land somewhere in the vibe vicinity of Rundle Bar. You can order the classics, of course, but I highly encourage you to choose a more adventurous drink from The Explorer’s Collection, including the Carnivale, made with caraway, yuzu, and Thai green chilis, or the Wayfinder, a palatable delight of cold-press citrus, cherry tomato, and Tom Yum spices.
Lara Kramer
Amenities and experiences
Being outdoors is an essential part of visiting the area, and Fairmont Banff Springs offers an abundance of options to help guests immerse themselves in the landscape. I recommend spending a lazy morning floating in the outdoor pool surrounded by chic loungers and stunning views of Mount Rundle, one of the most iconic peaks in the Canadian Rockies. Plan for ordering light poolside bites like the quinoa lentil salad and chicken ranch wrap and, if the morning calls for it, your favorite bubbly. Carrying with the theme of epic scenery, guests can also book the 360° Dome experience for immersive wraparound views while watching the sunrise over breakfast or sipping a night cap under the stars.
What to do nearby
With Banff National Park in your backyard, there’s an unending number of year-round outdoor adventures to be had. In the warmer season, hike, fish, forest bathe, and lake paddle, or hit the property’s Tunnel Mountain 9-hole or Stanley Thompson 18-hole golf courses. Bundle up in the winter months for epic skiing, snow tubing, and snowshoeing. After full days of activities, walk off sore muscles on a slow stroll through the charming town of Banff, or if you need something more serious, take a soak in the thermal waters at
Banff Upper Hot Springs
.
The backstory
The legacy of the Castle in the Rockies is owed to the foresight of railroad magnate William Cornelius Van Horne who wanted the world to see the beauty of Canada’s natural landscape. Construction of the original hotel began in 1886, and the property officially opened in 1888 as one of Canada's earliest grand railway hotels. After a 1926 fire destroyed much of the original wooden structure, it was rebuilt as the hotel guests experience today. Originally operating as only a warm-weather destination, the hotel was winterized in the late 1960s, allowing it to open year-round and become a major ski-season destination. As befits a grand castle, the hotel walls have housed some rather notable guests, including King George VI and Queen Elizabeth the Queen Mother. It also comes with plenty of ghostly lore—mostly of the friendly variety.
Insider tip
If you’d love to explore the culinary offerings across all 12 of the property’s food and beverage outlets but don’t have enough open meals on your itinerary to make it happen, treat yourself to Alberta Food Tours’ “Eat the Castle Culinary Tour.” One of my personal favorite afternoons of my stay, you’ll meet your guide in the hotel lobby and begin a 2.5-hour walking adventure tasting and drinking your way through every nook and cranny of the property. Along the way, you’ll have the chance to chat with the culinary teams and enjoy stories of the hotel’s history. Make sure to
book
this tour in advance, though; our tour was completely sold out.
Final considerations:
Family friendly:
Absolutely; beyond the complimentary supervised children’s program, Kids @ the Castle, the property offers dedicated bookable family excursions, rooms with built-in twin bunk beds and cozy dining areas, and an on-site bowling alley.
Accessibility:
Yes; more information available
here
Wellness:
With 40,000 square feet dedicated to treatment rooms, an indoor mineral pool, plunge pools, steam rooms, and lounges, the Fairmont Spa offers pure tranquility against dramatic views. You can expect all the usual suspects on the menu—facials, massages, body treatments—along with a dedicated hair and nail salon. I booked the Nature’s Renewal treatment where I was exfoliated from head-to-toe followed by a massage, leaving my skin the smoothest it’s ever been.
