History and Modernity in Dallas, Downtown Dallas Hotel in the Heart of Deep Ellum!

Welcome to Dallas, a city bursting with commerce, culture and high class charms. From the indie heart of Downtown Dallas Deep Ellum neighborhood, where legendary 20s blues musicians once played, The Kimpton Pittman Hotel celebrates its storied past with contemporary flourishes.

Here, our century old Beaux Arts exterior blends with modern glass and steel interiors. With clean lines and layered artisan details, our 165 guest rooms feel more like residential suites. A modern palette of white walls, unexpected pops of dark green, orange and gray and local artwork. At night, slumber comes easy, our beds are dressed with soft, signature linens.

Gather inside our American tavern for wood fired plates or settle around the outdoor deck for craft beers and live music. And, work or play, our art filled common areas make the perfect meeting place for coffee and cocktails.

Later, venture outside for vibrant street murals, cafes, craft breweries and 30 music venues.

### Pool

A luxe urban oasis in the heart of Texas, our seasonally heated outdoor pool is the best spot to unwind in Deep Ellum all year round. Soak up the sun (and the scene) in one of our lounge chairs and take a dip every once in a while to stay refreshed.

Learn more

### Elm & Good

Elm & Good is a modern American tavern and social lounge that celebrates the rich history and vibrant energy of this iconic neighborhood. Our chef draws inspiration from local and global markets for an ingredient-driven menu, featuring seasonal selections of eclectic meat and seafood offerings alongside globally inspired dishes.

Learn more

### Deep End

Welcome to Deep End, the ultimate cocktail bar in Deep Ellum, where laid-back vibes and urban charm converge. Our bar is more than just a place to grab a drink; it's a social hub that seamlessly transitions from day to night.

Learn more

### Wedding Weekend

There’s something about weddings that makes a single day feel as though it’s not enough to capture all of the revelry the occasion deserves. Of course, we’re all for extending the fun and can work with you to plan and host events throughout your wedding weekend from a rehearsal dinner to a farewell brunch and everything in between.

Learn more

### Hybrid Meetings

The ability to host a virtual meeting is a game changer for businesses, and keeps your teams connected near or far. Explore the future of connectivity with our hotel's comprehensive solutions tailored for hybrid events. Keep your teams connected with our solutions for hybrid or virtual events.

## FAQs for Pittman Hotel

Does Kimpton Pittman Hotel have a pool?

Kimpton Pittman Hotel has 1 outdoor pool.

How much is parking at Kimpton Pittman Hotel?

At Kimpton Pittman Hotel, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 55 USD .

Overnight valet parking available to hotel guests for $55 per night. Day rate is $24 for up to 6 hours. All diners at Elm & Good can have their ticket complimentary validated, for up to 2 hours

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton Pittman Hotel?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton Pittman Hotel.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge. If your pet fits through the door, we will welcome them in.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton Pittman Hotel for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton Pittman Hotel have a restaurant on site?

Kimpton Pittman Hotel has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton Pittman Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton Pittman Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton Pittman Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton Pittman Hotel is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton Pittman Hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton Pittman Hotel offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton Pittman Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton Pittman Hotel is conveniently located near Dallas Love Field Airport (DAL), approximately 6 miles away (9.66 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
