# Art

## Art at 45 Park Lane

45 Park Lane has always been a leader when it comes to art hotels – our floors and rooms are filled with original works, all carefully selected by our curator Lily Ackerman.

In this conversation, we sit down with Lily to reveal how she collaborates with our community of artists, why our space works well as a visual host, and the role that art plays in our heritage and identity today.

## Art is in our DNA

At 45 Park Lane we’ve always been dedicated to our passion for art: it’s a crucial part of our DNA. We invite you to explore at your leisure, to find yourself, to lose yourself. Discover which pieces resonate the most with you.

May 1 – September 13

Pop-art-inspired collection

Known for his unique, dynamic and immediately recognisable style, Dan Baldwin masterfully blends the abstract with symbolic motifs to produce playful prints and vibrant ceramics. The _Fragmented Landscape_ series layers silhouetted forms found in nature over vivid blocks of colour, creating a lively, pop-art-inspired collection that is a joy to look at.

September 16 – November 14

_Great ~~Women~~ Photographers_ at 45 Park Lane

To celebrate Phaidon’s latest publication, a curated selection of the book’s photographs will be on display at BAR 45, alongside limited edition Artspace works and an installation of the publisher’s ‘Great Women’ books on the ground floor.

Exclusive British art

Our hotel is decorated with striking contemporary artworks by some of Britain's leading artists - including Sir Peter Blake, Damien Hirst, Christian Furr, Joe Tilson and many more. As well as our public spaces, you'll also find pieces in every room and suite.

Barbara Hepworth

Rhythm & Form

Delve into the world of radical British sculptor Barbara Hepworth and the Hampstead Modernists on our bespoke art tour. Discover how her formative years in London shaped the mind, hands and sculptures of one of Britain’s most influential artists.
