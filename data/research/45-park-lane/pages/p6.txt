THE CHEF

Introducing Elliott Grover

Having spearheaded some of London’s most renowned restaurants, chef Elliott Grover now takes the helm at CUT at 45 Park Lane. Elliott’s fresh take on the menu celebrates locally sourced ingredients and his close relationships with our producers.

THE CONCEPT

More than just a great steak

The menu runs from Wagyu to USDA Prime and grass-fed Hereford beef, one of the widest selections of cuts in London – then there's the famous white truffle pappardelle, and seasonal salads that earn their place at the table. A steakhouse in Mayfair where every dish knows exactly what it's doing.

THE VIEW

A picturesque setting

The only dilemma at CUT at 45 Park Lane is choosing your view. Will it be Damien Hirst’s Diamond Dust Psalms series adorning the walls, or leafy Hyde Park beyond the windows?

THE SUNDAY ROAST

## Sunday, done properly

Roast beef carved with precision, crisp potatoes, seasonal vegetables and all the trimmings. Whether it's family or friends across the table, this is a Sunday roast in London that's worth clearing your afternoon for.

> Food is an opportunity to bring people together and what better place than CUT, with our relaxed vibe, exceptional location and superb dishes.

Elliott Grover
