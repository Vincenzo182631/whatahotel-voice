## Handcrafted cocktails

Every drink at BAR 45 starts with what's in the glass. No shortcuts, no theatre for its own sake. Just sharp ingredients, balanced with the kind of care you can taste. A cocktail bar near Hyde Park where the drinks are as considered as the room around them.

Delectable bar bites

Our bar menu of small bites includes succulent Wagyu sliders, Dorset crab rolls and really good fries. And they all go down terribly well with a glass of rare liquor or something from our extensive wine list.

## Our famous wine selection

BAR 45 and CUT share a wine list that runs deep. Over 600 bottles from across the world, chosen with the same precision as everything else here. Whatever the mood or the moment, the right one's waiting for you.
