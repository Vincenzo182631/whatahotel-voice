General hotel information

Everything you need to know before, during and after your stay at 45 Park Lane. From room amenities to our pet-friendly services, find answers to our most common guest enquiries below.

Where is 45 Park Lane located?

45 Park Lane is on Park Lane, London W1K 1PN, in the heart of Mayfair, overlooking Hyde Park.

How far is the hotel from Heathrow and London City Airport?

We're around 30-45 minutes by car from Heathrow Airport and 45 minutes by car from London City Airport.

What's close to the hotel?

We're within close distance of Buckingham Palace, Marble Arch and London's main shopping districts.

How do I contact the reservations office?

You can reach our reservations office on +44 207 898 9870 or email us at reservations.45l@dorchestercollection.com.

Is there a minimum size for a group booking?

Group bookings apply from six or more rooms.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, up to two small dogs per room (under 7kg/15lbs) at £35 per day to cover deep cleaning. We provide a bowl, bed and treats.

What is the parking fee?

Parking is £60 per night.

What facilities does the hotel offer?

We have a luxury spa with an indoor swimming pool, sauna, steam room, hydro pool and gym, plus several acclaimed restaurants and a bar.

Is the hotel close to Hyde Park?

Yes, 45 Park Lane sits on Park Lane overlooking Hyde Park. The park is just a 3-minute walk from the hotel.

Is the hotel well placed for sightseeing and shopping?

Absolutely, we're close to Buckingham Palace, Royal Academy of Arts, Wellington Arch and the main shopping districts in the heart of Mayfair.

What makes 45 Park Lane distinctive?

45 Park Lane is an art-forward boutique luxury hotel in Mayfair with just 45 rooms, each overlooking Hyde Park. Designed by Thierry Despont, its warm Art Deco style feels more private residence than grand hotel. It's home to CUT by Wolfgang Puck (his first European restaurant) and chef Shinji Kanesaka's Michelin-starred London outpost, Sushi Kanesaka. The hotel also houses a serious contemporary art collection featuring Damien Hirst and Sir Peter Blake, and a 10,000 sq ft spa with Park Lane's longest pool.
