Frequently asked questions

What are the spa opening hours?

The hotel's luxury spa is open daily from 8am to 8pm and is available exclusively for hotel guests.

What facilities does the spa offer?

The spa features an indoor swimming pool, sauna, steam room, hydro pool and fully equipped gym.

When is the indoor pool open?

The indoor swimming pool is open daily from 8am to 8pm.

What are the children's swim times?

Dedicated children's swim times are 9.30 to 11am and 4 to 6pm.

Can children use the pool?

Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool.

Is there an age restriction for the gym?

Yes, children are not allowed in the gym until the age of 16.

What are the gym opening hours?

The gym is open daily from 8am to 8pm, as part of the spa.

Does the spa have a sauna and steam room?

Yes, the spa includes both a sauna and a steam room, along with a hydro pool.

Is there a hydro pool at the spa?

Yes, the spa features a hydro pool alongside the indoor swimming pool.

Can children use the spa facilities?

Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult in the pool, and are not permitted in the gym until the age of 16.
