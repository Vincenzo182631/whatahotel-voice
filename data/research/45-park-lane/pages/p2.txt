Rooms & suites FAQs

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is at 12pm (noon).

Can I arrange early check-in or late check-out?

Early check-in or late check-out can be requested up to 1 hour before/after standard times. Late check-out (until 4pm) is chargeable at 50% of the best available daily rate.

How much does an extra bed cost?

For guests aged 13 and up, an extra bed is £84. For guests younger than 13, an extra bed is complimentary.

How do I make a reservation?

Call our reservations office on +44 207 898 9870, or email reservations.45l@dorchestercollection.com.

How many rooms qualify as a group booking?

Bookings of six or more rooms are treated as group reservations.

Can I bring my dog to the room?

Yes, up to two small dogs per room (under 7kg/15lbs) at £35 per day. We provide a bowl, bed and treats.

Is breakfast included with my room?

Breakfast is served à la carte at CUT; please check your specific rate for inclusions.

Can I add an extra night beyond my booking?

Additional nights can be arranged at the best available daily rate, subject to availability and excluding major events and high-demand units.

Is there a charge for parking when I stay?

Yes, parking is £60 per night.

Is an extra bed available for younger children?

Yes, extra beds are available. For guests younger than 13, an extra bed is complimentary. For guests aged 13 and up, an extra bed is £84.

What time do I need to check out by?

Check-out is at 12pm (noon), with late check-out guaranteed up to 1 hour after, subject to conditions.
