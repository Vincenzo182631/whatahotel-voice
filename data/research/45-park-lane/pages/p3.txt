Frequently asked questions

What restaurants are available at 45 Park Lane?

We have CUT at 45 Park Lane, Sushi Kanesaka, and Bar 45.

Tell me about CUT at 45 Park Lane.

CUT at 45 Park Lane is our American steak restaurant, open daily.

Tell me about Sushi Kanesaka.

Sushi Kanesaka is our exclusive Michelin-starred Japanese sushi restaurant, open Tuesday to Saturday with 6pm and 8.30pm sittings.

What are BAR 45's opening hours?

BAR 45 is open daily from 12pm to 12am (midnight).

What time is breakfast served and where?

Breakfast is served à la carte at CUT, from 7.30 to 10.30am.

Which days is Sushi Kanesaka open?

Sushi Kanesaka is open Tuesday to Saturday, with sittings at 6pm and 8.30pm.

Is CUT open every day?

Yes, CUT at 45 Park Lane is open daily.

Where can I get a drink in the evening?

BAR 45 is open daily from noon until midnight (12pm to 12am).

Do I need to book a sitting at Sushi Kanesaka?

Yes, Sushi Kanesaka has a limit of 13 seats so we recommend booking ahead. Its set sittings are available at 6pm and 8.30pm, Tuesday to Saturday.

Where is breakfast served for in-house guests?

Breakfast is served à la carte at CUT, from 7.30 to 10.30am. Guests can also choose to enjoy breakfast in their room.
