## A Crans-Montana hotel in Switzerland with chalet architecture and Alpine sophistication

Enjoy direct ski-in and ski-out access to a natural high-altitude playground on the Chetzeron red slope. Be part of a vibrant village life with a rich social scene, dining, shopping, and major events and activities in winter and summer. Restore tired legs in Six Senses Spa, which blends high-tech biohacking with high-touch therapies. At the end of each day, return under a blanket of stars to cozy up at the open-air cinema or relive the day’s adventures by the crackling fire in the bar. Welcome to Six Senses Crans-Montana hotel in Switzerland, where vitality meets serenity.

## Where every day is an adventure

Known for its champagne skiing and traditional architecture, Crans-Montana is a capital of world-class sporting events, a stylish center of culture and gourmet restaurants, and a shopper’s paradise of luxury brands. Crans-Montana’s natural charms include 300 days of sunshine a year and exceptional air quality. From winter snow sports to summer golf and equestrian pursuits, the marked contrast of the seasons adds personality to a host of experiences that reinvigorate the spirit and awaken all the senses.

## Accommodation

The Terrace Rooms and Suites enjoy a slope-side position with balconies facing the final turn of the Chetzeron piste: the only sounds are the chuckle of a stream and occasional swoosh of a ski.

- View all accommodation

## Residences

The residential apartments – ranging in size from 210 to 330 square meters (2,260 to 3,550 square feet) – are located in the upper part of the hotel. Choose from three to five bedrooms, with two to three bathrooms and private parking places. All can be sold as a secondary residence. By choosing the Six Senses interior design option, your apartment will be fully furnished and decorated and therefore can be included in the rental option managed through the resort.

## Destination

A prime ski-in, ski-out position just above the main gondola in Crans, a “Best of the Alps” resort that is celebrated worldwide.
