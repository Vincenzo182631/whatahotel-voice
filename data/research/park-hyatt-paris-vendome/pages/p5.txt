### A hotel that takes enormous pride in its people, and it shows

Our stay at the Park Hyatt Paris-Vendôme was memorable from the first minute. On arrival, our taxi driver's card terminal failed and I had no cash on me. Without any fuss, the hotel staff came out and paid the driver in cash on the spot. A small crisis resolved with total grace, and it set the tone for everything that followed.

The location could not be better. The hotel sits on Rue de la Paix, a minute's walk from Place Vendôme, with the Opéra Garnier at one end of the street and the Tuileries and the Louvre a short stroll away. Everything we wanted from Paris was on foot, and returning to the hotel each evening felt like coming home to the calmest address in the city.

And it really is calm. Despite being in the very heart of Paris, the suite was remarkably quiet. We never heard traffic, corridors, or neighboring rooms, and we slept beautifully every night. The atmosphere throughout the hotel matches that serenity: understated, elegant, and discreet rather than showy, with beautiful materials, soft lighting, and staff who seem to glide rather than rush. It is luxury with the volume turned down, which is exactly what we wanted.

Our suite was gorgeous, generously sized, and thoughtfully laid out, with a marble bathroom that was a pleasure to use. Housekeeping was superb. The suite was kept immaculate throughout, serviced constantly and always to the highest standard. In a week we never once found so much as a smudge; cleanliness here is close to obsessive, in the best way.

The in-room breakfast service was the highlight. The lady who served us on our last three mornings was exceptional. She remembered exactly how we liked everything laid out, brought the exotic fruits she knew we enjoyed, and made sure there were fresh flowers on the tray each morning. That level of attentiveness is rare anywhere, and it turned each breakfast into a genuine pleasure.

That breakfast story sums up the service as a whole. Every department, from the front desk to housekeeping to the engineering team, was responsive, warm, and genuinely proactive. The amenities matched the standard: excellent in-room dining, fast complimentary Wi-Fi, fresh water replenished daily, and every request handled promptly and without fuss.
