## Icon of understated elegance

Park Hyatt Paris-Vendôme, Place Vendôme's contemporary hotel, is an intimate Parisian residence where art elegantly blends with French classicism.

## Discover Park Hyatt Paris-Vendôme

Fitness Center

Located within the hotel's spa, the fitness center provides state-of-the-art Technogym® equipments. Access is complimentary for Park Hyatt Paris-Vendôme guests and available through a subscription for external visitors. A personal coach is also available upon request.

Le Spa

The spa invites both hotel guests and Parisians to indulge in its 2700 sq.ft. sanctuary of wellness. Nestled beside the inviting jetted tub, the cozy relaxation lounge is surrounded by a sauna, a steam room and 4 exquisite treatment rooms (including one duo). The hotel exclusively partners with the prestigious La Mer cosmetics brand.

L’Appartement du Park Hyatt Paris-Vendôme

### Pets Are Welcome

Experience Paris with your pet at Park Hyatt Paris-Vendôme, where your companion will enjoy their own bed and welcome amenities.

There is a daily fee of €50 per pet for pets under 10 kilograms and a €100 daily fee for pets over 10 kilograms. Any damage that your pet might cause in the room will be charged. Pets are not allowed at Restaurant PUR' - Jean-François Rouquette and our Spa.
