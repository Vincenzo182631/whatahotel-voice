## STK Restaurant San Diego

STK Steakhouse artfully creates an upscale dining experience. The central lounge's leather banquettes are surrounded by an elevated dining room offering a more formal atmosphere. Theatrical lights illuminate each table and large windows provide a view into San Diego while a DJ sets the mood. The restaurant is open for dine-in, takeout and delivery.
