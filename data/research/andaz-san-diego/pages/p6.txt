Getting around

San Diego Metropolitan Transit System (MTS)

One-way fares are $2.50; all-day fares are $6.50. Children under 5 ride free when accompanied by an adult.

PRONTO

PRONTO is the easy way to pay for public transit in San Diego County. Visitors or residents can get a PRONTO card at any station ticket machine (including the San Diego Airport), MTS Transit Store, NCTD Customer Service Center or retail outlet or download the app for easy accessibility. Just load money on your card and go. A one-way fare is deducted each time you travel, up to the value of a day pass. One-way fares include unlimited transfers for up to two hours.

Trolley Extension to UTC and La Jolla

Explore more of San Diego with the new UC San Diego Blue Line Trolley extension to UTC. The trolley now serves beach communities, UC San Diego, La Jolla and UTC. The San Diego Metropolitan Transit System bus and trolley network offers easy access to many top destinations in San Diego County, including Balboa Park, Gaslamp Quarter, Mission Valley, the airport and elsewhere.
