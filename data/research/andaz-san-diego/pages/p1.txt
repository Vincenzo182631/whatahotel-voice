# Explore Downtown San Diego from our Boutique Gaslamp District Hotel

Overlooking downtown, Andaz San Diego offers contemporary living spaces epitomizing Californian cool. Opened in 1913 for the World’s Fair celebrating the Panama Canal, this historic building is a half mile from Petco Park. Our boutique hotel is surrounded by vibrant nightlife, upscale dining and popular attractions. Experience one of San Diego's finest hotels and the Gaslamp Quarter's largest rooftop, featuring a restaurant, bar and pool.

## Rooms & Suites

Discover stylish downtown San Diego hotel suites with living spaces that express the creative spirit of the Gaslamp Quarter through local art and vibrant decor. We have the perfect room for you, whether you're looking for a quick getaway, an eight-person dining area or king bunk beds — or even access to a private cabana via spiral staircase from your suite up to the rooftop pool.

### The Rooftop by STK

The Rooftop by STK embodies the spirit of San Diego—open air energy, coastal sophistication, and effortless style. It’s the kind of rooftop where locals and visitors naturally gather to unwind, connect, and savor elevated dining in a setting that’s vibrant, high energy, and never overdone.

### In-Room Dining

Stay in, kick back, and let us take care of the rest. From crave-worthy entrées to shareable bites, In-Room Dining brings the experience to you—so you can relax, recharge, and enjoy it all on your own time. Available during restaurant hours. Dial ext. 3185.

### STK Restaurant San Diego

STK Steakhouse artfully creates an upscale dining experience. The central lounge's leather banquettes are surrounded by an elevated dining room offering a more formal atmosphere. Theatrical lights illuminate each table and large windows provide a view into San Diego while a DJ sets the mood. The restaurant is open for dine-in, takeout and delivery.

## Dogs Are Welcome

Andaz San Diego would love to welcome your furry little friends. Your canine companion can lounge in one of our cozy dog beds, stay hydrated with water from our Andaz bowls and get energized from a treat at the front desk before you go exploring through the Gaslamp District. We want their experience to be as memorable as yours. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy.
