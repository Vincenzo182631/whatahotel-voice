# Royal Malewane

Experience the wild heart of the African bush in comfort, style and seclusion at Royal Malewane, The Royal Portfolio’s safari collection in the Greater Kruger National Park. As one of Africa’s most celebrated luxury safari lodges in South Africa, choose from four distinctive lodges: Malewane Lodge, Waterside, Farmstead, and Masiya’s Camp, each offering a series of exquisite suites. For ultimate privacy, select one of our exclusive-use private villas: Africa House, Waterside House, or Farmhouse.

Why stay at Royal Malewane?

Royal Malewane is consistently ranked among the finest luxury safari lodges in South Africa, and for good reason. Situated within a vast, unfenced wilderness area in the Greater Kruger National Park, it offers an extraordinary combination of Big Five game viewing, award-winning guiding and four distinct lodge experiences under a single collection. Royal Malewane pairs the raw intensity of the African bush with an exceptional level of comfort, personalised service and culinary excellence. For guests seeking the definitive luxury Greater Kruger safari, it remains an unrivalled choice.

Where is Royal Malewane located?

Royal Malewane is located within the Greater Kruger National Park in Limpopo, South Africa, one of the largest and most biodiverse protected wilderness areas on the continent. Positioned within an unfenced 14,000-hectare reserve that forms part of the broader Greater Kruger ecosystem. The nearest airport is Hoedspruit (Eastgate), approximately 45 minutes away, with connections from Johannesburg O.R. Tambo International. For guests travelling to Royal Malewane, both road and charter flight transfers are available.

What accommodation options does the Royal Malewane lodge offer?

Royal Malewane offers four distinct luxury lodges in the Greater Kruger National Park: Malewane Lodge, Waterside, Farmstead and Masiya’s Camp – each with its own character, suite configuration and design sensibility. In addition, three exclusive-use private villas (Africa House, Waterside House and Farmhouse) are available for families, small groups or those seeking complete seclusion. Whether you choose a suite at one of the lodges or an entire villa to yourself, every option delivers the same hallmark of the Royal Malewane experience: extraordinary guiding, impeccable hospitality and an intimate connection to the Greater Kruger National Park.
