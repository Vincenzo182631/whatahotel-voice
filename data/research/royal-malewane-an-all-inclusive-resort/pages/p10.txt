Itineraries

## The Perfect Journey

On one of our curated luxury itineraries, you can enjoy ultimate peace of mind every step of the way. Combining our unique properties into a single itinerary lets you sample the sheer diversity of landscapes and activities on offer as you journey from Cape Town to Kruger. Customise your itinerary to focus on wildlife, culture, food and drink – or a perfectly balanced blend of the very best of South Africa.

Experience seamless transitions and the feeling of being part of our family. We’ll pass on your preferences to the other properties on your itinerary, so that we can customise the small but important details that add up to the complete experience. With consistently high standards of hospitality, dining and activities, you can rest assured that everything is taken care of.

Contact our experienced reservations team today to create your own perfect journey with The Royal Portfolio.
