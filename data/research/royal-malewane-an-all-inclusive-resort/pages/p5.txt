# Experiences at Royal Malewane

Led by the most qualified guiding team in Africa, a safari at Royal Malewane is unlike any other. Whether you are in one of the luxury vehicles, on foot or by air, our expert team will guide you on a journey of discovery through a vast wilderness rich in wildlife and across evocative landscapes.

Royal Malewane Experiences

All ExperiencesActivitiesExcursions

**Drive in the Wild** Royal Malewane

Spend your days with Field Guides and Trackers who’ve spent years reading this landscape. The heady aromas of the bush, light shifting across the savanna at dusk, the charged silence of tracking predators – these impressions remain as precious memories and a reminder to return.

Discover

**Safari on Foot** Royal Malewane

There’s something almost primal about a guided walk through the Greater Kruger National Park. Being so close to the sights and sounds of nature, every aspect of the landscape comes alive – from the smallest creatures underfoot to the grandest of wildlife encounters, all under the expert guidance of our team.

Walk in the Wild

**Conservation Activities** Royal Malewane

Through specially curated activities, we invite you to get a close look at endangered species and work alongside experts in the field, experiencing first-hand the life-saving conservation work being done for wild animals in the Greater Kruger National Park.

Get Involved

**Photographic Safaris** Royal Malewane

Capture remarkable moments in the bush with the assistance of our Guides, many of whom are passionate photographers. Their intimate knowledge of animal behaviour and movement, combined with an instinct for finding the best light, makes for an extraordinary experience behind the lens.

Capture Magic

**Birding Safaris** Royal Malewane

Whether an experienced twitcher or a first timer, we guarantee an incredible birdwatching experience in the Greater Kruger National Park. Our guides have earned the country’s top qualifications in birding, making this the perfect place to tick off even the most elusive species.

Discover

**Africa From the Air** Royal Malewane

Burst through the clouds to a breathtaking view of the world’s largest green canyon – the savanna of the Greater Kruger stretching out on one side, the dramatic rise of the Drakensberg mountain range on the other

Take Flight

**Little Explorers** Royal Malewane

Introducing children to nature at an early age encourages a lifetime of discovery and wonder at the creatures that inhabit our natural world. It is the start of a connection with wildlife that deepens perspective and builds a curiosity that stays with them for life.

Adventure Awaits

**Celebrations** Royal Malewane

Royal Malewane is a remarkable setting for life’s most special celebrations. From milestone birthdays to surprise proposals and secret elopements, the bush and its big African skies provide an unmatched backdrop for the moments that matter most.

Dream Wild

**Simple Sophisticated Dining** Royal Malewane

Dining at Royal Malewane is always an occasion. Every dish is designed to be savoured, complemented by bush settings of spectacular natural beauty and the finest Cape wines.

Experience

**Unwind in the Wild** Royal Malewane

Nourishment for body, mind and soul seems to come naturally in this part of the world. Guests are welcome to make the most of the meditation spaces, spa treatments, yoga sessions on the deck or a workout in the gym, with plenty of time for rest and relaxation.

Find Connection

**Kruger Park Golf Excursions** Royal Malewane

A helicopter flight to the exclusive Leopard Creek Country Club takes you to one of South Africa’s premier golf courses, designed by golfing legend Gary Player and unfolding across a breathtaking natural landscape.

Tee Off

**Star Safari** Royal Malewane

Far away from the city lights, the galaxy twinkles in the darkness as our Guides take you on a journey through the stars and constellations. Open your mind to what might lie beyond our atmosphere, where knowledge gives way to wonder.
