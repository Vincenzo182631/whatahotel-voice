Why stay at La Residence in Franschhoek?

La Residence is widely regarded as the finest luxury hotel in Franschhoek – a private estate unlike anything else in the Cape Winelands. Set on 30 acres of manicured gardens, vineyards and mountain scenery, it offers an exceptional combination of seclusion, personalised service and culinary excellence. Guests enjoy access to the acclaimed high tea experience, a heated pool and curated wine experiences across the valley. As a member of The Leading Hotels of the World and part of The Royal Portfolio collection, La Residence delivers an experience that extends well beyond a conventional Franschhoek hotel stay

Where is La Residence located?

La Residence is located in the Franschhoek Valley in the Western Cape, South Africa, approximately 75 kilometres from Cape Town. The estate sits within the Franschhoek Wine Valley, surrounded by the dramatic Drakenstein and Franschhoek mountain ranges. For guests seeking luxury accommodation in Franschhoek with easy access to both the Cape Winelands and Cape Town, the location is ideal.

How many rooms are at La Residence in Franschhoek?

La Residence in Franschhoek offers three distinct accommodation types: Hotel Suites, Vineyard Suites and the exclusive-use Franschhoek House – a private six-bedroom villa ideal for families or small groups seeking complete seclusion. Each suite is individually designed, blending eclectic European antiques with the warmth of a private home. Given the intimate scale of this luxury hotel in Franschhoek, early reservation is strongly advised, particularly during the Cape Winelands high season.
