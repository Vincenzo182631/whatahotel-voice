# Wellness at Royal Malewane

Malewane Lodge at Royal Malewane

## Royal Waters Spa

Recently reimagined and positioned around a central courtyard with a 25m heated lap pool are three tranquil treatment rooms (two double and one single) as well as a fully equipped fitness centre, sauna, cold plunge, outdoor baths and a hair salon.

With its refreshed design and expanded wellness offerings, the Royal Waters Spa ensures an immersive, restorative experience, inviting guests to relax, recharge, and reconnect with nature.

Farmstead at Royal Malewane

## Double Spa Treatment Room

Guests staying at Farmstead can restore body and mind in the double spa treatment room or choose to make use of the Royal Waters Spa located at Malewane Lodge. Mother Nature has a way of healing us, and that’s exactly what we’ve encapsulated in all our expert treatments.
