# Rooms and Suites at Sheraton Grand London Park Lane

## Accommodations

With a prestigious location on Park Lane overlooking Green Park, Sheraton Grand London Park Lane offers refined accommodation in London, England, in the heart of Mayfair. Elegant Art Deco interiors blend...See More timeless English charm with modern comfort, while guests often highlight the clean, comfortable rooms. Each of our Park Lane hotel rooms is designed for relaxation, with the Sheraton Signature Sleep Experience Bed for a restful night's sleep. Bathrobes, slippers and spacious wet rooms with Sheraton-branded toiletries add to the experience. For extra space, our luxury London suites and Club Rooms offer elevated comfort and a polished base for city breaks or business stays. Every room and suite includes a 32" HD plasma TV and free Wi-Fi, ensuring a stay that is both relaxing and convenient.See Less

## Discover Our Suites

Boasting beautiful art décor interiors, our suites take you back to an era of timeless elegance and class. Enjoy a truly luxurious stay in Mayfair, with exclusive access to the Sheraton Club Lounge and impressive views of the Royal Parks.

## Grand Park View Suite

The Grand Park View Suite is a prime location for a view of the Royal Parks.
The luxurious Grand Park View Suite is a one of a kind Park Lane suite situated in the heart of Mayfair. Boasting a luxurious art décor style, this suite takes you back to an era of timeless elegance, beauty and class.

## Club Rooms

Our glamourous Club Rooms are located on the higher levels of the hotel and invite to relish in the twenties style. All Club Rooms & Suites include exclusive access to the Club Lounge.
