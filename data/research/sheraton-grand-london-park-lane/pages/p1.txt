# Welcome to Sheraton Grand London Park Lane

## Discover a grand experience at our hotel in central London

Since its glamorous debut in the 1920s, Sheraton Grand London Park Lane has embodied the elegance and grandeur of Mayfair. This iconic Park Lane hotel in London overlooks Green Park and places guests within...See More easy reach of landmarks, making it stand out among hotels in central London and for travelers seeking hotels near Buckingham Palace and Piccadilly Circus. As a family hotel in central London, we offer luxurious amenities and pet-friendly accommodation, welcoming dogs up to 25kg. For special occasions and city breaks, signature suites and Club Rooms pair modern comfort with timeless sophistication and Club Lounge access. Guests praise the attentive service, and despite the central setting the hotel offers a quiet retreat feel. Enjoy award-winning afternoon tea in Palm Court, savour Italian cuisine at Mercante, or sample craft beers at Smith & Whistle. Whether exploring London's landmarks or celebrating in style, discover one of the luxury hotels in Park Lane, London, for a quintessential Mayfair stay.See Less

## Our Sustainability Story

We are committed to making a positive and sustainable impact by integrating sustainability and social impact practices across our hotel. Our goal is to improve our environmental footprint and strengthen engagement within our community through various initiatives. ## History & Heritage of the Building

The entire hotel is classified as a Grade II listed building, of particular note is The Silver Gallery, the Ballroom, the Oak Room and the Palm Court.

## Art Deco Heritage & Central Location

The compelling art décor interior and the refined location within Mayfair is what sets The Sheraton Grand London Park Lane apart from other London hotels. The hotel’s ideal location near Buckingham Palace and its regal position directly in front of Green Park allows guest to immerse themselves in true British culture and heritage.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Grand London Park Lane?

The check-in time at Sheraton Grand London Park Lane is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Grand London Park Lane allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Grand London Park Lane. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 25.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Grand London Park Lane?

Off-site self parking is available near Sheraton Grand London Park Lane at Q‑Park, W1K 7AN & Euro Car Parks, W1K 1AB nearby —contact Concierge to benefit from discounts..

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Grand London Park Lane have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Grand London Park Lane has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Grand London Park Lane booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Grand London Park Lane?

The closest airport near Sheraton Grand London Park Lane is London City Airport (LCY). LCY is located approximately 17.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Grand London Park Lane have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Grand London Park Lane does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does Sheraton Grand London Park Lane have a Sheraton Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Grand London Park Lane has Sheraton Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Sheraton Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
