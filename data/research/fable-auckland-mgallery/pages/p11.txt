## Our Hotel

A blend of historic charm and modern luxury

Dunedin’s 5-star gem with roots dating back to 1862, a symphony of Southern elegance and modern luxury. The hotel, a rebirth of the iconic Wains Hotel, promises a journey through time with 50 plush rooms, two suites, and The Press Club—a dynamic eatery celebrating Dunedin's gold-rush era. Award-winning design, captivating event spaces, and impeccable service create an unforgettable experience for discerning travelers. Blink, and you might miss this corner-of-the-eye treasure on Dunedin's scene—a world of charm and sophistication waiting to be discovered.

## Our Restaurant

The Press Club is a bespoke bar and restaurant paying homage to Dunedin’s original haunt for editors and publishers in the 1870’s.

In a nod to the esteemed media characters and prominent citizens of Dunedin who frequented the Hotel 150 years ago, a sense of subtle sophistication, with a good measure of frivolity, is echoed in the gorgeous interior design and exquisite service.

A range of dishes with an emphasis on using locally-sourced, organic produce often feature classically-Southern flavours, techniques and ingredients.

The extensive Whisky menu is a signature highlight at The Press Club, with a simple tipple or elaborate cocktail setting the scene for a superb dining experience, or finish off the evening in style.

From Wednesday - Sunday, 12pm - 3:30pm, indulge in a slice of tradition with a contemporary take on High Tea at The Press Club. Treat yourself to this decadent experience that is designed to be savoured and enjoyed with good company.

## FAQs For Fable Dunedin

Where is the hotel located and how do I get there?

We are located at 310 Princes Street in the heart of Dunedin within easy walking distance of the Octagon. To reach us from Dunedin Airport, you can take a 30-minute ride via taxi, shuttle or Uber. On top of that, we offer secure valet parking for $40 per day based on availability.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts from 2:00 PM and check-out is before 11:00 AM. In addition, early check-in and late check-out options may be available upon request. Extra fees may apply depending on room availability.

Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

Yes. We offer wheelchair access to our key areas including reception, elevators, public bathrooms and our restaurant. Since we are a historic heritage property, we recommend contacting our team before arrival so we can arrange the best setup for your stay.

Can I pay for my stay when I arrive?

Yes. For most flexible rates, your credit card holds the reservation and you can settle payment directly at the hotel. As a reminder, credit card payments at the hotel carry a 2.5% surcharge while cash or EFTPOS payments carry no extra fee.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation rules depend on the package you select when booking. For instance, flexible rates allow free cancellation up to 2:00 PM the day before arrival. Non-refundable advance purchase rates cannot be cancelled or refunded.

Do you have meeting and event facilities?

Yes. We offer four flexible meeting spaces ranging from intimate rooms to our large Chancellor Room that holds up to 110 guests. To support your event, our team provides AV equipment, catering, delegate packages and free Wi-Fi.

Can I host weddings or private celebrations at Fable Dunedin?

Absolutely. Our heritage venue hosts wedding ceremonies, receptions, gala dinners and special occasions. Furthermore, our dedicated events team assists with catering, venue layout, beverage selection and guest accommodation.

Is Fable Dunedin suitable for business travelers and couples?

Yes. Corporate guests enjoy our central location, premium rooms and event spaces. As a result of our heritage charm, fine dining and close distance to local attractions, we are also a popular choice for couples seeking a city getaway.
