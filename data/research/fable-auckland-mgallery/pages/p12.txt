## FAQs For Fable Auckland

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in starts at 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. ALL Accor loyalty members get priority perks like late check-out. If you need extra time, ask us when booking or stop by the front desk during your stay. We can often extend your check-out until 1:00 PM or 2:00 PM.

What services and amenities do you offer?

We provide free high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 room service, a gym, a jacuzzi, a sauna, and accessible facilities. We also offer valet parking for an extra fee along with meeting spaces for events.

What are the dining options at the hotel?

You can eat at Cooke's Restaurant & Bar, which serves dishes made from fresh local ingredients. Breakfast runs from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM on weekdays, extending until 11:00 AM on weekends. Kids' menus and room service are also available.

Can I pay for my stay when I arrive?

Yes. You can reserve online without paying in advance. Just select the Flexible Rate option when booking, then pay at the front desk when you check in.

Is the hotel wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Fable Auckland has 3 accessible rooms designed for guests with reduced mobility. All elevators and public areas—including Cooke's Restaurant & Bar—are fully accessible.

How does the cancellation policy work?

You can manage or cancel your booking online or through our customer service team. Cancellation terms depend on the rate option you choose when booking.
