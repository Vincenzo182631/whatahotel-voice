# Christmas

Make this Christmas one to remember at Fable.
With twinkling lights, festive feasts, and all the little touches that bring the season to life, it’s the perfect place to celebrate. Whether you’re gathering with loved ones or simply soaking up the magic, let us help you make memories to treasure.

## Host with us

We'll take care of the details.

#### Fable Auckland

### $65pp - $130pp

From bountiful buffets to elegant plated set menus, cocktail soirées to relaxed snack-and-sip gatherings, we’ve got your end-of-year celebration covered. Most packages include room hire, and naturally, drinks packages to help you toast to the year that was.
