## What's on

#### High Tea.

Nothing says indulgence more than high tea

Experience indulgence at its finest with our renowned high tea at Cooke’s Restaurant & Bar. Dating back to the mid-1700s, this cherished British tradition has captivated hearts worldwide, and our signature menu ensures an afternoon tea like no other in Auckland.

Served daily from 12pm to 3pm, our high tea is not just an ordinary affair. Whether it's a special event like Mother's Day or any other occasion, we strive to curate a decadent experience meant for sharing. Pair your tea with a delightful selection of Dilmah Teas, elevate it with champagne, or opt for the ultimate indulgence with a trio of cocktails.
