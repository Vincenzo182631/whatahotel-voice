# Hotel Fitzroy

## Our Hotel

A boutique haven redefined

Find your sanctuary at Hotel Fitzroy, where classic meets contemporary in the heart of Ponsonby. Unique décor and local art find a home in the old-world manor and modern annex, where 10 luxe rooms are equipped with the best of technology and comfortable beds. Fitzroy’s culture is encapsulated by breakfast in bed and afternoon tea, bookending each day with moments to savour. Enjoy the best of Auckland’s dining, shopping and entertainment just meters from your front door, or simply relax and recharge in the comfort of the property.

### Features & Facilities

- 10 beautifully designed, luxurious guest rooms
- Special welcome refreshment
- Guest library
- 48 inch LCD televisions with all SKY channels, Movielink and Chromecast
- Complimentary mini bar with drinks, snacks, coffee and teas
- Local and sustainable luxurious bathroom amenities, robe, and slippers
- Dyson hair dryer

### Guest Services

- 24-hour reception
- Paperless check-in
- 24-hour room service
- Complimentary high tea and drinks served in the library (5pm - 6pm)
- In-room breakfast service
- Unlimited complimentary WIFI connectivity throughout the hotel
- Dry-cleaning and laundry service
- Express check-out

## Our Rooms

### Superior Room

A carefully curated scene of refined relaxation awaits in each one of our Superior suites.

Taking the design lead from the lofty ceilings and statement windows of the original villa, our rooms are spacious, warm and bathed in natural light. Beautiful soft furnishings, high-tech entertainment and connectivity, generous ensuite and deluxe amenities make it irresistible to rest and relax.

Curl up on your King bed with a book or drink from your complimentary mini bar before exploring the Ponsonby area then return to a night of unparalleled comfort.

Book Now

### Luxury Room

Discover a level of truly unmatched luxury in this bespoke and intimate setting, designed to reimagine the boutique hotel experience.

From the sumptuous interior design to the bespoke furnishings, linens and amenities, our Luxury suites offer extra space and stunning views of Richmond Road and beyond, with a charming lounge nook and access to nearby balconies.

These suites possess the perfect finishing touches to make your stay utterly memorable, including premium linens and complimentary mini bar, King size bed, large ensuite and entertainment or connectivity at your fingertips.

Book Now

## Our Dining

Crafted for you

From sipping on a refreshing welcome drink, to relaxing in the library with high tea in the afternoon, and basking in the morning sunshine over your in-room breakfast service, our dining is a memorable part of your stay with us.

Fancy a snack? Your complimentary mini bar is replenished daily. Tray-service breakfast is a signature part of our experience and the perfect way to start your day.

## Explore like a local

Ponsonby

Vibrant Ponsonby Road is a mere 5 minute stroll away, boasting many of the best restaurants and bars in Auckland, with cuisines for all tastes. Cafes and boutique shopping stretch the length of this iconic road, while garden-like reserves nestled around the neighbourhood offer a restful spot to enjoy a coffee.
