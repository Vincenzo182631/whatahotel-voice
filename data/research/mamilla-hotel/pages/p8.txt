# Eat & Drink

## Taste the Variety of Jerusalem

The luxury Mamilla Hotel offers you a delectable range of dining options. Happy Fish Restaurant, Rooftop, Mirror Bar, and more! From classic recipes to contemporary culinary innovations; from Mediterranean to gourmet dishes and some of the finest Israeli wines. Come and savor the gastronomic delights of Jerusalem!

_The hotel is Kosher under the supervision of the Jerusalem Rabbinate._

### The Rooftop

High above the city, the Rooftop Outdoor Lounge & Restaurant embraces the fresh Jerusalem breeze while offering a magnificent view of the Old City. There can hardly be a better location anywhere in the world for delectable food, pleasant music and al fresco dining.

### Happy Fish

A Mediterranean-inspired fish restaurant, located on a terrace overlooking bustling Mamilla Boulevard. The restaurant offers you a winning combination – central location, fresh and full-of-flavor fare, and a happy, lively atmosphere.

### Mirror Bar

Bask in the opulence of the Mirror Bar, one of the sleekest and stylish bars in Jerusalem. Delicious fare, a choice of cocktails, and an in-house DJ playing an eclectic variety of music, always appropriate to the ambience of the evening.

RESTAURANT DETAILS

### The Ballroom

Your perfect morning! A fresh, crispy, healthy breakfast, partly prepared on live cooking stations in our Ballroom Dining Room. Tall windows, Jerusalem views, and a delightful way to start your day!

RESTAURANT DETAILS

### The Winery

Raise your glass at The Winery, with its extensive selection of award-winning Israeli wines and wine accessories, along with savory snacks and high-quality olive oils from Israel’s most celebrated producers.

### Executive Lounge

Mamilla Hotel’s Business Lounge provides everything business travelers require during their stay in Jerusalem. Located at the entrance level, our luxurious, spacious lounge includes a meeting room for intimate business gatherings, video conferencing capabilities, computers, refreshments, and more.

The Executive Lounge is currently closed. We look forward to welcoming you again soon.

### Espresso Bar

Follow the aroma of freshly ground coffee to the Espresso Bar in the Mamilla Hotel lobby. Indulge in gourmet coffee, freshly baked pastries, savory snacks and an impressive selection of refreshing drinks.
