## A Tranquil Oasis of Wellbeing in Jerusalem—breathe deeply, regain balance, and rejuvenate at Mamilla’s serene sanctuary.

_Before earth, water, fire, and air, there was Akasha — the source of life and harmony._

At the luxurious Mamilla Hotel, Akasha wellness offers a holistic experience, uniting nature’s elements in the heart of Jerusalem.

This tranquil haven invites you to slow down, breathe deeply, and renew your spirit, body and mind.

With serene spaces, advanced fitness equipment, classes, personal training, exceptional massages, and signature treatments, Akasha is celebrated as Israel’s leading hotel spa – the ultimate destination to pause and recharge.
