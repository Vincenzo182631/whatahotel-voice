Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay
Discover our Half Moon Bay resort in California
When the surrounding area is rich with natural resources and beauty, luxury comes with a distinctly local touch at our Half Moon Bay resort in California. At The Ritz-Carlton Half Moon Bay, guests can savor the Northern California sunset on a beautiful evening. Sample fine wines from vintners along the coast, craft s'mores over an open fire on their private terrace, discover the nourishing benefits of pumpkin at the spa, or tee off on two championship golf courses that are as beautiful as they are well-designed. Holding court on a bluff overlooking the ocean and just 20 miles from San Francisco International Airport, this luxury resort in Northern California celebrates its surroundings with an experience rooted in the element of fire and the discovery of wine.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 40.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay is San Francisco International Airport (SFO). SFO is located approximately 20.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
