Experiences
Quintessential Northern California, Half Moon Bay is one long stretch of gorgeous, wild coastline. Developed as a beach resort in the early 1900s, this tiny coastal community that lies between San Francisco and Santa Cruz now attracts die-hard surfers, golf aficionados and city dwellers looking for things to do in Half Moon Bay or a laid-back weekend beach escape. Perched on a bluff overlooking the Pacific Ocean, our Half Moon Bay spa hotel is an ideal launching point for experiencing the best of the region.

Whether you are currently enjoying your stay, planning a future visit, or looking to gift a memorable experience to a loved one, begin your journey here. Tennis Courts
The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay, in Northern California, offers a world-class tennis experience to our guests, with six lighted tennis courts and high-quality tennis instruction and programming. Included in your resort fee are tennis amenities such as court reservations, tennis rackets, tennis balls, and usage of the ball machine. Private lessons, group clinics, and organized corporate events are available for an additional fee. We also provide complimentary player matching for players of all levels, drawing from our extensive local tennis playing community.
Half Moon Bay Tennis Academy
Our tennis program is offered by Half Moon Bay Tennis Academy and more information can be found on their website including a schedule of tennis events, rates, and information about our tennis professionals. To set up a private lesson with one of our tennis professionals, or to inquire about our clinics, mixers, or corporate events, please contact us at the numbers provided below.

Private Lessons
A private lesson is a unique experience designed to help you achieve your best on court. Our tennis professional will help you understand the game of tennis by using a variety of methods, drills, and challenges to assist you in overcoming any hurdles. There is an additional fee for Private Lessons and advanced reservations are required.
Tennis Clinic
Our clinics are designed to encourage students to be their own coaches. With this in mind, our tennis pros encourage students to learn new and exciting concepts and practice them in a progressive environment. Practical drills will be implemented and progressive games will be applied in this hour-and-a-half skill session. There is an additional fee for Tennis Clinics and advanced reservations are required.
Doubles Mixer
Our Doubles Mixer program is a chance for Colony Club members, Resort guests, and local tennis players to enjoy a social afternoon of tennis. Players will rotate courts and switch partners. Our tennis pros will encourage and organize teams, as well as use a variety of formats to keep things interesting.

Half Moon Bay – A Surfer’s Destination
Learn the art of surfing at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay – the premier Northern California beach resort. Almost every year Half Moon Bay is home to the world-renowned Titans of Mavericks, a surf competition bringing big wave surfers and surf enthusiasts to California to witness some of the largest waves in the world. This Half Moon Bay area spa hotel sits high atop the southernmost part of Half Moon Bay overlooking the Pacific Ocean. If you are a seasoned veteran or just want to learn the basics of surfing, our concierge can create a memorable surf experience on your vacation.

Running
Featuring spectacular coastal views and stable trails, running is an exhilarating way to experience Half Moon Bay. Step out of our backdoors and explore the miles of Coastal Trails at the seaside resort. Pick up a running map from our concierges to plan out the perfect distance for tomorrow’s run.
Sea Kayaking
Explore the ocean from a different vantage point while sea kayaking in Pillar Point Harbor. Whether you're a beginner or advanced kayaker, classes, tours, or simply rentals are available that can be taken out onto the ocean. Sea kayaking allows guests to see diverse marine life in their natural habitat the Pacific.
Horseback Riding
Experience horseback riding through the beautiful Northern California coast. Whether you're galloping on trails, through grassy meadows, or on the beach next to the crashing waves of the ocean, a memorable and scenic journey awaits. Please contact our Concierge for information on horseback riding in the area.
Wine Tasting
Home to more than 70 wineries, the Santa Cruz Mountains region has geography and climate so distinct that it merits its own appellation. You’ll find the Northern California scenery as spectacular as the award-winning, handcrafted wine you can sample on tasting tours along the way. Please contact our Concierge for information on Santa Cruz Wineries including tastings and tours.
Whale Watching
Considered Northern California’s premier whale-watching destination, the Bay Area offers the opportunity to witness majestic gray whales as they migrate south. Whale-watching cruises depart from Princeton Harbor and venture 10 to 15 miles away from shore toward the migratory paths of these majestic mammals.
Bike Riding
Our Half Moon Bay spa resort offers beach cruisers, maps, bottled water, and protective gear for overnight guests. Rides start at the resort and travel north along the Coastal Trail that runs parallel to the Northern California coastline.

Signature Drive
From pumpkin farms and fruit stands to wineries and wind-swept beaches, Signature Drive offers a scenic trip through five distinct regions. Guests who devote a few hours of their day will experience some of the country’s most magnificent vistas.

Explore Half Moon Bay
Whether you’re traveling by car or motorcycle, let our Signature Drive be your travel guide to some of America’s most magnificent vistas. The drive unfolds in a series of five distinct regions in and around Half Moon Bay.
Historic Main Street
Nestled within the picturesque town of Half Moon Bay, Historic Main Street offers year-round sightseeing, shops, restaurants, and galleries.
The Pumpkin Region
This is California farm-to-market country, where motorists can pick their own produce from the bounty of pumpkin patches and local farms that make the area a foodie’s dream.
California's Redwood Forest
Close to Half Moon Bay lies the awe-inspiring Redwood Forest, shaded by a magnificent, mystical canopy of ancient California Redwood trees.
Santa Cruz Mountains
This scenic and dramatic mountain range offers tastes from more than 60 of the state’s cult wineries.
Pacific Coast
Guests can end their drive by viewing the spectacular coast in front of our Half Moon Bay spa hotel, where some of the largest winter surf in the world is recorded each year.
