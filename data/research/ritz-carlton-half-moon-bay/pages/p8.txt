Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay
Holidays in Half Moon Bay
Discover the magic of the festive season at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay, where holiday cheer seamlessly blends with the timeless beauty of the Pacific Coast.
Tis the Season for Coastal Cheer
Celebrate the season with a host of festive experiences and enhanced offerings at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay. This November, savor a delicious Thanksgiving feast at Navio, then carry the joy into December by starting new traditions with family and friends. The resort transforms into a holiday wonderland, adorned with shimmering lights, ocean-inspired décor, and a magnificent Christmas tree overlooking the Pacific Coast.

Autumn
Pumpkin Season
Celebrate the season with a thoughtful collection of coastal experiences and resort enhancements. From signature dining to revitalizing Spa treatments, guests can enjoy the ocean air, vibrant fall foliage, and activities for the whole family throughout autumn.

Golden Autumn Renewal
Embrace the restorative essence of fall with a luxurious facial powered by organic pumpkin enzymes and botanical extracts at The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Half Moon Bay. This seasonal treatment refines, nourishes, and revitalizes the skin, revealing a healthy glow inspired by the abundance of the harvest.
Discover
Diwali Celebration
Honor the Festival of Lights with an elegant Navio Brunch. Executive Sous Chef Sagar Gaikwad presents a thoughtfully designed menu that blends Indian culinary traditions with contemporary technique, celebrating bold flavors and seasonal ingredients.

Thanksgiving
Thanksgiving Dinner at the Conservatory
Gather around a thoughtfully crafted tablespread at The Conservatory and enjoy a timeless feast inspired by the season’s finest flavors.

Thanksgiving Dinner at Navio
Savor a Seven-Course Thanksgiving Dinner Tasting Menu that reimagines the cherished holiday with refined sophistication. Featuring mouthwatering Traditional Early Bird Organic Turkey and more, each course highlights the season’s finest ingredients in an unforgettable oceanfront setting.

Thanksgiving Weekend Brunch at Navio
Experience an elevated two-hour Weekend Brunch celebrating the best of the autumn harvest. Indulge in a thoughtfully curated menu featuring seasonal favorites inspired by Thanksgiving traditions, artfully paired with fresh coastal ingredients. Conclude your meal with decadent desserts, a perfectly sweet finale to a memorable holiday gathering.

Festive Season
Festive Dinner at the Conservatory
Experience The Conservatory for an enchanting evening inspired by the season. Enjoy a specially curated dinner menu with local and sustainable ingredients, ocean-fresh fare, and warm, festive flavors. Whether it's a gathering with friends or a special night out, come toast to the holidays in Half Moon Bay.
Reserve
Festive Dinner at Navio
Embrace the magic of Christmas with a sumptuous Seven-Course Tasting Menu, thoughtfully inspired by the season’s most festive flavors. Indulge in Christmas Canelon finished with Périgord Truffle, Half Moon Bay Black Cod and Rosewood Ranch Wagyu Ribeye. The experience culminates in whimsical holiday-inspired desserts, including gingerbread with eggnog ice cream and more.

Festive Weekend Brunch at Navio
Revel in the spirit of the holidays with a lavish Festive Brunch at Navio. During the two-hour experience, savor a thoughtfully curated menu featuring seasonal specialties. Whether indulging in savory dishes or delicate desserts, enjoy a memorable celebration set against the stunning backdrop of the Pacific Coast.

New Year's
New Year’s Eve Dinner at Navio
Raise a glass and welcome the New Year with an opulent dining experience at Navio. Relish a tantalizing Tasting Menu featuring American White Sturgeon Caviar to Maine Lobster and Miyazaki A-5 Wagyu. Exceptional flavors and celebratory toasts await this New Year's Eve.

New Year's Brunch at Navio
Start the year with coastal elegance and culinary flair at Navio. During an enhanced New Year's Brunch, delight in seasonal courses inspired by traditional holiday favorites. Gather with family and friends for a leisurely holiday experience filled with memorable flavors, stunning ocean views, and an atmosphere befitting the occasion.

New Year Rejuvenation at The Spa
Start the New Year feeling refreshed, renewed, and restored. Unwind in a tranquil wellness sanctuary where seasonal restorative treatments and rejuvenating spa amenities come together to create the perfect environment for relaxation, renewal, and balance.

The Ritz-Carlton Spa
Find relaxation in our festive-inspired seasonal treatments and products, thoughtfully designed to rejuvenate mind, body, and spirit.
Discover
Holiday Scotch Lounge
Nestled within The Conservatory Library, this intimate pop-up invites guests to indulge in an exclusive journey through rare Scotch expressions, curated tasting flights, and signature cocktails. Savor seasonal bites in the cozy, elegant space that captures the charm of a serene retreat. Enhance your experience with guided tastings led by industry experts, featuring esteemed distillers such as The Macallan.
Discover
Gingerbread House Decorating Workshop
Bring holiday enchantment to life with the cherished tradition of decorating freshly baked gingerbread houses this festive season. Engage in a personalized demonstration guided by the resort’s pastry chef, while indulging in cozy holiday beverages amidst breathtaking views of the Pacific. At the end of the experience, take home a special keepsake along with the candy-coated masterpieces.
Reserve
Festive Afternoon Tea at The Conservatory
Delight in the warmth and cheer of the season with a Festive Afternoon Tea at The Conservatory. Sip classic blends from the renowned French tea house, Dammann Frères, accompanied by handcrafted delicacies and a breathtaking coastal backdrop. From housemade scones and holiday tea sandwiches to specialty cocktails and champagne, raise a toast and add a sparkling, cozy touch to your holiday celebration.
Reserve
Holiday Carolers
Discover the enchantment of music as timeless holiday melodies fill the air at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay. On select dates throughout December, Dickens-era carolers serenade guests at the Porte-Cochère, Lobby, Ocean Lawn, and Navio, spreading warmth and joyful cheer throughout the resort.
Explore
Photos with Santa & Stories with Mrs. Claus
On Sunday, December 13 and 20, create cherished holiday memories with a festive photo alongside Santa Claus, set against Half Moon Bay’s sparkling coastal backdrop. Then, on Christmas Eve, gather for a magical storytelling experience as Mrs. Claus shares heartwarming holiday tales sure to delight children and the young at heart.
