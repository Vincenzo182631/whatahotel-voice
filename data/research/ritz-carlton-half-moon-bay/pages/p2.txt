Take In the Views at Our Half Moon Bay Accommodation
Suites & Rooms
Discover luxury in the guest accommodations and suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay. Enjoy sumptuous bedding, in-room dining, a deep soaking tub and expansive views of the golf course, Northern California mountains or ocean when you stay in our ocean view accommodations in Half Moon Bay.
Option To Upgrade to Club Level Access
The Ritz-Carlton, Club Level invites guests to discover Half Moon Bay in distinct ways. Hosted by a dedicated concierge, this stay enhancement extends beyond the luxury accommodations and into the Club Lounge, a space exclusive to Club guests to meet, work, relax, and dine. Here, guests will discover culinary presentations highlighting the quintessential flavors and traditions of Northern California, as well as invite-only immersive cultural experiences and events. Additional per-person Club Level guest pricing applies to guests above the allotted two per room.

The Ritz-Carlton Coastal Suite
Indulge luxuriously in The Ritz-Carlton Coastal Suite, offering a seaside sanctuary for up to ten guests. This lavish three-bedroom Half Moon Bay suite boasts vaulted ceilings, a Juliet balcony, and ensuite bathrooms with hues of blue, evoking the serenity of the ocean. The cozy media room invites relaxation, while expansive living and dining areas provide a backdrop against the coastal vistas.
The Signature Suite
Embrace the elements of the coast in the Signature Suite, where sophistication meets the sea at every turn. With its sweeping ocean vistas and refined design, this one-bedroom retreat is a perfect seaside escape. Unwind with a luxurious bath while gazing out at the bluffs or gather with cherished company in the expansive living area. Every aspect of The Signature Suite promises an unforgettable stay.
The Luxury Fire Pit Suite
Luxury intertwines with nature's grandeur in the Luxury Fire Pit Suite, offering panoramic views of the Pacific. This ground-level suite is designed for intimate stays, providing ample space to immerse oneself in the coastal atmosphere. Roast s’mores by your private fire pit or relax by the tranquil fireplace in the bedroom. At sunset, witness the captivating spectacle of the sun sinking beyond the horizon.
Coastal Opulence
Signature Suites
At The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay, each suite exemplifies coastal elegance. With expansive terraces offering mesmerizing ocean views, generous common areas for socializing, and private fire pits and balconies, these exquisite retreats provide the ideal blend of tranquility and refinement for those seeking a memorable escape.

The Ritz-Carlton, Half Moon Bay
Guest Houses
For an extra level of comfort, privacy, and relaxation, choose one of our Guest House accommodations or Half Moon Bay suites. Located approximately 450 yards from the main building of the resort (approximately an eight-minute walk), these residential-style buildings provide the same luxurious appointments as the main building rooms along with convenient access to the recreational area of the resort featuring a covered pool, practice golf course, Half Moon Bay Golf Links, tennis courts, and the Mar Vista Lawn and Terrace featuring five fire pits.
The Ocean House
Facing the ocean and providing a stunning view of the 18th Hole of the Old Course as it leads to the Ocean Lawn of the Main Building, this Guest House offers four luxury hotel rooms in the Bay Area on the second floor and four Oceanfront guest rooms with fire pits on the ground floor. Additionally, we have four one-bedroom Ocean Front Suites on the second floor and four one-bedroom Ocean Front Suites with fire pits on the ground floor.

The Cypress and Fairway Houses
Nestled among cypress trees and facing portions of The Old Course and private homes, these two houses provide luxury guest rooms in Northern California on the top-floor as well as ground-floor rooms with fire pits. While the walk from these guest houses is scenic, we provide complimentary transportation to and from the main building upon request. Additionally, the guest house accommodations provide for complimentary self-parking included in your room rate.
