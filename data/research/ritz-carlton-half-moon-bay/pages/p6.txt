Two Championship Golf Courses
The Ocean Course
Surrounded by sweeping oceanfront views from nearly every hole, The Ocean Course features world-renowned fairways, native greenery, and breathtaking sunsets on the horizon. Golf architect Arthur Hills designed the course in a traditional “Links style,” with elevation changes and a challenging par-four fit for the pros.
Learn More
The Old Course
Since 1973, Arnold Palmer and Francis Duane’s classically designed Old Course features distinct edges and corridor views of the Pacific. Its parkland design leads to various waterway and dog-leg challenges to entice the most strategic of players. The finishing hole, one of the highest ranked in the country, sits along resort grounds with a picturesque backdrop of Scottish architecture and sweeping oceanside.
Learn More

Frequently Asked Questions
Are guests able to make online reservations to Half Moon Bay Golf Links?
Online reservations are not available at Half Moon Bay Golf Links. Please call us at +1 650-726-1800 to learn more about reserving a tee time.
Do kids golf for free at Half Moon Bay Golf Links?
Kids golf for free at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.
Does Half Moon Bay Golf Links have a clubhouse?
There is a clubhouse at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.Enjoy a bite to eat or a cool refreshment at our clubhouse restaurant.
What is par at Half Moon Bay Golf Links?
Half Moon Bay Golf Links is a par-72, 18-hole course, with a total length of 7003 yards. To learn more about the specifications of Half Moon Bay Golf Links, please call +1 650-726-1800.
Does Half Moon Bay Golf Links offer a driving range?
There is not a driving range at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.
Does Half Moon Bay Golf Links offer equipment rental?
Equipment rental is offered at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.
Does Half Moon Bay Golf Links offer golf school instruction?
Golf school instruction is offered at Half Moon Bay Golf Links. Work with experts to improve your golf game.
Does Half Moon Bay Golf Links provide lessons?
Lessons are offered at Half Moon Bay Golf Links. Golfers in search of tips or tweaks to elevate their golf game can feel encouraged to do so.
Is there a children’s golf program available at Half Moon Bay Golf Links?
There is not a children’s golf program available at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.
What forms of payment does Half Moon Bay Golf Links accept?
Yes, Half Moon Bay Golf Links offers the following forms of payment: Cash,Credit Cards
Are spikes allowed at Half Moon Bay Golf Links?
Spikes are not permitted at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.
Is there a putting green available at Half Moon Bay Golf Links?
A putting green is available for guests at Half Moon Bay Golf Links.
