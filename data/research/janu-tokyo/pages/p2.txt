# Janu Tokyo, Azabudai Hills

Set in the creative new neighbourhood, Azabudai Hills in Minato, Janu Tokyo stirs the soul with exceptional design,

culinary prowess across eight dining destinations and a state-of-the-art wellness centre.

Explore, connect and find joy.

## FEED THE SOUL

Scintillated by the novel and prepared to play with tradition, our eight dining and socialising destinations are devoted to the collective joy of dining with others.
