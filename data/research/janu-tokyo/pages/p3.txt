# JANU DUBAI

Primely placed within Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Janu Dubai is poised to become a defining landmark. Designed by Herzog & de Meuron, the property welcomes a new era of urban living, encompassing a 150-key hotel and a considered collection of just 57 private residences, each with unique layouts. Opening 2029.

Offering a vibrant mix of seven distinct, innovative dining and entertainment destinations, an iconic wellness and fitness space spanning 1,700 square meters, and the inaugural Janu Club, collectively, Janu Dubai forms an elevated ecosystem for modern living.

Janu Residences

## DESIGNED FOR LIVING

Home to a strictly limited number of Janu Residences, ranging from two to five bedrooms, each home, conceived for personalised living at scale, features generous layouts, floor-to-ceiling windows and private outdoor spaces. Residents will enjoy privileged access to the hotel’s dining, social and wellness amenities and experiences, as well as the dedicated service that defines Janu.

## A DYNAMIC LANDING PLACE

Designed by Pritzker Prize-winning architects Herzog & de Meuron, Janu Dubai’s distinctive sculpted facade brings a new feel to the city, weaving Janu’s contemporary design language with Arabic architectural influences. Set to become a defining landmark in DIFC, with uninterrupted views of the Burj Khalifa and surrounded by the lush greenery of Zabeel Palace Gardens, Janu Dubai sits at the nexus of commerce, culture and creativity.
