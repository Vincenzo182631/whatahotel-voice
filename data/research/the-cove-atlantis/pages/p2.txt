# A Luxurious Suite Collection

## Accommodations

Discover the sophisticated side of The Bahamas at The Cove, a deluxe hideaway on its own private beach. Relax in your luxurious hotel suite, enjoying sweeping ocean views and impeccable service. Enjoy exclusive use of an adults-only pool with private cabanas and fine dining in Nassau, Bahamas.

### Breathtaking Views Await

At The Cove, every spacious hotel suite offers sweeping ocean views in Nassau, Bahamas, along with contemporary furnishings, luxurious bedding, and modern amenities

## Lapis at The Cove

Situated on the 22nd floor, Lapis at The Cove is a concierge-level club exclusively for guests 12 and older staying in a Lapis Club Ocean Suite, Azure Suite, or Sapphire Suite at The Cove. This luxurious club provides breathtaking ocean views and ample room to gather or reconnect throughout the day. A dedicated concierge can assist with all your needs, from making dinner reservations to arranging golf, spa, and private cabana bookings.

Private Cabanas

## Private Luxury Cabanas

Experience the peak of beachfront luxury in your private cabana at The Cove Pool & Beach, exclusively for guests of The Cove. Each Bahamian-inspired cabana features the touch of interior designer Lulu De Kwiatkowski.

Do the rooms at The Cove at Atlantis have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Cove at Atlantis have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Cove at Atlantis?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at The Cove at Atlantis. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Cove at Atlantis?

Some rooms at The Cove at Atlantis permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.
