# Welcome to The Cove at Atlantis

## A Paradise State of Mind

Discover the sophisticated side of The Bahamas at The Cove, a deluxe hideaway nestled between two private beaches. Experience impeccable service, fine dining, and captivating experiences on breathtaking Paradise Island. Each luxurious suite at our hotel at Atlantis offers sweeping ocean views, a private balcony, elevated in-room amenities, and separate living and sleeping areas. Enjoy exclusive use...See More of The Cove Pool, an adults-only area with four multi-level pools, 20 private beachside cabanas with butler service, and outdoor gaming. Take advantage of full access to all the attractions and amenities of Atlantis, including dining from three Michelin-starred chefs, the 141-acre Aquaventure water park, and award-winning golf and spa offerings. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Cove at Atlantis?

The check-in time at The Cove at Atlantis is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Cove at Atlantis allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Cove at Atlantis.

What are the parking options at The Cove at Atlantis?

On-site valet parking is available at The Cove at Atlantis. The daily valet fee is $21.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Cove at Atlantis have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Cove at Atlantis has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Cove at Atlantis booking.

What is the closest airport near The Cove at Atlantis?

The closest airport near The Cove at Atlantis is Lynden Pindling International Airport (NAS). NAS is located approximately 19.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Cove at Atlantis have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Cove at Atlantis does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Cove at Atlantis have a Lapis at the Cove lounge?

Yes, The Cove at Atlantis has Lapis at the Cove lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Lapis at the Cove lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services.
