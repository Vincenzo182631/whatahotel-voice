# Dining at The Cove at Atlantis

## Dining

Discover elevated dining at The Cove. Whether you’re craving fresh seafood, international cuisine, or refreshing cocktails by the pool, The Cove has a variety of restaurants and eateries to explore in Atlantis Paradise Island, Bahamas.

Cashless Policy Notice.

Please be advised that our hotel does not accept cash payments. All transactions must be completed using credit cards, debit cards, or other approved electronic payment methods. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation to ensure a smooth and secure experience.

Celebrity Chef Dining

## World‑Class Flavors, Curated for The Cove

Discover a collection of refined, chef‑driven restaurants in Atlantis Paradise Island that feel perfectly at home in The Cove’s relaxed, modern luxury. Paranza by Michael White brings a fresh coastal take on Italian, with handmade pastas, pristine seafood, and elegant plates made for lingering evenings. Fish by José Andrés celebrates the sea with bright, inventive dishes that highlight the best of The Bahamas' waters.

Nobu by Nobu Matsuhisa delivers iconic Japanese flavors in a sleek, intimate setting, while Café Martinique offers French‑inspired, plant‑forward cuisine in a quietly glamorous marina‑side dining room.

### Sunset Sips and Vibrant Nightlife

Experience the ultimate nightlife at Atlantis, where expertly crafted cocktails await. Whether you prefer a classic martini or a tropical concoction, our skilled bartenders will mix your favorite drinks. Enjoy live music and entertainment that create a lively atmosphere, making every evening an unforgettable experience filled with fun and relaxation.

## Atlantis Casino

Light up the night – and day – with Lady Luck by your side in our world-class casino. This Las Vegas-style destination offers exhilarating thrills for both seasoned high rollers and first-time players.

### Marina Village at Atlantis: A Culinary Haven

Welcome to Marina Village, where luxury meets leisure in a vibrant waterfront setting. Explore over 20 designer shops and indulge in a diverse array of dining options. From family-friendly eateries like Bimini Road and Murray's Deli to gourmet experiences at Café Martinique, savor unique Bahamian flavors and international cuisine. Enjoy a delightful day of shopping and dining in this picturesque marketplace.

### Patio Dining

The white sand beaches, ocean views, and friendly environment make our outdoor dining restaurants an ideal option while on vacation in Atlantis Paradise Island. Enjoy a wide variety of offerings including burgers, sandwiches, local treats, and tropical cocktails, all while basking in the sun and sea breeze, creating unforgettable moments during your stay in the Bahamas.

### Breakfast at Atlantis

Whether you're craving a bakery treat from a cozy cafe or the grandeur of a lavish buffet spread, our diverse selection of restaurants on Paradise Island has something for everyone in Nassau, Bahamas. Wake up to the aroma of freshly brewed coffee and the mouth-watering scent of fresh baked pastries. Indulge in hearty breakfast classics or opt for lighter fare to fuel your day of adventure.
