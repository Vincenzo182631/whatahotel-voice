## Mandara Spa

Indulge in the ultimate spa experience at the award-winning Mandara Spa at Atlantis Paradise Island. This serene sanctuary artfully combines ancient Balinese healing techniques, traditional European therapies, and natural elements unique to The Bahamas.

Relax in our tranquil environment, enjoy a warm or cold plunge pool, steam room, and sauna, or treat yourself to a rejuvenating facial. Couples can enjoy special treatments in our exclusive spa suites, while the women's area offers a soothing aromatherapy room for added relaxation.

## Fitness Centre

Get your body moving at the Atlantis Fitness Center, newly renovated to meet all your wellness needs while you enjoy your Bahamas getaway. Our state-of-the-art facility features advanced cardiovascular and strength training equipment, free weights, and dedicated studios for spinning and yoga classes.

Enhance your workout with access to a refreshing lap pool and a wellness area designed for relaxation. Conveniently located adjacent to Mandara Spa, the Fitness Center offers a holistic approach to health and well-being.

## Ocean Club Golf Course

Experience golf in paradise at the championship Ocean Club Golf Course, designed by renowned architect Tom Weiskopf. This 18-hole, par-72 Paradise Island golf course stretches over 7,100 yards along picturesque coastline, offering breathtaking views of the Atlantic Ocean and Nassau Harbor.

With its masterful design, the course presents a perfect blend of challenge and beauty, where crosswinds test even the most skilled players. Enjoy every moment on the greens as you navigate through creatively designed holes that enhance your golfing experience.

### Marine Habitats

Discover Atlantis, the world’s largest open-air marine habitat. Explore lagoons, caves, and coral formations teeming with life. Wander through exhibits filled with vibrant fish, sharks, rays, barracuda, eels, and the critically endangered Smalltooth sawfish—over 250 species await beneath the surface.

### Aquaventure

Splash into 20 million gallons of fun from our hotel near Aquaventure, the biggest water park in The Bahamas. With 141 acres of thrilling slides and 14 sparkling swimming pools, every twist and turn offers something unique.

### Pools & Beaches

Make a splash with over 20 swimming areas, including 14 uniquely designed pools and six inviting beaches that stretch across five miles of pristine coastline. Relax on the soft white sands of Cove Beach, Royal Beach, Paradise Lagoon, or Atlantis Beach, all while enjoying the azure waters of Paradise. Each beach offers a unique atmosphere, perfect for sunbathing or beachside activities.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Cove at Atlantis have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Cove at Atlantis offers a 6:00 AM-8:00 PM fitness center available for a fee to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Strength training machines, treadmills, and a full gym, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Yes, The Cove at Atlantis has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Does The Cove at Atlantis have an on-site spa?

Does The Cove at Atlantis offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?
