### Incredible Occasions at InterContinental Lisbon

Life’s special moments deserve to be celebrated in unforgettable ways, and that’s exactly what we offer with our Incredible Occasions at InterContinental Lisbon. Whether you’re planning a milestone birthday, a romantic proposal, or a stylish dinner party (pajamas optional!), we’ll help you create memories that last a lifetime.

Learn more

### Valet Parking

Our hotel features an exclusive Valet Parking service, so you don't need to worry about parking the car and unloading your bags somewhere else.
Once you arrive, a member of our staff will be very pleased to welcome you and take care of all these arrangements.

### Coimbra Ballroom

The Coimbra Ballroom features one of the highest ceilings in a hotel in Lisbon (5,5m) and can hold events up to 450 persons. Being divisible into 2, the room offers multiple settings and a wide range of possibilities, turning it into one of our most versatile rooms.

Learn more

### Terraces overlooking Lisbon

Our rooms and suites with balconies are distinguished by their exclusive location on the hotel's upper floors. Featuring a private terrace, these rooms boast an incredible view over the city and the river, making your stay in Lisbon unforgettable.

## FAQs for InterContinental Lisbon

Does InterContinental Lisbon have a pool?

No, InterContinental Lisbon does not have a pool.

How much is parking at InterContinental Lisbon?

At InterContinental Lisbon, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 25 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 25 EUR .

Covered parking is available with 107 spaces at a daily cost of €25,00.
For extended parking and electric parking please contact the hotel.

Parking fee: 25 EUR per day

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Lisbon?

Pets are welcome at InterContinental Lisbon.

Pet policy description. The hotel is Pet Friendly, so pets are welcome at the hotel. To keep all our guests happy, including those not traveling with four legged friends, some restrictions apply. Ask us about our pet policies, guidelines and fees.

Pet fee per night: 40 EUR

Pet weight limit: 15

2 pets allowed

Pets allowed: All pets allowed

Pet policy

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Lisbon for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Lisbon have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Lisbon has 1 restaurant.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Lisbon?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Lisbon.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Lisbon?

Check-in at InterContinental Lisbon is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Lisbon offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Lisbon offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Lisbon from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Lisbon is conveniently located near Lisbon International Airport (LIS), approximately 4.6 miles away (7.4 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Lisbon?

InterContinental Lisbon is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Jeronimos Monastery-4.85 miles away (7.8 kilometers)
- Belem Tower-4.6 miles away (7.4 kilometers)
- St. George's Castle-2.24 miles away (3.6 kilometers)
- Calouste Gulbenkian Museum-1.37 miles away (2.2 kilometers)
- Discoveries Monument-4.04 miles away (6.5 kilometers)
- Oceanarium-6.52 miles away (10.5 kilometers)
- National Coach Museum-4.85 miles away (7.8 kilometers)
- Santa Justa Elevator-1.43 miles away (2.3 kilometers)
- Park and National Palace of Pena-17.21 miles away (27.7 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Lisbon puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
