### Good but not Great

9 days ago

We enjoyed our stay at the InterContinental Lisbon. The location was ideal for exploring the city, and the breakfast offered a great variety of options, including fresh pastries. Our room was clean, comfortable, and well maintained throughout our stay. The staff was excellent, consistently friendly, professional, and attentive.

There were, however, a couple of disappointments. I had specifically requested rooms away from the elevators several months in advance, yet we were assigned a room directly outside the elevator exit. It was disappointing that such a simple request, made well in advance, was not accommodated.

As an IHG Diamond Elite member, I also expected a higher level of recognition and attention to guest preferences. While the overall experience was positive, I was surprised that these details were overlooked, particularly at a property of this caliber.

Overall, we appreciated the excellent staff, convenient location, and quality of the accommodations. With greater attention to advance requests and loyalty recognition, the experience could have been even better.
