# InterContinental LisbonRooms & suites

Boasting natural light and modern décor with soft neutral tones, our tranquil rooms and suites overlook Eduardo VII Park, Lisbon’s historic old town, and Tagus River. Spacious, minimalist and modern, our guest rooms and suites offer a welcome respite after a busy day in Lisbon.
