At 112 rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré in the heart of the City of Light, Le Bristol Paris is a symbol of Parisian chic, a grand house created in elegant Parisian style, where guests immediately feel at home. Blending charm and luxury, Le Bristol was the first luxury hotel in France to receive the Palace accreditation.

## Spa & Wellness

From the moment you step into Spa Le Bristol by La Mer you relinquish the cares of the world and place yourself in the hands of experts trained in relaxation and beauty treatments. A stunning indoor rooftop swimming pool with views over Montmartre and the Eiffel Tower completes this remarkable wellness offering in Paris.
