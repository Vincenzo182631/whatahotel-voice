Take pleasure in the richness and history of this magnificent hotel situated between two lakes and overlooking Interlaken’s central green. Uniting two nineteenth-century hotels, the property also offers views of the Alps. Rooms are decorated in Belle Époque style; the Bel Air spa suites are loft-like and are done in light grays, natural woods, Asian design elements, with deep tubs that fill in 60 seconds. At the Sensai Select Spa, experience a three-hour Indulgence in Silk, a treatment that includes a body exfoliation with silk gloves, a full-body massage, and a double-moisturizing treatment. The service is the best of the best in Switzerland. La Pastateca’s menu concentrates on Italian pasta, meat, and fish. Find a table on the terrace, order lunch, admire the view, and people watch.
