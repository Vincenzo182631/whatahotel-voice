# Discover Interlaken

Close your eyes and think of turquoise mountain lakes and snow-capped hilltops. Breathe in the clear mountain air and breathe out the stress of everyday life. Interlaken lies between Lakes Thun and Brienz, at the foot of the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau mountain range. River rafting, bungee jumping, hiking, biking, and golfing in summer; snowboarding, snowshoeing, or tobogganing in winter – Interlaken is a year-round destination in the heart of Switzerland. Discover the Kleine Scheidegg, the Schilthorn with its James Bond Trail, or the Brienzer Rothorn and its steam rack railroad. With the new Jungfrau Railway, you can reach the legendary Jungfraujoch even faster. A rich cultural offer complements the wide range of sporting activities – Lucerne, Bern, or Zermatt are only a day trip away. Our concierge will give you tips or put together a tailor-made offer. Are you planning a visit to the Thuner Seespiele? The best tickets are guaranteed!
