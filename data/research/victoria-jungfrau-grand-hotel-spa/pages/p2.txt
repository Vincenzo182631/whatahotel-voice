# Spa Nescens

### Falstaff Spa Guide Award 2026 \| 99 Points Best Classic Spa-Hotel in Switzerland

In the heart of an exceptional natural setting, the Spa Nescens invites you to immerse yourself in 5,500 m² dedicated to holistic wellbeing. Its refined interiors, subtly inspired by a 1940s boudoir, contrast beautifully with the panoramic mountain views visible from the outdoor brine spa and the new Pool Garden.

Inside, a 20-meter pool, steam baths, serene relaxation rooms, a state-of-the-art gym, and generously appointed treatment rooms create the ideal environment to disconnect and recharge. The sauna area features a mixed Finnish sauna and steam bath, while the remaining facilities are thoughtfully separated for women and men.
