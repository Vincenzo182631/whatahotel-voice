# The aristocratic mansion in the center of Rome has reopened its doors

For over 170 years, the luxurious rooms and suites of the Hotel d’Inghilterra have been welcoming intellectuals and international celebrities just a few steps from Piazza di Spagna - personalities like Mark Twain, Henry James, Elizabeth Taylor and Gregory Peck all chose to experience our hospitality. An elegant 16th-century Roman residence turned into an iconic hotel, where the timeless charm of the palace combines with the refined elegance of the interiors, completely renovated with the deepest respect for the history of the place.

Located in the heart of the Roman fashion district between Via Condotti and Via Frattina, Hotel d'Inghilterra offers unforgettable views of the city. The **suites** will captivate you with their precious details, **Café Romano Restaurant & Lounge Bar** will delight your senses with a refined culinary offer reinterpreting local and Italian traditional delicacies, while the brand new **Terrazza Romana Rooftop Cocktail Bar** and **Spa Suite** will be your haven of relaxation and well-being in the heart of Rome—perfect for an elegant after-dinner moment or a massage inspired by traditional Eastern treatments.

Hotel d’Inghilterra ranks among the world’s finest, earning the Four-Star Award from **Forbes Travel Guide 2026**.

Craft Experiences

We are in the Eternal City, home to a civilisation that brought greatness, art and beauty to the world in the past - just like it does today. And so, from the ancient jewellery of the Roman-Byzantine school to the more modern elegance influenced by its famous hat, Roman workshops are an expression of a tradition that is still alive and continues to enchant its admirers.
