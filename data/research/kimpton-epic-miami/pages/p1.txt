### Pool + Cabanas

A location among the skyscrapers, two shimmering pools, chic daybeds for lounging, creative cocktails, and private cabanas make Kimpton EPIC's pool area the place to be on the waterfront in downtown Miami. It's always beautiful up here, but sunrise and sunset are particularly scene-stealing.

Learn more

### Weddings

Live the luxe life on your wedding day with a complimentary bridal hotel suite, custom wedding menus and pampering at the on-site exhale mind body spa. We can host 300 for a sit-down meal or 400 for a cocktail reception. Unique venues include the spectacular 16th-floor pool terrace and the elegant, open-style Metropolis Ballroom.

Learn more

### Area 31

The panoramic vistas from our 16th-floor perch offering waterfront dining Miami style may steal your heart, but hopefully not your appetite. Area 31's blends worldly influences with local inspiration to create cuisine that’s elegant yet approachable.

Learn more

### Privai Spa + Fitness

Privai Spa + Fitness provides members and guests with world-class experiences and beautifully formulated products for the mind, body and soul. With a full menu of innovative and restorative spa treatments and a state-of-the-art fitness center offering a variety of classes and programs, you will find just what you need to bring balance to your day.

Learn more

### Art in Residence

EPIC Art—a bespoke Artist in Residency Program—was created to advance the works of local artists & provide guests of Kimpton EPIC Hotel with an insider’s look into Miami’s vibrant art scene. The yearlong initiative brings a wide variety of disciplines & mediums to the guest experience from both established & emerging artists of diverse backgrounds.

Learn more

## FAQs for EPIC Hotel

Does Kimpton EPIC Hotel have a pool?

Kimpton EPIC Hotel has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pools

Two rooftop pools overlooking Biscayne Bay provide a relaxing waterfront experience with poolside seating, sun loungers, cabanas, and food and beverage service in a sophisticated Kimpton setting. Guests can enjoy swimming, lounging, and skyline views from the hotel's rooftop retreat.

Pool information

How much is parking at Kimpton EPIC Hotel?

At Kimpton EPIC Hotel, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 58 USD .

On-site valet parking is available for hotel guests and visitors. Overnight parking includes in-and-out privileges, and oversized vehicles can be accommodated for an additional fee. Parking is located at the hotel and provides convenient access to the Kimpton EPIC Hotel.

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Truck

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to Kimpton EPIC Hotel?

Pets are welcome at Kimpton EPIC Hotel.

Pet policy description. We invite you to bring your pet no matter their size, weight, or breed, all at no extra charge. If your pet fits through the door, we will welcome them in. Pets (and their humans) can enjoy nearby walks at Bayfront Park.

Pet fee per night: 0 USD

Pet weight limit: 300

Can I cancel my booking at Kimpton EPIC Hotel for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does Kimpton EPIC Hotel have a restaurant on site?

No, there is no on site restaurant at Kimpton EPIC Hotel.

Is there free breakfast available at Kimpton EPIC Hotel?

No, Kimpton EPIC Hotel does not offer complimentary breakfast.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at Kimpton EPIC Hotel?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at Kimpton EPIC Hotel.

What are the check-in and check-out times for Kimpton EPIC Hotel?

Check-in at Kimpton EPIC Hotel is from 4:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does Kimpton EPIC Hotel offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the Kimpton EPIC Hotel offers free WiFi!

How far is Kimpton EPIC Hotel from the nearest airport?

Kimpton EPIC Hotel is conveniently located near Miami International Airport (MIA), approximately 7.6 miles away (12.23 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
