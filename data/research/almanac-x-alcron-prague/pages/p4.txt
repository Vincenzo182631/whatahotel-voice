# Art & Culture at   Almanac X Alcron Prague

Immerse yourself _in a **living gallery** where_ Prague’s creative pulse _comes to life, through **bold installations,** **local collaborations,** and **ever-evolving expressions.**_

## Vibrant Gallery in the Heart of Prague

Almanac is not just another hotel, it is a treasure trove of unique experiences and a vibrant guide to the city. Our philosophy is to introduce our guests to the most interesting aspects of the Czech culture, art, design, and fashion scene. The original historical heritage sophisticatedly merges here with contemporary design and modern works by Czech artists. Discover with us places where architecture, taste, and art transform into an all-encompassing experience.

## Art at First Sight

At Almanac X Alcron Prague, your art experience begins the moment you arrive: right behind the reception, a monumental mural by Czech artist Lu Skřivánková captures everyone’s attention and infuses the space with an unmistakable energy. This powerful visual impression is harmoniously complemented by further artworks elegantly scattered throughout the entire lobby.

The contemporary concept design by the renowned Qubus studio also takes center stage. For instance, while relaxing in the lobby, you can admire the iconic Starquiola vase—an exclusive limited edition by designer Jakub Berdych Karpelis and a true masterpiece of modern craftsmanship.

## Private Gallery

Spend the night surrounded by world-class design and let art inspire you in absolute privacy. The art experience at Almanac X Alcron Prague by no means ends in the public areas, but accompanies you seamlessly right behind the door of your room. Every room and suite harbors unique original works that perfect the unmistakable character and genius loci of each space.

## Art in Motion  Ondrash & Kasparek

Our iconic staircase features rotating exhibitions. Currently, it comes to life through the work of the duo Ondrash & Kašpárek. Their large-scale pieces are created by transferring acrylic paint from the water’s surface onto canvas.

## Crystal Dream: Rückl in the Presidential Suite

At Almanac X Alcron Prague, our Presidential Suite is adorned with hand-cut crystal objects from the legendary Rückl glassworks. With our special stay package, you can combine luxurious accommodation with an exclusive visit to the manufactory and try the craft of glassmaking for yourself.

## Creative Signature at Almanac X Alcron  Jan Bican

At Elias Coffee Shop, we love supporting local talents. Do you remember our collaboration with the artist Jan Bican? He created the design for our Nordbeans coffee packaging and decorated our storefront window live. Jan, known for collaborations with brands like PUMA or Forbes, brought great artistic energy to Elias.

## Creative Signature at Almanac X Alcron  July Haluzová

The Moravian-born artist effortlessly combines everyday reality with the poetry of dreams and floral motifs in her work. Her visually rich paintings were part of the regularly rotating art exhibitions in our hotel premises.

## Creative Signature at Almanac X Alcron  Michael Kováčik

A leading fashion designer who brought a long-term dialogue between fashion and hotel design into our premises. As part of a special stay package, you can enjoy rooms exclusively equipped with his custom-designed accessories, complemented by a personal styling consultation and cocktail at the Alcron Bar.

## Creative Signature at Almanac X Alcron  Adam Jílek

A graduate of the Academy of Fine Arts in Prague with an unmistakable signature, who combines classical painting techniques with modern satire and humor. In his high-contrast paintings, he often depicts animals in unusual roles, pointing out human emotions and societal phenomena with a wink.
