Your Journey Into Wellbeing
Spa
Discover serenity at The Spa & Health Club at The Ritz-Carlton Jakarta, Pacific Place. Located on the hotel’s lush 8th floor garden, our sanctuary offers a range of beauty and relaxation treatments using organic, customized products. Unwind in any of our 8 treatment rooms or relax in the 2 tranquil relaxation rooms. Enhance your wellness with our amenities, including a Jacuzzi, Sauna, Steam rooms, a Whirlpool, and a fully equipped Fitness Center. Whether seeking revitalization or pure relaxation, The Spa provides an escape into peaceful rejuvenation.

Sunset Floating Sound Healing
Indulge in the Sunset Floating Sound Healing, a unique wellness experience that combines water therapy with harmonic sound vibrations. Guided by renowned sound healer, this immersive session promotes deep relaxation, stress reduction, and overall well-being. Guests float effortlessly in the serene pool, supported by flotation devices, as crystal bowls, singing bowls, drums, and chimes produce soothing sounds that resonate through the water, intensifying the calming effect. Held in a tranquil poolside environment with stunning views, the experience blends luxury and holistic wellness in a peaceful setting. Available every Wednesday and Friday from 6 to 7 PM with limited capacity, each one-hour session includes a personal consultation and is suitable for all ages and fitness levels. This innovative therapy offers a perfect mid-week escape for refreshing the mind, body, and spirit.
Find Your Bliss
Relaxation Room
Begin your spa experience in our tranquil Relaxation Room, where you can unwind and personalize your journey by completing our health and preference form. After your treatment, indulge in the authentic Jamu Ritual, as our ladies prepare refreshing herbal drinks that celebrate Indonesia’s rich wellness heritage. Let yourself relax, restore, and reconnect in this serene sanctuary.
Relaxing Nusantara Massage
Relaxing Nusantara Massage is bliss for tired, tight and sore muscles. Allow our intuitive therapist adapt their eclectic massage techniques to suit your needs whilst focusing on areas of stress and tension. Designed to increase flexibility, circulation and improve recovery time.
Facial and Body Enhancements
Indulge in radiant treatments designed to rejuvenate and nourish your skin. From personalized facials—brightening and anti-aging—to body enhancements like foot cleansing, detox body balm, and collagen masks, each therapy is crafted to reveal your natural glow. Using advanced techniques and premium products, our therapists help you achieve smoother, more luminous skin.
More Spa Packages: Desert Heat Body Energizing
In partnership with Pevonia Botanica, the Desert Heat Body Energizing treatment revitalizes both body and mind. This luxurious experience begins with a soothing aromatherapy footbath, followed by a rejuvenating massage for the body and face. It concludes with a unique self-heating body wrap infused with Copper, Magnesium, Calcium, and Zinc, offering deep relaxation, enhanced health, and overall well-being. This nourishing treatment leaves the skin hydrated, luminous, and irresistibly smooth. Perfect for melting away stress, it promotes holistic renewal and profound relaxation, leaving you restored and invigorated.

Frequently Asked Questions
Perhaps the best thing about a truly luxurious spa experience is that you learn to relax the pace of your thoughts and actions, and appreciate more of life. Slowing down helps you to really understand what it means to live in the present.
What are the available Spa Packages?
We have treatments ranging from 60 minutes to 150 minutes service, and include spa therapy, body scrubs and facials. For shorter treatments, a one hour massage consists of body massage, body scrubs and body mask.
What is the age of requirements?
You must be 17 years of age or older to enjoy the spa relaxation areas, locker areas and pool. Massages and facials cannot be performed on anyone under the age of 17 years.
