Cuisine Celebrating Local Flavors
Dining
At PA.SO.LA Restaurant, the menu is thoughtfully curated to celebrate the dedication of local farmers and food artisans. Open for all-day dining, the restaurant offers refined culinary experiences ranging from exquisite à la carte selections and lavish buffets to leisurely weekend brunches. Guests may also enjoy the exclusivity of a private dining room or the elegant lounge for afternoon tea in Jakarta, served with sophistication. Reflecting the cosmopolitan spirit of Jakarta, PA.SO.LA is complemented by sweeping views of the city skyline, creating an unforgettable dining ambiance.
Loka Rasa: A Taste of Indonesia
Travel across Indonesia through taste with Loka Rasa. Ten beloved dishes, reinterpreted by five chefs, are served across our restaurants from April 2026 to March 2027. Discover the archipelago’s rich flavors, one plate at a time.

Candlelight Dinner at The Gazebo
Celebrate love with an unforgettable Candlelight Dinner at The Gazebo on level 8. Indulge in an intimate evening for two with a thoughtfully curated set menu, complemented by exclusive access to a private gazebo beneath the night sky. This package includes welcoming beverages, signature Ritz-Carlton coffee and tea, dedicated private butler service, rose hand bouquet and elegant decorations.

Cigar Lounge
Located on level 6, our Cigar Lounge proudly showcases a curated collection of premium cigars from around the world. A seasoned cigar or newbie may join our weekly event every Wednesday evening and discover the art of cigar-rolling and whisky pairing. Engage in the art of conversation and unwind in the company of like-minded aficionados.
Discover More
Archipelago Cocktails
Our curated cocktail collection celebrates the rich flavors of Indonesia through thoughtfully selected local ingredients and artisanal craftsmanship. From aromatic spices and tropical fruits to rare botanicals sourced across the archipelago, each cocktail tells a story of place and heritage.
Discover More
The Mediterranean Brunch
Embark on an elevated culinary journey every Saturday and Sunday with the new Mediterranean Brunch. Savor a specially crafted menu that showcases the region's iconic flavors, from the perfectly seared Tomahawk Steak to the vibrant Paella loaded with fresh seafood and rich saffron, and the creamy Risotto that embodies the essence of Italian comfort food. Enjoy an expanded selection of pastries and desserts, as well as the signature pass-arounds and à la minute creations to delight your senses. Let us take your weekends to new heights of sophistication and flavor.
