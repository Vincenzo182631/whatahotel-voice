Suites & Rooms
With skyline views and five-star amenities, accommodations at The Ritz-Carlton Jakarta, Pacific Place embody modern luxury. Choose from the largest hotel guest rooms and hotel suites in Jakarta, which feature floor-to-ceiling windows and sunken marble baths, or one of the fully furnished apartments.
