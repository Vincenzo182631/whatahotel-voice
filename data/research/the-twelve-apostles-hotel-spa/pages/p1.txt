## Twelve Apostles Hotel & Spa

ex. 12 Apostles Hotel And Spa

The 5-star smoke-free Twelve Apostles Hotel & Spa Cape Town is situated just moments from the Twelve Apostles and boasts a sauna and a sundeck. Featuring an à la carte restaurant, this Cape Town hotel is nestled in a forested area, just 1.2 km from the Oudekraal Beach.

The hotel is near the beach district of Cape Town, in close proximity to Antipolis. Surrounded by a mountain and gardens, the Twelve Apostles Hotel & Spa Cape Town overlooks Twelve Apostles Mountain. Nature lovers will appreciate the proximity to Cozy Beach, which is nearly a 5-minute walk away. The area also includes natural sights like the landscaped World of Birds Aviaries 3.8 km from the accommodation. This Cape Town hotel is in proximity to Oudekraal bus stop.

The allergy-free rooms comprise an adjoining terrace and a balcony, and offer complimentary wireless internet and a flat-screen TV with satellite channels along with a mini-fridge-bar, tea and coffee making equipment. The bedrooms come with baby cots. Guests will find a hair dryer and bath sheets in the luxurious bathrooms fitted with a separate toilet and a shower/bath combination. The Twelve Apostles Hotel & Spa offers rooms with views of the patio.

Each morning a free breakfast is served. Grill restaurant specialises in international cuisine and features views of the ocean. Guests can also visit the lounge bar and enjoy nibbles and delicious afternoon tea. You can try contemporary cuisine in a seafood restaurant which is located very close by.
