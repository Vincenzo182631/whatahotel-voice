# Awards & accolades

Our guests are our most passionate ambassadors and we are so appreciative of the superb endorsements we receive daily through direct feedback, online reviews and travel awards. These awards are wonderful recognition and reward for our extraordinary teams and their unrivalled efforts to create exceptional guest experiences.

### Condé Nast Traveller Triple Crown

We are honoured to announce that The Twelve Apostles Hotel & Spa, Cape Town, has been named among the inaugural recipients of the Condé Nast Traveller Triple Crown - a new definitive collection of the best hotels in the world.

This distinction is truly unique, bringing together three of Condé Nast Traveller’s most respected awards in one: the annual Hot List, recognising the world’s most exciting new openings; the Gold List, curated by editors’ favourites; and the Readers’ Choice Awards, celebrating the hotels most loved by guests.

Discover more

### Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice Awards 2026

The 12 Apostles Hotel & Spa is honoured to have received the Tripadvisor Travellers' Choice Award 2026, a recognition based on the reviews and experiences shared by our valued guests.

### Readers' Choice Awards

In the 2026 Readers’ Choice Awards, The Twelve Apostles Hotel and Spa was voted the No. 8 Hotel in Eastern & Southern Africa by Condé Nast Traveller UK, and the No. 4 Hotel in South Africa by Condé Nast Traveler US.
