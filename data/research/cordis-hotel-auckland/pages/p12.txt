### SKYTOWER

- Victoria Street West, Auckland Central, Auckland 1010

Auckland’s skyline wouldn’t be the same without the Sky Tower. Standing at 328 meters, it’s the tallest structure in the Southern Hemisphere and the views? Simply breath-taking. Watch the city sprawl out below you, stretching all the way to the distant mountains. Feeling brave? Try the Sky Walk—where you’ll inch along the edge of the tower with nothing but air beneath you. Want to up the ante? Take the Sky Jump and experience the closest thing to free-falling off a skyscraper. Not for the faint-hearted!

### AUCKLAND ZOO

- Motions Road, Auckland 1022
- 09 360 3805

If you’ve ever dreamed of getting up close with some of the world’s most fascinating creatures, Auckland Zoo is the place to be. Set within 17 hectares of lush parkland, this wildlife haven is home to over 135 species from around the globe. Wander through the immersive South East Asia Jungle Track, watch giraffes graze on the African Savannah, or come face-to-face with New Zealand’s unique native birds. More than just a zoo, this is a conservation hub dedicated to protecting wildlife for future generations. Whether you're exploring solo or with family, every visit is a chance to connect with nature and experience the magic of the animal kingdom.

### PIHA BEACH

- 55 Marine Parade North, Piha 0772

Dramatic, rugged, and utterly breath-taking, Piha Beach is a must-visit for those who crave raw natural beauty. Its striking black sand, carved by ancient volcanic activity, sets the stage for towering cliffs and the legendary Lion Rock, a defining landmark of Auckland’s west coast. Whether you’re here to surf the world-class waves, explore the hidden waterfalls nearby nature lovers or anyone who wants to feel the power of the Tasman Sea, Piha is a place to be. At sunset, when the sky ignites in fiery hues over the wild waves, you’ll understand why this beach is one of Auckland’s most beloved gems. Piha is a photographer’s dream.

### AUCKLAND WAR MEMORIAL MUSEUM

- Auckland Domain, Parnell, Auckland 1010
- 09 309 0443

Standing proudly atop the Auckland Domain, The Auckland War Memorial Museum is far more than just a museum, it’s a tribute to the heart and soul of Aotearoa. Step inside to uncover the rich history of New Zealand, from Maori and Pacific heritage to wartime sacrifices that shaped the nation. Marvel at the intricate carvings in the Maori galleries, stand before an awe-inspiring life-sized war canoe and reflect at the powerful war memorial. It’s a place of discovery, remembrance and deep cultural connection.

### AUCKLAND DOMAIN & WINTERGARDENS

- Wintergarden Road, Parnell, Auckland 1010

Auckland Domain is the city’s oldest park and it’s the perfect place to unwind and connect with nature. Stroll through the vast green spaces, take in the stunning views and don’t miss the breath-taking Winter Gardens. These elegant glasshouses are filled with exotic plants, vibrant flowers and tranquil ponds. Whether you’re admiring the Victorian-style architecture or enjoying a peaceful moment by the fernery, it’s a serene oasis in the heart of the city. If you love history, be sure to visit the Auckland War Memorial Museum, which sits proudly within the Domain and offers fascinating insights into New Zealand’s past.

### ONE TREE HILL & CORNWALL PARK

- Green Lane West, Epsom, Auckland

One of Auckland’s most culturally significant landmarks, One Tree Hill is rich in history and offers stunning panoramic views of the city. This ancient volcanic cone was once home to a large Māori settlement. Today, it’s a peaceful retreat where you can take a scenic walk through Cornwall Park, visit the obelisk at the summit and soak in the deep sense of history surrounding you.

### KAREKARE BEACH

- 2 Karekare Road, Huia, Karekare 0772

Karekare Beach is one of Auckland’s hidden gems. Less crowded than Piha, this black-sand beach is pure, untouched beauty. The towering cliffs, crashing waves and dramatic landscapes make it a favourite spot for photographers and nature lovers. If you recognize it, you might have seen it in the movie “The Piano”. A short walk from the beach, Karekare Falls offers a tranquil escape surrounded by lush greenery. If you want a beach with breath-taking scenery and fewer crowds, this is the place.
