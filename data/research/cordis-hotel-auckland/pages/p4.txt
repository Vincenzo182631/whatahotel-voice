TAKING CORDIS, AUCKLAND TO NEW Heights

Inspired by a visionary blend of contemporary design and the Maori concepts of Te Whai Ao (light), Manawa (heart) and Kaitiakitanga (guardianship). The Pinnacle Tower rises 17-storeys high, with a sleek exterior that looks out to the city skyline and harbour, offering breathtaking vista of Auckland and Rangitoto Island from its upper levels. Inside, you’ll find sophisticated modern spaces, custom made furnishings, privately curated artworks, and an extraordinary Club Lounge and VIP Suite.

Cordis, Auckland looks forward to welcoming you.
