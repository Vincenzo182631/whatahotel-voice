## A Towering, Elegant Tribute to the Spirit of Java

Presiding over Solo, Alila Solo features a soaring lobby ceiling, batik-inspired art and the highest rooftop lounge in Central Java, offering an urban getaway in the royal capital. See how Solo’s sophisticated heritage blends with modern development—including shopping, dining and entertainment—as you set out to explore this enchanting city. When you return to your sanctuary in the sky, enjoy the birds-eye view of the city from one of Alila Solo’s eight towers set at staggered heights.

## Accommodations

Our 255 well-appointed rooms and suites are spread across eight towers of staggered heights, so each offers a phenomenal bird’s-eye view of the city. The Alila Suite is particularly luxurious, as it includes a private terrace and plunge pool.

Harvest Bliss —A Journey Through Java’s Living Land

## Discover Alila Solo

Court Rituals and Culture

Solo’s court society is regarded as the cradle of Javanese culture, with its rich traditions in dance, music and art. In a nod to centuries-old traditions, royal festivals are still held with great pomp and majesty, and witnessing one is unforgettable. Explore classical Javanese dance with its intricate hand movements and the beauty and history of Solo’s traditional dyed batiks. About two hours from Solo, the 9th-century Borobudur temple is the world’s largest Buddhist monument.

Caring for Our Community

Alila Solo is committed to social and cultural responsibility and follows EarthCheck standards. The hotel incorporates considered design features, including the use of local and recycled materials, as well as water-management measures such as soakaways and rain gardens.Beyond the hotel, Alila Solo supports the Permata Hati Surakarta Child and Infant Care Foundation (YPAB), which provides care for abandoned children in Solo.

Discover a Deeper Sense of Place

Enjoy awe-inspiring landscapes, volcanic peaks, and ancient temples during your stay at Alila Solo, where we support experiences that celebrate local traditions, preserve cultural heritage, and connect guests with local communities.

Catering

A Modern Sanctuary

Innovative, avant-garde design meets traditional accents for a uniquely Javanese aesthetic. The monumental, high-ceilinged lobby features batik-inspired art that sets the tone for a distinctive stay, blending contemporary sophistication with local heritage.

Alluring Contrasts

Solo combines the charm of a small city with the ambitions of a metropolis. The older of the two royal capitals of Central Java, Surakarta—or Solo, as it is better known— preserves its rich history in elegant walled royal palaces. Today, the city is one of the fastest developing economic centers of Indonesia, with Alila Solo rising above a main avenue known for vibrant dining and retail. Discover a dynamic blend of heritage and modernity alongside an astounding landscape of rice fields and volcanic peaks.
