### Harvest Bliss —A Journey Through Java’s Living Land

Escape the pace of city life and find your rhythm among the golden rice paddies and waters of Delanggu. Begin your journey at Alila Solo with a warm welcome before traveling to Klaten, where local farmers and fishers guide you through the art of rice cultivation—from planting to harvesting—and catching fish by hand in time-honored traditions. Feel the earth beneath your feet, the splash of water, the joy of honest labor. Afterward, unwind with Javanese tea and snacks, taking in the pastoral beauty. Optional spa treatments round out this immersive journey from field to soul.

**A Warm Departure**

Begin your day at Alila Solo with a traditional oshibori—a warm, scented hand towel offered as a gesture of welcome and purification—alongside a short introduction before your journey to the countryside begins.

**Into the Paddies**

Arrive in Klaten and join local farmers to learn the rice lifecycle. Plant, tend and harvest alongside them, feet in the mud and heart in the moment.

**Catch the Moment**

Try your hand at bare-handed fish catching—a playful, age-old village ritual that connects you to both nature and tradition.

**Rest and Refresh**

Pause for curated Javanese tea and snacks while soaking in the tranquil rural surroundings.

**Optional Spa Add-On**

Ease tired muscles with a soothing spa treatment—an ideal ending to a day grounded in nature and renewal.

**Duration:** 4 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Modest attire is appreciated. Lightweight shorts or pants. T-shirts or other comfortable tops. Comfortable footwear. Sturdy walking shoes or trekking sandals are ideal. Avoid high heels.

**Additional Details:** Be prepared for potential mud or dirt. Curated lunch included. After the activities, relax and enjoy a specially curated lunch and traditional snacks at the site.

### To Market, With Morning Light—A Culinary and Cultural Stroll

Start your morning with a captivating stroll through Pasar Gede, Solo’s most iconic market—a vibrant blend of colonial architecture and local energy. As you wander through its bustling aisles, engaging with friendly vendors, you’ll gain a deeper appreciation for the cultural tapestry that shapes daily life here. Step into the calm of the nearby Chinese temple, where ornate carvings and quiet reverence offer a moment of reflection. Then head upstairs to discover an array of traditional delicacies. Enjoy your breakfast with a bird’s-eye view of the lively market below, taking in the flavors, sights and spirit of Solo in one unforgettable morning.

**A Sunrise Arrival**

Depart Alila Solo for an early morning visit to Pasar Gede, Solo’s most iconic market and a masterpiece of Dutch-Chinese architecture.

**Market Immersion**

Stroll through colorful stalls with your host, sampling local treats and interacting with vendors from a diverse range of cultural backgrounds.

**Temple Pause**

Step into Klenteng Temple, a serene and storied Chinese place of worship nestled beside the market. Take in its peaceful atmosphere and intricate design.

**Elevated Breakfast**

Enjoy a curated Javanese breakfast in a quiet space overlooking the market—an unforgettable pairing of flavor and perspective.

**Duration:** 3 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Modest attire is appreciated. Lightweight shorts or pants. T-shirts or other comfortable tops. Comfortable footwear. Sturdy walking shoes or trekking sandals are ideal. Avoid high heels.

**Additional Details:** Be ready to immerse yourself in the sights, sounds and smells of the market, engage in lively conversations and interact with friendly merchants.

### Threads & Blades—A Journey Through Javanese Artistry

Experience the living heritage of Java through its two most iconic art forms: batik and keris. Begin at the historic home of Go Tik Swan—a cultural luminary who preserved and elevated the art of batik for future generations. See rare patterns, hear stories of royal commissions and try your hand at the craft. Then, journey to the studio of a master keris maker to witness the forging of a traditional Javanese dagger. Every curve, every fold of steel carries symbolic power, believed to channel protection, wisdom, or strength. This quietly powerful experience offers a deeper understanding of craftsmanship, patience and tradition—connecting you to the quiet beauty of Java’s artistic legacy.

**A Noble Welcome**

Begin at the house of Go Tik Swan, cultural advisor and batik artist to Indonesian royalty. Learn about his life, legacy and lasting impact.

**The Language of Cloth**

View rare and historic batik patterns, then enjoy a hands-on demonstration to experience the meditative rhythm of wax and dye.

**Lunch & Local Flavor**

Pause for a simple Javanese lunch, reflecting on how food, cloth and culture are interwoven in daily life.

**Forged in Spirit**

Visit a traditional keris studio where a master smith demonstrates the ancient process of shaping folded steel into daggers believed to carry spiritual power.

**Art to Carry Forward**

End with a reflection on the values behind each craft—patience, heritage, protection—and how they continue to shape Java’s living identity.

**Duration:** 4 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Modest attire is appreciated. Lightweight shorts or trousers and comfortable footwear recommended.

**Additional Details:** Reservation required to secure the traditional lunch availability.

**Price:** IDR 525.000++ per person \| IDR 700.000++ per couple
