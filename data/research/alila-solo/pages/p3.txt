## Sky Lounge

Elevate your stay with exclusive access to the Sky Lounge on Level 30, available exclusively to guests staying in our Deluxe Rooms and Suites. Begin the day with a leisurely breakfast, savour afternoon tea above the city, and unwind during evening beverage hour as the sun sets over Solo's skyline. Perched at the highest point of Alila Solo, the Sky Lounge offers a refined sanctuary in the sky, where attentive service, elevated culinary moments, and panoramic views come together in an atmosphere of understated sophistication.

Open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
