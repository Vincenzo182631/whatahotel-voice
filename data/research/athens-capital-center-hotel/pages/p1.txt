# An artful Urban Break

# to feel the city vibes

## Athens Capital Hotel the first MGallery Collection Hotel in Greece, brings out the heart and soul of this cosmopolitan and vibrant city. Well placed at Syntagma square, opposite to the Parliament House, the hotel recreates an effortless blend of art and business. Its refined elegance, bespoke art, finely tuned accommodations sculpt a unique bond among visitors with this timeless city. Athens Capital Hotel - MGallery is the perfect starting point from which to discover Athens, a unique European metropolis which embodies an indulgent combination of ancient character and contemporary lifestyle through sightseeing, high-end shopping and art in all its forms. From the Acropolis of Athens to the Odeon of Herodes, a remarkable landmark awaits behind every turn. We commit to forging unforgettable memories for our guests by leading them on an evocative journey through the city's hidden gems.

# Artful

# storytelling

## Storytelling is an important part of every stay at an MGallery Collection hotel; this is why each one acquaints its guests with its own special Iconic Object, an item of great significance to the city, and Memorable Moment, an experience which acquaints guests with the distinctive character of the area. Athens Capital Hotel has its own unique Iconic Object and Memorable Moment, which reflect the history and traditions of Athens and play their parts in the story of this new beacon of Athenian hospitality. The hotel's Iconic Object is the "Akrokeramon", a decorative element and trademark of the architecture of Neoclassical Athens which symbolizes the timeless value of hospitality. Art is also an intrinsic part of Athens Capital Hotel - MGallery Hotel Collection. It is led by Mappemonde, an emblematic life’s work by notable Greek sculptor Georgios Lappas, through an impressive installation that begins with its central component on the hotel’s tenth floor, with about 3,000 parts that comprise it running through all its floors within the hotel’s atrium.

# Committed to Her ​

## At our luxury Athens hotel, female empowerment inspires both art and experience – from a striking sculpture of goddess Dimitra to refined suites celebrating Greek women who shaped history.​

### Women in Healthcare

We proudly support Women in Oncology, championing female leadership in medicine. With annual Pink October initiatives and year-round advocacy, we help advance women’s roles in healthcare leadership.

### Pioneering Spirits

Eleni Skoura was the first woman elected to Greek Parliament. Her election marked a major step for gender equality in Greece, paving the way for future generations of women in politics.

### Our commitment to Pink October

Each year, we join hearts and voices for Pink October, creating moments of awareness and solidarity with our NGO, to support prevention and research against breast cancer.

# Giving Back to Communities ​

## Near historic Athens sites, we’re building a brighter future. Through partnerships with Women in Oncology, Hope Genesis healthcare and Boroume food distribution, we’re strengthening our community one initiative at a time.​

### Heritage and Hop

As guardians of one of Athens’ historical sites, we meticulously maintain original features while supporting initiatives like Hope Genesis, bringing new life to both our building and our community.

### A Taste of Authentic Athens

From mountain honey by the Klitsidis family to Olympos’ eco-conscious feta, we celebrate Greece’s finest artisans. Our sustainably certified coffee, tea and seafood honour both tradition and environmental responsibility.

### Making a Difference Together

Through partnerships with Women in Oncology and Boroume food distribution, we’re helping create a stronger Athens. Every stay with us contributes to these vital community initiatives.
