# Welcome to Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum

## Discover a serene experience at our Bodrum resort and spa

Nestled in the heart of Bodrum within a private bay, Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa Bodrum, has 84 rooms. Our Bodrum resort includes 67 rooms, 9 suites, 6 One-Bedroom Residences, 1 Three-Bedroom...See More Residence, and a Caresse King Villa overlooking the Aegean Sea. All rooms are equipped with excellent amenities, modern technology, and Marriott's Signature Luxury Collection Beds. Every guest at Caresse Bodrum can enjoy the captivating view of the Aegean Sea, as each room features its own private terrace or balcony. The resort's luxury is evident in its chic furnishings and modern bathrooms, embodying lavish comfort. The special amenities at this Bodrum hotel in Turkey allow for relaxation and unique tranquility.See Less

A Luxury Escape for Every Season

Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, welcomes guests in every season. Enjoy our wellness and longevity facilities year-round, including spa rituals, longevity experiences, and personalized wellbeing programs.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum?

The check-in time at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 8.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €125.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum?

On-site parking is available at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum booking.

What is the closest airport near Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum?

The closest airport near Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum is Milas-Bodrum Airport (BJV). BJV is located approximately 41.0 KM from the hotel.

Does Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
