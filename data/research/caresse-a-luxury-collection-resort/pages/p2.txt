### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum?

The largest occupancy room at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum is the Premiere Pool Residence with a maximum occupancy of 10.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum?

Some rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have fridges?

No, rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have coffee/tea makers?

No, rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum do not have a coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have in-room whirlpools?
