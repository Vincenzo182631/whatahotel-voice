### Spa Caresse Highlights

### Treatments

The Longevity Spa Bodrum combines advanced science with ancient wisdom, offering signature rituals inspired by the world’s Blue Zones. Each treatment is tailored to restore vitality, balance, and inner calm whether through cryotherapy, LED therapy, natural body rituals, or bespoke facial protocols. Every session is crafted to guide you toward the best version of yourself.

### Products

Rooted in longevity and conscious beauty, our product philosophy focuses on natural, high-performance solutions that enhance skin health and radiance. Formulated to support rejuvenation and long-term wellbeing, these products reflect a commitment to both science and nature.

### Children's Policy

The Longevity Spa Bodrum is a serene sanctuary dedicated to relaxation and renewal. For this reason, access to changing areas, heat experiences, indoor pool, and fitness facilities is reserved for guests over 16 years of age. Children under 16 may enjoy the indoor pool daily from 10 am to 2 pm when accompanied by a parent.

## Discover Kids Academy at Caresse Bodrum

Caresse Bodrum presents its Kids Academy, where children can enjoy a diverse array of sports, games, creative activities, and fitness sessions guided by expert trainers from Powerium Bodrum. The academy is designed to nurture both enjoyment and skill development, offering a range of enriching experiences for young guests.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Caresse, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Bodrum offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Free Weights, and Reformer pilates facilities with personal trainer, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
