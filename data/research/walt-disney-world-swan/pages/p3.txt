# 23 Award-Winning Restaurants and Lounges

## Dining

Celebrity dining venues, award-winning chefs, extensive wine lists, unique cocktails, eye-catching restaurant designs and unmatched service are just a few of the elements that make the Walt Disney World...See More Swan and Dolphin a recognized leader in the resort dining experience. Featuring 23 restaurants and lounges, the resort offers something for everyone, from the most passionate foodie, to those seeking a quick, yet satisfying meal. Every palate can be satisfied whether served in richly appointed settings or outdoor and casual poolside. The resort’s extensive beverage program employs more than 70 sommeliers and rivals those in fine dining venues globally. The wine program features more than 1,400 selections, including organic and rare vintages, plus tastes from Greece, Turkey, Israel and South Africa.See Less

17th Annual Food & Wine Classic: Nov 20–21, 2026

Experience the resort’s exciting annual Food & Wine Classic featuring gourmet cuisine from award-winning chefs paired with savory spirits, plus live entertainment and more.

Specialty Dining

### Bourbon Steak

Bourbon Steak celebrates the details that make meals memorable, from perfect sears and tableside old-fashioneds to shared sides and milestone moments. Chef Michael Mina brings his fresh perspective to familiar favorites, balancing the joy of the classics with the thrill of the unexpected. With unique flavors, inventive cocktails, and local inspiration, Bourbon Steak brings new delight to time honored rituals.

Private Dining

### We’ll Help You Plan the Perfect Dining Event

When you’re looking for the perfect venue to host your next dinner party- look no further! At the Walt Disney World Swan and Dolphin Resorts, there are several options for private dining in our award-winning restaurants. Learn more about our favorite themed restaurants that promise the highest quality food prepared by renowned chefs from around the world. Whether you’re planning an important dinner meeting, an elegant dinner for the wedding party or a fun family reunion, we’ll help you plan the perfect dining event that your guests will appreciate and remember.

CASUAL DINING

Garden Grove (Currently on Fall Break)

Please note: Garden Grove is open seasonally and we are currently on fall break, however, Garden Grove will be open for our annual Thanksgiving Buffet! We serve a delightful selection of American breakfast favorites, including our popular Mickey Waffles. Designed with families in mind, Garden Grove offers a warm and inviting atmosphere for a perfect start to your day at the resort.

View Menu

Cabana Bar and Beach Club

Kick back poolside for a flatbread, fish tacos or grass-fed organic burger. Wispy white drapes, exotic woods and glass floor tiles surround you with South-Beach-chic ambiance. Located just steps from the Dolphin lap pool, the outdoor Cabana Bar and Beach Club encourages everyone to “come as they are” for savory treats including a sophisticated variety of appetizers, salads, and entrees.

View Menu

Splash Pool Bar and Grill

Just steps from our white sand beach, this playful poolside café is designed for guests who don’t want to leave the Florida sun for even a minute. The menu serves up specialty salads, sandwiches, and other snacks as well as a variety of refreshing libations. Beer and wine are also available.

View Menu

The Fountain

This ice cream fountain and snack shop is the ideal stop for crisp salads, custom-grilled burgers and a delectable array of hearty sandwiches and sweet treats. It’s hard to resist the hand-dipped and soft-serve ice cream, malts, and shakes. Open for lunch and dinner.

View Menu

Tangerine

Guests can enjoy signature drinks poolside along with casual fare including flatbreads, salads, burgers and sandwiches, and stay cool with the restaurant’s signature popsicle – tangerine flavored, of course. Open for lunch and dinner.

View Menu

Quick Service

### Fuel

On the go? Looking to stock up for the theme parks? Stop by fuel to recharge, refresh, and fill-up on coffee, ready-made sandwiches and snacks. fuel offers guests a convenient way to energize during the day, whether it’s filled with business meetings or exploring the theme parks. Our baristas can create your favorite caffeinated concoctions to carry you through the day.

### Lagoon Games, Lanes & Eats

Take a break and savor an array of delectable dishes and refreshing drinks at Lagoon Games, Lanes & Eats - with a menu designed to satisfy every palate, you can fuel your gaming experience with everything from gourmet snacks to mouthwatering meals.

### Chill

Guests can cool down and relax with a refreshing selection of beer, wine, and unique frozen libations. Some of the offerings include a Cold Fashioned, Mango Margarita and a Paloma, which contains a blend of Herradura Silver Tequila and fresh grapefruit juice. Family friendly options are available as well as sodas, snacks, chips, and cookies. Open weekends.

### Java

Perfect for starting your day. Enjoy coffee along with sweet and savory breakfast options as well as sandwiches, wraps, salads and desserts throughout the day at this convenient grab-and-go. The venue also serves a variety of beer, wines, juices, teas, espressos, frappés and a special Nitro Brew, cold brewed with liquid nitrogen. Open daily for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

### Grounds

Grounds features craft coffee, sandwiches, pastries and more, providing a great way to fuel quickly for a day of meetings or visiting the theme parks. Open daily for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

### Pies Pizza

Savor freshly made Pies Pizza after a day at the parks or resort. Enjoy a crave-worthy slice with convenient ordering across the resort: dine-in at Lagoon Games, Lanes and Eats via kiosk with table delivery; Lobby Bar via QR code at Phins for delivery; or in-room dining via Quick Service on website/app delivered to your room.

Lounges

Bourbon Steak Lounge

Step into refined comfort at the Bourbon Steak Lounge, where handcrafted cocktails, fine wines, and rare spirits invite you to linger a little longer.

Il Mulino Lounge

Relax and unwind amidst hip, comfortable surroundings with great classic cocktails and wines from Italy and beyond. Light menu offered. Open evenings.

Kimonos Lounge

The perfect mixture of fun and flavor. Enjoy a wide variety of Japanese sake or one of our unique cocktail creations. Open evenings.

Phins Lounge

Phins is a contemporary lounge centrally located in the Dolphin lobby. Guests can enjoy light bites, sipping on custom-crafted cocktails, a glass of vintage wine, or even a frosty mug of our very own Phins and Feathers beer, all under the sophisticated ambiance of a spectacular custom-made crystal chandelier. Open afternoons and evenings.

Todd English's Bluezoo Lounge

Toast the good life with handmade classic cocktails, fine champagne, or specialty cordials in a chic underwater setting around a blue, backlit bar. Those seeking a unique taste can find the latest creations from the resort’s expert mixologists, such as the Bazooka Joe Vodka, the flaming Burnt Orange as well as the venue’s signature cocktail, the Zooberry. Light menu offered. Open evenings.

Stir

Stir offers an airy, tropical feel in the lobby and serves specialty cocktails and light bites. Try Chef’s selection of handcrafted salamis of Spain & Italy and a Soppressata – red onion, torn mozzarella, San Marzano flatbread. Open afternoon and evenings.
