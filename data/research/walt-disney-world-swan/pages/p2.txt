# The Perfect Retreat for Rest and Relaxation

## Accommodations

All of our guest rooms are filled with amenities designed to make your stay at the Walt Disney World Swan one that you’ll remember for years to come.

### Our Suites

Enjoy the benefit of additional space with one of our many suites. Choose from our Junior Suites that can easily accommodate the needs of a business traveler or a family of four, to one of our ten luxurious Presidential Suites. Amenities range from comfortable conveniences to the latest in-room technology and flexible bedding options, to kitchens featuring full appliances, colors and surfaces found in upscale, private homes. These exclusive suites are only available to book by calling 800-227-1500 or 407-934-4000.

### Room With a View

Treat yourself to a better perspective from the comfort of a room overlooking the resort’s interior garden, the natural wooded landscape with partial views of the Boardwalk Resort area, or the Tower of Terror at Disney’s Hollywood Studios. Rooms with views of Crescent Lake are also available and boast breathtaking vistas of the scenic lake.

### Upgrade Your Stay

When you travel with family or are on a business trip you may opt to enjoy some extra space in an upgraded room. Our suites can easily accommodate the needs of a business traveler or a family of four. These larger rooms are the perfect solution to relax in after a full day of Disney fun, or when you need more space for work.

## In-Room Mobile Dining / Mobile Order Pickup

Place an order for breakfast, lunch, or dinner and it will be delivered to your guest room by one of our Mobile Dining attendants. Or are you looking for a quick snack to bring to the room at one of our quick-service restaurants? Why wait in line? Place your order ahead of time and then visit the outlet to pick it up.

### Stay Connected

Now is the perfect time for you and your family to stay with us at the Walt Disney World Swan and Dolphin hotels. Book any rate plan, then call us at 800-227-1500 to reserve a second guest room for up to 25% off regular rates. Be sure to ask for “Stay Connected” when you call! (Subject to availability at time of booking).
