## Mandara Spa

Discover the beauty of our renewed Mandara Spa, an ethos encompassing well-being from around the world. A 10,500-square-foot sanctuary for the senses, Mandara captures the essence of our Balinese heritage while charting a path to the New World – a journey that touches on regional influences infused into unparalleled Mandara experiences. Explore the interior gardens: our intimate Tea Garden, a unique sitting and sipping room; or the Meditation Garden, which features our signature “Meru Temple.” These exotic retreats provide a destination where mind, body and soul meet. Spa and salon services are as luxurious as therapeutic and as relaxing as results oriented. Transcend. Transform. Trust the Moment. Mandara Spa features world-renowned, premium product brands, including Elemis, bliss, Mandara Spa, Steiner Haircare, Phyto Haircare, Wella, OPI Nails, CND Nails, and La Bella Donna. The Mandara Spa at the Walt Disney World Swan and Dolphin offers an array of healing treatments.

## Three State of the Art Health Clubs

Those who want to stay fit while on the road will be glad, they chose to stay at this Disney Resort. The Walt Disney World Swan and Dolphin offers three, well-equipped health clubs to satisfy even the most demanding fitness enthusiast. The health clubs are offered to ensure your fitness routines are matched with world class equipment to meet your goals. You can take advantage of a wide array to technologically advanced equipment, free weights, cardiovascular equipment, elliptical machines and state of the art exercise bikes. Lockers, water, towels, and a popular jogging trail are available. Enjoy TV’s or beautiful resort views as you take care of your mind and body. With your resort room key, the health clubs are open 24 hours and staffed accordingly during peak hours daily. Join us for our daily instructor led fitness classes. A different class is offered each day, so please check the latest schedule.

Fitness Classes

- **Aqua HIIT!** \- This class offers a series of short fitness drills in the water at high intensity intervals to provide participants with a challenging and intense pool workout that offers results!

- **Zumba**\- This class is a Latin-dance-inspired aerobic fitness format! No dance experience is required, your instructor will show you all the moves and you will learn as you go! With dances like Salsa, Merengue, Bachata, and Cumbia- Zumba feels like more of a party than a workout. Sneakers recommended.

- **Cardio Splash**\- Bring your workout to the pool! Cardio-Splash uses water resistance to improve cardiovascular fitness, endurance, and flexibility with a high-energy pool workout that is fun for all fitness levels.

- **Beach Yogalates** \- This Class combines strength and flexibility movements from both Yoga and Pilates for a Unique class dynamic. Yogalates classes focus on increasing flexibility, strength, and the mind/body connection.

- **Beach Yoga**\- In this class, our Yoga instructor will guide you through a series of postures practiced to with a focus on alignment to help increase flexibility and strengthen the body. Class incorporates some breathing techniques and meditation to help quiet the mind and develop a deeper mind-body connection.

- **Aqua Aerobics**\- Aqua Aerobics uses a combination of moves that strengthen, tone, and help increase aerobic capacity with all low-impact pool workout. Class is low-impact and great for all levels!

Yes. Walt Disney World Swan offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
