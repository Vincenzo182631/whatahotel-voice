### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Walt Disney World Swan?

The check-in time at Walt Disney World Swan is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does Walt Disney World Swan allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Walt Disney World Swan.

What are the parking options at Walt Disney World Swan?

On-site parking is available at Walt Disney World Swan. Self parking is available at Walt Disney World Swan, with rates of $40.00 per hour, $40.00 per day, and $280.00 per week. Valet parking is available for $61.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Walt Disney World Swan have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Walt Disney World Swan has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Walt Disney World Swan booking.

What is the closest airport near Walt Disney World Swan?

The closest airport near Walt Disney World Swan is Orlando International Airport (MCO). MCO is located approximately 19.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does Walt Disney World Swan have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Walt Disney World Swan does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
