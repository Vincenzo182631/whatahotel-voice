Make your Disney memories last longer with extended park hours. Begin your day with 30-minute Early Theme Park Entry at all four Walt Disney World® parks, available daily. On select nights, experience Extended Evening Hours, where shorter lines give you more time for your favorite attractions.

Unlock more possibilities with first access to Lightning Lane Pass purchases 7 days before your stay. Ride when you want with no arrival windows to schedule, plus digital downloads of select attraction photos and videos.
