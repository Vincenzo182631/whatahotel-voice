## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Hamilton Princess & Beach Club?

Check-in time is from 4:00 pm and check-out time is by 11:00 am.

Is there a concierge service at Hamilton Princess & Beach Club?

Yes, the Concierge desk is located adjacent to our Main Hotel Reception. They will assist you with directions, event tickets, and dinner reservations.

Does Hamilton Princess & Beach Club have a spa?

Yes, our hotel offers a luxury spa. Driftwood overlooks Hamilton’s beautiful harbor. Our 8,000-square-foot spa and wellness facility is a peaceful oasis devoted to relaxation and vibrant health in the heart of Bermuda.

Does Hamilton Princess & Beach Club have a swimming pool?

Lounge, float, or swim at either of our two on-property pools. The infinity pool has uninterrupted views of Hamilton Harbour's scattered islands and is the perfect place to catch the glowing sunset. The Spa pool is adults-only and offers the perfect spot to relax and unwind in Bermuda.
