Semiramis InterContinental Cairo, Cairo
Chevron

Amenities
Rooms
726
Named for the Assyrian queen Semiramis, this "excellent high-rise" with hanging gardens is in "a great location" facing the Nile and near the Egyptian Museum and Cairo Opera House. Rooms with balconies have modern French decor and Elemis or L'Occitane bathroom amenities. Eleven "generally good" restaurants offer a variety of cuisines, from Lebanese to Italian to French. At Night & Day, patrons dine near cooking stations where they can watch chefs prepping sushi and teppanyaki. After dinner, step through the Arabian Nights-style doors of Rithmo for a late-night cocktail. "The wonderful staff are very helpful."
