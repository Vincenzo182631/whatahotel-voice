InterContinental Kyiv catering is anchored by exceptional restaurants and bars: Comme Il Faut, Olivera, Lobby Lounge Bar and b-hush, our rooftop lounge bar.

## Comme Il Faut

The Comme Il Faut restaurant presents the best dishes of French and Ukrainian cuisines. The sense of traditional French atmosphere is created by the exquisite decor, which can compare with the best restaurants in Paris. Enjoy the taste of dishes on the cozy summer terrace. The collection of our restaurant wine room will satisfy even the most demanding guests. Our professional sommelier is always delighted to help you.

## Lobby Lounge Bar

The Lobby Lounge Bar is situated on the ground floor of the hotel. A wide variety of cocktails, whiskies, snacks and hot beverages always contributes to the perfect day spent at our hotel. Enjoy an exquisite array of whiskies as the Lobby Lounge Bar gracefully metamorphoses into an enchanting cocktail lounge, offering the highest service level.

## B-hush

The ambience of the b-hush lounge bar exudes uniqueness. Embrace the breathtaking 270-degree view of Kyiv city from our panoramic windows and open-air terrace, extending a warm welcome to our guests. There is no better place if you wish to enjoy the sunset and starry night of the evening city.
We offer you a comfortable table in one of the two lounge zones, or to relish the privacy in one of the VIP rooms.
