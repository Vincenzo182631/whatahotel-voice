How much is parking at InterContinental Kyiv?

At InterContinental Kyiv, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 30 EUR .

To afford those with their own car complete peace of mind, InterContinental Kyiv hotel has secure underground parking for up to 50 vehicles, available for a charge. The maximum car height is 190 centimeters.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Kyiv?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Kyiv.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Kyiv?

Check-in at InterContinental Kyiv is from 2:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Kyiv offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Kyiv offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Kyiv from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Kyiv is conveniently located near International Airport Kyiv (Zhulyany) (IEV), approximately 4.97 miles away (8 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
