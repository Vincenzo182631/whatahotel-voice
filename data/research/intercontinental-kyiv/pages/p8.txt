# InterContinental KyivDestination

InterContinental Kyiv gives you an excellent opportunity to enjoy magnificent views, exceptional service and conference facilities in one of the best luxury hotels in Kyiv. Built to match its historical surroundings – St. Sophia’s Cathedral, St. Michael’s

## Things to do

### Golden Gate

Volodymyrska St. 40A, Kyiv, 01001

Discover the historic Golden Gate, a fortress and entrance to ancient Kyiv dating back to 1037, near the time of St. Sophia Cathedral's construction. Immerse yourself in history at the museum, which showcases the Golden Gate and the ancient city. During warmer seasons, enjoy the charming "Golden Gate" summer terrace. Just a 10-minute walk from InterContinental Kyiv.

### Peizazhna Alley

Velyka Zhytomyrska str., Kyiv,

Explore Landscape Alley (Peizazhna Alley), a popular attraction loved by locals and tourists alike. Situated on Old Kyiv Hill, the historic center of ancient Kyiv, this charming walkway was established in 1980 on the site of former city defenses dating back to the 10th-13th centuries. Just a 5-minute walk from InterContinental Kyiv.

### Botanical garden

1 Sadovo-Botanichna St., Kyiv,

After a hectic day, head to the National Botanical Garden of M.M. Gryshko for some natural relaxation. Get some well deserved rest and see exotic examples of tropical and subtropical flora, enjoy the aromatherapy of the fragrant flowers and take the opportunity to enjoy a picnic among many blossoming bouquets. It will take you about 15 minutes to get from InterContinental Kyiv to the National Botanical Garden by car.

Learn more

### Podil

Kyiv,

Travel around one of the most fascinating areas of Kyiv that encompasses numerous bars, cafes, boutiques and shops, historical and monumental sights with magnificent views over the Dnipro River. Take the opportunity to enjoy a short cruise on the river with the River Port station located right at Podil. The area dates back to the 18th century and has developed into a destination well worth a visit. It will take you 10 minutes to get from InterContinental Kyiv to Podil.

### Andriivskiy Uzviz

Andriyivskyy Descent 2b, Kyiv,

One of the most recommended landmarks of the city; if your time is limited this won’t be one to miss. This unique historical street is a pedestrian zone that is full of activities and entertainment. It features art galleries, the famous St. Andrew Church, the statue of Mikhail Bulgakov – author of ‘The Master and Margarita’, the One Street Museum and leads down to Podil – one of the oldest parts of the city. Andriivskiy Uzviz is a 5-minutes walk from InterContinental Kyiv.

### Kyiv Pechersk Lavra

Lavrska St. 15, Kyiv, 01015

The Kyiv Pecherk Lavra is a UNESCO World Heritage site and is known as the soul of Kyiv. Orthodox Christians view Kyiv Pechersk Lavra as the second most important place of religious worship after Jerusalem. The excursion will feature a tour of the caves where the relics of the saints are buried. It will take you around 30 minutes to get to Kyiv Pechersk Lavra from InterContinental Kyiv.

### Khreschatyk Street

Khreschatyk Street, Kyiv,

Khreschatyk is Kyiv’s main and central street and is located within walking distance from InterContinental Kyiv. It hosts national holiday celebrations, firework festivals and concerts. There are many stores, cafes, restaurants, cinemas, underground shopping malls and markets to catch your attention. The best time to explore the street is during weekends, when it closes to become a pedestrian only zone.

### Taras Shevchenko Park

Taras Shevchenko Blvrd, Kyiv,

The park is dedicated to Taras Shevchenko, one of the greatest and most notable Ukrainian authors of all time. Whilst heading to the city centre, be sure to walk through this park that soothes all with its tranquillity. A popular place for the locals to relax in the sun, or the shade of a tree. It will take you 10 minutes to get to Taras Shevchenko Park from InterContinental Kyiv.

### St. Volodymyr's Cathedral

Tarasa Shevchenko Blvd, 20, Kyiv, 02000

The Cathedral was built in the 19th century and dedicated to Saint Prince Volodymyr who baptized the country and made Christianity the official state religion. The cathedral itself is designed in the ancient Byzantine style. The internal frescos will leave an everlasting impression. Get ready to be charmed by vibrant colours and grand liturgies. It will take you 10 minutes to get to St. Volodymyr's Cathedral from InterContinental Kyiv.

### One Street Museum

Andriyivskyy Descent 2-B, Kyiv,

Despite its small size, the Museum of One Street houses so many historical elements that its antique interior, postcards, clothing, manuscripts, photos, and historical documents manage to create a virtual time machine. It features stories of the lives of prominent figures from Ukrainian scientific and cultural circles starting from the 19th century. Don't miss this opportunity to travel back in time. The Museum of One Street is a 10-minutes walk from InterContinental Kyiv.

Learn more

### National Opera House of Ukraine

50 Volodymyrska Str., Kyiv,

The National Opera House has entertained the elite and intelligentsia of Kyiv with magnificent balls since 1867. Today it is open for anybody who longs to see classic productions as well as those interested in contemporary ones. The true beauty of the Opera is that you don't need to be familiar with the local language, you rather search for emotions, within the musical tones and the dancing. The National Opera House of Ukraine is a 10-minutes walk from InterContinental Kyiv.

Learn more

### National Art Museum of Ukraine

Tereschenska Str, 9, Kyiv,

Over 40 thousand exhibits are presented in this museum, which includes Ukrainian art dating back to the times of the Ancient Kyivan Rus. The variety is rich, presenting Ukrainian baroque, ancient icons, exclusive contemporary art, and the ark work of Taras Shevchenko. Be sure to consult our concierge in order to book a tour in your preferred language. It will take you 15 minutes to get to National Art Museum of Ukraine from InterContinental Kyiv.

Learn more

### Mystetskiy Arsenal

Lavrska str, 10-12, Kyiv, 01001

National cultural, art and museum complex and one of the world's largest exhibition spaces. Its mission is to combine Ukrainian achievements and culture into one massive national project, as an artefact of world heritage. Enjoy your day at Mystetskyi Arsenal, great for culture and leisure. It will take you 30 minutes to get to Mystetskiy Arsenal from InterContinental Kyiv.

Learn more

### The House of Chimeras

Bankova str. 10, Kyiv, 02000

Visit the House of Chimeras, one of Kyiv's most atmospheric architectural monuments. This early Neo-Gothic masterpiece features mythological and hunting scenes, with sculptures of land and sea creatures, hunting motifs, and mythical beings. Highlights include the artist E. Sala's 1902 inscription and concrete heads of elephants, rhinos, antelopes, and more. Immerse yourself in the historical charm of Kyiv and experience the legends that make this building so iconic.

### Volodymyr's Hill

Volodymyrskyy Descent 1, Kyiv, 02000

Visit Volodymyr's Hill, a historic park in the heart of Kyiv on Mykhailivska Hill, offering stunning city views. Established in the mid-19th century with the erection of a monument to Volodymyr the Great, Kyiv's oldest sculpture, the park now features a pedestrian and bicycle bridge connecting to European Square. Enjoy numerous recreational spots, the preserved 1898 Kokorivska gazebo, and a monument to Dante Alighieri.Experience the picturesque beauty and unique vistas of Kyiv from this location

### The National Museum of Folk Architecture and Rural

Akademika Tronka Str., 1, Kyiv, 03026

Visit the National Museum of Folk Architecture and Rural Life of Ukraine and immerse yourself in the history of ancient Ukrainian settlements. This open-air museum, founded in 1969 on the historic land of Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra, showcases 300 monuments of folk architecture from the 16th to 20th centuries. Explore traditional huts, barns, and wells, and discover a collection of 100,000 exhibits, including folk clothes, tools, and crafts.

### Mezhyhirya Residense

Novi Petrivtsi, Kyiv region

Welcome to Mezhyhirya, a captivating destination where history, luxury, and nature intertwine harmoniously. Once the private retreat of a former Ukrainian president, this estate has evolved into a vibrant museum and park.
Discover the fascinating story of Mezhyhirya, from its origins as a state government residence to its transformation into a monument park reclaimed by the state in 2023.
