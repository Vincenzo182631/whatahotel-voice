# Dining

At Grand Hyatt Melbourne, we offer an array of dining options including bars and lounges to suit every guest’s dining needs: local meats and ribs smoked and grilled at Collins Kitchen; signature cocktails at Ru-Co Bar to travel around the world from the comfort of your chair; all-day dining or a business meeting at Lobby Lounge; beautifully crafted morning coffee at Bistro Bar; or two private dining rooms for your next event or celebration. Advance reservations are highly recommended.　※ A surcharge of 15% is applicable on public holidays and of 10% on Sundays.

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

## Collins Kitchen

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Collins Kitchen celebrates the art of cooking over smoke and flame, showcasing the finest locally sourced produce.
We have reimagined the classic Australian barbecue to create an unforgettable dining experience: think premium grilled meats like our signature wagyu tomahawk, slow-smoked American-style brisket and other premium cuts, wood roasted fish and seasonal vegetables. To finish, indulge in decadent house-made desserts.
Plus, we offer Grand Buffet Breakfast, 'Living Grand' Buffet Lunch and Dinner, and High Tea in Style by Cristina Re.

## Private Dining

For a special occasion dinner, Collins Kitchen offers two private dining rooms, both individually styled. The Kitchen Room offers a spacious and sophisticated private environment seating up to 14 guests. The Chef's Table seats up to 10 guests and is positioned directly off the show kitchen, allowing diners a closer experience of the theatrical show of the chefs.
