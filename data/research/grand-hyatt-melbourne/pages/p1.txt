## Explore Our Luxury 5-Star Melbourne Hotel

Discover a Melbourne icon. We are located prestigiously on Collins Street, surrounded by the city’s high fashion and fine dining and within walking distance of many of Melbourne’s top tourist attractions, including business, sporting and theatre districts. Our hotel features 546 spacious rooms and suites, delectable dining and a wellness centre. With our ideal location, coupled with exemplary service, we invite you to #GoGrand in one of the world’s most liveable cities.

MEETINGS & EVENTS

Grand Hyatt Melbourne’s event floor will undergo upgrade works from April 2026, and will remain closed until further notice. Smaller meeting rooms remain available for hire.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

City Club Health & Fitness Centre

Please be advised that our swimming pool, hot tub, sauna, and steam rooms on L9 are temporarily closed due to the upgrade works. Access to the rooftop tennis court will not be available during that time.

The gym space is relocated to L2, near the reception desk. Please follow the signs near the reception desk or ask our friendly Front Office team for directions.

Grand Club Lounge

Perched on the 31st floor, the Grand Club Lounge offers panoramic views of the Melbourne city skyline and is at the heart of the hotel’s Grand Club executive accommodation experience.

### Welcome with Pride Accreditation

Grand Hyatt Melbourne is now certified with Welcome with Pride Accreditation. Our team undertook a detailed training as part of our proactive stance on LGBTIQ+ inclusion – ensuring that all team members and guests, regardless of gender or sexuality, feel safe, respected, and free to express themselves authentically in our spaces. This initiative aligns with our purpose " _We care for people so they can be their best_", ensuring that both our team members and guests feel welcome, valued and respected.

### Collins Kitchen

Collins Kitchen celebrates the art of cooking over smoke and flame, showcasing the finest locally sourced produce.
We have reimagined the classic Australian barbecue to create an unforgettable dining experience: think premium grilled meats like our signature wagyu tomahawk, slow-smoked American-style brisket and other premium cuts, wood roasted fish and seasonal vegetables. To finish, indulge in decadent house-made desserts.
Plus, we offer Grand Buffet Breakfast, 'Living Grand' Buffet Lunch and Dinner, and High Tea in Style by Cristina Re.
