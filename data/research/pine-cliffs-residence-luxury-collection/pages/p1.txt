# Welcome to Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve

## Pine Cliffs Residence, a luxury family resort in the Algarve

Tucked away in one of the resort’s most tranquil corners, surrounded by nature, Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, is the ultimate home away from home, perfect for family holidays or longer stays. Choose from two- and three- bedroom apartments in this outstanding five-star resort in the Algarve, all fully equipped with the comforts of modern living, wrapped in a warm, classic style, complete with every amenity for a luxury escape.With generous living areas, ultra-comfortable master bedrooms, impeccable kitchenettes, laundry facilities, and furnished outdoor terraces, these apartments offer the perfect balance of privacy and connection. Set next to the golf course and surrounded by the landscaped gardens of this luxury resort in the Algarve, Pine Cliffs Residence is the perfect gateway to experience all that the resort has to offer, from one of the most beautiful beaches in the region to a choice of fine dining options, indoor and outdoor swimming pools, padel and tennis courts and much more.

Our fully-equipped two and three-bedroom apartments offer an exclusive homelike feel with all the convenience needed. The apartments are generously sized and decorated with indigenous algarvian elements making the space a fresh and relaxing retreat for our guests.

The beach where the resort is located was elected as one of the best beaches in the world by Trip Advisor and Condé Nast. ## The Unique Algarvian Resort

Our Resort is a unique place in the Algarve due to its location and its carefully maintained landscape associated with our unique luxury services that makes each stay a true experience at Southern Portugal. Guests will find a world to discover within the Resort and also will be able to explore the region from the central location of the Resort that allows day trips to nearby cities and attractions.

A Full Year of Celebrations

## Pine Cliffs' Events

During the year, our Resort promotes social and cultural events that makes all the seasons alive and a reason to celebrate at the best Resort in the Algarve. Our traditional exclusive Summer Night takes place every August, a luxurious party to celebrate summer. During Autumn our guests can enjoy the Resort at the sound of Jazz at Algarve Smooth Jazz Festival. The year could not end in a better way than at our radiant Christmas and New Year's celebrations in our vibrant Resort in Albufeira, Portugal.

## The Luxury Collection Concierge

Unlock the secrets of Southern Portugal with the experienced advice of our Concierge team leaded by Hugo Viegas, member of Clef D'Or Association. Our Concierge team is at your disposal to guide through all the grounds, activities and restaurants in-resort making sure you enjoy a memorable time at Pine Cliffs Residence.

More Information

Your experience at the Algarve will be elevated with the support of our Concierge team that is ready to provide you all the information and assist you with bookings and reservations for you and your family to unlock the region. Our team is led by Hugo Viegas that is part of "Les Clefs d'Or", the celebrated international concierge society. Plan ahead by contacting our concierge team: concierge.algarve@luxurycollection.com.

## Luggage Storage

Should you need to store your luggage before check-in or after check-out, we have a luggage storage room available and accessible 24 hours a day at no extra cost. Request assistance at the Front Desk and our Welcome Team will be pleased to assist you.

## The Deli

Our mini market known as The Deli is located right the entrance of Pine Cliffs Residence and has many basic products for your convenience. Products can also be shopped previous to your arrival and will be placed in your apartment. The Deli Mini Market is open from May to September. For the shop list, contact our Reservations Department: pinecliffs.reservation@luxurycollection.com.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve?

The check-in time at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 10:00 am.

Does Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve.

What are the parking options at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve?

On-site parking is available at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve booking.

What is the closest airport near Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve?

The closest airport near Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve is Faro Airport (FAO). FAO is located approximately 30.6 KM from the hotel.

Does Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
