## Experiences

Experience the best of Southern Portugal at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort. Nestled on the stunning Falesia Beach in Albufeira, our beachfront spa resort in the Algarve offers breathtaking...See More views of golden sands and red cliffs. Enjoy a range of dining and leisure options that will captivate your senses. Discover exclusive Algarve experiences, from the charming Fisherman’s Village in Albufeira to the historic Moorish Castle of Silves and the picturesque Ria Formosa Natural Park. Enhance your stay with our spa hotel in the Algarve, where relaxation and rejuvenation await, or out on the green at our Albufeira golf resort.See Less

## Pine Cliffs Golf & Country Club

The 9-hole and 68-hectare course at Pine Cliffs Resort in Albufeira, lies perched at 60 meters above the golden sand and blue water of the Atlantic Ocean, at the top of the cliffs.

Pine Cliffs Clubhouse

Located near to Tee 1 with mesmerizing views over the golf course, our Clubhouse is the place to go before and after a day at the course. The Restaurant offers a selection of light snacks, refreshing beverages and a great environment for social meetings. Get together with your fellow players for a drink, coffee while enjoying the best view of the golf course.

Golf Academy

Pine Cliffs Golf Academy is the place to go to sharpen your skills or even to give your first steps into the golf practice. Equipped with the latest technology in teaching systems, managed by Internationally Qualified PGA Professionals and tuition service, the academy provides the right environment for golf practice and improvement.

Golf Shop

Pine Cliffs Golf Shop features professional golf equipment and branded clothing.

## Weekly Tournaments

The Weekly Golf Tournaments are promoted weekly (every Tuesday) by our Golf Club from March to November. Winner's of each week gain a prize and access to the great Winner's Cup on the following year. Golf Club members, guests and outside players are all invited to participate.

More Information

Golf and Tennis practitioners will find the right place to test their skills and engage in a healthy competition with other guests and fellow players on our Weekly Tournaments that takes place every week on Tuesday and Thursdays. Winners of each week gain access to the Winner's Cup when they will compete with each other to become the great champion of Pine Cliffs Golf and Tennis each year.

Starting on March until November of each year, our golf team and tennis academy promotes the friendly weekly tournaments. The golf competition takes place every Tuesday from 9am until 5pm and the tennis competition takes place every Thursday at 5pm. The winners of each week gain an exclusive gift and access to the great Winner's Cup on the following year with payed stay and meals. The Winner's Cup takes place in the beginning of each year during one weekend when the great champions of the previous year compete with each other to become the great Pine Cliffs' Golf and Tennis champion. The winner get an exclusive luxurious prize.

## Winner's Cup

The great Golf and Tennis Championship is promoted every year by our Leisure team, the championship takes place in March and puts together the weekly winners of the previous year on competition to win the biggest prizes and become the great Winners of Pine Cliffs.

### Thermal Oasis

Relax at our Thermal Oasis with water-based treatments like the indoor hydro pool, herbal sauna, ice fountain, and experience showers. For ultimate relaxation, enjoy a massage in Albufeira at our spa hotel in Algarve.

### Senses of the Algarve

Our Signature Treatment relies on the most traditional ingredients of the Algarve, such as fleur-de-sel from Ria Formosa, sand from Falésia Beach, carob and orange oil, the treatment is a true journey through the best that the Algarve region has to offer.

### Serenity Aurum Suite

The Aurum Suite is designed to spoil and indulge the guests with their own private steam room, sauna, Jacuzzi, experience shower, double treatment room and lounge area. To complete the luxurious experience a complimentary bottle of champagne and fruits are included.

## Pine Cliffs Health Club

Pine Cliffs Residence Health Club is conveniently located at the main Residence building making it the right place to keep the track of your training. The health club has been newly renovated with state-of-the-art equipment by Techno-gym, including treadmills, bicycles, a climber, an elliptical trainer and a rowing machine. Added to that, at Resort's main building guests have access to Pine Cliffs Goes Active Health Club that features a complete gym, indoor heated pool and a Jacuzzi.

## Active by Serenity Health Club

Located at the Resort's main building, guests have access to Active by Serenity Health Club that features a complete gym with state-of-the-art equipment by Technogym, indoor heated pool for swimming practice and, jacuzzi and sauna.

## Destination Discoveries

Guests are invited to know more about the Algarve region and Portugal through two destination discoverie experiences designed by our Head of Concierge, Hugo Viegas. The tiles tour will take you to a cultural and historic trip through one of the most essential decoration and architectural details of Portugal. The botanical tour will show you the natural beauty of the region through our lush gardens that assembles beautiful flora from the Algarve and the world.

## Mimo Algarve Cooking School

Mimo Algarve, the cooking school at Pine Cliffs Resort offers daily cooking experiences for adults and families. The school has classes on Algarve cuisine, both traditional and modern Portuguese cuisine, as well as family classes, where parents and children come together around the stoves. It also has an integrated shop space, which features the best conserves, salts, charcuterie, cheeses, and gifts from the artisans of Portugal. All of the shop’s products have been specially curated, with lots of care.

The Cooking School & Gourmet Shop

Located at Lemon Square since 2018, in the heart of the Resort, the cooking school is assembled with state-of-the-art equipment and a luminous environment that invites for long afternoons paired with the best of Portuguese wine that accompanies the experiences. At Mimo you will also find a gourmet shop full of gourmet local products that you can take with you to prepare the delicious plates you learned when you get home.

## Kid's Activities & Services

Porto Pirata is the resort’s playground filled with two life-sized pirate ships, a sports court, a mini golf course and plenty of space for you and the kids to enjoy a great time. The multifunctional space has 7,000 square meters offers child care service for children from 6 months old to 8 years old. This service is supervised by an experienced and qualified team.

More Information

Daily activities are also organized by age, which included face painting, arts and crafts, treasure hunts, water games and various sports activities. The activities schedule is seasonal and may vary according to the time of the year you visit the resort.

Porto Pirata is currently open between 9:30 am and 5:00 pm. After closure guests are still able to access the outdoor facilities of the playground. All services of Porto Pirata Kid's Club are subject to seasonal availability, the playground is open all year-round. Please consult our Concierge to know about the Kid's Club program during your stay: concierge.algarve@luxurycollection.com or +351 289 500 100.

## Campioni Soccer Academy

During the summertime the little ones can learn the best soccer techniques and improve their game with ex Premier League champions at Campioni Soccer Academy. The daily sessions focus on the children improving their technical skills, their game & their approach & are delivered in a fun, safe environment on site at the resort.

More Information

For the third year in a row, our Soccer pitch receives Campioni Soccer Academy whose mission is to provide top league training to kids from 5-14 years old with ex-premier league champions. We have had players such as Andy Cole, Joe Cole, John Barnes and Wes Brown. During one week kids will have 2 hours of classes per/day when they will sharpen their soccer skills or even learn the first moves, all that in a chill and safe environment led by our champions. The Academy opens during Summertime and half-term. For bookings and more information contact: bookings@campioni.co.

Yes. Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
