# Enjoy Our Restaurants and Bars in the Algarve

## Dining

Portugal is renowned for its rich cuisine and culinary traditions. At Pine Cliffs Resort, we offer a diverse range of Algarve restaurants that highlight fresh, local ingredients and international flavors....See More Discover the best of Portuguese food in Albufeira, from traditional Algarve seafood at O Pescador to casual buffet dining at O Grill, and light, organic meals at Zest. The 15 restaurants and bars at Pine Cliffs Residence, a Luxury Collection Resort, Algarve are invitations to discover the best cuisine from Portugal and around the world. Restaurants and Algarve bars are subject to seasonal opening, please consult our Concierge on the daily openings: concierge.algarve@pinecliffs.com or +351 289 500 100.See Less
