# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Self-Parking

The driveway to the Q-Park parking garage at Potsdamer Platz is located in the Ludwig-Beck-Straße, 10785 and offers direct access to the hotel lobby. Please note that this is a public parking facility and we do not accept any responsibility for theft, loss or damage.

### Valet Parking

A charging station is located in the underground car park -2, type 2 plug, 11 KV. Please ask the concierge team for details. €35 per charge.
