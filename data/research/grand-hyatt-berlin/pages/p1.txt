## Contemporary Luxury in Berlin Mitte

Grand Hyatt Berlin stands at Potsdamer Platz, the square at the heart of Berlin's transformation. Since 1998, it has defined contemporary luxury in Berlin Mitte through striking design, a curated collection of original artworks and the energy of one of Europe's most vibrant cities. On the 8th floor, Club Olympus Spa & Fitness offers panoramic views across the Berlin skyline, while VOX Restaurant & Terrace remains one of the city's most celebrated dining destinations.

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Berlin offers 321 rooms and 21 suites within an extraordinary setting, each designed with a contemporary aesthetic that reflects the hotel's commitment to art and design. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame striking city views, while Grand Club access elevates select rooms and suites with exclusive benefits throughout the stay.

## EXPLORE

Your Grand Festive Season

Looking for the best festive dining in Berlin? Celebrate at Grand Hyatt Berlin, home to memorable Advent brunches, refined Christmas dining, and spectacular New Year's Eve dinners at VOX Restaurant & Bar. Located at Potsdamer Platz, our festive experiences bring together outstanding cuisine, seasonal specialties, and precious moments with loved ones in the heart of the city.

Our Art Collection

The Grand Hyatt Berlin is a place for art lovers. Our curated collection of over 120 pieces of art was carefully selected to intrigue, surprise and create a vibrant and interesting atmosphere throughout the Hotel. From our lobby over our restaurants to our rooms, the Grand Hyatt Berlin houses art pieces from artists from the world over.

Sylvie Fleury, John Armleder, Günther Förg, Dieter Roth and Jaehyo Lee are only a few of the names that have created the art you can explore throughout the hotel.

Unlike traditional hotel art, our interior architect and curators chose to display art pieces that challenge, intrigue and interest, weaving in seamlessly with the architecture and creating points of interest and awe all throughout the building.

Breakfast at Grand Hyatt Berlin

Begin the day at VOX Restaurant, where a generous breakfast buffet of fresh, high-quality ingredients is served daily in one of Berlin Mitte's most stylish dining rooms. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available alongside a selection of à la carte dishes.

Breakfast is available daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM, and on weekends until noon.

Grand Club

The Grand Club is a signature Grand Hyatt experience, a private social space reserved exclusively for the hotel’s most discerning and loyal guests. Located on the 7th floor, it offers a level of personal, individualized service rarely found elsewhere, with guest preferences gathered ahead of arrival to ensure every visit feels anticipated rather than arranged.

Each day begins with a complimentary continental breakfast buffet, followed by all-day refreshments in a lounge designed equally for relaxation, work or informal meetings. As evening approaches, the Grand Club hosts a cocktail hour with a creative selection of canapés. For guests discovering the Grand Hyatt brand for the first time, club access represents one of the most distinctive ways to experience the hotel beyond the room itself.

Discover Berlin’s vibrant world of Art, Design & Culture

Grand Hyatt Berlin sits at the center of the city’s most historically and culturally significant district. Brandenburg Gate, the Reichstag Building and the Memorial to the Murdered Jews of Europe are all within walking distance, alongside the Tiergarten, the Berlin Philharmonic Hall and the galleries of Berlin Mitte. Checkpoint Charlie, the East Side Gallery and Museum Island are easily reached from the hotel, making it the ideal base for experiencing everything Berlin has to offer.

Hyatt Catering Berlin

Hyatt Catering Berlin brings exceptional culinary expertise to meetings and events of every scale, from intimate private dinners to large-scale luxury galas. Creative menus, carefully sourced ingredients and a dedicated team combine to deliver a catering experience that is as distinctive as the occasion itself.

VOX Restaurant & Bar Culinary Highlights

VOX Restaurant & Bar hosts a series of distinctive culinary events throughout the year, from Sushi and Champagne evenings to the Kitchen Party, where guests are invited behind the scenes to discover the creative process of the hotel's chefs. Each event offers an extraordinary perspective on one of Berlin Mitte's most iconic dining destinations.

Discover Grand Meeting & Event Options

Grand Hyatt Berlin offers one of the city's most impressive and versatile settings for meetings, conferences, weddings and events. Nine intelligently designed meeting rooms feature natural daylight and original contemporary artworks, with a Grand Ballroom accommodating up to 850 guests. From executive board meetings to large-scale conferences and glamorous gala dinners, each occasion is supported by a dedicated events team.

Stay Relaxed on Business Trips

Grand Hyatt Berlin is designed as much for productive stays as for leisurely ones. Spacious rooms with complimentary Wi-Fi, 24-hour room service and a choice of dining across three restaurants and bars provide everything needed for a seamless business trip. Club Olympus Spa & Fitness is complimentary for hotel guests, with jogging routes through the Tiergarten nearby. World of Hyatt members earn points with every stay, while Grand Club guests enjoy complimentary breakfast, afternoon tea and evening cocktails with canapés throughout their visit.

Travelling with Family

Grand Hyatt Berlin welcomes guests of all ages with a range of thoughtfully considered 5-star family services. The Very Important Baby program provides cribs, nightlights, diapers, baby-changing facilities and children's bathrobes, with babysitters, buggies and bicycle child seats available on request. Children up to six years old enjoy complimentary breakfast, while child-friendly menus are available across the hotel's restaurants. Club Olympus Spa & Fitness, with its indoor pool, outdoor terrace and Chrissy the bear program, offers a memorable experience for younger guests and families alike.

Gift Vouchers

Give the gift of an exceptional experience at Grand Hyatt Berlin — from outstanding dining at VOX Restaurant to a day at Club Olympus Spa & Fitness or a stay in one of the hotel's 342 rooms and suites. Personalized gift vouchers are available to order online or pickup at the hotel Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Rooftop Spa with Pool & Fitness

Club Olympus Spa & Fitness occupies the 8th floor of Grand Hyatt Berlin, offering spectacular panoramic views across the Berlin skyline alongside an extensive range of wellness facilities. An indoor pool, whirlpool, outdoor terrace, sauna, steam bath, solarium and fitness area are all available, complemented by a full program of cosmetic treatments and massages. Access is complimentary for hotel guests throughout their stay, with the option to extend beyond checkout for €30. The fitness area is accessible 24 hours a day for hotel guests.

**Open daily 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM**

**Children's hours: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM**
