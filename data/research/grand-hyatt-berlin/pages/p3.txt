# Dining

From the acclaimed VOX Restaurant & Bar to the immersive Magic Table and the artisan offerings of 1838 Deli, every experience reflects the vibrant spirit of Berlin Mitte.

## VOX Bar

Adjacent to VOX Restaurant, VOX Bar is one of Berlin's most celebrated bars, home to a collection of more than 300 spirits and whiskies. Each bartender creates their own individual signature cocktail, complemented by classic drinks and spirit flights paired with chocolate for a more considered tasting experience. The Smoker's Lounge offers a curated selection of Cuban cigars.

## Breakfast at Vox

Each morning at VOX Restaurant, a generous breakfast buffet of fresh, high-quality ingredients sets the tone for the day. A broad selection of savory and sweet specialties is complemented by à la carte options, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices available throughout. The stylish atmosphere of one of Berlin Mitte's most celebrated dining rooms makes breakfast here as much about the setting as the food. Available daily for €39 per person.

## Bistro Mitte

Located on the ground floor of Grand Hyatt Berlin, Bistro Mitte is a stylish, fireplace-centered setting where a menu of international and local classics is served in a relaxed, open atmosphere. From pasta and burgers to Berlin Currywurst and Wiener Schnitzel, the weekly changing menu reflects both the international character of Potsdamer Platz and the culinary heritage of the city.
Leather armchairs, an open setting and a warm atmosphere make it equally suited to a casual lunch, an informal business meeting or the monthly Grand Brunch — held on the first Sunday of every month from Noon to 3:00 PM, featuring unlimited à la carte dishes and bottomless Crémant.

## 1838 Deli

1838 Deli brings artisan pastries, freshly prepared sandwiches and specialty coffee to Potsdamer Platz in a light, contemporary setting. All pastries and baked goods are prepared in the hotel's own in-house patisserie, with standout items including pain perdu, canelés, pastrami sandwiches with truffle cream and seasonal tarts. Julius Meinl coffee specialties, fresh smoothies and juices complement the menu throughout the day, alongside a considered selection of vegan options and milk alternatives. A natural stop for hotel guests and locals alike, from an early morning coffee to a midday break.

## LE PETIT CHEF - WORLD'S GREATEST CHEF

Join the world’s smallest chef at Grand Hyatt Berlin on his most ambitious adventure yet. This unique journey takes you from the historic origins of our ingredients straight to the perfection of culinary technique. Across five exciting lessons, Le Petit Chef teaches the essential skills of a master chef – including a beautifully plated dish inspired by Kandinsky’s shapes and colours. In the interactive finale, you’ll take the culinary tools into your own hands. Bring together everything you’ve learned to complete your dessert step by step – and prove that there’s a great chef in every one of us!

## The Magic Table

Please contact the hotel for current showtimes and reservations.

The Magic Table at Grand Hyatt Berlin transforms dinner into something altogether more extraordinary. A five-course fine dining menu unfolds alongside striking 3D projections, as guests are drawn into an interactive story where they solve puzzles, choose symbols and activate magical portals that shape the direction of the evening. Created in collaboration with 2Spicy Entertainment and internationally acclaimed illusionists Thommy Ten and Amélie van Tass, each dinner moves between culinary craft and theatrical magic in equal measure. A singular experience for couples, families and groups alike. Please note: The show is presented in German only.
€145 per person.
Children aged 6 to 12: €80 per person.
