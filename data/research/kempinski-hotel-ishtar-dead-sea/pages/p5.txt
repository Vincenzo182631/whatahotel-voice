### Celestial Pools

Bask in the therapeutic rays of the Dead Sea sun while decadently lounging or taking a refreshing dip in one of our nine celestial swimming pools all boasting magnificent views of the wondrous Dead Sea.

Each uniquely designed, open from sunrise till sunset, and with changing rooms available near the pool areas our pools are available exclusively for your delight and enjoyment.
