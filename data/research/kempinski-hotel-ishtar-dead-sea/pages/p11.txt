# Location

### Location

Kempinski Hotel Ishtar Dead Sea is located by the shores of the Dead Sea, known to be the world’s oldest natural spa.

**Arrival by car from Queen Alia International Airport:**

Our hotel is located 65.4 km away from Queen Alia International Airport by Car, to get there follow the directions below:

Get on Airport Rd./Route 15/Route 45M, Head east on ‫Queen Alia Airport Rd‎ toward Airport Rd. ‫Queen Alia Airport Rd‎ turns slightly right and becomes Airport Rd. At the roundabout, take the 5th exit and stay on Airport Rd. At the roundabout, take the 1st exit and stay on Airport Rd. then make a slight right to merge onto Airport Rd./Route 15/Route 45M.

Continue on Airport Rd. Drive from Route 40M and Jordan Valley Hwy/Route 65 to Swemeh, Merge onto Airport Rd./Route 15/Route 45M, Keep left to continue on Airport Rd./Route 15, Take the ramp onto Madaba Hwy, Take the exit toward Dead Sea/Na'ur, At the roundabout, take the 2nd exit toward Dead Sea/Na'ur, Keep left, Turn left to merge onto Dead Sea Rd./Route 40/Route 40M then Keep left to continue on Jordan Valley Hwy/Route 40M/Route 65, follow signs for Dead Sea.
