# Titan Tikes Kids Club

### Titan Tikes Kids Club

Relax and let your children take advantage of the various activities we offer at our Titan Tikes Kids Club.

Your children will not just enjoy their time at the Dead Sea, but our variety of activities will also help in developing them. We have also included plenty of outdoor activities so your children can benefit from the fresh air of the Dead Sea while enjoying their time with our fun-filled activities by our team.

**Location**

- Titan Tikes Kids Club is located on the ground floor of the Ishtar building behind the Akkad restaurant.

**Indoor Games**

- Wii games
- Computer games
- Kids slide games
- Storybooks
- Board games
- Kids plastic ball
- Game boy
- Monopoly game
- Puzzle game
- Lego and blocks
- Movies

**Kids Club Policy:**

- Access is for children aged between 2 and 12-years-old.
- It is a socks-only play area (no shoes permitted).
- Adults may supervise their own children and may have limited access free of charge.
- A registration card must be completed and signed by the parents or guardian who is at least 18-years-old before allowing the child/children admission into the Kids Club.
- Kids with influenza infections, as well as infectious illnesses, will not be allowed into the Kids Club.

**Kids Club Opening Times**

- Daily from 9:00am to 23:00pm

**Entrance Fees**

- A daily entry fee of JOD 10 plus Tax & Service charges will apply per child. The entry fee includes all the treats your child can eat and drink.
