# LUXURY ROOMS AND SUITES

## Devoted to music

**51 Rooms & Suites** which were completely and **extensively redesigned in the beginning of 2018** now offers a redefined Prague hotel experience, dedicated to a specific style of music and a particular artist. Our luxury Prague accommodation feature 29 Deluxe rooms, 16 Junior Suites and 6 Signature Luxury Suites all situated within four floors of the Aria Hotel Prague. Each floor is devoted to a different music genre: Contemporary, Classical, Opera and Jazz Music.

The rooms' interiors were created by two famous Italian designers, Rocco Magnoli and Lorenzo Carmellini. Their work can also be viewed in many Versace stores and hotels across the globe. Each room is equipped with carefully selected, custom-made furniture.

All of our luxury Prague rooms and suites are non-smoking and feature SAFFRON Luxury beds, flat-screen LED TV, Blu-ray player, Apple TV, an iPad (not available in Deluxe Rooms), high speed Wi-Fi internet access, Hi-Fi audio system with Marantz amplifier and a fully stocked minibar. Our luxury bathrooms feature a large vanity, a European soaking tub or a stand-up rain shower (or both), a telephone, make-up mirror, hair dryer, Molton Brown cosmetics, and thick terrycloth bathrobes and slippers.

Each room and suite also has its own private, secured Wi-Fi network, and guests can easily access AirPlay to send videos, photos, and music from their own Apple devices to the in-room TV and speakers. All rooms and suites have either a garden, street or atrium view, and are equipped with air-conditioning, an in-room laptop-size safe, writing desk, and telephone. An ironing board, iron and baby cots are available upon request.

All hotel guests also have access to our extensive Music Library, which is available via Apple TV to enjoy in the comfort of their own room (Aria Hotel Prague's notorious Music Library consists of more than 5,000 CDs and DVDs).
