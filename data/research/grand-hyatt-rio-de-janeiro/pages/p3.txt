# Dining

Guests at Grand Hyatt Rio de Janeiro will find incredible Barra da Tijuca restaurants right inside the hotel. Experience world-class quality cuisine with strong local influences at our hotel’s three signature restaurants: sushi and saki at Shiso; casual, fresh fare at Cantô Gastrô & Lounge; or breakfast and brunch at Tano, our exclusive morning restaurant with gorgeous lagoon views.

## Grand Club Lounge

The Grand Club Lounge, our executive and premium floor, offers exclusive benefits and services. Guests with access to the Grand Club service are entitled to a delicious breakfast buffet and an afternoon cocktail hour with various appetizers and alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, as well as complimentary coffee, tea and beverages throughout the day. The space has panoramic views of Praia da Barra and Lagoa de Marapendi.

## Quiosque

Open 24 hours a day for your convenience, the kiosk is located in the lobby and offers a variety of food and beverage options to suit all tastes. From sandwiches, salads, and fruits to sweets and breads made by the hotel's bakery, take advantage of the ease of having essential travel items available for purchase, such as flip-flops, towels, beach accessories, sunscreen, and more.

## Cantô Gastrô & Lounge

Immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Barra da Tijuca while enjoying creative dishes and signature cocktails at Cantô. Designed by Arthur Casas, the restaurant offers a casual yet chic ambiance, featuring a large mural inspired by Roberto Burle Marx. The dishes reflect the informality and relaxed vibe of true Rio neighborhoods, with a focus on vegan options, dishes to share, and Saturday Feijoada with live music.

## Shiso

Savor traditional Japanese cuisine at Shiso, considered by the Veja Comer & Beber Rio 24/25 awards as one of the top 3 hotel restaurants in Rio de Janeiro. Inspired by the fusion of traditional Eastern elements with contemporary and Carioca interpretations, Shiso’s open kitchen is an invitation to discover more about the behind-the-scenes of an award-winning restaurant. Explore our varied menu with diverse options and the Omakase Zen, a tasting menu featuring dishes selected by the chef. In addition, enjoy the sake bar and signature cocktails by Alex Mesquita, considered the best bartender in Rio in 2023.
