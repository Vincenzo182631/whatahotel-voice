### Perfect Spot for a Quiet, Comfortable Getaway

My room at the Grand Hyatt Rio was way bigger than I expected with a fantastic view of the beach and pool area. Housekeeping was amazing—they even organized the chaos I created in the bathroom with all my makeup and products. I also loved that there were always tons of towels. The Club breakfast was seriously the best I’ve seen compared to other lounges. The location is great if you want something a little quieter, but it’s still super easy to get around. There were plenty of transportation options, including tons of Ubers, so getting to Copacabana or anywhere else was no problem.
