## Escape to Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, at Our Barra da Tijuca Resort Hotel

Experience the ultimate Rio de Janeiro escape in Brazil's "Marvelous City," set between breathtaking mountains and pristine white-sand beaches. Our resort hotel overlooks a tranquil nature reserve, providing serene views, easy beach access, a refreshing on-site spa and pool and exceptional Rio restaurants. Discover Brazilian culture and explore the nearby Barra da Tijuca, one of Rio's most exclusive areas, known for its beach paradise, shopping and vibrant urban attractions. With daily activities designed for both relaxation and excitement, this getaway offers everything you need for a memorable stay.

## ROOMS & SUITES

While in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, unwind in one of our resort’s 436 beautiful hotel rooms, including 43 suites. Located in Barra da Tijuca and designed by the renowned international design studio Yabu Pushelberg, the rooms offer the perfect escape, inspired by local surroundings and incorporating native stones and exclusive fabrics. Book gorgeous ocean or lagoon views.

## EXPLORE

24-Hour Fitness Center

Keep up with your exercise routine while traveling. Our fitness center offers modern equipment for aerobic, strength-training and cardio workouts. Additionally, our large, multifunctional studio features training sessions and yoga classes.

Kids Club

Our Kids Club offers toys and activities like a ball pit, a trampoline, table football, a slide, video games, a baby area and much more. Children under six must be accompanied by a guardian. Open daily from 9 a.m.–5 p.m.

Atiaia Spa

The ideal place for balance, relaxation and beauty care, Atiaia Spa has nine exclusive suites with three designated for couples’ treatments. Atiaia—which means “luminosity” in one of Brazil’s native languages, Tupi-Guarani—is a true sanctuary, offering excellent therapists and high-quality products in a sophisticated, cozy environment.

Outdoor Pool

Relax with a refreshing swim in our spectacular hotel pool with stunning views of the surrounding area. Savor an exclusive drink while you enjoy one of Rio’s best poolside escapes.

### Grand Club Lounge

The Grand Club Lounge, our executive and premium floor, offers exclusive benefits and services. Guests with access to the Grand Club service are entitled to a delicious breakfast buffet and an afternoon cocktail hour with various appetizers and alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, as well as complimentary coffee, tea and beverages throughout the day. The space has panoramic views of Praia da Barra and Lagoa de Marapendi.
