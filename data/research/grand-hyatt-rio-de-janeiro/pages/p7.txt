# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Grand Hyatt Rio de Janeiro offers 24-hour valet service, as well as an electric vehicle charging station, with an extra fee. Our parking facility includes a 15-minute grace period and special rates are available for Meetings and Events.
