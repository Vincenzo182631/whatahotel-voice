Marvel at views of the Atlantic Ocean and breathtaking sunsets on the lagoon from the rooms at our Rio de Janeiro hotel. All 436 rooms and suites—each with a large private balcony—are perfect escape venues, reflecting the surrounding atmosphere with the use of native stones and exclusive fabrics. Opt for a Grand Club hotel room for exclusive lounge access, including all-day refreshments and daily afternoon cocktails. Upgrade to one of the 43 suites for enhanced features, including spacious dining and living areas with landscaped terraces or even an infinite pool.
