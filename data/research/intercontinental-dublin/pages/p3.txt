Indulge in exceptional dining at InterContinental Dublin. ## The Lobby Lounge

The heart of the house The Lobby Lounge, overlooking the Garden Terrace. Luxurious in space and effortless in style, it is the perfect venue for all day casual dining. Indulge in Afternoon Tea, a long standing tradition, serving finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones, pastries, and over 25 different Shibui loose leaf teas. Enjoy our expert resident pianist, Thursday to Saturday, from 7:00pm to 9:00pm.

## The Whiskey Bar

The Whiskey Bar at InterContinental Dublin offers a warm, intimate ambiance with a curated collection of over 100 whiskeys, including 69 Irish labels. Open to all, it's perfect for savouring a handcrafted cocktail or a Guinness alongside a whiskey selection tailored by the hotel's Whiskey Sommelier. Sit back, relax with a fine whiskey, and enjoy views of the Garden Terrace.
