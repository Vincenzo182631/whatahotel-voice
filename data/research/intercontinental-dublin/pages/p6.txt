### Spacious Guest Rooms and Suites

We offer the most spacious guest rooms and suites in all of Dublin, with large windows that bathe the rooms in natural light. Each space provides modern touches, including Nespresso coffee machines and Byredo bath amenities. Enjoy luxurious marble bathrooms and relaxing garden or city views—right from your luxury room or suite.

Learn more

### Sophisticated Wedding Venue

Celebrate your love story at our Dublin hotel, where historic charm meets modern luxury. Our elegant gardens and spacious ballrooms create the perfect setting for your big day. With our dedicated wedding specialists, you won't have to lift a finger. Our team will bring every detail to life—just the way you want it.

Learn more

### Award-Winning Spa

Our spa is a tranquil retreat featuring ESPA treatments in a serene, naturally lit space with an outdoor garden. Enjoy a heated pool, steam room, and sauna, along with award-winning holistic treatments, including massage and facials. Spa packages cater to solo pampering sessions and group experiences alike.

Learn more

### Elegant Meeting & Event Space

Host your next meeting or events at InterContinental Dublin. Our elegant spaces cater to everything from intimate social gatherings to large conferences. Attendees will benefit from state-of-the-art technology, natural light-filled rooms, and breathtaking garden views. Our expert planners will handle every detail of your event.

Learn more

### Gourmet Dining

Indulge in exceptional dining at InterContinental Dublin. Enjoy contemporary Irish cuisine at Simmons, our fine dining restaurant, or visit The Lobby Lounge for light bites and hearty dishes in a relaxed yet elegant setting. For a special experience, enjoy Afternoon Tea in The Reading Room or savour a drink at The Whiskey Bar.

## FAQs for InterContinental Dublin

Does InterContinental Dublin have a pool?

InterContinental Dublin has 1 indoor pool.

Indoor Pool

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Dublin?

At InterContinental Dublin, self-parking is available for a daily rate of 27 EUR and valet parking is available for a daily rate of 37 EUR .

Car parking is charged at an hourly rate of €4.40 up to a maximum of €27.00 per twenty-four-hour period. Valet parking is provided at a rate of €37.00 per twenty-four hours, or part of.
Electric chargers available, an additional charge may apply.

Parking fee: 27 EUR per day

In and out privileges

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Dublin?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Dublin.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Dublin for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Dublin have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Dublin has 2 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Dublin?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:30 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Dublin?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Dublin.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Dublin?

Check-in at InterContinental Dublin is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Dublin offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Dublin offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Dublin from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Dublin is conveniently located near Dublin International Airport (DUB), approximately 9.88 miles away (15.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Dublin?

InterContinental Dublin is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Visit Dublin Castle - 3.23 miles away (5.2 kilometres)
- Trinity College Dublin - 2.73 miles away (4.4 kilometres)
- Temple Bar - 2.73 miles away (4.4 kilometres)
- Jameson Distillery - 3.54 miles away (5.7 kilometres)
- Dublin Zoo - 4.47 miles away (7.2 kilometres)
- St. Stephen’s Green - 1.86 miles away (3 kilometres)
- EPIC Emigration Museum - 2.61 miles away (4.2 kilometres)
- Airfield Estate - 3.17 miles away (5.1 kilometres)
- St. Patrick’s Cathedral - 2.73 miles away (4.4 kilometres)
- Dublin Museums & Galleries

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Dublin puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
