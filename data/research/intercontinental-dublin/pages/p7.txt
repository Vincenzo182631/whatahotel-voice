### Looks Are Not Everything

23 days ago

Beautiful Site, But Looks Aren't Everything.

we arrived and were sent up to our room to find the bathroom ceiling damaged & peeling (significantly). alerted the front desk and were asked when was the soonest they could have access to the room - we said 15 mins. when we returned 6 hours later and asked about the status, the front desk had no idea what I was talking about. when I was able to show them a picture of the damage, we were transferred to a suite (which I'm grateful for).

for the bulk of our 5 day stay, the front desk team was typically obscured in an office to the side of the desk, indifferent to us coming and going, and never once text or called up to the room to check in on anything. most days we barely received a good morning or hello. no recommendations for dinner, drinks, etc. totally different from what we've come to expect from IHG. one of group who is an ambassador was never offered points/drink/breakfast, never given any details about the hotel's dining options - she said the checkin was curt and abrupt.

the pillows at this location are absolutely terrible. poor quality foam with no support, and every effort to utilize them was a struggle.

the breakfast team and breakfast experience were the best part of the visit. the team there was delightful, interactive, and remembered us everyday - asking about our plans, how we slept, and just being an overall exceptional start to the day.

At dinner in the hotel lobby, I tried to order an iced tea from the server and was met with a blank stare. the gentleman proceeded to ask me to explain what an iced tea was so he could "figure it out and try to find it". neither he, the other server or the hostess was able to comprehend the request, so I settled on a cranberry juice. the next morning I found iced tea on both your breakfast menu (in various flavors) and being offered by the bar adjacent to the lobby.

Our first time in Ireland and our first experience with IHG internationally, with friends and family, and I have to say - I'm absolutely stunned by the stark difference in standards and hospitality. the warmth we've always experienced at your Boston location, which inspired us to get the credit card, join the ambassador program and become loyal to the brand was no where to be found in Dublin.

I work in hospitality, running a winery and seeing clients all day long. My entire profession is welcoming, interacting and sharing experiences with a wide variety of guests - everyday. I can't say I would feel comfortable or confident telling any of those patrons to visit Dublin IHG and honestly, I'm second guessing international sites based on this initial experience.
