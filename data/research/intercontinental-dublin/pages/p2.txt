# InterContinental DublinRooms & suites

Discover spacious rooms and suites at InterContinental Dublin, thoughtfully designed to provide an elegant and comfortable setting in the heart of Ballsbridge. From Classic Rooms to luxurious suites, each offers plush bedding, generous space, complimentary Wi-Fi and marble bathrooms with deep-soaking tubs.

Enjoy views across the leafy neighbourhood, with selected rooms featuring private balconies and interconnecting options available for families and groups. Whether visiting for a city break, family stay or special occasion, our rooms and suites provide a refined base for your time in Dublin.
