# COMO Cocoa Island – Maldives FAQ: Villas, Transfers, Activities & Honeymoons

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### How do I get to COMO Cocoa Island?

COMO Cocoa Island is located just 40 minutes by speedboat from Velana International Airport (Malé), making it one of the most easily accessible resorts in the Maldives:

- No seaplane transfer required - saving time and cost
- Direct speedboat journey across the Indian Ocean
- Scenic views from the moment you arrive

### Do you offer overwater villas at COMO Cocoa Island?

Yes - our boutique resort features just 33 luxurious overwater villas, each designed for:

- Maximum privacy and tranquillity
- Direct lagoon access and private sundecks
- Uninterrupted ocean views - perfect for couples and honeymooners

### Do you have villas suitable for families or groups?

Yes - we offer spacious two- and three-bedroom pool villas, perfect for:

- Families with children or multigenerational stays
- Groups seeking additional space and amenities
- Private pools, generous living areas, and breathtaking views

### What wellness activities are available at COMO Cocoa Island?

Our resort blends natural surroundings with holistic wellness experiences:

- Barefoot sandbank meditation and breathwork sessions
- Daily yoga and Pilates with ocean views
- Restorative therapies at COMO Shambhala Cocoa Island
- Personalised treatments for mind-body balance

### How is the house reef at COMO Cocoa Island?

Our house reef is renowned as one of the finest in the Maldives:

- Rich marine biodiversity, vibrant corals, and clear visibility
- Complimentary snorkelling tours with our marine biologist
- Accessible directly from your villa - no boat required

### Do you offer night snorkelling experiences?

Yes - guests can experience our iconic UV night snorkelling adventure:

- Special torches reveal fluorescent colours beneath the surface
- Witness glowing greens, reds, and oranges in a dreamlike setting

### Can I snorkel with turtles or sharks?

Absolutely - our curated experiences include:

- Turtle Expedition Snorkel: encounter green turtles in their natural habitat
- Shark Adventure: swim with white tip and blacktip reef sharks just 15 minutes offshore

### Which is the best Maldives resort for couples or honeymooners?

COMO Cocoa Island is widely regarded as one of the Maldives' most romantic and secluded escapes:

- Only 33 villas, offering intimacy and quietude
- Direct lagoon access from every villa
- Personalised service with romantic touches throughout your stay

### What romantic activities can couples enjoy at COMO Cocoa Island?

We offer curated experiences designed for connection and celebration:

- Sandbank Bliss: private Champagne dining on a remote sandbank
- Tented Beach Dinner: lantern-lit evening meals under the stars
- Cinema Under the Stars: open-air movie nights with popcorn and bean bags
- Private Yacht Charters: cruise the lagoon in style with onboard dining

### Is COMO Cocoa Island suitable for families with children?

Yes - while intimate, our resort welcomes families and offers enriching experiences:

- Spacious multi-bedroom pool villas for comfort and privacy
- Coral planting workshops with our in-house marine biologist
- Family-friendly beach cinema evenings
- Marine biology talks to engage children in ocean conservation
