# Dining at COMO COCOA ISLAND

Our menus draw on South Indian flavours, with light curries, fresh seafood and salads served alongside contemporary Asian and Mediterranean classics. COMO Shambhala Kitchen offers healthful nourishment throughout the day, from morning juices to evening broths.

#### UFAA

Locally sourced produce and coastal flavours

Ufaa is our inside-outside, pool- and beach-side restaurant. It serves modern interpretations of Indian and Mediterranean cuisine with an emphasis on local produce and sustainably caught seafood.

#### Faru Bar

Poolside sips

Faru Bar is an easygoing poolside bar with direct beach access. Our bartenders create cocktails and mocktails to suit every taste, alongside a menu of fine wines, classic cigars and light snacks.

#### Sandbank picnic

Your private paradise

We set up an intimate dining table on COMO Cocoa Island’s eastern sandbank, where your personal butler serves you a three-course picnic meal, a bottle of prosecco and a fruit platter.

### PRIVATE DINING

We offer a variety of private dining experiences in unforgettable locations across the island, with menus for special events devised in consulation with our chefs. You can also discuss a specific set-up, for a proposal or family celebration. Or just order in-room dining to your private villa deck.

## COMO SHAMBHALA KITCHEN

COMO Shambhala Kitchen is a healthful cuisine concept full of flavour. In the Maldives, our chefs make ample use of the tandoor for fat-free cooking. COMO Shambhala Kitchen is served at Ufaa, and is available on in-room dining menus. Soups, salads and broths are among our classics.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1What breakfast options are available at COMO Cocoa Island?

Breakfast includes a gourmet à la carte selection with tropical fruits, homemade pastries, organic juices, and made-to-order hot dishes. Guests can also choose healthy COMO Shambhala Cuisine options for a balanced start to the day.

#### 2What type of cuisine is served at COMO Cocoa Island’s restaurants?

The resort’s main restaurant, Ufaa, serves a refined mix of Indian and Mediterranean cuisine, all crafted with a focus on freshness and flavor.

#### 3Does COMO Cocoa Island cater to specific dietary requirements?

Yes. The culinary team caters to all dietary needs, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and halal options, ensuring a personalized dining experience for every guest.

#### 4Does COMO Cocoa Island offer private dining experiences on the beach or in-villa?

Yes. COMO Cocoa Island offers private dining experiences in the Maldives, including romantic beach dinners, in-villa dining, and the popular floating breakfast served in your private pool or lagoon. Guests can enjoy tailor-made menus designed by our chefs, whether it’s a sunrise breakfast surrounded by ocean views or a candlelit dinner on the sand — each experience captures the resort’s signature intimate island luxury..

#### 5Can COMO Cocoa Island arrange destination dining such as sandbank dinners?

Yes, COMO Cocoa Island offers exclusive Maldives sandbank dining experiences, where guests can dine under the stars on a secluded sandbank surrounded by the Indian Ocean — a signature luxury experience.

#### 6Are healthy or wellness-focused dining options available at COMO Cocoa Island?

Yes. The resort features the renowned COMO Shambhala Cuisine, focused on nutrient-rich, organic, and wellness-inspired dishes that support a healthy lifestyle without compromising on taste.
