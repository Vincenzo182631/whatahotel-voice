### Event Spaces

**Ufaa and Faru Bar:** Ideal for cocktail parties and receptions, with Ufaa accommodating up to 60 dining guests, and Faru Bar accommodating up to 25 standing guests.

**Two-Bedroom COMO Sunrise Villa / Three-Bedroom COMO Sunset Villa:** These suit smaller dinner party settings, or an important group meeting, with seating for 20 guests or more.

**COMO Shambhala Retreat's Sandbank:** A popular destination for wedding blessings and can accommodate up to 30 guests.

**COMO Shambhala Retreat's Courtyard:** Our largest events space on the island, with room for larger family celebrations or wedding ceremonies with up to 80 guests

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Can COMO Cocoa Island be booked as a private island for events or celebrations?

Yes. COMO Cocoa Island offers a private island buyout experience in the Maldives for up to 80 guests. Ideal for weddings, milestone celebrations, or corporate retreats, this exclusive-use option provides complete privacy and personalized service across all villas, dining venues, and wellness spaces. It’s one of the few Maldives private islands suitable for small to mid-sized groups, combining luxury, intimacy, and island exclusivity.

#### 2Does COMO Cocoa Island provide event planning and catering services for weddings or special occasions?

Yes. The resort’s dedicated event planning team assists with every detail, from floral arrangements to photography and custom gourmet catering. Each celebration is tailored to the couple’s vision, ensuring a seamless and unforgettable experience.
