# Accommodation

COMO Cocoa Island's 33 overwater villas range from one to three bedrooms, each taking its graceful curves from traditional Maldivian dhoni boats. Arranged along a wooden boardwalk above the lagoon, the villas are spacious and contemporary, balancing open views across the water with a sense of seclusion. From your private terrace, you can step off and swim in the sea.

### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

#### 1Does COMO Cocoa Island offer overwater villas?

Yes. COMO Cocoa Island features luxurious overwater villas that sit above a crystal-clear turquoise lagoon. Inspired by traditional Maldivian dhoni boats, each villa offers panoramic ocean views and direct lagoon access, making it ideal for a romantic Maldives getaway or a luxury island escape.

#### 2Are there villas suitable for families or larger groups?

Yes. COMO Cocoa Island offers family-friendly villas in the Maldives, including the Dhoni Loft Water Villas, Two-Bedroom COMO Sunrise Villa, and Three-Bedroom COMO Sunset Villa. These spacious overwater accommodations are ideal for families and small groups, featuring generous living areas, large sun decks, and direct lagoon access.

#### 3Do all accommodations include a private pool?

Not all villas have private pools. Most offer direct lagoon access for a natural infinity-pool experience. However, select villas — including the Pool Villas, Two-Bedroom COMO Sunrise Villa, and Three-Bedroom COMO Sunset Villa — feature private plunge pools for added relaxation and privacy.

#### 4Do the villas feature private terraces with direct lagoon access?

Yes. Every villa at COMO Cocoa Island includes a private sun terrace that opens directly onto the lagoon, perfect for swimming, snorkeling, or sunbathing in total privacy.

#### 5Are the rooms equipped with modern amenities such as air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. All villas are equipped with air conditioning, high-speed Wi-Fi, flat-screen TVs, and premium bedding, combining modern comfort with COMO Cocoa Island’s signature understated luxury.
