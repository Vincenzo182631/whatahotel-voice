# Experiences at COMO Cocoa Island

Our private island sits in spectacularly clear waters, encircled by live coral reefs and serene sandbars — a dreamy Maldives experience where you can dive, fish, or watch dolphins play while you explore the atoll on a sunset cruise.

Explore COMO Compass — your guide to our iconic experiences and curated resort adventures, designed to enrich every moment of your stay.
