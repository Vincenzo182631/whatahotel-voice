# Best Hotel in Prague for an Unforgettable Experience

Andaz Prague, a lifestyle hotel nestled in the iconic Sugar Palace, sits right in the heart of Prague’s historical center. Stroll through the hotel’s halls and dive into the city’s enchanting tales and legends, or step outside to explore one of Europe’s most captivating metropolises. Just a short walk brings you to the famous Old Town Square and other must-see landmarks. Drift off under the watchful eye of a loyal lion, or chase the stars, just like Princess Libuše, all from the comfort of your beautifully designed room.

## Rooms & Suites

Our rooms and suites feature an inspiring blend of local heritage with contemporary creativity and precise detailing for a luxurious and comfortable stay with stunning views of the historic architecture surrounding the hotel.

## DINING

View All Dining

### ZEM Restaurant

ZEM is an avant-garde Czech bistro, contrasting the aesthetics of Prague's traditional coffee houses with 1920s retro-futuristic Czech art, literature and engineering. The menu is a creative blend of Czech produce with occasional hints of Japanese flavors, crafted from a theatrical kitchen stage of charcoal and steam.

Details + Reservations

### Breakfast

Start your day with a balanced breakfast. Enjoy a relaxing atmosphere, while enjoying a wide selection of delicious specialties from international and Czech cuisine.

Details + Reservations

### MEZ Bar

Just as the field border (from the Czech word, “mez”) is where a farmer rests, refreshes and meets with others after a hard day at work, MEZ bar is where locals and guests engage together and enjoy the exhilarating atmosphere. Our mixologists reconnect with nature to surprise you with perfectly balanced cocktails infused with the ancient power and wisdom of Bohemian mythology.

Details + Reservations

### Room Service

No matter the time of the day, we’ll bring you delicious classics from international and Czech cuisine to fully enjoy in the privacy of your room with our Room Service.

### Le Petit Chef and Friends

Join us for an unforgettable immersive 3D mapping dinner show featuring Pequeña Maria from Spain, Piccolo Luciano from Italy, Chisano Takahiro from Japan, and the delightful (and a bit clumsy) Petit Chef from France. Experience the magic as each character showcases their unique culture and flavors, right on your plate!

## Pets Are Welcome

Andaz Prague gladly welcomes your furry companions to our pet-friendly hotel during your stay. For guests traveling with a dog, we offer a VID (Very Important Dog) service. Service dogs are always welcome at no extra charge. Please note that your furry friend must be supervised at all times and on a leash in our public areas, bars, and restaurants. For your pet’s safety, we require that they are not left unattended in your room. Any damages incurred to the room by the pet are the responsibility of the guest.
