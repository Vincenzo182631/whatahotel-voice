# Scuba Diving

## Let’s discover the underwater world!

The island of Rangiroa is one of the best diving spots in the world.

You can observe dolphins, manta rays, grey sharks and many other species in tropical temperature water all year round.

The Blue Velvet Diving Center is located right at the Hotel Kia Ora Resort & Spa. Diving departures are made right from the hotel’s pontoon.

Famous among divers around the world, Rangiroa diving spots offer clear water and a prolific aquatic fauna. Dives in Rangiroa will bring you unforgettable diving experiences.

We highly recommend to book your dives ahead of time.

For information or reservation, you can contact them at bluevelvetrangiroa@the6passengers.pf.
