# Miki Miki Bar

OVER THE OCEAN

### A MAGICAL PLACE

The Miki Miki Bar is built on stilts over the magnificent lagoon of Rangiroa.

From the terrace, you can enjoy a breathtaking view of the lagoon with the fishes swimming just below your feet.

It is a prime location to admire the colorful lagoon and especially the sunsets, the highlight of the show!

A MOMENT OF RELAXATION

### POLYNESIAN BEER & COCKTAILS

The Miki Miki Bar, is the ideal place for a charming moment of relaxation while tasting a tropical cocktail from the Pacific, the famous local Hinano beer – a beer produced in Tahiti – or one of the cocktails specially designed by the bartenders of the Hotel Kia Ora Resort & Spa.

OPENING HOURS

### COMING TO THE BAR

The bar is open everyday from 9:00 am to 11:00 pm

A light menu is available from 11:00 am to 6:00 pm.

**Proper dress code is mandatory:**

During the day: Bathing suits are not allowed. T-shirts are permitted.

During the night: Bathing suits are not allowed. For men collared shirts are preferred; Tank Tops are not permitted; Bermuda shorts or trousers and shoes are required.
