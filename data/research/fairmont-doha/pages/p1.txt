## Frequently Asked Questions

How can I get to Fairmont Doha from the nearest airport?

The closest airport to Fairmont Doha is Hamad International Airport.

Hamad International Airport (DOH) is located approximately 16.7 miles (27 km) and is 35mins ride from Lusail Marina towards corniche heading to Ras Abu Abboud Express way.

Does Fairmont Doha have an airport shuttle?

Hotel Limousine services are available, please email our Concierge team fairmontconciergeteam@groups.accor.com for current schedules and pricing. Advance reservations are recommended.

Does Fairmont Doha have a spa?

Yes, Fairmont Doha has a full spa. Enjoy signature spa services showcasing age-old Arabian traditions, such as a cleansing Hammam ritual combined with a meditation session to nurture the mind, body, spirit and emotions. Other exceptional hydrotherapy experiences at your disposal include saunas, steam rooms, and an invigorating vitality shower.

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Doha?

Check-in: 3 pm

Checkout: 12 pm

Should you arrive at Fairmont Doha before 3:00 pm, our Guest Services department will be pleased to store your luggage until your room is ready.

Does Fairmont Doha have a swimming pool?

Yes, our pools are available to hotel guests. We also have a private pool reserved for our guests staying in Fairmont Gold rooms.

Is Fairmont Doha pet-friendly?

We don't accommodate pets at this time.
