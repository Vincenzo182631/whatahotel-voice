# Welcome to W Aspen

## Enjoy elevated luxury at our resort in Aspen, Colorado

W Aspen is a luxury resort in Aspen, near Aspen Snowmass, and nestled at the base of one of the world's most fabled mountains, offering access to coveted alpine slopes and hiking trails with stunning 360-degree views of this iconic destination. While offering a playful approach to a renowned location, the hotel features an architecturally striking ambiance and hosts the only year-round rooftop venue...See More in all of Aspen. Additionally, The Sky Residences at W Aspen present ownership opportunities that include access to exclusive, Residence Only WET Deck experiences on the rooftop. Discover our bold point of view on luxury at our hotel near Aspen Mountain and have a truly immersive experience at W Aspen.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Aspen?

The check-in time at W Aspen is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Aspen allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Aspen. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $120.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Aspen?

Off-site valet parking is available at W Aspen. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Aspen have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Aspen has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Aspen booking.

What is the closest airport near W Aspen?

The closest airport near W Aspen is Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE). ASE is located approximately 4.3 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Aspen have an airport shuttle to Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE)?

Yes, W Aspen has an airport shuttle to Aspen-Pitkin County Airport (ASE).

Does W Aspen have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Aspen has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
