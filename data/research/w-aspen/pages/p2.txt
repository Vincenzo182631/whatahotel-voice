# Retreat to our luxury Aspen hotel rooms after a busy day

## Accommodations

W Aspen Hotel features 88 guestrooms, four suites, and one Presidential suite, all offering modern takes on Ute patterns and furniture inspired by the decadence of the ‘70s Aspen “pleasure palaces.” All guest rooms and hotel suites are playfully marked with room plates in a style reminiscent of the pop culture icon Bob Ross, while interiors reference the cozy warmth of Swiss chalet architecture through...See More the use of rustic wood finishes, sloped rooflines and a fireplace-inspired W MixBar. All our luxury hotel rooms in Aspen, Colorado, feature pit and platform lounge seating complete with Ute-inspired accent pillows. Select rooms and suites feature a W design first, with quad-queen bunk beds, which can accommodate up to eight people to cater to a younger adult clientele. These special rooms also offer a wink to the more hedonistic qualities of Aspen’s past. You can also situate yourself in one of the most sought-after addresses in all of Aspen at The Sky Residences at W Aspen.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Aspen have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Aspen have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Aspen?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 8 at W Aspen. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Aspen?

Some rooms at W Aspen permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Aspen have fridges?

No, rooms at W Aspen do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Aspen have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Aspen have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Aspen have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Aspen have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Aspen have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Aspen have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
