### Live Exquisitely at The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm

Featuring 290 guestrooms and suites, the graciously appointed spacious accommodations offer ceiling-to-floor windows and exquisite views of the Palm Jumeirah and the Arabian Sea. The elegant décor offers distinctive style while guests enjoy a tranquil night’s sleep on the feather-soft mattress with Frette linen and down-filled pillows. All rooms boast a dedicated seating area and a wardrobe area with abundant closet and luggage space. All guests also enjoy 24-hour access to the highly-personalized St. Regis Butler Services.
