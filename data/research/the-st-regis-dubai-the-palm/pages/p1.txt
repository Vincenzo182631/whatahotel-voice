# Welcome to The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm

## Live Exquisite at The Best Address on The Palm Jumeirah

The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm has been honored with the prestigious Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star award, while our St. Regis Spa has achieved a remarkable Forbes Travel Guide Four-Star Rating for 2026. Experience the splendor of the city from our business hotel in Dubai, perfectly situated in the heart of the iconic Palm Jumeirah. Savor handcrafted menus at a variety of dining destinations, and rejuvenate at the St. Regis Spa. Relish the signature St. Regis Butler Service, available any time, day or night. Conveniently located, our luxury hotel is one monorail away from discovering Dubai's main attractions.

## KYMA Beach Club

Take a journey to Greece on the sandy shores of Palm West Beach. Offering a touch of luxury with access to the sparkling Arabian Gulf, KYMA Beach Dubai, is inspired by the multicultural yet vibrant spirit of the Mediterranean and the lively vibes of Greece. This beach club and restaurant invites guests above 21 years to a refined fare in a chic setting with a menu serving authentic Greek dishes, seafood, grilled meats, wraps and signature beverages. Complimentary luxury transportation is available to and from the hotel for in-house guests and bookings can be made through the Concierge team.

## The Best Address in the Palm Jumeirah

The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm is strategically situated, providing seamless access to the Palm's premier landmarks, including Palm Jumeirah Mall, the Palm Monorail, and The View at The Palm, a 240-meter observation deck. The hotel boasts a world-class rooftop dining venue, St. Regis Gardens, which hosts Michelin-starred restaurants and celebrity chef concepts. This open terrace embodies The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm's dedication to redefining the culinary scene, offering luxurious, innovative, and celebratory gastronomic experiences.

Learn More

### Sustainability

We are proud to highlight our commitment to sustainability through certifications and eco-friendly initiatives. Our achievements include Green Key Certification (UAE Initiative) and Green Globes Certification (US Initiative).

In-house efforts emphasize water conservation with sensor taps in staff areas and water restrictors in guest bathrooms. Energy efficiency is supported by systems like the Guestroom Management System, optimizing air conditioning, lighting, and curtains. Public areas feature automated lighting controls, and waste is segregated using color-coded bins. Used cooking oil is recycled through third-party services.

We offer electric car charging stations and eco-car limousine services. Our sustainable practices include eco-certified cleaning products, a plastic-free system, FSC-certified paper, and 300ml refillable amenities. Locally sourced glass water bottles are provided for rooms and breakfast. These initiatives demonstrate our dedication to a greener, sustainable future.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm is Al Maktoum International Airport (DWC). DWC is located approximately 24.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Dubai, The Palm has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
