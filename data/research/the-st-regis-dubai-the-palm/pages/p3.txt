### Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service has been a hallmark of our legacy for over a century. From a perfectly pressed suit to a thoughtful last-minute gift, no request is too small. Available at any time of day, our butlers ensure that every preference is understood and fulfilled with impeccable care - an enduring symbol of personalized service at St. Regis Hotels worldwide.

### The Evening Ritual

Champagne sabering began in the 1700s with Napoleon's cavalry, who celebrated victories by dramatically opening bottles. The Astor family later adopted the ritual to mark the transition from day to night. Today, we honor this timeless tradition daily at 6:45 pm at St. Regis Bar, Lobby Level - welcoming the night with elegance and celebration.

### KYMA Beach Club

Drawing inspiration from Greek life, KYMA is set to enthrall guests with authentic flavors on the sandy shores of Palm Jumeirah. This adult-friendly beach club and restaurant gives complimentary access to in-house guests over the age of 21 while providing shuttle service to/from the hotel.
