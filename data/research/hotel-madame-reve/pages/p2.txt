# ROOMS, SUITES & PENTHOUSE

With its 83 rooms and suites, the hotel Madame Rêve offers a unique visitor experience in the heart of the City of Light. Like an invitation to travel in the historical Paris, enjoy one of the 54 balconies and marvel at the unforgettable views. With carefully selected furniture, tailor-made fabrics and precious woods, each room and suite serve as a true urban cocoon for its guests.

This singular collection also includes a 130m² Penthouse, designed like a Parisian apartment suspended above the city. A free-flowing space to be arranged as desired, offering a breathtaking view of Paris’s iconic landmarks: the Centre Pompidou on one side, and the Saint-Eustache Church on the other.

A wraparound terrace bathed in a unique light.

### Exceptional View

Wake up facing the most beautiful Parisian monuments. The rooms and suites present their guests a view like no other in Paris. An exclusive opportunity to capture the essence of the city from your room. A haven of peacefulness in open sky offering unseen views over iconic landmarks.

### Paris View

Nestled under the Hotel’s glass roof, the rooms and suites open onto a breathtaking view of Paris and its urban landscape. From sunrise to sunset, Paris reveals itself before your eyes. Experience timeless moments from these rooms and suites bathed in natural light.

### Garden View

The spacious rooms and suites offer an outdoor space overlooking the suspended garden, designed as a haven of serenity in the heart of the city. Between the sky and the lush greenery, guests enjoy the quietness of the courtyard and appreciate their shaded terrace throughout the day.
