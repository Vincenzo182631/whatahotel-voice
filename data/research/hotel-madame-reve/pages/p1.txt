# Madame Rêve: A 5-Star Luxury Hotel in the Heart of Paris

Within the iconic precincts of the Poste du Louvre, Madame Rêve redefines luxury hotels in Paris. Combining contemporary elegance and timeless refinement, this 5-star hotel offers an exceptional experience, between historical heritage and French art de vivre. With 83 sumptuous rooms and suites, gourmet restaurants and a green rooftop overlooking the City of Light, Madame Rêve is a must for a prestigious stay in Paris.

## A luxury hotel in the heart of the 1st Arrondissement

Ideally located in the historic center of Paris, a stone’s throw from the Louvre and the Palais Royal, Madame Rêve is the perfect base from which to explore the capital’s treasures. Whether you’re on a romantic getaway or a business trip, the hotel offers privileged access to emblematic monuments such as Saint-Eustache church and the quays of the Seine.

## Elegant Rooms and Suites With Views of Paris

Madame Rêve offers 82 rooms and suites, the majority of which feature terraces or balconies offering spectacular views of Parisian rooftops and iconic monuments. Ranging from 28 m² to 80 m², each space is designed as a luxurious cocoon, dressed in walnut woodwork, warm velvet and bespoke zelliges. Ultimate comfort is combined with refined design, in a setting inspired by Art Nouveau.

## A Culinary Experience Signed Madame Rêve

The establishment is distinguished by its two renowned restaurants:

Kitchen, a 300 m² space where old-world Parisian charm is expressed through sumptuous décor and contemporary French cuisine. Generous dishes are served all day long in a spectacular setting with eight-metre high ceilings.

La Plume, a panoramic rooftop restaurant, offers a menu with influences from near and far. Its tree-lined terrace and breathtaking views of Saint-Eustache church and the Eiffel Tower make it an exceptional venue for timeless lunches and dinners.

## A Unique Rooftop and Hanging Garden

A veritable haven of peace suspended above Paris, the ROOF rooftop covers 1,000 m². Nestled between cherry trees and lush vegetation, it offers unparalleled views of the city’s most beautiful monuments. Reserved during the day for hotel guests, in the evening it becomes a convivial place open to all, perfect for sipping cocktails while admiring the sunset over the capital.

## Well-being and relaxation in a refined setting

Madame Rêve also offers an exclusive 150 m² wellness area featuring two treatment cabins, a sauna with views over Paris and a fitness room equipped with the latest technology. All in an intimate, soothing atmosphere, for a moment of relaxation after a hectic day in Paris.

## A Place to Live and a Responsible Approach

More than just a classic hotel, Madame Rêve is a lively and accessible place, promoting exchange and conviviality. The establishment is part of an eco-responsible approach, with demanding environmental labels, the production of part of its hot water by solar panels, and particular attention paid to the ecological impact of its services.

## Why choose Madame Rêve?

To stay at the Madame Rêve hotel is to experience the excellence of Parisian luxury with a touch of soul and poetry. Between the prestige of its services, the history of its building and the modernity of its approach, this 5-star hotel is a genuine invitation to savor the French art of living, right in the heart of Paris.
