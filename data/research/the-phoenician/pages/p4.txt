## The Phoenician Spa

Prepare for an unforgettable day and indulge with a complete spa experience at our luxury spa hotel in Scottsdale, Arizona, starting with our sauna or vitality pool and ending in our post-treatment lounge...See More or at our rooftop pool. ## Plan Your Personal Spa Ritual

Please allow yourself time to indulge in a complete spa experience during your visit to our spa resort in Scottsdale, AZ. To improve the benefits of both your treatment and overall well-being, begin your visit with preparatory hot-and-cold contrast hydrotherapy, for a duration of 30 minutes or less.

## Rooftop Pool

Providing dramatic views of both the resort and Camelback Mountain, the rooftop pool at our Scottsdale spa and resort serves as a tranquil retreat following your spa service. Enhance your experience by renting a private cabana, or relaxing in the lounge area by the fireplace. A healthy and delicious menu is also available during the day.

More Spa Services & Amenities
