# Settle into luxury hotel rooms in Scottsdale, AZ

## Accommodations

Curate a distinctive resort experience infused with contemporary elegance that transcends the everyday. The Phoenician, a Luxury Collection Resort, Scottsdale offers distinctively appointed hotel rooms,...See More beautifully fashioned casitas and luxury hotel suites in Scottsdale, Arizona. Elevate your stay in one of our distinguished two-bedroom hotel suites as you soak in the splendor of custom décor with finishes that reflect the rich tones and warm hues of the Sonoran Desert. Then, lounge in the soft abundance of Italian linen and let the retreat-like ambiance transform your well-being. Whether you stay in a room, suite, or casita, our luxury hotel in Scottsdale, AZ, is dedicated to making each moment a memorable experience.See Less

### Phoenician Suite

Perfect for hosting a meeting or small gathering, the Phoenician Suite also features a bedroom with king bed, a second entertainment center, a sitting area, walk-in closet and marble bath with separate tub and shower.

## 2-Bedroom Presidential Suite

Our spacious, luxury accommodations in Scottsdale offers breathtaking views of tree-lined fairways, mountains and the city beyond.

## In-Room Dining

Room Service is available for breakfast, lunch and dinner - or even a light snack.

## Casitas

Our casita luxury accommodations in Scottsdale, Arizona, are a collection of 12, two-story buildings featuring 119 guestrooms and suites. Casitas are opposite the main building, separated by The Phoenician Pools and Necklace Lake, each offering doorstep parking. Locations and views vary. Guests may either call for complimentary transportation to various resort areas or anticipate a brief stroll to property venues, including an approximate 300-yard walk to the main building.
