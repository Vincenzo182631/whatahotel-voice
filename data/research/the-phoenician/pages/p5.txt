## Desert Botanical Garden

Our hotel is located near the Desert Botanical Garden in Phoenix, AZ, which features the world’s finest collection of arid-land plants from deserts around the world. The Garden has more than 50,000 accessioned plants throughout its five thematic trails, which illustrate topics such as conservation, desert living, plants and people of the Sonoran Desert, and desert wildflowers.

### Sedona and Jerome Wine Tasting Tour

Guests leave from the resort for a 1.5 hour drive to beautiful Sedona and embark on an invigorating 2-hour hike at Red Rock State Park. The afternoon is spent enjoying a full picnic lunch (with choice of options) overlooking the majestic scenery. Next, it’s on to the historic mining town of Jerome, built in the early 1800s, and now home to artists, wine makers and ghost sightings. Guests visit 2 tasting...See More rooms and sample wines from Arizona’s Caduceus Cellars and Cellar 433 before returning to Scottsdale. The duration of the trip lasts approximately 9 hours and costs $370 per person.See Less

### Desert Off Road Excursion

Be picked up in a 4×4 off road vehicle by your cowboy guide and experience the beauty of the pristine Sonoran Desert. This tour is the perfect blend for groups that are interested not only in an adventure but also appreciates the cultural background and history of the desert.

### Humbeer!

Take the desert and a local brewery by storm with this mission called “Humbeer”! The adventure and amazement begins by climbing an old mining trail to 4,000 feet inside a boulder canyon with amazing rock formations and abundant desert landscape. This mission is not complete without a stop at a local brewery to sample some of the best beers Arizona has to offer.

### Horseback Riding

Hop in the saddle, and ride into the heart of the majestic the Sonoran Desert. We offer trails over hilly terrain that dip down into arroyos and climb up steep embankments or ride on the old cavalry, outlaw and Indian trails and laugh along with the “tall tales” told by our cowboy wranglers!

### Culinary Culture

Scottsdale is a foodie’s dream destination. Let us show you some of the hidden gems that Scottsdale has to offer. You will enjoy demonstrations, first hand tales and of course great food samples from some of the specialty shops that make Old Town Scottsdale so delectably unique.

### Flatwater River Float

Raft through the Desert! Our professionally trained guides will take you for a fun filled float down the beautiful Rio Verde or Salt River, surrounded by rough and rugged desert mountains. The float offers mild ripples combined with spectacular desert scenery and maybe a glimpse of Arizona wildlife along the way.

### Desert Mountain Biking

Enjoy the views that span the Sonoran Desert as you cruise the single track trails atop a high performance bike. The standard tour is designed so individuals of all ability levels will enjoy themselves; however, we can offer exclusive advanced rider tours as well.

### Kayaking

Flowing from the mountains in Northern Arizona, the Salt River is captured by four dams and released for use by Phoenicians and farmers year-round. While kayaking on this Class 1 river you are surrounded by spectacular, unobstructed 70-mile views into four different mountain ranges.

### Indoor Kart Racing

Indoor Kart racing is like no other motor sport! Sitting just inches off the ground, you feel every movement and turn. The tracks offer tight turns, fast sweepers and high-speed straight sections, just like a real F1 track. Safety barriers and trained course marshals keep the action fast, fun and exciting!

### Art Culture

Become acquainted with some of the Southwest’s nationally known and emerging local and regional artists on a personal tour of their studios. Learn how they are influenced by the desert and Southwestern culture, whether in glass making, pottery, mosaics, oil, water colors or printmaking. This small group tour is conducive to individual, personal interaction with each artist.
