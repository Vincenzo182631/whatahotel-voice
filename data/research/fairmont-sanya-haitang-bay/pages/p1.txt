## EXPERIENCE TRADITIONAL _culture_

Fairmont Sanya Haitang Bay is a luxury hotel that enjoys beachfront views across the renowned WuZhiZhou Island. Located in Haitang Bay, the hotel boasts fine craftsmanship on rare wood and is surrounded by the 1200-meter Yun River, contributing to a sense of tranquility, romance and leisure.

Fairmont Sanya Haitang Bay combines global hospitality and local rituals to offer guests refreshing luxury, imaginative food and drinks from all over the world, creative recreation and unparalleled wellness that will present a warm and delicate exploration journey for guests in this lush tropical coastal paradise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fairmont Gold?

Fairmont Gold is our exclusive offer for travelers seeking higher levels of personalized service. Upon arrival at the resort, guests will take the exclusive dragon boat to the Fairmont Gold wing and enjoy a series of warm and customized experiences.

Does Fairmont Sanya Haitang Bay have a gym/fitness center?

The 24-hour gym is equipped with aerobic machines and strength-trainingequipment. in addition, ourrecreation team customize diverse activities, such as golf putting, dragonboat riding on Yun River and kayaking, to bring more vitality and pleasureto moments staying at Fairmont Sanya Haitang Bay.

Does Fairmont Sanya Haitang Bay have a swimming pool?

There is an irregular jade-bottomed swimming pool available for guests at the hotel in the middle of the gardens for the ultimate relaxation.
