## Things to do

### Fort Canning Park

River Valley Rd, Singapore, 179037

Fort Canning Park
River Valley Rd, Singapore, 179037
An iconic hilltop landmark in Singapore, Fort Canning Park has played witness to several of Singapore’s key historical milestones including the decision made over the surrender of Singapore in 1942. Expect interesting sights including ancient artefacts, underground bunkers used during World War Two called Battle Box as well as the Spice Garden that is full of local spices.

### Singapore Botanic Gardens

1 Cluny Rd, Singapore, 259569

The Singapore Botanic Gardens is an 82-hectare tropical garden located at the outskirt of Singapore’s main shopping belt. The only tropical botanic garden on the UNESCO World Heritage list, the garden spans a distance of two and a half kilometres and houses more than 10,000 species of flora.

### National Gallery Singapore

1 St Andrew's Road, Singapore, 178957

National Gallery Singapore in a leading visual arts institution, which oversees the world's largest public collection of Singapore and Southeast Asian modern art. Situated at the birthplace of modern Singapore, in the heart of the Civic District, the gallery is housed in two national monuments - City Hall and the former Supreme Court - that have been beautifully restored and transformed into this exciting 64,000 square-metre venue.

### Gardens by the Bay

18 Marina Gardens Dr, Singapore, 018953

Singapore is a city full of man-made waterfalls, with the first man-made waterfall built in Singapore in 1970. Today, you’ll find the world’s second tallest indoor waterfall at the Cloud Forest, located at Gardens by the Bay. The waterfall forms the centrepiece of the main conservatory.
