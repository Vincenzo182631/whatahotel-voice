## Publico Ristorante & Garden

A gourmet playground by the waterfront, Publico Ristorante and Garden brings a little slice of Italy to Singapore, with an emphasis on authentic and evocative Italian flavours. With its scenic riverside setting, the 220 seat restaurant and bar emulate the vibrancy of an Italian trattoria, where you can enjoy contemporary Italian fare highlighting provenance and seasonality.

Note: Kitchen closes 1 hour before closing.

## Ishi

Perched on the second floor of the hotel, Ishi, loosely translated as "Stone", presents Japanese fine dining over an elegant sushi counter. A true Zen approach to dining, Ishi provides an intimate setting for up to 26 diners to enjoy an extraordinary cultural and gastronomic combination of creative cuisine and impeccable service. For reservations, call 65 9829 8239.

Ishi is third party owned and independent of InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay.

## CODA

An intimate 15 seat avant garde cocktail bar, CODA immerses guests in a world of meticulously crafted cocktails, where Asian spirits take centre stage, free from brand names or distractions. With an elegant Milan designed space, deep melodic house, and a breathtaking free standing monoblock bar, every sip is a journey through time, mood, and emotion. CODA is third party owned and independent of InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay.
