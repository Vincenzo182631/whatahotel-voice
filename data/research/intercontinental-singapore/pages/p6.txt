## FAQs for InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay

Does InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay have a pool?

Outdoor Pool

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay for hotel guests.

InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay provides complimentary self-parking for one entry per day. Charges apply for subsequent entries.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay has 5 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay?

Check-in at InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay is conveniently located near Singapore Changi Airport (SIN), approximately 13.61 miles away (21.9 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay?

InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Fort Canning Park - 1.24 miles away (2 kilometers)
- Singapore Botanic Gardens - 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers)
- National Gallery Singapore - 1.3 miles away (2.1 kilometers)
- Gardens by the Bay - 2.73 miles away (4.4 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Singapore Robertson Quay puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
