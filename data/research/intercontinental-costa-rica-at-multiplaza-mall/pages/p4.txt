## Swim and Relax at Three Glorious Pools

Our three heated outdoor pools provide a refreshing respite from busy days. Swim or lounge under the warm Costa Rican sun amid the lavish greenery and swaying palms of our gardens, and satisfy your cravings with poolside beverage and light-menu service.

## The Club Tower: Exclusive Access

Enjoy access to our exclusive Club Tower, our secluded level with a private entrance reserved for our most loyal guests. For those traveling on government business or corporate travel, the Club Tower provides the ultimate in discretion and privacy, allowing entry only to those who share this privilege.

## ONE DAY PASS by PRESENCE Spa & Wellness Center

Enjoy 3 warm pools and a jacuzzi, access to humid areas from 9 am to 5 pm, one locker included for storage, access to the gym installation, and 30% off any Spa Treatment (Face Massage and body treatments).
