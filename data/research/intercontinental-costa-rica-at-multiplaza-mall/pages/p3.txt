# InterContinental Costa Rica at Multiplaza MallDining

Embark on a culinary journey across five distinctive restaurants. From Japanese artistry and Italian warmth to premium cuts and local flavors, each dining experience is rooted in craft and shaped by Costa Rica’s vibrant gastronomic culture.

**In-room dining hours:** 6:00 AM - 11:00 PM

## The Highlights

1. Omakase at Nau: Entrust the Chef

2. Saturdays Belong to the Fish Market

3. The Art of the Business Lunch

4. Evenings Come Alive at Zambra Nights

### Omakase at Nau: Entrust the Chef

Beginning this October, Nau introduces an intimate omakase experience. Each course is composed in the moment by the chef, guided by the finest seasonal catch, an unhurried dialogue of precision, texture and trust. Reservations recommended.

### Saturdays Belong to the Fish Market

Every Saturday, the day's freshest catch takes center stage. Choose from an abundant display of seafood, prepared to your liking and served with the ease of a long weekend lunch, an invitation to linger with family and friends.

### The Art of the Business Lunch

Monday through Friday, The Factory and Pimento set the table for productive afternoons. An executive menu and a fresh salad bar offer a refined, efficient pause, ideal for closing a deal or simply reclaiming the middle of your day.

### Evenings Come Alive at Zambra Nights

As the city settles into evening, Zambra Nights sets a different rhythm. Live music, artfully crafted cocktails, and an atmosphere that is spirited yet sophisticated, the most elegant way to let the night unfold in San José.

### The Market

**Location: Lobby Area**

In our high-concept, 24-hour casual version of Costa Rica’s _sodas_, or food stalls, we invite you to build your own meal, from pizza or gourmet burger to breakfast omelet, and more. Our expert chefs craft it all with tantalizing local ingredients in our gleaming exhibition kitchen. Savor your creation on our terrace alongside the pool and gardens.

Executive Chef

### Marcial Cañas

The culinary proposal at the Real Intercontinental Hotel San José Costa Rica is led by Executive Chef Marcial Cañas, originally from El Salvador. With over 30 years of experience in the culinary industry and managing services in five-star hotels, he shares his vision: “My results-oriented approach is based on staff training, creativity, and excellence to stand out in demanding culinary environments,” says the Chef.

Specialized in international fine dining, Marcial Cañas has worked in Europe, the United States, and Mexico, accumulating experience in restaurants, banquets, special events, kosher cuisine, openings, and concept creation. His commitment to the highest standards of quality and food safety ensures an unparalleled culinary experience.

### Private Dining

Pimento Restaurant includes an intimate private dining room to celebrate special occasions for up to 10 guests.
