# InterContinental Costa Rica at Multiplaza MallDestination

Step into the rhythm of Escazú, where tradition and cosmopolitan energy meet. Explore local markets, nearby coffee estates, art galleries, and world-class shopping at Multiplaza, all just moments from the calm of our urban resort.

### Unspoiled Beaches

With two gorgeous shores – on the Pacific and on the Caribbean – and with a coast shaped by dramatic volcanic rock, Costa Rica is a beach lover’s paradise. Coated with sugary sand and kissed by white surf that kicks up from crystalline waters, our beaches (or playas in the local language) are pristine and soothing. Many of the most beautiful line the Pacific coast. At Playa Tamarindo, fiery sunsets close out each day while at Playa Santa Teresa, surfers head out to find the perfect wave.

## Where Music Meets Culture

InterContinental San José, at the Heart of Live Entertainment. Perfectly positioned beside the national stadium, InterContinental San José becomes the natural gathering place when international stars take the stage—where culture, music, and refined hospitality meet seamlessly.

## After the Applause

When the encore ends, the evening continues. On concert nights, The Market extends its hours until midnight, welcoming guests to unwind, dine, and savor the moment for an After Concert Dining in a relaxed, contemporary setting.

### Dramatic Volcanoes

Costa Rica boasts some of the world’s most magnificent active volcanoes. Each one strikes a dramatic pose, rising tall above lush rainforest and cloud forests. Arenal Volcano, overlooking the splendor of Lake Arenal, is the most popular. The thermal hot springs that it feeds provide soothing soaks to visitors. Closer to San Jose, the Paos Volcano provides the rare opportunity to drive up to its rim, giving you sweeping views of its steaming mile-wide crater.

## Discover San Jose

### La Paz Waterfall Gardens

Explore the captivating beauty of La Paz Waterfall Gardens, a hidden gem in Costa Rica's rainforest near our San Jose hotel. Engage with nature through cascading waterfalls, vibrant blooms, and exotic wildlife. Traverse hanging bridges, embark on guided wildlife tours, and relish the seamless blend of opulence and natural beauty. La Paz Waterfall Gardens invites you to experience the enchanting landscape of Costa Rica in a truly extraordinary way.

### Rainforest Eco-Adventures

The lush rainforests of Costa Rica are home to an astonishing 5% of the world’s biodiversity. Dense jungles, from Tortuguero National Park on the Caribbean coast to Manuel Antonio National Park on the Pacific, greet eco-conscious explorers with zip-lining, walkways through treetop canopies, and wildlife safaris by land and by river. For a thrilling adventure, embark on a whitewater rafting excursion along the Pacuare River, perhaps spotting toucans and scarlet macaws on the water’s edge.

### Hacienda Alsacia

Spread across almost 600 acres, the Hacienda Alsacia was born from the rich soils of the Paos Volcano. Located on the mountain’s slopes, this traditional finca – or coffee farm – is today the global agricultural headquarters of Starbucks. The focus here is on community support and sustainable farming, and a fascinating tour of its fields and facilities reveals how farmers are breeding new varieties of arabica seeds not only for Starbucks, but for coffee makers around the world.

### Museo de Oro

The Pre-Colombian Gold Museum near our San Jose hotel chronicles the story of Costa Rica’s indigenous peoples. This fascinating collection showcases almost 700 precious gold pieces, as well as ceramic and stone works curated from throughout the country. Its priceless repository serves as a springboard for exploration into the region’s settlement, social structure, metallurgy, iconography, gender, and environment, creating a richly layered view of both the past and present.

### Multiplaza Mall

The paragon of design and entertainment is right next door. San Jose's Multiplaza Mall caters to the well-heeled in the exclusive Escazú district. Find the latest fashions, peruse department stores, catch a movie, or pick up sundries at the supermercado.

### National Theater

Costa Rica’s Teatro Nacional is a spellbinding venue, an architectural masterpiece built in 1897 in grand neoclassical style. Tours of the theater begin every hour on the hour. Guides share stories of its construction and the magnificent artwork on its walls and lead you into rooms typically reserved for staff and performers. After, settle into the theater’s Alma de Café to sample delicious crepes, quiche, cappuccino, and an ambience the evokes an early 20th-century Vienna coffeehouse.
