An Exploration of the Elements
Experiences
With its 124 miles of breathtaking coastline and an archipelago of more than 90 pristine islands, diving in the Red Sea, Saudi Arabia, offers dream-like beauty and endless discovery. Amid this striking setting, Nujuma is a connection to the sea, the earth and the stars, tying stories from the region’s past to experiences in the present moment.
Pools of Peace and Possibility
At Nujuma, the art of water is reimagined. Our collection of swimming pools invites every guest to their own rhythm of relaxation.
A Beachfront Escape on the Red Sea
The pristine beaches of the Red Sea set the stage for an experience of effortless luxury and natural wonder. Here, endless azure waters and breathtaking views invite you to bask in the warmth of the sun while enjoying the perfect balance of serene solitude and vibrant activity.
Kids and Teen’s Club
Set between the sea and the stars, Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, is a vibrant playground for young explorers. Designed for guests aged 12 and under, our Kids and Teens Club offers engaging activities that spark creativity and foster a deep connection with nature, history, and local culture.
Celestial Wellness Package
Restore balance through a curated wellness journey inspired by the rhythms of the sea and stars. Enjoy spa experiences, private yoga or Pilates sessions, wellness classes, stargazing, and signature experiences designed to reconnect mind, body, and nature.
Neyrah Spa
Inspired by Saudi nomads who followed the stars, Neyrah Spa guides guests on a journey of self-discovery. Blending global expertise with regional roots, it features AlUla-sourced Moringa oil, and Arabian design.

Deep Sea Discovery
Galaxea Dive Center
Encompassing an archipelago of more than 90 untouched islands and surrounded by the world’s fourth-largest barrier reef, the Red Sea is an environment like no other. Its nearly 11,000 square miles offer an opportunity for once-in-a-lifetime encounters with hundreds of species of fish, technicolor corals, endangered hawksbill turtles, dolphins, stingrays and an abundance of other marine life. Led by the experts at Galaxea Dive Center, guests can learn how to dive, earn a certification and explore the depths of the sea through heritage, conservation and other diving excursions.

Fostering Connection to Culture and Community
Conservation House
A space to expand knowledge and community, the Conservation House is a bridge to Saudi Arabia’s marine life, history and heritage. Through hands-on activities hosted by resident sustainability, nature, astronomy and cultural experts, guests of all ages develop a deep connection with the destination. Set within an indoor-outdoor pavilion crafted with natural materials, the Conservation House features ample space for creative pursuits, a pantry, a boutique and multiple lounge areas.

Exploring the World Above
The stars guided nomads as they explored the Arabian desert and seas, and their skills and stories have endured for generations. Today, the star-filled sky above the Red Sea is a vital part of the Nujuma experience. Led by our resident astronomy expert, who marries his passion for stargazing and storytelling, guests are introduced to a whole new world where tradition, science and mystery come together.

Neyrah Spa
ARRIVE CURIOUS, DEPART ENLIGHTENED
Just as the nomadic tribes of Saudi Arabia relied on the stars for guidance, Neyrah Spa accompanies each guest on their journey of self-discovery. Bringing talent and traditions from around the globe, Neyrah Spa retains its regional roots in subtle ways: the enchanting scent of oud, Alula Peregrina Oil and intricately patterned Arabian doors. Designed to be a voyage rather than a stop, bespoke treatments take a holistic approach by encouraging guests to reflect on the past, live in the present and step into the future with clarity and energy. Rustically refined and inspired by nature, Neyrah Spa is a free-flowing space with sea-facing treatment rooms and outdoor treatment cabins; private and shared yoga pavilions, vitality and lap pools; hammam and rhassoul; a fitness center and the Neyrah Lounge where guests can connect over healing tea elixirs and super-food smoothies.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have a fitness center on-site?
Yes. Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.
The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and yes, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
Does Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have a pool on-site?
Yes, Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.
Does Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve have an on-site spa?
Yes, Neyrah Spa is located on-site at Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve.
