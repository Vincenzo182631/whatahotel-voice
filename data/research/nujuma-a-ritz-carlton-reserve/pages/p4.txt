Neyrah Spa
ARRIVE CURIOUS, DEPART ENLIGHTENED
Just as the nomadic tribes of Saudi Arabia relied on the stars for guidance, Neyrah Spa accompanies each guest on their journey of self-discovery. Bringing talent and traditions from around the globe, Neyrah Spa retains its regional roots in subtle ways: the enchanting scent of oud, Alula Peregrina Oil and intricately patterned Arabian doors. Designed to be a voyage rather than a stop, bespoke treatments take a holistic approach by encouraging guests to reflect on the past, live in the present and step into the future with clarity and energy. Rustically refined and inspired by nature, Neyrah Spa is a free-flowing space with sea-facing treatment rooms and outdoor treatment cabins; private and shared yoga pavilions, vitality and lap pools; hammam and rhassoul; a fitness center and the Neyrah Lounge where guests can connect over healing tea elixirs and super-food smoothies.

Voya Organic Beauty - Secrets From the Sea
VOYA, an organic beauty brand, is built on harnessing the powers of the sea, sustainably. Incorporating minerals and vitamins derived from hand-harvested seaweed, their extensive product range features anti-aging, conditioning and restorative properties. VOYA products are featured throughout the Neyrah Spa experience, and guests are invited to visit the VOYA Alchemy Bar to create bespoke blends for their spa journey.
A Ritual As Rare as You With Natura Bissé
Your skin holds a story as rare and remarkable as the place you find yourself in. At Natura Bissé, we thoughtfully craft each ritual to uncover what is most authentic to you. Every touch is tailored. Every moment immersive. Because true beauty begins with understanding. Savor a sensory experience that is as distinct and extraordinary as your skin. Inviting, serene, and deeply personal.
From the Land. For the Skin
Our ingredients are grown, harvested, and crafted in Saudi Arabia, shaped by the richness of the land. The Peregrina tree thrives in harsh, rocky terrain, developing powerful actives to survive. This natural resilience e gives it a rare ingredient, rich in skin-repairing properties. Virgin Peregrina oil helps protect skin cell DNA, supporting renewal and slowing visible aging.
A gift from nature, refined at Neyrah Spa.
Serene Spaces
Royal Neyrah Suite
Designed for couples seeking to rediscover and reignite their connection, the Royal Neyrah Suite offers a private space to embark on your healing journey together. In addition to hosting our full menu of experiences, the suite features a Jacuzzi, bathtub and steam shower.
Hammam
The perfect precursor to your Neyrah Spa journey, the hammam offers a head-to-toe experience that invites pause and presence with every step. The body is prepared with immersive heat and then cleansed, exfoliated and nourished, leaving you restored, relaxed and ready to receive the healing that awaits.
Outdoor Treatment Cabins
Named for the past, present and future, our three outdoor treatment cabins offer semi-private spaces overlooking the Red Sea. Set amid the spa gardens, the Sima, Hayat and Afaq cabins are designed to host massages and other select experiences.
Yoga Pavilion
Surrounded by our lush gardens, the two private yoga pavilions provide a space to connect with nature and your practice. Yogis of all levels are invited to join our yoga experts for an invigorating sunrise session or a calming sunset stretch.
Neyrah Spa Journeys - A Path to Restoration and Renewal
Embark on a transformative journey where balance is restored, the spirit renewed, and self-connection deepened. The Silent World by Natura Bissé detoxifies and revitalizes with exfoliation, an aloe vera wrap, a tailored massage, and an illuminating facial. A Voyage to Neyrah by VOYA, inspired by the resilience of Carnation Coral, harnesses sea minerals and essential oils in a soothing salt bath, Hammam, and restorative massage. The All-Encompassing blends sound healing, yoga, a spiced mud bath, Hammam rituals, and a customized facial and massage, concluding with a nourishing meal at Affia. For a truly bespoke escape, the Sailor Made Journey allows expert therapists to craft a personalized wellness experience. At Neyrah Spa, each journey is an invitation to surrender to tranquility, uncover inner harmony, and embrace self-care.
Wellness Rooted in Nature
Embark on a transformative wellness journey rooted in integrative nutrition and holistic living. Through personalized coaching, tailored nutrition plans, and natural wellness practices, this experience cultivates vitality, mindful weight management, and sustainable lifestyle enhancements. Expert-led programs are designed to inspire long-term well-being, nourishing the body, balancing the mind, and enriching the soul. With a bespoke approach to health, it fosters meaningful, lasting change through a deep connection to both self and nature.
