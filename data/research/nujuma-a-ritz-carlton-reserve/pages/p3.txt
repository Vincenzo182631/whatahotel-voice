Discover Culinary Cultures at our Red Sea Restaurants
Dining
Centuries ago, the world came together to exchange goods and ideas in Saudi Arabia. Today, our local culinary tradition reflects that. Fine, simple ingredients are brought to life through flavorful spices, and there's an open seat at every table for friends and strangers alike. At Nujuma, dining is beyond necessity; it’s a ritual rooted in history, driven by intrigue and rewarded with meaningful connections.

Sita
All-Day Dining With an Arabian Touch
Like the old bazaars that inspired its inception, Sita is an experience that unfolds the further one explores. Named for its six pavilions, the all-day dining venue features two main areas. In the French-style patisserie, delicate sweet and savory selections are paired with specialty coffee and tea. And in the Levantine restaurant, a centerpiece clay oven entices with the aroma of fresh bread, hummus is prepared at the moment and a vast spice library is used to flavor colorful dishes.

Breaking Bread
A Tribute to Tradition
Arrival at Sita is marked by two tributes to Arab culture: a monumental clay oven and the scent of freshly baked bread. Guests are encouraged to observe as our resident bakers ply dough into flavorful pitas and loaves that will arrive still warm to diners in the restaurant. However, they’re also invited to step into the open-air kitchen to experience this time-honored tradition themselves with our hands-on bread-making master class.

In-Villa Experiences
With their spectacular views, kitchenettes and open spaces, our villas offer the perfect setting for a quiet family meal or a celebratory gathering with friends. Meet with a dedicated chef to share your preferences, and then join them in the kitchen or simply savor the gastronomic experience they’ve designed for you.
Anything, Anywhere
Nestled among the dunes in the desert or the white sands of the beach, guests can enjoy an exquisite meal no matter where their adventures take them. Designed by our chefs and inspired by you, these dining experiences are just as memorable as the settings themselves.
Reserved for Romance
Surrounded by mangroves and set at the edge of the Red Sea, the resort’s two islands are filled with picturesque locations designed for a private escape. Allow our chefs to craft a custom, culinary experience to mark a budding romance or a lifetime of shared love.
