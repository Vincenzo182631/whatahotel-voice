Where Uncharted Meets Extraordinary
Born from the stars of the infinite sky above and the colorful reef below, Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve carries on the traditions of those who traveled across uncharted territory to discover pristine natural beauty, rich cultural heritage and flavors as vivid as the sea itself. Surrounded by crystal-clear water and soft white sand, Nujuma is an unspoiled sanctuary where exploration unveils a constellation of unforgettable experiences.

Neyrah Spa
The path toward transformation is illuminated at Neyrah Spa, where precious ingredients and simple ceremonies foster a profound connection to nature and self. Designed to inspire reflection and clarity, this indoor-outdoor oasis borrows from the sea and land to rejuvenate the mind, body and spirit.
Galaxea Diving Center
Experience the Red Sea with adventures at and below the water’s surface including kayaking, sailing, windsurfing and snorkeling and diving excursions that explore the thriving, colorful coral reefs.
Conservation House
The Conservation House is an opportunity for guests of all ages to form a genuine connection with the Saudi Arabian culture, community and environment through activities on land and in the sea.
Villas
Set amid the soft white sand or perched above the azure sea, villas mirror their surroundings with shell-inspired structures and interiors finished with found objects, local art and colors, textures and materials that borrow from nature. Designed to offer both elevated privacy and direct access to the outdoors, each of the 65 villas features panoramic windows, open living space, a sea-view pool and a telescope to discover Saudi Arabia’s enchanting star-filled sky.

Modern Dining with a Traditional Spirit
In Saudi Arabia, homes are gathering places for friends, family and strangers, and meals are an opportunity to share one’s good fortune and ideas around music, poetry, philosophy and science. At Nujuma, home and hospitality are expressed in distinct ways at each of its four dining venues. Whether browsing the spice library for a live preparation of hummus, savoring the fisherman’s prized catch of the day or hearing the tales behind astrology-inspired cocktails, dining at Nujuma blends the warmth of familiarity with the delight of surprise.
Discover All Dining
Sita
Named for its six pavilions, Sita features a French-style patisserie, a Levantine restaurant offering moment-prepared hummus, a spice library for flavorful mezze, and a central clay oven perfuming fresh bread while hosting bread-making classes.
Explore
Jamaa
Jamaa is a relaxed Red Sea outdoor restaurant offering respite from the sun, uninterrupted sea views, and light, fresh fare with refreshing beverages or indulge in a shisha.
Explore
Tabrah
Tabrah is a culinary interpretation of Saudi Arabia’s fishing tradition, a communal celebration of the sea’s daily bounty that has nurtured fishermen’s families through the ages.
Explore
Maia
As the sun dips below the horizon, Maia invites guests to catch a glimpse of the celestial world with guidance from our star experts. At this Red Sea bar, the focus on astronomy extends to mocktails, concoctions inspired by the night sky.

A Commitment to Pristine Stewardship
At Nujuma, each moment is inspired by the pristine beauty of the Red Sea. Our sanctuary is powered by the sun, our cuisine celebrates local harvests, and guests are invited to nurture the island through mangrove planting. With 28,000 mangroves thriving, turtle nests protected, and water renewed to sustain native flora, every stay becomes part of a timeless story of care and preservation.

A Design in Harmony With the Red Sea
At Nujuma, every curve, texture and detail tells a story of place. Inspired by the Red Sea’s untamed beauty, the resort’s design is a gentle dialogue between nature and culture, honoring the land, embracing the sea and weaving Saudi heritage into a setting of rare harmony and quiet wonder.
