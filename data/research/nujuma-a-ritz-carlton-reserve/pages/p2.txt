Unwind in our luxury Red Sea villas
Villas
Protective of privacy and nature and perfect in their form, villas at Nujuma capture the essence of barefoot luxury. Designed to reflect the shape and radiance of seashells, our Red Sea luxury accommodation blends seamlessly with its surroundings, whether floating above the water or set upon the sand. Interiors intrinsically link land and sea in both their aesthetic and function with natural tones and materials, wall-to-wall windows with panoramic views, private pools overlooking the water and telescopes to connect with the nomadic tribal tradition of seeking guidance from the stars.
Summer at Nujuma
Immerse in Nujuma’s magic, where young explorers discover and families savor togetherness in untouched tranquility with our Kids Stay & Eat Free offer.​
Private Beachfront Escape
Royal Nujuma Villa
Secluded on one side of the island amid a sprawling beachfront landscape, the Royal Nujuma Villa is our most expansive accommodation. Within its more than 3,800 sq. ft. is everything one needs and desires from a true retreat including three bedrooms, three bathrooms, a full kitchen and a spacious living and dining area. The experience extends beyond the walls of the shell-inspired design to include a sundeck, garden, pool and direct access to the Red Sea.

Discover Nujuma, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, in 360°
Journey into the heart of Nujuma, where the sea and stars meet in quiet harmony. Wander through its overwater villas and hidden coves, where every detail whispers of craftsmanship and calm. A Journey of Wellness and Enlightenment
Neyrah Spa
A destination for self-reflection and connection, Neyrah Spa guides each guest on their path toward relaxation and balance. Our holistic approach places equal importance on the body, mind and spirit, and bespoke journeys are designed by therapists who bring a wealth of global knowledge and tradition to their craft. Outside of the wellness center, with its sea-view treatment rooms, vitality and lap pools, hammam and fitness center, a wellness butler that can curate bespoke experiences to help guests continue their journey.
