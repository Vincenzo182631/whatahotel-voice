### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 5 at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi?

Yes, all rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have fridges?

No, rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
