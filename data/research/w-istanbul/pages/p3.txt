### Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants does The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have on property?

Corte Verde RestaurantPrimo Cafe & BarCorte Verde GardenCorte Verde Breakfast Section

Does The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi offer room service?
