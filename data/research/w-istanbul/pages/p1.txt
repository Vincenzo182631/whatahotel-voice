### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi?

The check-in time at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 8.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi?

Off-site valet parking is available at The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi. The daily valet fee is €20.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi?

The closest airport near The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi is Istanbul International Airport (IST). IST is located approximately 39.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Istanbul Nisantasi has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
