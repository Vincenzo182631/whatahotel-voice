At this "modern, beautifully designed beachfront all-inclusive," the light-blue and turquoise exterior reflects the waterfront setting, while the curved architecture mimics the waves. Rooms in cream and brown tones are "gorgeously appointed" with local artwork, dark-wood accents, and travertine floors. "Staff pamper you," whether serving drinks at the pool or on the beach. There are nearly a dozen restaurants on the premises, each with "fantastic five-star cuisine." Frida serves Mexican, Sen Lin Asian. "The spa has very luxurious treatments and attentive technicians."
