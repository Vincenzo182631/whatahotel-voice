# A Brilliantly Playful Riviera Maya Mexico Resort

Hugging the gorgeous white sand beaches of Playa del Carmen on Mexico's Caribbean Coast and the Yucatan jungle, Grand Velas Riviera Maya is an all-inclusive luxury refuge in one of Mexico's most sought after destinations. Lounge by the infinity pool, soak up the sun and the cool breeze along the beach, or embark on a soothing, sensory journey at a world-class spa. Culinary artists and renowned chefs work their magic on authentic gourmet dishes at seven fantastic restaurants, while you embrace a culture and setting of pure, playful paradise. Explore Mexico's most brilliant coast, and make it yours.
