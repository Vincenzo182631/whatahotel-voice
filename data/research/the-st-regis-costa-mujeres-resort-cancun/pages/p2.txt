# Rooms at The St. Regis Cancun Costa Mujeres Resort

## Accommodations

Step into the fine art of hospitality at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, an exclusive sanctuary north of Cancun. Within 163 beautifully appointed rooms and suites, each with private terraces, guests witness the sun rise over the sea and set behind the mangroves of Isla Blanca. Designed by award-winning firm Sordo Madaleno, the resort reimagines Mayan legends in stone, wood, and metal—an intricate play of color and balance where every design gesture elegantly whispers.

Suites

### Explore Our Distinctive Suites

Discover elevated luxury at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort Cancun. In our suites, astrological motifs come to life through a delicate play of light and custom fixtures. Experience total privacy in suites with private pools overlooking the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef and the sprawling mangrove reserve near Isla Blanca. Every moment is enriched by the tranquil rhythm of the sea and the timeless sophistication of St. Regis.
