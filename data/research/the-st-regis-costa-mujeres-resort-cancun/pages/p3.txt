# Dining at The St. Regis Cancun Costa Mujeres Resort

## Dining

The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort Cancun invites you to a refined culinary journey inspired by the vibrant spirit of the Mexican Caribbean. From a taste of local delicacies at our Cevicheria to inventive culinary traditions that blend Japanese techniques with Mexican cuisine, Costa Mujeres Finest Address features a rich array of extraordinary restaurants and bars. Experience an inspired take on Mrs. Astor's high tea ritual, enjoy our renowned signature spirits program at The St. Regis Bar, and relish in alluring sounds at our exclusive Speakeasy.

At This Hotel

## Costa Coral

Mexican

A contemporary expression of the Mexican Caribbean, our kitchen honors local heritage through refined technique and respect for the source. Rooted in Mexico's rich culinary legacy and the biodiversity of the milpa, we elevate luxury dining in Cancun.

Open for breakfast and dinner

## Fish Market

Caribbean

From the precision of our raw bar to signature ceviches and shared rice compositions, our seafood restaurant in Costa Mujeres honors the integrity of local produce. A space for long afternoons and shared stories, as fresh as the daily catch.

Open for lunch and dinner

## Palmare Grill

Seafood

Inspired by legendary street flavors, our oceanfront restaurant in Costa Mujeres serves artisanal breads, sun-seared meats, and coastal favorites crafted with local ingredients and designed for effortless sharing.

Open for Lunch

## The St. Regis Bar

Tapas

A surrealist liquid landscape where heritage mixology meets daring infusions, adjacent to the Agave Library, a communal immersive bar. At this luxury hotel bar in Costa Mujeres, each evening unfolds as a timeless St. Regis ritual.

Open for Dinner

Dress Code:
Smart Casual

## Olivia

Deli

An elevated specialty coffee hub, where each cup becomes a refined daily ritual showcasing premium Mexican coffees, artisanal brewing techniques, complemented by an artisanal bakery selection

Open for Lunch

What restaurants does The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun have on property?

Does The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun offer room service?
