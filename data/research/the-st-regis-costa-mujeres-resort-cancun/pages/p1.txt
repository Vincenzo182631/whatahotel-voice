### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun?

On-site valet parking is available at The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun is Cancun International Airport (CUN). CUN is located approximately 26.9 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Costa Mujeres Resort, Cancun does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
