## Elevate Your Senses

From therapeutic massages to cleansing facials and invigorating body treatments, each element of our luxury spa treatments is designed to stimulate the senses. Treat yourself to Glo2Facial's exclusive assets treatment, a breakthrough service that unlocks the body’s natural superpowers: Oxfoliation™ with O2 bubbles, amplify with LUX via a lite ultrasound experience, and detox with a lymphatic massage. This innovative technology provides the relaxation of a classic facial with the results expected in-clinic. Other popular treatments include a Desert Oasis Facial, Zen Mountain Glow, Nordic Waters, Ocean Currents, Around the World , Collagen and Corrective Oxygen Facials. Body treatments include a Riviera Relaxation Ritual, a Surf & Sand Body Buff, and various hand and foot salon treatments.
