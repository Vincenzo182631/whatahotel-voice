# Welcome to The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort

## Welcome to year-round fun in the sun

Discover Oceanfront Elegance at Our Fort Lauderdale Beach Hotel. Experience unmatched service and serene coastal beauty at our luxury hotel on the beach. Our Fort Lauderdale Beach resort provides everything...See More you need to feel refreshed and inspired. Settle into our oceanview rooms in Fort Lauderdale that travelers love and enjoy restorative sleep on the iconic Westin Heavenly® Bed. As a pet-friendly hotel in Fort Lauderdale, we welcome the whole family. Elevate your stay with treatments at our full-service spa, authentic Mexican flavors at Lona Cocina Tequileria, breakfast at Tinta, or poolside dining at Waves Bar & Grill. Our 35,000 square feet of venue space, including a grand ballroom and Sky Terrace, is ideal for meetings, weddings, and celebrations. Step across our private skywalk to the beach, explore Las Olas Boulevard, or catch a Water Taxi.See Less

Sample our Mexican restaurant in Ft. Lauderdale, helmed by renowned chef Pablo Salas. Choose from an eclectic, seasonal menu featuring ceviches, tacos, grilled meats, and a selection of agave-centric craft cocktails, tequila flights, and more.

Seas the Day at Our Oceanfront Oasis

### The Beach is Calling

Cross our hotel's cleverly designed skywalk, which is directly connected from the pool deck to the beach. Soak up the sun as you take a dip in the ocean, or stroll, jog, bicycle, or rollerblade along Fort Lauderdale's palm tree-fringed, beachfront promenade.

Learn More

### Explore the Waterways

Get out on the water and feel the wind on your face. Unwind in the sun and waves and immerse yourself in the turquoise waters of Fort Lauderdale. Enjoy a day of scuba diving, experience the excitement of catching the fish of a lifetime, or tour the "Venice of America" with a ride aboard the Water Taxi of Fort Lauderdale.

Learn More

### Eat Your Heart Out

Grab a cold brew from Lavazza Cafe, enjoy breakfast with a Mexican twist at Tinta, and indulge in eclectic cuisine oceanfront at Lona Cocina & Tequileria. With five onsite restaurants, bars, cafes, and coffee shops, you will never go hungry at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort.

Learn More

### Family Fun

Keep the kids entertained with a day of sightseeing! Be magically transported to the beginning of the 20th Century with a trip to Bonnet House, visit Hugh Taylor Birch Park which is known as the city's own "Central Park" or mix education with adventure while checking out the exhibits at the Museum of Science and Discovery.

Learn More

### Set Sail

Passengers planning to set sail from the Port Everglades cruise terminal will be delighted with The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort's convenient location. Our hotel is less than five miles from the cruise port as well as being convenient to the airport, the beach, downtown Fort Lauderdale attractions and much more.

Learn More

### Unique Treasures

Take advantage of the hottest deals at Sawgrass Outlet Mall, the most visited attraction in the area, or head to Galleria Mall for the newest trends. You'll also find a large selection of department stores and flea markets as well as an eclectic mix of fabulous boutiques and galleries on Las Olas Boulevard.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort?

The check-in time at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of $50.00 per night applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort?

On-site parking is available at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort. Self parking is available at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort, with rates of $35.00 per hour, and $62.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $62.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort?

The closest airport near The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort is Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL). FLL is located approximately 6.2 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
