Discover the Best of Fort Lauderdale

### Shopping by the Sea

Just moments from the hotel, Las Olas Boulevard offers premier shopping, upscale dining, and top attractions like the Art Museum and Museum of Discovery and Science. Easily accessible by Water Taxi, it's Fort Lauderdale’s most iconic destination.

### More Than a Boat Ride

The water temperature in Fort Lauderdale is warm year-round, perfect for snorkeling, diving, and fishing. For more adventure, take a scheduled Water Taxi ride, book a private charter through local companies, or explore the Everglades on an airboat tour.

### Cruising the Atlantic

Located In the heart of Greater Fort Lauderdale and the city of Hollywood, FL, Port Everglades is one of the busiest cruise ports in the world. Make your travel as stress-free as possible by staying at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort the night before or after your cruise, and wake up feeling rested and rejuvenated.

### Adventure Awaits

Escape the South Florida heat with some indoor family fun. Fort Lauderdale is home to some of the best museums, performing arts venues, and historic landmarks such as Bonnet House, Stranahan House, and the International Swimming Hall of Fame. Grab the kids and get ready for a day full of adventure.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Peloton Bikes, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort have an on-site spa?

Yes, Heavenly Spa by Westin™ is located on-site at The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort?

Water Taxi Stop, Broward County Convention Center, Galleria Mall, Fort Lauderdale Aquatic Center- Swimming Hall of Fame, Museum of Discovery & Science and more are nearby The Westin Fort Lauderdale Beach Resort.
