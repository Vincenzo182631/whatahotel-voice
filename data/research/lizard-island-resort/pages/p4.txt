# Great Barrier Reef Accommodation

## Rooms, Suites & Villas

Lizard Island presents elegant yet understated Great Barrier Reef accommodation in a variety of rooms, suites and villas. Lizard Island Resort offers an exclusive collection of private suites and villas designed for those seeking space, serenity, and an intimate connection with nature. Set within a protected National Park and perched above the iconic Great Barrier Reef, each accommodation option invites you to slow down, unwind, and take in uninterrupted views of turquoise waters and untouched coastline.

##### All of our luxury resort accommodation offers unparalleled and curated seclusion, providing the perfect environment in which to enjoy your stay.

Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in each of the resort’s guest rooms and at dedicated areas across the resort.
