### Take a private charter out for a day - a unique experience like no other.

A private charter for fishing, snorkelling and diving excursions will elevate your Lizard Island experience to a sublime, new level and leave you with no doubt as to why Lizard Island is like nowhere else. When you emerge from the water or put down your rod, our crew will be on hand to serve a menu of local produce and refreshments.

### Experience Lizard Island on the reef, on land or in the tranquillity of the spa.

Lizard Island offers a wide range of activities that provide opportunities to fully immerse yourself in the natural attractions of the island and surrounding Great Barrier Reef. Our experienced and enthusiastic guides are adept at personalising your experience to your individual tastes, creating memories that will last a lifetime.

### One of the greatest joys of fishing in and around Lizard Island is the ease of access to some of the world’s greatest fishing spots.

Just 15 minutes by boat and you will arrive at the inner reef. A further 35 minutes and you will be at the outer reef. Whether you’re a seasoned big game fisherman, or it’s your first time in the chair, our Marine team will create a fishing experience that meets your wildest dreams.

### Experience the nature of the island on a guided interpretive walk with our naturalist

We invite you to join us for a guided interpretive walk to experience the nature, flora and fauna on the island. Learn more about this fascinating reef and island environment, and the unique creatures that call it home.

### Lizard Island is located on the Great Barrier Reef with world famous dive sites, such as the Cod Hole, only 45 minutes from shore.

Lizard Island’s location on the Great Barrier Reef means more time exploring its natural wonders and less time on the boat. Stops along the way at diverse fringing reefs set up the backdrop for the wonders of the deeper waters.

### Explore the island on either a guided or self-guided tour, taking in the natural beauty of the surrounds.

Exploring and experiencing Lizard Island can be enjoyed by taking part in a number of guided tours, either to the inner and outer reefs, the Research Station, or on nature walks and challenging hikes to the top of Cook’s Look.

### Snorkel the Great Barrier Reef without the exhausting day-long boat trips just to reach the reef

Experience some of the most picturesque reef and marine life by simply stepping off the beach – this is one of the major attractions that makes Lizard Island like nowhere else. The “Clam Gardens” are only a short swim from the beach at Watson’s Bay, and excellent sites are also located at Anchor Bay, and Sunset Beach.

### Take some time for yourself to relax, rejuvenate and rebalance.

Enjoy sensory experiences that not only deeply relax, but connect you with the true heart of this ancient land.

### Great Barrier Reef Activities

**Lizard Island experiences and activities are exclusive for resort guests only.**

Guided and self-guided activities include snorkelling, diving, paddle boarding, kayaking, hiking, visits to the Lizard Island Research Station, tennis under night lights, morning yoga on the beach and indulgent spa treatments.

Please note: it is best to **pre-book your island activities** prior to arrival, especially private requests and scuba diving to avoid missing out. All tours are subject to change due to weather conditions and schedule changes. Experience prices are subject to change without notice.

### Medical Information

Should you have a pre-existing medical condition such as Asthma, Australian Scuba Diving Standards require you have a current Medical Form (AS4005.1) completed by a Medical Practitioner. If you do not have any pre-existing medical conditions, you will simply be required to sign a medical declaration before diving.

### No Diving 24 Hours Before Flying

Please be advised, safety regulations require that divers are not permitted to fly for a minimum of 24 hours after their last dive. As most guests will fly to and from the island, this is critical for your planning.
