###### Spa Etiquette

#### Arrival Time

Enhance your wellness experience by arriving 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment. In consideration of other guests, late arrivals will be subject to a time reduction from the scheduled treatment.

#### Age Limitation

Essentia Spa welcomes guests aged 16 years and older to enjoy our facilities and spa treatments.

#### Spa Environment

We strive to provide you with the most relaxing and tranquil environment, so we respectfully request that you switch off mobile phones. Your cooperation in providing a quiet environment is greatly appreciated.

#### Gift Vouchers

Gift vouchers are available for purchase and may be used for all Sodashi Spa treatments and retail items.

#### Retail Therapy

Essentia Spa offers a wide range of Sodashi products to take your spa experience home with you. Please ask therapists for recommendations.

#### Health

At the time of booking please notify the spa if you have any medical conditions so we can advise the best treatment for you.

#### Cancellation Policy

If you cannot make your appointment or need to re-schedule, we require a minimum of 24 hours’ advance notice. We regret that 100% charge of total services will be charged if notice is not given.

###### Frequently Asked Spa Questions

#### When should I arrive for my spa treatment?

We recommend that you arrive fifteen minutes prior to your appointment. This allows time for you to complete your consultation form, meet your therapist and be ready for your spa experience.

#### What if I am late for my appointment?

Arriving late will unfortunately limit the time of your treatment. Plan your special time out and ensure you arrive on time to experience the complete benefits and pleasure of pampering.

#### Should I pre-book my spa appointment?

It is recommended to arrange your treatments prior to arrival.

#### What should I wear to the spa?

Please wear whatever clothes are comfortable for you to the spa. During treatments our therapists are trained to drape you appropriately for privacy. Disposable underwear is available and there are fully equipped change rooms in the spa for you to use during your visit.

#### How do I choose my treatment?

We offer a full menu to relax, rejuvenate and revive face and body. Our staff at the spa can help you decide on specific treatments that are most appealing and beneficial to you.

#### What if I have health considerations or allergies?

If you have any concerns regarding your health, please notify us when booking your treatments; including allergies, colds, flu, skin conditions, etc.

#### What if I am pregnant?

Please notify us when booking your treatments. We have designed treatments with products and techniques that are safe after 12 weeks of pregnancy.

#### What should I bring?

We will of course provide robes, slippers, towels, toiletries, showers, hairdryers and lockers. Please leave your valuables at home as we cannot take responsibility for valuables left unattended in the spa.

#### What if I need to cancel my spa reservation?

We understand that sometimes you may need to change your schedule, but we ask that you give us 12 hours’ notice. Cancellation or no-show within 24 hours of your appointment will incur a 100% charge of the treatment(s) booked.
