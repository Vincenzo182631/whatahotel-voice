# Gourmet Food & Wine

## Restaurants and Bars at Lizard Island

Enjoy a variety of restaurant and dining experiences at Lizard Island, from gourmet meals with seascape views to deluxe picnics on a private beach. With daily changing menus that draw inspiration from the local environment, you will be presented with gourmet food and a fine selection of wine, Champagne, beer and spirits throughout your stay. In addition to your daily dining which is included in your nightly rate, there are several other culinary experiences to enjoy.

###### Finely curated partnerships with Australia’s finest farmers, winemakers and purveyors

#### Salt Water Restaurant

Salt Water is like no other restaurant with its openness creating a natural extension of the beautiful surrounds of Lizard Island.

Learn More

#### Driftwood Bar & Wine Cellar

In the evening, enjoy pre-dinner drinks at Driftwood Bar where you’ll get the chance to reconnect with others you may have met throughout the day, or simply enjoy a drink and watch the sunset.

Learn More

#### Beachside Degustation Dining

Two private gazebos are perfectly positioned on the shore of Anchor Bay, commanding spectacular views of the nightly sunsets.

Learn More

#### Private Beach Excursions

Select your own refreshments pack the night before your planned excursion and the following day, take a private dinghy and motor to a beach of your choice - or have one of our Marine team drop you off and return later to collect you.

Learn More

#### Signature Culinary Experiences

Great food and drinks is just what we do on Lizard Island, our culinary and cellar teams have a passion for creating the very best culinary experiences where the food and wine is elevated to new heights.

Learn More

#### Marlin Bar

The Marlin Bar is closed to the public throughout the peak occupancy period, ensuring capacity and service levels are maintained for in-house guests.

Learn More
