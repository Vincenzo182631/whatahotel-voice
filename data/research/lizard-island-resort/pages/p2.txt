# Oceanview Villas

## Luxury Lizard Island Accommodation

The stunning location provides access to a collection of secluded beaches and one of the Island’s greatest vantage points to watch the majestic sunsets. Oceanview Villas are nestled high along a ridge overlooking the Coral Sea with natural, meandering pathways leading down to some of the island’s renowned private beaches. Step inside from your private deck and the serenity of linen and blue hues create a peaceful oasis that can be closed off from the outside world, or keep the doors wide open to be integrated as one with the outside landscape. A padded box seat sits in a bay alcove with light streaming in, offering filtered views over the sea. Oceanview Villas are your small, private oasis. Bedding includes a King Bed or 2 x King Single Beds. Maximum of 2 people. Oceanview Villas are 59 square metres.
