Asian street food in harmony with nature

Funky offers an Asian culinary experience in perfect harmony with nature. ## Gastronomic proposal

,regionOfInterest=(1771.5,1181))

### Mystical and Natural Experience

At Funky Geisha, every space is designed to create a deep connection with nature. Buddha statues, floor cushions, handcrafted lamps, and rustic wooden elements evoke a mystical atmosphere from the very first moment. This design invites diners to enjoy an environment where the spiritual and natural worlds merge, infused with the vibrant energy of nature.

,regionOfInterest=(2240,3360))

### A taste of Thailand & Japan

Cuisine inspired by the flavors of Thailand and Japan. Each dish at Funky Geisha combines fresh ingredients to capture the essence of Asian markets. From sushi and nigiri to Thai curries, our chefs create an experience full of authentic flavors and textures. Enjoy freshly prepared dishes that will transport you to the heart of Asian gastronomy.
