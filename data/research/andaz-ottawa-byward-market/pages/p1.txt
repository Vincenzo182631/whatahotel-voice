# Contemporary Luxury Hotel in Ottawa’s ByWard Market

Uncover new experiences, whether you're here for a fleeting stay or a deserved vacation. Our luxury boutique hotel is in ByWard Market, a coveted urban locale near downtown Ottawa, and just steps from the historic Parliament Buildings and the National Gallery of Canada. Relax in our Canadian-designed hotel rooms, dine at our rooftop lounge - the highest in Ottawa - and explore the neighborhood.

## Rooms & Suites

We worked with award-winning Canadian interior design firm Mason Studio to bring our authentic vision to life. Our luxury hotel rooms and suites in ByWard Market feature locally inspired touches and floor-to-ceiling windows framing views of the city of Ottawa. Discover imaginative accents like rain showers and 400-thread count linens.

### Feast + Revel

Discover enticing dishes from breakfast to dinner. Chef Matthew McRae leads our dynamic ByWard Market restaurant, dedicated to creating dining experiences that reflect the spirit of the Ottawa region. Classic technique mixes with modern creativity and sustainability to produce vibrant, seasonal dishes, with flavours that are thoughtful, ingredient-forward and deeply connected to the community.

### Copper Spirits & Sights

Rise above the city at Ottawa’s highest rooftop bar. Savour Canadian whiskey, craft cocktails inspired by the seasons and elevated small plates at our restaurant’s indoor/outdoor lounge, served alongside panoramic views of Parliament Hill and ByWard Market. Whether it’s golden hour or last call, this is where Ottawa comes to unwind in style.

## Pet Policy

We gladly welcome your most cherished companions to travel on your journey with you. To enjoy these benefits, inform us that you will be bringing your dog (or dogs) with you prior to your arrival. Our Pet Program offers benefits including dog-friendly amenities, such as a doggy bed, doggy bowls and doggy cookies. All dogs must be housebroken.
