# The Inn at Middleton Place

Charleston, SC

Set on a bluff overlooking the Ashley River, The Inn at Middleton Place offers a serene Lowcountry escape just a short drive from downtown Charleston. Neighboring Middleton Place National Historic Landmark, home to America’s oldest landscaped gardens, the 55-room Inn places guests at the heart of one of the region’s most storied and scenic destinations.

Visit Site

A celebrated work of modern architecture, The Inn was originally designed in 1987 to blend seamlessly with its natural surroundings. Its spacious rooms feature dramatic floor-to-ceiling windows that invite the outdoors in, creating a true connection to the river, woodlands, and gardens beyond.

## Accommodations

Every stay includes complimentary access to Middleton Place, where winding pathways lead through 110 acres of landscaped gardens first envisioned in 1741, as well as the House Museum, stable yards, and Eliza’s House, an African American freedman’s dwelling that shares essential stories of the site’s history.

## Dining

Overlooking the Mill Pond and Azalea Hillside, The Restaurant at Middleton Place is Charleston's most stunning and charming outdoor dining scene. Offering a seasonal take on Lowcountry cuisine, every dish is rooted in tradition, crafted with local ingredients, and served with Southern grace.
