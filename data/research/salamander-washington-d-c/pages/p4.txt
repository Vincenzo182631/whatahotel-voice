The beauty of the game is that it belongs to all.

Our belief is that golf should welcome all who pursue it with joy. At Salamander, courses are designed to challenge, inspire, and connect players of every generation. To us, there is no single kind of golfer, only people bound by their love of the game and the moments it creates.

### The Valspar Championship

The Valspar Championship is a professional golf tournament held annually in Palm Harbor, Florida, at the Innisbrook Resort and Golf Club. It is part of the PGA Tour and typically takes place in March, attracting top-tier golfers from around the world. The event is known for its challenging Copperhead Course, which features winding fairways, strategically placed water hazards, and undulating greens. The tournament has a rich history, dating back to 2000, and has seen some of golf's biggest names take home the title, including legends like Jordan Spieth and Justin Thomas. The Valspar Championship is also recognized for its vibrant atmosphere, with fans enjoying both the competition and the beautiful Florida setting.

### The Cognizant Classic

Each spring, PGA National Resort hosts the Cognizant Classic, a premier stop on the PGA TOUR set on the renowned Champion Course. This celebrated event draws the game’s greatest talents to South Florida, where legendary names like Justin Thomas, Rickie Fowler, and Adam Scott have conquered the iconic Bear Trap. Blending world-class competition with the refined ambiance of Palm Beach, the tournament offers an unforgettable experience for players and spectators alike.

### Iconic fairways, timeless challenges

In Palm Beach Gardens, PGA National Resort stands as a true icon of the game. Six renowned courses, including the celebrated Champion Course, invite players to test their skill and embrace the joy of play. Rooted in a legacy of legends and renewed for the next generation, it remains one of the game’s most inspiring destinations.

### Golf Tips

Improving your golf game requires a blend of technique, consistency, and mental focus. A key tip for beginners is to maintain a solid grip on the club, ensuring it's neither too tight nor too loose, allowing for better control and power.
