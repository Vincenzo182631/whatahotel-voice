# Innisbrook Resort

Palm Harbor, FL

Nestled across 600 stunning acres of rolling hills and pristine lakes on Florida’s legendary west coast, Innisbrook feels like a secluded paradise, yet it’s only 20 miles from Tampa International Airport. This lush retreat boasts four championship golf courses and offers easy access to the serene waters of the Gulf of Mexico. Guests can indulge in signature dining experiences, luxurious spa treatments, relaxing poolside moments, and explore a wide range of nearby family-friendly attractions.

Visit Site Gift Cards

Innisbrook Resort is a golfer’s paradise, featuring four championship courses, including Copperhead, home to the PGA TOUR’s Valspar Championship. As you drive through the resort’s welcoming gates, you’ll be captivated by rolling hills and serene lakes that stretch across the expansive property. Perfect for group getaways, Innisbrook offers a wealth of activities for everyone—relax at six pools, savor delicious meals at our restaurants, enjoy tennis, and treat yourself to rejuvenating experiences at the luxurious Salamander Spa.

## Golf

Our Florida golf resort is the home of four outstanding courses, including the top-rated Copperhead Course — the host of the PGA TOUR's Valspar Championship and favorite among golf pros. Designed by the innovative and imaginative architect Lawrence "Larry" Packard, each course has its own unique essence, layout and breathtaking scenery.
