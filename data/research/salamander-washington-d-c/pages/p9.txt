# Aspen Meadows

Aspen, Co

When you arrive at Aspen Meadows Resort, there’s a shift in the air. Rooted in the history of the Aspen Institute and designed by Herbert Bayer, our 98-all-suite resort stands apart from the rest in this ski town. Here, openness and warmth greet you at every turn, inviting locals and travelers alike to a resort experience that is simultaneously exclusive and inclusive. As you wander through our 40 acres of rolling meadows and beautiful groves, you'll discover one of the largest art collections on any resort grounds. Renowned for its nature-inspired Bauhaus design and spacious accommodations, Aspen Meadows is within walking distance of Aspen’s year-round outdoor pursuits, skiing, shopping, and restaurants.

## All-Suite Stays

At Aspen Meadows Resort, guests can experience newly renovated all-suite accommodations. The spacious living areas are filled with natural light, offering a large table for dining, working, and family games, along with a Bauhaus-inspired sleeper sofa. Each bedroom features modern platform beds with integrated storage. Panoramic views of Aspen Mountain, Aspen Highlands, and Buttermilk provide a serene backdrop, inviting guests to connect more deeply with nature.

## Weddings

At Aspen Meadows Resort, we embrace the beauty of simplicity, where nature provides the perfect backdrop for your love story. Nestled on protected land with stunning, unobstructed views of Castle and Capitol Creek Valleys, Anderson Park offers more than just a moment—it ensures your ceremony is an unforgettable, timeless occasion. Celebrate in the McNulty Ballroom, one of only two above-ground ballrooms in Aspen, where floor-to-ceiling windows frame panoramic views, creating an extraordinary setting for your event. Here, your special day comes to life.

## Art & Adventure

As you wander through 40 acres of rolling meadows and aspen groves, you’ll encounter one of the largest art collections on any resort grounds which is best explored through our self-guided Walking Art Tour. Located on the campus of the Aspen Institute, The Resnick Center for Herbert Bayer Studies (Art Museum), Aspen Meadows is a living expression of Bauhaus design and creativity. Just moments from Aspen’s year-round pursuits, from skiing and hiking to shopping and dining, it's where art, nature, and adventure beautifully converge.
