# Wellness

The rarest retreat is the one that feels like home.

Wellness at Salamander is a sanctuary of calm, created for those who savor life well. Each experience is designed to nurture body, mind, and spirit, from thoughtful spa rituals to mindful movement and moments of stillness. Across our collection, every guest is invited to slow the rhythm of the world outside and rediscover a personal sense of balance within.
