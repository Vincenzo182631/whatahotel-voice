# Half Moon

Montego Bay, Jamaica

Spread across 400 acres of beautifully manicured tropical grounds, including two miles of pristine beachfront, Half Moon provides the ultimate Caribbean getaway. Unwind on the beach with the gentle sounds of the waves, stroll the lush fairways of the championship golf course, enjoy horseback riding at the fully-equipped equestrian center, or rejuvenate at Jamaica’s largest spa, all within the island’s most exceptional retreat.

Accommodations

## Rooms & Suites

Half Moon offers three unique accommodation experiences, set against the backdrop of the sparkling Caribbean Sea to the north and lush hillsides to the south. Guests can choose from Eclipse at Half Moon, Founders Cove at Half Moon, or the newly renovated Villas at Half Moon. The resort features 210 beautifully appointed rooms and suites, along with 19 villas, all offering convenient access to world-class amenities and two miles of pristine beachfront.

Tucked between the sparkling Caribbean Sea and verdant southern hills, Eclipse at Half Moon is one of the Caribbean's newest and most luxurious destinations. This exceptional property features 57 expansive accommodations, two exceptional restaurants, three stylish bars, a market café, the renowned Fern Tree - a Salamander Spa, a breathtaking infinity-edge pool, and a private beach with a serene natural swimming cove.

Food and Drink

## Dining

Embark on a unique culinary journey with our talented chefs, who bring the rich flavors of modern Jamaican cuisine to life, drawing inspiration from the traditions of farmers and fishermen from the countryside. Delight in authentic Italian dishes crafted with creativity, and savor the freshest seasonal ingredients. Enjoy a signature cocktail at any of our bars, or dine alfresco at restaurants offering breathtaking ocean views. Our signature restaurant, the Sugar Mill, features stunning views of the lush tropical landscape and the historic water wheel from the former Rose Hall Sugar Plantation.
