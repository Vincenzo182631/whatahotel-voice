Memberships That Go Beyond the Game

# Memberships

At Salamander, our members are more than guests — they’re part of a legacy of connection, camaraderie, and excellence. Whether you're drawn to the legendary fairways of PGA National Resort or the laid-back, nature-kissed surroundings of Innisbrook, membership is your key to an immersive lifestyle rooted in wellness, recreation, and shared experiences. Belonging, elevated. From championship greens to coastal wellness, Salamander members enjoy more than just access — they embrace a way of life.

### PGA National Resort

Palm Beach Gardens, FL

Rooted in a heritage of professional play and refined leisure, PGA National Resort offers a dynamic membership that redefines what it means to belong. Members enjoy privileged access to five championship courses — including the iconic Champion Course, home to the Cognizant Classic — as well as exclusive resort amenities designed for the discerning enthusiast.

Highlights include:

- Unlimited golf across five distinct courses

- Exclusive access to the resort’s racquet club, fitness center, and resort pools

- Invitations to member-only events and tournaments

- Preferred pricing on dining, spa, retail, and guest stays

- A vibrant social calendar and a warm, connected community

Whether your passion lies in the game or the gatherings after, PGA National’s membership delivers world-class experiences in an inviting South Florida setting.

### Innisbrook Resort

Palm Harbor, FL

Surrounded by serene woodlands and just moments from Florida’s Gulf Coast, Innisbrook Resort offers a membership experience that blends casual charm with championship spirit. From the famed Copperhead Course — host of the Valspar Championship — to family-friendly pools and locally inspired dining, members enjoy year-round access to authentic resort living.

Highlights include:

- Unlimited golf across four championship courses

- Access to the renowned Salamander Spa and fitness center

- Full use of tennis and pickleball facilities

- Member social events and seasonal programming for all ages

- Discounts on lodging, dining, spa, and retail services

At Innisbrook, membership is more than access — it’s a lifestyle surrounded by nature, comfort, and connection.
