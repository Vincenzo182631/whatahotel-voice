# Hotel Bennett

Charleston, SC

Known locally as the “Queen of King Street,” Hotel Bennett blends timeless elegance and refined luxury with colonial charm and warm southern hospitality. Its prime location, overlooking Charleston’s iconic Marion Square, is just the beginning of the exceptional amenities offered, including a rooftop pool, a signature restaurant with breathtaking park views, a renowned spa, and an exquisite French patisserie.

Whether you're a local or visiting Charleston, Hotel Bennett is the ultimate destination for exceptional dining, stunning design, breathtaking rooftop views, and a serene urban spa. Begin your morning with a coffee on the outdoor terrace or savor pastries at La Patisserie that will transport you to the heart of Paris. Celebrate with a glass of champagne at the renowned bar or relax with a revitalizing spa treatment. After a day of discovering Charleston, enjoy a sunset aperitif on the rooftop, taking in the panoramic city views.

## Rooms & Suites

At Hotel Bennett, every room and suite is a sanctuary of sophistication and comfort. Designed with timeless elegance, our accommodations blend modern luxury with classic charm. From spacious rooms featuring plush furnishings and marble bathrooms to expansive suites offering separate living areas and stunning city views, each space is meticulously crafted to provide a tranquil retreat. Whether you’re here for business or leisure, our rooms offer thoughtful amenities and a serene atmosphere, ensuring a stay that is both relaxing and memorable.

## Dining

Hotel Bennett’s dining experiences are sure to impress even the most discerning guests exploring Charleston, the culinary capital of the South. Each venue is thoughtfully designed to cater to every need. Camellia’s stunning ambiance has quickly become a beloved spot for both locals and visitors. Satisfy your pastry cravings at La Pâtisserie, a Parisian-inspired café offering delectable, handmade treats crafted by renowned French Pastry Chef Rémy Fünfrock. Savor Gabrielle’s dishes made with fresh, local ingredients on the outdoor terrace, where you can enjoy iconic views of Marion Square, or head to Fiat Lux on the rooftop for poolside bites and sunset cocktails.

## Weddings

Create the Charleston wedding of your dreams at Hotel Bennett, where the city’s timeless charm and elegance shine through every indoor and outdoor event space, including the sophisticated Crown Ballroom and stunning rooftop venue. From intimate bridal showers at Camellia’s champagne bar to pampering your bridesmaids at the Spa, we offer everything you need for the perfect celebration. Our dedicated wedding specialists will work closely with you to design a personalized ceremony and reception that reflects your unique style, ensuring every detail is flawlessly executed.

## Meetings
