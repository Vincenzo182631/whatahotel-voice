## Suites

Discover a new chapter at the edge of the Pacific with the Moana Surfrider Suite Collection, offering oceanfront living on Waikiki Beach. Inspired by Ulukou, the storied shoreline where the Moana Surfrider...See More stands today, the collection includes 47 junior, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom Waikiki suites that bring together contemporary comfort, thoughtful design, and a natural connection to the ocean. Generous living and dining areas offer more space to gather, while spa-inspired baths and private lanais open to sweeping views of the Pacific and Diamond Head.
