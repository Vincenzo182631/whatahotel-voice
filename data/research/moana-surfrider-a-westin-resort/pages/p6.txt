### Moana Surfrider History

In 1901, English businessman Walter C. Peacock and his Moana Hotel Company, Ltd. Developed the Moana Hotel at a cost of $150,000. An ode to Victorian elegance, luxuries included the Territory of Hawaii’s first electric-powered elevator and guestrooms with their own telephones and private baths. Two wings were added in 1918 to create the famous “H” shape recognized today. The Surfrider Hotel was built...See More in 1952 and the Surfrider Tower in 1969.​See Less

### The Moana Rocking Chairs

The quintessential Moana tradition, the wooden carved Moana rocking chairs are beloved by all who have entered the resort. Located on the Kalakaua Avenue-facing veranda, guests can sit and watch the bustling world go by, while trade wind breezes cool the air. Guests young and old enjoy this timeless tradition at the resort, found nowhere else in the islands.

### Deva the Banyan Tree

The famous Moana Surfrider Banyan Tree, under whose branches romance blooms, relationships renew, and secrets are shared, was planted at the resort over a century ago. The tree named Deva is a 75-foot-high natural wonder that stretches 150-feet across. She’s a millennium landmark tree, the designation reserved for the one tree in each state that holds important national and local historic value.

### Holiday Traditions at the Moana Surfrider

Creating holiday traditions since 1901, this hotel near Diamond Head in Waikiki​ has created incredible memories for families since the turn of the century. The soaring Christmas tree is placed in the middle of the Historic Lobby, accompanied by the annual gingerbread display, which changes every year. The high tea tradition at The Veranda takes on special meaning during the festive season, with unique...See More holiday flavors and a beloved Keiki Holiday Tea that can be enjoyed through season. Don’t miss the resort’s classic Christmas stollen, which is only baked in December.See Less

Top 3 Things to Do on Oahu

The North Shore

The North Shore of Oahu is a perfect way to get away from it all, whether it’s during the big wave surf season in the winter or the tranquil summer months.

The Pearl Harbor National Memorial

The Pearl Harbor National Memorial immerses you in history. Pay your respects at the Arizona Memorial, step onto the massive USS Battleship Missouri, or explore the Pearl Harbor Aviation Museum.

Oahu Beaches

Oahu beaches have something for everyone. From the legendary Hanauma Bay to the convenient south shore beaches right in Waikiki, go snorkeling, pack a picnic, or build sandcastles with your kids as you relax in the sun.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to the spa at the Moana Surfrider?

How do I get to the fitness center at the Moana Surfrider?

Head to the Tower Wing where you can take the escalators to the second floor near the Tower Wing's guest elevators.

How do I book a historic tour or other activities offered at the Moana Surfrider?

Please visit our What to Expect page for updated resort information and more information about activities.

How do I access the Moana Surfrider's private beach?

The private beach is located adjacent to the Moana Surfrider's pool, and is only accessible with an umbrella rentals.

What are the Moana Surfrider pool's hours of operation?

The pool at Moana Surfrider is open from 7 am to 6 pm.

Are there lifeguards on duty at the pool at the Moana Surfrider?

There are no lifeguards on duty at the Moana Pool.

Is there a fee for pool chairs at the Moana Pool?

No, there is no fee for pool chairs. However, chairs are available at a first come, first serve basis.

Where can I rent a surfboard on Waikiki Beach?

We recommend Aloha Beach Services, located right outside the Moana Surfrider on Waikiki Beach.
