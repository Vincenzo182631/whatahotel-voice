The transformed Tower Wing introduces a refined expression of modern beach luxury. ### Tower Wing: Now Welcoming Guests

Completed in Summer 2025, the renovated Tower Wing features new indoor-outdoor living, custom curved seating & dining area with adjacent wet bar, and a bespoke reading area.

Learn More

### Historic Banyan Wing

Built in 1901, the Historic Banyan Wing embodies the enduring elegance of the First Lady of Waikiki. Inspired by the limu that once flourished along these shores, refined furnishings and graceful architectural details create a timeless experience.

Learn More

### Surfrider Wing

Inspired by Waikiki's legendary surf culture, the Surfrider Wing offers a boutique guest experience shaped by light, color, and a connection to the ocean. Beach loft-inspired interiors capture the relaxed spirit of Waikiki's shoreline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which wing at the Moana Surfrider has the best views of the beach and Pacific Ocean?

The Tower Wing will have the best ocean facing views at the Moana Surfrider.

Which wing has the most spacious rooms at the Moana Surfrider?

The Tower Wing has the largest rooms by square footage.

Are there connecting rooms available at the Moana Surfrider?

Yes, please call our main line to learn about connecting rooms and/or book connecting rooms.

How close are the different wings from each other at the Moana Surfrider?

The entire resort is a comfortable 3 minute walk from one end to the other.

Which wings at the Moana Surfrider have the best views of Diamond Head?

The Tower Wing will have the clearest Diamond Head views.

Which wings at the Moana Surfrider feature a private lanai (balcony)?

Every room in the Tower Wing has at least one private lanai.

Do you offer cribs in rooms at the Moana Surfrider?

Yes, you may request a crib upon booking your room or during check-in. Please note that cribs may be limited by availability.

Are rollaways available in all rooms at the Moana Surfrider?

Generally, rollaways are only available in King bed rooms and suites for safety reasons. Please call for more details.
