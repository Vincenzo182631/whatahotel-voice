## Signature Treatments at Moana Lani Spa

Choose from a curated selection of wellness treatments at Moana Lani Spa in Oahu. From our Heavenly Spa Signature Massage and Deep Tissue Massage, to revitalizing facials and invigorating body scrubs, the expert therapists at our oceanfront Waikiki resort spa​ will guide you on a journey of relaxation and renewal.

Ola Tropical Apothecary hand-crafted skincare is centered on the Hawaiian concept of beauty and wellness.
