### Explore the Three Wings of the Fully Renovated Hotel

Centrally located in the heart of Waikiki, the Moana Surfrider is the only beachfront resort with its stately porte cochere on Kalakaua Avenue. Each wing’s design provides unique guest experiences and is intended to tell a story about the restorative properties of the resort’s natural environment and the significance of its location throughout history.

### Tower Wing

The Tower Wing offers modern beach luxury, with the resort’s most spacious rooms and suites, high above Waikiki. Each features a custom seating and dining area, an integrated refreshment center, a built-in reading nook and a deep lanai that opens the room to the Pacific Ocean breezes.

### Surfrider Wing

Opened as the Surfrider Hotel, this light-filled wing nods to that history with a contemporary design drawn from surf culture. Oceanfront rooms feature the beloved rocking chairs with Pacific views, while Waikiki City View rooms face the sparkling Honolulu city lights.

### Historic Banyan Wing

The Historic Banyan Wing honors architect Oliver G. Traphagen's original 1901 design, from the Beaux-Arts detailing to the beautiful crown molding. A thoughtfully designed work area, fresh flooring, custom entertainment and refreshment center, and artwork evoking the graceful lines of limu, complete the space.

### Introducing the Moana Surfrider Suite Collection

The new Moana Surfrider Suite Collection offers oceanfront living on Waikiki Beach. Comprised of junior and one-bedroom suites, the collection features incredible views and spacious residential-style luxury. Living rooms features gracious seating and dining options, baths are spa-scaled, and private lanais open to the Pacific and Diamond Head. Each suite carries the character of the First Lady of Waikiki...See More into spaces curated for longer, slower stays on Waikiki's most photographed stretch of sand. ### Resort Experiences

Cultural programming at the resort is crafted around the people, traditions and stories of Oahu, from hands-on practice to guided history. Each session is led by practitioners who call Hawaii home.

### Modern Wellness With an Oceanfront Twist

### Hoala Sunrise Ceremony

Greet the morning with Hoala, a healing sunrise ceremony deeply rooted in tradition. Guests set an intention for the day and enter the water as the sun clears Diamond Head.

### Lei Making

Learn to string a variety of lei and learn the meaning behind the beloved tradition: a gift of aloha given at arrivals, celebrations and farewells across the islands for generations.

### Historical Tour

Hear fascinating tales of the First Lady of Waikiki, from the restorative spring waters of Apuakehau to the renowned architecture, courtyard radio broadcast and legendary guests through the century.

Oceanfront Wellness Experiences

## Wellness by the Water

Wellness at the Moana Surfrider unfolds in rhythm with the ocean. A thoughtfully curated weekly calendar invites guests to embrace balance and renewal through beginner-friendly oceanfront yoga, meditation, and mindful movement classes. Set against the soothing backdrop of Waikiki Beach, each experience welcomes the day with gentle sea breezes, the sound of lapping waves, and the restorative spirit that has defined the First Lady of Waikiki for more than a century.

### The History of the Moana Surfrider

Opened on March 11, 1901, as the first hotel on Waikiki Beach, the Moana Surfrider marked the beginning of Waikiki's resort era and has remained at the heart of the destination ever since. Known as the First Lady of Waikiki, the resort welcomed guests with groundbreaking innovations at the turn of the century, including private baths, telephones, and one of Hawaii's first electric elevators, setting...See More a new standard for hospitality. Beneath the shade of the historic Banyan Tree, planted in 1904, generations of travelers, local residents, musicians, and storytellers have gathered. The legendary radio broadcast shared Hawaiian music and culture with audiences around the world from the resort's courtyard. Today, guests can relax in the Moana's iconic rocking chairs overlooking Waikiki, explore historical artifacts in the on-site museum, and join complimentary historic tours that bring the resort's living legacy to life.See Less

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the Moana Surfrider built?

The Moana Surfrider opened on March 11, 1901. The resort, originally known as the “Moana Hotel,” was built as the first hotel in Waikiki Beach.

Does the Moana Surfrider have a fitness center?

Yes, the Moana Surfrider features the WestinWORKOUT® Fitness Studio, overlooking Kalakaua Avenue.

Does the Moana Surfrider have a spa?

Located on the second floor of the Tower wing, Moana Lani Spa holds the proud distinction of being the only oceanfront spa in Waikiki. Our Honolulu spa offers massages, body treatments, facials and nail services.

What kind of family-friendly activities does the Moana Surfrider offer?

Resort guests enjoy a variety of complimentary activities, including Storytelling with Tutu, wellness classes, ukulele and hula lessons, and lei making. A timeless tradition of keiki tea is offered as part of the resort’s classic high tea tradition.

What is the maximum height of the Moana Surfrider’s parking garage?

The maximum height of the parking garage is 6 ft. 6 inches.

Is there an on-site laundry room?

Yes, the Moana Surfrider offers on-site laundry; please visit Front Desk to confirm availability. Alternately, dry cleaning is available for a fee.

Does the Moana Surfrider offer kamaaina rates?

Yes, the resort offers Hawaii resident kamaaina rates; a valid Hawaii drivers license in the registered guests’ name with proof of a Hawaii address is required. Search corporate code L9Z when booking your reservation.

Where can I get information on how to book my wedding at the Moana Surfrider?

Please contact (808) 931-8888 for our catering team

What should I do if I lost something on-property at Moana Surfrider, a Westin Resort?

You are able to report a lost item and/or initiate a search of items found here at Moana Surfrider, a Westin Resort through https://www.chargerback.com/ . Walk-ins, phone calls, and emails through our direct channels are also available to inquire about lost items.

Are there any flat rate deals on transportation from the hotel to the airport?

In addition to our airport shuttles that can be booked ahead of time, our taxi partners TheCab offer a flat fee of $45 from our hotel to HNL airport. This is available both on-demand, and can also be pre-arranged either through our Concierge Desk or the TheCab’s phone number at (808) 422-2222. Additional bags and oversized or heavy baggage may incur additional fees.
