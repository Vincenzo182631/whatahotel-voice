### Breakfast Buffet Returns

Back by popular demand, the Moana Surfrider's beloved Breakfast Buffet returns to the Veranda oceanfront restaurant in Waikiki​ after more than three years away. Enjoy both beautiful Honolulu views and our all-you-can-eat fare from the best of both east and west. The buffet features a wide range of hot and cold selections including eggs benedict, salad, smoked salmon, breads, fruits, and juices. Other...See More breakfast favorites like bacon, local-style fried rice, an omelet station, mochi waffles, and the Moana’s signature “beautiful pancakes,” are also available. The buffet also features a Japanese breakfast station, including items such as broiled or misoyaki salmon, pickled vegetables, miso soup and natto.See Less

## Stay At One, Dine At All

The “Stay at One, Dine at All!” program invites guests of the Moana Surfrider to sign their restaurant check to their room at any restaurant or bar at any of the four Waikiki Collection resorts, including Sheraton Waikiki Beach Resort, The Royal Hawaiian, and Sheraton Princess Kaiulani. By signing the charges to your room, Marriott Bonvoy members will also earn additional points. Explore the options across the four hotels and resorts: click below for a list of participating restaurants.
