## FAQ

### How many guests can the property accommodate?

Our estates offer flexible sleeping arrangements ideal for executive teams, department retreats, and full company offsites or wellness retreats. Each property includes a mix of private king suites and shared rooms, accommodating groups from 8 to 40+ guests depending on the estate. We provide a clear room-by-room breakdown so planners can match accommodations to team hierarchy and privacy preferences. If additional bedrooms are required our concierge can assist in booking local hotels and inns.

### What onsite amenities are available for corporate and wellness groups?

Each estate is designed for both productivity and relaxation. Amenities include high-speed WiFi, large common areas for informal meetings, outdoor lounges, yoga studios, fitness spaces, chef’s kitchens, hot tubs and waterfront or nature-based settings. Select properties also offer tennis courts, pickleball, pools, kayaks, paddleboards, and private docks.

### What is the minimum stay and pricing structure for corporate and wellness retreats?

Minimum stays vary seasonally, with most retreats requiring 3–5 nights. Pricing is based on the nightly rate, plus cleaning, taxes, and any optional add-on services. Custom proposals can include meeting support, extended stays, or multi-estate reservations. Concierge service is complementary to plan your stay. Call 800.787.5829 for your custom quote.

### Are catering, private chefs, or event services available?

Yes. We regularly partner with trusted private chefs, caterers, mixologists, and service teams who specialize in multi-day corporate groups. Whether you need daily meals, welcome receptions, or executive-level dining experiences, we can coordinate everything for you with preferred partners. Kitchens are fully equipped for both chef-prepared and self-service meals.

### Can you host meetings, workshops, wellness or team-building activities onsite?

Absolutely. Our estates include spacious living areas, dining rooms, outdoor spaces, and flexible layouts suitable for presentations, strategy sessions, and breakout groups. Many teams use the beaches, pool deck, waterfront, or gardens for informal meetings. Team-building activities—such as paddleboarding, tennis, pickleball, yoga sessions, massages, golf, and fishing charters, or private workshops—can also be arranged.

### What is included with the stay, and what can be added?

Every reservation includes full access to the estate, high-speed WiFi, linens, toiletries, kitchen essentials, and use of onsite amenities such as pools, outdoor equipment, and recreational spaces. Additional services—private chefs, event coordination, wellness instructors, transportation, boat rentals, or executive experiences—can be added upon request to create a fully customized retreat.

### How do I find out about pricing and availability?

Please call us at 800.787.5829 or complete this form for a personalized quote.
