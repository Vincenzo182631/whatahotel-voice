## FAQ

### How many guests can each estate accommodate?

Depending on the property, our estates can host 2-40 overnight guests, with expansive indoor and outdoor spaces for larger family gatherings, dinners, or events. Combining both Captiva estates offers the ability to host up to 60 family members.

### Can we hire a private chef or cater special meals?

Absolutely. Our complimentary concierge will assist in coordinating private chefs, family-style dinners, or customized menus for any dietary needs, including kosher. We can also assist with restaurant reservations and recommendations as well as event planning for a special meal.

### What types of activities are available for families?

From private pools, tennis and pickleball courts, guided cultural + nature excursions, skiing, hiking, golf, water sports, or wellness sessions, every estate offers curated and location-specific experiences for both active and leisurely days, for guests of all ages.

### How private are the properties?

Each estate is secluded, with a discreet and dedicated team. Privacy and peace aren’t just a feature, they’re a reality.

Estates include expansive living rooms, terraces, firepits, media rooms, and outdoor entertainment areas — perfect for shared meals, storytelling, or quiet restoration.

### Can the concierge team assist with planning our reunion itinerary?

Absolutely. Our concierge team handles everything from activity scheduling and workout sessions to in-home wellness treatments and local excursions, ensuring the reunion runs smoothly.

We can coordinate airport transfers, private drivers, and any other logistics needed to make arrival and departure effortless for your family.

### How far in advance should we reserve an estate?

We recommend booking as soon as you have confirmed your dates with your family. While 6–12 months is preferred, our team will always accommodate shorter timelines. Select holiday weeks can sell very far in advance so please reach out as soon as possible.

### How do we get started planning our reunion?

Reach out to our guest experience team at 800.787.5829 to explore available estates, discuss your family’s needs, and begin curating a personalized, unforgettable gathering.

### Is the property pet-friendly?

Generally, yes. Small and medium dogs are allowed with prior approval. Please contact our concierge for more details.

### How do I find out about pricing and availability?

Please call us at 800.787.5829 or complete this form for a personalized quote.
