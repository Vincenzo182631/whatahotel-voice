## A New Take On  The Old West

Gem Hacienda in Taos, New Mexico, blends adobe-inspired design with modern comforts across eight secluded acres, offering a peerless Southwest retreat framed by mountain and high desert views. With nine bedrooms, outdoor and indoor fireplaces, and curated concierge services, it’s a one-of-a-kind haven for guests seeking authenticity, art, and connection to nature.

## FAQ

How many guests can Gem Hacienda accommodate?

Gem Hacienda features nine exquisitely designed bedrooms, each with an ensuite bathroom and many with a private sitting area. The hacienda comfortably accommodates large groups, corporate and wellness retreats or extended families of up to 30 guests.

Who can help with planning activities and excursions?

Our dedicated complementary concierge is at your service from the moment you book, arranging every detail befroe your arrival so your stay is effortless and unforgettable. From chefs, bartenders, housekeeping to activities and wellness sessions, let our concierge help plan your perfect trip.

Can we have meals prepared at the estate?

Absolutely. Our concierge can arrange private, chef-curated meals, cooking classes, or intimate dining experiences tailored to your tastes and dietary requirements. The chef’s kitchen is also perfect for your group to make their own meals.

Is Gem Hacienda suitable for skiers outdoor enthusiasts?

Yes. The estate is surrounded by nature and located near world-renowned skiing and snowboarding at Taos Ski Valley. Hiking trails, and other outdoor adventures like White water rafting, hot-air ballooning, horseback riding are all a short drive from the hacienda. Then come back for a game of bocce ball onsite or sit in the hot tub and watch the stunning night skies.

Does the estate have modern conveniences like WiFi and air conditioning?

Yes. Gem Hacienda is a historic abode structure with all the modern conveniences. High-speed internet, smart TVs, air conditioning, washers and dryers are all available.

What makes Gem Hacienda ideal for art and culture lovers?

The estate is just minutes away from Taos galleries, museums, and cultural landmarks, offering guests the opportunity to conveniently explore the rich artistic heritage of the region. From Taos Pueblo, a UNESCO heritage site, to galleries and museums in the land of Georgia Okeeffe, everyone in your group will find your stay enriching.

Is Gem Hacienda pet-friendly?

Yes! Well-behaved dogs are welcome with a pet fee, so the whole family, including furry members, can enjoy a getaway in this expansive property. Please speak to our team before booking to have your dog’s stay approved.
