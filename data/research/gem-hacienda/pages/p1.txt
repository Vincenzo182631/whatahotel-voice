Welcome to Gem Hacienda, a premier Taos luxury rental designed for privacy, style, and curated experiences.

Nestled on eight private acres in the foothills of the Sangre de Cristo Mountains, this adobe-style estate blends natural beauty with luxury accommodations for a stay that feels both expansive and intimate. Just minutes from downtown Taos and the Taos Pueblo, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the oldest continuously inhabited communities in the U.S. This secluded Taos property offers the perfect place to relax, reconnect, and explore northern New Mexico.

From outdoor adventures to wellness experiences and fine dining, Gem Hacienda is a rare destination where modern comfort meets historic charm, ideal for both short-term vacation rentals and long-term stays in Taos.

### How many guests can the property accommodate?

With 9 bedrooms and multiple gathering areas, the home comfortably accommodates large groups while still allowing privacy for couples, families, or teams. Amenities include ensuite bedrooms with fireplaces, a chef’s kitchen, hot tub, outdoor cooking horno, multiple indoor/outdoor lounges, high-speed internet, and more. It is one of the best luxury vacation rentals in the area.

### Where in Taos is Gem Hacienda located?

Gem Hacienda is 5 minutes from Taos Plaza, with easy access to Taos Ski Valley, hiking trails, and other key attractions. For those comparing places to stay in Taos NM, the estate offers both privacy and proximity.

### How close is the property to Taos attractions like the plaza, ski valley, or hiking trails?

The Taos Plaza in town with it’s eclectic shops and restaurants is a convenient 5 minutes away. Taos Ski Valley and popular trailheads are within a 30-minute drive. Local trailheads can be reached in about 10 minutes, while the entrance to Carson National Forest is equally close for hiking and wildlife exploration. Guests are only 5 minutes from cross-country skiing and 30 minutes from Taos Ski Valley for world-class downhill runs. The iconic Rio Grande Gorge is also within easy reach, offering dramatic views and unforgettable sunsets. Gem Hacienda is perfect for those seeking Taos ski lodge convenience without compromising privacy.
