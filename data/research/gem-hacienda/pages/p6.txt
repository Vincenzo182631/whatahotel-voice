## Perfect For Outdoor Thrills

Taos Ski Valley is legendary among snow enthusiasts, offering some of the most challenging terrain in North America. With over 300 inches of annual snowfall and runs for every skill level, it’s a winter wonderland for skiers and snowboarders. After a day on the slopes, retreat to Gem Hacienda for hot tub soaks under starlit skies.

When the snow melts, Taos transforms into a hiker’s paradise. Explore trails through alpine meadows, ancient forests, and dramatic canyon landscapes. The Wheeler Peak Trail leads to New Mexico’s highest summit, while the Rio Grande Gorge offers breathtaking views and whitewater rafting opportunities. Mountain biking, hot air ballooning, and fly fishing round out the endless summer adventures.

For a few extra thrills, explore the epic Rio Grande Gorge while kayaking or white water rafting. Whatever you fancy, our dedicated concierge can arrange it all for you.

## Discover Our Thriving Art Scene

Taos has been a magnet for artists since the early 1900s, drawing luminaries like Georgia O’Keeffe who found inspiration in its dramatic landscapes. That creative spirit is alive and well today. Wander through dozens of galleries showcasing everything from traditional Southwestern art to contemporary works. Visit the historic Taos Pueblo, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that’s been continuously inhabited for over 1,000 years, or explore the Millicent Rogers Museum to discover Native American and Hispanic art and jewelry.

The town’s vibrant art scene extends beyond galleries. You’ll find murals, sculptures, and installations throughout Taos Plaza and the surrounding neighborhoods. Whether you’re a serious collector or simply appreciate beauty, Taos offers an art-filled experience unlike anywhere else.

## Why Taos?

Taos is a place where ancient traditions meet modern luxury, where rugged mountains give way to serene high-desert landscapes, and where every season brings new reasons to visit. It's a destination that feeds the soul—whether you're carving fresh powder, browsing world-class galleries, or simply watching the sunset paint the mountains in shades of gold and crimson.

Unlike crowded resort towns, Taos maintains an authentic, laid-back vibe. The community embraces visitors while preserving its rich cultural heritage. You'll find farm-to-table restaurants, cozy cafes, and local breweries alongside centuries-old adobe buildings and sacred sites.

Around the area

- Taos Ski Valley with legendary terrain and 300+ inches of annual snowfall
- Historic Taos Pueblo, a UNESCO World Heritage Site continuously inhabited for 1,000+ years
- Dozens of art galleries showcasing Southwestern and contemporary works
- Rio Grande Gorge for whitewater rafting, hiking, and breathtaking canyon views
- Farm-to-table restaurants and local breweries featuring New Mexican cuisine
