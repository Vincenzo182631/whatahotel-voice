Signature Safari Suites in Masai Mara
Signature Suites
Elevated among the treetops overlooking the Sand River, our Signature Safari Suites redefine safari living through exceptional space, privacy and personalized service. Designed as refined residential retreats in the heart of the Masai Mara, these exclusive tented suites seamlessly blend indoor and outdoor living with immersive wildlife experiences, dedicated Encholiek service and thoughtful luxury at every turn. Whether traveling as a family, celebrating a milestone occasion or seeking a more private safari escape, these Suites offer a rare sense of connection to nature, to loved ones and to the timeless rhythm of the Mara.
Presidential Suite
Positioned to embrace sweeping views of the Sand River and the surrounding wilderness stretching across the Masai Mara and Serengeti, the Presidential Suite offers the Camp’s most exclusive safari experience. This expansive 617 sqm two-bedroom residence features refined indoor and outdoor lounges, a private dining area, kitchenette, heated plunge pool and expansive viewing deck designed for elevated entertaining and private retreat living. From sunrise coffee overlooking the riverbanks to evenings beneath starlit skies, every detail is thoughtfully crafted for extraordinary moments in the wild.

Sunrise to Starlight at the Deck
Framed by sweeping views of the Sand River and the vast plains beyond, the Presidential Suite’s expansive outdoor deck invites moments of quiet indulgence surrounded by nature’s grandeur. Featuring a heated infinity plunge pool, elegant lounging spaces and open-air living areas, the deck is designed for sunrise coffees, private sundowners and evenings beneath the stars, embraced by the beauty of both Masai Mara and Serengeti.

A Private Lounge Experience
Thoughtfully designed for elevated gatherings and relaxed moments, the Presidential Suite’s living room blends refined residential comfort with sweeping views of the surrounding landscape. Featuring elegant lounge seating, a kitchenette, a private dining area and a dedicated bar, the space creates a seamless setting for entertaining, intimate dining and unwinding in complete privacy.

Two Bedroom Executive Suite
Designed for families and shared safari journeys, the 232 sqm Two-Bedroom Executive Suite offers a warm and elegant retreat immersed in the beauty of the Masai Mara. Featuring a king bedroom and a second bedroom with two queen beds, the suite includes spacious interconnected living areas, refined safari-inspired interiors, expansive outdoor decks and a heated plunge pool. Overlooking the Sand River and surrounding wilderness, it is thoughtfully designed for guests seeking comfort, privacy and meaningful moments together in the heart of the wild.

Where Every Journey is Shared
From sunrise game drives to evenings beneath the stars, the Two-Bedroom Executive Suite is designed to bring families and friends closer together. Spacious indoor and outdoor living areas create room to reconnect between adventures, while the privacy of two beautifully appointed bedrooms ensures every guest enjoys their own place to unwind. With your dedicated Encholiek curating every detail, each shared moment becomes part of an unforgettable Masai Mara story.

Included Experiences
Every stay is thoughtfully crafted to immerse guests in the spirit of the Masai Mara through personalized service, enriching experiences and elevated comforts. From tailored safari planning and dedicated Encholiek service to twice-daily game drives led by expert KPSGA guides, each journey is designed around meaningful discovery. Guests can savor curated culinary experiences in extraordinary settings, enjoy complimentary transfers to and from Keekorok and Serena Airstrips, and reconnect through restorative wellness rituals at Whispering Tree Spa inspired by nature, local traditions and the rhythms of the Mara.
