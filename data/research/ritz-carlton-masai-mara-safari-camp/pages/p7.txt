Discover a Dazzling Destination That Enthralls
Safari
Nestled in the Sand River region of the Masai Mara, along the Kenya-Tanzania border, our treetop Masai Mara safari lodge offers unrivaled views of the Mara-Serengeti ecosystem. Overlooking the Sand River, this location immerses you in nature’s raw beauty. Embark on a private guided safari in an open-air luxury Safari Land Cruiser witnessing dramatic river crossings and encountering the Big Five. Experience the untamed wilderness with uninterrupted views of sweeping savannahs and herds roaming freely. Explore the historic Kenya-Tanzania Border Stone, linking two legendary ecosystems, or immerse in Masai culture through storytelling, music, and beadwork. Whether tracking predators, soaring over the plains in a hot air balloon, or sharing a sundowner beneath the infinite African sky, every moment at our camp in the Masai Mara Game Reserve is a seamless blend of adventure, discovery, and the timeless spirit of the wild.

Led by expert guides in a private open-air luxury Safari Land Cruiser, this intimate experience offers a front-row seat to nature’s most breathtaking moments. The Great Migration: Nature’s Grand Spectacle
Follow the ancient path of the Great Migration as millions of wildebeest and zebra traverse the Sand River. Witness the drama of daring river crossings at the Mara River, the thrill of predator-prey encounters, and the raw beauty of the untamed savannah. With private guided safaris along the migration route, this once-in-a-lifetime experience places you at the heart of nature’s most extraordinary event.
Exclusive Destination Dining
Savor the magic of the Masai Mara with exclusive bush dining experiences. Enjoy a gourmet breakfast or picnic lunch in the heart of the wilderness, surrounded by roaming wildlife. Indulge in a champagne breakfast along the Sand River, where hippos bask and wildlife gathers, creating an intimate and unforgettable dining moment that blends luxury with the raw beauty of nature.
An Aerial Adventure
Soar above the Masai Mara at sunrise on a spectacular hot air balloon safari. Drift silently over golden savannahs, winding rivers, and roaming wildlife, capturing breathtaking aerial views of the Big Five. As you descend, celebrate with a champagne breakfast in the bush, making this once-in-a-lifetime experience truly unforgettable.
A Journey Into Tradition
Step into the heart of Masai culture with an immersive visit, where tradition and heritage come to life. Engage with the Masai people through storytelling, beadwork, music, and dance, gaining insight into their deep connection with the land. Witness age-old customs, learn about their nomadic lifestyle, and leave with a profound appreciation for this legendary tribe.

More Safari Experiences
Bird Watching Safari
Discover the Masai Mara’s rich birdlife on guided game drives and within the serene surroundings of the Camp. Spot over 500 species, from vibrant lilac-breasted rollers to majestic African fish eagles. Listen to the melodies of sunbirds at dawn and witness raptors soaring over the plains, creating an unforgettable experience for both avid birdwatchers and casual nature lovers alike.
Capture the Essence of the Mara
Enhance your safari experience with our Photography Studio, where advanced cameras and expert guidance help you frame the perfect shot. Whether capturing wildlife in motion, vibrant birdlife, or sweeping savannah landscapes, our resident photographers refine your skills, ensuring each moment is preserved in breathtaking detail. Immerse in the magic of the Mara, blending luxury with timeless memories.
Kenya Tanzania Border Stone
Stand at the historic Border Stone, a symbolic marker linking the Masai Mara and Serengeti—two of Africa’s most renowned wildlife reserves. Experience the seamless connection between these legendary ecosystems, where landscapes and animals know no borders. A visit here offers a rare chance to witness nature’s boundless beauty and the rich heritage of East Africa. This Stone is steps away from the Camp.
On Property Animal Sightings
From the comfort of your elevated suite, experience the wild unfold beneath you. Designed to blend seamlessly with nature, each suite is raised above the ground, allowing wildlife to roam freely below. Watch as antelopes wander, and elephants pass by, while vibrant birds and butterflies flutter through the air.
The Call of Dusk
As the golden hues of sunset reflect over the Camp, the deep, resonant call of a traditional Masai horn echoes through the air. A Masai Moran, adorned in full regalia, sounds the horn signaling the transition from day to night. The scent of burning olorien wood, a sacred blessing, fills the air. Guests gather for a vibrant Masai welcome dance and storytelling, sipping rich Kenyan tea in a moment of cultural connection.

Frequently Asked Questions
What types of safari experiences are included in my stay?
Your stay includes two daily exclusive game drives in a private open-air Safari Land Cruiser, guided by certified tour guides. You can also enjoy nature walks and birdwatching on property, and optional experiences such as hot air balloon safaris, Rhino Sanctuary visits and cultural visits.
When is the best time to witness the Great Migration?
Wildlife viewing is exceptional through-out the year in Masai Mara. However, The Great Migration typically passes through the Masai Mara between July and October, when millions of wildebeest and zebra cross the Mara River, braving dramatic river crossings and predator encounters.
What wildlife can I expect to see while on Safari?
The Masai Mara is home to the Big Five—lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino—along with cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, hippos, crocodiles, and many others animals including over 500 bird species in their natural habitat.
How close do we get to the animals on Safari?
Our tour guides are certified by The Kenya Professional Safari Guides Association (KPSGA) and ensure safe yet intimate wildlife encounters, following strict conservation practices to respect the animals and their natural habitat.
What should I pack for a Safari?
For an effortless and comfortable safari experience, we recommend light, neutral-colored clothing, a hat, sunglasses, and walking shoes. Mornings and evenings can be refreshingly cool, so a light jacket is ideal. To enhance your journey, we provide a traditional Masai shuka for warmth, along with sunscreen, binoculars, and professional Canon cameras—ensuring you capture every breathtaking moment in vivid detail. Closer to your arrival, our Reservations team will be happy to advise on the weather and any packing essentials.
Are Safaris suitable for Children?
We welcome families and offer enriching safaris for children aged six and above. Young explorers can join game drives and kid-friendly on-property nature walks, learning about animal tracks, flora, and the secrets of the savannah. At Camp, they can immerse in Masai storytelling, music, and traditional activities such as spear throwing, fire lighting, and beadwork. While safaris are exciting, parents should remain mindful, as wildlife is unpredictable. Our expert guides ensure a safe, memorable experience for the whole family, and child-minding services are available at the camp if needed for added convenience.
