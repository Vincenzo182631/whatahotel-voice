Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Masai Mara Safari Camp
Embrace the Wilderness on a Journey of a Lifetime
Elevated among lush trees in the heart of the Masai Mara National Reserve, The Ritz-Carlton, Masai Mara introduces its legendary service to a revered safari destination. Sustainably designed in harmony with nature, this elegant Masai Mara safari camp blends luxury, authenticity and adventure to craft once-in-a-lifetime moments. Architecturally unique and seamlessly integrated into the natural wilderness, the 20 suites at this Luxury Safari Camp in Masai Mara feature private decks, plunge pools, personalized butler service and stunning views. Awaken with a profound sense of nature’s awe as you witness the mesmerizing sights and sounds. Fuel your days’ adventures with expertly crafted dishes inspired by local and international flavors, complemented by an exclusive wine cellar. Indulge in wellness treatments integrating native traditions, explore the Map Room or discover the Photography Studio to capture the magic of your luxury safari in Kenya's Masai Mara and transform your memories into lasting treasures.
Concierge Service
Before arrival, enjoy a personal call with our General or Camp Manager to understand what matters most to you. From preferences to special moments, we tailor every detail - shaping a safari experience that feels deeply personal and entirely your own.

What to Know for Your Safari Adventure
What experiences are included in my stay?
Enjoy luxurious accommodation, heated plunge pool with a personalized butler service, and private game drives in an open Safari Land Cruiser with expert guides. Capture stunning moments with complimentary Canon cameras and guidance from resident photographers. Indulge in all-inclusive gourmet dining, Champagne breakfasts, and wine-pairing sessions. Experience bush breakfasts, sundowners, and cultural visits. Stay active at our Wellness Hub with an open-air heated pool, gym, and studio. A pair of binoculars, laundry service, Wi-Fi, and Serena Keekorok airstrip transfers are included, ensuring a seamless and unforgettable safari experience.
Do I need a Visa to enter Kenya?
Yes, visitors must apply for an Electronic Travel Authorization (eVisa) before arrival. Some exemptions apply, and details can be found on the Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) website. Ensure your visa is secured well in advance to avoid delays.
Do I need any vaccinations prior to my travel?
Your journey to the Masai Mara begins with thoughtful preparation, ensuring a seamless and worry-free adventure. While no specific vaccinations are universally mandatory for entry into Kenya, requirements may vary based on your country of origin or recent travel history—some travelers may need to present a Yellow Fever vaccination certificate.
We strongly recommend checking with your local health authorities or travel clinic at least eight weeks before departure to confirm the latest health requirements and recommendations. Though malaria risk is minimal in the Masai Mara, your healthcare provider can advise on any preventive measures suited to your itinerary, allowing you to travel with confidence and peace of mind.
What is the Child Policy at the Camp?
To cultivate an atmosphere of tranquility and ensure the well-being and safety of all our guests, we have established a minimum age requirement of six years. Families can enjoy tailored activities that make the safari adventure engaging for young explorers. Contact us for customized family-friendly experiences.
How do I get to the Camp?
By Air: The quickest way to get to Masai Mara is by air. The closest local airstrip is Keekorok (KEU) Serena (HKOA), and flight time from Nairobi is approximately 1-hour. Flights are available from Nairobi Wilson Airport (WIL), which is approximately 12 km from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO).
On arrival at the Keekorok airstrip, you will be met by our team, followed by a 1-hour short game drive to the Camp.
Luggage is limited to 15kg per person (soft bags only) on flight. Should you have additional luggage for the rest of your journey, we would be delighted to assist with storing it in a safe locker.
What currency should I bring, and are credit cards accepted?
The Kenyan Shilling (KES) is the local currency, but US dollars are widely accepted.
We recommend exchanging money at the international airport or city hotels, though ATMs are available in Nairobi for withdrawals.
Credit Cards:
Major credit cards are widely accepted. When possible, opt for card payments over carrying large sums of cash. However, please stay vigilant as fraud is still a threat.
At the camp, there’s no need to carry cash—your experience is designed for seamless indulgence. Any purchases, whether a bespoke treasure from the gift shop or a rejuvenating spa treatment, can be effortlessly settled by credit card.
What should I pack for my Safari?
Pack light, breathable clothing in neutral tones, long-sleeved shirts and pants for sun and insect protection, and comfortable closed-toe shoes for safaris. Bring a jacket or sweater for chilly mornings and evenings. Don’t forget a hat and sunglasses for sun protection.
Remember to pack light and consider any luggage restrictions that may apply to your mode of travel. Most importantly, pack an open mind and a sense of adventure for your unforgettable safari in Masai Mara.
What is the weather like in Masai Mara?
Kenya’s unique position along the equator ensures a consistently pleasant climate throughout the year, with only subtle seasonal shifts. From December to March, expect warm, sun-kissed days averaging 30°C (86°F), while the migration months of July to September bring slightly cooler, refreshing temperatures.
Evenings in the Masai Mara are beautifully crisp, with temperatures dipping to around 15°C (59°F)—a perfect contrast to the warmth of the day. Though it rarely gets colder, the gentle evening breeze adds a touch of wilderness serenity to your nights under the African sky.
When is the best time to witness the Great Migration in the Masai Mara?
The Great Migration is one of nature’s most breathtaking spectacles, a timeless journey of over two million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles across the Serengeti and Masai Mara in search of fresh grazing lands.
From July to October, the Mara becomes the stage for the dramatic river crossings, where herds brave the crocodile-laden waters of the Mara River—a sight both thrilling and awe-inspiring. January to March marks the calving season, when thousands of newborns take their first steps, drawing the attention of Africa’s great predators. As the cycle continues, the herds move north between April and June, before slowly returning south in November and December.
Our Camp is perfectly positioned near the migration route, offering unparalleled front-row seats to one of the world’s greatest natural wonders.
How much are the Masai Mara National Reserve Park fees?
2025 Park entry fees per day are $100 (Jan–June) / $200 (July–Dec) for adults and $50 for children (9-17 years). Children below 8 years of age enter for free.
Fees can be paid directly at the entrance gate of the park, or we could pre-pay for you in advance for a seamless experience. A prepayment of the park fees and a passport copy of each guest will be required.
