# Welcome to Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel

## Experience Downtown from our 5-star hotel in the Saigon Ward

Perfectly situated in Ho Chi Minh City's District 1, steps from the Saigon Opera House and Nguyen Hue Walking Street, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel offers an unrivalled address in the heart of the city's vibrant energy, combining world-class hospitality with authentic Vietnamese warmth. Retreat to 500 elegant rooms and suites, including the Grand Opera Tower, a private luxury hotel within a hotel featuring opera-inspired design and bespoke service. Rejuvenate at the heated saltwater pool, Sheraton Fitness, and Evolve Wellness Center. Discover destination dining at Saigon Café, Li Bai, The Lounge, and Hai Bar, Dong Khoi's highest rooftop bar. Host conferences, weddings, and MICE gatherings across 2,075 sqm of flexible event space, including Summit, Dong Khoi's highest meeting and event venue. The hotel was the first Marriott International property in Vietnam to earn LEED Gold certification and is also Green Globe certified, reflecting its commitment to responsible hospitality.

## First Marriott Hotel in Vietnam to Achieve LEED Gold

Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel has officially achieved LEED Gold Certification (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design), recognizing its performance in energy efficiency, water conservation, indoor air quality and waste management. The milestone makes the Dong Khoi landmark the first and only hotel under Marriott International in Vietnam to earn this prestigious certification.

## The Leading Hotel for Meetings, Incentive, Conference and Exhibitions

Plan meetings and events in the heart of Ho Chi Minh City at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel, located on Dong Khoi Street near Saigon Opera House. Venue options range from executive boardrooms and flexible breakout rooms to the 720-square-metre pillarless Grand Ballroom, accommodating up to 1,000 guests theatre-style or 650 for a banquet. On Level 23, Summit offers natural daylight, skyline views, a 14-seat boardroom and flexible configurations for up to 212 guests theatre-style. Dedicated convention specialists, audiovisual support and tailored Asian and Western catering bring meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions (MICE) to life—from initial planning through final execution.

### Discover Saigon from Dong Khoi Street

Saigon, the economic capital and the gateway to explore Vietnam, is the perfect mix of tradition and modernity. With significant landmarks, historical sites, and sleek skyscrapers, Saigon is an alluring gathering place for both locals and travelers from around the world, offering numerous recreational activities and providing a gastronomic paradise for self-confessed foodies. Start your journey from our Ho Chi Minh District 1 hotel and explore a collection of nearby attractions, museums and cultural hubs including the Opera House, Notre Dame church, Takashimaya and Saigon Post Office. Or simply explore Saigon through the eyes of a local, from inspiring art tours to the bustling alleyways, visit our Experiences page or meet the team at our Concierge desk to start your adventures.

## Make Responsible Choices During Your Stay

As the sustainable hotel operator, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel invites guests to join us on a journey towards responsible travel. Together, we encourage you to contribute to a more sustainable planet and help reduce our global carbon, water, and waste footprints towards net-zero carbon emissions. Over the years, this family hotel in downtown Ho Chi Minh City has been encompassing initiatives including energy saving alternatives, responsible sourcing, waste reduction, etc. In 2025, the hotel obtained the prestigious recognition from Green Globe, a global leader in sustainable tourism certification. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel?

The check-in time at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel?

On-site self parking is available at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel. Self parking is available at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel, with rates of ₫100000.00 per hour, and ₫0.00 perstay.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel?

The closest airport near Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel is Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN). SGN is located approximately 7.9 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel have an airport shuttle to Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN)?

Yes, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel has an airport shuttle to Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN).

Does Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel have a Sheraton Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel has Sheraton Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Sheraton Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
