## A Luxury Hotel Within a Hotel in District 1, Saigon

Steps from the Saigon Opera House, Grand Opera Tower is a luxury hotel within a hotel: a quieter, 120-room wing of Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel at 88 Dong Khoi Street, District 1 (Saigon Ward), Ho Chi Minh City.

## Reserve our Luxury Hotel Suites near Saigon Opera House

The Tower welcomes guests through a private lift lobby to The Salon, an exclusive lounge for check-in, breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails and all-day refreshments. Every room includes a wellness kit for yoga and stretching, while suites up to 68 sqm and selected Specialty Rooms offer wellness corner, fitness equipment, kitchenette or laundry facility.

## What Is Grand Opera Tower?

Grand Opera Tower is a separately serviced luxury wing of Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel. It is not a separate hotel: you simply choose a Grand Opera room type on Marriott.com and keep every Sheraton facility and Marriott Bonvoy benefit. What changes is the stay itself, in a smaller, quieter tower with its own arrival, its own lounge and its own team.

## The Salon: A Private Lounge for Grand Opera Tower Guests

Inspired by the salons where music and conversation once met, The Salon is Grand Opera Tower's private lounge and, for Grand Opera Tower guests, the hotel's front door. Its day unfolds in three acts, breakfast, afternoon tea and evening cocktails, with refreshments always within reach.

## Private Check-In and Check-Out

Arrive and depart at The Salon's own counter, welcomed by a host who soon remembers how you take your coffee.

## Breakfast

From 6 am, breakfast is prepared for Grand Opera Tower guests and served at an unhurried pace, until 10 am on weekdays and 11:30 am at weekends.

## Afternoon Tea

From 2:30 to 4:30 pm, tea arrives with sweets and drinks created only for Grand Opera Tower guests.

## Evening Cocktails

From 6 to 8 pm, evenings turn to crafted cocktails at the bespoke bar, among displays of traditional Vietnamese instruments.

## Grand Opera Privileges

Refreshments all day, priority service, nightly turndown and a parting gift come with every Grand Opera Tower stay.

## Rooms and Suites at Grand Opera Tower

Grand Opera Tower's 120 rooms and suites come in four categories, from 42 to 68 sqm. Each is inspired by the neighbouring Saigon Opera House and by Vietnamese craftsmanship, expressed in lotus motifs, quatrefoil doors and mother-of-pearl inlay. Every room includes The Salon, a wellness kit, wireless charging and a garment steamer.

## Wellness in Your Room and Across the Hotel

Grand Opera Tower is made for guests who keep their routine on the road. Wellness begins in your room and continues one lift ride away, on the hotel's fifth-floor wellness level.

## Inspired by the Opera. Defined by Saigon.

Just steps from the historic Saigon Opera House, the Grand Opera Tower reflects a deep-rooted connection to the city's cultural heartbeat. Drawing inspiration from the architectural grandeur of its iconic neighbor and Vietnam's rich artistic heritage, this luxury accommodation in downtown Ho Chi Minh City tells a story of craftsmanship and pride through every thoughtfully composed detail.

## Location: Dong Khoi Street, District 1

Grand Opera Tower stands at 88 Dong Khoi Street in Saigon Ward, the area long known as District 1 and the historic heart of Ho Chi Minh City. The Saigon Opera House, Nguyen Hue Walking Street and the metro are a 1–2 minute walk from its quiet floors.
