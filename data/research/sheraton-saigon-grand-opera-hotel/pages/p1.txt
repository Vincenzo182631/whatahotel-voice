# Stylishly-Renovated Rooms & Suites

## Accommodations

Welcome to your urban retreat at Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel. Discover 500 elegantly appointed downtown Ho Chi Minh City hotel rooms and suites, including 120 newly renovated luxury accommodations at the Grand Opera Tower. Each space is thoughtfully designed to reflect the city's vibrant spirit, blending contemporary style with Vietnam’s rich cultural heritage—highlighted by the graceful lotus flower motif and interior inspiration from the Saigon Opera House. Whether you're here for business or leisure, enjoy modern amenities, convenient layouts, and views overlooking the stunning city skyline or the Saigon River. Your ideal sanctuary awaits in the heart of Saigon.

### Cultural Design Elements

Our accommodations are adorned delicate lotus motifs that celebrate Vietnam's national flower, symbolizing purity and optimism. These artistic elements blend with contemporary furnishings, crafting spaces that honor local heritage while offering modern comfort. From custom wall coverings to locally inspired artwork, each room invites guests to immerse themselves in the rich cultural tapestry of Saigon.

### Designed for the Global Professional

Designed for business travelers, our rooms feature ergonomic workstations, high-speed Wi-Fi, and multi-device power outlets for optimal productivity. Located in the heart of the city, the hotel is just minutes from major corporate offices, banks, and the business district. Additionally, the nearby metro station offers convenient access to industrial parks and key business hubs throughout Ho Chi Minh City.

### Your Cultural Gateway

Relax in accommodations that serve as your perfect base for exploring Saigon’s rich culture. Our prime location places you within walking distance of iconic landmarks like the Opera House, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and Central Post Office, as well as leisurely strolls to high-end boutiques along Dong Khoi Street. Our dedicated Concierge team is always on hand to assist with city tours and cultural experiences.

## Newly Reimagined Luxury Accommodation

Grand Opera Tower is a sanctuary of privacy and distinction – a luxurious hotel within a hotel inside Sheraton Saigon Grand Opera Hotel. Its design-led rooms, enriched with Opera House–influenced details and Vietnamese heritage, create a refined and intimate atmosphere. Guests enjoy exclusive access to locally-inspired dining, The Salon, a private lounge, and bespoke, anticipatory service that elevates every stay. Beyond its doors, curated cultural experiences connect travelers to the artistry, style, and energy of Saigon. Just steps from the city’s iconic Opera House, Grand Opera Tower redefines what it means to stay at the heart of the city with discretion, elegance, and ease.
