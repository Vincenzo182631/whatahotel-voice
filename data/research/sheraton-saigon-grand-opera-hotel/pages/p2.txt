## Signature Rooms

The Grand Opera Tower presents 120 newly renovated rooms and suites, where Opera House-inspired design and Vietnamese craftsmanship harmonize to create a setting of refined intimacy. From the 42 sqm Grand...See More Opera Studio to the 68 sqm Premier Suite, each space is thoughtfully appointed with modern luxuries, including wireless charging stations and in-room garment steamers. Select rooms offer tranquil views of the Saigon River and historic Bason Bridge, while Bay View elevate the experience with sweeping cityscapes and exclusive chill-out nooks. Guests enjoy exclusive access to The Salon, a private lounge offering bespoke service, daily breakfast, and seamless check-in and check-out.
