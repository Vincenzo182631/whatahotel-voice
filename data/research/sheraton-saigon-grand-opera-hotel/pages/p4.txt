As night falls, embark on a culinary journey from East to West at our downtown Ho Chi Minh seafood restaurant​. Immerse yourself in a sea breeze of freshly cooked seafood, the star of the gastronomic showcase, from freshly grilled lobsters, oysters, prawns and scampi cooked to order to our remarkable chilled seafood displays adorned with the freshest crabs, prawns, shrimps and snails. ## Best Innovation Restaurant of the Year

Awarded by the prestigious LUXUO ASIA AWARD 2024, this recognition celebrates Li Bai's dedication to pushing the boundaries of Cantonese cuisine while honoring its rich heritage. The restaurant's innovative approach stems from a unique blend of time-honored techniques and creative interpretations. With a 22‑year reputation for serving up to 30,000 diners across three generations, Li Bai’s culinary team, backed by over 30 years of hands‑on experience, continues to masterfully employ traditional methods. These foundations are then elevated with inspired dishes drawing influence from various regions of China, using locally-sourced ingredients and a keen focus on aesthetic presentation, creating a truly unique and unforgettable dining experience.

An Imperial Dish for Royalty

## 10 Day Dry Age Peking Duck With Pancake

More than just a legendary dish, our Peking Duck is a symbolic experience of dining in Saigon - meticulous in preparation and sophisticated in service. At Li Bai, Peking Duck is served with the finest iteration and live carving. Caramel in color, its crispy skin is the star of the show featuring a fragrant yet refreshing combination of flavors. The tender meat is cut up and served as the second course with a choice of XO fried rice or noodles. Based on our “Go Local” mantra”, the dry-aged 10 days cage-free ducks from Mekong Delta are notably used to bring a more succulent yet juicy taste like no other.

Popular Weekend Yum Cha in Saigon

## Top Spot for Flawless Dim Sum

Bring your family together at our restaurant near Saigon Opera House with yum cha and make the most of your weekend gatherings. Since 2003, Li Bai restaurant has never failed to satisfy even the most discerning palates with an authentic house crafted yum cha experience, exceptional quality dim sum and unforgettable hospitality. Menu offerings range from abundant variations of har gow, char siu, xiao long bao, siew mai, egg tarts to creative and modern interpretations such as Superior Abalone dumplings, Truffled Pork Xiao Long Bao, Steamed ‘Sacha’ Prawn dumplings and Steamed sea cucumber and asparagus dumplings in abalone sauce. Highlights include elaborate dishes of BBQ meats and soups exemplifying Cantonese culinary excellence as well as tempting desserts, namely silken tofu pudding with pineapple carpaccio. All are paired with an extensive collection of premium teas such as Pu-erh, oolong, Te Kwan yin and the signature Tay ho lotus, refereed as the historic treasure tea in Vietnam.

Newly-Furbished Ambiance

## The Story of Li Bai

With our latest uplifting renovation, Li Bai features brand-new ambiance embodying contemporary aesthetics inspired by Li Bai’s, the poet, his stories and poems, a harmonious interplay of his life journey with nostalgic elements. The tranquil landscape sets the mood from the entrance whilst notable décor stars the koi fish represent an auspicious blessing in Asian culture as you walk through the gateway. Inside the restaurant, our newly designed interior furnished with gold tones features exquisite artworks depicting Li Bai’s milestones, from the beautiful Nga Mi mountains in Sichuan where Li Bai showcased his talent at the age of 15 to the following years when Li Bai embarked on a journey to explore magnificent mountains and rivers, two most distinctive elements in his poems.

Explore the Saigon Food Scene

## Eat Like a Local

Its not only Pho and Banh mi, two most popular Vietnamese’s cuisine, experience a wide range of mouthwatering local dishes to indulge in. Get excited and dine out with our hand-picked selection of the best eateries in the city downtown. Love to explore outside? Walk through street food staples or onto fine dining experiences located just few steps from the hotel. Scroll down to check our top recommendations signature.

Top recommendations

### Pho Minh

No elaborate sign, Pho Minh nestled in the tiny alley has captivated Saigonese’ hearts with its traditional pho bowl for more than 7 decades. It is one of the oldest pho stands in Saigon.

### Hu Tieu Thanh Xuan

Serving “hu tieu” - For over 70 years, this eatery with an old hand-painted sign and greetings from the woman with traditional Ba Ba shirt has fulfilled local diners’ stomachs with their signature noodle originating from My Tho.

### Com Tho Chuyen Ky

Founded in 1948 and serving clay pot rice, this is such a rare eatery in Saigon reserving Cantonese clay pot rice style - served in the same vessel it’s cooked in with an assortment of side dishes.

### Banh Mi Huynh Hoa

You can find banh mi anywhere in Vietnam, but Banh mi Huynh Hoa is like no other. Favored among the locals and visitors, the banh mi here is doubled in size comparing with a normal banh mi, featuring generous fillings with pate, a variety of meats, fresh herbs and pickled vegetables inside a soft and crunchy baguette. Taste the heaven-like bite!

### Ca Phe Trung

Egg coffee originated during the 1940s with the idea of mixing egg yolks with coffee when milk became scarce in Ha Noi. Creamy, soft, and heaven-like - it is a must for coffee snobs!

### Mien Ga

Serving “mien ga” - rice vermicelli with chicken soup & “pho ga” – chicken pho. Established in 1975, Mai Xuan Canh located just a few steps from Notre Dame Cathedral of Saigon is famous for its see-through broth with delicate taste.
