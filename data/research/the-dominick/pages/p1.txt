# HOTELS IN NEW YORK

## Artfully   Forward

Here at The Dominick, every detail is designed with intention. Its sleek glass tower stands 46 stories tall and spans 374,000 sq. ft., anchored by a quiet stretch of Dominick Street—an homage built into its name. Inside, minimalist design meets warmth through natural materials and Fendi Casa furnishings. Each of the 391 guestrooms, one of which is a penthouse-turned-gallery, features floor-to-ceiling windows, offering unparalleled views.

Step outside and into the cultural pulse of Lower Manhattan. From the cobblestone streets of SoHo to the creative energy of Hudson Square, The Dominick places you at the intersection of world-class fashion, renowned art galleries, and New York City’s most sought-after dining.
