# Welcome to The St. Regis Aruba Resort

## An Exquisite Debut in Aruba’s Renowned Palm Beach

Bon Bini to The St. Regis Aruba Resort, a new beachfront Noord resort offering breathtaking views and endless sunsets over the Caribbean Sea. Our resort in Aruba features 252 luxurious rooms, including 52 suites with beach access and stunning ocean vistas. Indulge in curated activities and top-tier amenities, all delivered with the warm hospitality of Aruba. Relax in one of our private cabanas by the infinity pools, bask in the sun, or treat yourself to a bespoke experience at The St. Regis Spa, a serene haven designed to soothe and rejuvenate at our Noord, Aruba resort.Discover the vibrant nightlife at Aruba’s first St. Regis Casino or savor the iconic Bloody Mary at The St. Regis Bar. If you’re searching for the ultimate resort in Palm Beach Noord, Aruba experience, our dedicated Hosts ensure your stay is nothing short of exquisite. Experience luxury and personalized service at The St. Regis Aruba Resort.

St. Regis Rituals

### Bloody Mary

Taste our Bon Bini Mary, an ode to the original Bloody Mary recipe created by barman Fernand Petiot in 1934 at St. Regis New York’s King Cole Bar. Named for the Papiamento phrase for “welcome,” this concoction blends locally sourced papaya, fresh lime, and bold habanero chili to capture the essence of Aruba’s natural beauty and the warmth of its people.

### Evening Ritual

Our champagne sabering ceremony, held daily 6:00 PM at our St. Regis Bar Terrace, marks the transition from day into night. We invite you to enjoy this time-honored tradition and moment of gratitude for another day on the island of Aruba.

### Afternoon Tea

Inspired by Caroline Astor’s intimate gatherings, delight in an afternoon tea service presented in exquisite Ginori 1735 porcelain at our St. Regis Bar.

### St. Regis Butler Service

Other welcoming approaches you can always expect include our personal St. Regis Butler service to ensure every detail and desire is attended to during your stay, and our enticing nightly turndown that assures the most sumptuous sleep possible.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Aruba Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Aruba Resort is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The St. Regis Aruba Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Aruba Resort. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 lbs.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Aruba Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Aruba Resort. Self parking is complimentary for guests. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Aruba Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Aruba Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Aruba Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Aruba Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Aruba Resort is Queen Beatrix International Airport (AUA). AUA is located approximately 7.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Aruba Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Aruba Resort does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
