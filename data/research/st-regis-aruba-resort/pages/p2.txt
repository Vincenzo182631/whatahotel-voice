## Accommodations

Unwind indoors while your mind drifts toward the breathtaking views of Palm Beach. Our accommodations invite the landscape in through thoughtful design—natural elements like warm wood and cool stone create a serene, grounded atmosphere. Each of our 252 rooms is a study in balance, where neutral tones are brightened by the colors of sea, sky, and sand just beyond. From ocean-view Deluxe and Superior Rooms to an array of expansive Suites, every space blends the ease of island living with the sophistication of bespoke design.

A Suite Escape by the Sea

### Endless Space, Timeless Elegance

Live exquisite in our 52 expansive suites, where refined design meets the beauty of sea and sky. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame breathtaking views, while natural textures add warmth and depth. Unwind on your private terrace or gather in spacious living and dining areas. Among these elegant accommodations are Two- and Three-Bedroom Suites, ideal for families and extended stays, and two distinguished Presidential Suites. With a serene palette and panoramic ocean views, each suite invites you to embrace the harmony of paradise and the sophistication of impeccable style.

Presidential Suites

### Unwind in Elevated Comfort

A haven of sophistication, the John Jacob Astor Suites and Caroline Suites are among the resort’s most distinguished accommodations—spanning 2,150 sq. ft. and showcasing sweeping views of the Caribbean Sea. Each suite features a master bedroom, stylish living room, office space, and an expansive balcony styled with plush furnishings. As a guest of these iconic suites, enjoy exclusive inclusions from the moment you arrive in Aruba, including private airport transfers, a dedicated butler and daily access to a reserved pool or beach cabana.

St. Regis Butler Service

### Unpacking & Packing Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can relax and settle in for your stay. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the butler service desk to dispatch a Butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

### Beverage Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast.

### Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day.
