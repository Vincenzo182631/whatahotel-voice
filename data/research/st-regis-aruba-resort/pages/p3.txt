An Exceptional Rooftop Restaurant

### Akira Back

Akira Back, the namesake restaurant, is a vibrant tribute to the ever-evolving world of Steak and Sushi. Here, dining is elevated to an art form, where the highest standards of culinary excellence, unparalleled service, and an immersive atmosphere converge to create an unforgettable experience. Within these restaurant walls resides a mosaic of Chef Akira Back's heritage and life experiences - intricately woven with the threads of his culinary training. The menu draws inspiration from Chef Back's globetrotting adventures, and the myriad spices and influences he has gathered during his journey. As both a professional snowboarder and seasoned chef, his life story infuses each dish with a unique touch, revealing the essence of his life's journey. Chef Back's passion lies in crafting a space and dishes that transcend the ordinary, forging an emotional connection between people and the facets of his food, his personality, and his life.

### Sachi Sushi Bar

Discover a taste of welcome at Sachi Sushi Bar, a contemporary sushi bar where the art of sushi is on full display. From delicately crafted nigiri to bold, modern rolls, each dish is a celebration of artistry and flavor, perfectly paired with curated sake and refined cocktails.

### Afternoon Tea

A cherished St. Regis tradition inspired by Mrs. Astor’s intimate gatherings, Afternoon Tea at The St. Regis Aruba Resort seamlessly blends timeless elegance with the vibrant spirit of the Caribbean. Presented in exquisite Ginori 1735 porcelain, this sophisticated ritual celebrates the art of hospitality through meticulously crafted artisanal delights. Available at The St. Regis Bar.

### Evening Ritual

Dating back to the battles of Napoleon Bonaparte—who famously opened champagne with his saber—the art of sabrage is the signature ceremony of the St. Regis Evening Ritual. Offered daily, this storied tradition is a spirited tribute to celebration. “Champagne: in victory, one deserves it; in defeat, one needs it.” Experience the ritual daily at 6:00 PM on The St. Regis Bar Terrace.

### Bloody Mary

Taste our Bon Bini Mary, an ode to the original Bloody Mary recipe created by barman Fernand Petiot in 1934 at St. Regis New York’s King Cole Bar. Named for the Papiamento phrase for “welcome,” this concoction blends locally sourced papaya, fresh lime, and bold habanero chili to capture the essence of Aruba’s natural beauty and the warmth of its people. Available at The St. Regis Bar.
