# Borghese

Inspired by the city’s legendary Borghese family, this four-bedroom apartment connects our Piranesi and Bernini apartments with unrivalled views across Piazza di Spagna and Via del Babuino, creating the perfect home for groups visiting Rome. Four double bedrooms each enjoy super king-sized beds and an Italian marble bathroom complete with a stand-alone bathtub, shower and double wash basins. The apartment includes two separate guest bathrooms for added convenience Both living spaces are adorned with stylish artworks, velvet furnishings and ambient lighting. Two dining rooms, bathed in natural light, have their own kitchens for seamless service. The apartment is accessible for disabled guests and has a studio that can be converted into an additional bedroom.
