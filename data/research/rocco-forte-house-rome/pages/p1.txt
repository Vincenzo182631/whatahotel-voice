For those seeking something special, Rocco Forte House: the intimacy and exclusivity of a private home; the mark of quality of a Rocco Forte hotel.

At Piazza di Spagna, five palatial private apartments within an 18th-century palazzo. Above, a stately roof terrace surveying the Spanish Steps. Inside, elegant, opulent design by Tommaso Ziffer. A dedicated house manager on hand. Unbridled access to the spa, the bars and Fulvio Pierangelini restaurants of Hotel de la Ville. A contemporary proposition, built on Rocco Forte’s rich Italian tradition. This is the height of elegant Roman living.
