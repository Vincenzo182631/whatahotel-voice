# Wine Country Dining Excellence

## All Day Dining in Sonoma County

At Vinarosa, we understand that great Sonoma dining experiences shouldn’t be limited by time. Our versatile dining venues ensure you’re well-served from sunrise to sunset.

From seasonal cuisine at John Ash to coffee and wine at The Parlor, drinks and bites poolside, and in-room dining, Vinarosa Resort & Spa offers delicious ways to savor the flavors of Sonoma throughout your day.

## John Ash Restaurant

Nestled in the heart of our Sonoma Russian River Valley vineyard, John Ash Restaurant stands as a testament to wine country’s culinary excellence. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, this refined dining establishment offers unparalleled farm-to-table dining experiences.

## The Parlor

Start your day at The Parlor, our lobby café where freshly baked pastries and artisanal coffee await. This convenient grab-and-go spot offers artisanal foods, fine wines & charcuterie boards by night.

## Pool Dining

The Pool Bar provides a relaxed outdoor dining experience with stunning vineyard views. During peak season, enjoy full food and beverage service Thursday through Sunday, with modified service available during off-peak times.

## In-Room Dining​

Savor the privacy and comfort of your room with our comprehensive in-room dining menu.  Where modern convenience meets wine country comfort.
