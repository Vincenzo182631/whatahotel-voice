## Vineyard View Rooms & Suites

Vinarosa offers an exquisite escape with unparalleled vineyard views and luxurious accommodations. Our meticulously designed rooms and suites provide a serene retreat, blending rustic charm with modern elegance.

Here in the heart of Sonoma wine country, we invite you to relax and enjoy the beauty and serenity of Vinarosa. Our 78 rooms and suites are set within intimate two-story buildings, always keeping you close to nature. Escape into your own private oasis, designed with comfortable, casual luxury.

At Vinarosa, we believe in providing an unforgettable experience beyond the guestroom.

### The Spa

Rejuvenate your senses at our Spa, where expert therapists offer a range of indulgent treatments designed to relax and revitalize.

### recharge & explore

Maintain your fitness routine at our state-of-the-art fitness center, equipped with the latest equipment or explore the grounds for guided vineyard walks. There are also a host of activities onsite and offsite to explore.

### fine dining

Savor the flavors of Sonoma at our exquisite on-site dining venues. Connect to the land at John Ash, one of Sonoma’s original farm-to-table restaurants. Relax over drinks and seasonal fare.
