## Wedding Venues

Create lasting memories at one of the most romantic Sonoma venues available for weddings. Our property offers multiple ceremony and reception locations, each providing a unique wine country ambiance. The Fontana Lawn features market lighting and vineyard views, creating an enchanting setting for your special day. Our indoor venues combine elegance with functionality, featuring high ceilings, natural light, and sophisticated décor. Each wedding venue includes essential amenities such as white folding chairs, dance floors, and professional setup support. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, our spaces adapt to your vision while maintaining the charm of wine country.
