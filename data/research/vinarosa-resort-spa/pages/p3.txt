Immerse yourself in the comfort of our luxury guest rooms at Vinarosa. Our first-floor rooms open to furnished patios, while second-floor rooms include furnished balconies and vaulted wood-beamed ceilings. Every room is decorated with elegant warmth – most including a fireplace – and detailed with an array of thoughtful amenities. Choose from views of the lush vineyard or our garden courtyards, featuring brilliant flowers and sparkling fountains.

Rooms and suites are sprinkled throughout our property in quaint buildings located around the heart of the Resort hugging the Courtyard and by our Vineyard.

All rooms and suites are appointed with the following luxury amenities and services:

- Bed(s) with plush down comforters
- Environmentally friendly electric fireplace
- Luxury Bath Amenities Byredo
- Large HDTV with Streaming Capabilities
- Bedside alarm clocks and lamps with USB charging ports
- Individually controlled air conditioning-cooling/heating
- Personal refrigerator stocked with water, soft drinks and a half bottle of wine
- Nespresso coffeemaker with an assortment of coffees and teas
- Electronic safe
- Hair Dryer
- Ironing Board or Steamer available on request
- Umbrella
- Nightly Housekeeping Turndown service
- In-room dining menu from 7am-9pm
- High Speed Internet Access
- Overnight snack menu available
