# Contact Us

Set amidst 92 acres of vineyards in Santa Rosa, California, Vinarosa Resort & Spa Resort is your gateway to adventure. Considered the heart of Sonoma County due to its central location, Santa Rosa has established the region’s reputation for quality wine where some of the most renowned Pinot Noir and Chardonnay grapes are grown. Hike and bike amidst the redwood forests of the Russian River Valley, picnic on the Sonoma Coast, or explore Sonoma County’s famous wineries and culinary destinations. We’re just 60 miles north of San Francisco and a 10-minute drive from Charles M. Schulz-Sonoma County Airport (STS). We offer complimentary self-parking, as well as a parking valet and access to luxury transportation services.
