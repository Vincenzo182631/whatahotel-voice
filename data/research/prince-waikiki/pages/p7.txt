# Prince Waikiki Pools

To Infinity…

Stunning sunsets, chilled drinks, and good times are always on deck at Prince Waikiki. Float between three ocean-view oases: our iconic adults-only infinity pool, our saltwater pool, and our hot tub. Find a sweet escape for mind and body at our poolside paradise and watch everyday life vanish into the horizon.

## Poolside Cabanas

A shady reprieve from sun-soaked afternoons, our private poolside cabanas and daybeds are available for rental from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and are ideal for all-day dining, diving into a good book, or just dozing off for a while.
