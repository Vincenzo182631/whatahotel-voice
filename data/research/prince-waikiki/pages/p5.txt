# Hinana Bar

Cold Drinks on Deck

Cocktails by the pool? Check. Afternoon indulgences and creative libations are abundant at Hinana Bar, serving up casual eats and refreshing drinks. Lounge or plunge, it’s an all-day place to play.

Every Friday – Sunday, Hinana Bar is home to SunSets LIVE—hours of upbeat tunes and poolside entertainment from favorite local artists, including DJ KK every Saturday and DJ Jem every Sunday on the pool deck.
