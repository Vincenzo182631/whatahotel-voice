# Explore the Wonders of Prince Waikiki

Dive Into a Wave of New Experiences

Take in incredible art installations, play the golf of your dreams, or float the day away with a cocktail on standby — this is a place to go with the ebbs and flows of the everyday. At one of the best hotels in Waikiki, you can choose to do a little or a lot, with island-inspired indulgences and elevated experiences around every sunny corner.

### Dine at Prince Waikiki

Inspired classic and contemporary dishes with fresh island cuisine is our passion.

View Dining

### Take the Plunge

Every day’s a pool day at Prince Waikiki. Lounge without limits at our saltwater oasis, float in our oceanfront infinity pool, or seek some shade in a private cabana.

Discover Our Pools

### Curated Culture

Exploring the imaginative art scene of Honolulu begins at Prince Waikiki. Discover the rich legacy of Hawaii’s contemporary culture through artwork inspired by elements native to Oahu.

Discover Our Art

### Rituals of Relaxation

Indulge in a moment all for yourself at Naio Bliss. Experience harmony of spirit with treatments and traditions inspired by elements of the land and ocean.

Discover Spa Services

### Fitness

Sneak in a sweat or stretch session between beach visits at our fitness center. Our on-site gym is equipped with treadmills, elliptical trainers, exercise bikes, strength machines, and free weights.

Open 24/7
