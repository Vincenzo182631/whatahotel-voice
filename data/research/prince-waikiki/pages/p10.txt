# A Pristine Honolulu Golf Course

Hawaii Prince Golf Course

In Oahu, even the greens seem just a little greener. Designed by Arnold Palmer and Ed Seay, this dramatic Honolulu golf course boasts three interchangeable nine-hole courses with views of the rain-carved Waianae Mountains. Its 270 acres of pristine play and natural beauty lie within the sunny Ewa Plain—about a 40-minute drive from Prince Waikiki.
