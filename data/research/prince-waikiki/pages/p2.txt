## Prince Waikiki FAQ

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in time is 3:00 PM. Check-out time is 11:00 AM.

What is your cancellation policy?

A one-night deposit is required upon booking. The deposit is refundable if cancellation is received by the hotel at least 72 hours before arrival day. Any no-shows or cancellations received within 72 hours will be assessed a one-night room and tax charge.

_Special Holiday Deposit/Cancellation Policy_

Applies for stays between December 24 – 31, 2024. One (1) night deposit required at time of reservation. Cancellation must be made at least 7 days prior to arrival to avoid one (1) night room and tax charge.

Is the Prince Waikiki pet-friendly?

Prince Waikiki welcomes service dogs.

What is Prince Waikiki’s smoking policy?

Smoking is prohibited on the Prince Waikiki property.

Do you offer accessible rooms?

Accessible rooms are available. Please notate a request for an accessible room during check-in or contact our Reservations Department. Reservations can be contacted at (844) 207-9998 or reservations@princewaikiki.com.

What amenities are available in your rooms and suites?

Each of our guest rooms and suites includes:

- One king or two queen beds
-  Separate showers and tubs
- TOTO washlets
- Sheer and Blackout Remote Control Blinds
- Large In-Room Safe
- LCD Smart TV
- Coffee Maker & Tea Kettle
- USB Charging Ports
- Floor-to-Ceiling Windows
- Mini Refrigerator
- Work Nook

Is there a resort charge?

Unless otherwise noted, all bookings will incur a daily resort charge. Learn more

Does the Prince Waikiki offer dry cleaning services?

Prince Waikiki offers dry cleaning services through Hakuyosha. Please have items placed out in your dry cleaning bag by 8:30 AM for same-day service. Service is not available on Sundays and holidays.

Are housekeeping services available?

For your peace of mind, we offer above-and-beyond daily housekeeping services.
