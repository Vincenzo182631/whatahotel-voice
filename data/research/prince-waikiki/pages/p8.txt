# Naio Bliss

Rituals of Relaxation – Massages and Body Treatments

Indulge in a moment all for yourself at Naio Bliss, our laid-back Waikiki spa. Experience harmony of spirit with treatments and traditions inspired by elements of the land and ocean. Coconut milk body wraps, hydrating and anti-aging mask applications, hot stone massages, and transportive packages for an afternoon on cloud nine—Naio Bliss blends restorative techniques with rejuvenating island botanicals.
