# Weddings & Events in Oahu

Hosting: It’s in Our Nature

The island of Oahu is known as The Gathering Place. Between the lush beauty of its terrain and the famed sparkle of Honolulu, it’s no wonder why. Plan your wedding, meeting, or special celebration in Oahu at Prince Waikiki alongside our dedicated team, with ocean-view accommodations, award-winning dining, and indulgent on-site experiences that make getting together even better.

## Weddings by The Ocean

A cosmopolitan affair with a coastal flair or a low-key elopement for two, say “I do” surrounded by boundless hues of blue. Exchange vows in one of Waikiki’s most dazzling wedding destinations, with scenery so stunning the décor takes care of itself.
