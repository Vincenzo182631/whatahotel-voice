# Welcome to Sanctuary Cap Cana, a Luxury Collection Resort, Dominican Republic, Adult All-Inclusive

## Rediscover paradise at Cap Cana Adult All-Inclusive Resort

Adults-only Sanctuary Cap Cana is set along a stretch of pure white sand beach in the Dominican Republic, just 15 minutes from Punta Cana International Airport. This luxurious 324-suite resort breathes...See More romance, resembling a colonial Spanish castle town perched on a cliff by the ocean. Combining beautiful architectural design with impeccable service and lavish accommodations, this all-inclusive Cap Cana resort close to airport is a vacation paradise like no other. Sanctuary Cap Cana resort is located in Cap Cana, a private gated community retreat inside Punta Cana. Cap Cana, a gated sanctuary encompassing 30,000 acres, boasts breathtaking oceanfront vistas and historic valley roads. Our Cap Cana resort in Punta Cana is the ideal destination for any type of traveler,with a variety of things to do, such as water sports, outdoor activities, and high-quality restaurants. This destination is also home to Punta Espada Golf Club, which is considered one of the best golf courses in the Caribbean.See Less
