## A 360° Approach to Wellbeing

Recline in a sanctuary designed for deep rejuvenation. Featuring signature treatments administered by a handpicked team of therapists, C Club promises an experience that leaves guests glowing from the inside out.

SPA MENU

LE C CLUB SPA WELCOMES LA PRAIRIE

Le C Club Spa partners with the prestigious Swiss skincare brand La Prairie to offer an exclusive sanctuary devoted to beauty and the art of longevity. Inspired by the tranquility of Switzerland’s lakes, the majesty of the Alpine peaks and the vitality of untouched forests, La Prairie’s visionary treatments are tailored to each skin’s unique needs while nurturing deep sensory and emotional well-being.

**EXCLUSIVE SIGNATURE TREATMENT**

Created exclusively for the Spa at the Carlton Cannes, this bespoke facial combines expertise with refined luxury. Following a personalized skin diagnosis, the therapist carefully selects the most suitable products and techniques to smooth, brighten and revitalize the complexion, while delivering a uniquely sensorial wellness experience.

Fitness Centre

Enjoy an elevated training experience in our premium fitness centre. Exceptional facilities, including a Pilates zone, boxing and cryotherapy, are coupled with bespoke health plans to take physical vitality to the next level.
