##### CARLTON CANNES, A REGENT HOTEL

##### The icon of the Riviera, to be lived

Advantageously positioned on the French Riviera's La Croisette Boulevard, the Carlton Cannes, a Regent hotel, is a ravishing emblem of the Côte d'Azur. Exuding a Belle Epoque soul with ample modern twists, this lively icon fuses the lavish energy of La Croisette with a serene ambience for an unimaginable stay.

* * *

###### 58 boulevard de la Croisette, 06400 Cannes, France

+33(0)4 93 06 40 06

carlton@ihg.com

For guests arriving and departing by air, Nice Côte d'Azur Airport is 30 minutes by car from the hotel. You can also enjoy a 50-minute train journey between the airport and Cannes Station, followed by a 5-minute taxi ride to the hotel. A transfer can be arranged at your request.
