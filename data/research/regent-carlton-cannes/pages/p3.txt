# Restaurants & Bars

## Riviera Restaurant

Savour the full flair and flavour of the most iconic meeting point on La Croisette. Steeped in cinematic heritage and dedicated to the finest local terroir, join our chefs for a celebratory dining experience characterised by intimacy, freshness and perfectly put together brasserie fare.

* * *

- Tel.
+33 (0) 4 93 06 46 00

### Rüya Restaurant

Evocative and otherworldly, encounter a range of Anatolian classics in the vibrant atmosphere of Rüya. Taking inspiration from Turkish traditions, an ever-changing seasonal menu celebrates sophisticated and contemporary dishes from regions stretching from the Mediterranean all the way to the Black Sea.

* * *

- Tel.
+33 (0)4 93 06 42 00

### Carlton Beach Club

Exuding echoes of the 50s’ French Riviera, our beach club offers Franco-Italian dishes that capture the spirit of the Blue Coast. Enjoy a lively atmosphere and views of the Esterel mountains, or languish on plush lounge sofas while basking in Mediterranean rays.

* * *

- Tel.
+33 (0) 4 93 06 44 94

### Bar°58

Pull up a seat at Bar°58 and ready yourself for enchantment. Honouring the Grande Dame’s history, sleek interiors complement a playful ambience while a menu of classic wines, Champagnes and creative cocktails provide the perfect excuse for indulgence.

* * *

- Tel.
+33 (0) 4 93 06 43 00

### Camelia Tea Lounge

Take tastebuds on an unexpected voyage of discovery in our illustrious tea lounge. Featuring over 30 custom blends, a dedicated tea master will be on hand to customise a sensorial experience based on mood and preference. A decadent patisserie menu satisfies the most elevated sweet tooth.

* * *

- Tel.
+33 (0) 4 93 06 44 00

### Viñales Cigar Lounge

A fitting tribute to the renowned UNESCO World Heritage Site, Viñales is an intimate den for cigar aficionados. Step inside to relish the old-world charm of this haven, and enjoy a collection of cigars, Whiskeys and Cognacs served up alongside a menu of exquisite bites.

* * *

- Tel.
+33 (0)4 93 06 43 00
