Highlights include our outdoor heated pool, which is open year-round, fire pits, poolside cabanas, our lushly landscaped Secret Garden, and plenty of Insta-ready backdrops. We're also home to AWAY Spa, offering bespoke treatments and wellness...See More sessions in an award-winning setting. Lastly, we regularly put together dynamic programming, including curated pop-ups with Philadelphia-based creatives and live music performances. ## FIT

Open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, FIT is a gym and fitness center designed for the modern traveler. With state-of-the-art equipment to push your strength and endurance exactly where you need them to go – and beyond – FIT also offers personal training sessions and neighborhood-guided runs. Level up your fitness routine with CONTRAST, our sauna and cold-plunge experience available as an add-on to your stay, designed to support recovery, boost performance, and leave you feeling recharged long after your workout ends.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does W Philadelphia have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. W Philadelphia offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as CONTRAST Sauna and Cold Plunge available for $25 daily fee., Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does W Philadelphia have a pool on-site?

Yes, W Philadelphia has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does W Philadelphia have an on-site spa?

Yes, AWAY Spa is located on-site at W Philadelphia. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near W Philadelphia?

University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia Sports Complex, Kimmel Center for Performing Arts, JFK Plaza (Love Park), Philadelphia Museum of Art and more are nearby W Philadelphia.
