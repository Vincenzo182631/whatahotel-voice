Why book?
W Philadelphia is a stylish urban retreat with an independent Philly spirit and plenty of appealing draws—a rooftop pool, plush spa, and creative cocktail program included—all within walking distance of some of the city’s best attractions.
Set the scene
In the heart of Center City, W Philadelphia adds a dash of style to the city’s historic landscape. The clientele is a mix of travelers (young professionals, creative industry types, couples on weekend getaways) and 20- and 30-something locals here for a pop-up rooftop yoga class, or to meet friends at one of the hotel’s two cocktail bars: the Living Room lobby and the Wet Bar, overlooking a heated outdoor pool on the seventh-floor rooftop.
The backstory
W Philadelphia, part of Marriott International, opened in August 2021 after several years of development, occupying a 51-story building that also houses its more buttoned-up sister property, Element by Westin. What sets this W apart is how deliberately it incorporates Philadelphia’s history and culture into its ultramodern aesthetic—from the lobby’s architectural nods to the city’s historic grid system (the floor tiles are an actual gridded map art installation reproducing a cheeky 19th-century guidebook) to subtle references to the city's rebellious spirit (the Philly toile-patterned bed linens show off a muscle-y Ben Franklin designed by a local art school graduate).
The rooms
The hotel’s 295 rooms and suites strike a balance between playful extravagance and more refined comfort. Some rooms have mural-like headboards with abstracted views of Philly neighborhoods or custom light fixtures inspired by Benjamin Franklin's lightning rod experiments. Bathrooms come with rainfall showers (suites have staggeringly big tubs) and Davines amenities. The “marvelous” suites on the higher floors are worth the splurge for their floor-to-ceiling windows offering sweeping views of the city.
Food and drink
The hotel’s restaurant, Dolce, serves breakfast, lunch, and dinner, and is best for housemade pastas and Neapolitan-style pizzas. When it comes to F&B, though, W’s standout is its bar programs, led by Isai Xolalpa. The lobby’s Living Room bar and the rooftop bar Wet Deck have separate menus, and each inventive cocktail has its own nonalcoholic counterpart. The menus change seasonally, but look out for anything mixed with Xolalpa’s zero-proof rum, which he makes by steeping cardamom, cloves, and other aromatic spices. If you’re ordering room service (which you should do, especially if you happen to be in one of the suites equipped with a dining table and floor-to-ceiling windows showing off the city skyline), get the cheesesteak empanadas.
The spa
The Away Spa on the fifth floor is a supremely peaceful retreat from the hotel’s more energetic spaces. Checking in for a treatment starts with a signature ginger shot, and the spa menu features detox facials and the soothing Balance CBD massage. Both hotel and spa day guests have access to the year-round heated pool, which is lovely for a lofty swim surrounded by skyscrapers.
The neighborhood/area
Just south of City Hall in Center City, the W Philadelphia offers prime access to the city's business district. Don't miss Reading Terminal Market, a short walk away, for quintessentially Philly food. The Avenue of the Arts with its theaters and music venues stretches just south, making the hotel an ideal location for culture buffs, too.
The service
Service at the W is both efficient and full of personality, professional but never stuffy. The Whatever/Whenever concierge philosophy means the staff will help with everything from scoring hard-to-get restaurant reservations to arranging a private tour at the Philadelphia Museum of Art.
For families
While W Philadelphia’s glossy aesthetic and social atmosphere primarily attract couples and business travelers, the hotel makes surprisingly thoughtful accommodations for families. The concierge can arrange family-friendly city tours and secure tickets to attractions like the Please Touch Museum or Philadelphia Zoo. Suites are outfitted with massive bathtubs and one stand-out offering: They’ll wheel up a cart of elite snacks sourced from the nearby Reading Terminal Market (Miller’s twist Pennsylvania Dutch soft pretzels, chocolate-covered pretzels from Mueller’s chocolate) for an unforgettable family movie night.
Accessibility
ADA-accessible rooms feature roll-in showers, visual alarms, and accessible vanities. All public spaces, including the rooftop pool and wet deck (equipped with a pool lift), are accessible via elevators.
Anything left to mention?
W Philadelphia successfully delivers the brand's signature blend of style and energy while incorporating lots of local character—no small feat for a chain hotel in a city that prides itself on independence. The hotel has local touches throughout, including artwork commissioned from Philly art school students and alumni.
