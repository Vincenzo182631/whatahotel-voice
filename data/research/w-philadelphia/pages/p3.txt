### Cocktails and Inspired Bites

From locally-roasted craft coffee to seasonal cocktails to small and shareable plates, the Living Room restaurant and bar in Center City, Philadelphia boasts innovative drinks and bites into the evening.

### Tuned In

Music is the focus in the Living Room, where local and internationally renowned artists take over the decks as the sun goes down, delivering vibe-setting house, soul, disco, and global sounds.

### Stevens' Prophecy

Behind a two-way mirror at the Living Room, a select few will find Stevens’ Prophecy, an intimate salon-style space. Inspired by the city’s own Grace Kelly, the hidden room is a luxurious homage to old Hollywood—Philadelphia style.

### Wet Bar

WET Bar is an oasis above the streets. Here we offer fresh juices, light seasonal dishes, and refreshing, low-ABV cocktails. Stop in for a drink, it will feel like a trip. Or, book a room for the complete experience. Hotel guests are always welcome to enjoy a refreshing plunge in our WET Deck pool, and cabanas with pool access are available to reserve for hotel guests and locals alike. ### Poolside Dining

WET Bar offers seasonal-curated light bites, fresh juices, and specialty low-ABV cocktails. Perfect for: Post-swim snacks, mid-sun bath refreshments, or an evening of drinks and conversation by the fire. Don’t miss it: if just stopping by for a drink or a snack, WET Bar is open to the public, seasonally – it’s an ideal, unique escape from Philadelphia’s Center City.

### Cabana Reservations

Our luxury cabanas offer the ultimate poolside experience. Reserve the cabana of your preference and relax by the pool, cocktail in hand. Limited day passes may also be available. Cabana Reservations and WET Deck programming available to book seasonally.

### Urban Oasis

A lush, poolside oasis above the street with iconic views of Philadelphia’s City Hall and historic architecture, the seventh-floor rooftop terrace includes our WET Deck, cabanas, fire pit, and event space. It's designed for everything from solo moments of R&R to private events and gatherings. Our favorite tip? In the mornings, head up with a book and coffee or enjoy a year-round dip in the pool to start the day.

### Dolce

From morning to night, our Italian restaurant in downtown Philadelphia serves inventive regional Italian fare. With an atmosphere drawing on the spirit and aesthetics of Federico Fellini’s Rome in the 1960s, and a vibe that is pure Philadelphia.
