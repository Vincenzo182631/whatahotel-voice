# Take it easy in our hotel suites in Philadelphia

## Accommodations

Ease into our luxury suites and hotel rooms in Philadelphia that bring to life the artful, rebellious spirit of our city. Look for bespoke features, nods to local history and craftsmanship, and all the amenities you’ve come to expect from W. Also, worth noting: Floor-to-ceiling views in your hotel room in downtown Philadelphia bring the city to your bed.

The Suite Life

### Suites

Philly soul meets French rebellion chic in the playful, artful suites of W Philadelphia. Choose from eight indulgent options, from the unforgettable Superior Suite to the opulent Penthouse. Many of our luxury hotel suites in downtown Philadelphia feature soaking tubs or private balconies to soak in the view.

Luxury Suites

### Penthouse

Our Penthouse Suite has two bedrooms and 2,300 square-feet of vibe-defining luxury. Take in Philadelphia’s river-to-river views from your expansive private balcony. Then take over the decks from the in-room DJ booth. With custom foosball, a soaking tub, a pantry kitchen, a premium MIX Bar, and two luxurious full bathrooms, there’s simply nothing like it.

### W Suite

Set against the skyline, the W Suite is Impressive, energizing, and opulent. This hotel suite in downtown Philadelphia includes high-design copper finishes, a separate living room and dining area with an in-room DJ booth, a soaking tub, and a fully furnished balcony. You can't help but settle in and be wowed.

### Supreme Suite

There’s something decidedly unique and luxe about the Supreme, our 1650-square-foot King bed outfitted suites. A full bedroom, a living room, and a separate dining area leave ample space for socializing. A few of our Supreme Suites include a spacious terrace that gets you closer to the skyline. This suite, with luxury amenities and design details, has it all.
