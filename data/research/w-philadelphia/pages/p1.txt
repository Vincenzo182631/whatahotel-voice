# Welcome to W Philadelphia

## Unwind and relax at our luxury hotel in Philadelphia

Discover a bold new side to the "City of Brotherly Love" at W Philadelphia. We've packed our hotel in Center City, Philly, full of unforgettable amenities and eye-catching designs. Explore from our Center City location near Rittenhouse Square and City Hall. Relax in a pet-friendly hotel room with our signature W beds. Many of our luxury suites offer private hotel balconies with views of downtown Philadelphia’s...See More iconic skyline. Indulge yourself with the beauty and wellness treatments at AWAY® Spa, then converse over craft cocktails in our hotel's Living Room bar. Relax in our outdoor pool or sip drinks on the WET® Deck, open 365 days a year. Impress guests with three floors of dynamic meeting spaces that pay homage to the iconic art and music of Philadelphia, PA. Discover everything Center City has to offer, from historic Old City to critically acclaimed restaurants to the world-renowned Philadelphia Museum of Art from our downtown Philadelphia luxury hotel. Philadelphia is a city waiting to be discovered.See Less

AWAY Spa by W Hotels - our in-house wellness sanctuary - offers an immersive array of body treatments and beauty services, created with restoration and relaxation at heart. ## WET Deck

Our WET Deck is one of Philadelphia’s most in-the-know social hubs. The pool - heated year-round, and open 365 days - beckons for a dip, while luxe cabanas can be reserved (seasonally) to host gatherings (or, simply, lounge). Indulge in fresh juices and sun-kissed low-ABV cocktails over light, locally-sourced bites. Gather around the fire pit on a chilly night. Calm or social, turn up or wind down: WET is whatever you want it to be.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Philadelphia?

The check-in time at W Philadelphia is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does W Philadelphia allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Philadelphia. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at W Philadelphia?

On-site valet parking is available at W Philadelphia. The daily valet fee is $75.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Philadelphia have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Philadelphia has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Philadelphia booking.

What is the closest airport near W Philadelphia?

The closest airport near W Philadelphia is Philadelphia International Airport (PHL). PHL is located approximately 12.5 Miles from the hotel.

Does W Philadelphia have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Philadelphia has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
