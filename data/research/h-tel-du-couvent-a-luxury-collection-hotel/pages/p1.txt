# Welcome to Hôtel du Couvent, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Nice, France

## 17th-Century Luxury Hotel Inspired by Local Heritage

In the old Nice, nestled in the North-Italian part of Provence, lies a convent from the 17th century with a luxurious garden. Our spa hotel in Nice, France is a picture of light and simplicity. At the...See More Hôtel du Couvent, time is passed by reading in the shade of a fig tree, indulging in a few strokes in the lap pool or chatting with the next table at the guinguette cafe. You can take time to meditate at the Movement Studio, seek advice at the herbalist in the cloister or enjoy a thermal treatment at the Roman baths, before stepping out into the hustle and bustle of the centre of Nice.See Less

Access to Hôtel du Couvent

We are located in the pedestrian Old Town of Nice. Arriving by car, go to 2 rue Centrale, in front of the Jean Jaurès bookshop, to access the Old Town entrance. You can also contact our Valet Service on +33 6 85 83 10 33, 15 min prior to your arrival to escort you to the hotel.

### A Former Convent Turned Hotel

In the heart of the old town, secluded and overlooking the Mediterranean, the former Visitation convent welcomes visitors into an architecture steeped in the memory of the place. Once the porch is crossed, they find themselves at the heart of this centuries-old site, among ancient stones, wisteria shade, and the scent of the courtyard’s orange trees. Guests are received under the linden tree and greeted...See More by the aromas and flavors of a glass of natural sparkling wine, our welcome cocktail made with herbs from our garden, or an herbal tea from our herbalist, accompanied by a freshly baked madeleine, warm and comforting. A simple and warm sensory experience, imbued with joyful hospitality.See Less

Hôtel du Couvent offers three distinct dining experiences, each rooted in the spirit of the place and the flavors of Nice. You can take time to meditate at the Movement Studio, seek advice at the herbalist in the cloister or enjoy a thermal treatment at the Roman baths, before stepping out into the hustle and bustle of the centre of Nice, where...See More you could take a dip in the Mediterranean, dine in town, or go to the opera.See Less

What To Do in Hotel Du Couvent in Nice

### Pool With a View

Hidden within the serene convent gardens, our 20 meter pool invites you to drift between sky and sea. Framed by fragrant greenery and ancient stone, it offers an unspoiled panorama over Nice, the city’s terracotta rooftops on one side, the shimmering Mediterranean on the other. A tranquil sanctuary where time slows, and every swim feels like a quiet escape above it all.

### Movement Studio

Our daily classes combine diversity and effectivenes: functional training to strengthen the body, breathwork to calm the mind, yoga, pilates, floor barre, and animal flow. The studio is open 24/7, allowing you to train freely, even outside of class hours. You’ll find all the necessary equipment (mats, weights, resistance bands, etc.) for an independent and personalized practice tailored to your needs.

### Jardins Classés

Nestled in a lush, historical setting, these terraced gardens and peaceful cloisters offer a sanctuary of biodiversity rooted in monastic tradition. Guests can enjoy a tranquil escape between aromatic orchards and swimming pools, all while overlooking the rooftops of the city and the Mediterranean Sea.

Spa and Wellbeing in Nice

## The Roman Baths

In keeping with the spirit of the place, the Roman Baths offer spaces designed for silence and care. Treatments draw on botanical actives, echoing the garden and the convent pharmacopoeia that once brought...See More life to these walls. Here, body and mind find, simply, rest.See Less

Discover Our Wellness Rituals

### Children at the Convent

During school holidays, Hôtel du Couvent welcomes children from 4 to 10. Supervised by qualified animators, they enjoy a varied program: cooking and baking with our chefs and baker, creative workshops to paint like Matisse or try theater, gardening in the historic gardens, and yoga and meditation. Lunches and snacks are freshly prepared. Open to all children aged 4 to 10.

Learn More

### Herbal Shop

In homage to the sisters who once tended their herb garden, the hotel’s resident herbalist offers guests personalized guidance, handcrafted remedies, and artisanal teas infused with herbs from the garden. Tailored consultations are also available upon request a living tribute to tradition and natural wellness.

### La Boulangerie du Couvent

In true convent tradition, flour is freshly milled on-site from locally sourced ancient grains. The dough and yeast are crafted in-house, and each loaf made from a blend of premium grains carries the legacy of artisanal expertise. Freshly baked bread is available daily at La Boulangerie du Couvent, where workshops can also be arranged upon request to share the secrets of this time-honored craft.

Learn More

Nice and the French Riviera

### Nice and the French Riviera

Located on the Promenade des Anglais, Plage du Galet welcomes guests for a relaxing day by the Mediterranean. Sunbeds are reserved for Hôtel du Couvent guests.Access daily, by reservation.

### The Old Town

Just steps from the hotel, Nice’s Old Town comes alive through its charming alleys perfect for a stroll before diving into the sea, enjoying a terrace dinner, or taking in an opera night.

### The City, Museums, and Surroundings

Neither entirely French nor truly Italian, Nice carries a singular, rich history. This heritage lives on in its museums Matisse, Picasso, Chagall… and extends to the diversity of its surrounding landscapes. From hiking trails in the Mercantour to coastal paths, time here flows to the rhythm of discovery.

## Give the Gift of a Moment at the Couvent

To introduce your loved ones to this unique place, Hôtel du Couvent offers gift vouchers. A dinner at the Restaurant du Couvent, a treatment at the Thermes, or a night in one of our suites. A simple and thoughtful way to share what makes this place truly singular.
