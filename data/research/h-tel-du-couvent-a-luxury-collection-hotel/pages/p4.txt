## Spa

As a tribute to the remains of the Roman baths on the hill in Cimiez, Nice, Hôtel du Couvent presents a Roman-inspired thermal circuit. True to ancient tradition, guests can move through three successive...See More baths : the tepidarium (temperate), the caldarium (hot), and the frigidarium (cold), before unwinding in the unctuarium, which offers a range of natural treatments and manual techniques as part of a personalized program in partnership with selected brands and specialists. In addition to the open-air swimming pool in the baths, the hotel offers a 20-metre lap pool in the garden, a smaller plunge pool adjacent to La Guinguette, and the Movement Studio, a space dedicated to dance, functional workouts, pilates, yoga, and other ground-based exercises.See Less

### The Roman Way

Inspired by ancient Roman tradition, the bath circuit at the Hôtel du Couvent is almost circular, with a succession of pools, each increasing in temperature. To enjoy the greatest benefits, bathers are invited to start with the warm water of the tepidarium, followed by the hot caldarium, and finish with a plunge in the cool frigidarium.

## Movement Studio

Discover a space entirely dedicated to movement, designed to enhance your strength, flexibility, and endurance. Our daily classes combine diversity and effectiveness, offering a variety of disciplines: functional training to strengthen the body, breathwork to calm the mind, yoga, pilates, floor barre, and animal flow. The studio is open 24/7, allowing you to train freely, even outside of class hours. You’ll find all the necessary equipment (mats, weights, resistance bands, etc.) for an independent and personalized practice tailored to your needs.
