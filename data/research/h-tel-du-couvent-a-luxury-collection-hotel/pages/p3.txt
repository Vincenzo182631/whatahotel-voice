### Convent Cuisine

Hôtel du Couvent offers a sincere and vibrant cuisine, shaped by the history of the place and the terroir of Nice. Inspired by tradition, it highlights local, seasonal ingredients and precise craftsmanship. Humble in form yet generous in flavor, it blends convent heritage, culinary memory, and modernity. It is a cuisine of emotion, authentic, joyful, and deeply rooted in its territory.

## Our Farm

In Touët-sur-Var, in the hills of Nice’s hinterland, Ferme Notre-Dame supplies the hotel’s kitchens exclusively with its 3,000 m² of crops and 250 hens. Each day, Avit, the farmer, delivers fresh, seasonal eggs, fruits, and vegetables. Every product is carefully selected, reflecting the hotel’s commitment to remaining rooted in its surroundings and at the heart of the local community.

## La Boulangerie du Couvent

Inspired by convent tradition, the hotel’s bread is crafted on-site: the flour is milled, the dough kneaded, and the bread baked in the bakery oven located in the very room where the sisters once prepared their daily loaves. Each morning, warm, golden bread is served at breakfast and in the hotel’s three restaurants. Made with natural sourdough and heritage grains, it is more nourishing and develops a subtle, authentic flavor. This bread, shared with the community, is sold each day straight from the oven to local regulars and passersby.
