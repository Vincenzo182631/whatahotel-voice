## Accommodations

With 88 rooms, Hôtel du Couvent offers a diverse range of accommodations, from minimalist cells to expansive suites featuring private terraces or gardens and butler service. Each room provides serene views...See More of the garden, the rooftops of the old town, or the distant horizon. Hôtel du Couvent blends simplicity and comfort with the freedom to live at your own pace.See Less

The unique character of rooms

Spread across the convent's three buildings, each room is unique; interiors and views vary from room to room. Photographs are for illustrative purposes only.

## Butler in Suite

Each suite has a dedicated Butler, attentive to your needs throughout your stay. Whether it’s reserving a table at a restaurant, arranging an in-suite cooking show, or ordering a bouquet of fresh flowers, we ensure your experience is remarkable.

## Unique Suite

Located in the historic Convent building, the suites offer serene views of the cloister, the Cour des Orangers, the old town, or the Mediterranean. Each suite is unique, designed as a private apartment that reflects its own distinct character. Some feature large kitchens equipped with Lacanche ranges, while others highlight a spacious living room and dining area. They are decorated with bespoke furniture, antiques, sculptures, paintings, and rare books. Certain suites can be interconnected, providing ideal flexibility for families.

## Private Gardens and Terraces

To relish Nice’s gentle climate, some suites offer a private outdoor retreat: a garden with a dining table to linger over long summer evenings, or expansive terraces and loggias overlooking the Cour des Orangers, ideal for reading in the dappled shade of wisteria.

## In-Room Dining

For extended stays or intimate evenings in, guests can relish a basket of fresh produce from the hotel’s farm to prepare their own meals, entertain friends with an in-suite dinner, have the chef craft a private meal, or even take part in the cooking themselves.
