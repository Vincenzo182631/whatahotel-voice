## The Roman Baths

Faithful to ancient Roman tradition, the Hôtel du Couvent features a circular thermal bath circuit, where guests progress through a succession of pools at varying temperatures: the tepidarium, caldarium, and frigidarium. An open-air indoor pool and a relaxation basin complete the experience. To enhance this journey, personalized treatments rooted in the benefits of botanical ingredients and expert manual techniques offer a fully tailored experience. Access is for guests aged 16 and over.

Discover Our Treatments

## Movement Studio

Discover a space entirely dedicated to movement, designed to enhance your strength, flexibility, and endurance. Our daily classes combine diversity and effectiveness, offering a variety of disciplines: functional training to strengthen the body, breathwork to calm the mind, yoga, pilates, floor barre, and animal flow. The studio is open 24/7, allowing you to train freely, even outside of class hours. You’ll find all the necessary equipment (mats, weights, resistance bands, etc.) for an independent and personalized practice tailored to your needs.

Discover the Schedule

## Children at the Convent

During school holidays, the team at Hôtel du Couvent welcomes children from the age of 4.

Supervised by a team of qualified animators, children enjoy a rich and varied program designed to awaken their senses and curiosity while having fun. Activities include cooking and baking workshops with the convent’s chefs and our baker, creative workshops to learn to paint like Matisse or try out theater, gardening workshops in the convent’s historic gardens, as well as yoga and meditation sessions.

Lunches and snacks are carefully prepared by our chefs to delight both children and adults alike.

Open to all children aged 4 to 10.

## Herbal Shop

In homage to the sisters who once tended their herb garden, the hotel’s resident herbalist offers guests personalized guidance, handcrafted remedies, and artisanal teas infused with herbs from the garden. Tailored consultations are also available upon request a living tribute to tradition and natural wellness.

### The Farm

The hotel’s farm, located in Touët-sur-Var in the hinterland of Nice, supplies the hotel’s kitchens daily with seasonal, fresh, fruits, vegetables and eggas. Covering 3,000 m² of cultivated land and home to 250 hens, the Notre-Dame farm can be visited with Avit, the farmer, as your guide.

### Producers' Market

Nestled in the heart of La Cour des Orangers, one Saturday morning each month, Hôtel du Couvent comes alive with a festive atmosphere celebrating the changing seasons. Colorful stalls brim with delicious local products, inviting visitors to fill their baskets with a bounty of seasonal delights.

### Cultural Events

Throughout the year, the hotel comes alive with cultural events that bring together all generations around music, dance, festive tables hosted by guest chefs, and much more…

## La Boulangerie du Couvent

In true convent tradition, flour is freshly milled on-site from locally sourced ancient grains. The dough and yeast are crafted in-house, and each loaf made from a blend of premium grains carries the legacy of artisanal expertise. Freshly baked bread is available daily at La Boulangerie du Couvent, where workshops can also be arranged upon request to share the secrets of this time-honored craft.

## Le Centre de Documentation

Located on the ground floor of the convent, the documentation center offers a valuable selection of works on Fine Arts, sculpture, religious art, as well as the culinary traditions of Nice. It features numbered editions, screen-printed magazines, and catalogues raisonnés that showcase the finest artistic and historical achievements. A rare collection dedicated to the "École de Nice", the avant-garde movement from Nice, is also available. All volumes can be consulted on-site with free access.
