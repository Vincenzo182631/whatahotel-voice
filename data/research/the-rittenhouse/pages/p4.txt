# Philadelphia Hotel Suites

Overlooking iconic Rittenhouse Square, our expansive Philadelphia hotel suites are the very essence of contemporary luxury. Our generously proportioned hotel suites measure between 650 and 2,000 square feet. Each suite – with its long list of accessories and amenities - has everything the modern traveler could wish for.

Before a busy day, or after walking the city from dawn till Afternoon Tea time, you can freshen up in your marble bathroom, stay in touch with colleagues and clients from your desk, or just stretch out and relax in the well-appointed comfort of the bedroom.

Reserve

### Our Luxury Suites in Philadelphia’s Center City Feature:

- Inviting, separate seating area with queen-sized sofa sleeper
- Twice daily housekeeping including evening turndown service
- Plush bed with pillow-top mattress, duvets and king-size down pillows (foam pillows are also available)
- State-of-the-art technology featuring two large flat-screen televisions and iPod docking station. Easily connect your laptop as well as other devices.
- Entertainment on demand
- Complimentary wireless and wired high-speed Internet access
- Individual climate control
- In-room safe
- Private bar stocked with a selection of cold beverages and snacks
- Luxurious marble bathroom featuring Acqua Di Parma bath amenities
- Glass enclosed shower with massage showerhead (select suites have soaking bathtubs)
- Separate vanity and dressing area with illuminated makeup mirror
- Plush bathrobe and slippers
- 24-hour in-room dining
- Overnight shoe shining service

**Specialty Suites at The Rittenhouse vary in size and many include:**

- One or two bedrooms
- Butler's Pantry
