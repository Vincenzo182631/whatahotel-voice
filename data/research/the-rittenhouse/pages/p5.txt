# Specialty 1- and 2-Bedroom Suites

For a more spacious stay in Center City, indulge in a Specialty Suite at The Rittenhouse Hotel. Featuring 1 or 2 bedrooms, inviting entertainment and dining areas, and a kitchenette, Specialty Suites have all the essentials for luxurious stays — and more.

Enhanced amenities and services, up to 2,000 square feet, and spectacular views of Rittenhouse Square or Center City make ours the most coveted luxury suites in Philadelphia.

### Our Specialty 1- and 2-Bedroom Suites Feature:

- Butler's pantry
- Individual climate control
- Marble bathrooms with massage showerheads, and luxurious Acqua Di Parma amenities (select suites have soaking bathtubs)
- High-speed wired & wireless Internet
- In-room dining
- Separate vanity and dressing area
- Entertainment & dining areas
- Plush bathrobe and slippers
- 2 Flat-screen HD TVs, iPod/iPhone docking station, and on-demand entertainment
- Overnight shoe shining service
- Twice-daily housekeeping and evening turndown service
- Pet-friendly suites available
