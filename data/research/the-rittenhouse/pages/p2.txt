## Relax in Elegance and Comfort at The Rittenhouse

The Rittenhouse places the emphasis on refined elegance. These are the very finest Philadelphia luxury accommodations, matching the charm of Rittenhouse Square and complementing the city’s rich history. The newly-redesigned Philadelphia hotel rooms and suites, as well as the newly built Park Suites offer an array of amenities and services. Each of our Rittenhouse hotel rooms quietly overlooks Rittenhouse Square or the city. Discover which one suits you best.

Once you’ve found the perfect fit, check availability and book your stay at our accommodations near Rittenhouse Square today. The Rittenhouse is the premier choice among places to stay in Philadelphia.
