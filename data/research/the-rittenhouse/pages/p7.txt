# Holiday Magic on the Square

A festive, elevated take on classic holiday traditions, bringing wonder, warmth, and timeless celebration to The Rittenhouse.

This season, The Rittenhouse Hotel unveils Holiday Magic on the Square, an elegant celebration of the festive season. Indulge in refined holiday dining and bespoke winter spa experiences, all set against the enchanting backdrop of Rittenhouse Square. A timeless expression of holiday splendor awaits.

## Lacroix

### Holiday Pie Boutique

Returning for another season, Lacroix’s pastry team presents the Holiday Pie Boutique, featuring a refined selection of seasonal creations made for holiday gatherings and gifting. For Thanksgiving, choose from Apple Cinnamon Crumble, Pumpkin Pie with Speculoos and Pumpkin Brittle, or Pear and Almond Tart. For Christmas, the collection features French Silk made with Valrhona 62% chocolate, Pumpkin Pie with Speculoos and Pumpkin Brittle, and Cranberry and Orange Tart.

**Thanksgiving Preorders:** November 1–17

**Thanksgiving Pick-Up:** November 23 & 25, 11 AM–3 PM at Lacroix

**Christmas Preorders:** December 1–15

**Christmas Pick-Up:** December 22 & 23, 11 AM–3 PM at Lacroix

### Thanksgiving Day Brunch

Celebrate Thanksgiving at Lacroix with an elegant holiday buffet served from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. Begin with East and West Coast oysters, caviar, and chilled seafood, then savor seasonal salads, traditional Thanksgiving sides and a carving station featuring prime rib and turkey. A sophisticated take on the holiday, thoughtfully prepared for gathering and indulgence.

Reservations

### Christmas Eve & Christmas Day Brunch

Celebrate the holidays at Lacroix with an indulgent brunch buffet, served from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Enjoy East and West Coast oysters, caviar and chilled seafood, festive canapés and seasonal favorites, alongside a lavish carving station featuring prime rib, lamb and duck. Settle in for an elegant afternoon of exceptional food, festive flavors and good company.

Reservations

### Christmas Eve Dinner

Celebrate Christmas Eve with a five-course prix fixe dinner at lacroix, featuring seasonal ingredients and thoughtful preparations. The menu includes smoked calabaza agnolotti, golden tilefish with champagne and truffle, and American Wagyu short rib, followed by a special dessert to complete the evening.

Reservations

### Christmas Dinner

Join us at Lacroix for Christmas dinner from 5:30 to 9:30 PM, featuring special selections from the chef and a thoughtful chef’s gift to take home. Menu to come soon.

Reservations

### New Year’s Eve Brunch

Gather at Lacroix for a festive New Year’s Eve brunch buffet from 11 AM to 3 PM. Enjoy a generous array of brunch favorites and chef-crafted dishes before the evening’s celebrations begin. Menu to come soon.

Reservations

### New Year’s Eve Dinner

Welcome the New Year with an exceptional six-course prix fixe dinner at Lacroix, featuring an amuse-bouche and a special chef’s gift. Three seatings are available at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 PM, with an optional wine pairing available to complement the evening. Menu to come soon.
