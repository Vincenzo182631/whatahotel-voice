# Your Choice of Luxurious Hotel Rooms in Philadelphia's Center City

Newly-redesigned guest rooms at The Rittenhouse are spacious, comfortable and come with the very latest in modern luxury features and amenities. Across the entire hotel, our accommodations near Rittenhouse Square exude supreme luxury and comfort. Our guest rooms are generously proportioned, measuring between 450 and 600 square feet - the largest guest rooms in the city.

Stunning marble bathrooms, plush bedding and the finest accessories underscore The Rittenhouse’s standard of elegance.

### Our Luxury Hotel Rooms Feature:

- Twice daily housekeeping including evening turndown service
- Plush bed with pillow-top mattress, duvets and king-size down pillows (foam pillows are also available)
- State-of-the-art technology featuring a large flat-screen television and iPod docking station. Easily connect your laptop as well as other devices.
- Entertainment on demand
- Complimentary wireless and wired high-speed Internet access
- Individual climate control
- In-room safe
- Private bar stocked with a selection of cold beverages and snacks
- Luxurious marble bathroom featuring Acqua Di Parma bath amenities
- Glass enclosed shower with massage showerhead (select guest rooms have soaking bathtubs)
- Separate vanity and dressing area with illuminated makeup mirror
- Plush bathrobe and slippers
- Overnight shoe shining service
