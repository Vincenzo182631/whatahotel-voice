# Restaurants in Rittenhouse Square

## The Best Dining in Downtown Philadelphia

As a culinary center, our city provides some of the best restaurants in the country. From the deliciously innovative cuisine of Lacroix, the AAA Four Diamond Award restaurant heralded as one of the finest in the U.S. with stunning views overlooking Rittenhouse Square, to hand-crafted cocktails in the modern seclusion of the Library Bar, The Rittenhouse’s culinary offerings are true standouts among restaurants in Rittenhouse Square.

Explore our in-house offerings below, the best among restaurants in Downtown Philadelphia.

### Lacroix

A beloved fixture on Rittenhouse Square for over two decades, Lacroix has evolved into a modern expression of refined dining—where French bistro influence meets global flavors, and the cocktail program is as considered as the cuisine. Expect seasonally driven menus crafted with the finest local ingredients, an expertly curated wine cellar, and a bar program spotlighting housemade infusions and timeless techniques. In-room and private dining are also available.

### Library Bar

A spirited hideaway just off the lobby, The Library Bar is where classic cocktails, rare spirits, and thoughtful small plates come together in an intimate, book-lined setting. Beloved by locals and travelers alike, it’s a place to settle in with a well-crafted drink and explore a curated collection of fine and rare books—ranging from Philadelphia’s storied past to its vibrant creative present.

### Mary Cassatt Tea Room

Savor the timeless tradition of afternoon tea with globally sourced infusions, handcrafted pastries, and seasonal tea sandwiches, all served in a serene setting that feels like a true escape from the city. Nestled within the Mary Cassatt Tea Room, this cherished ritual is best enjoyed with a reservation.

### Ocho Supper Club

The Rittenhouse welcomes back Ocho Supper Club for a limited residency at Lacroix, where Chef RJ Smith brings thoughtful cooking, Caribbean influences and a sense of occasion to the table on select Sundays from September 13 through December 20. Featuring a rotating selection of culinary experiences and special guest chef collaborations, the series offers a unique opportunity to experience Ocho at The Rittenhouse. Discover the full series, upcoming dates and reservation details.

### In Room Dining

Indulge in the exceptional with In-Room Dining at The Rittenhouse, available daily from 6 AM to 10:45 PM. Savor curated dishes from Lacroix, thoughtfully prepared and delivered to the comfort and privacy of your room. Experience refined dining elevated by the elegance and impeccable service synonymous with The Rittenhouse.
