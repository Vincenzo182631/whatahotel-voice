Less than two hours from Bangkok, InterContinental Pattaya Resort is a tropical beach oasis that offers unsurpassed views of the Gulf of Thailand, Koh Larn (Coral Island), and beyond. The luxury five-star resort is home to spacious guestrooms, suites and villas, acclaimed restaurants, a spa, and versatile meeting venues perfect for every occasion. The hotel’s convenient location offers easy access to Pattaya’s famous attractions, including Pattaya Floating Market and Walking Street.

### The wedding of your dreams

Bring your dream beach wedding to life. Our attentive wedding specialists will curate and personalise every detail for your big day. Choose your mesmerising setting, from picturesque ocean views to a romantic indoor ambience and let our team handle the rest of the details for you.

### Amburaya Spa

Featuring organic botanicals for facial and body treatments, Amburaya Spa has six private suites perfect for both couples and individuals. Enjoy signature treatments from our experienced masseuses, including the Amburaya Embrace--a full-body massage that features the combination of five massage therapies for a truly relaxing experience.

Learn more

### Infiniti Restaurant

Overlooking the Gulf of Thailand, Infiniti Restaurant offers panoramic sunset views and signature Mediterranean dishes like Seabass Carpaccio with Avocado Purée, Salsa Verde, and Wild Rocket. Complement your meal with locally inspired cocktails, beers, and wines as you enjoy the breathtaking views.

Learn more

### Elements Restaurant

Embark on a culinary journey with Thai and international delights that will satisfy your palate, including the Marvellous BBQ Seafood Buffet every Saturday, which features fresh seafood. Experience a gourmet meal at the elegant dining room inside or take in the ocean views on the patio.

## FAQs for InterContinental Pattaya Resort

Does InterContinental Pattaya Resort have a pool?

InterContinental Pattaya Resort has 1 outdoor pool.

Spirituality Lagoon, Splash Lagoon and Serenity Pool

Our three stunning lagoon-style pools, surrounded by lush greenery, are perfect for swimming and sunbathing, with plenty of sun beds for relaxation and refreshing drinks close by. The shallow Splash Lagoon is ideal for our younger guests and kids.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Pattaya Resort?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Pattaya Resort for hotel guests.

The resort offers covered outdoor self-and valet-parking. The parking lot has a 2.5 meter height restriction.

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Pattaya Resort?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Pattaya Resort.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Pattaya Resort for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Pattaya Resort have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Pattaya Resort has 2 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Pattaya Resort?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Pattaya Resort.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Pattaya Resort?

Check-in at InterContinental Pattaya Resort is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental Pattaya Resort from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Pattaya Resort is conveniently located near Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), approximately 78.91 miles away (127 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Pattaya Resort?

InterContinental Pattaya Resort is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Jomtien Beach - 4.29 miles away (6.9 kilometers)
- Sriracha Tiger Zoo - 21.06 miles away (33.9 kilometers)
- Pattaya Park - 1.62 miles away (2.6 kilometers)
- MiNi Siam - 5.97 miles away (9.6 kilometers)
- Sanctury of Truth - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Pattaya Resort puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
