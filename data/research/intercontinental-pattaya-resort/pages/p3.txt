### Infiniti Restaurant

Overlooking the Gulf of Thailand, Infiniti Restaurant offers panoramic sunset views and signature Mediterranean dishes like Seabass Carpaccio with Avocado Purée, Salsa Verde, and Wild Rocket. Complement your meal with locally inspired cocktails, beers, and wines as you enjoy the breathtaking views.

## Elements Restaurant

Discover a luxurious dining experience at Elements Restaurant, where Thai and international cuisine come together to delight your senses. Indulge in the renowned Marvelous BBQ Seafood Buffet every Saturday, offering the freshest seafood and an array of gourmet dishes. Enjoy your meal in the elegant air conditioned dining area or the inviting alfresco terrace, both providing the perfect setting for a memorable evening. Elements promises a sophisticated and unforgettable dining experience.

Infiniti Restaurant offers an unforgettable Italian grilled dining experience, set against stunning sunset views over the Gulf of Thailand. A must visit in Pattaya, the restaurant combines exceptional cuisine with a luxurious atmosphere, perfect for those seeking a memorable evening. Enjoy a curated selection of fine wines, handcrafted cocktails, and premium beers as you take in the panoramic beauty of the ocean sunset.
