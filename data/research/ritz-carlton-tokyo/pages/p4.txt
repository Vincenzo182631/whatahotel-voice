Cuisine Journey in Tokyo
Dining
Each of the seven restaurants and bars at our Midtown hotel broadens your horizons, much like the vistas stretching across the Tokyo skyline. Discover French cuisine at Héritage by Kei Kobayashi, our Japanese restaurant at Hinokizaka, and seasonal produce paired with local seafood at Towers.
Nina at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo
Our new patisserie, Nina at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo, invites you into a world of refined French sweets. From delicate pastries to beautifully crafted desserts, each creation reflects precision, balance, and seasonal inspiration—an indulgent moment designed to be savored in the heart of Tokyo.
