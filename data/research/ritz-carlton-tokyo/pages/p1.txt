Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo
Experience true luxury
Occupying the top nine floors of the 53-story Midtown Tower in the Roppongi District, the capital’s entertainment and design-related business hub, The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo features 245 elegantly appointed guest rooms. Our 5-star hotel in Tokyo city features a Club Lounge and fine dining with views of the Shinjuku area, the Imperial Palace, or Mount Fuji. Guests at our luxury hotel in Tokyo can enjoy an indoor pool, a soothing spa, and a floor dedicated to health and wellness. Our business hotel in Tokyo also features reimagined meeting spaces with free Wi-Fi available throughout the property. Whether you are traveling for business or leisure, a luxurious welcome awaits at The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo. Self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo, with rates of ¥800.00 per hour, ¥8000.00 per day, and ¥56000.00 per week. Valet parking is available for ¥8000.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo is Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND). HND is located approximately 13.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
