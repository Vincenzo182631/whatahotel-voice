Elevate Your Stay
Embrace the Ritz-Carlton Club experience in our Tokyo lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience in Tokyo, Japan, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Tokyo Club Lounge in Roppongi, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay at our Tokyo hotel with concierge services by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Reserve Now
Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay with us in our exclusive Club Lounge, where you’ll be greeted by our Club concierge and enjoy a dedicated check-in, exclusively for our Club guests. For the rest of your stay, consider the Club Lounge your private sanctuary in Tokyo.
*Guests below 12 years of age are welcome between 7 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. daily.
*For a third guest enjoying Club Lounge privileges, a supplement of JPY 25,520 per person per night will be applied for adults and children 12 years old and above; and JPY 5,104 per person per night for children from 6 to 11 years old.
