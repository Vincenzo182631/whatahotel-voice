Explore Tokyo From Our Hotel
Experiences
The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo soars above Roppongi District and captures spectacular landmarks such as National Stadium and Mount Fuji. Attractions such as Mori Museum, Billboard Live and The National Art Center, Tokyo are steps away from the hotel, along with foreign embassies and high-end shopping. Areas such as Akasaka, Omotesando, Ginza and Shibuya are further afield, promising worthwhile expeditions with bird’s-eye views from one of the tallest hotels in Tokyo. Guests can even immerse themselves in the traditions of Tokyo without leaving the hotel. A dynamic selection of Japanese cultural experiences range from manga to Ikebana.
Enhance your stay​
Curated Moments ​
The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo is pleased to present our esteemed guests with an inspiring selection of authentic and thoughtfully curated experiences, each designed to illuminate the beauty, culture and traditions of Japan. Whether taking in the vibrant energy of the city or discovering the quieter nuances of local heritage, let us take you on a truly unforgettable journey.​ ​For further information, please contact our Guest Relations team directly.

Signature Experiences​
Japanese Calligraphy Experience​
Experience the meditative art of Japanese calligraphy - Totonoeru Shodō - under the guidance of a revered calligraphy master. This immersive practice offers a serene path to inner balance, harmonizing body and mind through deliberate, flowing brushwork. Allow yourself to sink into a quiet rhythm as each stroke becomes a meditation in motion.​
City Rickshaw Journey
Embark on a private rickshaw tour through the vibrant streets of Roppongi, where old and new coexist in striking harmony. Sit back and glide through the bustling metropolis in a historical Japanese passenger cart, discovering hidden sanctuaries along the way. Led by a knowledgeable guide, savor a rare, intimate perspective of Tokyo’s timeless traditions set against its contemporary spirit.
Spirit of the Katana
Embark on an unforgettable journey as you step into the forge of one of Japan’s remaining master swordsmiths. This immersive experience offers a rare glimpse into the traditional craft of samurai sword making, allowing you to witness centuries-old techniques yourself. Shape a genuine katana through honoring the craftsmanship and heritage passed down for generations.
The Art of Karuizawa
Discover the refined charm of Karuizawa through a curated journey, with artist and entrepreneur David Stanley Hewett, whose gold-leaf artworks adorn the walls of The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo. Explore local shrines, discover the legacy of Karuizawa, and enjoy an intimate lunch in one of Japan’s most favored escapes from the city.
Private Yacht Cruise
Spend the day out on the water and glide past some of Tokyo’s most iconic skylines. Enjoy the vistas of Rainbow Bridge, Asakusa, Tokyo Skytree or even Yokohama Harbor, from a breathtaking, novel perspective. Let yourself be carried away by the rhythm of the waves, and enjoy the timeless allure of life on the water.

Ritz Kids
From the moment of your arrival at our Tokyo hotel with a swimming pool, we welcome kids to The Ritz-Carlton by encouraging them to explore the sights and sounds of the hotel and its environment. Guided by playfulness and a sense of wonder, Ritz Kids delights and engages your children to interact with their surroundings in a fresh and exciting way, while introducing them to the four important pillars of Ritz Kids discovery: Water, Land, Environmental Responsibility, and Culture.

Night Safari
An extraordinary in-room experience, The Ritz Kids Night Safari embraces the creative spirit and thirst for new adventures. After entering the room, children will discover a kids tent, floor mat, and curated camping amenities. A selection of exclusive items is also offered for the family to bring home as memorable souvenirs including lion backpack and lion doll, designed exclusively for The Ritz-Carlton.
Kids Menu
Experience the delight of in-room dining with a selection of both Japanese and Western favorites for children, available all day. Families with children under 12 are also invited to enjoy the Kids Afternoon Tea at The Lobby Lounge. This special experience features a menu of kid-friendly treats alongside our traditional afternoon tea for parents.
Stamp Rally
Explore in the hotel for a stamp rally around 4 significant sites and win a special prizes.
Kids Flower Arrangement
In this enriching and sustainable experience, young guests are invited to craft their own floral arrangements using carefully selected blooms previously displayed throughout the hotel. A refined, hands-on introduction to the art of floristry and mindful living.
Dream Tour
Step into the world of hospitality with our exclusive “Dream Tour” for kids. Dressed in the same uniform as our Ladies and Gentlemen, young guests will enjoy a hands-on experience as they learn how to greet guests, assist with check-ins, and prepare rooms—offering a fun and inspiring glimpse into the art of luxury service.
