Exceptional Luxury Suites in Tokyo
Signature Accommodations
Discover the pinnacle of sophistication in our four signature suites — the Ritz-Carlton Suite, Presidential Suite, Modern Japanese Suite, and Club Carlton Suite. Each offers a distinct expression of luxury, blending refined design, cultural elegance, and breathtaking views high above Tokyo.
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
The Ritz-Carlton Suite in Tokyo combines refined Japanese aesthetics with modern sophistication. This elevated retreat offers expansive space, perfect for both private moments and elegant gatherings.
Timeless Design
The suite features exquisite details such as Edo-Kiriko pendant lights, ebony wood flooring, and a cloud-patterned carpet. A Kumiko wood headboard and Kyoto Nishijin-textile wallpaper reflect Japan’s artisanal heritage.

The Presidential Suite
Crafted Comfort, Elevated Elegance
From a kumiko-crafted headboard made of rare Jindai cedar to a refined bathroom in calming tones, every detail is designed to evoke warmth, depth, and sophistication—creating a serene, beautifully harmonized sanctuary above the city.

Club Carlton Suite
Nestled in the corners of each floor, the Carlton Suite offers 120㎡ of serene luxury. With panoramic views—Tokyo Bay to the southwest or Nishi-Shinjuku’s skyline to the northeast—this suite blends elegance with a true sense of place.
