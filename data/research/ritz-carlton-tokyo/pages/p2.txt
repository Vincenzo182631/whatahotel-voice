Suites & Rooms
Guest rooms and hotel suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo are a welcome respite to the vibrant city below. Our Tokyo luxury accommodations merge an East-meets-West design including marble bathrooms, floor-to-ceiling windows, and a residentially inspired layout. Our Club Lounge on the 53rd floor includes four ambient spaces, culinary offerings, and exclusive events.

Blend of East and West
Suites at the Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo
Suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo offer a beautiful blend of East and West. Recognized throughout Asia for their luxurious spaces and amenities, our hotel suites in Tokyo, Japan, feature residentially inspired layouts, contemporary finishes, and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the cityscape, from National Stadium to Mt. Fuji.
Truly Spectacular Stays
The hotel’s two most luxurious suites offer exclusive amenities including a personal attendant, aroma butler service, chauffeured transportation, in-room foot draining, priority Club Lounge seating, an in-room flower arrangement, and more.
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
The largest suite at The Ritz-Carlton, Tokyo at 3,300 sq. ft. offers a panoramic view from the 53rd floor, two bathrooms, a living room, and a dining area.
