Club Business Studio
Large rooms feature a sitting area seperated by a beautifully crafted wooden screen. Separate bathtub and shower facilities. Sliding rosewood closet and bathroom doors, plus extended wardrobe. Contemporary Chinese décor, furnishings and artwork Sealy beds with 400-thread count bed linens, cozy down feather pillows and throws and a 55-inch LCD TV. Large work desk equipped with personalized stationery. Multisensory alarm clock with bluetooth streaming and USB charging. This accommodation is eligible for Club Level Access.

A Bespoke Experience in a Redefined Setting
Explore the Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
The newly-renovated Ritz-Carlton Club features luxuriously appointed rooms and suites, including four Penthouse Suites on Level 45 and The Ritz-Carlton Suite on Level 44. The Club is exclusive for meetings or gatherings with complimentary pressing. Concierge staff can make travel arrangements, theatre and restaurant reservations and local itineraries. The non-smoking Club Lounge is the focus of quiet, refined activity.
