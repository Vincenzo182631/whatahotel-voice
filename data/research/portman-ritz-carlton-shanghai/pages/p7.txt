Amenities
Rooms
610
This conveniently located contemporary tower has large rooms with Chinese artwork, onyx panels, and fresh flowers. Each penthouse suite has a living room, a bathroom with a walk-in closet and an oversized tub, and views of Shanghai. Public spaces are done in Spanish sandstone and marble, with silver leaves on the walls, and Chinese lanterns.
