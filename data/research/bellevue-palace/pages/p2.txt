# Discover Bern

Bern is the Capital of Switzerland and the Bellevue Palace stands right next door to the Bundeshaus, or Federal Palace. With its miles of arcades and historic fountains, the old town of Bern is a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site; of particular note is the Zytglogge astronomical clock created in 1530. The BärenPark is the symbol of a city with a broad cultural heritage that includes the Zentrum Paul Klee. In the summer, the recreation area along the banks of the River Aare is ideal for strolling, jogging and swimming. The environs of Bern open onto the Emmental valley and the Schwarzenburger District, one of the loveliest pre-alpine landscapes in Switzerland.

Activities

Explore the BärenPark, home to Bern’s symbolic bears, or hike along the scenic Aare River. Discover the Naturpark Gantrisch, offering outdoor adventures amidst stunning landscapes. Walk the Häxewääg trail around Schwarzsee and immerse yourself in local myths.

Art & Culture

Visit the Einstein Museum to learn about the famous physicist’s time in Bern. Admire masterpieces at the Kunstmuseum Bern, showcasing works from eight centuries. Experience creativity at Zentrum Paul Klee, with interactive workshops for all ages.

Attractions

Entertainment

Whether a visit to the world-famous Jungfraujoch, a boat trip across the turquoise-green Lake Thun or the discovery of Gruyère Castle, there is something for everyone.
