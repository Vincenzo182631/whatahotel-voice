# Fiat Lux

#### Charleston’s premier rooftop dining

Perched above King Street on Hotel Bennett’s ninth floor, Fiat Lux is a rooftop bar and dining destination with panoramic views of downtown Charleston and the Holy City skyline. The indoor-outdoor space offers a front-row seat to Charleston’s famous golden light, making it especially memorable around sunset.

Settle in for a specialty cocktail, a bottle of rosé or a glass of wine, or enjoy relaxed rooftop dining with poolside bites and shareable dishes. During the day, Fiat Lux offers an easygoing place to lounge above the city; after sunset, the atmosphere shifts into an elevated setting for cocktails and conversation.

Daytime Attire - Casual dry pool or swim attire is acceptable. Shirt, swimsuit cover-ups and footwear are required.

Evening Attire - Collared shirt required for gentlemen.  Well-kept denim and dress shorts are acceptable. Hat Policy - Gentlemen’s hats and caps are not permitted in any of the indoor dining or bar venues. Gentlemen are permitted to wear outdoor hats at outdoor venues. Women are permitted to venue-appropriate hats prior to 5 p.m. or dressier hats before or after 5 p.m.
