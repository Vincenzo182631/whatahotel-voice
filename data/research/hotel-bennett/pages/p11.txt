# Relax Atop Charleston

Take in Charleston from a different perspective at Hotel Bennett’s heated rooftop pool, where panoramic views of downtown meet an elevated, unhurried poolside experience. Lounge above Marion Square on the 9th floor, soak up the Charleston sunshine or settle in for golden-hour views as the city unfolds below.

Open year-round exclusively to overnight guests, the rooftop offers a peaceful escape above the energy of King Street. For an even more private afternoon, reserve one of our rooftop cabanas, complete with reserved chaise lounges, privacy curtains, refreshments and a complimentary bottle of Prosecco or six domestic beers.

#### Hours

Daily 7:00 am to 8:00 pm \| Overnight guests only

Food & Beverage Service 11:00 am to 6:30 pm

Heated year-round

#### CABANA RENTALS

Private cabanas can be rented exclusively for in-house guests both for full days and half days. Rental times will be 10:00 am - 6:30 pm for a full day. Half days can be either 10:00 am - 2:00 pm or 2:30 pm - 6:30 pm.

Cabana rentals will include:

- TV, Refrigerator, Oscillating Fan, Couch, Privacy Curtains

- Sodas (Coke, Diet Coke, Sprite, Ginger Ale)

- 1 Complimentary bottle of Prosecco (1 bottle per cabana) or 6 domestic beers (Bud Light or Michelob Ultra)

- Two (2) reserved chaise loungers per cabana

- Take-home SunBum Day Tripper Bag: sunscreen, aloe lotion, and lip balm
