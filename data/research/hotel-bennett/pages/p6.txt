# Eat, Drink and Enjoy Charleston

Hotel Bennett’s dining impresses the most discerning guests exploring the culinary capital of the south. Each space meets your every need. Camellia’s immaculate design has become a notable destination for Charlestonians and visitors alike. Get your pastry fix at La Pâtisserie, the Parisian-inspired Café with homemade goods baked by renowned French Pastry Chef Rémy Fünfrock. Enjoy Gabrielle's locally sourced ingredients on the outdoor terrace with iconic views of Marion Square, or head to the rooftop at Fiat Lux for poolside bites or sunset drinks.
