# Where Special Moments Are Made

Step into a design and architectural wonder. Inspired by the renowned Fabergé jewels, Camellias' etched-mirror ceiling and crystal chandelier creates a breathtaking world unlike any other champagne lounge. Named "Most Instagrammable Restaurant" in South Carolina by Food Network, Camellias boasts an extensive collection of bottle and by-the-glass drinks paired with a decadent selection of caviar, charcuterie, local seafood and homemade desserts by La Pâtisserie. The bold pink interiors include marble reclaimed from the library that stood in this spot, a nod to the area’s rich history and tradition. Whether you’re looking to celebrate over Afternoon Tea or treat yourself with friends, Camellias will exceed your greatest expectations.

### Cristal at Camellias

In an exclusive partnership with Louis Roederer, Camellias is proud to be the only venue in South Carolina offering Cristal by the glass, including rare access to the coveted 2013 Vintage. This is a one-of-a-kind opportunity to experience one of the world’s most prestigious champagnes in a setting as glamorous as the pour itself.

Whether you’re celebrating a milestone, savoring a summer evening, or simply enjoying the finer things, A Cristal Summer at Camellias invites you to sip, sparkle, and escape in style.
