## Fitness & Movement Center

Elevate your stay at JW Marriott Crete with our state-of-the-art fitness and movement space. Enjoy 24/7 complimentary access to cardio and strength equipment, including ellipticals, exercise bikes, stair climbers, free weights, and weight machines. Join our daily group sessions and stay energized while embracing the ultimate spa and resort experience.

Fun, Learning & Adventure

## Family by JW

At the JW Kids Club, young guests can dive into a world of fun, creativity, and adventure. From interactive games and arts and crafts to supervised activities designed for all ages, every moment is filled with excitement and discovery. While children play and explore safely, parents can relax knowing their little ones are entertained in a nurturing and inspiring environment.

Wellness & Relaxation

### Anoseas Spa

Step into Anoseas Spa, a sanctuary of serenity and luxury. Rejuvenate your body, mind, and spirit with signature treatments, soothing therapies, and tailored wellness rituals. From calming massages to revitalizing facials and holistic experiences, every detail is designed to restore balance and leave you feeling renewed. Discover a place where tranquility meets indulgence, and every moment is dedicated to your well-being.

Moments of Discovery

### Adult Pool

Step into a haven of tranquility at our adults-only pool, where sophistication meets relaxation. Lounge in comfort, soak up the Cretan sun, or take a refreshing dip in serene surroundings designed for ultimate peace and privacy. Every detail is crafted to offer a luxurious retreat for guests seeking calm, elegance, and rejuvenation.

### Main Pool

Experience refined relaxation at our exquisite main pool, the centerpiece of JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa. Surrounded by elegant design and sweeping sea views, it offers a tranquil setting to unwind under the Cretan sun. Enjoy attentive poolside service, serene ambiance, and a sense of effortless sophistication that defines the JW experience.

### Kids Pool

A place designed especially for our youngest guests, the Kids’ Pool offers safe, joyful moments under the Cretan sun. Surrounded by comfortable seating and gentle waters, it’s the perfect spot for children to splash, laugh, and play while parents relax nearby in complete peace of mind.

### Retail Shop

Discover curated elegance at our Resort Boutique, where style meets sophistication. Explore a refined selection of resort wear, local artisan pieces, wellness essentials, and exclusive JW Marriott memorabilia. Every item is thoughtfully chosen to capture the essence of Crete and the timeless spirit of JW.

### JW Garden

Discover a tranquil outdoor retreat where nature sets the tone for relaxation and reflection. The JW Garden invites guests to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with the natural beauty of Crete. Thoughtfully designed, it reflects JW Marriott’s dedication to mindfulness, balance, and a sense of place.

### JW Market

Step into JW Market and explore a handpicked selection of local and international delicacies. From artisanal Cretan specialties to premium gourmet products, every item is carefully chosen to elevate your stay and create a truly indulgent experience.

## Journey Through Crete

Embark on an unforgettable journey where outdoor adventures seamlessly blend with cultural immersion. Engage in authentic local experiences such as hiking, wine or olive oil tasting, and create lasting memories through unique hands-on activities at our resort near Marathi Beach. Traverse scenic trails for awe-inspiring vistas, indulge in leisurely moments on Crete’s pristine beaches, and unwind with therapeutic massages and signature treatments at Anoseas Spa. Every experience is thoughtfully curated to elevate your stay and celebrate the essence of Crete.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Adult Pool (Outdoor)Main Pool (Outdoor)Kids Pool (Outdoor)

Does JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have an on-site spa?

Yes, Anoseas Spa is located on-site at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa?

Hike the Gorge, Sail to Serenity, Ceramic Workshop, Wine and Olive Oil Tastings, Chania Old Town Walking Tour and more are nearby JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa.
