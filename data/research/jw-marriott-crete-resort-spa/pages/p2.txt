## Accommodations

Featuring 160 rooms, suites, and villas, many with private pools and sea views, the resort offers accommodation in Chania that blends architecture, mindfulness, and authentic hospitality. Designed by Athens-based studio Block722, each space integrates seamlessly into the landscape through green rooftops, olive and carob trees, and natural materials such as marble and wood. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have a work space?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 5 at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa?

No, the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have fridges?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have a Refrigerator.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
