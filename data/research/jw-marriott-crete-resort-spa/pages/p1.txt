# Welcome to JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa

## Discover Cretan joy at our resort in Chania, Crete

Nestled near Chania on Crete's pristine coastline, the seafront JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa spans 100 coastal acres with 160 rooms, suites, and villas, each shaped by impeccable design and stunning sea views. Secluded yet well connected, with the airport and port nearby, this Chania luxury resort invites you into a land of millennia-old history and living tradition expressed through sun-drenched...See More beaches, rocky mountains, fresh water, blossoms, herbs, olive oil, and the island's very own soil. For wellness-led stays, indulge in rejuvenating spa treatments and holistic rituals; for family escapes, our family resort in Crete offers a comprehensive Kids Club that fosters creativity and connection with nature. Guests praise the peaceful setting, while authentic Cretan and international flavors from JW Garden and local producers complete the experience at our luxury resort in Crete.See Less

The Facilities

### Family by JW

Family by JW™ offers experiences that appeal to every generation, ensuring that the whole family can come together for their most meaningful moments. Developed with Worldwide kids, cultural discovery and imaginative play come to life with shaded outdoor spaces and a vegetable garden.

Learn More

### Anoseas Spa

Embark on a journey of self-discovery, inspired by traditional Cretan healing rituals and enhanced with modern, immersive therapies with five treatment rooms, an exclusive couple’s suite, a steam bath, a KLAFS sauna, and a beauty room offering hand and foot treatments with LEMI applications. Complementing the experience is a sunlit yoga deck overlooking the sea, an indoor vitality pool for moments of relaxation.

Learn More

### Fitness & Mobility Studio

Find a diverse range of movement options that engage both the body and mind at the state-of-the-art fitness and mobility studio. From sunrise yoga with sea views on the wooden deck, to high-intensity training, TRX, core workouts, to guided hikes along the surrounding hills. A custom-designed parkour trail invites playful fitness, while personal trainers create tailored programs.

Learn More

### Pools & Beach

Unwind in style at the breath-taking beach and pool areas, set on a pristine stretch of sand and coastline, beside an inviting collection of outdoor pools.

Learn More

### JW Garden

Inspired by our founders love for the outdoors, The JW Garden is a signature element of the JW Marriott experience focused on creating sensory connections with the natural world, enabling you to appreciate sustainability and rejuvenate by all that we harvest. Our JW Garden concept feautures a variety of local herbs grown and sourced to flavor the restaurant and bar menus with authentic Cretan aromas.

Learn More

### JW Market

With a curated selection of locally sourced produce, wines, teas, and cold-pressed juices, this welcoming café-style market, encourages you to start your day in Crete island on the perfect note.

## Coastal Escape

Escape the ordinary and sail into a day filled with secluded beauty, crystal-clear waters, and untouched beaches , with crowd-free stops and exclusive access to natural wonders. The boat trip invites you to explore Balos Gramvousa, Crete’s most breathtaking coastal treasure protected by the Natura 2000 program, a natural gem with pink and white sand and serene turquoise waters, while the stop at the secluded Menies Cove features a sacred site dedicated to the goddess Vritomartis (a figure closely linked to Artemis).

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 8.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of €100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa is Chania International Airport (CHQ). CHQ is located approximately 8.8 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Crete Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
