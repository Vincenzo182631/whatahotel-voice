# Busan Hotels \| Luxury 5-Star Park Hyatt Busan, South Korea

## Top Luxury Hotel in Haeundae, Busan

A luxurious landmark address with sprawling views of the ocean and the famed Gwangan Bridge, Park Hyatt Busan is situated in the heart of Haeundae, the city’s premier shopping and beach destination, alongside world-class shopping malls and Busan Exhibition and Convention Center (BEXCO). Be it for business or leisure travel, Park Hyatt Busan offers residential comfort with personalized experiences, welcoming guests with fine art, unique food and beverage offerings, and gracious service.

## Discover Park Hyatt Busan

Business Services

Designed with the corporate traveler in mind, Park Hyatt Busan is the premier choice among the city’s business hotels. Located on the 30th floor, our 24-hour business center offers a range of services for our corporate guests, including a fully equipped private boardroom with panoramic city views. For further information please contact the business center at +82 51 990 1234 or busan.park@hyatt.com.

Prepare for a More Sustainable Stay

As part of our commitment to sustainability, certain room amenities are discontinued or only available upon request. We invite you to learn what essentials you may need to bring so that you can ensure a more comfortable stay while helping us care for the environment.

Spa

In pursuit of harmony between humans and nature, Ocelas Spa offers seven treatment rooms, including two designed for couples. Our internationally trained expert therapists use the finest organic ingredients—as well as stones and herbs sourced from nature—to deliver treatments infused with natural energy, restoring calm and tranquility to body and mind.

Pool & Sauna

Our 20-meter granite indoor swimming pool offers guests the chance to experience true relaxation. Swim or sunbathe within our natural garden interior and enjoy the light breeze from our floor-to-ceiling windows, which offer ocean views. The peaceful sauna—offering dry and steam options—assures privacy with personal shower booths and large lockers. Our pool and sauna area is particularly gorgeous at night, with ambient lighting and stunning views of Gwangan Bridge.

Open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM with a break from 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM.​

\*The swimming pool is accessible until 9:00 PM on Saturdays, in accordance with the operation of the wellness program.​​

Fitness Center

Enjoy our fitness center, equipped with premium Italian fitness equipment from "Technogym," for effective physical activity programs and a private studio for personal training.

Open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM
