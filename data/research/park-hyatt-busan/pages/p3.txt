# Restaurants & Bar

With dramatic views from every seat where you almost feel to be able to reach out and touch Gwangan Bridge, all our restaurants, bar and Lounge in Park Hyatt Busan present a diversity of culinary experiences that await your discovery.
Park Hyatt Busan has received a globally recognized and self-audited food safety management system ISO22000, HACCP certification and GBAC STAR™ facility accreditation. We remain committed to providing a dining environment with strict food safety and hygiene protocols so that you and your loved ones can dine with confidence and peace of mind.
