### Disappointing Experience at Park Hyatt Busan

There were definitely some positives during my stay, but overall, the experience did not live up to what I would expect from a Park Hyatt property.

1\. Arrival & Doormen — Excellent
The doormen at the arrival lobby were fantastic. They were warm, attentive and welcoming, and honestly gave the best first impression of the hotel. They deserve special mention.

2\. Check-in Experience — Underwhelming
Unfortunately, the check-in experience was quite disappointing. I went to the World of Hyatt counter, but the lady there did not greet me, make eye contact, or even acknowledge me properly. She also did not direct me to the normal check-in counter. For a luxury hotel, I expected a much warmer and more attentive welcome.

3\. Breakfast — A Major Disappointment
I was specifically advised to go for breakfast early as the hotel was supposedly full house. However, the breakfast itself was extremely underwhelming.

I had breakfast for two mornings and the spread and selection were practically unchanged. There was very little variety, and the overall offering felt far below what I would expect from a Park Hyatt. The pastries were particularly disappointing — they honestly looked and tasted like something purchased commercially rather than freshly prepared for a luxury hotel. The overall breakfast selection was so lacklustre that I decided to skip breakfast altogether on the third day.

Being told to come early because the hotel was full house made me expect a busy but well-managed breakfast service. Instead, the issue was not really the crowd — it was the quality and selection of the food itself. For a hotel positioned at this level, I expected much more attention to detail, especially when it comes to breakfast and guest experience. Even Le Meridien did better or even a 4-star hotels did a better job.

### Disappointed with Park Hyatt Busan

I recently stayed at the Park Hyatt Busan from 13 to 28 August 2026 and was erroneously charged a laundry bill of 324,820,000 KRW / $235.25 USD at the end of my stay.

My reservation with the US Navy group booking stated that I was entitled to three (3) complimentary pieces of laundry per day. The concierge at the Park Hyatt when I checked in explained to me that this could be any 3 items regardless of type of garment and that any amount of underwear, socks and cotton shirts would be free. On several days, I put out more than 3 pieces of laundry, but on most days I did not and certainly not in the amounts that are shown on my bill. It appears I was charged full price for all laundry items during my stay, which is incorrect. The hotel has refused to provide an itemized list to prove what they actually charged me for.
The Park Hyatt Busan staff refuses to refund the excess charges.

Additionally, my experience at this hotel was that the staff were frequently rude and pushy, there was a persistent leak in my room's ceiling that almost broke my computer, the communication with guest services was often not clear, my room had a colony of some kind of moth-like bug that was all of the walls the entire visit, and on multiple days the cleaning service did not attend to my room. The general rudeness was evident even in the tone of the hotel manager's response to my charge dispute.
