# PINE CLIFFS GOLF COURSE

## The golf course at Pine Cliffs is perfect for beginners and advanced players

The Pine Cliffs Golf Course is one of the most unique and beautiful golf courses in the country. It runs alongside the ocean, perched above the cliffs, amid lush pine trees and the vibrant blue sea. This...See More incredible 9-hole course is perfect for both beginners and advanced players.

An impeccably maintained, superb 9-hole course perched 200 feet above the crystal-clear waters of the Atlantic. Designed by architect Martin Hawtree the course preserves the site’s natural beauty while spanning 168 acres of stunning coastline. The course offers superb play and magnificent views, but the real highlight is the Par 3 sixth hole, known as the Devil’s Parlour. This thrilling hole features a 197-metre carrying shot over the cliffs, overlooking a ravine.See Less

## Pine Cliffs Golf Academy

Superbly equipped with the latest technology in teaching systems the Golf Academy is manned by a team of Internationally Qualified PGA Professionals with a combined experience of over 20 years of teaching. The Pine Cliffs Golf Academy team offers a tuition service second to none for all parts of the game. The dedicated short game practice area is specifically designed to aid the beginner, improver or experienced golfers.

The indoor video swing analysis room is equipped with the V1 swing analysis software which is ideal to analyse your faults and get back on the right track quickly and permanently. For beginners it's the ideal venue to ingrain good habits,or correct the bad ones before it's too late!
Sharpening individual game skills such as putting, bunker play, chip shots and tee shots is one of the Golf Academy's specialties - using expressly patented and enjoyable exercises to hone the player's technique to get more fun and enjoyment from the game.

The Pine Cliffs Course Experiences

### 9 & Dine Package

Immerse yourself in the perfect combination of golf and dining with our exclusive 9&Dine Package at Pine Cliffs. This all-inclusive offer includes premium golf equipment, high-quality academy balls, and a delicious pre-selected lunch at the Clubhouse.

### Golf Shop

Visit the Pine Cliffs Golf Shop, open daily from 7:30 AM to 7:30 PM. We offer a curated selection of premium golf equipment, apparel, and accessories. The first tee time starts at 8:00 AM, with the last at 6:00 PM. Stop by before your round to find everything you need for an exceptional golfing experience.

### The Clubhouse

With an open terrace overlooking the first and nineth hole of the Pine Cliffs Golf Course, the Clubhouse is the ideal location to fuel up for a round of golf and to finish with a refreshing cocktail on the terrace. International dishes, salads, and a kid’s menu are available.

### Signature Hole

Experience the iconic Devil’s Parlour at Pine Cliffs—an unforgettable Par 3 stretching 200 metres over dramatic cliffs, with panoramic views of the Atlantic.
