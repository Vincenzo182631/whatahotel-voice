# Welcome to Pine Cliffs Ocean Suites, a Luxury Collection Resort & Spa, Algarve

## A spa resort in the Algarve infused with Portuguese spirit

Set in a privileged location overlooking one of the most beautiful beaches along the Atlantic coast, Pine Cliffs Ocean Suites invites guests into an authentic Algarve experience. With a contemporary spirit...See More and strong commitment to sustainability, our Algarve resort captures the essence of the destination while expressing a renewed vision of luxury. This award-winning Spa resort in the Algarve offers signature treatments inspired by local culture, blending relaxation and sophistication. Defined by modern architecture and vibrant contemporary style, our family resort in the Algarve is seamlessly integrated into the surrounding natural landscape, where historic gardens meet ochre cliffs crowned with pine trees. Discover the Atlantic heart of this beach resort in Algarve: a refined expression of luxury crafted for the most discerning travellers. Indulge in restorative treatments, curated dining, world-class tennis and fitness facilities, or an unforgettable sunset over the ocean.See Less

Signature Accommodations

## Serene and Sophisticated

Our signature accommodations add another level of exclusivity to the Pine Cliffs Ocean Suites experience. With incredible views over the Atlantic, ultra-spacious interiors, superior comfort, and impeccable contemporary design, our exclusive suites and apartments offer a truly elevated stay. Discover the ultimate Algarve luxury in these serene dwellings where every detail, from the subtle touches to the breathtaking views of the iconic gardens and the Atlantic, has been carefully curated to cater to discerning guests. Find balance and embrace tranquility in the generous terraces and the elegant interiors, designed to create an immersive experience where space, serenity and sophistication converge.

Exceptional Accommodation Options

## Luxury, Reinvented

Pine Cliffs Ocean Suites features 148 exceptional suites and one-, two- and three- bedroom apartments, blending the Algarve’s architectural heritage with contemporary design, to offer a contemporary take on luxury. Whether shared with family, as a couple, or while travelling solo, this beach resort in the Algarve offers an effortlessly comfortable and memorable stay, where luxury is found in the smallest details. Each unit features sleek, light-filled interiors, private balconies overlooking the resort’s historic gardens or the Atlantic, and state-of-the art amenities, complemented by a dedicated Concierge service. Interconnecting units add flexibility, making them ideal for family stays and relaxed escapes.

The Charm of the Algarve

### An Atlantic Gem

Perched above the Atlantic on dramatic ochre cliffs, Pine Cliffs Ocean Suites is just moments from Praia da Falésia, one of the most striking beaches in the Algarve, with direct access from the resort via a private lift. Any time of the year, enjoy the breathtaking and soul-stirring views of the sea at sunset. Down on the beach, feel the golden sand beneath your feet, and marvel at the deep blue waters...See More and sweeping coastline. Immerse yourself in the cool mornings and blissful afternoons of the Algarve, with rose-tinted skies and shifting shades of blue.See Less

Family and Kids

## A Premier Family Destination

Our iconic clifftop retreat has been repeatedly recognized as Best Family Luxury Resort, offering a privileged setting in which to create lasting memories. Expansive gardens, nine swimming pools and a pristine beach create the perfect setting play and unwind, embracing the Atlantic way of life. With 72 hectares of beautifully landscaped grounds, 32 years of history, and endless activities, this iconic beach resort in the Algarve is where generations come together to create memories that last a lifetime.

Explore

## Endless Adventure

Children will adore Porto Pirata. Nestled in the heart of our resort  and surrounded by beautiful trees, it is the largest and most captivating playground in the Algarve. Fresh air and endless activities await children of all ages in a 7000 sq meters adventure zone with bouncy castles, a basketball court, two kids-only swimming pools, a mini golf course and two life-size pirate ships designed to spark their imagination and creativity.

Tennis and Padel

### Matchpoint

Experience the energy of tennis and padel matches at our state-of-the art facilities, framed by the resort’s beautiful natural scenery. At the Annabel Croft Tennis Academy, guests can choose from four world-class tennis courts (two clay and two hard courts) and four state-of-the-art padel courts, complemented by expert coaching and tailored programs. Play, improve your technique and enjoy each match in a unique and inspiring setting.
