### Find Serenity

Embark on a journey of relaxation and balance at our award-winning Serenity Spa. Discover our signature treatments inspired by local culture and the wisdom traditions of the world, in an idyllic setting, just steps from the Ocean.

An immersive environment designed for complete relaxation, featuring a hydrotherapy pool, outdoor jacuzzi, sensory showers and an ice source. Guests staying at Ocean Suites enjoy complimentary access to the Spa's fabulous Thermal Oasis, where they can pamper themselves with soothing treatments.
