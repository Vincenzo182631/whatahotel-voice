## Thermal Oasis

The luxurious Thermal Oasis at Serenity Spa provides the perfect setting for relaxation and rejuvenation. Guests can enjoy a range of exclusive facilities, including the steam room, Kneipp pool, hydrotherapy pool, herbal sauna, Himalayan salt sauna, ice fountain, indoor and outdoor relaxation areas, experience showers, and outdoor jacuzzi – all thoughtfully designed to elevate your wellness journey. Access to the Thermal Oasis is complimentary on the day of your treatment when booking any of our bespoke experiences. For more informations and bookings please contact us.

Move Well

## Active by Serenity

The Health Clubs at Pine Cliffs Resort are equipped with state-of-the-art Technogym strength and cardio equipment, offering a comprehensive fitness experience.
The hotel’s Health Club features a gym, jacuzzi, indoor pool for swimming and aquatic exercises, sauna and steam room. Access to the gym is available 24/7, while the pool, jacuzzi, sauna, and steam room are open daily from 7:00 am to 8:00 pm. Swimming caps are mandatory in the indoor pool. At Casa Pine Cliffs, the Health Club operates seasonally and includes a fitness space, sauna, and steam room, providing a serene environment for wellness. In our dedicated Active Studio, guests can enjoy group classes and Pilates Reformer sessions. There are also Personal Training sessions and an outdoor Bootcamp circuit for a complete fitness journey. Please note that access to the gyms, sauna, steam room, and jacuzzi is restricted to guests aged 16 and above. For more informations and bookings please contact us.
