## Discover The St. Regis Spa

The St. Regis Spa is a sanctuary where time-honored traditions seamlessly blend with contemporary sophistication. Here, amidst the tranquil embrace of pristine beachfront surroundings, discerning guests embark on a transformative journey of the senses. Our expert therapists curate bespoke experiences that transcend the ordinary, ensuring that every moment is a harmonious symphony of relaxation, rejuvenation, and the pursuit of exquisite serenity

Learn More

### Tennis Courts

We are pleased to provide guests with two outdoor, lighted, hard tennis courts as well as complimentary rackets and balls, cold water, and towels. Tennis clinics and hitting partners are available upon request for a supplementary fee.

Pools

Adult Pool

Reserved exclusively for adults, this picturesque pool unites cabanas and Jacuzzis with a spectacular view of the Arabian Gulf. Additionally, food and beverage and St. Regis Butler service ensure that complete relaxation is at guests’ fingertips.

Outdoor Lap Pool

Relax by one of three outdoor pools on our private beach with the St. Regis Pool and Beach Pass. Spend your day on our pristine beach, so sublime that Hawksbill Turtles find refuge in the dunes and dolphins play in the surf along the shore.

Indoor Lap Pool

Maintain your workout routine in our indoor lap pool or sign up for our fun-filled swim program. Catering to all ages, it is best way to take to the water, with private classes also available.

Children's Pool

Kids will enjoy splashing around in our outdoor children's pool, complete with waterfalls and toys.

See More

### Discover Saadiyat Island

Just seven minutes from downtown Abu Dhabi and 20 minutes from Abu Dhabi International Airport, the 27 square kilometre Saadiyat Island is undergoing a remarkable transformation into a world class leisure, residential, business and cultural hub of global proportions, housing the world's largest single concentration of premier cultural assets. These will include the Zayed National Museum, Louvre Abu Dhabi and Guggenheim Abu Dhabi - all designed by Pritzker prize winners.

Learn More

Family Experiences

### Falcon World Tour

The award-winning World Falcon Tour provides a truly unforgettable experience. Meet with expert veterinarians and veterinary technicians, see how the falcons are cared for, and learn more about captive-bred falcons, their health, and falconry traditions.

Learn More

### Mangrove Kayaking

Experience a unique marine eco-system with spectacular mangrove scenery on a guided 90-minute kayaking adventure. Encounter a variety of wild life like egrets, herons, Crab Plovers, Sandpipers, cormorants, and pink flamingos while paddling through the mangroves.

Learn More

### Ferrari World Abu Dhabi

Families are invited to explore the world’s largest indoor theme park, Ferrari World Abu Dhabi. Enjoy over 20 thrill rides including the world's fastest roller coaster and advanced simulators, as well as a variety of restaurants, shopping, and entertainment.

Learn More

### Warner Bros. World Abu Dhabi

Discover six immersive lands inspired by legendary Warner Bros. characters, with rides, shows and attractions for everyone.

Learn More

### Yas Waterworld

Feel the rush of a looping freefall waterslide, surf the waves of excitement, and hang on the Middle East's longest suspended rollercoaster. With more than 40 exhilarating rides, slides, and attractions, we bring you the world's leading water park right here in Abu Dhabi.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Treadmills, cross-trainers, bicycles, cardio equipment, and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi has multiple pools on-site for hotel guests:

Indoor Lap Pool (Indoor)Outdoor Lap Pool (Outdoor)Adult Pool (Outdoor)

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi have an on-site spa?

Yes, The Spa at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort is located on-site at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi. Click here to book a service now.

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi offer golf course access nearby or at the hotel?

Yes, hotel guests have access to Saadiyat Beach Golf Club.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi?

Heritage Village, Abu Dhabi Corniche, Louvre Abu Dhabi, Yas Mall, Galleria Mall and more are nearby The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi.
