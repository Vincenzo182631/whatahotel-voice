## Accommodations

Setting the stage for an engaging, uncompromising stay on Saadiyat Island in Abu Dhabi, each of our luxury hotel rooms and grand suites combines Mediterranean-influenced architecture with expansive views of the glittering Arabian Gulf or breathtaking resort from private balconies with chaise lounges. Indoors, smooth Calcutta marble floors and natural materials—ranging from chisel-edged stone and petrified wood to sustainable teak, woven cane and resin rattan—complement cool leather and glass, while rich turquoise, red and coral accents punctuate a beach-inspired sand, brown and white palette. Modern, clean-lined hotel furnishings create inviting spaces to utilize the room's state-of-the-art technology, including a 42-inch flat screen television with cable channels, iPod docking station and complementary Wi-Fi, as ceiling fans combine with air conditioning to create a soothing, rejuvenating ambiance.

Room and Facilities Enhancement Notice

The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort is enhancing its guest rooms and suites in phases until the end of 2027. The Sandcastle Kids’ Club is under refresh until 31 October 2026, with a temporary play area available for younger guests. All other resort facilities remain operational.

St. Regis Butler Service

St. Regis Butler Service ensures that unique preferences are understood and addressed flawlessly at our St. Regis hotels around the world.

Signature Services:

Beverage Service:

Upon arrival, allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast.

Unpacking & Packing Services:

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can simply relax and enjoy. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the Butler Service Desk to dispatch a butler to assist with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

Garment Pressing:

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This gracious service includes pressing of two garments per person, per day.

eButler:

During your stay, whether you are inside or outside the hotel, St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via email. Simply send your request and allow the Butler Service Desk to assist.

For all other wishes or requests, St. Regis Butler Service is pleased to assist.
