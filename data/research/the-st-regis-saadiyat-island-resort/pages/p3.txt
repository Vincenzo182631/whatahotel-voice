# The Most Exclusive Restaurants on Saadiyat Island

## Dining

Discover an exquisite culinary journey at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, where six distinctive restaurants and bars celebrate global flavors in breathtaking island settings. Start your day with one of the best breakfast buffets in Abu Dhabi at Oléa, our Mediterranean-inspired all-day dining venue. Savor the vibrant aromas of Southeast Asian cuisine at Sontaya, nestled among floating pavilions with magical views. For a relaxed afternoon, indulge in artisanal pastries and afternoon tea at Café Ginori Saadiyat Island, served in elegant Ginori 1735 porcelain. Then, as the sun sets, embrace the coastal rhythm at Buddha-Bar Beach Abu Dhabi —where Pacific Rim flavors, exotic cocktails, and electrifying beats set the stage for unforgettable evenings. Whether it’s casual dining or a refined culinary experience, every meal is crafted to satisfy every palate.

## Ginori Afternoon Tea

Inspired by the timeless St. Regis ritual, Ginori Afternoon Tea is a celebration of connection, elegance, and gracious hospitality. Served on Ginori's iconic porcelain, this refined experience features an artful selection of delicate pastries, savoury finger sandwiches, and sweet delights, paired with premium selection of teas and expertly brewed coffee. Perfect for a late afternoon indulgence or catching up with friends, it's a serene moment to savour on the shores of Saadiyat Island.

The Manhattan Lounge

## Arabian Snapper

The legendary Bloody Mary, perfected in 1934 at The St. Regis New York’s King Cole Bar, has become a signature of the brand, with each hotel offering its own twist—here, it’s the Arabian Snapper, crafted with zaatar, a spice native to the region. Discover this storied ritual at The Manhattan Lounge.
