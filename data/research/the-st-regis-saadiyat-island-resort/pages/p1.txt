# Welcome to The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi

## Stay at one of Abu Dhabi's finest 5-star beach resorts

Overlooking a pristine beach, the cerulean Arabian Gulf and the celebrated Saadiyat Beach Golf Club, our 5-star destination resort presents a serene escape and captivating wildlife just minutes from downtown Abu Dhabi and the Saadiyat Cultural District. Boasting the island's preeminent address, we invite resort guests to rejuvenate at The St. Regis Spa, energize in our luxury indoor lap pool or three outdoor pools, or bask on our private Abu Dhabi beach before dining seaside at one of six destination restaurants and bars. Mediterranean-inspired architecture unites with contemporary interiors and tactile natural materials in our refined hotel rooms and suites, which each boast a private balcony and signature St. Regis Butler service-available any time, day or night. Visit our luxury beach resort in Abu Dhabi for the ultimate getaway.

Room and Facilities Enhancement Notice

The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort is enhancing its guest rooms and suites in phases until the end of 2027. The Sandcastle Kids’ Club is under refresh until 31 October 2026, with a temporary play area available for younger guests. All other resort facilities remain operational.

### Saadiyat Beach

Overlooking a pristine beach, the cerulean Arabian Gulf and the celebrated Saadiyat Beach Golf Club, our destination resort presents a serene escape and captivating wildlife just minutes from downtown Abu Dhabi and the Saadiyat Cultural District.

### Golf

The Arabian Gulf's first beachfront course, the Saadiyat Beach Golf Club, harmonizes breathtaking scenery with an impressive variety of golf experiences, thanks to an innovative, unique course design by world-famous golfer Gary Player.

### Sandcastle Club

Abu Dhabi-themed, children-oriented décor greets guests in the indoor play area, which includes mats, blocks, puzzles, table tennis, and toys for all ages—along with state-of-the-art entertainment ranging from a PlayStation and Sega to Wii and Xbox.

### Squash

Additional fitness options come via two complimentary indoor squash courts. Available to in-house guests of all ages, the squash courts include balls, rackets, and towels—plus a playing partner, if needed.

### Unique Meeting & Event Space

The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi offers a truly unique setting for gatherings of all kinds in the finest island location.

### Exceptional Venues

From the grandeur of the 3,000 sqm Regal Ballroom—one of the largest ballrooms in Abu Dhabi—and its expansive outdoor terrace to 13 versatile meeting spaces, a scenic beachside lawn, and the intimate rooftop at Lemoni Garden, every setting is designed for unforgettable gatherings. Whether hosting a lavish wedding, corporate event, or private soirée, each space offers a unique backdrop for a truly exceptional experience.

Family Amenities & Services

While in residence, discover an array of thoughtful services and amenities designed especially for children and families. Allow our dedicated team to provide a flawless experience and every convenience a family needs to enjoy cherished time together.

###### Nanny & Babysitting

Our Concierge team maintains a list of babysitters who are able to care for your children in the convenience of your guest room. Please contact us for assistance with reserving nanny and/or babysitting services prior to your stay. If needed during your stay at the hotel, the concierge will be pleased to assist.

###### Necessities & Provisions

Our resort is supplied with a variety of family necessities such as wipes, diapers, cribs, and rollaway beds. Allow St. Regis Butler Service to assist you with preparing these items for you during your stay or before your arrival at the resort. Please contact the eButler for assistance.

Concierge Desk

Our multilingual concierge staff is always on hand to ensure that each stay exceeds expectations. Head Concierge is a certified destination expert proudly wearing the "Les Clefs d’Or," the gilt insignia of the celebrated international concierge society, and eager to reveal the secret treasures of Abu Dhabi.

Prior to guests’ arrival and throughout their stay, our team is pleased to assist with transportation services, ranging from airline reservations and confirmations to airport transfer and car rental. Within the hotel, they are also on hand to recommend a visit to the area's celebrated beaches and cultural sites, as well as arrange tickets, nearby excursions, and more.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi is Zayed International Airport (AUH). AUH is located approximately 30.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Saadiyat Island Resort, Abu Dhabi has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
