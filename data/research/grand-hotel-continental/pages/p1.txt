## Grand Hotel Continental \| Tirrenia \| Italy

Largo Belvedere 26, 56018 Tirrenia, Italy

### ➤ Grand Hotel Continental \| Pisa

Set on Tirrenia’s seafront, the Grand Hotel Continental is only 15 km from the Leaning Tower and 10 km from Pisa Airport. It offers free Wi-Fi, an Olympic-sized pool and a free private beach. Paid private parking is available at the property upon reservation.

Rooms at the Hotel Continental are decorated in a Mediterranean style with light-wood furniture. Each comes with air conditioning and satellite TV, and some rooms feature a balcony with Tyrrhenian-Sea views.

A daily buffet breakfast is provided. The restaurant serves both international and Tuscan dishes.

The hotel's beach includes free deckchairs and parasols. Fitness lovers will appreciate the hotel’s gym.

The A12 Autostrada Azzurra is 9 km from the hotel. Livorno, where ferries depart for Palermo and Cagliari, is a 15-minute drive away.

#### Brigitta

7,0Good

Purpose of trip: Leisure trip

What was the highlight of Grand Hotel Continental

Good

The hotel has two wings, an older one, where the furniture and bathroom are older, but the terraces are covered and separated by a wall. The beds are comfortable, the mattresses are harder, on which even heavier people can sleep comfortably. The bathroom is large and equipped with a bidet, but the 80 cm shower is unreasonably small. The towels are changed every day, we did not experience cleaning on a daily basis, hand wash, shower gel, shampoo and body lotion are also available and they smell good. The air conditioning cools well, but in the old department...it's not very clean, unfortunately. If you approach the staff kindly, they try to help you with anything just as kindly. The breakfast is very varied, with quality cheeses, cold meats, divine baked goods and cookies, which lasts for about 5-7 days, but since it is always the same, it is not varied enough for 10 days. Sunbeds are free by the pool, the pool took everything for us, we loved it. You have to walk a little bit to the part of the beach belonging to the hotel. The sun loungers on the beach are free, but you have to pay for the sun lounger there.
