# The only 5-star luxury hotel in the heart of Siena

Once a private palazzo, Grand Hotel Continental is the **only 5-star hotel** in the **historical centre of Siena**, located just steps from Piazza del Campo and the Duomo. It is housed in a magnificient **16th-century building** boasting cotto floors, frescoed ceilings and original chandeliers. Highlights include an opulent ballroom aptly named the **Salone delle Feste** and an impressive underground **wine cellar** where wine tastings are offered daily.

The hotel is the **perfect base** to explore **Tuscany**, its glorious Renaissance masterpieces, gently rolling hills and cutting-edge wine cellars. In August and July, Grand Hotel Continental offers signature experiences to enjoy **Il Palio**, the famed historic horse race run each year around the edges of Piazza del Campo.

Craft Experiences

Visiting Siena is truly a journey into the Middle Ages and its ancient crafts. In the small workshops that dot the well-preserved historic center, still protected by its imposing walls, you can find everything from yellow marble to ceramics, along with a variety of handcrafted items tied to the Palio and its districts.
