### A Hyatt Globalist Review

A Hyatt Globalist Review
Park Hyatt was the highlight of our trip. We never stayed at a Park Hyatt before and were simply blown away by the service and attention to detail. The Team at Park Hyatt did an excellent job with my stay. This made our Cambodia stay very comfortable. Service was also swift when needed. I had an absolutely amazing 5 day stay with pure luxury in every area. The attention to detail was pretty meticulate.
We were celebrating our Anniversary, and team did an excellent job with our rooms. As a Globalist, The checkin process was very seamless, and they even offered us complimentary welcome drinks- beer etc. I genuinely appreciated the upgrade to suite and also upgrade for the king deluxe room for my dad. The Decorations in the room were very lovely and thoroughly loved the refreshments offered in the room including a delicious cake and a Bayon themed chocolate
The complimentary City Tour Tuk Tuk ride is a must do and there is an option to extend it for the entire hour as well. Recommend Mr Vireak -Number 6
The Apsara show dinner was another highlight as the performances were really breathtaking. Me and my family really enjoyed this.
I like the location of the Park as well as it fell centered around everything. Pub street was only couple of minutes away along with other attractions
I highly recommend the Mango IPA they have on tap here. It was pretty unique.
Pool was another highlight as it was an oasis in a very busy part of city. I highly recommend the steam room as well. It was a very unique Steam Pod
Hotel had many activities, but we didn’t get a chance to do them. Also recommend checking Relax Spa near the hotel
Dining Highlights:
•Breakfast: The Spread was very huge. They even had special veg offerings which my parents really appreciated. The Complimentary Coconut water was a huge hit along with the Complimentary massage offered after breakfast.
We really appreciated the Local Khmer cusine . My fav were Num Banchok, Bay Moan and Khor Ko. The Khmer coffee both Hot and Cold were real treats as well.

• Apsara Dinner: One of the biggest highlights of the trip. Really Amazing performance . we had some amazing drinks with some of the best presenattions I have ever seen and some amazing food as well. This is a must do on your stay.
Staff Recognition
Mouyee, Mouet ,Kin, Ravi and Pich really helped us throughout our stay. Mouyee is the sweetest person we have ever met.

Our time at Park Hyatt Siem Reap was nothing short of extraordinary. It was one of those rare hotel experiences that stays with you long after you’ve left.
Firstly, the location is absolutely perfect. Situated right in the heart of Siem Reap, it offers immediate access to the vibrant markets, charming cafés and a short drive to Angkor Wat. Despite its city center location, the hotel feels like an oasis of calm away from the hustle and bustle.
What truly sets this property apart, is the incredible, genuine hospitality and deeply personalized service. From the moment we arrived, every interaction felt warm, thoughtful, and authentic. The team around General Manager Himanshu Kapoor has mastered the art of anticipating needs while making you feel completely at home.
A special mention must go to a few standout team members who made our stay unforgettable:
Sam, whose welcoming presence set the tone for a flawless experience.
Fung, who went above and beyond with genuine care and thoughtful touches throughout our stay.

It’s rare to encounter a team so committed to excellence while maintaining such warmth and authenticity. Their passion shines through in every moment.
From the elegant design and tranquil ambiance to the impeccable service and exceptional dining, Park Hyatt Siem Reap delivers on every level. I cannot recommend it highly enough and will absolutely be returning. A true gem in Siem Reap. Thank you for an unforgettable stay.
