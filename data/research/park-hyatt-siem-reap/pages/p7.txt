Why book?
A stylish urban oasis, Park Hyatt Siem Reap is centrally located in the heart of the city, making it an ideal jumping-off point for anyone looking to kick their feet up after a long day exploring nearby Angkor Wat.
Set the scene
In the heart of Siem Reap, just between the Royal Gardens and the Old Market, Park Hyatt Siem Reap has long been the city hotel of choice for travelers en route to Angkor Wat. The hotel offers a serene retreat from the hustle and bustle, yet it's right in the fold. Lush grounds, multiple saltwater swimming pools, a serene spa, wonderful on-property dining, and spacious guestrooms and suites make this an all-around great pick for travelers seeking a resort-style hotel that blends playful design with Western comfort and plenty of amenities.
The backstory
The Park Hyatt was originally known as Hotel de la Paix. Considered one of Southeast Asia’s most well-respected design hotels when it opened in 2006, the boutique hotel introduced a bold, Khmer-inspired modernism that helped transform Cambodia's luxury hotel scene into what it is today.
Following a major multi-year renovation, the hotel reopened as Park Hyatt Siem Reap in 2013, marking the brand's first foray in Southeast Asia. Rather than abandoning its roots, the transformation preserved the integrity of the hotel's vision while adding modern touches and amenities to the rooms and suites. Today, the Park Hyatt continues to blend Khmer craftsmanship and Art Deco decor in its public spaces, with modern-meets-minimalist guestroom design. Its central location in the heart of the city further cements its appeal to first-time travelers and World of Hyatt members, while offering a luxurious home base for exploring Siem Reap's markets, galleries, gardens, bars, and cafés on foot.
The rooms
There are 104 rooms and suites that blend Western comfort with a modern Southeast Asian design sensibility. Rooms come furnished in dark woods, with Khmer touches woven into the textiles and finishes. For a city hotel, the rooms are extremely generous in terms of size. Each guest room has a built-in seating area or daybed, and the floor-to-ceiling windows allow plenty of natural light and views of either the inner courtyards or the city. Word to the wise, book a room facing the courtyard for a quieter stay, as city view rooms tend to come with city view noises.
Bathrooms are a highlight here, thanks to their generous size and deep soaking tubs and walk-in rain showers. Ideal for families or anyone looking to splash out a bit, or for those eligible for a World of Hyatt upgrade, premier suites come with a private plunge pool.
Food and drink
There are three dining outlets on the property, including The Dining Room, The Living Room, and Glasshouse Deli. You’ll find a generous daily breakfast buffet that includes a mix of Western and Asian influences in The Dining Room. The Living Room is a great place to sit and experience the hotel’s Khemer signature menu, cocktails, or afternoon tea. Glasshouse Deli, meanwhile, is your go-to spot for coffee and snacks.
The spa
The are certainly no shortage of spas and foot massage spots in and around Siem Reap, but the hotel’s on-property spa is easily one of the best in town. Offering an array of Khemer-inspired rituals, massage treatments, and body scrubs, the Traditional Khemer treatment blends energy healing with a targeted deep-tissue pressure-point massage, the perfect antidote after a day of exploring in and around the city.
The neighborhood/area
One of the biggest draws of this hotel is its walkability. Located off a busy main road in the heart of the city, most of the city’s main attractions are either a short walk or a tuk-tuk ride away. Located near Pub Street, the epicenter of nightlife, and less than 15-minutes from Angkor Wat, everything is within reach here.
The service
The service here is top-notch, and the hotel offers a variety of Signature Experiences, from cooking classes to visiting a local market with the hotel’s executive chef.
For families
This is an excellent option for families. With the spacious rooms, multiple pools, and central location, families will find the ease and convenience of staying here a big draw.
Accessibility
There are elevators and ramps in place, as well as ADA-compliant guest rooms.
Anything left to mention?
While the design remains elegant and well-appointed, some signs of wear are beginning to appear, and the property would benefit from a light refresh in the near future. Even so, the rooms remain an appealing choice for travelers who prioritize space. Just make sure to book a room facing the inner courtyard and not a city view on a lower floor, which tends to be noisier.
