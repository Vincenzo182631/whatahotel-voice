# Luxury 5-Star Hotel Near Angkor Wat l Park Hyatt Siem Reap

## A Luxury Retreat Awaits at Our Award-Winning Hotel in Siem Reap

Park Hyatt Siem Reap is a reimagined heritage icon and hotel in the heart of the city, an Oasis of Enrichment where Khmer artistry meets refined Art Deco elegance. Formerly known as Hotel De La Paix and redesigned by award-winning architect Bill Bensley, the hotel draws inspiration from Cambodia’s cultural legacy and the timeless beauty of Angkor Wat, offering a luxury arrival point for discovering the destination.​ ​Located near Angkor Wat and the UNESCO-listed Angkor Archaeological Park, this 5-star luxury hotel in Siem Reap offers a sophisticated base for exploring Cambodia’s living heritage. From curated temple journeys near Angkor Wat and refined Khmer-inspired dining to restorative spa rituals and immersive experiences, every luxury stay invites a deeper connection to Siem Reap and its storied surroundings.​​ Set close to Pub Street, the night bazaars, and the vibrant city center, Park Hyatt Siem Reap brings together destination discovery, quiet elegance, and intuitive hospitality. For travelers seeking a luxury hotel near Angkor Wat, the hotel offers a refined city retreat in Siem Reap, shaped by culture, comfort, and the enriching spirit.

Curated Experiences

Beyond the familiar paths of Siem Reap, our guests step into quiet history alongside our expert specialist, discovering hidden temples and stories seldom told. Time slows as stone warmed by centuries reveals its secrets, and curiosity deepens into understanding. More than a visit, this shared journey is an intimate dialogue with the past, where learning becomes meaningful and connection feels personal.

Oasis of Enrichment

Beneath the ancient banyan tree, time slows. At the heart of our tranquil courtyard, nature, history, and quiet reflection come together—an oasis where guests unwind after a bustling day, allowing moments of stillness to enrich every stay.

Immersive Dining Experiences

Dining at Park Hyatt Siem Reap is an invitation to experience culture through flavor, setting, and storytelling. Each venue offers more than a meal—it is a thoughtfully curated moment shaped by local heritage and contemporary refinement. From intimate culinary journeys to evocative evenings such as Apsara Dinner at The Park, where Khmer cuisine meets classical dance under the open sky, every experience is designed to engage the senses and create meaningful connections to place.

Siem Reap Exploration

Explore Siem Reap through thoughtfully curated journeys designed to deepen connection with the destination. From iconic temples to lesser-known paths, each experience is guided by cultural insiders who bring history, stories, and local life into focus—inviting guests to discover Siem Reap beyond the expected.

Swimming Pools

The lush and tranquil lap pool on the ground floor is shaded by parasols and cabanas. The free-form pool on the 1st floor offers a relaxing oasis with a lagoon design, green tiles and verdant nature. Children of all ages must be accompanied by an adult.

Park Hyatt Daily Rituals

From sunrise to evening’s gentle descent, the Courtyard sets the rhythm of the day at Park Hyatt Siem Reap, with daily rituals thoughtfully curated to create meaningful and unforgettable moments.

Begin your morning with our signature breakfast, enjoyed amid lush greenery while exploring cultural crafting stations that celebrate local flavors and traditions. As dusk approaches, the Courtyard becomes a serene setting for the Candle of Gratitude Ceremony, inviting guests to pause, reflect, and connect with the moment.

Evenings unfold with our Apsara Dinner, where Khmer culinary artistry and graceful dance performances come together beneath the Banyan tree, softly illuminated by candlelight.

Cultural Immersion

Thoughtfully curated experiences invite guests to engage with living traditions, local stories, and centuries-old heritage—guided by cultural insiders who bring depth and context to every encounter. Each journey is tailored to suit individual interests, allowing guests to explore at their own pace and connect with the aspects of culture that resonate most personally.

The Living Room

A refined blend of modern luxury and Khmer heritage, The Living Room offers timeless décor, curated cuisine, and handcrafted cocktails with quiet sophistication. Discover our signature Khmer Journey of Flavors, presenting authentic regional specialties from 25 Provinces. From leisurely lunches and themed afternoon tea to evening cocktails and gourmet dinners, each experience celebrates Cambodia’s culinary story through local ingredients and thoughtful craftsmanship.

Elevated Suite Experience

An elevated stay begins with space to breathe. Thoughtfully designed suites blend Art Deco–Khmer elegance with modern comfort, creating a tranquil sanctuary where every detail is curated for rest, privacy, and enrichment. A considered selection of suites—including distinctive signature and specialty suites—offers guests the freedom to choose a stay that best suits their needs, whether for extended relaxation, celebration, or a deeper sense of retreat.

Le Petit Chef

Exclusively at Park Hyatt Siem Reap, Le Petit Chef brings a playful yet refined dining experience to life. Through immersive visual storytelling and a carefully curated menu, guests are taken on a culinary journey where imagination meets gastronomy—creating a one-of-a-kind evening unlike any other in the destination.​

The Living Room Bar

Blending Khmer character with Western elegance, The Living Room Bar reflects relaxed sophistication. Bill Bensley’s signature design, bold colors, sculptural accents, and refined detailing, creates an inviting setting to pause, connect, and unwind, complemented by Khmer‑inspired cocktail creations crafted with local ingredients and contemporary finesse.

Temple Dinner

Events at Park Hyatt Siem Reap are thoughtfully curated to create meaningful moments. From elegant celebrations to intimate gatherings, each occasion is shaped by distinctive settings, attentive service, and bespoke catering that reflects both the event’s character and the spirit of the destination. Among these signature experiences, the Temple Dinner at Kravan Temple offers an exclusive, once‑in‑a‑lifetime evening within the sacred Angkor grounds. Under the open sky, guests enjoy chef‑curated cuisine and warm Cambodian hospitality, framed by the grandeur of a twelfth‑century temple.
