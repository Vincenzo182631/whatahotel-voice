## Khmer Journey of Flavors

The Living Room’s signature Dining Experience.
Khmer Journey of Flavors invites guests on a curated culinary exploration across Cambodia’s 25 provinces, where each dish reflects the land, rivers, and traditions that shape Khmer cuisine—from Battambang’s rice fields and Kampot’s pepper farms to the abundance of the Tonlé Sap and the tropical orchards of Siem Reap. Thoughtfully crafted by Executive Sous Chef Kimsan, the experience elevates countryside flavors through refined technique, drawing on family kitchens, communal gatherings, and provincial culinary heritage.

## The Dining Room

Nestled within An Oasis of Enrichment, The Dining Room blends Art Deco elements with Khmer ceramic tableware, bespoke furnishings, plush fabrics, and polished marble floors. Adorned with curated artwork, the space exudes refined style and timeless charm, designed by the award-winning Bill Bensley.

As one of Siem Reap’s culinary gems, The Dining Room offers an authentic taste of Khmer heritage and food culture. Whether enjoying an intimate dinner or hosting an event, the experience is shaped by the expertise of Chef Kimsan and Chef Sugizaki.

## Seasonal Experience: Rice Field Sunset Experience

Beyond the ancient temples and busy streets of Siem Reap lies a quieter Cambodia, shaped by open skies, golden horizons, and generations of rural life. Available seasonally from November to March, our Rice Field Sunset experience invites guests on a refined countryside journey, combining Park Hyatt Siem Reap’s signature Luxurious Ride with an immersive rice field encounter. This curated expedition reveals hidden temples, the rural village of Preah Dak, and the rhythms of agricultural life.
