# San Juan Restaurants

### A Taste of Puerto Rico

## Culinary Delights

Discover the flavors of Puerto Rico with The Royal Sonesta San Juan’s selection of exceptional on-site restaurants. From the vibrant atmosphere of Karaya to the oceanfront bliss of Ocean Breeze, the resort houses some of Isla Verde’s top-rated bars and dining options.

## Ocean Breeze

Escape to Ocean Breeze, Isla Verde’s only beachfront dining destination. Enjoy Mediterranean-inspired cuisine and soak up the idyllic ambiance while savoring lunch or dinner with stunning ocean views.

**Hours (Daily):**

- **Lunch**: 11:00 AM – 4:30 PM
- **Dinner:** 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM

View Lunch Menu View Dinner Menu

## Aleli

Aleli offers indoor and outdoor seating overlooking our sparkling lagoon pool, offering a relaxed and breezy setting for a delightful buffet or a la carte breakfast.

**Breakfast Hours:**

- **Mon–Fri:** 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM
- **Sat, Sun & Holidays:** 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Make a Reservation View Breakfast Menu

## Akua Pool Bar

Experience the epitome of relaxation at Akua, our swim-up pool bar. Indulge in refreshing cocktails, savor light bites, and soak up the sun while you enjoy your time in the pool.

**Hours (Daily):**

- **Bar:** 11:00 AM – 6:30 PM
- Kitchen closes at 6:00 PM daily

View Dining Menu

## Karaya Lobby Bar & Terrace

Discover a captivating ambiance at Karaya, the resort’s main bar. Enjoy locally inspired tapas, handcrafted cocktails, and breathtaking views of the lagoon pool amidst a backdrop of flickering fire pits.

**Hours (Daily):**

- **Lunch:** 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM
- **Dinner:** 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Kitchen closes at 11:00 PM nightly. Hours may be extended depending on business levels.

View Dinner Menu View Lunch Menu

Light snacks, grab and go, variety of local coffee.

## Sottovento Ristorante

At Sottovento Ristorante, dining is more than an experience—it’s a celebration of tradition and craftsmanship. Experience a symphony of flavors inspired by Italy’s rich culinary heritage. Indulge in handcrafted dishes, expertly paired wines, and create unforgettable moments with every bite.

**Hours:**

- Daily: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Indulge in the comfort of in-room dining. Whether you prefer a quiet meal or need to maintain your focus, our room service is at your disposal.

**Hours:**

- **Breakfast**: Daily 07:00 AM – 11:00 AM
- **Diner:** Daily 05:00 PM – 10:30 PM
