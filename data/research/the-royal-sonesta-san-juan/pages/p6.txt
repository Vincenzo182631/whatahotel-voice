## Destination Fee

Room rates are subject to a daily Resort Fee of 22% (plus applicable taxes), which includes:

- 24/7 access to fitness center
- Basic WiFi for unlimited devices
- 2 bottles of water upon arrival
- In-room coffee/tea offering
- Beachfront access including towels & beach chairs/umbrellas
- Luggage storage, boarding pass printing capability, PressReader availability (news and magazines)
- Bottled Water Re-Fill Station in the Pool Area
