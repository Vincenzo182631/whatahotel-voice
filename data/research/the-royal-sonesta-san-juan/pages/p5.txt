# Local Area

### Explore San Juan with Sonesta

## Located on Isla Verde Beach in San Juan

## Pristine Puerto Rican Beaches

Nestled in the upscale neighborhood of Isla Verde, The Royal Sonesta San Juan is ideally situated on Isla Verde Beach — where you can soak up the sun, swim in crystal-clear waters, or try your hand at water sports.

One of the top beaches in Puerto Rico, Isla Verde Beach offers calm, swimmable waters against a long stretch of golden sand beach lined with shops and restaurants.

## Explore San Juan

#### Old San Juan

A historic district, Old San Juan is a captivating blend of vintage charm and modern vibrancy. Explore the cobblestone streets of Old San Juan, marvel at centuries-old forts, and relax on pristine beaches. Located 7 miles away from the resort.

#### Museum of Contemporary Art of Puerto Rico

Discover the dynamic art scene at the Museum of Contemporary Art. Located just 4.6 miles from our resort, this cultural gem showcases a diverse collection of contemporary art, innovative exhibitions, and thought-provoking installations.

#### La Plazoleta de Isla Verde

Isla Verde at La Plazoleta is a charming plaza just steps away from the resort. Immerse yourself in the authentic culture of Puerto Rico, savor delicious local cuisine, and enjoy lively music and entertainment.

#### Castillo San Felipe del Morro

A UNESCO World Heritage Site, the majestic El Morro Fort is located 7.4 miles from our resort. Explore the ancient fortifications, wander through underground tunnels, and climb to the top for breathtaking panoramic views of the city and the Caribbean Sea.

## Guided Tours

Experience the magic of San Juan with our expert-led tours. Discover iconic landmarks, hidden gems, and fascinating stories that bring the city’s history to life.
