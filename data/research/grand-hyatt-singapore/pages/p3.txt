# Dining

Discover a world of dining at Grand Hyatt Singapore, right in the heart of Orchard Road.​ From modern European plates at Le Pristine Singapore and comforting Italian classics from Pete’s Place to rediscovering familiar local favourites at the halal-certified StraitsKitchen and flame-grilled delights by the pool at Oasis, there is something for every craving. You can also linger over afternoon tea and an evening indulgence at 10\|SCOTTS or unwind with a tipple at Martini Bar and live entertainment at BRIX. Before you leave, stop by The Shop for freshly baked pastries, breads and sweet treats to take home.​ Click to discover more or call us at +65 6732 1234 for reservations.​

## Pete's Place

An icon since 1973 for classic Italian cuisine, the refreshed Pete’s Place is the place to visit for a heartwarming meal with your family and friends. Tenderly cultivated through the years, feast on your favourite Italian fare featuring signatures like the Conchiglie Cioppino and Tiramisu alongside an exuberant selection of pizzas, freshly-baked bread and others amidst the rustic charm of a reimagined trattoria.

## StraitsKitchen

Uniquely Singaporean, savour authentic and refined local dishes in a vibrant marketplace setting. Celebrating Singapore’s diverse food culture, the halal-certified buffet is Muslim-friendly and enjoyed by guests of all backgrounds. Savour all-time favourites such as Popiah, Chilli Crab, Butter Chicken, Satay and Nyonya Kuehs, alongside homemade local beverages including Teh Tarik.

## BRIX

Revel in BRIX, one of the liveliest underground nightspots in Singapore. The bar features three different sections – Main Bar, Wine Bar and Whisky Bar. Daily ‘live’ music is the highlight of BRIX. ## 10\|Scotts

10\|SCOTTS brings back relaxed elegance with a Mediterranean touch. Indulge in an elegant tasting journey of curated sweets and savouries like Crab Tartlet, Seafood Vol-au-vent and more, thoughtfully paired with fine teas from Monogram. Whether it’s a relaxed afternoon over artisanal teas and tapas, or a long-overdue lunch with friends, 10\|SCOTTS offers a warm and stylish setting designed for connection and celebrations in the heart of Orchard Road.
