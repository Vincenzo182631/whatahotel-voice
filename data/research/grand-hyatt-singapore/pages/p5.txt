### Great Hotel, Breakdown in Customer Service (Perhaps due to Race Week)

The hotel and facilities were beautiful but our experience was dampened by a breakdown in customer service, perhaps attributable to race week but nonetheless we expected more for a club booking at a Grand Hyatt:
1\. Our first room had a musty smell on entry, so we had to request a new room. There was no acknowledgement or apology from the front desk staff but they granted another room which we were grateful for
2\. After the room change, our luggage was misplaced. I had to call from the room to check, then since it was taking so long come down to the lobby to ask again, and finally concierge called my room to ask for help in identifying our bags. It took 30–45 minutes to reach our room. This was an inconvenience as I was unable to go to the gym before an appointment. Again, no apology from the staff.
3\. When winding down to watch our favorite shows, I found the Chromecast on the TV wasn't working. When I called concierge, they arranged for a technician had to come and fix the issue. This wasn’t a big deal but another hindrance. Again, no apology from concierge, but I appreciate the technician fixing the issue.
4\. On check in, the check in attendant told us with our club booking we could choose between breakfast at the club and Strait’s kitchen. When we arrived at Strait’s kitchen we were told we had to pay another $45 dollars per person even after we told them the check in attendant told us different. We escalated this to club concierge and they didn’t immediately make it right, which upset us. They just told us it was not included and I had to express my frustration at being told the wrong thing before the concierge attendant offered the breakfast as a way to make it right. Again, no apology!
5\. Lastly on check out and final bag retrieval we had to help identify the bags again. At least this time we appreciate the bald gentlemen was very apologetic and kind. This went a long way.

I had to email first day issues to the hotel customer service email. The in concierge assistant manager reached out to me the next day right before checkout to apologize which I appreciated and ask what she could do to make it right. But at that point we were already about to check out so I asked for a points credit and she offered very modest one. Overall I was grateful for the apology but I felt shortchanged by the points given the magnitude of the service breakdown we experienced.

The room itself was great. But for a Club-level booking at a Grand Hyatt, this was not at all the experience we expected.

In fact our customer service at the Standard Singapore has been far superior.

### Beautiful hotel in the heart of Singapore

Grand Hyatt Singapore is a beautiful, modern hotel in an excellent central location, making it convenient for shopping, dining, and exploring the city. The rooms are comfortable, well-appointed, and the property is maintained to a very high standard.

That said, the hotel felt extremely busy throughout our stay. It seemed to cater primarily to business travelers, which gave it a more corporate atmosphere, then in the late hours it was quite obvious that a lot of the business men were entertaining certain local “hostesses „
While the service was professional and efficient, we felt it lacked the warmth and personal touch that transforms a good stay into a truly memorable one.

For our next visit to Singapore, we will likely look at staying in the Marina Bay area, where many of the city’s iconic attractions, entertainment, and vibrant nightlife are located. Sentosa Island is also very appealing, with its beaches, resorts, and wide variety of activities, making it another destination we would love to experience.

Overall, Grand Hyatt Singapore is an excellent hotel with a prime location, especially for business travelers. While it may not have been the perfect fit for our style of vacation, we still enjoyed our stay and would certainly consider returning if the prices remains competitive.
