## MODERN LUXURY AT GRAND HYATT SINGAPORE IN ORCHARD ROAD

Welcome to your Grand Living Room and Wellness Haven. Nestled in the Orchard Road area, Singapore’s iconic shopping and entertainment district, Grand Hyatt Singapore offers a one-stop destination served by a trusted team of associates. The reimagined luxury hotel with bold, vibrant spaces will provide distinct atmospheres that blend seamlessly in both Grand Wing—a Grand Living Room where guests convene and celebrate—and Terrace Wing, an urban oasis and wellness haven steps from the bustling streets with lush landscaping and a cascading waterfall. Whether you're on a business trip, or enjoying a family vacation, you'll embrace the new and the familiar at Grand Hyatt Singapore.

## EXPLORE

Sustainability Commitment

Grand Hyatt Singapore is committed to protecting the planet for future generations by pioneering sustainable initiatives in food sourcing, food waste management, energy, water and hotel operations.

Hotel Vicinity Map

Discover exciting experiences and attractions within driving distance from the hotel, or stay active with our curated running routes.

POOLS

Discover unparalleled comfort and tranquillity amidst our outdoor aquatic facilities, nestled within serene lush surroundings. Whether you seek to relax with a refreshing swim or soothing hydrotherapy, our pools are tailored to suit your every need. Enhance your fitness by swimming laps in the semi-Olympic-sized pool or unwind with a massage in the hydro-massage saltwater pool.

GRAND CLUB

Experience the exclusive amenities of the new Grand Club on Level 1M in an intimate atmosphere, including breakfast options, all-day refreshments, and an evening cocktail experience.

DAMAI - SPA

Damai, meaning "peace" in Malay, is a reimagined wellness haven in Grand Hyatt Singapore dedicated to helping guests reconnect, recover, and reclaim their physical and mental wellbeing. Step into a tranquil sanctuary where every detail is crafted with your wellbeing in mind. From start-of-the-art facilities to therapeutic rituals backed by science and traditional remedies, every detail is designed to invite balance and renewal.

FITNESS CENTRE

Elevate your fitness routine and achieve your wellness goals at the Fitness Centre, featuring state-of-the-art equipment designed to support every workout regimen.
