## Discover New Experiences

### Herbal Cuddle

Inspired by various herbs and ancient Thai medicinal healing, DoublePool Villas offer guests a daily herbal cuddle such as herbal baths, herbal steams, and herbal infusions to enhance their vitality and wellbeing.
The herbal bath brings together fresh turmeric, lemongrass, and kaffir lime into a beautifully scented concoction perfect for a jetlag recovery bath.

### Coconut Boat

Showcasing the beautiful ancient Thai riverside living community - The Floating Market - our coconut man represents a floating vendor traveling along the waters and serving local fresh coconuts directly to your garden. Locally sourced young coconuts cut on the boat and freshly served to guests among the natural surroundings of DoulePool Villas.

### Welcome Ceremony

Welcome Ceremony
Inspired by the Thai Village Tradition of Water Offering, the villagers would place a clay water jar together with "kra buay" in front of their house to offer water to the passing by travelers. In DoublePool Villas, we use this symbolic tradition to extend our warm welcome to our guests while the water changed to be our freshly-made bale juice which is commonly well known in Thailand for its freshness and health benefits.
