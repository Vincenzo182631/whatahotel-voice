# DISCOVER THALANG

Discover our associates' home town and the community that surrounds Banyan Tree Phuket. From the heroines of Phuket to the Gold Buddha and a pioneering sustainable goat farm, learn the stories behind the community we embrace.

## ELEPHANT SANCTUARY

The Phuket Elephant Sanctuary is a final home for retired working elephants, set on 30 acres of lush tropical jungle. Observe how elephants rehabilitate into forest life after decades of abuse, and experience how incredible the largest land mammal on earth is during a day at our sanctuary. Visits to the Sanctuary include an educational video and presentation on the sanctuary and elephants, followed by a walk through the sanctuary where at the end you will be able to meet and feed an elephant

## LOCAL MARKET & TEMPLE

Kickstart your day at the local market in Pasak, wandering around the various stalls and discovering new fruits, vegetables, tastes and scents.

Enjoy breakfast at a local coffee stand. Try the Thai Iced Tea made right in front of you as well as the local snack ""Patongko"".

The next stop will be Muang Temple, where you will be able to offer a blessing to Ya Mook and Ya Jun, the Phuket Heroines, as well as learn the history of the temple and take a visit to the soldiers' training centre.

The final stop will be at the Thalang Battlefield Monument, a now peaceful location that once was the grounds for the well-known Phuket battle against Myanmar troops.

##### Plane Spotting

Uncover the allure of plane spotting in Phuket, an exciting addition to our list of Best Things to Do. This top-rated attraction offers a captivating experience, with planes gracefully landing against the backdrop of the beautiful beach, making it an ideal photo playground. For the best views, visit between December and June when planes descend over the ocean. During the low season from July to November, the wind patterns shift, altering the aircraft approach and take-off direction.

##### Sense of Plance

Your first stop will be at a local village where you will be welcomed to a local pineapple farm, followed by a sustainable goat farm. Have you ever milked a goat?

The next part of the adventure takes you on a trek through the forest where our team will teach you about local trees and herbs found in the wild as well as their uses.

Finally, you will reach Bangtao viewpoint for photos and refreshments.

Price

THB 3,500 per couple

Timing

10:00 am onwards \| 2 hr experience

##### Phra Thong Temple

The Temple is a popular attraction for both visitors and local residents. Located in the temple is the upper half of a large Buddha statue which seems to emerge from the ground.

Legend has it that during the invasion of Thalang town in 1785, the Burmese made several attempts to excavate the statue in order to take it back to Burma.

Each time they dug into the ground, swarms of hornets would swoop on them and they had to give up in the end. Later, the visible part of the statue was covered with gold by the villagers and has remained as such until today.

The statue is named Luangpho Phra Phut (""Buddha that emerges from the ground"" in Thai)

PRICE

THB 3,500 per couple

TIMING

9:00 am onwards \| 1hr experience

##### Peranakan Phuket Museum

Discover a world of new experiences at the captivating "Peranakan Phuket Museum," the first of its kind on the island. Step back in time and immerse yourself in the rich history and culture of old Phuket. Explore local art, traditional lifestyle, historical exhibits, and traditional costumes, as well as the intricate Masterpieces of Peranakan Jewelry. This museum seamlessly combines local wisdom with interactive technology, creating a modern and engaging experience that showcases the essence of Phuket's heritage.
