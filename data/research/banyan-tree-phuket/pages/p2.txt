## Ultimate Experiences in Phuket Luxurious Pool Villas

Every villa at Banyan Tree Phuket is a private pool villa - eleven layouts in all, from a 140 sqm Serenity villa to a 2,500 sqm DoublePool residence, each with its own pool. Our villas meld tradition with innovation and nature with sophistication, designed as a home away from home while you explore the local culture, restore your inner peace and continue your wellness journey.

Enjoy a Couple’s Retreat -

Our one-bedroom luxury pool villas in Phuket are built for two - secluded, garden-wrapped, and quiet enough that the loudest thing you hear is your own pool. Banyan Tree Spa is a short walk away, and nine dining venues sit within the estate, so a day can be as full or as empty as you want it.

Relax with The Whole Family -

Our two- and three-bedroom family pool villas in Phuket sleep four to six, with kitchenettes, separate living areas and Thai salas built for a private BBQ. Terra Kids Club runs daily, and two children under 12 stay and dine free on our family offer. Beyond the estate: the Phuket Elephant Sanctuary, beach cleans and almsgiving.

### Pool Villas

Our pool villas in Phuket offer the perfect retreat with their contemporary take on traditional Thai design providing you with a luxurious local experience. Large windows allow you to enjoy natural light and take in the ambience outside of your private villa space throughout the day. From one-bedroom private pool villas to larger two-bedroom pool villas and three-bedroom pool villas that perfect for all retreats, Banyan Tree Phuket has something unique to offer each visitor.

### DoublePool Villas

Our DoublePool Villas sit in a 26-villa hamlet in a secluded corner of the estate, each with two pools - a 15-metre infinity pool and a jet pool - plus a personal villa host and breakfast at Tre, our restaurant for DoublePool guests only. One, two and three-bedroom layouts, from 1,300 to 2,500 sqm.

Spa Pool Villas offer adults-only privacy, daily wellbeing rituals inspired by Thai traditions, and curated dining from in-villa meals to shared culinary moments, all designed for relaxation, connection, and living each day at your own pace.
