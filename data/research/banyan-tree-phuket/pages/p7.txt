## Signature Spa Journeys

From the signature Royal Banyan to Sense of Place, the Master Therapist Experience and our evidence-based Purposeful Massage, every Phuket massage spa treatment takes place in a private treatment villa - whether you are recovering from a flight or reconnecting with your own rhythm.

Spa & Wellbeing Menu Book now

### Sense of Place: Rise and Shine

Inspired by the golden calm of early morning, this bespoke experience blends energising stretches, traditional Thai treats and gentle rituals that reflect the serenity and charm of local Phuket life.

### Royal Banyan

Experience our most cherished treatment – Royal Banyan. This signature therapy combines Eastern and Western massage techniques to improve circulation and ease deep-seated tension. Featuring a warm herbal pouch steeped in sesame oil, it offers a profoundly restorative spa journey.

### Purposeful Massage

Our evidence-based Purposeful Massages are crafted to address the pressures of modern life. From sleep difficulties and digital fatigue to posture-related discomfort, each treatment targets common concerns with tailored techniques and measurable impact. Available in 60- or 90-minute formats.

## WELLBEING ACTIVITIES

Experience a world of wellbeing at Banyan Tree Phuket. Enjoy yoga, meditation and invigorating fitness sessions. Create aromatherapy oils, savour Thai cooking classes and indulge in traditional spa treatments. Find harmony and rejuvenation during your stay with us.

### Sound Healing

Experience the resonant power of Tibetan singing bowls. This soothing vibrational therapy eases stress, calms the nervous system and promotes restful sleep – making space for emotional release and energetic renewal in Phuket’s tranquil surroundings.

### Gentle Yoga

Join a peaceful Gentle Yoga session, ideal for all levels. This calming practice combines deep breathing, mindful movement and gentle stretching to enhance flexibility, release tension and cultivate inner stillness.

### Lotus Labyrinth

Step into mindfulness at the Lotus Labyrinth beside the serene Laguna Lake. Surrounded by nature, this walking meditation offers a contemplative path to clarity, grounding and inner peace – an unforgettable practice set beneath the shade of a sacred tree.

## Banyan Tree Connections

In a world that rushes forward, find space to be present. Rediscover the romance of travel with Banyan Tree Connections – a private holistic wellbeing journey for two designed to nurture togetherness, inviting you to pause, breathe and reconnect with loved ones.

## About Banyan Spa & Wellbeing Academy

Discover the birthplace of our spa excellence - the Banyan Tree Spa & Wellbeing Academy, here in Phuket. Asia's first accredited institution of its kind, it trains therapists in anatomy, aromatherapy and touch-based healing, and has produced over 2,000 graduates now working across our global spas. It is the depth of training that separates a luxury spa in Phuket from a hotel with treatment rooms.
