# Dining

## Banyan Tree Phuket

## Restaurants and Bars in Phuket

Nine restaurants and bars across the estate, from the best Thai restaurant in Phuket to a tapas bar floating on the lagoon. Saffron, our signature restaurant, serves modern renditions of Thai classics; Banyan Cafe does an easy lunch overlooking Laguna Golf Phuket; and Rava Beach Club runs the length of Bang Tao Beach.
