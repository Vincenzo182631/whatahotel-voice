# Luxury Suites in Edinburgh

## Accommodations

W Edinburgh offers luxury 5-star accommodations, set in the heart of Edinburgh's St James Quarter in Scotland, featuring 244 rooms including 45 exceptional luxury suites - many with panoramic terraces overlooking the city. Inspired by Scotland’s culturally rich capital city, W Edinburgh is comprised of three buildings located on St James Square. From the Ribbon to James Craig Walk and the Quarter House,...See More each room and suite offers a new perspective on modern luxury.See Less

Suite Life

### Modern Icon or History in the Making?

W Edinburgh is set across three distinct buildings. Two new and one historic. Stay in our Ribbon building and Quarter House and discover playful design and iconic views close to the buzz of our restaurants and bars or discover the more intimate and private surroundings of James Craig Walk – our impressive Georgian Townhouse located on St James Square.

## James Craig Walk

James Craig Walk, our historic Georgian Townhouse on St James Square where history blends with modern luxury. Originally a terrace of townhouses dating from 1775, James Craig Walk epitomises the character of historic Edinburgh, with its stone façade, high ceilings and tall sash windows. Discover a private entrance, your own dedicated W Insider concierge service and enhanced amenities.

## Ribbon and Quarter House

A modern icon of Edinburgh, our Ribbon building interconnects with the Quarter House offering modern rooms and suites with innovative design. Discover vibrant, unique culinary experiences at our restaurants and bars. Rise to the rooftop and discover the 360 degree W Deck offering panoramic vistas of Scotland’s capital.

### Recharge & Rebalance

Complimentary to our guests and accessible 24 hours a day—stay in shape in W Edinburgh's very own FIT® gym. Exercise to energise with state-of-the-art equipment by TechnoGym®, including treadmills, cross-trainers and more. Stay fit even while you're away.

## Whatever, Whenever

At W Edinburgh we'll make it happen with our Whatever/Whenever® service, turning your special dreams into reality for a truly meaningful stay. Whatever you want, whenever you want it. Whether it’s special occasion and you need a last minute restaurant booking, insider knowledge of the city or you've forgotten a necessity - we’ve got you covered. Whatever the occasion…just ask.

### In Room Dining

Whether it’s a relaxed start to the day with breakfast in bed or satisfying a late-night craving, W Edinburgh's in-room dining menu is available at any time of the day or night for you to indulge from the comfort of your room or suite.

### FIT® Gym

Stay in shape in W Edinburgh's very own FIT® gym. Equipped with Technogym equipment for Cardio, Functional and Strength based workouts, which is accessible 24 hours a day for our guests.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Edinburgh have a work space?

No, the rooms at W Edinburgh do not have a work space.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Edinburgh?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 2 at W Edinburgh. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Edinburgh?

No, the rooms at W Edinburgh do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at W Edinburgh have fridges?
