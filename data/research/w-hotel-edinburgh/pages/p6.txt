### New Year’s Eve at W Edinburgh

Celebrate New Year’s Eve in timeless style with an evening designed for true indulgence. Begin with an elegant caviar and oyster bar, perfectly paired with cocktails, before taking your seat for a four-course dinner. ### NYE After Dark at W Lounge

From 10pm, enter the W Lounge and immerse yourself in NYE After Dark. Expect a late night New Year’s Eve experience defined by a carefully curated DJ set, sophisticated energy and unmistakable W style. At midnight ascend to the W Deck to welcome the New Year surrounded by breathtaking 360 degree views of Edinburgh. After midnight, return to the W Lounge and continue the celebration into the early hours of 2027.

### Sambazonia New Year’s Eve

Enjoy an unforgettable evening at SUSHISAMBA Edinburgh with our special SAMBAZONIA tasting menu, a vibrant multi-course journey blending Japanese, Peruvian and Brazilian flavours. Celebrate with lively entertainment and sparkling décor while taking in breathtaking views across Edinburgh. Join us at 10pm for a late-night sitting and celebrate the New Year with stunning views of Edinburgh's iconic fireworks.
