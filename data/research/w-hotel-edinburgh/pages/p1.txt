## Inspired Design

W Edinburgh comprises three distinct buildings, two new and one historic, with a rich and varied, but complementary architectural presence. While part of the wider ​St James Quarter, these buildings, around their own square, form their own new quarter, the heart of W Edinburgh.​ Conceived as a bundle of coiled ribbons, culminating in a pennant flying in the Edinburgh breeze, the Ribbon Building is designed to capture the very spirit of festivity. The bronze coloured metal ribbons - a nod to the iconic festival city.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Edinburgh?

The check-in time at W Edinburgh is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Edinburgh allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Edinburgh.

What are the parking options at W Edinburgh?

On-site parking is available at W Edinburgh. Self parking is available for £25.00 per day. Valet parking is available for £40.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Edinburgh have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Edinburgh has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Edinburgh booking.

What is the closest airport near W Edinburgh?

The closest airport near W Edinburgh is Edinburgh Airport (EDI). EDI is located approximately 7.8 KM from the hotel.

Does W Edinburgh have an airport shuttle to Edinburgh Airport (EDI)?

No, W Edinburgh does not have an airport shuttle to Edinburgh Airport.

Does W Edinburgh have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Edinburgh has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
