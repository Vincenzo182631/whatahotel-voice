# Taking Edinburgh Dining to New Heights

## Dining

Whether you're enjoying breathtaking dining with panoramic views of the city or indulging at our cocktail bar in Edinburgh, allow us to make your visit to W Edinburgh extraordinary. With our signature SUSHISAMBA Edinburgh restaurant, we're bringing new flavors to the city. Step into our intimate cocktail bar, Joao’s Place, or unwind at the W Lounge or our unique rooftop deck with the finest 360-degree...See More views of Edinburgh. W Edinburgh offers authentic and fresh dining experiences from morning to night. The signature W Lounge offers all-day dining with direct access to a unique rooftop W Deck, rewarding visitors with the finest, unfettered 360-degree views of Edinburgh. SUSHISAMBA Edinburgh, the hotel’s lively restaurant, celebrates Japanese, Brazilian and Peruvian cuisine, and features a private dining room perfect for intimate gatherings. For those who know the best bars in Edinburgh, step into João’s Place for curated cocktails – a destination bar in its own right.See Less

## New Horizons at W Lounge

The place that brings the pulse of the city to the hotel’s doorstep, W Lounge, is an any-hours destination, fueled by connections and cocktails inspired by the mythical creatures of Scotland's folklore. Celebrating individuality and diversity, it is where guests can roll with the moment with 360 degree views of iconic Edinburgh landmarks from Calton Hill and Edinburgh Castle to the Pentlands and the Firth of Forth.

## W Deck

Revel at the unrivalled 360 degree views of Edinburgh from our W Deck - you can count on us to create the vibe at our rooftop bar. When the sun is shining, enjoy signature cocktails, wine and spirits coupled with regular live music. The hottest place to spend the summer, our W Deck offers unfettered views of the city from Calton Hill to Edinburgh Castle like you've never seen before. Visit in August for the nightly fireworks display over Edinburgh Castle or party into the wee hours at New Year for the city's Hogmanay celebrations.

## Joao’s Place

Join us in Joao's for incredible cocktails inside the intimate studio inspired by the Liberdade neighborhood of São Paulo Brazil. The drinks are a mix of classics celebrating Brazil and Joao’s community in Liberdade, São Paulo’s largest Japanese community outside of Japan, and a Bossa Nova selection inspired by the huge wave of international artists who swarmed to Brazil in the 1980s.
