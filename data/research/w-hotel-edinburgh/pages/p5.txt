### Small City, Big Personality

Where the Old Town meets the New, W Edinburgh sits in the heart of the city, placing you at the crossroads of culture, design and discovery. Step out to explore Scotland’s capital, from the shopping delights of Multrees Walk and the architectural gems of the Royal Mile to the iconic sights of Edinburgh Castle. Venture further to experience the city’s distinct neighbourhoods - Leith for late-night dining...See More and creative energy, Stockbridge for slow mornings and boutique charm and Bruntsfield for café culture and a relaxed atmosphere. Edinburgh may be small but its personality is bold with a year-round calendar of world-famous festivals waiting to be explored.See Less

A Season of Renewal

### See Edinburgh Differently

From the Vennel Steps to the W Deck, Edinburgh is all about the views. Wander up Calton Hill or Arthur’s Seat for sweeping city scenes or skip the climb and head to the W Deck at W Edinburgh for a fresh perspective with 360 degree panoramic views of the Castle, Old Town and beyond. Golden hour or after dark – Edinburgh is always iconic.

### Autumn in Edinburgh

This autumn, W Edinburgh is your front-row seat to the season's most unforgettable Edinburgh experiences. As the city's historic streets transform into vibrant shades of gold and amber, Edinburgh comes alive with world-class events, seasonal dining and cultural discoveries. Sip your way through Edinburgh Cocktail Week, soak up the electric atmosphere of the Autumn Nations rugby fixtures at Murrayfield...See More Stadium, and enjoy spectacular views of the Samhuinn Fire Festival and Bonfire Night fireworks from W Lounge and Joao's Place. From rooftop cocktails overlooking the Edinburgh skyline to iconic attractions, immersive experiences and hidden local gems, discover the very best of autumn in Edinburgh.See Less

## Edinburgh’s Style District

Perfectly positioned in the heart of Edinburgh's leading luxury shopping destinations, Multrees Walk and St James Quarter, W Edinburgh places the city's most coveted fashion, beauty and lifestyle brands on your doorstep. Discover world-renowned labels, exclusive boutiques and iconic Scottish brands, all within walking distance of the hotel, in Edinburgh's luxury retail district.

### Global Icons. Scottish Discoveries.

Explore Multrees Walk, Scotland's premier luxury shopping destination, home to world-renowned luxury brands alongside celebrated Scottish designers and heritage labels. Perfectly positioned in the heart of Edinburgh's luxury retail district, W Edinburgh offers an unrivalled base for discovering the very best luxury shopping in Edinburgh, just moments from your room or suite.

### Shop Like an Insider

Enjoy discreet access to the St James Quarter Galleria directly from the hotel, where luxury fashion, beauty, jewelry and lifestyle brands await. Looking for something special? Our W Insider team can curate the perfect shopping day with local recommendations and introductions to some of Edinburgh's most exclusive retail experiences and luxury shopping destinations.

## Curated Experiences

Immerse yourself in Scotland's rich cultural and natural heritage with our unique curated experiences and W Insider knowledge. Create music in W Edinburgh's Sound Suite, explore Scotland’s whisky distilleries with a private tour or set sail and surf on local shores. Each adventure offers our W Insider’s expert guidance and exclusive access, from private transfers in our Genesis car to music lovers, artists, whisky enthusiasts, thrill-seekers, and golfers.

### Create Your Own Soundtrack

Step into the Sound Suite at W Edinburgh and immerse yourself in a world of musical creativity. Whether you're a seasoned musician or just looking to explore your artistic side, our state-of-the-art recording studio offers the perfect environment to create, record, and produce. With cutting-edge equipment and expert guidance, you'll feel like a true rockstar as you bring your musical vision to life.

### Embark on a Whisky Journey

With over 130 distilleries dotting the Scottish landscape, our passion for whisky runs deep. Join us for a private whisky distillery tour, where luxury transportation meets unparalleled expertise. Explore the rich history and craftsmanship behind Scotland's finest Scotch whiskies and savor the distinct flavors that make each distillery unique.

### Discover the Home of Golf

Scotland boasts some of the world's finest golf courses. Whether you're teeing off at Edinburgh's nearby courses or exploring legendary greens across the country, our W Insiders can arrange tee times, golf club hire, and provide insider tips for an exceptional experience. Perfect for golf enthusiasts of all levels, this is your chance to play on Scotland's iconic courses and enjoy the sport in its birthplace.
