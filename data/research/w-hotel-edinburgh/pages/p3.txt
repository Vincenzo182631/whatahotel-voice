Penthouse

## Design Details

Traditions are honoured with Georgian style paneling and saltire inspired wood flooring throughout. Unique artworks by Scottish photographer Jodie Mann include 'Waiting at Oceans End' and 'Capercaillie' both inspired by Scottish history, folklore, nature, poetry, music and design. The suite's pantry is located beside the vast dining room, perfect for entertaining your guests whatever the occasion.

Penthouse

## Unique Surroundings

In the bedroom, a unique round super-king-sized bed pivots towards the skyline view and a spacious walk-in wardrobe with adjustable mirrors reflects the suite's accent lighting and views of the historic rooftops of Edinburgh. Step out onto the Penthouse's panoramic private terrace offering a warming fire-pit for entertaining, relaxed lounge furniture and breathtaking views of Edinburgh.

Penthouse

## Bespoke Features

Our Penthouse features your very own private sauna. Benefit from the healing powers of the heat or if cooler temperatures are your thing - step into the outside shower on the private terrace for fresh air and views of Edinburgh Castle. In the bathroom you’ll find a free-standing bath, walk in shower and vanity.

### James Craig Deluxe Suite

Our James Craig Deluxe Suites are located in James Craig Walk, our historic Georgian Townhouse on St James Square where history blends with modern luxury. James Craig Walk was originally a terrace of townhouses dating from 1775, that epitomises the character of historic Edinburgh, with its stone façade, high ceilings and tall sash windows. Legend has it that Scots Poet, Rabbie Burns, partied here when...See More he was a resident of St James Square in the late 1780s. James Craig Deluxe Suite guests can benefit from their own exclusive private entrance to James Craig Walk. Stay close enough to the buzz of the Ribbon Building yet discreetly tucked away for a home-from-home ambiance.See Less
