# Hotel Nantipa — Barefoot Luxury Hotel in Santa Teresa, Costa Rica

## Welcome to Hotel Nantipa

Santa Teresa, Costa Rica

Hotel Nantipa is a luxury beachfront boutique hotel in Santa Teresa, Costa Rica, where refined comfort meets the relaxed rhythm of the Nicoya Peninsula. Set along one of the country's most sought-after stretches of coastline, the hotel offers a stay defined by thoughtful design, genuine hospitality, and a strong sense of place.

Located in the heart of the Blue Zone Costa Rica is known for, Nantipa brings together barefoot luxury, natural beauty, and the character of a true Tico beach experience.
