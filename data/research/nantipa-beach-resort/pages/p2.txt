# Rooms

## Ninta Beachfront Bungalow

80 m²  1 King Bed  Up to 2 guests

"Ninta" is the Chorotegan word for "beach", and the inspiration behind our sleek units designed to make your beachfront experience as vivid as possible. The Ninta bungalows are made up of 80 square meters (800 square feet) of luxury space including an ample terrace, a private 3.5 x 3.5 meter (10 x 10 ft) plunge pool, hammock, king bed and double indoor/outdoor shower. Our floor to ceiling glass doors open to ocean views, making Ninta ideal for a memorable honeymoon or just a romantic getaway surrounded by raw nature and the hypnotic sound of the ocean.

## Nanku Suites

65 m²  1 King bed or 2 Queen beds  Up to 3 adults + 1 child

"Nanku" means "home" in Chorotega, which is what we aim to be for you! Our Nanku Suites are perfect for small families or groups. They offer a sleeping area with either one king bed or two queen beds (bedding preference can be requested but is not guaranteed), and a large living room space with two twin daybeds. A large bathroom and terrace or balcony complete the unit, making it the perfect home setting to relax after a long day of adventures.
