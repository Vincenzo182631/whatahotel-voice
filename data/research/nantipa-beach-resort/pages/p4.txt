## How to Get to Nantipa

From San José or Liberia, there are multiple ways to reach the pristine shores of Santa Teresa on the Nicoya Peninsula.

Recommended

### By Air

Fly into San José (SJO) — both SANSA and Green Airways operate daily scheduled flights to Cóbano Airport (ACO), the closest airstrip to Santa Teresa. From the airport, Nantipa is just 15–20 minutes by taxi or private transfer.

Flight time25 – 30 min

AirportCóbano (ACO)

AirlinesSANSA · Green Airways

FrequencyUp to 6 flights/day

SANSA also operates daily direct flights SJO › ACO. Schedules subject to seasonal changes — book in advance.

### By Land

Drive or arrange a private transfer from San José via Route 27 and the Puntarenas-Paquera ferry. The scenic route takes you through lush mountains and coastal roads.

Duration5 – 6 hours

RouteVia Paquera Ferry

OptionsPrivate transfer, rental

### By Sea

Take the Puntarenas–Paquera ferry crossing across the Gulf of Nicoya. The ferry departs multiple times daily and connects to a 2-hour drive to Santa Teresa.

Ferry time~1.5 hours

OperatorNaviera Tambor

DeparturesMultiple daily

## Getting Around Santa Teresa

Santa Teresa is a laid-back beach town best explored at your own pace. Here is how to navigate the area.

### ATV & Golf Cart Rental

The most popular way to get around. Rent an ATV or golf cart and explore the dusty roads of Santa Teresa at your leisure. We can arrange rentals for you.

### Bicycle

For the eco-conscious traveler. Bicycles are available at the hotel, perfect for short trips along the coast and nearby shops.

### Private Transfers

Our concierge team can arrange private drivers for day trips to Montezuma, Cabo Blanco Nature Reserve, or the Nicoya Peninsula's hidden gems.

### On Foot

Many of the best experiences are within walking distance: the beach, surf breaks, local restaurants, and the vibrant Santa Teresa strip are just steps away.
