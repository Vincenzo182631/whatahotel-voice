### Luxury family-friendly hotel near Cocotal Golf and Country Club

Located near Bávaro Beach and Downtown Punta Cana, Paradisus Grand Cana – All Suites – All Inclusive offers exceptional amenities, including complimentary access to a water park, an adjacent golf course with a driving range and lessons, and a lively beach bar. Indulge in rejuvenating treatments like Thai massages, facials, and manicures at the onsite Maia Spa, or savor diverse cuisine across seven restaurants. Stay active at the 24-hour fitness center or enjoy beach volleyball, then unwind with free in-room WiFi (50+ Mbps), a refreshing poolside bar, and a relaxing terrace.

### Activities

Guaranteed fun! Paradisus Grand Cana Resort by Melia offers a wide range of activities for the whole family, always adapted to your needs and preferences. Sports, leisure, relaxation, open air, art ... Sounds good, right?

ART & WINE

Art and wine, a perfect pairing. Imagine a cosy art studio where you can be an artist for a day. A creative hobby to help you have fun, relax, meet new people and surprise yourself with all your inner creativity. All accompanied by a local artist and an exquisite wine tasting session. Let yourself go!

WOGA

Discover Woga, combining the benefits of yoga with water exercises. An excellent way to perform the classic yoga asanas in the smoothest style. Relax both body and mind, soothe anxiety and reduce stress.

RHYTHM & MOVES

We also have activities for the little ones. Leave your children in the hands of professional experts who will help them have fun to the beat of the music. Guided activities full of fun to help them discover and develop their balance, self-awareness and coordination. A perfect chance for the kids to have fun and explore their body and develop their skills.

GOLF COURSE

Practice your best strokes at the 27-hole course at the Cocotal Golf and Country Club. Complete your stay by practicing your favourite sport at a short distance from the hotel. There are golf greens of several levels for the enjoyment of professionals as well as novices, where you'll be able to attend either a golf tournament or just enjoy classes at the golf club. The perfect physical activity to activate your body during your holiday, and the best way to relax after a day of work.
