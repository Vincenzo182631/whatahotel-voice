**Purnama and New Moon**

'Purnama' means 'full moon' in Indonesian: complete, infinite and perfect. On these sacred evenings, Amandari marks the occasion with a thoughtfully curated culinary gathering. A bespoke menu highlights local ingredients, seasonal flavours and Balinese favourites, served beneath the moonlit sky - a quiet celebration of light, renewal and the rhythms of the valley.

Purnama date: 27 August, 26 September, 25 October, 24 November and 23 December 2026.

\\
**Ngejot Balinese feast**

Ngejot is an exquisite Balinese Hindu tradition and a symbol of interfaith harmony that involves sharing food with neighbours as an expression of gratitude during celebratory occasions. Upholding this tradition, Amandari offers Ngejot Balinese Feast at The Restaurant during dinner time, which features Balinese finest local cuisine and highlights native rare ingredients such as Balinese mansur rice from Payangan village and Balinese heritage pig from Tabanan regency.

A private Ngejot Balinese Feast experience with Penyembrama Dance performance at the dining bale is available with 24-hour notice.

\\
**Jamuan makan nusantara**

Jamuan Makan Nusantara offers diverse regional Indonesian archipelago dishes. Featuring a curation of expertly prepared vegetables, seafood, and meat dishes, this dining experience showcases Indonesian cuisine’s bold and complex flavours as well as its aromatic herbs and spices.

\\
**Sundowners on the hill**

Known as the golden hour, when the setting sun gilds every leaf, stone and smile, late afternoon at Amandari is the moment to stroll up the hill, settle into your own private picnic setting and toast another magical day with your drink of choice.
