## Provence _meets_ the Philippines

Mirèio is an elegant brasserie-style restaurant that offers gastronomic French-style cooking. Dining, whether for lunch, dinner or Sunday brunch, becomes an exquisite experience complemented by a magnificent view of the cityscape. The restaurant is named after Mirèio (1859), a celebrated poem by Nobel Laureate Frédéric Mistral, who was a favourite son of Provence.

## Frequently asked questions

### What amenities does Raffles Makati offer?

Raffles Makati is an all-suite hotel offering thoughtfully designed accommodations and a collection of refined experiences. Guests may enjoy signature dining across its restaurants and bars, moments of quiet restoration at the spa, and unhurried relaxation by the Raffles Pool.

### How can I book a stay at Raffles Makati?

Get in touch with Room Reservations by calling (63) 2 8555 9711, or by email at BookUs.Makati@Raffles.com. Explore our website to find specially designed offers for your ideal Raffles Makati stay.

### What dining options are available at Raffles Makati?

At Raffles Makati, guests may explore a collection of distinctive dining experiences in refined settings. Mirèio, on the 9th floor, presents French & Mediterranean cuisine, while Mistral Rooftop Bar offers artisanal cocktails alongside Makati's sunset views. On the lobby level, The Writers Bar & Long Bar are signature venues integral to the Raffles experience. 24-hour in-suite dining is also available.

### What is the location of Raffles Makati?

Raffles Makati is located at 1 Raffles Drive, Makati Avenue, 1224 Makati, Philippines, right in the Central Business District.

### When is the best time to visit Raffles Makati?

The dry season of January - April provides perfect weather for city exploration with Raffles Makati at its heart, while the cool Christmas season from October to December puts the hotel within arms' reach of bustling shopping, unique dining experiences and vibrant festive events. During March to May, Raffles Makati becomes the ideal escape from tropical summer heat.
