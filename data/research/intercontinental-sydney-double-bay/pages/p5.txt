Welcome to Bayside Bistro & Bar at InterContinental Sydney Double Bay, your new place to dine, sip and unwind. Enjoy classic favourites, hearty dishes, share plates and daily specials paired with curated wines and timeless cocktails, all served in a relaxed and stylish atmosphere.

### Pampered Paws, Peaceful Stays

Some stays are better shared. At InterContinental Sydney Double Bay, four-legged companions are warmly welcomed. Walk by the water together, return to easy comforts, and let the day find its own rhythm, a place where companionship feels effortless and every detail falls into place.

### Explore Double Bay & Beyond

Just beyond the hotel, Double Bay offers a gentler pace of Sydney life. Wander past charming storefronts, linger at waterside cafés, or follow the coastline to stretches of sand. Neighbourhood corners are there to be discovered, each adding to a sense of place that feels personal. Every outing is its own pleasure, and every return, a welcome one.

### Quiet Luxury, Moments Away

Just minutes from Sydney’s CBD, discover a tranquil retreat where coastal calm meets refined luxury. Tucked away in Double Bay, the hotel offers a peaceful escape from the city's pace - where quiet mornings, gentle harbour breezes, and elegant spaces invite you to truly unwind.

How much is parking at InterContinental Sydney Double Bay?

At InterContinental Sydney Double Bay, valet parking is available for a daily rate of 80 AUD .

Arrive with ease, with valet parking available at $80 per car per day. Guests who prefer to self-park will find Wilson Parking just beside the hotel, offering convenient access and a 2.0m height clearance for a convenient arrival and departure.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Sydney Double Bay?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Sydney Double Bay?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Sydney Double Bay.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Sydney Double Bay?

Check-in at InterContinental Sydney Double Bay is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Sydney Double Bay offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Sydney Double Bay offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Sydney Double Bay from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Sydney Double Bay is conveniently located near Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (SYD), approximately 7.89 miles away (12.7 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Sydney Double Bay?

InterContinental Sydney Double Bay is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Sydney Opera House - 2.98 miles away (4.8 kilometers)
- Redleaf Beach - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Double Bay Organic Markets - Double Bay - 0.12 miles away (0.2 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Sydney Double Bay puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
