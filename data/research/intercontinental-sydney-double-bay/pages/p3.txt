## The Dining Room

At The Dining Room, breakfast is thoughtfully prepared. The buffet presents a curated selection of hot and cold dishes, from crisp bacon and golden hash browns to delicately scrambled eggs made fresh each morning. Artisan breads from Baker Bleu and seasonal fruits add a gentle balance of flavour. Designed to be both nourishing and satisfying, it offers a moment of pause before the day begins. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 am to 10:30 am.
