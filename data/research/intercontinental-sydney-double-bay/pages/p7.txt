# InterContinental Sydney Double BayDestination

In Double Bay, classic architecture meets modern living. Cafés spill onto sunlit streets, boutiques invite browsing, and the harbour is never far. Cultural icons and local favourites sit side by side, creating a neighbourhood that feels vibrant.

1. Explore Double Bay & Beyond

2. Quiet Luxury, Moments Away

### Explore Double Bay & Beyond

Just beyond the hotel, Double Bay offers a gentler pace of Sydney life. Wander past charming storefronts, linger at waterside cafés, or follow the coastline to stretches of sand. Neighbourhood corners are there to be discovered, each adding to a sense of place that feels personal. Every outing is its own pleasure, and every return, a welcome one.

Learn more

### Quiet Luxury, Moments Away

Just minutes from Sydney’s CBD, discover a tranquil retreat where coastal calm meets refined luxury. Tucked away in Double Bay, the hotel offers a peaceful escape from the city's pace - where quiet mornings, gentle harbour breezes, and elegant spaces invite you to truly unwind.

## Things to do

### Sydney Opera House

Bennelong Point, Sydney, NSW 2000

The famous white sails of the Sydney Opera House marks the cultural hub of city. Imagine dining with a glass of wine while taking in the view of Sydney Harbour Bridge and surrounds before experiencing the show of your choice from the opera, to a musical, ballet and key speakers.

Learn more

### Redleaf Beach

536 New South Head Road, Double Bay, NSW 2028

One of Double Bay's hidden secrets, idyllic Sydney harbourside Redleaf Beach is a must-visit. Enjoy a walk on the
pontoon or take a swim in Murray Rose Pool which provides swimmers with a safe swimming area.

Learn more

### Double Bay Organic Markets - Double Bay

Guilfoyle Park, Double Bay, NSW 2028

Experience the local Double Bay produce markets, held each Thursday from 8.00am- 2.00pm at Guilfoyle Park, Knox Street.Enjoy a varied and exciting choice of local produce.
