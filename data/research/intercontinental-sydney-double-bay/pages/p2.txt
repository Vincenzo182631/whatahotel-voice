# InterContinental Sydney Double BayRooms & suites

Step inside and the light greets you first; it fills the room and opens it up in a way that immediately puts you at ease. No adjusting, no settling in. It simply feels right.

All 140 rooms and suites face something a little different. The bay glimmers through some windows, its mood changing with the light. Some look down over the neighbourhood, while others open onto a tucked-away French-style courtyard.

The rooms are pared back and considered. A generous bed, room to breathe, and everything where you'd expect it, so your time here remains uninterrupted and entirely your own.

Mornings begin without rush; coffee in hand, light coming through the window, the day slowly assembling outside. By evening, things soften, and the room becomes a peaceful place to let go.
