## luxury supercar driving experiences

## prancing horse

Enhance your stay at Sequoia and experience the beauty of South Australia on an intimate supercar driving experience across the beautiful Adelaide Hills.

An immersive driving experience in the latest specification of supercars. Drive through Carey Gully into a blur of vibrant Spring colours, winding along the roads of Lenswood, Lobethal and Cudlee Creek. As the hills unravel into vineyards and farmland, follow the River Torrens down to Kangaroo Creek Reservoir and the beautiful bends of Gorge Road.

A once-in-a-lifetime experience where you will be able to drive the latest collections of model supercars – Ferrari F8 Tributo, Lamborghini Huracán and McLaren 570S.

Please note: Pricing is for the drive experience only, please book Sequoia Lodge separately.

## McLaren Vale & Fleurieu Peninsula

## 18 - 20 APRIL 2025 - EASTER WEEKEND

Explore the beauty of South Australia’s McLaren Vale and Fleurieu Peninsula on a romantic weekend featuring fast cars, slow food and a luxury stay at Sequoia Lodge.

An immersive Luxury Driving Experience with multiple swaps along the 250km+ journey, so you and your partner can drive many different supercars.

## Details

- Autumn 2025 driving experience – 1 day / 2 nights
- Intimate and exclusive – Maximum 6 couples
- 6 super cars to drive – all the latest models
- 250km+ in distance covered
- Multiple drive swaps, 6 sections
- Lead car – 2-way radio support
- HVR, SEQUIOA Lodge and The Currant Shed dining

From $9,990 per couple

## Luxury Driving Experience

## March/APRIL 2026 and October/November 2026
