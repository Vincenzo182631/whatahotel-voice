## Life is exciting

Sequoia Lodge in the misty Adelaide Hills, where winery hopping is delightfully accessible. Our advice? Start your day with a soothing dip in one of three thermal hot pools before getting ready to hit the road: there’s innovative sparkling at Tapanappa, floral grenache at Yangarra and vintage Grange at Penfolds; all within a half hour drive of the property or you may prefer to enjoy our many complimentary lodge activities. ## Frequently asked questions

What is the best luxury accommodation in the Adelaide Hills?

Sequoia Lodge is an award-winning, adults-only luxury lodge on Mount Lofty in the Adelaide Hills, offering just 14 suites overlooking the Piccadilly Valley. A proud member of Relais & Châteaux, Sequoia has been recognised among Australia’s leading luxury accommodation experiences, including being named Best 5-Star Luxury Accommodation at the South Australian Tourism Awards. Guests enjoy highly personalised service, exceptional dining, wellness experiences and immersive South Australian food, wine and nature experiences, all within 20 minutes of Adelaide.

Is Sequoia Lodge for adults only?

Yes. Sequoia Lodge is an adults-only luxury retreat welcoming guests aged 18 years and over. Its intimate setting, private suites and curated experiences make it ideal for couples, celebrations, romantic getaways and peaceful escapes in the Adelaide Hills.

How far is Sequoia Lodge from Adelaide?

Sequoia Lodge is located on Mount Lofty in the Adelaide Hills, approximately 20 minutes by car from Adelaide CBD and around 35 minutes from Adelaide Airport. Its location makes it easy to combine a luxury Adelaide Hills escape with time in the city or surrounding South Australian wine regions.

Does Sequoia Lodge have hot pools?

Yes. Sequoia Lodge features artesian spring-fed hot pools overlooking the Piccadilly Valley. Reserved exclusively for lodge guests, the thermal pools are designed for relaxation and are one of Sequoia’s signature wellness experiences.

What is included in a stay at Sequoia Lodge?

Guests enjoy access to more than 32+ signature experiences and inclusions throughout their stay. These may include sunrise yoga, guided nature walks, artisan food and wine experiences, tastings, the artesian hot pools and infrared sauna, along with a curated in-suite minibar. Experiences vary throughout the year, with selected premium experiences available at an additional cost.

What dining is available at Sequoia Lodge?

Sequoia Lodge offers guests access to exceptional dining across Mount Lofty Estate. Hardy’s Verandah Restaurant delivers an acclaimed fine dining experience as well as High Tea every day, celebrating seasonal South Australian produce, while additional dining and bar experiences are available throughout the estate. Sequoia Dining serves curated food and wine experiences.

What wineries and experiences are near Sequoia Lodge?

Sequoia Lodge is surrounded by some of the Adelaide Hills’ leading wineries, cellar doors, artisan producers and natural attractions. Guests can explore nearby wineries, Mount Lofty Botanic Garden and Cleland National Park, or join privately curated experiences including winery visits, food and wine tastings, nature experiences and touring across the Adelaide Hills and neighbouring wine regions.
