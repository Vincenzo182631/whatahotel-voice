## ultimate indulgence in the adelaide hills

# sequoia dining

With a view spanning the vast expanse of the stunning patchwork Piccadilly Valley, the Sequoia Lodge lounge is the ultimate space to relax and indulge and discover the South Australia you never knew existed.

The menu focuses on locally sourced and seasonal produce offering a range of contemporary Australian dishes, with fresh, high-quality ingredients. Sequoia Lounge also features an extensive curated wine list, showcasing the best of South Australia’s renown wine regions.

Sequoia Lodge’s scenic location, relaxing atmosphere and bespoke food and wine program is the perfect location for any occasion, so come for something special or just come to indulge.

## SEQUOIA LODGE HEAD CHEF

# CHEF NICOLAS REVERTE

Chef Nicolas Reverte, a French-born and trained chef, had been working in Melbourne before quickly relocating to Queensland to become the head chef at The Reef House Hotel in Palm Cove, a sister property of Sequoia and MLH. After three years in tropical Queensland, he transferred to Sequoia Lodge for the opportunity to lead as head chef with a new culinary vision.

Drawing on his French training and his experience working in Spain for Martin Berasategui—who holds 11 Michelin stars—to add European flair to modern Australian cuisine. For Chef Nicolas, discovering South Australia presents a new challenge in a region renowned for its exceptional produce. It is an exciting opportunity for him to apply his French culinary skills by designing a new menu that features seasonal products for guests to enjoy at Sequoia.

“As the Head Chef of Sequoia Lodge in the picturesque Adelaide Hills, my culinary journey has been shaped by a deep passion for authenticity, sustainability, and innovation. I believe that a truly remarkable dining experience is built upon high-quality, locally sourced ingredients, and I am committed to showcasing the very best this region has to offer.”

“At Sequoia Lodge, we take a hands-on approach to our menu by preparing everything from scratch—including our bread, brioche buns, English muffins, and lavosh. This dedication to artisanal craftsmanship not only enhances the flavours and textures of our dishes but also fosters a closer connection between our guests and the food they enjoy.”

“Beyond the ingredients, my approach to cooking is rooted in storytelling. Every dish we create reflects the landscape, the seasons, and the passionate producers who make the Adelaide Hills such a rich culinary destination. Through thoughtful presentation and meticulous attention to detail, we aim to provide an experience that is both indulgent and deeply meaningful. Ultimately, my goal is to redefine fine dining by embracing sustainability, celebrating local flavours, and offering a dining experience that resonates with guests long after they leave our table.”

## Truffle season

## truffle season dinners

Winter in the Adelaide Hills is a magical time, especially for food and wine lovers. Known as the “ **_Truffle and Red Wine_**” season, it is the perfect time to indulge in the finer things in life. The Adelaide Hills boast some of the finest truffle farms in the world.

Indulge in the unique and earthy flavours of black truffles and enjoy dishes that have been expertly crafted to showcase their delicate flavour. Our truffles are locally sourced from Quercus Truffles – Adelaide Hills producers of Perigord (black) truffles. Herbicide & pesticide free. Read more >
