# sequoia suites

Your private open-plan suite is perched on the edge of the Piccadilly Valley, boasting panoramic views from the sunken lounge, bedroom and bathroom.

Sink into the large bath tub, take a nap or relax on your spacious private balcony day bed with a bottle of sparkling wine and a charcuterie platter. Watch the wallabies and kangaroos forage beneath your feet as you indulge in losing track of time.

## design & interiors

The suites have been brought to life by local suppliers, craftsmen and artisans, creating a tranquil oasis with a calming colour palette punctuated by warm accents of wood and stone.

Each suite features an original oil painting by resident artist Stephen Trebilcock who is renowned for capturing unique local native florals in incredible detail, colour and depth.

An exquisite collection of artisanal pieces and amenities have been handpicked including Jurlique products, Nick Mount glass, Jam Factory ceramics, handmade tea & coffee cups by ES Ceramics and Hahndorf Soap Factory bath salts.

## Frequently asked questions

Are Sequoia Lodge suites adults-only?

Yes. Sequoia Lodge is an adults-only retreat for guests 18 years and over.

What experiences are included with a suite booking?

Inclusive on-site experiences—such as artesian hot pools, guided nature walks, yoga and star gazing—are complimentary unless \*marked. Some experiences are seasonal and subject to change.

Can I book exclusive experiences and dining in advance?

Yes. Dining, spa treatments, picnics and curated experiences can be pre-booked. We recommend reserving early to secure preferred times.

What packages are available at Sequoia Lodge?

Sequoia Lodge offers two curated year-round packages: the Experience Package, showcasing seasonal inclusions and Adelaide Hills highlights, and the Indulgence Package, designed for a deeper, luxurious escape. Both combine accommodation with bespoke inclusions and exceptional experiences. Availability is limited and subject to change.

Where is Sequoia Lodge located?

Sequoia is located on Mount Lofty Estate in the Adelaide Hills, South Australia—renowned for its wineries and exceptional produce. The lodge is just 20 minutes from Adelaide CBD and around 30 minutes from Adelaide Airport, making it both secluded and easily accessible.
