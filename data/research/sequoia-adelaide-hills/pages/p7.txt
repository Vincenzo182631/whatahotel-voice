## The Lane Vineyard

Discover one of the Adelaide Hills’ most acclaimed wine destinations. Set among rolling vineyards and sweeping valley views, The Lane Vineyard invites guests to immerse themselves in a world of fine wine, exceptional cuisine and authentic regional experiences.

Guests can enjoy guided wine discovery, seasonal dining experiences, behind-the-scenes insights into vineyard life, and a range of curated food and wine encounters designed to showcase the very best of the estate. Whether exploring the vineyards, lingering over a long lunch, or simply taking in the breathtaking surrounds, every visit offers a deeper connection to the landscape, flavours and craftsmanship that define the Adelaide Hills.

## Overview

- Guests can visit The Lane Vineyard at any time.
- Enjoy guided wine discovery, seasonal dining experiences, behind-the-scenes insights into vineyard life.
- Delight in curated food and wine encounters showcasing the very best of the estate.

## details

- Cellar Door open daily 10am – 5pm
