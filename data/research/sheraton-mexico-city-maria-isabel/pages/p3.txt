## Dining

Embark on a culinary adventure at Sheraton Mexico City Maria Isabel Hotel. Reflecting the international influences on Reforma Avenue, our property hosts four diverse restaurants and the Lobby Bar, located...See More moments from downtown CDMX. Revel in the melodies of live Mariachi music at Jorongo Bar as you dine on traditional Mexican dishes. Or go global with Ristorante Amici's signature Italian fare and the NYC-inspired dining of Manhattan Restaurant. At Impulse, fuel your day with quick bites, coffee, juices, and sodas from our coffee bar, which offers freshly brewed coffee, freshly baked bread, and sandwiches to go. On days you can't choose head over to our globally inspired Club Lounge for menus featuring a little bit of everything. A world of gourmet flavors awaits you in our Mexico City restaurants.See Less

## Sheraton Club

Enjoy and relax at our Club Lounge with a drink and a snack. Our executive floor is designed to offer an experience that combines warmth and sophistication. The spaces created are ideal for working, meeting or simply relaxing, and are equipped with stylish essentials that ensure a pleasant stay. The Sheraton Club is available to our Elite guests. Service Hours: Monday to Sunday from 06:30 to 22:00 hrs
