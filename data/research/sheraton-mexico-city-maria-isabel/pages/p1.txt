## Mexico City Comfort: Where Your Journey Begins

Enhance your Mexico City experience with a stay at the Sheraton Mexico City Maria Isabel Hotel, ideally located. Immerse yourself in the vibrant city life. Take a stroll along the avenue to marvel at The Angel of Independence, and continue your exploration to discover the lush greenery of Chapultepec Park. Enjoy the elegance of our hotel in Paseo de la Reforma as you retreat after a day of sightseeing. Indulge in a gourmet meal at Amici restaurant, or unwind with cocktails at our lobby bar. Recharge your energy with a dip in our heated outdoor pool or a workout session at the Sheraton Fitness Center, complete with a sauna. Discover a hotel near Angel of Independence in Mexico City that offers comfort and convenience. At the end of the day, relax in our stylish and pet-friendly hotel in Mexico City, featuring panoramic city views and Sheraton Signature Sleep Experience beds for a restful night's sleep. Your unforgettable Mexico City getaway begins at the Sheraton Mexico City Maria Isabel Hotel.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma?

The check-in time at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma?

On-site parking is available at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma. Self parking is available at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma, with rates of $3.10 per hour, and $10.50 per day. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma booking.

What is the closest airport near Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma?

The closest airport near Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma is Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX). MEX is located approximately 13.4 KM from the hotel.

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a Sheraton Club lounge?

Yes, Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma has Sheraton Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Sheraton Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
