### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a work space?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma?

The largest occupancy room at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma is the Marco Polo Presidential with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma?

Some rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have fridges?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have in-room jettubs?

Yes, some room types at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have jettubs. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
