## Sheraton Fitness

Workout in our state-of-the-art hotel fitness center in Mexico City. Our modern 420-square-meter gym energizes you upon entry via bright colors and locally inspired fitness-themed murals.

Life Fitness cardio and strength-training equipment, with iPod adapters and USB connections, ranges from treadmills, stationary bikes, and stair machines to nautilus-style machines and bench presses. Free weights are also available, as is a stretching zone stocked with stability balls, foam rollers, massage sticks and stretching bands.

## Angel de la Independencia

One of the most recognizable landmarks in Mexico City, this victory column commemorates the centennial of the beginning of Mexico's War of Independence on Reforma Avenue, close to our hotel near the Angel of Independence Mexico City.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma offers a complimentary, 6:00 AM-10:30 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Free Weights, Sauna, locker rooms, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have a pool on-site?

Yes, Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma have an on-site spa?

Yes, Zenia Spa is located on-site at Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma.

What are some local attractions and things to do near Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma?

Templo Mayor Museum, Siemens, Mexican Stock Exchange, Anthropology Museum, Rufino Tamayo Museum and more are nearby Sheraton Maria Isabel Mexico City Reforma.
