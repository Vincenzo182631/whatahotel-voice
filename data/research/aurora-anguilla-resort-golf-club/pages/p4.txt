THE WONDERS OF WELLNESS

# Sorana Spa

Let your worries melt away with treatments that refresh and revitalize the body and soul. Our experts use refined techniques and natural elements from the island to create a serene oasis. Find steam and sauna, spa treatments, beauty salon, a fitness studio and hydrotherapy experiences that will leave you feeling pampered and rejuvenated.

### **Seasonal Closure**

Sorana Spa will be temporarily closed from **August 17 through October 15** as part of the seasonal closure of the Rendezvous, Aurora Anguilla Resort & Golf Club.

The spa will reopen on **October 16**. We look forward to welcoming you back for a renewed season of relaxation and wellness.

SPA TREATMENTS

## Time to Recharge

Step into Sorana Spa to restore your inner and outer serenity. The idyllic setting has a range of holistic specialty treatments that leave you feeling relaxed and rejuvenated. In addition to a la carte treatments — facials, massage, body, rituals — spa amenities include a co-ed relaxation, steam, sauna, and hydrotherapy experiences as well as a signature product line from Subtle Energies, Biologique Recherche, and OSEA.

## Luxurious Treatment Rooms

Step into Sorana Spa and take a deep breath. Our twelve treatment rooms include two couples suites and a custom-designed with a sand quartz table made for specific treatments. With luxurious facilities and views of Rendezvous Beach, this is the ultimate health and wellness oasis.
