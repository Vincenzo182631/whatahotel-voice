## Our Vision

To offer an exclusive 380-acre island sanctuary where world-class amenities, exceptional service, and immersive experiences create lifelong memories for every guest.

## Our Mission

At Aurora Anguilla Resort & Golf Club, we craft unforgettable island experiences from the moment you arrive to the moment you leave us, carrying memories of our sanctuary of wellness, relaxation, and adventure along miles of pristine white beaches. Committed to preserving Anguilla’s natural beauty, we blend sustainability with exceptional service to exceed every expectation. Our dedicated team brings the island’s culture, charm, and traditions to life, ensuring every moment is as unique as the island itself.

One Team, One Dream—delivering the ultimate resort experience just for you.

## About Us

Aurora Anguilla® Resort & Golf Club is located on the tranquil island of Anguilla, the most northerly of the Eastern Caribbean’s Leeward Islands. Nestled in the crescent curve of Rendezvous Beach, this intimate and casually sophisticated beachfront resort celebrates the ultimate Caribbean escape.

With a choice of 178 luxurious suites and villas, guests are enticed by the magnificent beaches, delighted by the carefully crafted, locally sourced cuisine through six unique restaurants, and excited by a range of activities including tours of the hydroponic farm, cooking classes, wine tastings, and golf clinics with PGA-certified professionals. Relaxation and rejuvenation abound at the award-winning Sorana Spa, and a thrilling challenge awaits golfers at Aurora International Golf Club’s stunning Greg Norman Signature Design 18-hole International Course and 9-hole short course, Avalon Links.

Experience Anguilla’s premier entertainment venue at the new Aurora Amphitheatre where guests can enjoy a state-of-the-art venue set against the picturesque backdrop of Cove Bay. The amphitheatre boasts a diverse range of events, performances, and movie nights. Indulge in delectable food trucks and stunning sunsets, all within the resort’s vicinity.

The new Aurora Entertainment Park features a world-class tennis center, pickleball courts, climbing wall, beach volleyball, clay bocce, basketball courts, and a 9-hole mini golf course. The stunning family-friendly pool complex features a splash pad, zero entry pool, lazy river, slide tower, and private and family cabanas. Poised to be the premier activity complex and amphitheatre in the Caribbean, we are pleased to share these offerings with our guests.

Our hospitality also extends beyond the resort. As good corporate citizens, we continuously look for ways to enhance the Anguillan community. Having a positive impact on our employees and island residents is just as important as creating an unrivaled experience for our guests. As part of our overarching commitment to our core values, we support initiatives that focus on youth, education and local culture. Doing well as a world-class resort means that we’re also doing good for the community.”

## A New Horizon

Nestled between land and sky, Aurora Anguilla® is pure paradise from the pristine sands to the panoramic water views along Rendezvous Beach. The natural beauty of Anguilla, the immersive local culture, and endless opportunities for discovery make the island an idyllic getaway.

## Experience Paradise

Whether rest and rejuvenation at the refreshing holistic Sorana Spa or finding adventure the island way that speaks to you, you’ll find it amongst our over 380 acres of alluring paradise. 2.5 miles of beachfront accommodations ensure a blissful stay and our seven exceptional restaurants and bars are ripe for discovering new culinary horizons that satisfy and tantalize.

## A Golf Dreamscape

The ultimate golf experience in the Caribbean with breathtaking views and challenging, dramatic holes. As the one and only golf course on the island, our Greg Norman-designed 18-Hole International Course and Avalon Links short course provide a thrilling setting for an unforgettable golf excursion.

## Connect with Anguilla

Discover the undiscovered at your home away from home where the vibrant local culture is always warm and joyful, welcoming you to the island for an unforgettable experience. Here, we celebrate an authentic connection to Anguilla where local flavors inform our culinary offerings, the natural splendor of the island shapes your day’s journey, and hidden gems spark adventures that cultivate your own deep connection with Anguilla.
