### Merrywing Bay

A tranquil stretch of beach located directly in front of Aurora Anguilla. Known for its calm waters, golden sand, and stunning sunsets — perfect for swimming, paddleboarding, or a romantic beach stroll.

### Clayton J. Lloyd International Airport (AXA)

Anguilla’s main airport, located just minutes from Aurora Anguilla. Offers direct and connecting flights from San Juan, St. Maarten, and other Caribbean hubs — making arrival on the island seamless and scenic.

### Blowing Point Ferry Terminal

The island’s primary ferry port, connecting Anguilla with St. Maarten and other nearby islands. Visitors often arrive here before heading up to Aurora — just a short, picturesque drive away.

### Rendezvous Bay

One of Anguilla’s most beautiful and expansive beaches, located just next to Aurora Anguilla. Famous for its turquoise waters and breathtaking views of St. Martin across the channel — ideal for lounging, swimming, and beach walks.

### Aurora Anguilla

Home to beautiful beaches, luxury accommodations, the only golf course on the island, activity center, water park and so much more.
