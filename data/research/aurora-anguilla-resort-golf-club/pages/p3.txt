# Dining at   Aurora Anguilla®

#### LOCALLY SOURCES CULINARY HOSPITALITY

Embark on a unique culinary journey at Aurora Anguilla, where you’ll find six diverse dining opportunities, each offering a different experience from casual to upscale. Our menus are crafted by talented chefs, drawing inspiration from the island’s local flavors and our 10,000-square-foot hydroponic gardens. These gardens provide fresh produce, herbs, and fruits every day.

At Aurora Anguilla, we prioritize sustainability by using hydroponics to conserve water and enhance ingredient quality. Our garden harvests influence our dishes and cocktails, ensuring a memorable dining experience.

Share your moments with #FlavorsofAurora on social media

## Chef’s Table

Chef’s Table is the ideal start or end of the day, and for leisurely moments in between. Fresh-baked pastries and family-style feasts prepared in an open kitchen and wood-burning oven feel as if you’re in the warmth of a friend’s home. Look forward to classic favorites with local ingredients harvested daily.

## C Level

Beachside bites and sips have never tasted better. With the sun shining down and your feet in the sand, enjoy an array of fresh and flavorful favorites that leave you feeling nourished.

Learn More

## D Richard’s

Located at the Aurora International Golf Club, D Richards is the best (and only) steakhouse in Anguilla. Uniquely luxurious yet approachable, it’s the perfect place to unwind and refuel between rounds of golf or treat yourself to a superb ribeye expertly paired with a rare vintage. Grab breakfast before teeing off, or relax with a refreshing spritz and small plates at the peak of day.

Learn More

## Tokyo Bay

Tokyo Bay is unlike any other experience in Anguilla. It’s the vision of renowned Executive Chef Joe Richardson whose deep respect and study of Japanese culture inspired this authentic, modern take on classic Japanese cuisine. Explore a robust menu of fresh sushi and sashimi, delectable teppanyaki and Japanese steak made with the finest seafood sourced from all over the world.

Learn More

## Sole di Mare

Sole di Mare invites you on a culinary journey across the Mediterranean coast. From handmade pastas and pizzas to vibrant dishes inspired by the South of France, Greece, Spain, and Morocco, each plate celebrates sun-kissed flavors and fresh ingredients. Enjoy your meal while taking in the stunning view of the turquoise-blue sea and the neighboring island of Saint Martin.

Learn More

## Rendezvous Café

Indulge in the perfect harmony of flavors at Rendezvous Café, where every moment is a treat. Savor a curated selection of hot and cold beverages, decadent cakes, freshly baked pastries, artisanal bread, and creamy homemade ice cream, all crafted to delight your senses. Whether you’re starting your day with a bold espresso or indulging in a sweet escape, Rendezvous Café offers a cozy yet stylish ambiance that’s ideal for any occasion.

Learn More

## Merrywing Café

Merrywing Café is your go-to spot for delicious bites and sweet treats for the whole family. Enjoy a delightful selection of hot and cold beverages, freshly baked pastries, waffles, homemade ice creams, and savory items, all crafted to please every palate. Whether you’re starting the day with a warm coffee or treating yourself to something sweet, Merrywing Café offers a welcoming atmosphere with cozy, stylish charm.

Learn More

## Aurora’s Organic & Hydroponic Farm

Our self-sustaining hydroponic farm is the backbone of the resort’s exquisite dining experience. Every morning, our chefs are inspired by the farm’s freshest vegetables, fruit and herbs of the day. Immerse yourself in its natural beauty with an exclusive private dining party under the stars or a stroll through the gardens.

Learn More

## Bars & Lounges

Unwind in style at the Merrywing and Rendezvous lobby bars, where guests can enjoy handcrafted cocktails, refreshing drinks, and delicious small bites in a warm, stylish setting that makes every gathering unforgettable.

Dine from the comfort of your Anguillian home away from home. With Aurora’s in-room dining selection, you’re free to enjoy delectable breakfast, lunch and dinner — prepared fresh with local ingredients — without lifting a finger. Or leaving your accommodations.
