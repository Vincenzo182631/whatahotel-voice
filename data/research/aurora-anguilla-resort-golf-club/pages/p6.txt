# Make a Splash in Your Secluded Playground

Rediscover adventure at Aurora Anguilla®, where we offer an array of activities and events designed for guests of all ages. For pricing and availability regarding all activities, please reach out to Experiences@AuroraAnguilla.com.

## Diving

The crystal-clear waters of Anguilla offer an incredible underwater experience with vibrant coral reefs and fascinating marine life. The island is home to a variety of dive sites, ranging from shallow coral gardens to intriguing shipwrecks like the El Buen Consejo, a 1772 Spanish galleon. Anguilla’s warm, calm seas make it ideal for divers of all skill levels, while encounters with colorful tropical fish, sea turtles, and even rays create unforgettable moments.
