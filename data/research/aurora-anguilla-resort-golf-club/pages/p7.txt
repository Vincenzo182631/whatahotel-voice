# Experiences

From underwater adventures to island wellness, Aurora Anguilla’s curated experiences—Diving Excursions, Pickleball Trips, Wellness Retreats, Sailing Adventures, and Festive Season celebrations—invite you to explore, play, and unwind in unforgettable ways.

## Watersports

_Freedom on the open sea._

Cruise Anguilla’s turquoise waters, explore hidden coves, and watch the sun dip below the horizon—pure island escape.

Sailing Adventure

## Diving Excursions

_Discover coral gardens and underwater wonders beneath the turquoise waves._

Explore vibrant reefs, shipwrecks, and marine life with guided dives through Anguilla’s crystal-clear waters. Each excursion blends adventure and tranquility beneath the surface.

Diving Excursions

## Pickleball Trips

_Where sport meets sun, sea, and spirited connection._

Enjoy daily matches, clinics, and friendly play on our ocean-view courts. A perfect mix of competition, connection, and Caribbean leisure.

Pickleball Trips

## Wellness Retreat

_A restorative journey inspired by the rhythms of the island._

Recenter with yoga, oceanfront meditation, and restorative spa rituals. Every detail is designed to bring peace and renewal.

Wellness Retreat

## Horseback Riding

_An unforgettable riding experience along the shores of the Caribbean_

Whether you’re trotting along powder-soft sands or into the warm, crystal-clear Caribbean waters, this is a truly unforgettable experience.

Wellness Retreat

## Wine Tastings

_Discover the world of wine, one exceptional pour at a time_

Explore celebrated wine regions, distinctive varietals, and inspired pairings through intimate, sommelier-led tastings in the elegant setting of D Richard’s Wine Cellar.

Wellness Retreat

## Sushi Making Class

_Master the art of sushi with the flavors of the island_

Create, roll, and savor your way through an intimate, chef-led experience featuring fresh seafood, Aurora’s farm-grown produce, and breathtaking views of Rendezvous Bay.
