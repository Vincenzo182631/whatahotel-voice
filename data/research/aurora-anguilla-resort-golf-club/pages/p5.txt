# The International Course named Caribbean's Golf Resort of the Year

Aurora Anguilla®  Golf Resort proudly hosts the island’s exclusive links golf courses, featuring the acclaimed Greg Norman Golf Course Design International Course, recently honored as the Caribbean’s Golf Resort of the Year. As you step up to the tee box on the 384-yard hole, you’ll be greeted by an awe-inspiring panorama of St. Martin and the Caribbean Sea. Prepare for a round that’s as exhilarating as it is demanding at our golf resort.

PAR 72 \| 7,165 YARDS \| BREATHTAKING VIEWS

## Explore the Course

The 18th hole is a test of skill, known as one of the most challenging finishing holes at a golf court resort in the Caribbean. It begins with a demanding tee shot over the salt pond, followed by an ascent of forty feet to reach a narrow green meticulously guarded by three imposing bunkers.

## Elevate Your Game at Aurora International Golf Academy

Our golf resort offers golf courses at our academy with cutting-edge technology, Trackman equipment, and expert instruction to identify and improve your swing mechanics. With two instructional bays equipped with Trackman simulators, pressure mats, 3-D motion analysis, and Trackman 4 ball-flight launch monitors, our golf academy welcomes golfers of all levels and ages to the best golf resort.

AVALON LINKS \| PERFECT FOR FAMILIES

## Discover Our Unique Short Course

Tucked among the rolling hills with a picturesque bay view, our exceptional links golf course invites seasoned golfers to unleash their creativity and introduce newcomers to the game’s delights and challenges. Experience an enticing blend of short and medium-length par-threes golf courses, complemented by a 300-yard hole, offering the perfect campo de golf for a range of shot-making skills.
