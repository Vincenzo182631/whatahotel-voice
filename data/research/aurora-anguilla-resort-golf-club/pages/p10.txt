## ADDITIONAL FLIGHT OPTIONS

No matter where you’re visiting from, arrive with ease. Anguilla is easily accessible with direct commercial flights from Miami or a short transfer upon arrival from major cities across the nation through St. Maarten (SXM) and now San Juan (SJU).

### COMMERCIAL FLIGHTS

There are many daily flights from across the United States, Europe, and South America that provide convenient access to Anguilla via the neighboring island of St. Maarten (SXM). As flight routes continue to expand, travelers can also reach Anguilla with connecting flights through San Juan, Puerto Rico (SJU).

New flight options now include routes from Newark (United Airlines), Tampa, and Orlando (JetBlue) to Anguilla, connecting through San Juan.

Flights routes to Anguilla.

Direct commercial flights are available daily through Miami (MIA) to Anguilla (AXA) with American Airlines.

### BERMUDAIR NOW PROVIDING AIR SERVICE FROM TRI-STATE AREA

BermudAir, through its new subsidiary AnguillAir, will begin service from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Anguilla’s Clayton J. Lloyd International Airport (AXA) starting December 18, 2025. Flights will operate twice weekly on Thursdays and Sundays, providing direct access to Anguilla from the New York Tri-State Area.

#### KEY DETAILS

- Airline: BermudAir (operating as AnguillAir)
- Departure: Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR)
- Arrival: Clayton J. Lloyd International Airport (AXA)
- Start Date: December 18, 2025
- Frequency: Twice weekly, on Thursdays and Sundays

#### HOW TO BOOK

Tickets can be booked directly through the BermudAir website at FlyBermudAir.com

### AIR TRANSFER OPTIONS TO AND FROM ST. MAARTEN

#### MEET & GREET SERVICE

Upon arrival into Anguilla, you will complete the Immigration and Customs process. Once you exit the building into the Arrival Hall, our Greeter will gladly escort you to the next available taxi or your pre-arranged private ground transfer.

**Shared Air Charter** – These flights are scheduled flights priced $156.55 USD per person each way, inclusive of St. Maarten’s transit tax of $64.00 USD. The departure tax for Anguilla is $28.00 USD and will be collected at the Clayton J. Lloyd International Airport.

**Private Air Charter** – These flights can be scheduled for your convenience between the hours of 7:15 am and 6:00 pm. Private charters are $535.00 USD which covers up to 7 passengers with luggage. The departure tax for St. Maarten is $64.00 USD per person and Anguilla’s tax is $28.00 USD per person and is paid locally.

Anguilla Air Services,  facilitates these transfers. These transfers can be booked directly with Anguilla Air Services or via the hotel and added to your room folio. The flight spans approximately 15 minutes. Anguilla Air Servicesis the entity assisting with shared or private air charter flights.  Please contact Anguilla Air Services directly for these charter air transportation options and for fare information.  Anguilla Air Services also can assist in booking your  accommodations at Aurora Anguilla®.

## WATER TRANSFER OPTIONS

### WATER TRANSFER OPTIONS TO AND FROM ST. MAARTEN

#### MEET & GREET SERVICE

Upon arrival into the Princess Juliana International Airport and after completing the Immigration process, you will collect your luggage and exit into the Arrival Hall. An Aurora Anguilla® representative will be on the outside of the Arrival Hall to meet and greet you and may be easily identified by the “Aurora Anguilla® Resort & Golf Club” sign. The agent will assist with your luggage and transportation to the dock. At the dock, a boat agent will collect your passport to register your details for your boat transfer. Prior to boarding, you will need to be cleared by an Immigration officer at the dock.

Once you have arrived in Anguilla, you will proceed to the Immigration & Customs departments. Once cleared, you will exit the Arrival Hall where our Greeter will be eagerly awaiting you and will be holding an “Aurora Anguilla® Resort & Golf Club” sign. You will then be escorted to your taxi or pre-arranged private ground transfer to the resort.

#### SHARED SEA SHUTTLE

This option can be prearranged and departs at scheduled times, starting at 9:00am and ending at 5:45pm.

$121.00 per person each way

$55.00 per child each way ages 3 to 11

Complimentary for children under 2

A tax of $36.00 per person is required to be paid at the Blowing Point Ferry Terminal for all departing persons and is payable to the Government of Anguilla. For our guests’ convenience, when selecting the above boat option as your departure transfer option, the tax will be added to your room folio and paid for upon check out.

#### PRIVATE SEA CHARTER

This option can be pre-arranged and departs at your convenience between the hours of 7:00 am and 6:00pm. Private boat can accommodate up to 12 passengers.

$600.00 for the first 4 persons each way ($75.00 per additional person)\*

Complimentary for children ages 0 to 2

A tax of $36.00 per person is required to be paid at the Blowing Point Ferry Terminal for all departing persons and is payable to the Government of Anguilla. For our guests’ convenience, when selecting the above boat option as your departure transfer option, the tax will be added to your room folio and paid for upon check out.

## GROUND TRANSFER OPTIONS

### GROUND TRANSPORTATION TAXIS

Taxis are readily available at the Blowing Point Ferry Terminal and the Clayton J Lloyd International Airport (AXA) ports of entry. Rates are issued by zones and are based on the number of persons and the number of luggage pieces.The first two individuals and the first two pieces of luggage are approximately $30.00 USD. An additional extra $5.00 USD for each additional person and $2.00 USD for each additional piece of luggage.

### PRIVATE GROUND TRANSFERS

Private ground transfers can be pre-arranged and require at least 24-hour advance notice.

Pre-arranged Taxi: $40.00 per trip from the Blowing Point Ferry Terminal, $45.00 per trip from the AXA Airport for a party of 2. Each additional person is $6.00, accommodates up to 5 passengers.

Transit Van: $95.00 per trip from the ferry terminal, accommodates up to 10 passengers.

Towncar: $105.00 per trip, accommodates up to 3 passengers

Large SUV: $205.00 per trip, accommodates up to 6 passengers

Large Luggage Van: $95.00 per trip
