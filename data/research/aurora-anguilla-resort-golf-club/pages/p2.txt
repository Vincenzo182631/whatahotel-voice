Rooms & Accommodations

## Four Distinguished Experiences, One Renowned Resort.

## The Tower

Contemporary, spacious King & Double rooms flooded with natural light, provide an immersive experience with sweeping views in a prime location with easy access to the beach, water park, and golf. Seasonal offers ensure a vibrant and enjoyable stay for family members of all ages.

### VIEW DETAILS

## Merrywing Beach

Merrywing Beach offers an elevated, multi bedroom sea-front experience just steps from the beach and a large family pool. Accommodations include a collection of flexible suites, as well as private estate homes with extraordinary living spaces.

### VIEW DETAILS

## Rendezvous Beach

Rendezvous Beach at Aurora Anguilla® is an intimate, stylish, and serene enclave within Aurora, offering an elevated experience along a wide expanse of beautiful beach, featuring elegant sea-front suites and multi-bedroom villas, a sweeping pool, the relaxing Sorana Spa and well-equipped fitness center, organic and hydroponic gardens, and exquisite convenient dining experiences at Chef’s Table, C Level, Tokyo Bay and the Lobby Bar.

### VIEW DETAILS

## Estates & Villas

Estate guests can lounge by their private swimming pools or roam the ample interior and exterior spaces for a private vacation experience on the Caribbean’s most exclusive island. Amenities include access to personalized concierge, housekeeping, and more.
