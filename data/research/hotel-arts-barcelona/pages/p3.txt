Discover Our Suites With Panoramic Sea and City Views
Signature Suites
Suspended between sea and skyline, the suites unfold as luminous sanctuaries where space, light and perspective come together in effortless harmony. Our Skyline and Horizon Suites, shift towards a palette of refined greens inspired by the surrounding Tibidabo and Montjuïc mountains, framing the city through sweeping panoramic views, inviting a constant dialogue between city and sea, as they overlook the most iconic landmarks of the city, such as the Sagrada Familia. Open, fluid interiors balance elegant living with moments of quiet retreat, evoking the feeling of a private residence above the city, this corner-positioned suites are created for those who value privacy, artistry and a profound sense of place. At its pinnacle, The Arts Suite redefines the experience entirely, an expansive, light-filled retreat where contemporary luxury, wellbeing and exclusivity converge in a seamless expression of refined living.
Mediterranean Way of Living
The Arts Suite
Rising above Barcelona with 180-degree views of the Mediterranean and the city, The Arts Suite embodies the pinnacle of contemporary luxury at Hotel Arts Barcelona. Spanning 210 square meters, it offers distinct day and night zones, combining expansive elegance with hyper-personalised service and exclusive access to The Club.

Endless Mediterranean Views
Horizon Suite
Wake to the Mediterranean at your feet. From 80 sqm, the Horizon Sea View Suite occupies a privileged corner position with open-plan interiors and sweeping sea-facing views. Floor-to-ceiling windows fill the suite with natural light, transforming every moment into a living coastal panorama. Flowing seamlessly between the bedroom, living space and bathroom, the suite evokes a refined private residence above the shoreline. A dressing and vanity area, deep soaking bath, rain shower, personal wellness corner, integrated technology and a curated en-suite bar complete a stay designed for space, privacy and an extraordinary connection to the Mediterranean.

Skyline Suite
Sagrada Família views take centre stage in the Skyline Suites, where curated furnishings, thoughtful design elements, integrated technology and dedicated wellness touches create an elevated sense of retreat. Behind the sleeping area, a beautifully integrated dressing and vanity space introduces a more intimate, residential layer, balancing the openness of the setting with a sense of privacy and ritual.
