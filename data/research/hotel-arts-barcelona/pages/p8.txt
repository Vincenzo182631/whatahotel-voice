A Five-star Beach Resort in the Heart of Barcelona
Experiences
Standing on the edge of the Mediterranean, its soaring steel-and-glass exterior a landmark on the city skyline, Hotel Arts Barcelona embraces both the art and the vitality of its surroundings. It is a luxury beach hotel, and is a sophisticated city-center destination. It invites guests to relax amid spacious gardens and a waterfront swimming pool, and it enables guests to explore Barcelona’s vibrant cultural attractions.
Take advantage of this luxury beach hotel’s enviable Barcelona address to create a stay tailored to your interests. Spend the day at Gaudí’s Parc Guell and La Sagrada Familia, or relax over tapas, rooftop cocktails and leisurely strolls along the beach. From fine dining and upscale shopping to uninterrupted relaxation by the sea, Barcelona awaits discovery from this perfectly located luxury resort.

Children's Activities
A special menu for children is available in our restaurants, to suit their taste while offering them to discover local savors. The menu is handed to them along with a drawing book to entertain them throughout the meal.
In our Club Lounge, the food and beverage presentations always include special touches to please younger guests, such as hot or cold chocolate, large selections of fruits, chocolates or sweets. From Port Olimpic to the Best of Barcelona
With its seafront location near the Port Olimpic marina, Hotel Arts Barcelona acts as a gateway for quintessential Barcelona experiences. Visitors can spend the day on the beach as easily as they can lose themselves in the bustle of La Boqueria food market or the whimsical architecture of Antoni Gaudí at Parc Guell. From museums and architecture to cultural must-sees like La Sagrada Familia, the best of Barcelona is just moments away.

Mercado de la Boquería
Undoubtedly the most famous market in Barcelona, la Boqueria was founded in 1840 as a small pig market and today boasts over 250 stands, including many casual restaurants and bars where you can taste the very best Catalan and regional Spanish cuisine.
Well known for the incredible selection of produce, fresh seasonal vegetables, and Iberian cold cuts - among others –, it is today one of the finest outdoor markets, a popular spot to stop for lunch, and was recently recognized as the “best fresh market” in the world by CNN.
La Barceloneta
The neighbourhood covering the ports and beaches, La Barceloneta, was once a fishermen neighbourhood. Today, you will find seafront restaurants, casual beachfront bars and cozy bistros, many specialising in seafood and fish dishes.
Mercado de Santa Caterina
A fascinating market from its appearance, with a wavy and colourful roof from its 2005 rebuild, the Santa Caterina market is hard to be missed. The modern space - known for its quality fish, meat and vegetable stalls - it’s the go-to market for locals and hosts a variety of interesting restaurants and bars featuring modern Catalan dishes.
Bonus point: it’s located a couple of minutes’ walk from the cathedral, so it will make for a natural stop in your sightseeing tour.
