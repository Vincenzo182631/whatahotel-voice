Suites & Rooms
Discover refined comfort in each of the 432 spacious guestrooms and suites at Hotel Arts Barcelona. Starting from 45 sqm, every room is a masterful blend of contemporary design and Mediterranean charm, thoughtfully curated for the discerning traveller.
Floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interiors with natural light, highlighting bespoke furnishings, rich textiles, and sustainable materials that reflect our commitment to eco-conscious living. Enjoy panoramic views of the sea, the city skyline, or both. Each one offering a unique perspective of Barcelona’s vibrant spirit. Whether you are seeking a romantic escape or a serene city retreat, our luxury accommodations provide an elevated experience of comfort, elegance, and sophistication.

Hotel Arts Barcelona
Design Inspiration
Embodying the spirit of Barcelona, where the city meets the sea, our design draws from the Mediterranean’s rhythmic waves. Fluid curves blend with bold geometric lines in a harmonious contrast. A palette inspired by ocean, sunlight, and mountains fills each light-filled room, reflecting Barcelona’s seamless mix of natural beauty and urban sophistication.
Artistry
Art and craftsmanship define each room at Hotel Arts Barcelona, where every detail reflects the city’s creative spirit. Handcrafted elements, like exclusive headboards by Lorena Canals, blend with bold Mediterranean colors, rich textures, and iconic patterns. A fusion of tradition and contemporary design, each room feels like a personal gallery.
A Look Into the Future
Every detail in our rooms is crafted with sustainability in mind. Designed using responsibly sourced materials, they offer eco-conscious luxury without compromise. Exclusive amenities support local artisans and green initiatives. From energy-efficient lighting to plastic-free touches, each space reflects our commitment to a conscious future.
Inspiring Suites
Experience the refined elegance of Hotel Arts Barcelona, where nature and resort style living come together in our premium accommodation. Each suite offers generous space, stunning views, and organic hues inspired by the nearby mountains. A palette of earthy tones and rich textures creates a serene and luxurious atmosphere, complemented by advanced technology and thoughtfully selected amenities. Positioned at the corners of the building, our Skyline City View and Horizon Sea View suites feature open plan layouts that connect bedroom, living area, complimentary drinks bar, and spacious bathroom. Two exclusive signature suites, located on the upper floors, reveal breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. Guests enjoy an elevated experience with personalized hospitality including return airport transfers, a private cocktail station, wellness corner, sleep kit, and more.
City View
Skyline Suite
Inspired by its Mediterranean setting, the Skyline City View Suite at Hotel Arts Barcelona offers an exceptional corner vantage with sweeping views of the city through oversized windows. Spanning 80 square meters, the suite includes a spacious master bedroom, a separate living room, and a custom drinks bar. Furnished in natural earthy tones that reflect the nearby mountains, the space blends textures, organic forms, and luxurious materials to create a serene retreat shaped by refined hospitality. Guests enjoy premium features such as a complimentary minibar with cocktail making tools, a wellness corner, bedside room controls, and integrated sound systems. Two sliding walls frame the ensuite bathroom, allowing smooth flow between the bedroom and vanity area with privacy when desired. The lounge is designed for relaxation and connection, with comfortable seating placed to enjoy the spectacular views and natural light, offering a truly elevated living experience.

Sea View
Horizon Suite
Located mid tower, the Horizon Sea View Suites occupy a corner position that fully embraces panoramic views of the sparkling Mediterranean Sea. A generous floorplan with dual aspect and large windows invites abundant natural light, while earthy tones inspired by the surrounding Catalan mountains enhance the sense of calm and connection to nature. Textural materials and custom furnishings complement modern comforts, including an integrated sound system and bedside room controls. Spanning over 80 square meters, the suite offers a spacious master bedroom, separate living area, bespoke drinks bar, and a dedicated wellness corner. The ensuite bathroom is cleverly placed for seamless access from both the bedroom and dressing area, with sliding walls on two sides for flexibility and privacy. Sustainably sourced materials and organically shaped fixtures pay tribute to the natural landscape. Expect exceptional views, thoughtful design, and outstanding hospitality throughout.
