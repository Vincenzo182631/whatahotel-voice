The Hotel Arts Barcelona Club Experience
The Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” the Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
The Reimagination Journey

Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
