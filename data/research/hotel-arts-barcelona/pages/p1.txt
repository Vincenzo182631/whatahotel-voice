Welcome to Hotel Arts Barcelona
BEAUTIFUL CONTRADICTIONS MAKE THE IRRESISTIBLE COMPOSITION
A beachfront haven for those seeking culture and relaxation without compromise, Hotel Arts Barcelona balances the seemingly impossible, combining city hotel and beach resort, boutique feel and generous footprint. As Europe’s only artistic urban resort, it is a celebration of art and an encounter of calm, colour and creativity. Each luxurious guestroom and suite is pleasingly spacious, with natural light streaming through generous windows and views that target the Med, urban or both. Gastronomic indulgence is sated at the culinary spectrum of restaurants serving two-starred Michelin specialties and age-old Catalan dishes. Outside, the lush, landscaped gardens are a tranquil oasis, overlooked by infinity and family pools, while Arts Wellness further extends the Mediterranean pace of life. An iconic skyline landmark, this is where you can escape the city without leaving it, elevated by the hotel’s renowned service culture.
The Reimagination Journey is Entering its Final Phase
Following the transformation of our rooms and suites, meeting spaces and public areas, the final stage of this project will continue from now through June 2027. Over the coming months, selected indoor and outdoor spaces will experience temporary and partial closures as we complete this next chapter of Hotel Arts Barcelona. We are deeply grateful for your continued trust and understanding throughout this journey. As we look ahead to what is to come, our commitment remains unchanged: to ensure every stay at Hotel Arts Barcelona is defined by genuine care, thoughtful service and the attention to detail our guests have come to expect.

Merge with the Seaside Way of Living - Our Pools
At our artistic urban resort, every moment by the water is designed around a different way of experiencing the Mediterranean. Whether seeking quality time with family or a peaceful retreat above the city, the resort's two outdoor pools offer a setting to suit every occasion. The Family Pool provides a welcoming space where guests of all ages can relax together, surrounded by landscaped gardens and overlooking the Mediterranean. For a more tranquil escape, the Gehry Pool is an adults-only infinity pool offering an elevated sanctuary with sweeping views of the Mediterranean and Frank Gehry's iconic Peix sculpture. A perfect spot to enjoy the sunny days of the city, the sea and Barcelona's distinctive coastal skyline.

In the Heart of Barcelona
A Five-Star Artistic Urban Resort
Overlooking the Mediterranean, Hotel Arts Barcelona offers a distinctive artistic urban resort experience where the energy of the city meets the tranquility of the coast. Ideally located between the beachfront and Barcelona's cultural heart, the hotel invites guests to experience the city's unique contrasts, from the Mediterranean shoreline to iconic landmarks such as the Sagrada Familia. From relaxing by two outdoor pools to exploring the city's vibrant attractions, every stay is designed around effortless luxury. The family pool provides a welcoming setting for guests of all ages, complemented by the Arts Kids programme, where curated activities and child-friendly amenities create memorable moments for the youngest members of the family.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at Hotel Arts Barcelona?
The check-in time at Hotel Arts Barcelona is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does Hotel Arts Barcelona allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at Hotel Arts Barcelona.
What are the parking options at Hotel Arts Barcelona?
On-site parking is available at Hotel Arts Barcelona. Self parking is available at Hotel Arts Barcelona, with rates of €10.00 per hour, and €60.50 per day. Valet parking is available for €60.50 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does Hotel Arts Barcelona have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, Hotel Arts Barcelona has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your Hotel Arts Barcelona booking.
What is the closest airport near Hotel Arts Barcelona?
The closest airport near Hotel Arts Barcelona is Barcelona-El Prat Airport (BCN). BCN is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.
Does Hotel Arts Barcelona have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, Hotel Arts Barcelona does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does Hotel Arts Barcelona have a Club Lounge?
Yes, Hotel Arts Barcelona offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
