Michelin-Starred Innovation in Barcelona
At Enoteca Paco Pérez, creativity and simple elegance define every dish. This acclaimed two Michelin-starred restaurant offers one of Barcelona’s most refined culinary journeys. Chef Paco Pérez brings his philosophy to life in each creation, highlighting the freshness of the sea and the essence of farm and mountain produce.
Rooted in the authentic spirit of Mediterranean cuisine, the seasonally inspired menu is thoughtfully paired with a curated selection of over 700 wines, enhancing every flavor and elevating the dining experience.

Marina Coastal Club
Discover Marina Coastal Club, Hotel Arts Barcelona’s signature multi-space outdoor venue. Entirely al fresco and spanning over 1,500 sq meters of Mediterranean charm, it overlooks Frank Gehry’s iconic ‘Fish’ sculpture and invites you to eat, drink, socialize or simply unwind at your own pace.
Featuring two state-of-the-art swimming pools and an exquisite restaurant, Marina Coastal Club operates from early morning to late evening. Guests can enjoy an extensive yet thoughtfully curated culinary and beverage offering, all complemented by a handpicked selection of live music performances that enhance the vibrant atmosphere.
Marina Coastal Food
Overlooking the Mediterranean Sea and with a distinctive chiringuito flair, this restaurant is the epitome of summer ‘by the beach’ dining. Enclosed by Marina’s pools and framed by palm trees, the spacious terrace of Marina offers a wide variety of elegant seafood dishes inspired by the local Mediterranean culture of tapas, all of them designed to be shared, enhancing daytime eating with an informal quality.
Marina Family Pool
Open every day during summer season and exclusive to hotel guests, is located in a quiet area where both children and adults can relax swimming or sunbathing while enjoying the best views over the Mediterranean Sea and the Olympic Port. Offering a wide selection of comfort food and fresh salads combined with house press juices and cocktails that you can enjoy on the comfort of your sun lounger.
Gehry Pool
Open daily during the summer season and exclusively for adult guests, the Gehry pool offers a serene escape above the city, with breathtaking views of Frank Gehry’s iconic Peix and the Mediterranean skyline. A calm, elevated setting designed for relaxation and quiet indulgence, where guests can unwind while enjoying the sun, the water, and the unique Mediterranean beauty of Barcelona.

Bites & Sips
Hidden Coffee Roasters
At Bites & Sips we’re proud to serve Hidden Coffee Roasters, a celebrated local specialty coffee brand roasted right here in Barcelona. Known for sourcing exceptional beans from around the world and roasting them in-house to highlight their unique character, Hidden Coffee Roasters brings craftsmanship and passion to every cup. Whether bright and floral or rich and complex, each cup reflects a dedication to quality and traceability that resonates with coffee lovers and complements our culinary experience.

Enoteca Paco Pérez
Terres de Llum
Experience Terres de Llum at Enoteca Paco Pérez, a premium extra virgin olive oil crafted in total darkness to preserve its pure, vibrant taste—the true essence of the Mediterranean. Made from carefully selected native olives and harvested at their peak, it reveals a perfect balance of sweet and bitter notes, with delicate, complex aromas that elevate every dish.
More than an ingredient, Terres de Llum is a tribute to local craftsmanship and terroir—a refined, exclusive taste you can savor only at Enoteca Paco Pérez.

Off-Premises Catering
The culinary and hospitality expertise of Hotel Arts Barcelona extends beyond the hotel’s walls. For events anywhere in the city, our catering team at Arts Catering brings their talent and creativity to craft bespoke experiences. Every menu is fully customized to suit your chosen theme, cuisine or tradition, supported by the impeccable service of our dedicated team, ensuring a seamless and exceptional event from start to finish.
