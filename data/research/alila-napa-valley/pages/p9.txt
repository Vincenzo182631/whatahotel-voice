Alila Napa Valley
Bespoke activities and easy access to vineyards await at this Wine Country oasis located just over a mile from downtown St. Helena.

Best for:

Location:

Can’t-miss feature:
Many of the rooms abut a working vineyard, so it's hard to miss the sweeping views of Beringer Winery and the surrounding Napa Valley peaks
In a nutshell:
Alila Napa Valley is effortless luxury. Think of it as a one-stop destination for top-notch wellness, incredible vino, and unique custom experiences in California’s Wine Country.
Jennie Nunn
Rooms
There are 64 guest rooms and 4 suites. Most include decks or private patios with firepits ignited by the turn of a switch. (The four rooms in the main historic building—an all-white Georgian Revival–style mansion dating back to 1905 that also houses the two on-site restaurants—do not have outdoor fireplaces.)
Designed by notable furniture and interior designer
Yabu Pushelberg
(whose projects also include
JW Marriott Hotel Tokyo
), rooms are awash in warm gray tones and feature upholstered linen beds. I stayed in a King Vineyard View Balcony room, and I felt like I had instantly moved to a well-appointed villa in Wine Country—without the hassle of lugging any furniture or belongings. Bathrooms are fashioned with hand-selected tile and sinks meticulously crafted by a stable of Italian artisans (who flew in and lived on-site during installation). Guests will also find Le Labo amenities and separate powder rooms with Toto toilets. Other in-room perks include iPads for ordering in-room dining and scheduling spa services and activities, floor-to-ceiling black-out curtains for optimal shut-eye, and mini fridges for your own wine and beverages.
Jennie Nunn
Jennie Nunn
Food and drink
Strong attention to detail is evident here. Salvia Terrace & Bar is open daily for breakfast and lunch, while Violetto serves dinner Wednesday through Sunday. For a caffeine fix paired with handmade confections, indulge in the Dolce Vita Afternoon Tea on weekends. There’s also a non-alcoholic bar (Napa’s only dedicated one) showcasing a new spirit-free menu—though there is the option to add the liquor of your choice should you so desire. My favorite zero-proof concoction was the Salvia Citrus, with agave, grapefruit, lime, citrus soda, and Tajín.
At Violetto, overseen by chef Mark Shoemaker, you can choose from flexible prix fixe dining experiences, including four- and eight-course tasting menus. Server Colin Pring will astutely help you navigate the menu and advise on his favorite dishes and wines. To start, be sure to order the sea salt–drizzled focaccia della Violetto. As tempting as it will be to eat the whole loaf, be sure to save room.
Jennie Nunn
Amenities and experiences
Offerings include an outdoor swimming pool lined with cabanas, a 24-hour fitness center and yoga studio, and an array of complimentary and fee-based activities including tai chi classes, e-bike tours, wine tastings, and sound healing meditation. But what sets this property apart is the sheer variety (and volume) of bespoke rituals and experiences available to guests, like personalized astrology readings, falconry, custom fragrance-making sessions with
FLWR Napa Valley
, and guided forest bathing in nearby
Bothe-Napa Valley State Park
. Another standout is “Pedal and Pour,” an eight-mile wine and cycling tour led by winemaker Ted Osborne of
Olabisi Wines
.
Don’t leave without visiting
Spa Alila
. I opted for the seasonal Harvest Cabernet Ritual (offered September through November), consisting of an exfoliating grapeseed scrub followed by a full-body customizable massage. It was one of the best treatments of my life thanks to my expert therapist, Amanda Sellai. In the retail boutique, browse a well-edited mix of apothecary goods like body butter by
Oleema Skincare
, detox drops by
Juna
, and floral bath soak blends by
Vertly
.
Jennie Nunn
Jennie Nunn
What to do nearby
Reserve a “Taste The View” guided wine tasting experience at
Beringer Winery
, accessible via the resort’s footbridge—dubbed the “Bridge to Beringer”—located by the fitness center. For shopping and dining, make a beeline for Main Street, which is lined with brick buildings dating to the 1900s that house high-end boutiques, galleries, and interior design troves spanning
Elysewalker,
Erin Martin Design, and
Caldwell Snyder Gallery
. At
Oliver Napa Valley
, pick up take-home gifts such as French milled soaps, lemon-infused olive oils, and artichoke caper tapenades. Winery hopping is also an option at nearby wineries like
Faust
,
Freemark Abbey
,
Brasswood
, and
Alpha Omega
. Just be sure to book in advance.
The backstory
Originally masterminded in 1905 by architect William Henry Corlett as a private residence, the main Georgian Revival–style building at the front entrance (which houses four guest rooms and the two primary restaurants) has undergone several transformations and iterations. In 1921, it became a 30-bed hospital, followed by stints as a retirement home and apartments. Additional buildings were added in 2017 when the property opened as a hotel, then named Las Alcobas Napa Valley. It transformed into Alila Napa Valley in 2021.
Insider tip
If your schedule permits, allot at least two days here. One day won’t cut it—especially if you plan on taking part in some of the resort’s experiences. Need a lift? A house car is available on a first-come, first-served basis for trips anywhere within a four-mile radius along Highway 29 or the Silverado Trail. Tyler Kelly, one of the jovial valets, will happily answer any questions or assist with luggage to the car.

No, this is an adult-oriented property
