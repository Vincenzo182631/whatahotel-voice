# Drink & Dine

Alila Napa Valley offers an elevated fine dining experience featuring local St. Helena ingredients and creative cuisine that appeals to any palate. Our culinary team curates unforgettable dishes from California’s finest organic and sustainable ingredients. Enjoy a craft cocktail from the hotel bar on our wraparound veranda and take in views of the Napa Valley vineyards, or get a taste of Italy at our signature restaurant Violetta.

## Violetto

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Violetto, our St. Helena hotel restaurant, blends the elegance of French culinary techniques with the warmth of Italian flavors for a refined dining experience. The prix fixe menu features handmade pasta and signature fine dining dishes like braised Rabbit Violetti. Vegetarian options highlight local Napa Valley ingredients. Peruse the menus and make reservations online.

## Salvia Terrace & Bar

Participating Restaurant

World of Hyatt members are eligible to earn points for qualifying purchases charged to their room at participating restaurants.

Salvia Terrace & Bar is a Napa Valley restaurant and indoor-outdoor bar offering a relaxed, vibrant dining experience. Open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, it features seasonal dishes like avocado toast, grilled vegetables and farm-to-table salads. A top brunch spot, perfect while wine tasting, with craft cocktails and cozy seating.
