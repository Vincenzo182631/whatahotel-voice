The Alila is supposed to be one of the best hotels in Napa Valley. It did not live up to the hype this time. We have stayed at this property several times. Our room was not clean upon check in. This started as a work trip so i tried not to be disappointed when the service was lackluster at check in and i got a room overlooking a stream/parking lot vs the vineyard.
During the event i over heard employees complaining about the group and how they thought all the flowers on the table were dumb cause they would just need to be moved. Super disrespectful to the event team who took so much time to make things look so beautiful.
Before I arrived I decided to stay an extra night on my own dime so I asked if I could be moved for my final night and maybe get a room overlooking the vineyard, the girl didn’t really know if that could happen and she said she’d reach out to me. Well then the day they reached out via text and were like we can help you move to your new room. So I was pretty excited and I hurried up and got all of my stuff ready to then only be told well your room now won’t be ready until 4 o’clock.
So then I had to go from noon until four without a room again. So since it was really hot, I decided that I would take the shuttle into the town to only be told by the valet team, but they were not running the shuttle and they were too busy. I asked when it would be running Or are there other options? And got a response that they were too busy… Like seriously?!? I go, but you’re busy, but maybe you could offer a guest that’s actually staying here some options.
The entire trip I have felt more of an inconvenience to the staff, then welcomed by the staff. I will not be returning and I do not recommend this hotel.
