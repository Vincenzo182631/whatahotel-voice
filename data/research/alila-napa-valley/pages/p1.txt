## Bask in Napa Valley’s Luxury and Tranquility

Tucked in the heart of Northern California’s wine country, steps from Beringer Vineyards and minutes to downtown St. Helena, Alila Napa Valley is a journey of epicurean discovery in a scenic landscape rich with culture and history. Thoughtfully designed with adults in mind, our luxury hotel offers expansive vineyard views and golden sunsets that showcase the region’s unspoiled beauty. Enjoy immersive, regionally inspired experiences and restore your mind and body with our spa’s extensive wellness offerings and rejuvenating treatments.

Pedal & Pour – A Winemaker’s Ride

Experience Napa Valley alongside the person who knows it best—a winemaker. Join Ted Osborne, founder and vintner of Olabisi Wines, for an intimate guided journey through the vineyards of the St Helena and Calistoga AVAs. This exclusive experience offers rare access to the people, places, and craftsmanship behind one of Napa Valley's most distinctive boutique wineries. Traveling by bike allows you to move at the pace of the vineyard, taking in the landscape while hearing firsthand how each vintage is shaped by the land, the season, and the decisions made in the cellar. Along the way, enjoy an exclusive barrel tasting at a celebrated winery, where you'll sample wines in their earliest expression and gain insight into the art of blending and aging. Midway through the journey, optionally savor a chef-prepared seasonal lunch at Violetto paired with a curated selection of Olabisi wines before continuing to the Olabisi tasting room in downtown Calistoga for a hosted tasting and celebratory final pour.

Your Experience  -

**Meet Your Winemaker**

Begin at Alila Napa Valley, where you'll be welcomed by Ted Osborne and fitted for your bike before an introduction to the day's route, the Calistoga AVA, and the vineyards you'll explore.

**Ride Through Wine Country**

Pedal the Napa Valley Vine Trail at a relaxed pace, stopping throughout the ride as Ted shares insights into vineyard practices, terroir, and the decisions that influence every bottle.

**Exclusive Barrel Tasting**

Step inside a working winery for a private barrel tasting not available during traditional tasting experiences, sampling wines before they are bottled while learning how they evolve in the cellar.

**Seasonal Lunch & Wine Pairing**

Optionally enjoy a chef-prepared lunch from Violetto thoughtfully paired with Olabisi wines, highlighting the harmony between local cuisine and Napa Valley wines.

**Hosted Tasting at Olabisi**

Conclude at Olabisi's downtown Calistoga tasting room for a guided tasting and final toast with Ted, reflecting on the day's discoveries while enjoying the winery's acclaimed small-production wines.

**Duration:** Approximately 3 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Comfortable, non-loose clothing and closed toe shoes.

**Price:** $349 (E-Bike included)

**Book Now**

**Additional Details:** Sunscreen and sunglasses are recommended. Hydrate in advance, though water bottles will be provided. Meet a few minutes early at Alila Napa Valley's Valet to pick out your bike and helmet and to meet your guide.

## Discover Alila Napa Valley

Pool

Set against the stunning Mayacamas Mountains, swim laps or lounge at our heated outdoor pool that features comfortable loungers and plenty of unobstructed sunshine.

Environmental Sustainability

Sustainability is the new luxury, and at Alila Napa Valley, we are deeply committed to minimizing our impact on the environment and preserving the way of life and traditions of the surrounding agricultural community. Guests are invited to participate in unique sustainability experiences during their visit.

Designed for the Senses

This Napa Valley resort, designed by the renowned team at Yabu Pushelberg, offers 64 luxuriously appointed rooms and suites that feature oversize balconies or wraparound terraces with outdoor fireplaces. Select rooms overlook the beautiful vineyard to the rear of the property or the iconic Beringer Winery next door. Rooms are crafted for comfort with luxe Italian linens, bespoke furnishings and floor-to-ceiling marble bathrooms to spoil the senses, while high-tech amenities provide convenience at a touch.

The Best of Napa Valley

Situated just steps from downtown St. Helena, the hotel offers unparalleled access to all the local, cultural delights, with more than 400 wineries and tasting rooms that call this picture-perfect town home. Between tastings, experience downtown’s many charms, from chic boutiques and contemporary art galleries to artisanal bakeries and Michelin-star restaurants.
