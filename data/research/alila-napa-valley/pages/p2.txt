# Napa Valley Suites & Accommodations \| Alila Napa Valley

# Accommodations

Check-In4:00 PM

Checkout11:00 AM

Alila Napa Valley’s 64 luxury hotel rooms and suites, infused with earthy tones, crisp whites and natural materials, feature floor-to-ceiling windows and sliding glass doors that let Napa’s pastoral beauty shine through. With artisan touches and interiors designed by the renowned team at Yabu Pushelberg, the rooms and suites include bespoke Italian furnishings, luxury linens and spa-inspired bathrooms with rain showers and soaking tubs. Most also offer a fire pit on the private balcony or wraparound terrace, along with vineyard and mountain views—creating the ideal spot to unwind with a glass of wine before or after a walk into St. Helena. Personalized Mini-bar selections are provided by request prior to arrival by pre-ordering after reserving your room. Alila Napa Valley is tailored to adult guests and those seeking tranquility. To preserve this peaceful atmosphere, our accommodations are limited to a maximum of two guests per room, including minors.
