### Scented Escapes: A Bespoke Ritual

Awaken your senses and creativity in this hands-on Alila experience, where the natural beauty of Napa Valley comes to life through scent. Guided by an expert perfumer, you’ll explore nine unique fragrances inspired by the land—each designed to spark memory, lift the mood and help ensure you feel present. As you learn about the distinctive qualities and benefits of each aroma, you’ll craft your own custom room and aura mist using local botanicals. It’s a sensory journey that blends relaxation, personal expression and a lasting connection to your surroundings—leaving you with both a keepsake and a fresh perspective to carry through the rest of your stay.

**A Scented Welcome**

Join a trained perfumer for an interactive scent experience designed to improve wellbeing and nurture your connection to the nature of wine country.

**Fragrance as Reflection**

Embark on a journey as you learn about the powers of nine fragrant local flora of wine country and how scent encourages mindfulness that can shape our emotions, memory and wellbeing.

**Blending Your Own**

Create your custom scent by blending a personalized fragrance inspired by the Napa Valley landscape, while exploring the connection between scent, place and wellness.

**Scented Storytelling**

Take time to reflect through journaling using guided prompts that tap into your impressions on scent, current emotions and personal memories both old and new. Take home your journal and continue to explore how scent impacts your emotions during future travels.

**Duration:** 90 Min.

**Recommended Dress:** Resort Casual

**Price:** $125

### Color Around the Vine – Art & Wine with Nimbus

Immerse yourself in a guided journey of creativity and connection, inspired by the intimate beauty and abundance of Napa Valley. Led by the community-rooted artists of Nimbus Arts, this experience invites participants to explore the familiar in fresh and meaningful ways. While savoring local wines or teas, you’ll let your creativity flourish, uncover artistic talents and embark on a path of self-discovery. Each class is shaped by new themes and mediums—ranging from painting on canvas and watercolor to pottery and wine bottle art—all thoughtfully designed to foster human connection and a deep sense of place in the heart of Napa Valley.

**Set the Mood** Begin with a warm welcome from the Nimbus Arts team, and choose wine or tea to accompany your creative process.

**Inspiration, Found** Get grounded in the natural palette and textures of Napa Valley—then pick your medium: canvas, pottery, wine bottle or beyond.

**Flow into Creation** Let go of judgment as you paint, sculpt or illustrate, guided by artists who emphasize exploration over perfection.

**Art to Remember**

Take home your finished piece as a personal symbol of expression—and a memory shaped by place, people and presence.

**Duration:** 2 hours

**Recommended Dress:** Resort Casual. Apron will be provided

**Additional Details:** Wine or tea will be served based on guest preference.

**Price:** $125.0 for scheduled group class OR $500.00 for private offerings (2-4 people)

**Currently unavailable.**

Step into the storied world of Spring Mountain Vineyard with an unforgettable encounter among birds of prey set against the backdrop of the iconic Miravalle Mansion. Get eye-to-eye with these magnificent raptors and learn how they help protect the land in a captivating, hands-on experience. **Vineyard Meets Wild**

**Private Estate Tour**

Connect on a deeper level with an immersive tour of Spring Mountain Vineyards, enjoying a wine tasting while being introduced to new approaches to the art of winemaking in Napa Valley.

**Savor the Legacy**

### Pedal & Pour – A Winemaker’s Ride

Experience Napa Valley alongside the person who knows it best—a winemaker.

Join Ted Osborne, founder and vintner of Olabisi Wines, for an intimate guided journey through the vineyards of the St Helena and Calistoga AVAs. This exclusive experience offers rare access to the people, places, and craftsmanship behind one of Napa Valley's most distinctive boutique wineries. Traveling by bike allows you to move at the pace of the vineyard, taking in the landscape while hearing firsthand how each vintage is shaped by the land, the season, and the decisions made in the cellar.

Along the way, enjoy an exclusive barrel tasting at a celebrated winery, where you'll sample wines in their earliest expression and gain insight into the art of blending and aging. Midway through the journey, optionally savor a chef-prepared seasonal lunch at Violetto paired with a curated selection of Olabisi wines before continuing to the Olabisi tasting room in downtown Calistoga for a hosted tasting and celebratory final pour.

**Your Experience**

**Meet Your Winemaker**

Begin at Alila Napa Valley, where you'll be welcomed by Ted Osborne and fitted for your bike before an introduction to the day's route, the Calistoga AVA, and the vineyards you'll explore.

**Ride Through Wine Country**

Pedal the Napa Valley Vine Trail at a relaxed pace, stopping throughout the ride as Ted shares insights into vineyard practices, terroir, and the decisions that influence every bottle.

**Exclusive Barrel Tasting**

Step inside a working winery for a private barrel tasting not available during traditional tasting experiences, sampling wines before they are bottled while learning how they evolve in the cellar.

**Seasonal Lunch & Wine Pairing**

Optionally enjoy a chef-prepared lunch from Violetto thoughtfully paired with Olabisi wines, highlighting the harmony between local cuisine and Napa Valley wines.

**Hosted Tasting at Olabisi**

Conclude at Olabisi's downtown Calistoga tasting room for a guided tasting and final toast with Ted, reflecting on the day's discoveries while enjoying the winery's acclaimed small-production wines.

**Duration: Approximately 3 hours**

**Recommended Dress:**

Comfortable, non-loose clothing and closed toe shoes.

**Additional Details:** Sunscreen and sunglasses are recommended. Hydrate in advance, though water bottles will be provided. Meet a few minutes early at Alila Napa Valley's Valet to pick out your bike and helmet and to meet your guide Price: $349 (E-Bike included)
