# Travel & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

For your convenience, we offer valet parking and electric vehicle charging stations. Complimentary day-use valet parking is available for Spa Alila and dining guests. Please note that only valet parking is available; self-parking is not offered. Guests using our EV charging stations are required to leave their vehicle key with our valet team to ensure their vehicle can be charged as requested.

Private Car Service

Whether booking an intimate getaway with friends or an all-hands-on-deck corporate retreat, Pure Luxury transportation maintains one of the largest chauffeured luxury fleets in Napa Valley. Their extensive fleet includes new sedans, luxury sprinters, executive shuttle buses, and motor coaches, and as a Certified Green ground transportation company, every aspect of Purse Luxury company functions with the awareness of our impact on the environment. This dedication has earned them multiple awards for their green efforts. Transportation for guests at Alila Napa Valley can be set up to bill to the guest room. Call 800.626.5466 for details.

Shuttle Service

Alila Napa Valley offers a complimentary guest shuttle within a four-mile radius of the resort in St. Helena, California. The shuttle provides convenient transportation to many of the area's renowned wineries, restaurants, tasting rooms, and boutique shops, making it easy to explore Napa Valley without driving. Whether you're heading to a wine tasting, dinner reservation, or shopping in downtown St. Helena, the shuttle offers a seamless way to experience Wine Country. Available throughout the day for guests' convenience, the complimentary shuttle accommodates requests based on arrival, with typical wait times of 15 minutes or less, subject to availability.
