### A Tranquil Oasis with Skyline Views

High above the bustling streets, the 46th-floor open pool provides a serene escape with breathtaking views of Doha's skyline—a sanctuary of relaxation amid the city's energetic pace.

### Health & Fitness Club

Perched high on the 46th floor, experience unparalleled luxury with state-of-the-art cardiovascular machines, expansive free weight zones, and a tranquil rooftop shaded pool. Bask in the breathtaking cityscape views, where natural light pours through floor-to-ceiling windows by day, and a radiant skyline illuminates your evening workout.

Learn more

### Your Ultimate Spa Boutique Experience

Welcome to Lamara Spa, a serene retreat on the 46th floor of InterContinental Doha The City. Enjoy panoramic views and natural light in Doha’s premier spa, offering bespoke treatments and state of-the-art amenities. Indulge in our four treatment rooms, Sauna & Steam temple, and luxurious Hammam suite.
Open daily from 11 am to 10 pm.

Learn more

### A Gourmet Adventure Awaits

Discover a culinary wonderland at our hotel with renowned dining venues offering international flavors. Savor vibrant Indian and French cuisines, nuanced Asian dishes, and hearty Lebanese and Latin fare. Each restaurant delivers a unique and exquisite gastronomic adventure.

## FAQs for InterContinental Doha - The City

Does InterContinental Doha - The City have a pool?

InterContinental Doha - The City has 1 outdoor pool.

Health and Fitness Club Open-Air Shaded Pool

A stunning view of the city from an open pool located on the 46th floor.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Doha - The City?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Doha - The City for hotel guests.

Underground parking on 2 level.

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Doha - The City?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Doha - The City.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Doha - The City for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Doha - The City have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Doha - The City has 7 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Doha - The City?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Doha - The City.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Doha - The City?

Check-in at InterContinental Doha - The City is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Doha - The City offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Doha - The City offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Doha - The City from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Doha - The City is conveniently located near Hamad International Airport (DOH), approximately 8.7 miles away (14 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
