### A Tranquil Oasis with Skyline Views

High above the bustling streets, the 46th-floor open pool provides a serene escape with breathtaking views of Doha's skyline—a sanctuary of relaxation amid the city's energetic pace.

### Health & Fitness Club

Perched high on the 46th floor, experience unparalleled luxury with state-of-the-art cardiovascular machines, expansive free weight zones, and a tranquil rooftop shaded pool. Bask in the breathtaking cityscape views, where natural light pours through floor-to-ceiling windows by day, and a radiant skyline illuminates your evening workout.

Learn more

### Your Ultimate Spa Boutique Experience

Welcome to Lamara Spa, a serene retreat on the 46th floor of InterContinental Doha The City. Enjoy panoramic views and natural light in Doha’s premier spa, offering bespoke treatments and state of-the-art amenities. Indulge in our four treatment rooms, Sauna & Steam temple, and luxurious Hammam suite.
Open daily from 11 am to 10 pm.

In the heart of West Bay, InterContinental Doha The City redefines urban luxury. Our 58-story landmark offers sweeping views of the Doha skyline, the Arabian Gulf, and the city streets below. ### Sustainability

#### Sustainability Certification

- Our hotel has a third-party sustainability certification.

**Certifications: Green Key (FEE)**

Our hotel is certified with Green Key (FEE), a globally recognised eco-label run by the Foundation for Environmental Education that is awarded to tourism and hospitality facilities that demonstrate outstanding commitment to environmental sustainability. Certified hotels must have implemented a range of measures across multiple categories including energy (e.g. energy-saving lightbulbs), water (e.g. water-saving taps, showers, and toilets) and waste (food waste and single-use plastic reduction schemes).
