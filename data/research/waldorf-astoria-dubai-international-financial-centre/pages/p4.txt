Spa Suites

## Grooming Suite

Embark on a journey to the Grooming Suite and emerge as a the best version of yourself. This treatment space offers reflexology, signature massages, and bespoke facials.

## Floatation Suite

Experience weightlessness in a floating bed. Once your senses are free to relax, let your mind wander into the abyss.

The floatation pod provides a sensory deprivation experience enhanced by 600 kg of Epsom salt – a true reflection of the Jordanian Dead Sea.

## Couples Suite
