## The Epitome of Luxury in the heart of Dubai

Spanning floors 18 to 50 of the Burj Daman, our urban retreat is within 10 minutes of the Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, and The Museum of the Future. We are home to the best-in-class award-winning restaurants and bars, a state-of-the-art Waldorf Astoria Spa, and world-class hospitality. Your stay includes a dedicated Personal Concierge on hand to assist with excursions and special requests.
