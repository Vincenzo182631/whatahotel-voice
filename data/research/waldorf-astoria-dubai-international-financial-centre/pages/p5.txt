## Personal Concierge

Personal Concierge

Your Personal Concierge will put your preferences into operation as soon as you book. When you arrive, your Personal Concierge will escort you to your room and attend to your requests during your stay, including the best support for your sleep. Select the perfect pillow from our Pillow Menu and expect a fluffy bathrobe waiting in your closet.

For more information and to order your room amenities, please contact your Personal Concierge a minimum of 48 hours before arrival.
