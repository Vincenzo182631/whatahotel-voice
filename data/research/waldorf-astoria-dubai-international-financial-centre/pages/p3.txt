# Dining & Drinks

Dining & Drinks

Experience a renowned steakhouse with expertly crafted cuts and flavours. Unwind with a signature afternoon tea, enjoy the rooftop oasis, or retreat to the Cigar Lounge. Each dining venue on the 18th floor offers stunning city views. Room service is available 24 hours a day.

## Bull & Bear

Our signature restaurant and steakhouse exemplifies culinary excellence, with expertly crafted dishes in an elegant Art Deco setting. Perched on Level 18 with breathtaking Burj Khalifa views, it’s ideal for business lunches, special occasions, or the traditional Sunday roast.

Breakfast: 7 am – 11 am

Lunch: 12:30 pm – 4 pm

Dinner: 7 pm – 12 am
