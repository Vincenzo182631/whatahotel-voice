## EXPLORE NUSA DUA BEACH FROM OUR LUXURY 5-STAR HOTEL IN BALI​

Grand Hyatt Bali is a family-friendly beachfront resort in Nusa Dua, Bali. Located just minutes from a world-class convention center and steps from the Bali Collection shopping center. Just 15 minutes from Ngurah Rai International Airport, our hotel is suitable for both business and leisure with its blend of culture & Balinese hospitality.​

## EXPLORE

Sustainability Commitment

In our mission to continuously elevate the guest experience, we're making conscious decisions to make your stay more sustainable. We’re committed to serving responsibly sourced food—including seafood procured strictly from ASC-certified suppliers—and eliminating single-use plastic in our hotel. Following Hyatt's World of Care initiatives, we are working on waste management, composting and eco-enzyme making.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our ongoing commitment to reducing waste, our hotel no longer provides bottled water or disposable in-room amenities for staying guests. We recommend bringing your own essentials and a drinking vessel to refill with drinking water available throughout the hotel.

Fitness Center

Located below the main hotel lobby, the Bay Club Fitness Center offers a variety of cardio equipment and machine weights, squash courts and a yoga studio. The 24-hour fitness center also includes an adults-only swimming pool, a sauna, a steam room and plunge pools. A selection of classes ranging from yoga﻿ to cultural Balinese activities﻿ are available for free and at cost.

Outdoor Pool

Our luxurious Balinese swim resort features five swimming pools designed to suit your holiday needs. Spend the day in the spacious lagoon-style Main Pool surrounded by tropical gardens, or ride down our two waterslides in the River Pool. Find your peace at our secluded Balinese Feature Pool, with emerald green water and cascading waterfalls, or at the adults-only Bay Club Pool located in the fitness center. The Grand Club Pool is exclusively available for our club guests. Pools are open 8 a.m.–7 p.m. daily. You must be 16 years old to visit the Bay Club Pool; slides at the River Pool are open 9 a.m.–6 p.m.

Kids Club

During your stay, your kids can enjoy an exciting journey of fun and adventure at our Oceania Kids Club. We aim to offer an experience above and beyond, allowing your little ones to learn about Balinese culture and explore the resort’s local flora and fauna in a safe and welcoming setting. Enjoy a variety of fun activities (fee applies for some activities). Open 9 a.m. – 6 p.m. daily

Kriya Spa

Drawing inspiration from Balinese water palaces, Kriya Spa offers bespoke spa and wellness treatments delivered by professionally trained therapists. Our expertise lies in personalized evaluation to curate tailored services that awaken the inner well-being. Step into our serene sanctuary and experience the profound benefits of our holistic offerings, designed to deliver a transformative journey towards relaxation, renewal and self-discovery. The spa is open 9 a.m.–9 p.m. daily. The last appointment of the day is available at 8 p.m. and only for a 60-minute treatment. Online reservations must be booked a minimum of 24 hours in advance.
