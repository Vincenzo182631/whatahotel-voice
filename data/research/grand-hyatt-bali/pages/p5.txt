### Our stay was completely ruined.

First and foremost, there is virtually no access to the sea. The water is extremely far from the shoreline, and the seabed is covered with large, sharp rocks, making it practically impossible to swim. Most guests only walk in knee-deep before turning back, which is why there are hardly any sun loungers near the water—nobody stays there. Second, the pools are extremely shallow. Because of the large surface area, the water overheats to the point where going in is actually unpleasant. In short, you can't swim in the ocean, and you can't enjoy the pool either, leaving no real reason to stay at this hotel. To make matters worse, the resort is overcrowded, requiring you to wait in long lines just to get a breakfast table, and it is full of noisy children. Overall, I have no idea who this hotel is intended for.
