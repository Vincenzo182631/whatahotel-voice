New Orleans Luxury Suites
Discover our penthouse suite in New Orleans French Quarter
At The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans, guests indulge in luxurious accommodations that blend Southern elegance with modern amenities. The hotel offers a variety of premium rooms and luxury suites, including a Penthouse Suite. Each of these New Orleans' French Quarter suites offers access to the exclusive Maison Orleans Club Level, an elite enclave often described as a hotel within a hotel, offering a dedicated concierge, an intimate lounge setting, and unparalleled comfort and exclusivity. Whether it’s savoring Louisiana specialties or enjoying the French Quarter vistas, The Ritz-Carlton ensures a memorable stay for every guest.
The Residence
The Residence is an opulent two-bedroom penthouse suite in New Orleans, epitomizing French Quarter luxury and elegance. This exquisite retreat boasts an elegant dining room with a fully equipped kitchen, four grand fireplaces, and a private rooftop terrace offering breathtaking views of the French Quarter. Guests also enjoy exclusive access to the prestigious Maison Orleans Club Level. The Residence is the ultimate luxury getaway, with suites in New Orleans designed to provide an unparalleled experience.
The Residence
Take In The French Quarter Views
Step out onto the expansive rooftop terrace of The Residence, an ideal setting for entertaining, where you’ll be captivated by sweeping views of the majestic Mississippi River and the enchanting French Quarter.

Maison Orleans Suite
Named for the most exclusive area of the hotel, our Maison Orleans Suite harmoniously celebrates contemporary luxury while embracing a timeless classical aesthetic. As one of our luxury suites in downtown New Orleans, it offers more than just accommodation, inviting guests to experience the enduring beauty and culture of the city in a uniquely elegant setting.
Maison Orleans Suite
Surrender To Luxury
Every detail defines the Masion Orleans Suite, from the soft glow of crystal chandeliers to French-inspired mural wallcoverings. Elegant drapery and curated works by local artists create a refined sense of place, where historic elegance meets modern design paying homage to the storied legacy of The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans.
