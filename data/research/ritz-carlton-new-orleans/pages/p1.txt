Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans
Discover Unforgettable Elegance at our Luxury Hotel
A city for the senses, from the enticing scent of hearty gumbo to the energizing beat of a jazz band. The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans is a luxury hotel in downtown New Orleans, where the city comes to life as soon as guests cross the threshold. Set within the 1908 Beaux Arts Maison Blanche building, our hotel highlights traditional Southern ambiance with an elegant interior that reflects the graciousness of Garden District mansions.

The Story Behind This Historic New Orleans Hotel
Opening our doors in 2000, The Ritz Carlton, New Orleans is set within the historic Maison Blanche Building and carries a history spanning more than a century. Built between 1906 and 1909, this grand Beaux Arts structure was originally home to the Maison Blanche Department Store, as well as local businesses and the city’s first radio station, WSMB Radio. Today, this address is recognized as a Historic District Landmark and known as the ‘Crown Jewel of Canal Street.’ It welcomes guests to a luxury hotel experience defined by storied heritage, timeless elegance, and classic charm unique to The Ritz Carlton, New Orleans.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 30.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $300.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans. The daily valet fee is $62.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans is Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY). MSY is located approximately 14.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites.
