Fine Dining in the French Quarter
Dining
Where a jazz restaurant in New Orleans’ French Quarter sets the tone, The Ritz-Carlton celebrates local culinary and musical traditions. Savor farm-to-table Louisiana cuisine at M Bistro, then indulge in cocktails and light fare accompanied by live jazz at Davenport Lounge.
Afternoon Tea
Experience a luxury New Orleans Afternoon Tea at Davenport Lounge, offered every Saturday and Sunday at 11:00 AM and 2:30 PM. Enjoy a refined selection of teas and pastries in an elegant setting. Reservations are required for this cherished Ritz Carlton tradition.

Indulge In New Orleans
Dining Experiences
The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans offers an inspired collection of restaurants in downtown New Orleans, where chef-crafted experiences, private dining, and curated events are thoughtfully designed to delight the senses.
Private Dining
Cheaters’ Booths
Escape into one of the three plush booths. Choose from Stella, Desire, and Blanche. Relax with a group of your friends while dining and enjoying the sounds of Jeremy Davenport as you feel like a true V.I.P. Reservations are highly recommended.
Book a Table
Wine Room
This private setting is housed within the hotel’s exclusive wine room and it features a table that can seat up to twelve. Set amidst the backdrop of hundreds of bottles of wine, enjoy an unparalleled experience. Wine selections take the lead as our culinary team creates dishes that pair perfectly with the vintages. Allow us to take you through the layers and flavors of your wine choices.
Book Now
Afternoon Tea
Afternoon Tea at The Ritz-Carlton, New Orleans offers Southern elegance in the Davenport Lounge. Enjoy fine teas, scones, finger sandwiches, and pastries amid plush seating, elegant décor, and live harp music—a truly refined experience.
