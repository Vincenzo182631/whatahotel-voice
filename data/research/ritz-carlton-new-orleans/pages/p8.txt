Discover our New Orleans hotel with a club lounge
A hotel with Club Lounge access in downtown New Orleans
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Enhance your entire stay at our hotel with Club Lounge access in downtown New Orleans. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” the private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, dedicated Concierge, and exclusive experiences, immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*. Our hotel with a Club Lounge in downtown New Orleans opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
