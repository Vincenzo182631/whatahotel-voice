## Arizona's Icon, Reinvented

Our resort, with architecture influenced by Frank Lloyd Wright, has been a Phoenix icon since 1929. We provide a glamorous oasis with lush gardens and a full-service spa, seven dining outlets, seven swimming pools, and a 65-foot waterslide. Enjoy our pet-friendly rooms plus access to two championship golf courses, a modern fitness center, and outdoor event spaces.

## Where Past Meets Present

We have created a haven for a modern traveler, honoring the best of our history and creating a stylish and magical place. Plan your next stay with us and explore vibrant spaces, our new incredible spa, and more, all with highly personalized service.
