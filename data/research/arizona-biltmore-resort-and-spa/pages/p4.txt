## Treatments and Rituals

Experience the healing properties of the desert with our spa offering. Our treatments and rituals center on desert botanicals, cosmic connection, and dynamic recovery.

**Open daily: 9 am – 7 pm**

## Sol Garden

Step into Sol Garden and take a healing journey through the powerful elements of fire, water, ice, and earth. Experience rituals that utilize stones, muds, crystals, and loofahs to bring the body, mind, and spirit together. Sol Garden is the place in which to relax, restore and open your senses to the spirit of the desert.

Access to the refreshing tranquility of Sol Garden is offered with any 50-minute treatment enjoyed at the Tierra Luna Spa.

## Spa Boutique

Our curated collection of products at Tierra Luna Spa Boutique allows you to create your very own spa journey at home.

Find items including our Palo Santo Signature Scent Candle, sage bundles, jewelry by local artisan Lucky Star, Lilfox skincare line, and more.
