# Pursuit of adventure

Pursuit of adventure

From authentic encounters with local culture to moments shaped by stunning natural beauty, embark on a journey beyond the expected and discover a richer, more meaningful side of travel.

## Arrival & Welcome Dinner at Arizona Biltmore

Begin the journey with an intimate welcome dinner at Arizona Biltmore, where guests are immersed in the spirit of the American Southwest through thoughtfully curated cuisine, storytelling, and elevated hospitality. Inspired by the region’s rich landscapes and culinary traditions, the evening features locally sourced ingredients, refined desert flavors, and bespoke touches from Southwest experience partners. Set against the timeless architecture and ambiance of the Jewel of the Desert, this opening experience establishes the tone for an unforgettable luxury adventure.

## Private Aviation Journey Over the Southwest

Embark on an exclusive private aviation experience departing from Deer Valley Airport aboard a scenic flight to Page, Arizona. Guests soar above the dramatic desert terrain of the American Southwest, taking in breathtaking aerial views of canyons, mesas, and vast landscapes rarely experienced from this perspective. Designed in partnership with Westwind Aviation, this elevated journey blends adventure, luxury, and discovery while showcasing the natural grandeur of Arizona from the sky.

## Antelope Canyon & Horseshoe Bend Exploration

Discover some of the Southwest’s most iconic natural wonders through a guided exploration of Antelope Canyon and Horseshoe Bend. Guests journey through sculpted sandstone passageways illuminated by shifting desert light before experiencing panoramic views overlooking the Colorado River’s dramatic curve below Horseshoe Bend. Curated in partnership with Detours of the West, this immersive excursion offers a deeper connection to the region’s beauty, geology, and spirit of adventure

## Lake Powell Scenic Lunch Experience

Set along the tranquil shores of Lake Powell, guests enjoy a scenic lakeside lunch surrounded by sweeping desert vistas and serene waterfront views. This thoughtfully curated dining experience highlights the beauty and tranquility of the region while offering moments of relaxation and connection following the day’s exploration. In partnership with Lake Powell Resorts & Marinas, the experience combines elevated cuisine, natural beauty, and the quiet luxury of the Southwest landscape.

## Desert Restoration & Wellness Retreat

Conclude the journey with restorative wellness experiences inspired by the healing energy of the Sonoran Desert. Guests unwind with luxurious spa treatments at Tierra Luna Spa before departing Arizona Biltmore refreshed and renewed. Rooted in mindfulness, relaxation, and connection to nature, this final experience offers a tranquil conclusion to an immersive multi-day adventure blending exploration, indulgence, and elevated desert hospitality.

## Architectural Adventure

Embark on an architectural adventure across 39 acres of desert gardens, winding pathways, and iconic architecture at the Arizona Biltmore. Discover a landscape where the beauty of the Sonoran Desert meets the visionary design legacy of Frank Lloyd Wright and his protégé, Albert Chase McArthur. As you explore, you’ll encounter geometric patterns, signature Biltmore Blocks, tranquil courtyards, and thoughtfully designed spaces that seamlessly blend with the surrounding desert environment.

## Fitness Center

Our new state-of-the-art 3,000 square-foot fitness center has everything you need to stay healthy including Life Fitness Cardio equipment, four Peloton bikes, two Lifecycle Recumbent Bikes, one Assault Air Bike, two Assault Air Treadmills, a TRX Station, free-weights, and fitness classes.

For individual training, a personal trainer is available at the Biltmore.
