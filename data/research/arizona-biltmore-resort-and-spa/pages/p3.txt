# Dining in Arizona Biltmore

Dining in Arizona Biltmore

From bold American flavors to light snacks by the pool, our onsite bars and restaurants offer a wonderful array of food with a vibrant atmosphere.

## Discover the Wrigley Mansion

Discover new heights in dining at Wrigley Mansion, where exceptional cuisine, acclaimed wines and sweeping Valley views come together. From MICHELIN-recognized Christopher’s to Geordie’s Restaurant and Jamie’s Wine Bar, the Mansion invites discovery through intimate dinners, sunset cocktails and memorable celebrations. Now part of the Arizona Biltmore experience, this historic hilltop destination also offers distinctive settings for private dining, weddings and special occasions.

Discover the Mansion

## McArthur's

Nestled at the heart of the Arizona Biltmore is our beloved eatery, McArthur’s Restaurant & Bar. Formerly known as Frank & Albert’s, this reimagined restaurant celebrates a century of great taste with New American flavors, featuring simple yet uncommonly good food.

**Open daily.**

Sunday – Thursday: 7 am – 9 pm

Friday – Saturday: 7 am – 10 pm

## Cup & Cone

A new art deco inspired café offering hand roasted espresso and coffee beverages, homemade gelato and gourmet desserts. The whole family can enjoy the whimsical display of spinning gelato – where waffle cones are akin to magic.

**Open daily.**
