# Arizona Biltmore PoolsSeven Pools, Endless Ways to Stay Cool in Style

Arizona Biltmore Pools Seven Pools, Endless Ways to Stay Cool in Style

With seven unique pools, luxury cabanas, poolside dining, and curated events and activities, Arizona Biltmore offers a pool experience for every kind of getaway. Spend the day racing down waterslides, unwinding in an adults-only oasis, gathering with friends over cocktails, or discovering a quiet retreat tucked among the resort’s iconic grounds.

From the legendary Catalina Pool to vibrant social scenes and family-friendly adventures, every pool invites you to soak in the timeless glamor and effortless luxury that have defined Arizona Biltmore for generations.

Find Your Perfect Pool Experience.

## Paradise PoolFor Families

Paradise Pool For Families

The heart of family fun at Arizona Biltmore, Paradise Pool brings together everything needed for an unforgettable day in the sun.

Splash through the dedicated splash pad, race down a triple 65 foot waterslide, enjoy refreshments from the swim-up bar, and settle in for Movies at the Pool after sunset every Friday night in the summer.

Air-conditioned deluxe cabanas provide a private retreat between adventures, while poolside dining keeps everyone fueled for another round of fun.

**Pool Open 24/7**

## The Twist Waterslide

Admire the looping shapes of the Twist that are a manifestation of Wright’s signature geometry and straight lines. This incredible and fun triple water slide is a must-try for our guests. Come see that the fun never stops at the Twist.

## Poolside Drinks and Snacks

Everyone needs a break in the hot Arizona weather. Grab refreshing drinks and light snacks in our swim-up bar at the Paradise Pool.

**Food Service Hours:**

Sunday – Thursday: 11 am – 4 pm

Friday – Saturday: 11 am – 5 pm

## Saguaro PoolFor Couples

Saguaro Pool For Couples

Reserved only for adults, Saguaro Pool offers an advanced escape where relaxation and refinement meet. Private cabanas, attentive service, and handcrafted cocktails create an atmosphere designed for lingering afternoons.

Every Friday and Saturday in the summer, DJ sets and Champagne moments create a vibrant social hour and one of the resort’s most stylish poolside scenes.

**Pool Open 24/7**

## An Adults-Only Oasis

## Catalina Pool

A beloved landmark of the Arizona Biltmore, Catalina Pool embodies the resort’s enduring legacy. Many famous people have gathered around its waters, from Marilyn Monroe to Frank Sinatra. This makes it one of the most famous and photographed places on the resort. Today, it remains a timeless reminder of the glamor, history, and spirit that continue to define the Arizona Biltmore experience.
