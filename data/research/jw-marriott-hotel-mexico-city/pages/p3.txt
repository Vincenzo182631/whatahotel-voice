JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City
Set in the upscale Polanco District, the JW Marriott delivers name-brand luxuries, including plush accommodations, 24/7 room service, a rooftop pool and hot tub, and more.

Reviewed by
Susannah Rigg
How did it strike you on arrival?
The beautiful lobby, filled with plush carpets and comfortable sofas, has exactly the kind of classic luxuries you'd expect from a JW Marriott.
Nice. What’s the crowd like?
Business travelers and older couples who trust in name-brand luxury and prefer to be in the upscale neighborhood of Polanco, also home to the Museum of Anthropology and the National Auditorium.
The good stuff: Tell us about your room.
The rooms are spacious, with solid dark wood furniture, cushy carpets, and floor-to-ceiling windows.
We’re craving some deep, restorative sleep. They got us?
The pillow-top mattresses and down duvets make for an incredibly comfortable night's sleep.
How about the little things, like mini bar, or shower goodies. Any of that find its way into your suitcase?
The room has everything you'd imagine from a large chain hotel.
Please tell us the bathroom won’t let us down.
Large marble bathrooms are outfitted with a shower and Aromatherapy Associates amenities. The Presidential Suite is the only room with a Whirpool tub (that fits two).
Maybe the most important topic of all: Wi-Fi. What’s the word?
Wired Internet is free but Wi-Fi comes at an extra cost—how '90s!
Room service: Worth it?
Room service is 24/7 and offers a selection of Mexican and international favorites.
Anything stand out about other services and features? Whether it’s childcare, gyms, spas, even parking—whatever stuck with you.
The rooftop has a heated outdoor pool and hot tub with beautiful views of the city.
Bottom line: worth it, and why?
Impeccable service and attention to detail are almost always guaranteed at a luxury hotel chain like the JW Marriott.
