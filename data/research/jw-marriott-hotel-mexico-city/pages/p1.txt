# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco

## Experience refined luxury at our hotel in Mexico City

Set in the heart of Polanco, one of Mexico City's most celebrated neighborhoods, JW Marriott Mexico City offers a refined retreat where culture, gastronomy and timeless elegance converge. Thoughtfully redesigned rooms and inviting public spaces create an atmosphere of understated luxury, complemented by warm, intuitive service. Embark on a curated culinary journey, from contemporary interpretations...See More of Mexican cuisine to intimate mezcal experiences that celebrate the richness of the country's traditions. Unwind by our terrace pool overlooking Chapultepec Park or restore balance in our wellness spaces. Whether your journey brings you for business, leisure or celebration, every moment is thoughtfully designed to inspire a deeper connection with Mexico City while embracing the mindful spirit of JW Marriott.See Less

## At the Heart of Polanco

From tree-lined avenues and renowned galleries to award-winning restaurants and designer boutiques, Polanco reflects the sophisticated spirit of Mexico City. Moments from Chapultepec Park, Paseo de la Reforma and the iconic Avenida Presidente Masaryk, JW Marriott Mexico City Polanco places guests at the center of the capital's most coveted cultural, culinary and luxury experiences.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 20.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco?

On-site parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco. Self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco, with rates of $5.00 per hour, and $25.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $27.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco is Benito Juarez International Airport Mexico City (MEX). MEX is located approximately 17.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Mexico City Polanco has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
