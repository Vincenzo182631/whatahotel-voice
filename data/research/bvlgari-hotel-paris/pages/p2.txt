## The Bvlgari Bar

The perfect place to meet, the Bvlgari Bar embodies the expansiveness and elegance of the Maison. Black granite floors, high-gloss ceiling and brass accents conjure contemporary sophistication and savoir faire, with lacquered walls and vintage-style mirrors completing the theatricality of the space.

Drinks Menu Snack Menu Healthy Menu

* * *

### The Aperitif

Classic cocktails are mixed to perfection and the Italian institution of Aperitivo is served the classic way, with a profusion of bites and antipasti, creating a late-afternoon spirit of conviviality and cheerfulness.

### Opening Time

Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Sunday: from 10am to midnight (00:00).

Friday and Saturday: from 10:00am to 01.00am
