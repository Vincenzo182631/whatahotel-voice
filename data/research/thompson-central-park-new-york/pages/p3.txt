# Restaurants & Bars

Thompson Central Park isn't just a place to stay; it's a must-visit culinary destination in New York City, boasting a world-class collection of restaurants that will tantalize your taste buds. Indian Accent transports you with authentic international influences and their traditional, much-loved favorites. For a more low-key, yet equally satisfying experience, seek out the mysterious Burger Joint, a hidden gem serving up acclaimed hamburgers and fries in an unexpected setting. Parker's rounds out our impressive gastronomic scene. Step into a moody, sophisticated setting where you can enjoy a playful menu of sharable dishes alongside inventive cocktails, with live music filling the air to create an unforgettable experience. And, of course, our exemplary around-the-clock in-room dining ensures you can savor our culinary delights from the comfort of your own space. No stay at our 56th Street hotel is complete without taking in the many contrasting flavors and experiences our restaurants have to offer.

## Parker’s

Situated just beyond the lobby, at the heart of the Hotel is Parker’s, a contemporary expression of the city’s love for music. Parker’s offers a seasonal menu that encourages shared dishes which complement the cocktail menu. This locale is a destination for those seeking the iconic spirit of New York City paired with Thompson’s signature playful sophistication. ## Burger Joint

No longer a secret but still the best burger in Midtown Manhattan, Burger Joint welcomes guests to graffitied walls and unpretentious bites from its no-frills, flavorful hamburgers and fries menu. If you want to dine at this hidden gem, you’ll have to find it first. Reservations not available – walk-ins only.

## Indian Accent

With an artful approach to traditional Indian recipes, Chef Manish Mehrotra puts an inventive spin on classic dishes that are as much a feast for the eyes as the palate. Savor prawn koliwada and malvani-dried shrimp pula, or crispy jackfruit and Kerala pumpkin curry with a doda barfi treacle tart for dessert. This upscale Manhattan restaurant boasts numerous awards and an extensive wine list that deserves its own attention.
