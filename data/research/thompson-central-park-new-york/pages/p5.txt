### Frightening Elevator Malfunction and a Stay Far Below Expectations

I booked Thompson Central Park several months in advance for my two children’s first trip to New York, based on its excellent location, reputation, and reviews. I even upgraded to a higher-floor room with lounge access when that option was offered. Unfortunately, the experience was far below expectations.

The room and bathroom were quite small, the amenities felt dated, there was no bathtub, and the shower had weak water pressure. Soundproofing was also very poor. Having flown from the West Coast, we were still adjusting to the time difference, yet we were awakened around 6:00 AM ET (3:00 AM PT) by the neighboring room’s door opening and later by people simply walking and talking in the hallway.

Most concerning was a frightening elevator malfunction. We were staying on the 27th floor, and while my two children and I were inside one of the elevators, it suddenly dropped steeply and then stopped. The doors would not open, and we were trapped inside for approximately 10–15 minutes before someone was able to get us out. All three of us were terrified.

Afterward, that elevator remained out of service, leaving only two working elevators. They were frequently overcrowded, with guests on intermediate floors sometimes unable to board. Despite the seriousness of what happened, no hotel manager followed up with us afterward to ask whether my children and I were okay or to address our concerns.

Overall, the property felt dated and inadequately maintained for a hotel of this reputation and price point. The staff appeared overextended, and the elevator system was clearly under considerable strain. The lack of management follow-up after such a frightening safety incident was particularly disappointing.
