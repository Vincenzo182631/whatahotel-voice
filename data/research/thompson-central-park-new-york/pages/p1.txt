# Luxury Hotel Near Central Park \| Thompson Central Park, by Hyatt

## REDEFINING A NEW YORK CLASSIC AT OUR SOPHISTICATED HOTEL NEAR CENTRAL PARK

Thompson Central Park New York is your gateway to the best of Manhattan. Poised on the south edge of Central Park, our 56th Street locale is a short walk to Carnegie Hall, MoMA and legendary fine dining destinations. Inside the hotel, redesigned public spaces and guest rooms add sophistication and flair to our iconic structure. Check into a high-floor room with views of the lush greenery of the park or the bustling sidewalks below. With our award-winning venues and re-imagined spaces, here is where you'll want to meet, gather, dine, listen to live music or set out to explore Midtown’s world-class cuisine, nightlife and entertainment.

## Rooms & Suites

Step into polished midcentury rooms and suites where sculptural lighting reveals inspired, chic details at every turn. Revel in refined finishes and linens and sweeping views of New York’s iconic Central Park—and for a personalized experience, elevate your Midtown stay with our signature Upper Stories accommodations and lounge access.

Upper Stories

Upper Stories offers an elevated New York City experience with a collective of rooms and suites perched on our topmost guest floors and features access to daily culinary celebrations hosted in the Upper Stories Lounge located on the third floor. Upper Stories guests are also extended preferred access to on-site entertainment, neighborhood shopping experiences and wellness perks.

Dining & Nightlife

No stay at Thompson Central Park New York is complete without exploring the incredible dining experiences right here at the hotel. Our restaurants truly capture the diverse culinary spirit of the city, offering something for every palate. Craving a classic? Head to Burger Joint, a hidden gem serving up tried-and-true burgers and fries. For a more adventurous culinary journey, discover the inventive flavors and rich traditions of Indian Accent. Be sure to also indulge in Parker's contemporary American cuisine, classic jazz-inspired cocktails, and live music, all embodying the vibrant influences of the Big Apple.

### Pets are Welcome

Thompson Central Park New York is a pet-friendly hotel. Check with the concierge for assistance with walking, sitting, or grooming arrangements. A pet waiver must be completed and signed upon check-in. Dogs cannot be left unattended in guest rooms and all fees are subject to change. Our current fee is $75 per stay, including a $25 donation to PAWS NY. PAWS NY helps New York City’s most vulnerable residents remain with their pets by delivering critical programs and services through a community of partners and volunteers. \*We welcome guests with disabilities traveling with their service animals. Service animals are not pets and are not subject to this Pet Policy. For more information, please contact the hotel.

#### Pet Fees

- Damage Fee: **$250**
