# The Spa

## A New Chapter for The Spa Awaits

Our spa is currently undergoing a thoughtful renovation designed to further elevate your future wellness experience. This project reflects our continued commitment to providing exceptional comfort, luxury, and tranquility for our guests.

As we prepare for this chapter, treatments and services will be offered in the fitness center and Room 15. Guests will check in and relax in our Wellness Nook, a thoughtfully curated space featuring red light face masks, grounding mats, and a wellness-inspired menu curated by our culinary team. The main lodge hot tub will also remain available for use either before or after your appointment.

During this time, the fitness center will be temporarily unavailable as it is relocated and thoughtfully integrated into the new spa, where it will reopen with views overlooking the Yellowstone River later this year. We invite you to embrace a different kind of movement, one shaped by the season and the landscape that surrounds us. In addition, we will be offering enhanced wellness experiences to help you feel grounded, restored, and fully present in the season.

Please note that our main spa amenities, including the hot tub, sauna, and steam room, will be unavailable during this time. We appreciate your understanding as we thoughtfully enhance these spaces for your future enjoyment.

While the spa experience evolves, our team remains dedicated to delivering the same exceptional service and care you’ve come to expect. If you have any questions or would like to schedule a treatment, we’re here to help.

## Spa Policies

We want your visit to The Spa at Sage Lodge to be as relaxing and comfortable as possible. To make the most of your experience, please note the following spa guidelines:

Arrival and Check-In

- Please arrive 15 to 20 minutes in advance of your appointment to check in, change, and relax.
- To maintain a tranquil environment, we kindly ask that you observe our no cell phone policy. Please turn all cell phones and devices to silent or off upon entering The Spa. All emergency calls can be directed through our front desk.
- Robes, sandals, and toiletries are also provided.

Spa Facilities

- The hot tub is co-ed and bathing suits must be worn.
- To maintain a tranquil and harmonious environment, these facilities are silent.
- Please assist us in our goal to be environmentally conscious by limiting excessive towel use throughout your spa experience.

Treatment Requirements

- The minimum age for treatments and use of the Spa amenities is 18, with identification.
- Certain treatments are not suitable for those with serious medical conditions. Inform a spa attendant of any special medical conditions or physical needs at the time of booking. Some conditions may require a doctor’s approval.
- We reserve the right to terminate a session because of inappropriate behavior, intoxication, infectious conditions, or sexual advances.
- We recommend you do not wear jewelry or bring valuables to The Spa, as we cannot be responsible for items that are lost or stolen.

Booking Details

- A credit card is required to secure your appointment time and date.
- An optional 22% gratuity will be added to all spa treatments, which will be distributed to the providers and personnel taking care of you during your visit. This gratuity can be changed or removed at your discretion.

Cancellations

- For individual appointments, we ask that you please notify us at least 48 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule. If an appointment is canceled within 48 hours of the scheduled time, a cancellation fee equal to 50% of the treatment amount, including enhancements, will be charged to the card on file.
- For group bookings, we ask that you please notify us at least 14 days in advance to cancel or reschedule. If an appointment is canceled within 14 days, a cancellation fee equal to 50% of the treatment amount, including enhancements, will be charged to the master account or credit card on file.
- Payment: The Spa accepts all major credit cards or Sage Lodge room charges. Cash is accepted.
- Late Policy: Treatment times will not be extended due to late arrivals. If 5 minutes pass after the scheduled appointment time, the appointment will be considered a no-show, and cancellation charges equal to 50% of the treatment amount, including enhancements, will be charged to the credit card on file.
