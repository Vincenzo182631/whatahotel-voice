# Adventures

## Run yourself rugged

We can safely say that during your time at our Montana ranch resort, you’ll never be at a loss for something to do unless you need a minute to take in the scenery in silence. Big Sky Country simply begs to be explored; fly fishing, rafting, wildlife viewing, mountain biking and hiking the expansive open lands are all within your grasp. And if it is indeed fly fishing that you love, Sage Lodge is the perfect destination, located in the heart of Montana’s fly fishing country.

Explore the categories below and discover all the adventure opportunities available when you stay with us at Sage Lodge. Just reach out to our Concierge Team at concierge@sagelodge.com to discuss any questions or to book your Sage Lodge adventures. Advance reservations are **strongly recommended**, especially for Yellowstone Tours and Fly Fishing, which can book-out well in advance. And remember, Montana’s weather can be unpredictable (it’s part of the adventure!), and some adventures are weather-dependent.

## Guided by Passion, Rooted in Montana

Montana is best experienced alongside those who know it most. Whether casting a line on legendary trout waters, exploring Yellowstone National Park, or discovering Paradise Valley, our expert guides bring each adventure to life with local knowledge and a passion for sharing this remarkable place. Meet the guides who make every experience unforgettable.
