Whether you’re hoping to kick back and relax in the stylish comfort of our modern guestrooms or seeking out new sights around the city, JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria offers on-site amenities and nearby attractions for everyone. Enjoy a workout or yoga session in our fitness center, a dip in our outdoor pool, or a moment of repose in one of our luxurious poolside cabanas. ## Explore the Best of Houston’s Arts & Culture

Located within walking distance of The Galleria attractions and nearby Gerald D. Hines Waterwall Park, our guests don’t have far to travel to get a taste of Houston’s cultural landmarks. For those who want to explore further, we recommend taking a short drive to Houston’s Museum District. There you can behold world-class displays of modern & classical artwork at the Museum of Fine Arts, explore surreal and fascinating artifacts ranging from ancient to contemporary at the Menil Collection, uncover prehistoric marvels at the Museum of Natural Science, and more. Or for a live performance, Houston’s Theater District is the place to be. For an updated list of Houston’s latest events, we recommend Vist Houston’s excellent calendar.

### Uncover Houston’s Lush Green Spaces

It may surprise visitors to learn that Houston ranks first in the U.S. for total green space among comparable cities, but locals know the city has plenty of great outdoors waiting to be discovered. Explore a sprawling 155-acre urban forest at Houston’s Arboretum and Nature Center, explore the Japanese Garden inside Hermann Park, or escape to the lavish 4.4-acre gardens at the Museum of Fine Arts’ Rienzi Collection.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria have a pool on-site?

Yes, JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

What are some local attractions and things to do near JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria?

Downtown Aquarium, Galleria Shopping Mall, Herman Park / Houston Zoo, Holocaust Museum Houston, Houston Museum of Natural Science and more are nearby JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria.
