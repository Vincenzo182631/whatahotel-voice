JW Marriott Houston Downtown
This straightforward hotel downtown attracts elite business travelers seeking top-notch service and comfortable amenities without any fuss or pretension.

Zoom out—what's the big picture?
Located in one of the buzziest business districts in Houston, this regal hotel fits right in.
Nice. Who's staying here?
Well dressed business travelers from near and far.
Thoughts about your room?
We booked the Poet King Guest Room, which was impressively sleek and spacious.
Were there any details in your room that impressed you?
In-room amenities include free wifi, an iPad for ordering room service or book spa treatments, and Nespresso machines.
What's the dining scene like?
There are two restaurants and a coffee shop; you can also order delivery from other local restaurants—a nice perk.
Would you like to give a shot out to a member of the staff?
The bellman will jump to your aid to help with luggage.
Are there any other standout features at the hotel?
The Spa by JW offers an array of interesting treatments, including foot soaks, massages, and facials.
What's the bottom line?
The JW Marriott attracts an elite group of business travelers seeking top-notch service and amenities without the fuss that's sometimes associated with boutique hotels.
