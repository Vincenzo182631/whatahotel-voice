Explore the fresh and flavorful cuisine at Stray Horse, our on-site restaurant serving breakfast, lunch and dinner. ## Dine Like a Local

Indulge in the flavors of Houston at Stray Horse, our on-site restaurant and bar. Rooted in the bold tastes of the local areas and using only the freshest ingredients, Stray Horse’s menu offers unique and elevated menus that celebrate cuisine first made famous in Houston. To pair with your evening fare or if you are looking for a nightcap, enjoy a selection of local craft beer and reimagined cocktails from our expansive bar.

Book a Table

## Environmentally Thoughtful Programming

Our sustainability efforts are infused throughout our dining program. We are proud to offer hyper-local greens and herbs grown on-site that are thoughtfully added to dishes and our hand crafted cocktails. To reduce food waste and support our community, we have partnered with Second Servings, a local organization whose mission is to fight hunger while reducing waste. Any unused food from our kitchens is donated to Second Servings.

## Room Service? Be Our Guest.

Explore Houston’s Thriving Food Scene

### Local Favorites, Fine Dining, and More

Houston has made a reputation for a diverse and ever-growing food scene. Discover creative fusions like Viet/Cajun, Chinese/Puerto Rican, and Korean/Tex-Mex. If stylish steakhouses, boutique bistros, and fresh gulf seafood are more your preference, Houston has a plethora of choices. Check out some of the best below

Explore More

### Travel the World, One Plate at a Time

Although Houston is consistently ranked as one of the most diverse cities in the U.S., visitors might be surprised at how broad the food scene is. But if your ideal adventure involves stopping for a bite in every continent, chances are you won’t have to leave Houston to make that dream a reality.

Explore More

### A Glass Worth Raising

If the night calls for celebration, Houston has more than its fair share of world-class bars to fit the bill. From creative but classic cocktail essentials like Anvil, Captain Foxheart’s Bad News Bar, and James Beard award-winning Julep, to themed oases such as tropical Lei Low, intergalactic Roswell’s Saloon, and vinyl-lined speakeasy Off the Record, there’s plenty of good times to be had.
