Boasting over 500 rooms and suites that have been fully renovated as of 2023, we provide a truly upscale experience with modernized spaces designed to make your stay as relaxing and enjoyable as possible. With picturesque views of the city’s skyline, easy access to shopping in The Galleria, a near...See More walk to the iconic Gerald D. Whether you’re driving through the Historic Heights area, strolling through one of the city’s many green spaces, or taking a tour of Houston’s extensive downtown tunnel system,...See More there’s always more waiting to be explored.See Less

### Shop Local

The Galleria attracts more than 30 million visitors yearly, making it Texas’ largest shopping center and Houston’s most sought-after retail space for locals and visitors alike. Spanning 2.4 million square feet and housing 400 stores and restaurants, a full-sized ice rink, a state-of-the-art children’s play area, two swimming pools, and endless opportunities for upscale shopping, there’s always more to take in.

Learn More

### A Short Stroll to Houston’s Iconic Waterwall

The Gerald D. Hines Waterwall Park, Houston’s celebrated multi-story sculptural fountain, sits just opposite the Williams Tower in Uptown Houston. Completed in 1983, the 64-foot-high fountain spills over with 11,000 gallons of water per minute. Open from 8 AM to 9 PM daily, visitors will find it a conveniently quick walk from the hotel and an even quicker walk from nearby Galleria attractions.

Learn More

### Make the Most of Your Stay

Houston’s iconic shopping & dynamic architecture are just the beginning. For culture-seekers, Houston’s world-class Museum District and Theater District can’t be missed. Families and couples will enjoy Houston’s Zoo, Aquarium, and Botanic Garden, and visitors of all ages will find adventure at Houston’s NASA Space Center. For nature lovers, Houston’s Arboretum, Memorial Park, and Bayou Park will not disappoint.
