# Welcome to JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria

## Explore Our Luxury Hotel Near the Galleria Houston

Welcome to the completely renovated JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria. Situated across from the Galleria mall, our hotel in Uptown Houston allows guests easy access to shopping and dining experiences. For those traveling with family, our Houston, TX Galleria hotel is conveniently located near the Houston Zoo and Downtown Aquarium. Our on-site restaurant, Stray Horse, is open for breakfast, lunch,...See More and dinner and features elevated cuisine with regional flavors. Visit JW Market for coffee and grab-and-go options. Stay on track with your fitness goals in our spacious fitness center and yoga room. Enjoy the fresh air at our rooftop pool overlooking the city or enhance your experience with a private pool cabana. Our 41,500 sq. ft. of renovated space offers memorable settings where events can thrive. Throughout your stay at our luxury hotel in Houston, let us treat you to seamless service and an enriching experience. ### Sustainable Stay, Elevated

Our hotel proudly partners with CIRQU, a smarter way to recycle that keeps plastic and aluminum out of landfills. Powered by PepsiCo and Replenysh, the program provides real‑time impact data, transparent recycling practices, and effortless collection, all helping us reduce waste and support true circularity during your stay.

Learn More

### Houston’s First Green Leaf Hotel

As the first hotel in Houston to earn Green Leaf Certification, we proudly align with the city’s bold environmental initiatives. Guided by sustainability strategies that encourage waste reduction, efficiency, and long‑term resilience, this recognition reflects our commitment to supporting Houston’s greener future while offering guests an eco‑forward luxury experience.

Learn More

### Nourish Houston Together

By donating surplus, chef‑prepared food to Second Servings, Houston’s only prepared and perishable food rescue organization, our hotel helps ensure high‑quality meals reach local shelters and nonprofits. Our hotel values their mission to fight hunger and reduce waste for communities across the city.

Learn More

### Grown On-Site, Served Fresh

Powered by Babylon Micro‑Farms’ on‑site vertical farming technology, our hotel elevates its F&B offerings with ultra‑fresh, hyper‑local produce grown sustainably right on property. This eco‑conscious system enhances culinary creativity while reducing waste, and its presence further amplifies the JW Garden philosophy, bringing the farm even closer to your table.

Learn More

### Designed for a Greener Future

As a LEED‑certified property, our hotel follows the U.S. Green Building Council’s rigorous sustainability framework, reducing energy use, water consumption, and environmental impact while creating a healthier indoor environment for every guest. This commitment is core to who we are and your stay supports a hotel that truly prioritizes sustainability.

Learn More

### Where Events Meet Sustainability

By participating in Marriott’s Connect Responsibly program, our hotel advances meaningful sustainability in every event we host. From responsible sourcing and reduced single‑use items to food‑waste prevention and access to carbon‑offset options, we’re committed, along with Marriott International, to delivering impactful, eco‑focused gatherings for every guest.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria. Self parking is available for $37.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria. The daily valet fee is $69.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria is William P Hobby Airport (HOU). HOU is located approximately 16.6 Miles from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria have an Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Houston by The Galleria has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
