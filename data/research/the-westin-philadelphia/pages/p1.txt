# Welcome to The Westin Philadelphia

## Wellness-Inspired Stay at Hotel in Downtown Philadelphia, PA

Stay refreshed and rejuvenated at The Westin Philadelphia, one of the premier hotels in downtown Philadelphia. Perfectly located between City Hall and Rittenhouse Square, our hotel puts you within easy...See More reach of top attractions—just 6 minutes from the Philadelphia Museum of Art, 10 minutes walk away from the Franklin Institute & Pennsylvania Convention Center, and 1.3 miles from historic Independence Hall. You’re also just minutes from the Christmas Village, Philadelphia Eagles and Phillies. Whether you're here for business, a weekend getaway, or a celebration, we offer everything you need to feel your best. Relax in newly renovated accommodations featuring our signature Heavenly Bed®, stay energized in the elevated fitness center, and enjoy 24-hour room service. Savor creative fare at Lea Restaurant & Bar, or plan exceptional events with discounted group rates and versatile venues equipped with expert planning, AV, and catering. ### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Philadelphia?

The check-in time at The Westin Philadelphia is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Philadelphia allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Philadelphia. with a maximum weight of 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $100.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Westin Philadelphia?

On-site valet parking is available at The Westin Philadelphia. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Philadelphia have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Philadelphia has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Philadelphia booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Philadelphia?

The closest airport near The Westin Philadelphia is Philadelphia International Airport (PHL). PHL is located approximately 6.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Westin Philadelphia have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Philadelphia does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
