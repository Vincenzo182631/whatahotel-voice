Modern Sophistication in Chengdu
Signature Accommodations
Cutting a striking silhouette of Chengdu skyline, The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu evokes timeless luxury and urban sophistication offering sweeping views of the city, and luxury hotel accommodations highlight this enviable feature with large windows and breathtaking vistas. Suites and Club Level accommodations offer additional space, services and inspiring amenities.The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy and comfort with personalized service and attention to detail.
Club Ritz-Carlton Suite
Boasting iconic views over Chengdu city skyline, the Club Ritz-Carlton Suite is an expression of timeless and distinct luxury. Designed with materials and bespoke furnishings inspired by the city, it is the ultimate space with 105sqm for a tranquil retreat. Indulge in separate living room spaces, spacious bedroom, inviting bathtub and more with the option to interconnect with the adjoining room, ensuring you can make the most of the luxurious space.
Club Premier Suite
Presenting panoramic views across the city, Club Premier Suite of The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu is an expression of timeless and distinct luxury with 97sqm, and offers a sweeping view of Chengdu downtown view and breathtaking city skyline.
