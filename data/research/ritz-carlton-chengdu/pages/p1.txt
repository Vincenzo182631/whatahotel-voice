Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu
A truly luxury hotel in Chengdu
The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu, overlooking the city's central Tianfu Square, offers guests an ideal base to explore the best of Chengdu. With guest rooms from the 28th to the 41st floor, this 350-room luxury hotel features unmatched views of downtown Chengdu paired with the comfort of personalized service. 105 Ritz-Carlton Club Level rooms and 54 suites feature dedicated concierge service and a host of thoughtful accouterments. Dining options include Li Xuan, an elegant Chinese restaurant with eight private dining rooms, FLAIR, the highest lifestyle restaurant and bar in town, and SPICES, an international all-day dining restaurant. A contemporary lobby lounge offers signature English Afternoon Tea. Meeting spaces include a Grand Ballroom, Junior Ballroom and seven additional meeting rooms totaling 1,716 square meters of function space. Guests will find relaxation and wellness within a full-floor luxury spa and an indoor swimming pool with underwater music.
Chengdu, Sichuan, China
Begin your exploration of this dynamic cultural destination with the tourism destinations of Tianfu Square, Chengdu Museum, and the bustling center of commerce with further tourism destinations such as the Chengdu Research Base for Giant Panda Breeding, Mount Qingcheng, Dujiangyan Irrigation System (with an exceptional hotel in Chengdu’s city center). Within walking distance from many of the city’s landmarks and attractions, The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu offers amazing views of Tianfu Square and Chengdu 339 TV Tower, while offering great convenience to travelers wishing to discover Chengdu’s rich cultural heritage and modern pioneering spirit.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu?
On-site self parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu. Self parking is complimentary for guests.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu is Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU). CTU is located approximately 19.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu have electric vehicle charging stations?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
