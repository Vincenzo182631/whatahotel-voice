A Sophisticated Sichuan Restaurant in Chengdu
Dining Room, Private Terrace Chef’s Table
Carefully curated wine pairing menu features Sichuan local produce and native spices. This dining experience is themed around terroir, spotlighting two local ingredients: a wild‑foraged herb from a specific Western Sichuan mountainside, and heritage‑breed pork from our partner farm, aged for a set number of days to enhance its flavor. Secure exclusive terrace use for up to four guests, with fully personalized table decor, a live chef crafting your bespoke main course to your preference, and a sommelier on hand throughout. Daily available from 18:00-22:00. Advance booking of 2 days is required.

Li Xuan Private Dining Room
Trinity of Sichuan Tea, Caviar & Cocktail Experience
Exclusive dining experience featuring Sichuan teas and fine caviar. This curated dining experience weaves Sichuan tea culture and premium Ya’an caviar into one harmonious journey. We present 10 signature Sichuan teas that fill every moment with lingering fragrance, paired with 3 vintages of Ya’an caviar nurtured by snow-melt spring water from the snow-capped mountains. Centered on caviar as the star ingredient, the exclusive menu delivers an elegant fusion of oriental tea elegance and world-class luxury, creating an unforgettable sensory feast. Daily available from 12:00 to 21:00. Advance booking of 2 days is required.

Frequently Asked Questions
What restaurants does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu have on property?
The on-property restaurants at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu are:
Li Xuan Chinese Restaurant
SPICES All-day Dining Restaurant
Dining Room
The Lobby Lounge
In Room Dining
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu offer room service?
No, room service is not offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu. You can view other dining options on our
dining page
.
What cuisine is offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu?
The cuisines offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu are:
Mediterranean at Dining Room
Chinese at Li Xuan Chinese Restaurant
Other at The Lobby Lounge
International at SPICES All-day Dining Restaurant, In Room Dining
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu offer breakfast?
Yes, breakfast is available onsite at SPICES All-day Dining Restaurant and In Room Dining for a fee.
