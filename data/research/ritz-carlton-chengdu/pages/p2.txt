Modern Sophistication in Chengdu’s Premier Club Room
The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining intimacy and comfort with personalized service and attention to detail. The Club Lounge at The Ritz-Carlton, Chengdu is located on the 38th floor and features a dedicated butler and concierge staff. This exclusive suite offers global cuisine throughout the day, prepared to order at live cooking stations. Private meeting rooms are available upon request.

Exceptional Club Level Accommodations
Located on levels 38 to 41, The Ritz-Carlton Club® provides the finest luxury Chengdu suites and our undivided personal attention. Our Club Level in Chengdu features impressive views of Tianfu Square in addition to spacious walk-in closets, paired wash basins, separate showers and bathtubs and more. Ritz-Carlton Club® Suites feature 400-thread-count bedding, state-of-the-art amenities and personal wake-up service for the ultimate Ritz-Carlton experience.
