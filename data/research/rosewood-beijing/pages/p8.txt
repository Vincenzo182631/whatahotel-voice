Dark woods, curated art, and shelves filled with curios and books give Rosewood Beijing the feeling of a sophisticated private residence rather than a typical high-rise luxury hotel. The property leans heavily into residential warmth, balancing contemporary design with subtle references to Chinese craftsmanship and history. Guest rooms are exceptionally spacious by Beijing standards, with marble bathrooms, deep soaking tubs, and floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the city skyline. The hotel’s dining scene is a major draw, particularly Country Kitchen, with refined northern Chinese dishes, and The House of Dynasties, awarded one Michelin star.
