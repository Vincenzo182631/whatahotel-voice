# Dining & drinks

With two spectacular restaurants, a beautiful lounge overlooking the Royal Mews, two bars and 24-hour room service, The Rubens at the Palace gives diners plenty of choice for every occasion. From classic British fare to globally inspired dishes, our chefs curate a symphony of flavours that transcends the ordinary. At The Rubens, we invite you to immerse yourself in a world where every meal is an affair to remember, and each sip is a celebration of taste.
