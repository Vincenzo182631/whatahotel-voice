### Interconnecting rooms

**Interconnecting rooms** have a door inside that connects two rooms together.

**Interleading rooms** feature an external door that links multiple rooms, allowing access to all rooms booked as interleading.

We also offer **family wings**, which connect several rooms with internal doors.

These rooms can accommodate four to 12 people, depending on availability, and must be booked directly through our reservations team. To check availability, please email us.
