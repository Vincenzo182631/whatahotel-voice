# London Family Stays Opposite Buckingham Palace

Proudly recognised as a Gold winner in the 2026 Junior Travel Awards for Best City Hotel for Families, The Rubens at the Palace is celebrated as one of London’s leading choices for family and multi-generational stays. With spacious rooms, flexible layouts, and a welcoming team, The Rubens offers a warm and characterful base in the heart of royal London.

Whether you are planning a school holiday escape, a celebratory London break, or a special stay bringing together grandparents, parents and children, The Rubens creates a royally enchanting experience for every generation.

### Royal London at your feet

Overlooking the Royal Mews of Buckingham Palace, The Rubens places you at the heart of royal London, with iconic landmarks, green parks and cultural highlights all within easy reach - making it effortless to fill each day with something new for every generation.

\- Buckingham Palace and the Changing of the Guard, right on your doorstep

\- St James’s Park, perfect for relaxed walks and spotting pelicans

\- The Household Cavalry Museum, offering a glimpse into ceremonial life

\- Westminster Abbey, a short stroll away for a moment of history and grandeur

\- The West End theatres, ideal for an unforgettable family outing to Hamilton or Wicked

\- Elizabeth Street in nearby Belgravia, perfect for a gentle afternoon of boutique shopping and cafés

## Family dining at the Rubens

At The Rubens at the Palace, family time is often shaped around the table, from relaxed breakfasts before a day of exploring to celebratory lunches, early suppers with little ones, and evenings filled with laughter, stories and shared moments across generations. Children under 12 dine complimentary when accompanied by a full-paying adult.

Dietary requirements are accommodated wherever possible, with menus carefully designed to delight younger guests while ensuring everyone feels completely at home.

### The Palace Lounge

The Palace Lounge at The Rubens at the Palace offers a quintessentially English setting for afternoon tea, with panoramic views over the Royal Mews of Buckingham Palace. Light-filled and elegant, the space reflects its royal surroundings through plush furnishings, intricate detailing and a timeless sense of occasion.

From morning coffee and pastries to a refined Royal Afternoon Tea served from midday, including the much-loved Prince and Princess Afternoon Tea for younger guests, the Palace Lounge provides a relaxed yet sophisticated setting for every generation.

Discover more

### The English Grill

The English Grill is The Rubens at the Palace’s elegant dining room, offering refined British cuisine served with warm, intuitive hospitality. From celebratory family lunches to relaxed evening dining, it provides a welcoming setting where every generation can feel at home, with beautifully crafted dishes and thoughtful service.

Children are warmly welcomed, with The Young Royals’ Table menu specially created for guests under 12. This dedicated children’s menu features comforting favourites alongside healthier options, including vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free choices.

Discover more

### The Curry Room

The Curry Room at The Rubens at the Palace offers an intimate and richly atmospheric setting, where authentic Indian cuisine is elevated through refined presentation and warm, attentive service. The menu celebrates the depth and diversity of regional Indian flavours.

The Curry Room provides a welcoming and flavourful dining experience, with dishes to suit a variety of tastes and preferences, ensuring every guest feels considered. Whether for a relaxed family dinner or a special occasion, it is a setting where vibrant flavours and shared moments come together effortlessly.

Discover more

### Pre-Theatre Dining for the Whole Family

We’re delighted to offer families and theatre-goers a 15% saving on pre-theatre dining at our welcoming bars and restaurants. Whether you’re enjoying a special outing with children or gathering before a West End favourite, it’s a wonderful way to begin your experience together.

**Please note:** To receive the 15% theatre-goer discount, guests must present a valid same-day theatre ticket for _SIX_, _Hamilton_ or _Wicked_ at the time of dining. If a valid ticket cannot be shown, the bill will be charged at the full price.

Discover more

### Prince & Princess Afternoon Tea for Little Royals

A truly delightful treat for younger guests, our Prince and Princess Afternoon Tea is thoughtfully created to bring a sense of occasion to every family visit. Enjoy a selection of delicate finger sandwiches, freshly baked scones served with preserves and cream, and beautifully crafted pastries, all accompanied by a choice of milkshake, hot chocolate, tea or a soft drink.

**Price**: £45 per child under 12
