# What not to miss

Discover what to taste and sip, where to slumber and venture, what experiences to enjoy, and who to connect with during your stay at The Rubens at the Palace.

> "I am deeply proud of everything we have to offer, but a few key moments do stand out to me. Allow me to introduce what not to miss during your stay."

Malcolm Hendry

Managing Director

### Eat

Delight your palate with the fish of the day, expertly prepared to highlight the natural flavours of the day's catch. Our chefs carefully select the freshest and most exquisite specimens from Billingsgate Market to create truly extraordinary plates.

Discover The English Grill

### Drink

Step into the timeless elegance of The Cavalry Bar and indulge in the classic sophistication of the Negroni. Crafted with precision and care, our Negroni is a testament to the enduring allure of this iconic cocktail, promising an unforgettable first sip.

Discover The Cavalry Bar

### Sleep

Indulge in the regal splendour of our Royal Rooms, where every detail is designed to immerse you in luxury and history. Adorned with exquisite portraits and royal accents, each room is a tribute to the illustrious heritage of the British monarchy.

Discover our Royal Rooms

### Explore

From the bustling energy of the Changing of the Guard ceremony to leisurely strolls through the picturesque gardens, the allure of Buckingham Palace is palpable. The proximity of this historic site ensures you're never far from the heart of London's royal heritage.

Discover our neighbourhood

### Experience

Immerse yourself in the vibrant tapestry of London's culinary scene with our exclusive sustainable experience. Bringing the essence of renowned Borough Market directly to our guests, we offer a gastronomic journey unlike any other.

Discover our experiences

### Meet

Meet Charlie Banks, the warm and welcoming face of The Rubens at the Palace as our esteemed Rooms Division Manager. With an infectious enthusiasm for hospitality, Charlie is always ready to go the extra mile to make guests feel at home.
