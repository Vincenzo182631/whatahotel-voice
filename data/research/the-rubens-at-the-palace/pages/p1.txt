# A royal London stay

A warm welcome to The Rubens at the Palace, part of The Red Carnation Hotel Collection. We are proud to be located directly opposite the Royal Mews at Buckingham Palace, just a five-minute walk from Victoria Station. Our welcoming team are on-hand this summer to ensure that your stay with us is both comfortable and memorable. Whether you are coming to stay, visiting for a Royal Afternoon Tea, or celebrating something special in The English Grill – we look forward to exceeding your expectations.
