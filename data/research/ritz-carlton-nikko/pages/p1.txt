Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko
Embrace soothing luxury at our hotel in Nikko, Japan
Nestled within Nikko National Park, our luxury hotel in Nikko, Japan offers spacious guest rooms with graceful Japanese design, showcasing serene views of Lake Chuzenji and Mount Nantai. Indulge in local Tochigi ingredients at our restaurants and bars. Elevate your stay with the world's first hot spring for the Ritz-Carlton brand, sourced from the Yumoto Onsen. Explore our activities and discover a place where nature, traditional culture, and spirituality converge at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko.
Onsen Partial Renovation
Please be advised that our Onsen facilities will undergo partial renovations on the following dates: July 6 – July 10, and July 13 – July 17. Our Onsen facilities will undergo a rotating schedule during these periods. We apologize for any inconvenience caused.

Introducing Our New Omamori
As part of our annual hotel anniversary tradition, we're proud to introduce the newest color of our signature Omamori, Shinsui. Journey to the Heart of Things
Set within Nikko National Park in the mountainous region of Oku-Nikko (Inner-Nikko), our luxury hotel in Nikko, Japan, immerses guests in the ever-changing palette of Japan’s natural beauty. Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko allow pets?
No, pets are not allowed at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko?
On-site parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko. Self parking is available for ¥3000.00 per day. Valet parking is available for ¥3000.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko is Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT). NRT is located approximately 208.0 KM from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
