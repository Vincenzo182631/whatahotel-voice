Admire lake and mountain views from our Nikko hotel rooms
Suites & Rooms
The shrines and temples have earned Nikko, Japan a place on UNESCO’s World Heritage list. The region's history and beauty is told through our hotel rooms and suites. Each offers a spacious layout with an engawa, or porch, a lake or mountain view, and décor that blends minimalist and traditional touches.
Onsen Partial Renovation
Please be advised that our Onsen facilities will undergo partial renovations on the following dates: July 6 – July 10, and July 13 – July 17. Our Onsen facilities will undergo a rotating schedule during these periods. We apologize for any inconvenience caused.

The Ritz-Carlton Suite Privileges
Thoughtfully designed to further elevate your stay, The Ritz-Carlton Suite now includes a collection of exclusive privileges available year-round. Guests reserving The Ritz-Carlton Suite may enjoy an elevated stay experience with a collection of exclusive privileges, including complimentary daily breakfast for two guests, valet parking for up to two vehicles, one-way or round-trip transfer service between the hotel and Haneda Airport, Narita Airport, or Tokyo’s 23 wards*, as well as complimentary in-room minibar access throughout the stay**.
*Advance reservation is required at least one week prior to arrival.
**Additional charges apply for selections from the wine cellar service.
