Achieve Balance and Harmony at Our Nikko Day Spa and Onsen
Spa, Wellness and Hot Springs
The Spa at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko, is a haven of serenity and sophistication. Here, your well-being is enhanced not only by the sublime natural setting and the full range of luxury treatments on offer but also by the extensive indoor and outdoor Yumoto onsen hot-spring facility, where cares slip away in the healing geothermal waters. Ever-attentive, genuine service completes this remarkable journey to balance and harmony.

Onsen Experience
Our hotel spa is home to the world’s first The Ritz-Carlton branded onsen, which draws its natural hot spring water direct from Nikko Yumoto Onsen, a renowned magical hot spring destination ever since its discovery more than 1200 years ago. Immerse in the warm embrace of relaxation to soothe body and soul.
Quality
Our hydrogen sulfide simple sulfur spring maintains a temperature of 78.6 °C. The source water is low in acidity, colorless, and transparent, but turns milky white when exposed to air with a natural sulfuric scent.

Onsen Policies
Hours
Staying guests are welcome to use the facility between their check-in and check-out times. The facility is open from 5:30 a.m. to 11 p.m., with the exception of a daily two-hour cleaning period between noon and 2 p.m.
Day Visitors
Non-staying guests who have reserved the Day Spa Treatment may use the Onsen. Otherwise, the facility is for staying guests only.
Photographs
No photographs. For the privacy and comfort of all guests, cellphones and cameras may not be used anywhere in the Onsen facility, including the dressing rooms. Social media posts are strictly prohibited.
Tattoos
Tattoos are welcomed at The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko. Use of the facility by those affiliated with criminal groups is not.
Small Children
Potty-trained children may use the baths; others may not. Swim diapers are not allowed in the Onsen.
Minimum Age
Guests aged 16 and under must be accompanied by someone aged 20 or older.
Bathing With Children
Mixed bathing (with a parent or caregiver of the opposite sex) is allowed for children under the age of six.
Frequently Asked Questions
Do the guest rooms have hot-spring water too?
No, hot-spring water is featured only in our Onsen facility.
Do you have private Onsen baths for family use?
No, we do not. However, guests who have made a spa appointment have the option to reserve a connecting private open-air hot-spring.
I’ve had a mastectomy. Can post-surgery bathing wear be worn in the Onsen?
Yes. Please bring your own bathing wear and feel free to use it.

The Art of Wellness
Bring healing and wellness to the mind, body & soul with this unique transformative treatment that creates a state of pure bliss. Bamboo Muscle Reviving Massage
This energetic full body treatment is ideal for improving flexibility and joint mobility. Free flowing focused massage using warm bamboo releases deep tension, slows a busy mind, and helps elongate tight muscles to leave you feeling relaxed and revitalized.
Natural Resilience Facial
This specialized facial is the complete approach to skin health. Powerful age defying techniques inspired by Japanese Kobido massage, along with cool and smooth Jade rollers that facilitate lymphatic drainage and provide results, leaving the skin firm, lifted, and beautifully radiant.
