The Discipline of Craftsmanship
Japanese Restaurant by The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko
Japanese Restaurant by The Ritz-Carlton, Nikko's unpretentious name hints at a simple formula – traditional Japanese dedication to hand-crafted culinary expertise, presented within a sophisticated space for a more contemporary feel. The result is a refreshingly unique dining experience that honors the beauty and precision of local craftsmanship on display throughout the Tochigi prefecture. The master chefs and experienced staff at this Nikko Japanese restaurant serve impeccably prepared meals using the region’s plentiful natural ingredients. Local meat and fish are sourced fresh from nearby markets, while exciting craft sakes from local brewers each tell a story to add heritage and flavor for an extra, refined twist.
