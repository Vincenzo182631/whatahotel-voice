### Best stay ever, we will be back soon!

13 days ago

From the moment we arrived until the moment we left, this stay was exceptional in every way. We stayed in the Mediterranean Suite, and it was beautiful, with a breathtaking view and an amazingly clean, fresh feel every single day. A heartfelt thank you to the cleaning staff for their care and attention to detail.

The staff throughout the hotel were super kind, professional, helpful, and accommodating. A special thank you to Leah, Sofia, Martina, and Zehavit, who made us feel truly welcome and cared for. The reception team and general management set a remarkable tone, and it shows in every part of the hotel.

We loved the famous Jaffa Court, with its wonderful food and gracious service. The Club InterContinental Lounge is simply amazing, with warm, attentive service and a stunning view over the Mediterranean.

The location could not be better. The hotel sits right on the beachfront promenade, with the charming streets of Neve Tzedek on its doorstep and the Carmel Market and Old Jaffa just a short walk away. It is the perfect base for enjoying the sea and exploring the best of Tel Aviv.

This is hospitality at its very best. We love Israel, and we will come back again soon to show our love and support. Thank you all for making our stay unforgettable!

### Poor service

a month ago

I have stayed at quite a few hotels around the world, including numerous five-star properties, and I have never experienced such dismissive and irresponsible treatment, especially from a hotel brand that presents itself as a luxury brand.

Even before I arrived at the hotel, I was already having issues with my reservation because I had booked it through the Israeli Teachers’ Organization. All I wanted was to have the points from my stay credited to my IHG account. Eventually, after a great deal of back-and-forth with IHG itself, the points were added — because, of course, the hotel did not think it was necessary to assist me with this matter.

Then came further confusion with the hotel. For example, my reservation clearly stated that the room included lounge access, yet when I arrived, I was told that it did not. Why mislead a guest at the time of booking and then provide completely different information upon arrival?

There was also the issue of valet parking. After a long drive to Tel Aviv, and being unfamiliar with the parking facilities, I politely asked at reception if someone could take the car down to the parking area, as valet service is listed among the hotel’s services — and especially given that this is supposed to be a five-star hotel. Instead, I was simply told to park the car myself.

Later, we had an issue with the air conditioning in the room. I went down to reception to ask someone to come and take care of it, as no one was answering the phone, and yet no one ever came to fix the problem.

The room itself was pleasant enough, with a good shower and a beautiful view of Tel Aviv, although it did not feel particularly luxurious for the standard one would expect from a five-star hotel. The pool was also pleasant and quiet. I stayed at the beginning of the week, so fortunately it was relatively peaceful. On my second visit to the pool, a member of staff accompanied me to the area and brought me a towel, which gave me a slightly better sense of personal service — something that is otherwise noticeably lacking throughout this hotel.

The restaurant in the evening serves good food (unfortunately, only the dairy restaurant was open), but there was also a mistake with my order. After waiting quite a while for the dish, I then had to wait again for the correct order. We were eventually offered some compensation in the form of complimentary drinks, which was at least something.

Ultimately, based on Zara’s response from the hotel management and the response I received from IHG Customer Care, it seems that neither the hotel nor the chain itself is willing to take responsibility for mistakes and failures that occur both before and after check-in. This significantly reduces my willingness to return to this hotel, or even to choose this chain again.

Unfortunately, because of the numerous mistakes and, most importantly, because I received no adequate response or resolution from the hotel, I feel compelled to give it a poor rating.

What seems to be missing here in Israel, but is well understood by luxury hotels abroad, is that service is everything. Once the InterContinental understands that, perhaps it will become enjoyable to return. At the moment, that is simply not the case.
