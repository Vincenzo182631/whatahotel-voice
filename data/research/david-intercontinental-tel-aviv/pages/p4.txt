### Poolside Paradise

Lounge by our sparkling pool with stunning Mediterranean views. Whether you’re basking in quiet time or diving into family fun, our pool is the perfect place to enjoy a breath of fresh air, a light snack or a cocktail from the MAYIM pool bar. Our kids’ pool offers a shaded and secure play area for your little ones. Our pool is open seasonally.

Learn more

### Family Focused Time

Create unforgettable family memories with a range of seasonal kids' activities and spacious pool facilities. Our large connecting rooms provide ample space for the whole family to relax and unwind. Young travelers can enjoy a dedicated check-in area at our reception and special welcome amenities tailored just for them.

Learn more

### The Art of Tea, Rooted in Israel

Discover the essence of Israel through every sip. At InterContinental David Tel Aviv, guests can enjoy Adanim Tea, a family-owned Israeli tea house crafting organic blends since 1982. A thoughtful local touch that brings authentic flavour, heritage and craftsmanship to your stay.

### The executive lounge experience

Overlooking the Mediterranean, the Executive Lounge is an elegant retreat designed for moments of connection and relaxation. Open daily from 12:30 to 22:00, this family-friendly sanctuary invites guests to unwind with curated refreshments, attentive service and a refined atmosphere throughout the day.

### THE Spa

Intimate and serene, THE Spa boasts seven treatment rooms including a luxury couples’ suite, saunas, and a relaxation space. Each treatment on the diverse menu has been selected from world cultures to present you with the finest collection of traditional and innovative experiences. No matter what you choose, you'll feel rejuvenated at THE Spa.

Learn more
