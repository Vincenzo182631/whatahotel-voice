# InterContinental David Tel AvivDestination

Discover the wonder of Tel Aviv from our prime location within walking distance of top attractions, major business centres, and embassies. From beaches and Neve Tzedek to The Carmel Market, and ancient charm of Jaffo, Tel Aviv offers a nonstop adventure.

1. Tel Aviv’s Beachfront Promenade

2. Stunning Sea Views

### Tel Aviv’s Beachfront Promenade

Step out and into the rhythm of the Mediterranean. The Tayelet, Tel Aviv’s iconic promenade, stretches 14 km from the lively Tel Aviv Port to historic Jaffa. Joggers, cyclists, café lovers, and sunset seekers gather here, just steps from your sanctuary at InterContinental David Tel Aviv.

### Stunning Sea Views

More than 90% of our rooms feature breathtaking Mediterranean Sea views, inviting you into a realm of tranquility and connection. Whether enjoying breakfast on your balcony, relaxing by the pool or savoring a drink at the Club Lounge, the ever-present Mediterranean Sea weaves peace and inspiration into every moment.

## Things to do

### The Carmel Market Tel Aviv

44 Rue HaCarmel St, Tel Aviv-Yaffa,

Discover the vibrant atmosphere of The Carmel Market. This bustling street market immerses you in the essence of Tel Aviv. Stroll through its lively stalls and encounter a rich tapestry of sights, sounds and flavors. From colorful displays of fresh produce to aromatic spices and a wide array of clothing and souvenirs, this market is a sensory delight. Engage with friendly vendors, taste local delicacies and find unique treasures that capture the spirit of Tel Aviv.

Learn more

### Neve Tzedek

Rue Shabazi, Tel Aviv-Yaffa,

Neve Tzedek is a historic neighborhood that weaves together old-world charm and modern creativity. Its picturesque streets are lined with quaint boutiques, trendy cafes and vibrant art galleries. As you explore the neighborhood, you'll be captivated by its architectural beauty that defines the heart of the city.

Learn more

### The Promenade of Tel Aviv (the Tayelet)

Promenade Herbert Samuel, Tel Aviv-Yaffa,

Take in the breathtaking beauty of Tel Aviv's shoreline as you stroll along the Promenade of Tel Aviv. This scenic esplanade stretches 14 kms along the city's stunning coastline, providing panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the iconic Tel Aviv skyline. Jog along the path or unwind in one of the many parks along the way. The Tayelet offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and urban charm.

Learn more

### Old Jaffa

Place Kedumim, Vieux Jaffa, Tel Aviv-Yaffa,

Step back in time and explore the historical wonders of Old Jaffa. This captivating area is where ancient history and contemporary artistry meet. Stroll narrow, winding alleys filled with art galleries, charming cafes and unique boutiques. Old Jaffa's rich history as a port city is evident in its architecture and the compelling stories embedded within its cobblestone streets. Visit Old Jaffa and experience the essence of Tel Aviv.

Learn more

### White City UNESCO Heritage Site

Boulevard Rotschild, Tel Aviv,

Discover the rich history and architectural splendor of Tel Aviv's White City, a UNESCO World Heritage site nestled around Rothschild Boulevard. Stroll the tree-lined boulevard and marvel at the iconic Bauhaus buildings, renowned for their distinct modernist design and historic significance. This cultural hub features a blend of art, culture, and innovation, with charming cafes, boutique shops, and art galleries.

Learn more

### Chelouche Gallery​

Hazorfim St. 15, Old Jaffa​, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

A staple of Tel Aviv's contemporary art scene, this gallery is set in the historic charm of Old Jaffa. It presents a dynamic collection of works from Israeli and international artists, offering a dialogue between modern creativity and ancient ambiance.​

### Sommer Contemporary Art​

Shvil Hatnufa St. 6, 4th Floor, Tel Aviv-Yafo

Focused on innovative and thought-provoking exhibitions, this gallery provides a platform for emerging and established artists in a sleek, modern space that encourages exploration and reflection.​

### Corridor Contemporary​

Shabazi St 39, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

Located in the artistic heart of Neve Tzedek, this vibrant gallery combines contemporary art with urban sophistication, featuring curated exhibitions and limited-edition prints.​

### Tel Aviv Museum of Art​

The Golda Meir Cultural and Art Center, Sderot Sha'ul HaMelech 27, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

A cultural landmark showcasing an impressive collection of modern and classical art, alongside rotating international exhibitions. Its architecture is as striking as the art within.​

### Bauhaus Foundation

Bialik St 21, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

A tribute to the city’s iconic architectural style, this museum offers an insightful look at Tel Aviv’s modernist heritage through design exhibits and historical context.

### Tel Aviv City Museum​

Bialik St 27, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

Housed in the city’s former town hall, this museum traces Tel Aviv’s history through multimedia displays, urban models, and personal stories.​

### Ilana Goor Museum​

Mazal Dagim St 4, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

This eclectic museum in Old Jaffa blends art, design, and sculpture in a unique personal collection that reflects the artist’s global influences.​

### TLV Fashion Mall​

Hachashmonaim St. 94​, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

One of the city’s most stylish retail destinations, the mall features top fashion brands, local designers, and trendy dining spots, all within a sleek and modern setting.​

### Cameri Theatre​

Sderot Sha'ul HaMelech 19, Tel Aviv-Yafo,

A leading venue for Israeli drama and performance, the Cameri offers a rich repertoire ranging from classic plays to contemporary productions.​

### Beach Water Sports Center​

A go-to destination for sea lovers, this center offers everything from paddleboarding to jet skiing, right on the Tel Aviv shoreline.​
