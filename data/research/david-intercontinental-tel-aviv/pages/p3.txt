# InterContinental David Tel AvivDining

Experience the captivating flavors of Tel Aviv's Mediterranean-inspired cuisine at our restaurants. Our chefs highlight the finest Israeli ingredients, crafting each dish as a fusion of creativity and tradition.

**In-room dining hours:** 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM

1. The Art of Pastry by Alon Shabo

2. InterContinental Club Lounge

3. Flavours of Tel Aviv

4. Atrium Lobby Bar

### The Art of Pastry by Alon Shabo

Discover the creations of Alon Shabo, one of Israel’s leading pastry chefs, now available at our Atrium Lobby Bar. Renowned for his artistry, impeccable craftsmanship and premium ingredients, his handcrafted pastries bring one of Tel Aviv’s most celebrated culinary experiences to InterContinental David Tel Aviv.

Learn more

### InterContinental Club Lounge

Located on the 24th floor of the hotel with breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea, Club InterContinental is the ultimate private urban haven. Available exclusively to guests with permitted access, it offers check-in and check-out services, breakfast, afternoon tea, all-day refreshments, and evening drinks with canapés for ages 18 and up.

Learn more

### Flavours of Tel Aviv

Indulge in the vibrant culinary scene of Tel Aviv at our restaurants and bar. Our chefs expertly blend Mediterranean flavors with locally sourced Israeli ingredients, creating a fusion of creativity and tradition in every dish. Experience the essence of Tel Aviv's gastronomy in every bite.

Learn more

### Atrium Lobby Bar

Enjoy all-day dining in a vibrant and inviting atmosphere at Atrium. The market-inspired menu emphasizes the finest in-season ingredients, delivering innovative, and flavorful dishes. Enjoy a classic Israeli breakfast, house-made pizzas, fresh green salads, signature wraps, and specialty cocktails.

Learn more

## Jaffa Court

Start your day with our renowned Israeli breakfast, featuring an abundant and diverse spread. Enjoy fresh salads, local cheeses, breads, pastries, egg dishes like shakshuka, and smoked and pickled fish. Sample a variety of fresh fruits, juices, cereals, and yogurts, along with specialties like hummus, halva, and tahini. This hearty breakfast highlights local flavors and ingredients, providing a rich and varied culinary experience.

## Atrium Lobby Bar

The Atrium Lobby Bar is the beating heart of our hotel where people come together to socialize and unwind. Enjoy our lively atmosphere, complete with happy hour specials, good music, and a varied menu. Whether you're catching up with friends or relaxing after a long day, the Atrium Bar is the perfect spot to gather and create memorable moments. Located in the middle of our spectacular and spacious lobby with 27 meters of high ceilings, the Atrium Bar boasts impressive architecture.
