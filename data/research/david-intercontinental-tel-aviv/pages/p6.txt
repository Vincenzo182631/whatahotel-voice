### Newly Renovated Rooms

Step into rooms where natural wood, layered textures and soft, considered lighting create a stillness that goes far beyond décor. Inspired by the concept of hygge, our newly renovated floors 5–10 have been designed to calm the senses—a retreat from the city that feels genuinely, deeply yours.

Learn more

### The Art of Pastry by Alon Shabo

Discover the creations of Alon Shabo, one of Israel’s leading pastry chefs, now available at our Atrium Lobby Bar. Renowned for his artistry, impeccable craftsmanship and premium ingredients, his handcrafted pastries bring one of Tel Aviv’s most celebrated culinary experiences to InterContinental David Tel Aviv.

Learn more

### InterContinental Club Lounge

Located on the 24th floor of the hotel with breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea, Club InterContinental is the ultimate private urban haven. Available exclusively to guests with permitted access, it offers check-in and check-out services, breakfast, afternoon tea, all-day refreshments, and evening drinks with canapés for ages 18 and up.

Learn more

### Poolside Paradise

Lounge by our sparkling pool with stunning Mediterranean views. Whether you’re basking in quiet time or diving into family fun, our pool is the perfect place to enjoy a breath of fresh air, a light snack or a cocktail from the MAYIM pool bar. Our kids’ pool offers a shaded and secure play area for your little ones. Our pool is open seasonally.

Learn more

### Family Focused Time

Create unforgettable family memories with a range of seasonal kids' activities and spacious pool facilities. Our large connecting rooms provide ample space for the whole family to relax and unwind. Young travelers can enjoy a dedicated check-in area at our reception and special welcome amenities tailored just for them.

Learn more

## FAQs for InterContinental David Tel Aviv

Does InterContinental David Tel Aviv have a pool?

InterContinental David Tel Aviv has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Our outdoor pool is an oasis of luxury and relaxation. Spacious, with beautiful views and a kid's shallow pool, it's the perfect place to soak up the sun. Enjoy the comfort of sunbeds and great refreshments from the pool bar, all with perfect service.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental David Tel Aviv?

The hotel has ample parking facilities, available to both hotel guests and conference attendees, at an additional cost. Charging station for Electric Vehicles (EV) is available in the parking area.

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental David Tel Aviv?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental David Tel Aviv.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental David Tel Aviv for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental David Tel Aviv have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental David Tel Aviv has 4 restaurants.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental David Tel Aviv?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental David Tel Aviv.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental David Tel Aviv?

Check-in at InterContinental David Tel Aviv is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

How far is InterContinental David Tel Aviv from the nearest airport?

InterContinental David Tel Aviv is conveniently located near Ben Gurion Airport (TLV), approximately 12.43 miles away (20 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental David Tel Aviv?

InterContinental David Tel Aviv is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- The Carmel Market Tel Aviv
- Neve Tzedek
- The Promenade of Tel Aviv (the Tayelet)
- Old Jaffa
- White City UNESCO Heritage Site
- Chelouche Gallery​ - 1.37 miles away (2.2 kilometers)
- Sommer Contemporary Art​ - 2.42 miles away (3.9 kilometers)
- Corridor Contemporary​ - 0.31 miles away (0.5 kilometers)
- Tel Aviv Museum of Art​ - 2.3 miles away (3.7 kilometers)
- Bauhaus Foundation - 1.55 miles away (2.5 kilometers)
- Tel Aviv City Museum​ - 1.55 miles away (2.5 kilometers)
- Ilana Goor Museum​ - 1.55 miles away (2.5 kilometers)
- TLV Fashion Mall​ - 1.68 miles away (2.7 kilometers)
- Cameri Theatre​ - 1.99 miles away (3.2 kilometers)
- Beach Water Sports Center​ - 0.93 miles away (1.5 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental David Tel Aviv puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
