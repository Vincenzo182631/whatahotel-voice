# Royal Suite

148 sqm / 1593 sq ft

1 King

Panoramic Old City & Pool View

Shower and Bathtub

Large Private Terrace

Separate Living & Dining Area

Kitchen with a Separate Entrance

Designed for visiting dignitaries and international heads of state, the Royal Suite in the David Citadel Hotel is named in honor of former Israeli Prime Minister, Yitzhak Rabin. Exhibiting designer Piero Lissoni's exceptional level of craftsmanship, this climate-controlled exclusive Jerusalem suite has a unique atmosphere of classical elegance.

It includes a spacious balcony with mesmerizing views of the Old City of Jerusalem, dining/living room with oak parquet flooring, vintage artwork, as well as a well-equipped kitchenette which includes a large refrigerator, microwave oven and espresso machine. The kitchenette has its own separate entrance for hotel servers.

The Royal Rabin Suite of the David Citadel features a generous bedroom with a king size bed, a study area with work desk and the luxurious Carrara marble bathroom has a separate bathtub, shower and bidet.

Room Size: 148 sqm / 1593 sq ft

Balcony Size: 63 sqm / 678 sq ft

Bed Type: 1 King

Occupancy: 2 adults
