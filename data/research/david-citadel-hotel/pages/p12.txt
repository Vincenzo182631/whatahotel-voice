# Eat & Drink

## Glorious Culinary Experiences

Kfir Misnikov, our “Conductor of Culinary Arts,” selects his menus from a smorgasbord of tastes, colors and styles, as our variety of restaurants and dining spaces represent the gamut of cultures that constitute Israeli society. With a focus on freshness, quality and diversity – oven-baked pastries,  choice cheeses and crunch-fresh salads for breakfast; quality meat cuts marinated and slowly cooked in the exclusive Josper basque grill for dinner – the only problem when dining at The David Citadel is which restaurant to try first…

The hotel is Kosher under supervision of the Jerusalem Rabbinate

### Veranda

In one of Jerusalem’s most iconic locations – with a stunning view of the Old City Walls, Veranda Fine Grill Restaurant offers you elegant gourmet cuisine and first-class personal service. Menu by Chef Kfir Mysnikov.
