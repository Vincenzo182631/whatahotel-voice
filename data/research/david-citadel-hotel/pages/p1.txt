## Your Address in Jerusalem

It takes a lifetime to know Jerusalem but after only one stay at the David Citadel, you’ll feel completely at home. Staff who know your family by name, stunning views you’ll remember forever, and endless pampering in a serene and sophisticated setting. Enjoy the comfort of our spacious, luxurious rooms, rich with indulgent surprises, from Frette linen to Bvlgari amenities. Savor outstanding meals on our resplendent garden terrace, a paradise for families and couples to relax and cherish those special moments. Our iconic location connects the Old City with the new so that you can stroll to world famous sites at your leisure and easily experience Jerusalem’s most desired attractions, both contemporary and ancient.

We invite you to be our guests, and feel the warmth and welcome of home in the most celebrated city on earth.
