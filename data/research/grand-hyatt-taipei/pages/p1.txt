## Grand Hyatt Taipei: A few steps from touching the city’s heart.

Discover the exclusive path to Taipei 101, where the largest shopping district intersects with international business. As the first international five-star hotel in Taiwan and the largest-scale hotel, take a five-minute stroll to major public transportation. A harmonious blend of ancient and modern architecture, luxury echoes in 850 rooms, 13 grand event spaces, and 8 international dining restaurants, complemented by high-quality professional services. This is more than a hotel; it's a "city within a city," a place where mirthful memories and echoes of laughter are woven—a voyage that commences at Grand Hyatt Taipei, tuned to rhythmic heartbeat of Taipei.​

## ROOMS & SUITES

Grand Hyatt Taipei's rooms and suites offer stunning views of Taipei 101, which is Taiwan’s tallest skyscraper and next door, as well as other city views while you’re surrounded in luxury. Relax in comfort and enjoy in-room technology in all of the hotel’s 850 elegantly appointed suites and rooms.

## EXPLORE

Grand Club Lounge

Located on the 22nd floor of the hotel, the Grand Club Lounge offers panoramic views of the Taipei skyline, an intimate atmosphere along with a continental breakfast, all-day refreshments and an evening cocktail experience. Grand Club Lounge service hours are 7 a.m.–9 p.m.

Prepare for a more sustainable stay

As part of our ongoing commitment to reducing waste, our hotel no longer provides bottled water or disposable in-room amenities for staying guests. We recommend bringing your own essentials and a drinking vessel to refill with drinking water available throughout the hotel.

Club Oasis

Located on level 5, Club Oasis features a 24-hour fitness center with modern equipment and an exercise studio offering classes such as yoga and aerobics. Guests can relax in separate men’s and women’s wet areas, including a sauna, steam room, and whirlpools with micro bubble and jet systems. Swimwear or clothing is not permitted in the wet areas, and access is limited to guests aged 16 and above.

Pool

Taipei's unique temperature-controlled outdoor swimming pool on level 5, with its lush landscape, stylish yet comfortable sun beds, lounge areas and a pool bar, provides the perfect retreat from a busy day in Taipei city.
