# Hotel Policies

Prepaid Offer Cancellation Policy

- Cancellations made 3 days or more before the check-in date will receive a 100% refund of the prepaid amount.
- Cancellations or date changes made 1–2 days before check-in will receive a 50% refund of the prepaid amount.
- Cancellations, date changes, or no-shows on the day of check-in are non-refundable.

Sustainability Policies

A healthy environment is important for all people to be their best. Hyatt is committed to advancing environmental action so that destinations around the world are vibrant for our guests, colleagues, and communities.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation in helping us protect the environment. Learn more about how our hotel is advancing care for people, the planet, and responsible business here.

**Disposable Guest Amenities**

- In accordance with the Taipei City Government’s plastic reduction policy, and effective July 22, 2025, all hotels in Taipei will discontinue the provision of single-use plastic toiletries and amenities. This includes items such as a toothbrush, toothpaste, razor, shaving cream, comb, and shower cap, among other disposable products. This policy will be implemented in both guest rooms and the Club Oasis.​

“Furry Friend” Pet-Friendly Package and Policy

We believe that your four-legged companion deserves a luxurious stay too. Book the “Furry Friend” Pet-friendly Package and stay in our guest room and suites with amenities and services specifically designed for your beloved dog companion.

Our “Furry Friend” Pet-friendly Package includes:

- Special welcome amenity for your dog companion
- Daily buffet breakfast at Café or in-room breakfast set (choice of American, Continental or Taiwanese set)
- A complimentary welcome drink at Cheers outdoor terrace

Please call +886 2 2720 1234 for more information and reservation.

**Pet Policy Details:**

- Reservations being made with dog guests are required to be completed at least one (1) day in advance of arrival.
- Only dogs weighing 25 kilograms and under or two dogs under a combined weight of 25 kilograms are permitted.
- Dog guests will be staying in one of our designated pet-friendly rooms. These rooms are specially prepared for our dog guests and include food and water bowls, a sleeping mat, shampoo, towels, waste bags and a door hanger. “Dog on Vacation” door hanger must be on the outside of the door at all times.
- During your stay, please arrange a time for your room to be cleaned with Housekeeping. Dog guests must be removed from the room during cleaning.
- When your dog leaves the guest room, it must always be on a leash, and it is recommended to use a carrier or stroller. Dogs are not allowed in public areas such as banquet halls, swimming pools, and fitness centers. Except for the outdoor area of Cheers, dogs are not permitted in other restaurants.
- Dog owners are responsible for keeping their dogs quiet. If a dog continuously barks, the hotel reserves the right to ask the dog to leave the premises. In cases of harassment or aggression towards guests or hotel staff, or any display of aggressive behavior, the hotel has the right to request the immediate removal of the dog.
- The behavior of the dog is the sole responsibility of the owner, and the hotel shall not be held liable under any circumstances. Should the dog cause any damage to or soiling of hotel property, the owner shall be responsible for all associated repair, cleaning, and restoration costs.​
- Guide dogs are permitted to access all public areas of the hotel and are exempt from any cleaning charges.​
- The hotel will not be responsible for the care, custody, or supervision of any pets, nor for any loss or disappearance thereof.​
- The dog owner shall be fully liable for any damage to or loss of guestroom furnishings, fixtures, or hotel property caused by the dog or the dog owner, and agrees to bear the full cost of compensation and restoration.​

​Grand Hyatt Taipei reserves the right to amend, suspend, or terminate this program at any time without prior notice. This package and related offers are not applicable to group bookings and may not be used in conjunction with any other promotions.

Restaurant Drinking Policy

The prices in our hotel restaurant are all calculated in New Taiwan Dollar (NTD),and an additional 10% service charge will be applied. If customers bring their own alcoholic beverages, there will be a corkage fee: NT$1,000 per bottle for wine and spirits.

Grand Hyatt Taipei Flash Sale/ Travel Fair (9funban) Voucher Refund Q&A

**Q: Does the hotel accept exchanging vouchers for other products?**

Grand Hyatt Taipei only accepts refunds for vouchers and does not offer exchanges for other products or vouchers.

**Q: What documents are required for a voucher refund?**

Please keep the original invoice safe. To process a refund, you must present the original invoice, the gift voucher, and its accompanying coupon. A 3% handling fee based on the voucher's discounted value will be charged for refunds, except in cases where the reason is not attributable to the consumer.

1. **Refund for Physical Vouchers:** Please provide the physical voucher, the original invoice, and the credit card slip (for credit card payments). If the invoice is lost, please call the banquet reservation hotline to request a Refund Allowance Form. Print, sign, and stamp the form, then return the first and second copies along with the product. Keep the third copy for your records.
2. **Refund for Electronic Vouchers:** Log in to your membership account, go to the Member Center tab, select the voucher number you wish to refund, and click View Details. Then, click Request Refund to proceed.

**Q: Must package tickets be returned as a set?**

Package tickets or sets that include complimentary vouchers must be returned as a complete set. Partial refunds are not accepted. If part of the package has been used, Grand Hyatt Taipei will calculate the value of the used vouchers and complimentary coupons at their original price and refund the remaining amount.

**Q: Do I need to return the free gift received with a purchase?**

If you received a free gift with your voucher purchase, it must be returned in its original condition. If the gift is not returned or is damaged, Grand Hyatt Taipei will charge the market value of the gift. Please ensure the product is intact when applying for a refund to ensure a smooth refund process.

**Q: How long does it take to receive a refund?**

The refund method will follow the payment method used for the voucher purchase.

1. **Credit Card Refund:** Credit card refunds take 20 business days to process. The refund timing may be affected by the credit card billing cycle, and the refund amount may appear on your next two credit card statement. Please check your next two statement or contact your credit card issuer for details.
2. **Bank Transfer/Check Refund:** Bank transfers or checks take 15 business days to process. Refunds will be issued on the company's fixed remittance dates (10th and 25th of each month).

**Important Reminder:**

Grand Hyatt Taipei and its partners will never request credit card information or instruct you to operate an ATM over the phone. If you receive a suspicious call, please immediately contact the anti-fraud hotline at 165. If the above Q&A does not address your concerns, please call the banquet reservation hotline: +886 2 2720 1230 or the room reservation hotline: +886 2 2720 1234.
