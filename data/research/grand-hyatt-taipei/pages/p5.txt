### Globalist Experience not experienced

I came to Grand Hyatt Taipei with high hopes. Returning to Taiwan, a place I know well, I was looking forward to enjoying the hotel’s renowned Globalist breakfast. I even used a Suite Upgrade Award for our 3-night stay. Unfortunately, the experience fell short of expectations. I left disappointed that I had spent valuable points and a precious SUA on this stay.

1\. Room humidity and unpleasant odors: Our room on the 20th floor was HUMID. I could feel the moisture in the air, on the sheets and furniture. Our parents stayed on the same floor, and their room had the same problem, so this is not an isolated issue, at least on our floor. Worst of all, the room smelled like old gym socks. When we adjusted the temperature, the smell changed to something chemical, like hair dye products. The ventilation clearly needs attention. Regardless of the room category or loyalty status, a comfortable, fresh-smelling or odorless room should be a basic expectation.

2\. Misguided Globalist benefits and breakfast: Before our stay, I contacted the hotel to ask whether my parents’ reservation could be canceled/rebooked under my account so they could enjoy Globalist benefits, particularly breakfast and maybe a suite upgrade. I would have done this myself, but the hotel was fully booked, and I did not want to risk losing their room. Other Hyatt properties around the world have accommodated this type of request without a hitch. Grand Hyatt Taipei Club people told me that rest assured, they linked my parent’s stay to ours, so I believed they took care of it. Turns out, linking the accounts (if they even did that) did absolutely nothing. They had access to the Club, which was what they originally booked, but it’s a far cry from the Globalist breakfast experience. To make matters worse, the only breakfast option communicated to our family was the Club breakfast. No one explained the available breakfast options nor where they’re located.

The only reason my parents booked a night at the hotel with us was to experience the much-talked-about Globalist breakfast at Grand Hyatt Taipei. They stayed left without getting to enjoy it. This was particularly disappointing because I had contacted the hotel in advance specifically to avoid this kind of misunderstanding.

3\. No follow-up on a lost item: After my parents checked out following their one-night stay, my mother realized she left her facial cleanser in the shower. It’s not an expensive item, but she had just opened it and was understandably disappointed to leave it behind.

She called the hotel to inquire, and my wife and I also followed up with the front desk on her behalf. We were told the hotel would check, but neither my mother nor we ever received an update. Perhaps housekeeping had discarded it; perhaps it had not been found. The point isn’t about getting an item back but the complete lack of follow-up.

Overall, any one of these issues might have been manageable on its own. Taken together, they made for a disappointing stay at a property from which I expected much more.

We have been loyal to Hyatt for many years. GH Taipei needs a quality check.

The Globalist bfast is the only positive thing worth pointing out, but we only got to enjoy it without my parents. Providing takeout bags was a nice touch for people checking out early, but had I not noticed the takeout bags on a cart myself, they wouldn’t have offered it to us.

### A Wonderful Stay, Thanks to Exceptional Leadership

I stayed at the Grand Hyatt Taipei for 10 nights and really enjoyed many aspects of the hotel. The location was great, the amenities were excellent, and I loved how clean, spacious, and comfortable the room was.

My room included lounge access, which was a particularly nice perk. The breakfast, happy hour, and snacks and drinks available throughout the day made the stay even more enjoyable. There was also always someone available to answer questions and assist whenever I needed help.

Unfortunately, my stay was affected by an incident involving another guest that left me extremely unsettled. The initial response from a few of the staff lacked the empathy and understanding I needed at the time, leaving me feeling anxious. As someone who travels solo and has a hidden disability, feeling safe and understood is especially important to me.

I later had the opportunity to meet with the General Manager, Francis Tsai, who provided more insight into what had happened. She was warm, understanding, and genuinely empathetic. She took the time to listen and truly held space for my experience, which was what I had wanted from the beginning. I don't expect people or organizations to never make mistakes, but I do believe it's important to understand the impact of those mistakes from the guest's perspective.

Francis is an exemplary leader. Her presence was reassuring, and she helped put me at ease so I could enjoy the remainder of my trip. She went above and beyond to assist me where she could. I neither expected nor needed that extra effort, but I genuinely appreciated it.

While the initial handling of the incident was disappointing, the care and understanding Francis showed made a meaningful difference to my experience. Knowing the kind of leadership the hotel has under her gives me confidence in recommending the Grand Hyatt Taipei.

I would recommend this hotel for its location, comfortable accommodations, excellent amenities, and, especially, the leadership that helped turn a difficult experience into one where I ultimately felt heard and supported.
