## Dining

The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort offers a pleasurable epicurean experience that brings to life the style and color of Mediterranean and international flavors, inspired by Spanish and Mallorca cultures. Dining at our hotel means enjoying a glass of vintage wine on a terrace overlooking the splendor of the sea while being served world-class dishes with impeccable elegance. From Es Fum, our Michelin-starred...See More restaurant in Palma with a Sol Repsol award, to Mar Sea Club, Terra and the renovated lobby lounge, each setting reflects the resort's refined atmosphere.
