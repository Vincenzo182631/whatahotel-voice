# Welcome to The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort

## Your Mediterranean Sanctuary

St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort, an idyllic luxury spa hotel in Palma, combines sophistication and modern luxury to create an exceptional retreat. Spread over four terracotta-toned buildings, the 125 rooms and suites have large private terraces with access to the garden or overlooking the Mediterranean. For spa and golf leisure travelers, the resort offers an extensive spa with a heated and saline pools, ice room and sensation shower. Es Fum, its Michelin-starred and Sol Repsol award-winning restaurant, adds refined dining to this 5-star hotel near Palma, Mallorca. Guests praise the attentive service, immaculate grounds and relaxing pool area, making this Palma, Mallorca beach hotel resort a respected choice for sea-facing luxury stays.

Experience the Ultimate Mediterranean Escape

Our exclusive Mediterranean Escape offer promises an unforgettable stay in the heart of the Mediterranean. Enjoy a complimentary upgrade to a Junior Suite, where breathtaking sea views and personalized amenities await you.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 11.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort. Self parking is available for €35.00 per day. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort is Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI). PMI is located approximately 20.0 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
