## Accommodations

With more than a century perfecting the art of hospitality, The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort invites you to discover 125 meticulously designed rooms and suites near Palma, each overlooking the Mediterranean Sea or the resort's lush gardens. Each accommodation has a private terrace or balcony, ideal for alfresco dining, toasting with friends or simply relaxing. ### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort have a work space?

Yes, rooms at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort?

The largest occupancy room at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort is the Royal 3 Bedroom Penthouse with a maximum occupancy of 7.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort?

Some rooms at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at The St. Regis Mardavall Mallorca Resort have fridges?
