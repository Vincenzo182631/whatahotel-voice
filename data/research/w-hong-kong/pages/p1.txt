# Welcome to W Hong Kong

## Get in the mix at W Hong Kong, our West Kowloon hotel

Go further at W Hong Kong. Our West Kowloon hotel in Hong Kong redefines how you spend your time on the road. With our location near Kowloon Station, you have easy access to Airport Express, mainland China and Hong Kong's best sites and bites. Experience the city center at Tsim Sha Tsui or Central with a short drive or station stop. Connect with trendy destinations such as West Kowloon Cultural District,...See More and when you return to our Kowloon luxury hotel, fuel up at KITCHEN with sumptuous worldwide cuisine or take a sip at our social hub WOOBAR. One of the highest rooftop pools in Hong Kong is our WET Pool and Deck boasting electrifying views of the city and Victoria Harbour. For meetings, weddings and special events with extra punch and pizzazz, book our Great Room. Award-winning Bliss Spa is our five-star amenity waiting to sweep you off your feet, while our rooms and suites do the same but in a different way. Keep it fun for the whole family with a pet-friendly hotel room at W Hong Kong.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Hong Kong?

The check-in time at W Hong Kong is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Hong Kong allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at W Hong Kong. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 15.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at W Hong Kong?

On-site parking is available at W Hong Kong. Self parking is available at W Hong Kong, with rates of $80.00 per hour, and $600.00 per day. Valet parking is available for $80.00 per hour.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Hong Kong have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Hong Kong has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Hong Kong booking.

What is the closest airport near W Hong Kong?

The closest airport near W Hong Kong is Hong Kong International Airport (HKG). HKG is located approximately 30.0 KM from the hotel.

Does W Hong Kong have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Hong Kong has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
