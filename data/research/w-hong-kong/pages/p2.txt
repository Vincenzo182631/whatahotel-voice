### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Hong Kong have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Hong Kong have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Hong Kong?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 3 at W Hong Kong. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Hong Kong?

Yes, all rooms at W Hong Kong permit rollaway beds. Please see room-specific information for policy and pricing details.

Do the rooms at W Hong Kong have fridges?

Yes, rooms at W Hong Kong have a Mini fridge.

Do the rooms at W Hong Kong have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Hong Kong have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Hong Kong have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Hong Kong have hair dryers.

Do any of the rooms at W Hong Kong have in-room whirlpools?

Yes, some room types at W Hong Kong have whirlpools. Book with Marriott Bonvoy directly to stay at one of these rooms during your stay.
