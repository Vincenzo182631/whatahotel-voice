The Ritz-Carlton Club Experience
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay at our Amelia Island resort with concierge by providing a private Club Lounge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences that immerse you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Club access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.

Club Lounge Access
The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island Club Level offers an exclusive retreat within the resort, a haven of excellence for the most discerning of guests. Delight in a unique lounge experience, replete with an endless array of sumptuous beverages and culinary delights, spanning from exquisite breakfast to delectable lunch, mid-day snacks, and an assortment of hors d'oeuvres, desserts, and cordials. This enclave is the quintessential sanctuary for you to unwind and savor the finer moments.

Club Beach at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island
Club Beach
The relaxed ambiance of The Club extends to Club Beach, offering guests a personalized beach experience. At the Club Lounge, Guests receive their personalized packed lunch to take to beach. Upon arrival to Club Beach, guests are greeted with a complimentary cocktail and escorted to a private section of the beach, where they can choose from a selection of lounge chairs with plush towels and umbrellas. Throughout the day, refreshing snacks and cold beverages are available, along with lotions and other amenities to complete the experience. It’s the perfect way to enjoy a day on the beach.
