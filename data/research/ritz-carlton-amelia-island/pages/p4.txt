Fine Dining, Farm to Table, Cocktails
Dining
Our Amelia Island restaurants' culinary focus is on elemental ingredients- transforming them through technique and seasoning. MICHELIN-recommended Salt offers AAA Five Diamond fine dining, Coast highlights local seafood, Tidewater Grill ambiance is Lowcountry-inspired and Coquina samples the Amelia Island’s Eight Flags culinary heritage.
The 4th Annual Amelia Island Cookout
With a backdrop of dunes and ocean, enjoy sizzling seafood, grilled and slow roasted meats and vegetables from chefs and pitmasters at the 4th Annual Amelia Island Cookout. Additional curated offerings include mouthwatering desserts, artful cocktails, mocktails and carefully selected wines. Our beautiful barrier island is the perfect setting for those who appreciate the art of preparing and presenting inspired flavors.

The Kitchen Table Experience:
Step into the heart of the MICHELIN recommended Salt restaurant for an exclusive, immersive dining experience with no walls or barriers, placing you within the rhythm and precision of The Kitchen Table. Far more than fine dining, this is a front row view of the artistry and discipline of an award winning culinary team. Guided by Chef Okan Kızılbayır, enjoy a curated five course journey with artful plating, refined sauces, and expertly paired wines. Salt Wine Cellar Dinner Series
Experience the Salt Wine Cellar Dinner Series, an exclusive collection of intimate dining events celebrating some of the most prestigious wineries and wine estates from around the globe. Set within Salt’s refined atmosphere, each evening features a thoughtfully curated multi-course menu crafted by Chef Okan and his culinary team, expertly paired with wines selected to enhance every course.
From acclaimed Napa Valley producers to internationally recognized labels, these limited-seating experiences offer guests the opportunity to discover exceptional vintages, meet industry experts, and savor unforgettable pairings in an elegant oceanfront setting. Explore upcoming dinners and reserve your place for a remarkable celebration of wine, cuisine and craftsmanship.

Coast Reimagined
Coast will undergo a thoughtful transformation, introducing refreshed dining spaces, an enhanced bar and lounge experience, and a sophisticated design inspired by Amelia Island's natural beauty. Drawing inspiration from driftwood, sea oats, coastal textures, and the island's storied shrimping and maritime traditions, the reimagined Coast will offer a warm and welcoming atmosphere that remains true to its beloved identity and longstanding commitment to Southern coastal cuisine. Guests will also discover a new semi-private dining area designed for intimate celebrations, family gatherings and special occasions. Upon reopening in December 2026, Coast will once again serve as the setting for many of the resort's most cherished traditions, including the annual gingerbread house display, holiday teas, Christmas celebrations, seasonal dining experiences, and New Year's festivities.
