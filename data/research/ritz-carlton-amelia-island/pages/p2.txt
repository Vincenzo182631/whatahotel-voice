Suites & Rooms
The beauty of the Fernandina Beach oceanfront is the centerpiece for the hotel rooms and suites at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island. Each of our luxury accommodations in Amelia Island features a private balcony and ocean views, while suites offer large living spaces and marble bathrooms with standalone tubs and direct water views.
Luxurious Accommodations
The Ritz-Carlton Suite
Luxurious Specialty Suite: Two large, oceanfront balconies to enjoy the Florida sun and outdoors. Located on top floor of the resort, this ocean-view suite on Fernandina Beach enjoys exclusive access to the Club Level. Spacious living room with elegant and comfortable furnishings. Cozy fireplace for you to enjoy in your living room. Formal dining room with seating for six.

The Ritz-Carlton Club® Level
Reserved for those who seek the finest experience possible, The Ritz-Carlton Club Level is exceptional in every way. With a unique set of services and amenities, the Club Level ensures an added measure of excellence at every moment during your stay. Indulge in the ultimate Ritz-Carlton, experience on our renowned Club Level. This exclusive lounge features an endless array of beverage and culinary offerings throughout the day, including breakfast, lunch, mid-day snacks, hors d’oeuvres, desserts, and cordials. It's the ideal place to relax and unwind. Our accommodations artfully blend the barrier island’s natural surroundings with amenities designed to provide a luxuriously relaxing retreat.

Kid-Friendly
A Kids' Wonderland
The Club Level is a kids’ wonderland amidst candy jars filled with treats- candy sea shells, chocolates, and cookies. Adults are also invited to sample, with permission. Our children's treasure chest is also fully stocked with fun family classics and amusements.
Pirate Island
Families on Club Level can celebrate the rich legacy of this pirate island with an up-close visit from our pirate captain or his swashbuckling crew. Follow the captain as he makes his way down to the Lobby where he will share a lively tale of life on the sea, and join in a rousing toast for all.
More Exclusive Experiences
Club Beach
Enjoy a magnificent day on the beach while continuing to savor the Club Level experience. Soak in the sun on a private area of our beach with lounge chairs and umbrellas. Relax and sip on individually bottled cocktails in small coolers. Spend the day at the beach with Club lunch packed and delivered to you.
Private Shopping
Take the stress out of shopping with a curated shopping experience and exclusive access to goods from the world’s top designers. Let our expert staff help you find the perfect gift for yourself or someone else and you can claim all the credit.
Island Adventures
Club Level concierges are local area experts who can design an itinerary around your interests. Choose Hook, Line, and Supper, an interactive fish-to-dish experience with our Executive Chef. Take an inner journey with an afternoon of mindful meditation or explore the outdoors on a customized tour of Cumberland Island National Seashore. Club Level can even help you craft the perfect proposal.
Daily Experiences
Join us each day at 5 p.m. in the Club Level Lounge for First Call featuring a specially crafted cocktail using a different spirit each day. On weekday mornings, enjoy a Mimosa bar open from 7 a.m. – 2 p.m., and on the weekends a Bloody Mary Bar from 7 a.m. – 2 p.m. Delight in an elevated breakfast experience as Sundays with a Sunday Chef Omelet Station for brunch until 2 p.m.
Inspiring Stories
A young family was due to celebrate a combined Father’s Day weekend and 39th birth-day at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island. In the pre-arrival process, the Club Level team learned that the father succumbed to illness prior to their arrival. As it was his hope to give his two daughters and wife a long-dreamed of vacation at Amelia Island, the family decided to honor his memory and his wish and stay the weekend at his favorite place. Appreciating the delicate balance of privacy and discretion, the Club Level team compiled a photo album with help from our photo concierge which was placed in the room prior to arrival and arranged for the purchase of four stars, visible together in the night sky, named after the father, wife and daughters. With the help of the resort’s naturalist and Club Level team, the daughters were able to look into the night sky and see their stars which will always follow them wherever they travel.
