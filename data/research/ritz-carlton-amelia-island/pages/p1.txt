Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island
Embrace nature at our Amelia Island, Florida, resort
Nestled along 13 miles of immaculate shorelines in North Florida, The Ritz-Carlton Amelia Island stands as an exquisite testament to refined luxury. This Amelia Island resort beckons with the allure of southern charm, where vistas seamlessly embody the sublime essence of our beachfront haven. Bask in the allure of Florida coastal panoramas that unfold before your very eyes from the secluded haven of your hotel room balcony. Each meticulously appointed room at our Amelia Island beach resort offers a serene retreat. Surrender to a realm of serenity within the tranquil confines of our spa, where your senses are transported to a state of bliss. Savor extraordinary culinary journeys as each tantalizing morsel unfolds a symphony of flavors and artistry, awakening your palate to new realms of indulgence. For the avid golf enthusiast, traverse the verdant fairways of our exclusive private golf course, an oasis adorned with time-honored moss-draped oaks.

Cashless Resort
The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island is an exclusively cashless resort. Guests must present a credit card at check-in and may apply charges from the spa, restaurants, and bars to their rooms during their stay. Major credit cards are accepted. For added convenience, an ATM is located on property.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island is 4:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $250.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island. The daily valet fee is $50.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island is Fernandina Beach Municipal Airport (FHB). FHB is located approximately 1.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island have an airport shuttle to Fernandina Beach Municipal Airport (FHB)?
No, The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island does not have an airport shuttle to Fernandina Beach Municipal Airport.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
