Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island
Celebrate a Season of Wonders by the Sea
Discover the magic of the festive season where ocean breezes meet refined luxury. At The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island, holiday traditions, culinary artistry, and oceanfront serenity create a seasonal escape infused with warmth, coastal charm and timeless joy.
’Tis the Season for Coastal Cheer
Celebrate the season at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island. Gather for the Thanksgiving Grand Feast in November, then embrace holiday traditions amid twinkling lights, coastal décor and a breathtaking Christmas tree overlooking the Atlantic. Enjoy festive dining, including select holiday experiences at Michelin-recommended Salt and the debut of the all-new Coast restaurant this December.

Festive Immersions
Christmas Tree Lighting
Guests and neighbors gather for the snowy arrival of Santa Claus by Christmas train and the lighting of our 45-foot Christmas tree, illuminated by hundreds of twinkling lights. The evening also features festive programming, live performances, and entertainment for all ages. Proceeds from the event benefit local charities, helping make the season brighter throughout our community. November 25 | 5:30 p.m.
Reserve Now
Santa’s Magical Dinner
Guests are invited to join Santa for a magical holiday wonderland experience, where children can share their Christmas wish lists and receive a commemorative photo. Enjoy a lavish buffet-style dinner featuring favorites for both adults and kids, along with a decadent holiday dessert buffet. The whimsical evening is inspired by the enchanting theme of The Nutcracker. November 25 | 6:45 p.m.
Reserve Now
Thanksgiving Grand Feast
Gather with loved ones in the elegant Talbot Ballroom for the ultimate holiday culinary celebration. Savor traditional favorites and festive trimmings, indulge in decadent desserts, and delight in seasonal touches. Children will enjoy holiday crafts and games, making it a joyful experience for all ages. November 26
Reserve Now
Nutcracker Tea
Experience the enchantment of the season with a delightful Nutcracker Tea and performance by The Florida Ballet. Enjoy a charming afternoon tea, amidst holiday cheer and festive treats, including an exquisite presentation of tea sandwiches, pastries, holiday cookies and savory bites. Don’t miss this perfect opportunity to celebrate the season and create cherished memories for the whole family.
Reserve Now
Build-A-Gingerbread House
Gingerbread houses are a Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island tradition featured throughout the resort during the festive season. Families look forward to experiencing the joy and magic of creating their own Gingerbread house with guidance from a pastry chef eager to share their secrets and expertise. The completed gingerbread house is yours and carefully packed and prepared for safe transport home.
Reserve Now
Breakfast with Santa
Gather in The Lobby Lounge for a festive morning where children of all ages can share their holiday wishes with Santa Claus while the whole family enjoys an extravagant breakfast buffet amid magical gingerbread displays and festive holiday creations. December 5 | December 19 | 8:30 a.m.
Reserve Now
Santa's Storybook Tea
Experience an enchanting holiday afternoon in The Lobby Lounge, where Santa Claus brings beloved stories to life in a storybook reading. Set amidst sparkling holiday décor and exquisite gingerbread displays, this elegant tea experience features a lavish selection of pastries, sweets, cakes, and finger sandwiches, complemented by a treasured keepsake photo with Santa. December 5 | December 19 | 12 p.m.
Reserve Now
16th Annual New Year’s Eve Black & White Gala
Ring in the New Year at The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island’s 16th Annual Black & White Gala. Indulge in exceptional cuisine and decadent desserts in the elegant Ritz-Carlton Ballroom. Dance the night away with live entertainment, enjoy an open bar, marvel at a spectacular fireworks display, and toast to 2027 with Champagne at midnight. Guests must be 21 years of age or older to attend. December 31 | 9 p.m.
Reserve Now
