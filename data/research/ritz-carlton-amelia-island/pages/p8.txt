The 4th Annual Amelia Island Cookout
Where Ocean Views Meet Extraordinary Flavor
With a backdrop of dunes and ocean, enjoy sizzling seafood, grilled and slow-roasted meats and vegetables from chefs and pitmasters at the 4th Annual Amelia Island Cookout. Additional curated offerings include mouthwatering desserts, artful cocktails, mocktails and carefully selected wines. Our beautiful barrier island is the perfect setting for those who appreciate the art of preparing and presenting inspired flavors. There are over 20 curated experiences including Fishing With Chefs, Chef Demonstrations, After Glow Parties and live music. Plus, The Amelia Island Cookout extends across the island and into historic Fernandina Beach with collaborative dinners, a wine sunset cruise, a cigar and port experience and a Southern-style oyster roast. Tickets are limited so make your reservations earlier.
