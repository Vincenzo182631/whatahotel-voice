# Welcome to The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center

## A Heavenly® experience in our San Isidro hotel

Located in the vibrant San Isidro financial and shopping district, The Westin Lima Hotel and Convention Center is minutes from splendid restaurants, galleries, and parks. As one of the top hotels in San...See More Isidro Lima, Peru, we offer the perfect escape from the daily hustle with wellness services and healthy facilities. Rest in rooms or suites with impressive city views in our Lima hotel. Savor exquisite proposals at Maras and Market 770, featuring Peruvian and international options, or enjoy signature experiences at Alphonse Bar and El Salar Bar. Elevate your routine at the Westin WORKOUT Fitness Studio and relax at the Heavenly Spa by Westin. Our property stands out among San Isidro Lima convention center hotels, boasting the largest in Lima with capacity for up to 1,600 people and 19 spaces for meetings, events, and weddings. ### Peruvian Gastronomy Steps Away Your Room

Peru's culinary scene has accelerated to a rapid boom, becoming a source of national pride and an engine of the economy. It is no wonder that an increasing number of tourists head to Lima every year, to sample the first-class, original and varied cuisine.

### Peruvian Haute Cuisine

Our Maras Restaurant offers Peruvian cuisine that captures the typical flavors of the coast, sierre and jungle of Peru. Respect the product and at the same time experiment with sophisticated textures that will not cease to surprise you.

### The Best Gastronomic Experience in Lima

At Market 770 we have the best Peruvian and international gastronomic offer. It is the ideal place to start the day with a fantastic breakfast, complemented by a variety of Peruvian options. Enjoy lunch or dinner, from salads to local food and a dessert buffet.

### Private Dining Market 770

A reserved space within Market 770 designed to offer an intimate and sophisticated dining experience. Its ambiance draws inspiration from the aesthetics and philosophy of Chinese design—harmony, elegance, and restraint—thoughtfully integrated into the restaurant’s overall concept.

### Dinner With the Best View of Lima

With splendid city views from every angle, comfortable conversation areas and a cozy bar, our Westin Club Lounge on the 29th floor offers guests of our Westin Club Rooms, Suites and SPG Platinum members a comfortable place to chill out.

### Peruvian Mixology

When it comes to having a cocktail or spending a relaxing night, our Lobby Lounge, Alphonse Speakeasy and El Salar bars, all inside the hotel, are great proposals, each one with a different personality that includes everything you need to enjoy every moment.

## A Pet Friendly Hotel

We greatly appreciate the four-legged companions of our guests. Our hotel only accepts dogs that weigh less than 18 kilograms, with an additional cost of $50 USD plus tax, per night. Only one dog per room allowed.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center?

The check-in time at The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 18.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center?

The closest airport near The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center is Jorge Chavez International Airport (LIM). LIM is located approximately 15.8 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center have a Westin Club lounge?

Yes, The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center has Westin Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Westin Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
