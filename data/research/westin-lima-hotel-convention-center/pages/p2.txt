## Accommodations

Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center offers its guests an enriching stay in San Isidro. Our 301 versatile rooms and suites are a modern refuge from the stress of the bustling city. Each of our rooms has floor-to-ceiling windows that offer optimal natural light and invigorating views of downtown. A large walk-in closet makes it convenient to unpack and settle in. For our business travelers, we have free Wi-Fi, large work desks, and premium cable channels like CNN and ESPN. Crisp sheets, down pillows and our signature Westin Heavenly Beds ensure a restful sleep so you can wake up recharged and ready to start your day off right. Enjoy a spa experience in the comfort of your own room with marble bathrooms and refreshing bath amenities. For a truly luxurious stay, upgrade to our executive level rooms and suites. You'll get access to our Westin Club, which offers complimentary breakfast and healthy snacks throughout the day, as well as evening bar service.

## Presidential Suite

Exquisite room with city view, decorated with elegant wooden details, Peruvian textiles and colorful art. The Presidential Suite overlooks Lima with grace and style from the 30th floor.

Presidential Suite Details

Exquisitely decorated with elegant wooden details, Peruvian textiles and colorful art, the Presidential Suite is 270m2 and offers an impressive view of the urban landscape with grace and style from the 30th floor.

With large, airy spaces and the latest technology throughout, every element of this exceptional suite is designed to revitalize comfort. Relax in the spacious living room with plenty of plush seating and a 42-inch LCD TV with great audio system. Entertaining guests or conducting business meetings is easy in the separate dining room with a table for 12.

A guest bathroom off the living room adds to the suite's versatility. A separate office is equipped with a large desk, comfortable chair, Jack Pack, 37-inch LCD TV, and a half bathroom. It also offers the conveniences of free high-speed Internet access and a Philips-based entertainment system that doubles as a speaker or charger for your iPhone. For those who like to start the day with energy or relax at the end in the privacy of their room, the suite also includes a mini gym with a treadmill and stationary bike.

A haven of relaxation, the private master bedroom features a sitting area with easy chair, chaise lounge, sumptuous king-size Heavenly® Bed, and 37-inch LCD TV. A walk-in closet offers ample space for guests traveling with additional luggage. The spacious marble bathroom is a spa-like retreat with a jetted tub, separate Heavenly® shower, and dual vanities. Pampering White Tea by Westin™ amenities, including soothing bath salts and luxurious lotions, make the experience even more relaxing.

Suite guests enjoy exclusive access to the sleek and modern Westin Executive Club Lounge, offering inspiring views of the city from the 29th floor. Enjoy a complimentary continental breakfast each morning and happy hour with light snacks and beverages every night. You will also receive free ironing of one garment per stay. Reserve a connecting room with two queen beds to form a two-bedroom suite.
