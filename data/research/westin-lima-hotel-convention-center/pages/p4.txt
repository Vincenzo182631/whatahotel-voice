# Discover Lima

## Experiences

Our hotel and executive club in Lima, San Isidro is located in the heart of Lima's vibrant financial and commercial district. Our hotel's prime location is ideal for travelers looking to explore the local area or attend exciting annual events. Our guests appreciate the easy access to many iconic restaurants, cultural galleries and lush parks, as well as banks and corporate headquarters. Immerse yourself in the local culture with visits to historical sites and museums. Stroll through the quiet paths of Parque el Olivar, home to centuries-old olive trees and surrounded by beautiful Germanic mansions. Visit the ancient pyramidal building, La Huaca Pucllana, the urban center of a lost civilization. Nearby museums and historical sites include the Museo Larco, where you can peruse an impressive collection of pre-Columbian gold and silver from Peru. Marvel at a large selection of Inca art at the National Museum or visit the ruins of Pachacamac, an ancient Inca city.

### Heavenly Spa by Westin

Immerse yourself in the beauty and serenity of the Heavenly Spa by Westin™. With a soft color palette of white and green, accented by natural woods, our spa is designed to revitalize comfort.

## Recompose Body and Mind

Our exclusive spa has a neutral and relaxing color palette, with natural elements for maximum comfort. At the largest urban spa in Latin America, we offer 17 treatment rooms, 4 whirlpool suites, and a full-service Redkin salon. Enjoy a refreshing dip in our Turkish baths or release toxins in our invigorating sauna.

## Spa Services

At our hotel spa in Lima,​ San Isidro, we offer various treatments such as deep exfoliation, roller massages, affusion showers and more. Our enriching facials include a free hand and arm massage to increase circulation and release tension. Join us in the Shiatsu Corner for a 25-minute lesson on this traditional Japanese massage.

## Spa Details

Schedule your spa treatments to ensure availability. We ask that you arrive 30 minutes before your appointment to allow time to change and enjoy a cup of fragrant herbal tea. Appointments will be booked by credit card or by providing the room number. We ask you to tell us about any medical conditions or special requirements that we should know about. Gift certificates for our Heavenly Spa are available at the spa reception through our reservation agents.

### Fitness Studio

Located on the second floor of the hotel, our 3,000-square-foot fitness center is well-equipped with a variety of cardio, strength, and stretching machines, along with supplies to stay hydrated.

Vary Your Training

Our Fitness Studio is equipped with modern TechnoGym strength and resistance machines. In addition, you can find weights, treadmills and stationary bikes perfect for working on the cardiovascular system.

High Tech Equipment

All of our cardio machines have a TV. We have courtesy headphones available at the spa reception. The fitness center offers instructors every day from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Consult availability and more information at the spa reception.

## Heated Pool

Our San Isidro, Lima, Peru hotel with pool features a heated pool located on the second floor, perfect for relaxing. Blue tile and lush indoor trees add to the serene atmosphere. Our pool area offers complimentary towels. Children under the age of 16 must be accompanied by an adult while in the pool.
