# Dining at The Westin Lima Hotel & Convention Center

## Dining

For a truly revitalizing culinary experience, explore the distinctive dining options at our Lima financial district hotel bars and restaurants​. Awaken your senses with innovative Peruvian cuisine at Maras, our elegant fine-dining restaurant in San Isidro, offering private dining experiences tailored to your needs for business meetings, social gatherings, or romantic dinners. Market 770 invites you to enjoy healthy, seasonal dishes in a relaxed atmosphere, while El Salar Bar tempts with a curated selection of premium gins and signature cocktails. Discover Alphonse, the only speakeasy inside a five-star hotel in Lima, where refined cocktails meet the smooth sounds of jazz and bossa nova. And at our Lobby Lounge, located on the hotel’s first floor, savor exquisite small plates paired with an exceptional selection of Peru’s national spirit, pisco.
