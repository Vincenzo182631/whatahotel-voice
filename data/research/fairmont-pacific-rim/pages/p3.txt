Fairmont Pacific Rim
A very special hotel with great dining and drinking, Pacific Rim is a luxurious, stylish stay you’ll remember for years.

Set the scene for us. What's your initial impression as you enter the lobby?
The Fairmont Pacific Rim has the buzziest hotel lobby in
Vancouver
, with a lounge that's one of the most popular after-work spots in town. Straight ahead, just past the museum-like glass display case featuring beaded vintage couture, is a massive staircase leading to The Botanist, the second-floor restaurant. The entire lobby level, from its white stone flooring and pillars, to the planter boxes that (barely) separate the lounge from the entrance, drips in understated opulence.
Any other context we should know about?
The 367-room hotel is part of the Fairmont Hotels and Resorts chain which, in Vancouver, also includes the landmark Hotel Vancouver, a veritable piece of the city’s history. Other Fairmont-managed properties include The Plaza in New York City and The Savoy in London.
Got it. Take us up to the rooms—are they as opulent as the lobby promises?
From the rich custom millwork and Stearns & Fosters beds, to the Mascioni robes and towels and marble soaking tubs with separate showers, this is a full-on luxury experience. Although all the views are good, there's something almost surreal about the northern view, which takes in the living roof of the dramatic Vancouver Convention Centre across the street, as well as the Burrard Inlet and North Shore Mountains.
How's the dining scene at the Pacific Rim?
The Botanist is one of the best restaurants in Vancouver; the Lobby Lounge and RawBar, a commendable drinking den. There’s also the more casual Giovane, an Italian bakery and café that serves pasta, pizza, panini, and desserts.
How's the service?
Everything runs exactly as it should. Your expectations are high, and the hotel staff will not disappoint.
What's the crowd like?
Lots of black? Yep. Five-hundred-dollar jeans? Sure. Miu Miu Dahlia clutch purse? But of course. This crowd is fashionable and although they probably won’t be invited to the
Vanity Fair
Oscar party (exceptions: Hugh Laurie and George Clooney), they know exactly where it will be held.
How does the hotel fit into the surrounding area?
Coal Harbour isn’t so much a neighborhood as it is a collection of hotels and high-rise condominiums. So yes, it that respect, Pacific Rim fits in just fine.
Anything else we should be sure to check out while staying here?
The 8,500-square-foot Willow Stream Spa, with its outdoor deck and meditation pods, is well worth a visit; the 75-minute “Four Hand Massage,” where you’re pampered by two therapists, is pure indulgence.
Bottom line: What elevates this hotel over other options in Vancouver?
A very special hotel with a top-flight restaurant and bar, Pacific Rim is a luxurious, stylish stay you’ll remember for years to come.
