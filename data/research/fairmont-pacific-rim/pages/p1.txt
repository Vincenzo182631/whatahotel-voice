## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Pacific Rim?

The check-in time at Fairmont Pacific Rim is at 3:00pm and the check-out time is by 12:00 noon.

Does Fairmont Pacific Rim have a spa?

Fairmont Pacific Rim is home to a Forbes Five-Star spa, located on the fifth floor of the hotel. Fairmont Spa offers a holistic self-care escape, tailored specifically to each guest's lifestyle.

What is the closest airport to Fairmont Pacific Rim?

Vancouver International Airport is around 30-40 minutes away by car, and 40 minutes by Skytrain. The closest Skytrain station to the hotel is Waterfront.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Pacific Rim?

Our Concierge team is on-hand to assist with any requests, from making dinner reservations at Vancouver's top restaurants and getting VIP access to events around the city, to organizing personal shopping excursions and outdoor adventures.

Is Fairmont Pacific Rim pet-friendly?

We are delighted to welcome cats and dogs to our hotel. Pets must be on a leash at all times and must not be left unattended in the room; any damage incurred by the pet is the responsibility of the guest. Please refrain from taking pets into restaurants and bars.
