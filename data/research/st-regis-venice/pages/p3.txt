# Suite Collection

Hotel Overview Opens a new window

## Luxury Retreats

Celebrating the modern spirit of Venice, the Suite Collection at The St. Regis Venice surrounds visitors with the captivating beauty of the destination. Suite accommodations are an invitation to live exquisite in residential luxury while indulging in the most quintessential views over Venice's Grand Canal and iconic landmarks, the private Italianate garden, or the city's romantic terracotta rooftops and labyrinth of calli and campi.

## Indulge in Your Suite Life

Experience the unparalleled luxury of The St. Regis Venice with our Signature Suite offerings, designed to elevate your stay and indulge you in unforgettable moments. Reserve one of our exquisite Suites or Top Suites and enjoy the following exclusive benefits. Special benefits apply to paid suites only:

Suites:

Astor Suite, Venetian Suite, St. Regis Suite, Grand Canal View Suite, Roof Garden Suite

- Daily breakfast
- Welcome Bottle of Champagne

Top Suites:

Terrace Grand Canal View Suite, Santa Maria Suite, Monet Suite, Presidential Suite, Penthouse Suite

- Daily breakfast
- Welcome Bottle of Champagne
- One St. Regis Bloody Mary ritual
- One In-suite 3-course dinner, excluding wines and other alcoholic beverages (blackout dates may apply)
- Welcome Refreshment Center complimentary upon arrival (no     complimentary refill)
- One St. Regis private Sabrage ritual

## The Artist Residences

True artist's residences, the Presidential and Monet Suites are infused with artistic spirit. Focal points of the living spaces alongside unrivaled outlooks are a series of bespoke contemporary artworks entitled "Hommage à Monet" commissioned to artist in residence, French painter Olivier Masmonteil, which celebrate the eternity of luminescence so unique to Venice.
