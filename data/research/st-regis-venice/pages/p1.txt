# Welcome to The St. Regis Venice

## House of Celebration at our luxury hotel in Venice

The St. Regis Venice pairs striking modern interiors with its historic provenance as the best address in Venice. A glamorous destination where art, music, and exclusive events converge to create a vibrant atmosphere. A property on the Grand Canal with views across iconic landmarks, this 5-star luxury hotel in Venice has stood in this privileged position for over a century and a half. Whether visiting with family or friends, the stylish design and St. Regis Butlers cultivate an environment of comfort in 124 guestrooms and 39 suites, some graced with a private terrace. The preserve for relaxation and socializing, our Ginori Terrace presents access to dining and beverage options at Gio’s restaurant, The St. Regis Bar and Arts Bar. Host up to 110 guests for celebrations, weddings and meetings, or unwind in the Valmont SPA, the Beautick hair salon, or the Exercise Room. Behind the hotel, luxury boutiques and contemporary art galleries lead the visitor to Teatro La Fenice and the treasures of Piazza San Marco.

### The St. Regis Rituals

The St. Regis brand is distinguished by its rich historical heritage and time-honored traditions, experienced across its global portfolio of over fifty hotels. Central to the St. Regis experience are its Signature Rituals, including the iconic Afternoon Tea, Bloody Mary Ritual, and Evening Ritual, all reflecting the elegance and legacy established by the Astor family since the hotel's founding in 1904. These rituals offer guests a glimpse into the brand's refined heritage and contribute to a luxurious and memorable stay.

### Bloody Mary

A St. Regis ritual that honors the Bloody Mary cocktail created by barman Fernand Petiot in 1934. Santa Maria, our local interpretation of the cocktail, is characterized by innovative techniques and a modern approach.

### Sabering Ritual

The Art of Sabrage. Celebrate the transition of the day into the night with our champagne sabering ceremony, the St. Regis signature ritual transforming evenings into superb celebrations. Dress Code: Elegant Attire

### Afternoon Tea

The St. Regis Venice Tea ritual is an avant-guard experience that merges the refined traits of high-end design with the freshest and most biting ones of street culture while facing the impressive views of the Grand Canal. Our exquisite Italian afternoon tea experience is exquisitely presented in Off-White™ c/o Ginori 1735 porcelain.

### Art & Design

A locus of style, design and contemporary art, The St. Regis Venice is where uncompromising luxury and elegant surroundings flawlessly merge with the spirit of the city to attract the vanguard of today.

### Muse of the Arts

The Venetian tradition of glassmaking also lives on, in collaboration with the artisans of Berengo Studio. This project marries contemporary art & historical glass blowing techniques, inviting artists of various disciplines to collaborate with Murano Maestros to create one-of-a-kind works of art. Located at the Gran Salone, the “White Chandelier” by Ai Weiwei serves as the protagonist of this collaboration.

### A Contemporary Artists' Residence

The St. Regis Venice features a curated collection of contemporary masterpieces, with works by Olivier Masmonteil, Jaume Plensa, Lorenzo Quinn and others. These exceptional pieces enrich the hotel’s refined ambiance, offering a truly distinctive art experience.

### Homage to Monet

Inspired by the Venetian masterpieces of famed Impressionist Claude Monet, former guest of the hotel, the Monet Suites are characterized by unique palettes reflecting the soft, warm Venetian light that inspired Monet’s iconic works. Bespoke contemporary pieces by artist Olivier Masmonteil also adorn other suites, paying homage to Monet and enriching the refined and sophisticated ambiance.

### Extraordinary Experiences

Unwind at a leisurely pace, with all details flawlessly curated.

Canal Boat Tour

Glide gracefully through Venice's shimmering waters on a private boat tour where every ripple tells a story and each glance reveals hidden treasures. ## St. Regis Butler Service

A hallmark of flawless and intuitive service, the anticipatory personalized service of the St. Regis Butler is available to all guests of The St. Regis Venice and ensures that their unique preferences are understood and realized. The anticipatory, personalized St. Regis Butler Service is one of the hotel's signature offerings. St. Regis Butler Service is dedicated to understanding and addressing your unique preferences and fulfilling every detail, no matter how small, for the duration of your time with us in Venice.

Guests staying in our suites will enjoy the careful attention of a personal butler, who will assist with everything from arranging romantic celebrations on private terraces overlooking the city to personal shopping experiences at the hotel or after hours in designer boutiques - as well as a well-packed gourmet hamper for a day's cruising on the Venice lagoon.

### Beverage Service

Upon arrival, allow a St. Regis Butler to arrange coffee or tea service in your room. And if you wish, a St. Regis Butler will arrive after your morning wake-up call to draw your shades, deliver your coffee or tea as well as the day’s newspaper and weather forecast.

### Unpacking & Packing Services

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage—so that you can relax and settle in for your stay. Similarly, before you depart, simply contact the butler service desk for assistance with gathering and folding your garments to prepare your luggage for the journey home.

### Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, simply let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. This service includes pressing of two garments per person, upon arrival.

## Our Green Key Commitment

Exceptional hospitality is enriched by a commitment to sustainability. As a proud Green Key certified hotel, The St. Regis Venice is dedicated to responsible practices that support the preservation of Venice's unique environment, allowing guests to enjoy a stay where luxury, heritage, and conscious travel come together in perfect harmony. Green Key is an international sustainability certification programme for tourism establishments and hospitality businesses. The certificate represents a commitment by establishments to operate according to environmental and social sustainability criteria set by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE).

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Venice?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Venice is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Venice allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Venice. One pet is allowed per room, with a maximum weight of 11.0 kgs.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Venice?

The St. Regis Venice does not offer on-site parking.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Venice have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Venice has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Venice booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Venice?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Venice is Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE). VCE is located approximately 16.7 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Venice have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The St. Regis Venice does not have electric vehicle charging stations.
