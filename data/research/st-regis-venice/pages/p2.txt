## Accommodations

Among the most spacious and elegantly appointed luxury accommodations in Venice, each of the 124 guest rooms and 39 luxury suites of The St. Regis Venice celebrates the modern spirit of the city and the immersive embrace of its water and light plays. Unwind in hotel rooms in Venice featuring bespoke design furnishings and curated artworks as you enjoy extraordinary views across the Grand Canal and iconic landmarks. For romantic getaways, choose suites with private terraces and let our St. Regis Butlers anticipate every request. For upscale family stays, we offer family and pet-friendly accommodation with space to settle in. Guests praise the elegant rooms, attentive service and high standards of cleanliness, a hallmark of our five-star hotel.
