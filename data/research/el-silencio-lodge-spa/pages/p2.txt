# Luxury Suites & Villas in Costa Rica’s Cloud Forest

## Discover our exclusive Costa Rica suite hotels & riverside lodge retreats

Imagine waking to the gentle serenade of the Costa Rican rainforest, with sunlight streaming through a canopy of emerald leaves. Tucked within the misty embrace of our cloud forest reserve, El Silencio Lodge & Spa is one of the most enchanting Costa Rica suite hotels, offering 24 private suites in Costa Rica and signature villas designed for true immersion in nature’s beauty.

Each spacious suite and villa blends rustic charm with refined comfort, featuring floor-to-ceiling windows, private outdoor decks, and open-air Jacuzzis that frame breathtaking forest or riverside lodge views.

Here, serenity flows naturally — wake up to birdsong, wander to the riverbank, and fall asleep to the gentle sounds of the cloud forest. Whether you are seeking a romantic escape, a family adventure, or a tranquil wellness retreat, our suites and villas provide an unmatched sanctuary to reconnect, recharge, and savor the essence of Costa Rica.

### At El Silencio Lodge & Spa, your rate includes a variety of complimentary activities to enhance your experience:

#### \- Yoga Class

A one-hour session suitable, held daily at the Samana Wellness Center. Available for guests ages 5 and up, accompanied by an adult.

#### \- Archery

A one-hour introductory class, no prior experience needed. Available for guests ages 10 and up, accompanied by an adult.

#### \- Mystic Hike

A guided walk along the Trail of the Four Elements, exploring the significance of fire, earth, air, and water in nature and human life.

#### \- Tree Planting

By reservation, take part in reforestation efforts by planting native species in designated areas of the property.

#### \- Mountain Bike Use

Complimentary bikes for all ages, no reservation needed, perfect for exploring the property and its surroundings.

#### \- Farm Visit

In the morning, join our staff to feed trout and chickens, harvest vegetables, and tour our sustainable farm.

#### \- Organic Hot Chocolate with Cookies

Every afternoon, enjoy this delightful treat at Sophia’s Coffee & Pastries.

#### \- Complimentary Breakfast

Enjoy our delicious breakfast at Las Ventanas Restaurant

## A Relaxing Stay

Each stay with us is infused with a harmonious blend of luxury and rustic charm. Vaulted ceilings, adorned with locally sourced wood, create an airy ambiance while floor-to-ceiling windows frame breathtaking vistas. Unwind on your private deck, where a bubbling Jacuzzi invites you to soak in the tranquility of the forest. Inside, the soothing palette of beige linens and accents of dark wood, punctuated by the rhythmic sway of bamboo reeds, evokes the essence of Costa Rica’s cloud forest. Every detail of our lodging, from the plush amenities to the thoughtful touches, has been meticulously curated to ensure your complete comfort and immersion in these pristine surroundings.
