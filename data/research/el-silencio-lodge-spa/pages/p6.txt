# Three-Bedroom Villa

El Silencio Lodge & Spa's grand Three-Bedroom Villa welcomes families and friends with the full comforts of a large forest-trimmed abode, accommodating up to 8 guests. In addition to the spacious deck with heated outdoor jet pool, it features a full kitchen, ample living spaces, and a stacked stone fireplace. The Master Bedroom is outfitted with a king bed, while the second and third bedrooms each feature two queen beds. All have an en-suite full bathroom. This stunning option is ideal for larger families.
