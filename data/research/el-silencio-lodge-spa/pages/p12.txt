# Contact El Silencio Lodge & Spa

## Located in Costa Rica

Nestled within the mystical cloud forest, just a short drive from the airport, El Silencio Lodge & Spa offers a serene escape. Our secluded hotel is the best base for exploring the natural wonders of Costa Rica, from hiking and birdwatching to cultural immersion.

Whether you're planning a romantic getaway, a family adventure, or a corporate retreat, our stunning setting and world-class amenities provide the ideal backdrop for an unforgettable stay in Costa Rica.

For more information about our hotel, to make reservations for an upcoming visit, or to leave a comment or suggestion about a recent experience with us, please contact us today.
