# Elevate Your Wellness and Relaxation at El Silencio Lodge & Spa

## Embrace Tranquility in Costa Rica

## Wellness Center

Samana Wellness Center is a private sanctuary surrounded by nature, offering 180-degree views of the forest and river. Built across two levels, it allows you to observe the tree canopy while exercising. It features state-of-the-art resistance training equipment and a multifunctional room where yoga classes, meditation therapies, sound sessions, and various other holistic therapies are held.

## Spa

Immerse yourself in a haven of tranquility at Esencia Spa, where the healing embrace of the Costa Rican rainforest intertwines with ancient wellness traditions. Surrounded by the soothing symphony of nature, let the stresses of the world melt away as you embark on a journey of rejuvenation. Our resort spa is a sanctuary where time stands still, allowing you to reconnect with your inner peace. From invigorating massages to transformative body treatments, each experience is meticulously crafted to restore balance and harmony.

## Yoga & Meditation

Discover the magic of our open-air yoga deck, where mind and body unite in perfect synchronicity. Or, surrender to the enveloping warmth of our Conical Room, a space infused with the life-giving energy of the Costa Rica cloud forest.

Complimentary yoga classes are held daily at 7:30 am in our new Wellness Center.

Classes include variations of Hatha yoga.

## Host your Retreat

Escape the ordinary and host your next retreat at our tranquil spa, where relaxation, rejuvenation, and transformation await. Whether you’re planning a wellness retreat, corporate getaway, or a private group experience, our serene environment offers the perfect setting for deep connection and personal growth.

Our luxurious facilities, expert therapists, and customized treatments are designed to enhance every aspect of your retreat. From calming spa therapies and yoga sessions to healthy, nourishing cuisine and peaceful accommodations, we ensure a holistic and unforgettable experience for your guests.

With a variety of packages tailored to suit different needs, we can create the ideal retreat experience for your group. Let us take care of the details, so you can focus on creating lasting memories and meaningful connections.
