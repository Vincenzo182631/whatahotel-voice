# Two-Bedroom Riverside Villa

Fully immersed in nature, the Two-Bedroom Riverside Villas capture the charm of a lavish treehouse. Standout features include a vast deck and two private outdoor heated whirlpools. Homelike amenities include a kitchenette, a dining area, and a cozy living room with a flickering fireplace. The Master Bedroom features a king bed and the second bedroom two double beds. Both are appointed with en-suite full bathrooms. A queen-sized sofa bed in the common area allows for a party of six, making it a suitable option for families or friends.
