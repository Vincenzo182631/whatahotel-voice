# Two-Bedroom Suite

El Silencio Lodge & Spa’s Two-Bedroom Suites are an excellent option for friends and families who wish to spend time together in premium accommodations. Their comfortable layout features two separate bedrooms and a shared bathroom. The master bedroom is outfitted with a king bed and the second room with two queen beds. All are furnished with cloud-comfort bedding to grant the most regenerative sleeping experience in Costa Rica. Floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors guarantee amazing views of the cloud forest or crystalline streams. Be sure to enjoy a relaxing soak in the heated outdoor jet pool!
