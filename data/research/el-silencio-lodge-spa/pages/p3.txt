# Deluxe Suite

Deluxe Suites at El Silencio Lodge & Spa are terraced along the hillside granting extra privacy and picture-perfect mountain or forest views. Each spacious suite is equipped with premium amenities, including a private outdoor viewing deck with traditional handcrafted rocking chairs and soothing heated outdoor jacuzzi.  All rooms offer cloud-comfort king sized beds for the most regenerative sleep experience in Costa Rica. Floor-to-ceiling windows guarantee amazing views of the mist-covered cloud forest surroundings.
