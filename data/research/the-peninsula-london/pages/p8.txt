## A World Within by KOZO

This October, The Peninsula London presents a new collaboration between contemporary art and automotive excellence. Created by artist KOZO in collaboration with CART Department, six vehicles from The Peninsula London’s signature fleet are transformed into a single, moving work of art, travelling across the streets of London throughout October.

## The Artwork

A story revealed in motion.

Across six vehicles, KOZO’s artwork brings together fragments of British landscape, London landmarks and the history of The Peninsula, creating one continuous composition that unfolds across the fleet.

Inspired by the idea of a page being torn open, the designs reveal pastoral scenes beneath The Peninsula’s iconic Pages, uncovering stately homes, rolling hills, rivers, bridges and wooded landscapes alongside recognisable landmarks from London and The Peninsula story. Elements including the Guardian Lions at the hotel’s entrance and Wellington Arch appear throughout the work, creating unexpected connections between the hotel, its surroundings and the wider British landscape.

Each vehicle reveals a different part of the story, while together the six cars form a moving gallery across London.

## Meet the Artist: KOZO

KOZO is a contemporary artist and globally recognised tattoo innovator whose distinctive microrealism style has influenced both the fine-art and tattoo worlds.

For The Peninsula London, KOZO translated his visual language onto an entirely different canvas: six luxury vehicles, each with its own proportions, curves and character. Working directly with the shape and existing Peninsula Green finish of each car, he created a series of compositions designed to work individually while connecting as one larger artwork.

“The collection brings past and present into conversation, creating unexpected juxtapositions that allow history and myth to feel alive again, inviting the viewer to become part of the story, quite literally, by journeying within the Art Fleet.”

## In Collaboration with CART Department

The project has been created in collaboration with CART Department, an art platform founded by publisher and art connector Larry Warsh, dedicated to bringing contemporary art into the world of the automobile and making creative Art Cars accessible to wider audiences.

CART Department’s collection includes more than 60 one-of-a-kind Art Cars created by leading contemporary artists, including Keith Haring, Damien Hirst, Barbara Kruger, Jenny Holzer, Lawrence Weiner, Rashid Johnson and Olafur Eliasson.

For this collaboration, CART Department brings KOZO’s artwork together with The Peninsula’s longstanding relationship with automotive excellence, creating a new expression of art in motion.

## Experience the Art Fleet

Make the journey part of the experience.

From 1 to 31 October, Peninsula guests can experience the artwork first-hand by travelling in one of the six transformed vehicles.

From Rolls-Royce Phantom VIIs and a Bentley Bentayga to an electrified Austin Taxi, BMW i7 and LEVC, each journey offers a different perspective on KOZO’s work as it moves through London.
