The Peninsula Spa & Wellness Centre item 2

The Peninsula Spa and Wellness Centre is a tranquil retreat, located on a serene floor of the hotel. It offers a blend of ancient and contemporary therapies designed to enhance guest wellness and deliver rejuvenating beauty results. Guests can indulge in a range of treatments, including massages, facials, and full-body therapies, as well as rituals incorporating Ayurvedic aromatherapy. All treatments are provided in the privacy of seven exclusive treatment rooms, including one couples treatment room. Separate steam and sauna rooms are available for men and women, along with a relaxation lounge featuring heated lounge chairs.

**Errol Douglas Hair Salon**

As the exclusive partner of The Peninsula London, Errol Douglas Hair Salon presents Errol Douglas MBE and his team's professional hairdressing skills to hotel guests. As an internationally renowned and award-winning hairstylist, Errol is a highly sought after industry expert known for his exceptional techniques and outstanding hairstyle design and styling. The Atelier has skilled and passionate hairstylists who can meet the needs of various hairstyles, hair qualities, and hair colours, providing personalized experiences to ensure that you leave with a radiant and confident look. The Atelier offers a range of hairdressing, hair cutting, and blow drying services, as well as personal beauty services such as nail art, pedicures, eyebrow shape, and hair removal. Upon request, hotel guests can also comfortably enjoy these hair and nail services in their rooms.

The Ricari Method

Beneath the skin is a complex network of connective tissue that supports and links the body’s systems. True health and radiance begin by restoring this vital foundation. Ricari, meaning ‘recharge’ in Italian, is a luxury longevity and aesthetics brand offering tech-forward, non-invasive treatments that enhance circulation, lymph flow, and skin tone while delivering deep relaxation.

Available exclusively at The Peninsula London Spa & Wellness Centre, each treatment is personalised to your needs, whether post-travel, pre-event, or for lasting vitality.

The Peninsula Spa Membership

Elevate your wellbeing with a Peninsula Spa Membership, available in Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers, with individual and joint options to suit your lifestyle. All members enjoy 24-hour gym access, while additional benefits vary by tier.

Gold and Platinum members receive unlimited access to the pool, thermal suites, spa facilities, exclusive treatment and retail discounts, Personal Training, and concierge services.

Silver memberships are available in 3-month, 6-month, and annual options, offering flexible access to core wellness amenities.
