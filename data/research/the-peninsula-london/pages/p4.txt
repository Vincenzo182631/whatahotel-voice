# THE LOBBY

A bustling, light-filled space at the heart of the hotel, with soaring ceilings, hand-blown crystal chandeliers and de Gournay murals depicting scenes of nearby Hyde Park.

Open from breakfast through to after-dinner drinks, The Lobby serves an all-day menu of international favourites and is home to The Peninsula's signature Afternoon Tea.

Surrounded by the elegant interiors of The Lobby, it’s a beautiful way to settle into the season, London-style.
