### Frequently Asked Questions

Do the rooms at W Suzhou have a work space?

Yes, rooms at W Suzhou have a desk for working.

What is the largest occupancy room offered at W Suzhou?

There are a number of room types with the largest occupancy of 4 at W Suzhou. See individual room details for more occupancy information.

Are rollaway beds permitted in any of the rooms at W Suzhou?

No, the rooms at W Suzhou do not permit rollaway beds.

Do the rooms at W Suzhou have fridges?

No, rooms at W Suzhou do not have a fridge.

Do the rooms at W Suzhou have coffee/tea makers?

Yes, rooms at W Suzhou have a Coffee/tea maker.

Do the rooms at W Suzhou have hair dryers?

Yes, rooms at W Suzhou have hair dryers.
