# Welcome to W Suzhou

## Discover a vibrant, luxury hotel in Suzhou, China

Slip into a brilliant world of floating art pieces as W Suzhou offers a modern interpretation of the city’s architectural and verdant heritage through playful installations of glass, light and other fantastical elements. Filled with vibrant hues inspired by local opera and furnishings that appear to defy gravity, our hotel in Suzhou, Jiangsu has luxury hotel rooms dazzle with signature W beds, pampering...See More bath products, high-speed Wi-Fi and ample workstations, as well as delightful views of Suzhou and Jinji Lake. If you need to extend your stay in China, select one of our 60 serviced apartments, all accentuated with swirling color. Escapism entices with larger-than-life nature motifs at our indulgent AWAY Spa, FIT and WET pool. Our dazzling venues elevate your event from ordinary to extraordinary. Exquisite gastronomy and a vivacious cocktail culture complete this remarkable hotel narrative. W Suzhou is in Building 7 of Suzhou Center, near Jinji Lake, trendy shopping and numerous corporate offices.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at W Suzhou?

The check-in time at W Suzhou is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does W Suzhou allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at W Suzhou.

What are the parking options at W Suzhou?

On-site self parking is available at W Suzhou. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near W Suzhou.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does W Suzhou have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, W Suzhou has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your W Suzhou booking.

What is the closest airport near W Suzhou?

The closest airport near W Suzhou is Sunan Shuofang International Airport (WUX). WUX is located approximately 40.5 KM from the hotel.

Does W Suzhou have an airport shuttle to Sunan Shuofang International Airport (WUX)?

Yes, W Suzhou has an airport shuttle to Sunan Shuofang International Airport (WUX).

Does W Suzhou have an airport shuttle to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA)?

Yes, W Suzhou has an airport shuttle to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA).

Does W Suzhou have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, W Suzhou has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
