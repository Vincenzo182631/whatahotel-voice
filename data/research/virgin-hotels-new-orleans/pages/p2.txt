## Features

**Comfortable Spacious Rooms.** Enjoy your privacy with discreet partitions separating the sleeping quarters from the dressing room. Finish your conference call in bed while room service drops off breakfast in your dressing room. Or get dressed for the day while your partner lounges in their dressing gown. It’s your stay, your way.

**Fast Free Wi-Fi.** All the Wi-Fi, all the time. Watch, play, call or scroll to your heart's content. With no hidden charges.

**A Dream Mini Bar.** You don't even have to leave your room to get a snack or a nightcap. Our signature SMEG fridges are smartly stocked at street prices, so you won't be paying a premium for the convenience.

**Vanity Goals.** Get ready to shine with the perfect vanity setup—take a seat at our dedicated makeup vanity and dressing area. Fitted with flattering lights and all the space you need to look and feel your best.

**Designed for Style & Storage.** All dressed up, but nowhere to put it all? Our closets are thoughtfully designed with ample shelving, hanging space, shoe storage, drawers and a full-length mirror—giving you plenty of room to store your wardrobe and accessories.

**Enjoy a Contactless Stay.**

Meet the Virgin Hotels App, your pocket-sized personal concierge. It connects your mobile device to your chamber controls and hotel staff for a personalized stay. Order room service, adjust the temperature, control the lights and TV or access your chamber.

**Pet Friendly.** We are proud to be a pet friendly hotel and welcome your pup to join you in sharing in the memories of your stay.

**Enjoy the View.** Our rooms with a balcony offer breathtaking views, perfect for relaxing and watching the world go by. Enjoy the beauty and excitement of New Orleans right from your private space.
