## Excursions

Anse Chastanet is located near most of St. Lucia’s most important sightseeing attractions: The Sulphur Springs, St. Lucia’s famous walk-in volcano, the Louis XVI Mineral Baths, the Tropical Rain Forest and the famous Twin Pitons – all these sites are within a short distance of Anse Chastanet.

In addition to the great variety of resort activities available on the 600 acre resort property itself, Anse Chastanet offers a daily exclusive excursion schedule for resort guests to sign up for and partake in. Excursions can also be arranged on a private basis. Excursions are offered both by land and by sea. Helicopter tours can also be arranged, as the resort has its own helicopter pad. For the truly unstoppable, island day hopping is also available, to the island of Martinique or to the Grenadines.

### Here is an overview of the most popular tours:

Very popular with Anse Chastanet guests are the half day, full day and sunset cruises offered on the resort yacht ‘Suzi Q’. Very often, schools of dolphins can be seen swimming alongside.

Once a week the resort double-decker motor catamaran Miss Babsie takes guests out on a “Jazzy Champagne Sunset Cruise”, with live music by one of St. Lucia’s foremost saxophone players. This is a lively event with many of the guests dancing into the sunset on the upper deck. The Miss Babsie or one of the smaller motorboats take guests to Zip Lining, a new and popular excursion on the east side of the island, reachable by a combination of water taxi and land taxi and well worth the time spent to get there.

The very fit and active may consider one of the Rainforest Hikes or even climbing Gros Piton itself.

No St. Lucia visit is complete without a visit to St. Lucia’s famous walk-in volcano, the Sulphur Springs and the waterfall and botanical gardens part of the Louis XVI Mineral Baths. Why not take a bathing suit along and soak away in the small pools with hot spring water.

There are many more excursions available including deep sea fishing, whale watching, horseback riding, around-the-island tours, tours to Marigot Bay, the island capital of Castries, and to the North of the island including a visit to Rodney Bay and Pigeon Island National Park. The 18-hole Cap Estate Golf & Country Club welcomes non-members and also is located in the very North of the island. The resort can arrange a water-land taxi combination to get there.
