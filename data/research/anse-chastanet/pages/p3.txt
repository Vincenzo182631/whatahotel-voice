# Dining at Anse Chastanet

Delectable Fine Dining Experiences

### Cuisine At Anse Chastanet

Some of the best dining in Soufriere and St. Lucia can be found at Anse Chastanet. An enticing selection of menus is available in a number of intimate hillside and beachside locations and also, you can pre-book private dining arrangements at the water’s edge on Anse Chastanet beach or in our spa cottage Kai Mer overlooking the Caribbean. Whether you fancy fine tropical Caribbean Cuisine, exotic Creole flavors, or simple grilled fare, you will find it available and more. Our culinary options also include our St.Lucia-Indian menu titled Apsara, a wholly vegan restaurant called Emeralds and last but not least our Jungle Grill for day time laid back dining on our second beach Anse Mamin. And of course, all of our kitchen receive organic produce from our resort farm Emeralds in the Soufriere hills.

###### Balawoo Menu

In keeping with Anse Chastanet’s eco-conscious philosophy, the resort’s Treehouse restaurant is offers a menu, titled Balawoo – a Creole name for a small fish – which embodies the sea-to-plate, farm-to-table philosophy for which the legendary resort is known around the globe.

###### Emeralds Menu

The resort introduced an entirely vegan concept menu as part of their daily culinary offerings. Anse Chastanet offers guests a choice of 4 menus most nights, in 2 different locations, ranging from Modern Caribbean to Seafood and East Indian- St.Lucian fusion. ## Trou Au Diable Restaurant

The Trou au Diable Restaurant is located right on the Anse Chastanet beach. Here we serve lunch daily. The lunch menu offers a wide and varied choice of appetizers, salads, sandwiches, burgers and Caribbean and Creole specialities. On Tuesday evenings, all guests enjoy a special Caribbean culinary evening in the Trou au Diable Restaurant, which commences with a cocktail reception at 6:30 pm The atmosphere is festive with the entire stretch of beach in front of the restaurant lit up by flambeau candles.

Lionfish Dinner

## Eat Them To Beat Them

Lionfish are one of the most destructive invasive fish species ever to reach the Caribbean, are wreaking irreparable damage on coral reefs by eating practically everything they come across.

One way to beat them is to eat them. We are serving a multi-course dégustation menu with paired New World wines, in which we showcase the quality and taste of this very unique fish.

A perfect dinner setting to savor a lionfish meal at two of St. Lucia’s leading resorts.

Diners, seated at a romantic beachside candlelit restaurant, are served lionfish as pure sashimi, citrus ceviche wrapped in a crispy tortilla, and either grilled or stewed with the flavors of St. Lucia. It is always delicious and a very special dinner experience prepared by our best passionate chefs and sommeliers.

## Cooking Classes

Join our Executive Chef on his once weekly harvesting excursion to our organic farm Emeralds. Included in this visit is an interactive cooking class or join one of our Chefs in the Anse Chastanet kitchen for a gourmet dining cooking class with wine pairing. Contact us for more details.

## Bars

The two bars – Piti Piton Bar, the Treehouse Restarant and the Trou au Diable beach restaurant are open daily and offer a wide selection of tropical cocktails, beers, liquor including also fine malt whiskeys, eau-de-vies and after dinner drinks. Why not start your vacation with a STAIRWAY TO HEAVEN or how about a CARIBBEAN KISS!

## Afternoon Tea

Afternoon tea is served from 3:30 p.m. – 5:30 p.m. daily in the Piton Bar, and an afternoon snack menu is also available at that time in both the Piton and Beach Bar.

## Dress Code

Our dress code in the evenings is smart casual. Gentlemen usually wear collared shirts and bermuda shorts or trousers in the evenings. Light cotton or linen clothing is the most comfortable. At lunch, we request dry clothing in the beach restaurant and a light cover up.
