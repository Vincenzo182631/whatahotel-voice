# ROOMS & SUITES

With interiors by the highly-acclaimed designer Paul Bishop, the 254 expertly-appointed guest rooms are decidedly luxe, with a playful twist. Each room features an expansive terrace or spacious balcony, from which you can take in your chosen view: the azure hues of the Dubai Creek, the twinkling lights of the Burj Khalifa district or the city as a whole, beautifully lit by the rising or setting sun.

Check-In: 3:00pm

Check-Out: 12:00pm

A security deposit of AED 300 is required per stay. The deposit is collected upon arrival by credit card or cash. Cash deposits are refunded at check-out, while card holds are released within 14 days.

## PETS POLICY

We are pleased to welcome small dogs at SLS Dubai. Only one dog per room is permitted, with a maximum weight of 9 kg.

A refundable deposit of AED 1,000 is required upon check-in for guests staying with pets.

For stays of less than 2 weeks, a cleaning fee of AED 500 will be charged upon departure. For stays over 2 weeks and up to 30 days, a cleaning fee of AED 1,000 will apply. For stays exceeding 30 days, this will be considered a long stay, and a refundable deposit of AED 10,000 will be required upon check-in, with a monthly fee of AED 1,000.

Pets must be kept on a leash in public areas and are not permitted in restaurants, bars, or by the pool, in line with Dubai Municipality regulations, except for certified service dogs. Pets must not be left unattended in guest rooms.

Additional cleaning charges may apply after check-out if required. Guests are responsible for any damages caused.
