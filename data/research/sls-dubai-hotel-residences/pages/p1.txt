# Dazzling Sky-high Luxury IN DUBAI

Make your entrance under the glow of a stained-glass window reading “Fortune Favours the Bold.” Soar to the opulent 71st floor Sky Lobby. Take in unobstructed views of the Burj Khalifa from two magnificent infinity pools atop the 75-story tower. Dine in a steakhouse, discover cocktails in a speakeasy, linger over lattes in the cafe. Welcome to SLS Dubai hotel, a wonderland of experiences, from rooms and suites to restaurants and bars, that each sparkle brilliantly.
