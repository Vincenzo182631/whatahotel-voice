### Monthly Culinary Workshop by Executive Chef

Embark on a monthly culinary voyage with our monthly Epicurean Workshop, curated by our esteemed Executive Chef. Executive chef will unveil the culinary secrets behind the city's rich gastronomic heritage. Guests will be visiting the fish market and select fish and salt from the Spice Bazaar. ### Exclusive Tasting Menu

Located in the heart of Istanbul, Siran Restaurant takes the 'fine dining' experience to higher levels with its seven-stage exclusive tasting menu paired with local wines, in addition to its signature dishes. This exclusive journey offers a gastronomic experience inspired by the Byzantine, Ottoman, Mediterranean and Anatolian flavors that touch the history of Istanbul.
