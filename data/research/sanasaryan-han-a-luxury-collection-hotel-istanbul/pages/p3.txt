## Stamp Ritual

The journey begins with our stamp ritual that will be written by our guest's and continue at one of Europe’s most historic buildings - the Grand Post Office. As the second largest post office in Europe and the historical birthplace of Türkiye’s first radio broadcast, this symbolic starting point invites reflection, and a pause to send loved ones a postcard featuring iconic city photographs by Harun Doğan.
