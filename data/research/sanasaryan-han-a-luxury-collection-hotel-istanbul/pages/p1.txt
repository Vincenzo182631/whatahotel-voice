## A Beloved Beacon of Culture and Learning in the Old City

The majestic structure was built in 1895, in a neo-classical style. Designed by Ottoman architect Hovsep Aznavur and commissioned by philanthropist Migirdiç Sanasaryan, the Han Inn lived as a building full of offices where all rents were paid into a foundation for Sanasaryan School. The Sanasaryan Han has been restored to its original glory, honouring its heritage as a beacon of education and with a curated collection of artifacts & antique books waiting to be discovered. Within the light-bathed atrium of the Sanasaryan Han - a grand chandelier takes centre stage and offering global explorers a rare luxury stay in a storied destination. The hotel is located steps away from treasured landmarks such as the Spice and Grand Bazaar, the Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, and the Karaköy. Displays of handmade ceramic parchmen are housed in modern glass cases alongside contemporary art installations & a collection of monochromatic photographs by Harun Doğan, capturing the allure of the city.

Destination Discoveries

## Discover the Allure of Authentic Istanbul in the Heart of Old City

The journey begins with our stamp ritual that will be written by our guest's and continue at one of Europe’s most historic buildings - the Grand Post Office. As the second largest post office in Europe and the historical birthplace of Türkiye’s first radio broadcast, this symbolic starting point invites reflection, and a pause to send loved ones a postcard featuring iconic city photographs by Harun Doğan. Then on to the historic Spice Bazaar near the Avian Square, to be swept away by the exotic fragrances and flavours of the pulsing city. At the New Mosque Square, take the chance to live like a local, and absorb the tranquillity of feeding the birds. As Turkey's oldest confectioner and one of the world's first hundred companies, Hacı Bekir has a legacy that spans generations. This hands-on experience allows guests to explore the age-old traditions of the atelier while tasting hard candies and exquisite almond paste.

### The Tea Ritual at the Library Bar

Every day, a dedicated Tea Master explains the origins & characteristics of the tea leaves, sharing stories about daily life in Turkey. The experience continues in-room with a custom-made Turkish tea set featuring aromatic tea blends from the Sanasaryan Han Tea Master, to be enjoyed in traditional glass cups and copper plates. Two locally inspired types of tea, named Charm and Allure are curated for the guests.

### Momentum Exhibition by Harun Dogan

The Rooms & Corridors, adorning the walls, discover the curated Momentum collection of monochromatic photographs of Istanbul by Turkish – Swiss photographer Harun Dogan, evoking the storied history of the city that has captivated hearts for centuries.

### Discover Culinary Travel by Executive Chef

Embark on a monthly culinary voyage with our monthly Epicurean Workshop, curated by our esteemed Executive Chef. Executive chef will unveil the culinary secrets behind the city's rich gastronomic heritage. Guests will be visiting the fish market and select fish and salt from the Spice Bazaar. They will choose freshly sourced spices from the Spice Bazaar and locally sourced fishes from the fish market.
