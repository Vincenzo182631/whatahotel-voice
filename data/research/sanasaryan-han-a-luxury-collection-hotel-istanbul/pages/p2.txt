## Accommodations

Step inside stylishly serene rooms & suites in the heart of Istanbul, where curated contemporary design echoes the iconic blend of East meets West. Choose from a selection of 51 sophisticated hotel rooms...See More in Istanbul or from 12 luxurious suites showcasing an abundance of space and style. The high ceilings and spacious large rooms feature serenity in the heart of the Old City. From the plush bed with Turkish motifs and premium Frette linens, to marble bathrooms with handmade copper trays and traditional hammam bowls inspired by the tradition of the Turkish bath ritual, the Sanasaryan Han offers both a window to the past and a flourishing future. Each accommodation in Istanbul has a Turkish tea set featuring aromatic tea blends from the Sanasaryan Han to be enjoyed in traditional glass cups and copper plates. ## Sanasaryan Han Suite

Experience the pinnacle of luxury in the 70-square-meter Sanasaryan Han Suite, a haven of sumptuous slumber. Featuring a bedroom, bathroom and living area in the unique sophisticated style of the Sanasaryan Han, understated elegance is infused with the opulence of the Ottoman era. A palatial King bed offers 300-thread-count Frette linens, while the marble bathroom with dual sinks reflects the traditional Turkish hammam ritual. With breathtaking views of old Istanbul, the Atrium Living room is a sanctuary of style, beckoning you to indulge in an aromatic cup of hand blended Turkish tea. A black piano bar offers a nostalgic nod to the regency of the past, while designer furniture ensures contemporary comforts and panache.
