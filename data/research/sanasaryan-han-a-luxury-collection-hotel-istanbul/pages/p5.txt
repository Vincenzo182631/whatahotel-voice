## Discover the Allure of Authentic Istanbul

Open your elegant Sanasaryan Han invitation and join us on a tour of authentic Istanbul, where a curated itinerary invites you to live in the moment and embrace life’s simple pleasures. The journey begins at one of Europe’s most historic buildings -the Grand Post Office, designed by architect Vedat Tek in 1905. As the second largest post office in Europe and the historical birthplace of Türkiye’s first radio broadcast, this symbolic starting point invites reflection, and a pause to send loved ones a postcard featuring iconic city photographs by Harun Doğan . As the route unfolds, follow our local guide down winding streets to the bustling local markets, where a kaleidoscope of colours, sounds and scents envelop the senses. Then on to the historic Spice Bazaar near the Avian Square, to be swept away by the exotic fragrances and flavours of the pulsing city. At the New Mosque Square, take the chance to live like a local, and absorb the tranquillity of feeding the birds.

## Spice Bazaar

The route continues through the bustling local market, where life and colors continue to flourish, leading to the Spice Bazaar with cultural historical stories.

## Avian Square

Adjacent to it is the Avian Square, where the concierge team will assist guests acquiring bird feed and accompany them to the New Mosque Square where the tradition of feeding the pigeons will allow for a moment to get immersed and live the city like a true local.

## Ali Muhiddin Hacı Bekir Atelier

Join us for the sweetest meet-and-greet in Türkiye’s oldest original confectionary store, the Hacı Bekir atelier. Just steps away from the Sanasaryan Han, you’ll be transported back in time to the family sweet shop that has been handcrafting world-famous Turkish Delights since 1777. Hacı Bekir has a legacy that spans generations and at our atelier, we offer a weekly invitation to guests to be immersed in the art of cultivating their own clotted cream Turkish Delight. On the same marble that has witnessed centuries of confectionery craftsmanship, this hands-on experience allows guests to explore the age-old traditions of renowned artisans, while sampling a variety of delicious hard candies and exquisite almond paste. A journey through time and taste, connect with the craftsmanship that made Turkish Delights famous throughout Europe, and create your own sweet memories of timeless Türkiye.

### Hagia Sophia

A symbol of Istanbul’s rich heritage, Hagia Sophia has served as both cathedral and mosque throughout its remarkable history. Famous for its majestic domes, intricate mosaics, and exceptional architecture, it ranks among the world’s most celebrated landmarks. Within walking distance of Sanasaryan Han, it invites guests to experience centuries of Byzantine and Ottoman history.

### Blue Mosque

Officially known as Sultan Ahmed Mosque, the Blue Mosque is admired for its elegant silhouette, six minarets, and magnificent İznik tilework. As one of Istanbul’s most distinguished Ottoman landmarks, it continues to captivate visitors with its architectural beauty and serene atmosphere. Located within walking distance of Sanasaryan Han, it offers an enriching cultural experience in the heart of the city.

### Grand Bazaar

With a history spanning more than five centuries, the Grand Bazaar is one of the world’s oldest and most renowned covered markets. Its historic streets are home to artisan workshops, jewelry stores, textiles, ceramics, and traditional Turkish goods. Located within walking distance of Sanasaryan Han, the Grand Bazaar offers an authentic glimpse into Istanbul’s vibrant commercial and cultural heritage.

## Bosphorus

Stretching between Europe and Asia, the Bosphorus is one of Istanbul’s defining natural landmarks. Its scenic shoreline is lined with historic waterfront mansions, elegant palaces, lively districts, and panoramic viewpoints that reveal the city’s unique character. Whether explored by boat or from its picturesque promenades, the Bosphorus offers an unforgettable perspective on Istanbul’s beauty, history, and vibrant waterfront life.

## Galata Bridge

Spanning the Golden Horn, Galata Bridge connects Istanbul’s historic peninsula with the vibrant Karaköy district. Its lively waterfront is lined with fishermen, local eateries, and views of the city’s iconic skyline, creating a unique blend of everyday life and historic character. Whether crossing on foot or taking in the scenery from the water, Galata Bridge offers a distinctive perspective on Istanbul’s rhythm, heritage, and waterfront charm.

### Galataport Istanbul

Located just minutes from Galataport Istanbul, Sanasaryan Han offers effortless access to the city's vibrant waterfront and international cruise terminal. Perfectly positioned between the Bosphorus and the Historic Peninsula, the hotel provides a seamless start or finish to your voyage, complemented by timeless heritage, refined comfort, and authentic local experiences.

### Sarayburnu Cruise Port

Just moments from Sarayburnu Cruise Port, Sanasaryan Han welcomes guests with a seamless connection to Istanbul's historic heart. Ideally located for cruise travelers, the hotel offers effortless access to iconic landmarks, cultural treasures, and authentic local experiences, creating an elegant beginning or memorable finale to every journey.

### Your Cruise Gateway to Istanbul

Begin or conclude your cruise journey with an unforgettable stay at Sanasaryan Han. Ideally located near Istanbul's international cruise ports, the hotel places the city's iconic landmarks, vibrant culture, and rich culinary heritage within easy reach, offering a seamless transition between sea and shore in an atmosphere of timeless elegance.
