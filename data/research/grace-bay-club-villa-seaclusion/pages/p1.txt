# Seaclusion Private Villa – 16 Adults And 4 Children

9 Bedroom, 9.5 Bathroom Private Villa

(Standalone Villa – 8 minutes from Grace Bay Club)

Located on the tranquil shores of Grace Bay Beach, Seaclusion epitomizes luxury island living. This chic 9-bedroom, 9½-bathroom oceanfront villa offers world-class amenities, unobstructed ocean views, and thoughtfully designed accommodations, including a dedicated bunk room with 4 twin beds sleeping up to 4 children (ages 12 and under).

Showcasing striking modern design indoors and out, the villa features expansive living spaces totaling more than 11,800 square feet. Guests of Seaclusion enjoy elevated, personalized service and an exclusive collection of ultra-luxury amenities, creating an experience that feels both effortless and refined. Indulge in handmade luxury at this stunning Turks and Caicos retreat.

For larger gatherings, Seaclusion may be combined with neighboring Seascape to create a 14-bedroom, 14½-bathroom private estate.
