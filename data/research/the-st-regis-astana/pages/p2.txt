## Accommodations

Thoughtfully designed, the Astana hotel rooms and suites at The St. Regis Astana blend timeless sophistication with modern luxury, offering the finest Astana accommodations in the heart of the city. From elegantly appointed rooms to the opulent Presidential Suite, each space is meticulously crafted and beautifully furnished. Classic features, including grand hallways and refined crown moldings, are enhanced by exquisite photography and contemporary touches. For those traveling with loved ones, our spacious family hotel rooms in Astana provide comfort and elegance for an unforgettable stay. With the legendary St. Regis Butler Service available day and night, every need is anticipated and fulfilled, ensuring a seamless and exceptional experience in vibrant Astana.

### Collection of Suites

The designer collection of suites at The St. Regis Astana, crafted by Wimberly Interiors, seamlessly blends contemporary elegance with the rich tapestry of traditional Kazakh culture. The interior design reflects a harmonious fusion, where modern aesthetics meet the timeless charm of Kazakh heritage, creating a luxurious and culturally immersive experience for guests.

## Ambassador Suite

Luxurious Getaway with our Ambassador Suite, spanning an expansive 110 square meters. Revel in the breathtaking design and panoramic views of Central Park or the Ishim River, seamlessly framed by floor-to-ceiling windows. Alternatively, step onto the balcony with stylish furniture or onto the spacious terrace with a cozy sitting area, providing both privacy and opulence. This iconic suite is a true embodiment of elegance and style, reflecting the rich heritage of the St. Regis brand. Immerse yourself in the luxury of your surroundings as you enjoy the exclusivity of the Ambassador Suite. As our distinguished guest, you'll experience the epitome of personalized service with our signature 24-hour St. Regis Butler Service. This includes complimentary offerings such as Butler-served tea or coffee upon arrival and with your daily wake-up call.

## St. Regis Suite

Immerse yourself in opulence with the St. Regis Suite, a spacious retreat spanning 150 square meters, offering breathtaking views of Central Park. This chic haven, adorned in natural tones, is meticulously designed with elements inspired by the local culture, creating a unique and inviting ambiance. Stay seamlessly connected with complimentary high-speed internet and a multi-line wireless phone, ensuring you can focus on important matters and business endeavors without any interruptions. Our dedicated St. Regis Butler Service is at your disposal, ready to pamper you with complimentary coffee or tea upon arrival, setting the tone for a memorable stay. Additionally, enjoy the convenience of pressing services for two items of clothing, ensuring you always look and feel your best. Whether for business or leisure, this distinctive suite provides the perfect setting for meetings with friends, family, or colleagues, both within the chic interiors and on the cozy furnished terrace.

## Royal Suite

Indulge yourself in an unforgettable stay in our luxurious Royal Suite. Spanning over 200 square meters, this opulent accommodation boasts a well-appointed living room, a cozy bedroom, a dining area, and an additional space for work or relaxation. Revel in the sophisticated interior design, characterized by natural tones that create a warm and inviting atmosphere for you to unwind after a busy day. The suite's panoramic windows provide breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape, allowing ample natural light to illuminate the space. Equipped with all the essential amenities for a comfortable stay, including complimentary high-speed internet and a multi-line wireless phone, the Royal Suite ensures a seamless experience. Upon arrival, savor complimentary coffee or tea, and take advantage of personal ironing services for up to two garments. Additional services extend to assistance with packing and unpacking, shoe shining, and delightful welcome treats.

## Presidential Suite

Elevate your stay to unprecedented levels of luxury with our Presidential Suite, a sprawling 510 square meters of pure extravagance. Positioned on the pinnacle of the hotel, this suite offers unparalleled views of Central Park and the majestic Ishim River, providing a breathtaking backdrop to your unforgettable experience. Inspired by the rich tapestry of Kazakh culture, the interior of the Presidential Suite seamlessly blends sophistication with first-class amenities, ensuring a refined and exclusive stay in the heart of the capital. The Presidential Suite includes a living room, study, library, wine cellar, an open terrace of 139 sq. m with a jacuzzi and a dining table for 8 people - ideal conditions for a new generation of modern travelers. Step onto the lavish terrace, adorned with a cozy fireplace and a well-stocked bar – an exquisite setting for hosting unforgettable parties or simply indulging in outdoor relaxation with a view.

### Signature Butler Service

Experience the epitome of personalized luxury at St. Regis Astana, where every guest room and suite booking provides access to the refined St. Regis Butler Service. Throughout your stay, a dedicated butler will be at your service, attending to every aspect of your experience. From impeccably pressed ensembles for crucial business affairs to thoughtful, last-minute surprises sourced from local treasures, our Butler Service is tailored to understand and fulfill your unique preferences. Your time with us at St. Regis Astana is elevated by the commitment to perfection that our Butler Service brings to even the smallest details.
