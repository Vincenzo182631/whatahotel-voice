## Dining

Led by visionary chefs creating both local and international flavors, The St. Regis Astana offers exceptional dining experiences among the finest restaurants in Astana, Kazakhstan. La Rivière, our signature Astana restaurant, serves authentic Italian cuisine all day, featuring a lively open kitchen and the option of elegant indoor or alfresco seating. For an Asian-inspired journey, Asakusa presents casual izakaya-style dining with bold pan-Asian flavors. Guests can also unwind at the sophisticated Astana bar, where handcrafted cocktails, rare vintages, and the iconic St. Regis Bloody Mary are served in a vibrant atmosphere. For private dining at any hour, our executive chefs curate bespoke menus, delivered with the legendary St. Regis Butler Service, ensuring a truly personalized culinary experience. Whether for intimate dinners or social gatherings, our dining venues set a new standard for luxury gastronomy in Astana.

## Afternoon Tea Ritual

Indulge into the St. Regis Afternoon Tea Ritual with traditional or Kazakh teatime offerings.

Learn More

Since its creation in England in the early 1800s by Anna, Duchess of Bedford, Afternoon Tea has long been associated with the entertainment of society. In the late 19th century, it was Caroline Astor, mother of the St. Regis founder John Jacob Astor IV, who elevated Afternoon Tea to a new level by introducing the ritual at dignified gatherings at her house in Manhattan.

Today, this ritual comes alive every afternoon at every St. Regis around the globe, where guests are invited to enjoy distinct interpretations of this legacy inspired by the respective traditions of each location. You're invited to a wide choice of refined teas, fingerfood and assorted pastries.
