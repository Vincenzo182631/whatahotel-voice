# Welcome to The St. Regis Astana

## Iconic Kazakh Elegance

Experience the pinnacle of luxury and refined elegance at The St. Regis Astana. Blending timeless sophistication with modern grandeur, our hotel in Astana, Kazakhstan redefines luxury through its distinctive design, where traditional Kazakh art and architecture are reimagined with a contemporary touch. Nestled in a serene park along the picturesque Ishim River, our exclusive hotel offers a tranquil sanctuary in the heart of Astana’s vibrant business district. Every detail is crafted to provide an unparalleled and unforgettable stay, setting a new standard for exceptional hospitality.

### Unpacking and Packing

Allow a St. Regis Butler to take care of the unpacking and storage of your luggage so you may simply relax and enjoy your time. Before you depart, contact the Butler Service Desk to assist with gathering and folding your garments for the journey home.

### Beverage Service

Experience the impeccable hospitality of The St. Regis Butler from the moment you arrive. Indulge in a tailored coffee or tea service arranged in the privacy of your room. Our dedicated St. Regis Butler will be at your service after your morning wake-up call, taking care of delivering your choice of coffee or tea and providing you with the latest newspaper and a personalized weather forecast to enhance your day.

### Garment Pressing

Once unpacked, let your St. Regis Butler know which garments require pressing and they will be pleased to assist, returning the garments to your wardrobe promptly. Two garments per person per day may be pressed complimentary.

### E-Butler

During your stay, whether you are inside or outside the hotel, St. Regis Butler Service is accessible at any hour via email. Simply send your request and allow the St. Regis Butler Service Desk to assist.

### Service Desk

Honored to carry on a century of tradition of making guests feel at home, the St. Regis Butler Service Desk is available at The St. Regis Astana. Should you need anything, at any time, please use the guest room phone to dial the St. Regis Butler Service Desk for prompt assistance.

## Signature Rituals

The St. Regis, Astana Hotel is proud to present a collection of Signature Rituals, each carefully curated to elevate your experience and create cherished memories. Immerse yourself in these distinguished traditions that celebrate culinary excellence, refined indulgence, and timeless elegance.

### Sunday Brunch

Elevate your weekend with our Sunday Brunch at La Rivière restaurant. Enjoy a delightful selection of dishes crafted by our talented culinary team. Join us every Sunday from 12:30 PM to 4:00 PM.

### Afternoon Tea

Immerse yourself in the art of Afternoon Tea, where we honor both global and local flavors. Alongside the classic assortment of pastries and finger sandwiches, we proudly serve the signature Kazakh tea with milk—a nod to the rich cultural heritage of Kazakhstan. Every day from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM, join us at The St. Regis and partake in this cherished tradition.

### Bloody Mary

Savor our twist on the classic Bloody Mary during the exclusive Bloody Mary Ritual at The St. Regis, Astana. Paying homage to its birthplace at The St. Regis, New York, our expert bartenders blend premium vodka, tangy tomato juice, Worcestershire sauce, and secret spices to create a traditional yet distinctive flavor profile. Immerse yourself in the tradition and unparalleled taste of our Blood Mary variation.

### Art of Sabering

Like Napoleon once said, "Champagne - in victory one deserves it - in defeat one needs it." Opening a bottle of champagne with a saber has since been a very dramatic way of serving sparkling wine.

### A Signature Ritual

We invite you to join us as we celebrate and welcome the beginning of a new evening with champagne and the art of sabrage. This captivating tradition is preserved in the elegant setting of the hotel’s atrium.

### Iridium Spa

Experience pure tranquility as you take a refreshing dip in our pool, boasting a captivating panoramic view of the serene Yesil river. Let the breathtaking scenery envelop you as you relax in the crystal-clear waters. Continue your journey of rejuvenation in our luxurious hammam, invigorating steam room, or tranquil sauna. For an unparalleled indulgence, seek refuge in our thoughtfully curated treatment rooms, where our skilled therapists from Bali will provide personalized care, ensuring your every desire is met.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Astana?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Astana is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Astana allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The St. Regis Astana. One pet is allowed per room. A non-refundable pet fee of 〒30000.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Astana?

On-site self parking is available at The St. Regis Astana. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

Off-site parking is also available near The St. Regis Astana. Valet parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Astana have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Astana has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Astana booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Astana?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Astana is Nursultan Nazarbayev International Airport (NQZ). NQZ is located approximately 10.2 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Astana have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Astana has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
