### Discover Kazakhstan

The St. Regis Astana boasts the best address in Kazakhstan's vibrant capital. Discover the the intriguing history, cultural institutions, and indelible landmarks from the steps of our hotel.

Kid's Club

As part of the Family Traditions at St. Regis program, the Children's Club at The St. Regis Astana offers an exciting schedule of energetic, creative and highly satisfying activities for children aged 5-12 years. All the games and excitement have been planned to provide optimum levels of entertainment and fun while nurturing each child's inquisitive and creative mind. Facilities include a library, gaming area, movie lounge, activity area and refreshment area.

**Recreation**

Open to children of all ages, the Children's Club makes an ideal setting for families to spend time together. The facility houses a library and movie lounge. Our activity coordinators are happy to provide games and refreshments.

**Dining**

We recommend La Rivière, which offers a more casual setting, or Private Dining to be enjoyed in the comfort of your room. Menus that appeal to all ages are available at any of these dining options.

**Nanny & Babysitting**

Our Concierge team maintains a list of babysitters who can care for your children in the convenience of your guest room. Please contact us for assistance with reserving nanny and/or babysitting prior to your stay. And during your stay at the hotel, the Concierge will be pleased to assist.

**Necessities & Provisions**

Our resort is supplied with a variety of family necessities such as wipes, diapers, cribs, and rollaway beds. Allow St. Regis Butler Service to assist you with preparing these items for you during your stay or before your arrival at the hotel. Please contact the eButler for assistance.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Astana have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Astana offers a complimentary, 7:00 AM-10:00 PM fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Stair Climber(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), Weight Machines, and Yoga Mats, Free Weights, Pilates ball, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Astana have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Astana has one Indoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Astana have an on-site spa?

Yes, Iridium Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Astana. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Astana?

Hazret Sultan Mosque, Astana-Baiterek Monument, National Museum of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Astana Circus, Palace of Peace and Reconciliation and more are nearby The St. Regis Astana.
