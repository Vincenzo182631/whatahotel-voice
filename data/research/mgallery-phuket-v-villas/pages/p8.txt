## 6 Bedroom Presidential Beachfront Villa

Situated closest to the beach, the 1000sqm, ultra luxurious three-storey beachfront villa offers spectacular ocean and Ao Yon Bay views. Six spacious bedrooms feature ocean views, including five king bedrooms and one twin bedroom. Each bedroom boasts luxurious bedding, walk-in closets, and large en-suites with twin spas.Floor to ceiling windows, contemporary designed living and dining areas offer seamless indoor-outdoor flow.

The private infinity pool is surrounded by a wooden deck and sun loungers, as well as alfresco dining and relaxation spaces. Footpath access to Ao Yon Bay beach for ocean swims, water activities and sports, and beachside picnics. The exceptionally private and well-designed villa is the resort’s masterpiece and is ideal for extended family and friends holidays, and celebrations.

A 24-hour Private Personal Butler is on hand to assist with all guest needs, including curating memorable in-villa private dining and floating breakfasts experiences.

\*Our Pool Villa is not part of the upgrade program for **ALL – Accor Live Limitless** Members.
