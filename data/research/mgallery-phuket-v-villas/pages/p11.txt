## **Akoya \| Star Lounge**

### The Lounge

|  |
| --- |

Soaring 55 metres above sea level with 360° night sky and ocean views, Akoya \| Star Lounge is a landmark rooftop bar experience. Inspired by the delicate colours and iridescent lustre of the local oyster pearl, Akoya \| Star Lounge features chic lounge seating and a casually sophisticated ambiance, making this the perfect spot for a romantic evening and celebratory nights out with friends.

Akoya \| Star Lounge serves a fabulous mixology menu with exotic ingredients and spirits, including original cocktails. An extensive Champagne and wine list features world-class wines, and the bar pours a distinctive selection of premium spirits from around the world. The tapas-inspired menu represents a culinary road trip across the Thai kingdom, highlighting dynamic regional flavours.

Live music and hip DJ sets feature notable musicians and celebrity spin-masters.

**Opening Hours:**

_5 PM – 11PM_

Monday – Sunday  (hours may differ on festive days)

**Additional Notes:**

- Please note that we kindly request that outside food and drinks not be brought into the venue.
- Child Policy: For the safety and comfort of all our guests, children under the age of 6 are not allowed on the premises.
- Photography Policy: We kindly request that guests refrain from using the space for photography or filming without prior arrangement. Drones are also not permitted.
- Exclusive Use of Venue: The hotel reserves the right to use our venue for private events such as pre-wedding photoshoots, proposals, and other special occasions. During this time, the venue may be closed to outside guests. Please contact us if you are interested in our exclusive venue offers.
- Late Arrival: We will hold your table for 15 minutes from the time of your booking. If you anticipate being late, please let us know in advance to avoid any inconvenience.
- Weather-Related Closure: In case of unfavorable weather, Akoya \| Star Lounge will be closed. We will gladly relocate you to Yon \| Ocean House, maintaining the same minimum spend requirement.
- Pet Policy: For the safety and comfort of all guests, pets are not allowed in the venue.
- Dress Code: Smart casual attire is required.
- Front Row Seats: All front-row sofa beds are reserved exclusively for in-house guest
