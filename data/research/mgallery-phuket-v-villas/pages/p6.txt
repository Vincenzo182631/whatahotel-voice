## 3 Bedroom Executive Sea View Villa

The exceptionally spacious 2 storey private pool villa boasts incredible Ao Yon Bay views from all living and dining areas, including each of the three bedrooms. Two bedrooms are located on the lower level, while one bedroom sits on the upper level.

Each bedroom features a luxurious king size bed and private en suite with twin spa baths. Floor to ceiling windows provide spectacular ocean views and seamless flow between indoor and outdoor spaces. Large deck with sun loungers, alfresco dining and relaxation spaces. A magnificent infinity pool which merges with Ao Yon Bay horizon.

A 24-hour Private Personal Butler is on hand to assist with all guest needs, including curating memorable in-villa private dining and floating breakfasts experiences.

\*Our Pool Villa is not part of the upgrade program for **ALL – Accor Live Limitless** Members.
