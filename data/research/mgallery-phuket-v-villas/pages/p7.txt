## 4 Bedroom Presidential Hilltop Villa

Poised on a clifftop above sparkling Ao Yon Beach with outstanding ocean views, this is the ultimate in luxury villa living! Ideal for weddings, celebrations, and families and friends traveling together, the two-storey villa features a spacious, light-filled living room, dining room, and gourmet kitchen on the upper level. The owner’s passions and interests are reflected in a personally-curated art gallery and wine cellar.

Spacious bedrooms with king beds include private ensuites with twin spa baths. A infinity pool, large natural wood decks with sun loungers and stunning views provide wonderful opportunities for celebrations, social gatherings, and private moments of reflection and relaxation.

A 24-hour Private Personal Butler is on hand to assist with all guest needs, including curating memorable in-villa private dining and floating breakfasts experiences.

\*Our Pool Villa is not part of the upgrade program for **ALL – Accor Live Limitless** Members.
