# Welcome to The Alexander, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Yerevan

## Experience a unique stay at our luxury hotel in Yerevan

Set in the historic center, The Alexander is Yerevan’s first internationally branded luxury hotel and a defining social address. Its 114 guestrooms and suites offer calm, contemporary elegance enriched...See More with subtle Armenian design and warm gold accents.A collection of distinctive venues bring together guests and locals alike. Gabriel’s Bar & Lounge serves refined all-day dining and cocktails, while rooftop Italiano Ristorante presents elevated Italian cuisine with sweeping city views. The Keys Aficionado’s Private Club hosts intimate private gatherings, and Gabriel’s Patisserie on Abovyan Street offers elegant French and Armenian confections.Wellness is at the heart of the experience, with a serene spa, indoor pool, hammam and tailored treatments designed for complete restoration.Our Les Clefs d’Or concierge team serves as a true destination authority, curating personalized moments and opening the door to Yerevan’s most authentic experiences.See Less
