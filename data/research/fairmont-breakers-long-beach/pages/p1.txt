#### Aquarium of the Pacific

Discover the wonders of the Aquarium of the Pacific. Dive into immersive exhibits and explore marine life from around the world at this family-friendly local attraction.

#### Explore the Canals aboard a Gondola

Established in 1982 in Naples Islands, Long Beach, the Gondola Getaway boasts America's oldest and largest gondola fleet. Enjoy enchanting canals, meandering paths, and well-trained gondoliers for a romantic adventure.

#### Recreation Park Golf Course

Enjoy a great game on one of Long Beach’s best courses. Recreation Park 18 Golf Course offers an enjoyable 18-hole, par 72 experience with challenging elevation changes, tough bunkers, and well-kept greens.

#### Boat Rentals and Water Sports

London Boat Rentals lets you skipper your own boat around Alamitos Bay and Naples canals. Their 21-foot electric vessels accommodate 8 and cruise at 5 mph. Wave Runners are also available for an adrenaline rush.

#### Explore Long Beach's Art Museums

Explore vibrant art and architecture in Long Beach. Visit world-class museums, murals, and historic buildings. Highlights include the Long Beach Museum of Art, MOLAA, and the Pacific Island Ethnic Art Museum.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at Fairmont Breakers Long Beach?

Check-in is from 4:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM at our hotel.

Does Fairmont Breakers Long Beach have a spa?

The hotel is home to Fairmont Spa which features treatment rooms and steam rooms.

Does Fairmont Breakers Long Beach have a swimming pool?

Yes, there is an aqua pool at the hotel.

Does Fairmont Breakers Long Beach have a gym/fitness center?

Yes, there is a fitness center for guests to enjoy during their luxury stay with us.

How can I get to Fairmont Breakers Long Beach from the nearest airport?

There are three airports near our hotel that you can fly to for your dream vacation. Long Beach Airport (LBC) is located 6.5 miles east of the hotel, John Wayne Airport (SNA) is located 22 miles south of the hotel and the Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is located 25 miles north of the hotel.
