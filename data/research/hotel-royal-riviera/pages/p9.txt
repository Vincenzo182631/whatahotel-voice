**1.Use of cookies**

When you browse our website, advertising cookies will be placed on your computer to understand your interests.

**2\. What is a cookie?**

This is a text file deposited in a dedicated space on the hard disk of your terminal (computer, tablet, cell phone or any other device optimized for the Internet), when consulting content or online advertising. This cookie file can only be read by its issuer. It allows its issuer, during its period of validity, to recognize the terminal concerned each time this terminal accesses digital content containing cookies from the same issuer. The cookie does not identify you personally, but only the browser of your terminal.

**3\. Types of cookies**

- so-called “session” cookies, which disappear as soon as you leave the browser or the site;
- permanent” cookies, which remain on your terminal until their lifetime expires or until you delete them using your browser’s functions.

Cookies are anonymous and are not used to collect personal information.

**4\. Our use of cookies**

We use cookies and other tracking devices to make it easier for you to navigate our website and to optimize your experience.

Functionality cookies are used to adapt our websites to the display preferences of your terminal (language, currency, display resolution, operating system used, configuration and parameterization of the display of the pages of the sites according to the terminal you are using and its location, etc.). ); to memorize certain information that you enter on our websites in order to facilitate and personalize your future browsing (for example, displaying your first and last names if you have a user account and have identified yourself); to allow you to access your personal areas more quickly by memorizing identifiers or data that you have previously indicated.

The purpose of audience tracking cookies is to improve your browsing experience by helping us to understand your interactions with our sites (most visited pages, applications used, etc.); these cookies can be used to produce statistics, or to test different displays in order to improve the interest and ergonomics of our services.

When you browse our website, advertising cookies will be placed on your computer to understand your interests. Our display advertising partners may then allow us to serve you retargeting ads on other sites based on your online activities on our website. The techniques our partners employ do not collect personal information such as your name, e-mail address, mailing address or telephone number.

**5\. Deactivating cookies**

You have the ability to accept or decline cookies from all websites you visit by changing the settings in your web browser. Each browser’s configuration is different. It is described in the help menu of your browser, which will allow you to know how to modify your wishes regarding cookies.
