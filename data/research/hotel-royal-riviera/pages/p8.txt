## Royal    Kid

### **YOUR FAMILY STAY AT THE**  **ROYAL-RIVIERA**

**Kid’s welcome**

- Exclusive V.I.K treatment
- A special hotel “passeport” will be given upon check-in
- Age-appropriate welcome gifts and kids amenities in room (Plush, candies, lollipops…

**Kids accommodation**

- Free stay in parents’ room under 12 years old (Deluxe room and Suites only)
- Connecting rooms available in the main building and in the Villa L’Orangeri

**Kids and restaurant**

- Free fruits cocktail offered at the bar
- A large selection of ice cream in our pool bar
- Kids Menu with a draw competition
- Children’s menu available in Restaurants and Room Service

**Your Kids’ facilities and activities in the hotel**

- Outdoor heated pool
- Private sandy beach
- Buckets and spades available at the Royal-Riviera private beach
- Water-sports for kids: paddle, trampoline, water-ski, lessons available on request
- Swimming lessons on request
- Table tennis (bats and balls available from the Concierge)
- List of DVDs available from the Concierge

_(No Kids’ Club – activities not supervised by hotel staff)_

**Kids and activity**

- Treasure Hunt at the Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild (7-12 years old)
