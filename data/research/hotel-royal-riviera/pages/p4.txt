## Family    Holidays

### **YOUR FAMILY STAY AT THE ROYAL-RIVIERA**

As a family-friendly hotel, the Royal-Riviera offers specific accommodation to welcome Guests traveling with kids.

Free stay for kids sharing parent’s room until 12 years old. Extra bed at 110€ per night for guests over 12. (extra bed available in Deluxe rooms, Junior Suites and Suites).

Complimentary baby crib on request (available in Superior, Deluxe rooms, Junior Suites and Suites). Royal Kid Program

**JUNIOR SUITES PANORAMIC SEA VIEW**

- **Family up to 3 people**

45 sqm / 484 sq ft. King or twin beds, and one extra bed.

Large marble bathroom with natural daylight, bath & shower separated.

- **Family up to 6 people**

Junior Suites are connecting with a Deluxe room Sea & Garden view (total area: 79 sqm / 850 sq ft) **.**

**ONE BEDROOM SUITE SEA & GARDEN VIEW**

- **Family up to 3 people**

60 sqm / 645 sq ft – Terrace 22 sqm / 237 sq ft

King or twin beds, one extra bed or one baby cot. Large marble bathroom **.**

- **Family up to 5 people**

The One Bedroom Suite is connecting with a Superior room (total area: 86 sqm / 946 sq ft).

**ONE BEDROOM SUITES ORANGERIE**

- **Family up to 3 people**

80 sqm / 860 sq ft. King or twin beds, one extra bed or pullout sofa or one baby cot.

Large marble bathroom + walk in shower and bath separated.

- **Family up to 6 people**

One Bedroom Suites Orangerie are connecting with one or two Deluxe rooms Orangerie (total area: 118 sqm / 1268 sq ft or 156 sqm / 1680 sq ft).
