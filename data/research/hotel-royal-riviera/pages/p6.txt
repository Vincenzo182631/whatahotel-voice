## The Royal-Riviera

The Royal-Riviera Hotel is a five-star hotel in the heart of Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, an iconic location on the Côte d’Azur. With a prime spot on the Cap-Ferrat peninsula with access to the beach, the hotel overlooks Provençal gardens and mountains as well as the sparkling Mediterranean sea.

The hotel boasts a total of 96 rooms and suites, 78 of which are in the main building. Designed to reflect the landscape of the south of France, the majority of the rooms overlook the sea and gardens. The private villa l’Orangerie features 18 additional rooms and Suites decorated in the purest Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat style.

## The destination

An old fishing village, Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat is a seaside and winter resort ideally located and much sought after for its serenity. The Cape, at the southernmost point, gives its name to the whole peninsula which shelters on one side the Rade of Villefranche on one side, and on the other, the Baie des Fourmis towards Beaulieu.

In Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat as in the region, amateurs can discover villas, museums and gardens, often draped in noble history. Also worth seeing: the village, the port, the 18th century Chapelle Sainte-Hospice and the Vierge Noire.
