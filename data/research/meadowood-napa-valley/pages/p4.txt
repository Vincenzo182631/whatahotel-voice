# Activities

## Recharge, engage, and play amid nature and experience Meadowood's joyful approach to recreation and relaxation.

Embrace wine country’s active approach to leisure, celebration, and rest both in and out of doors. Everyday life well lived at Meadowood celebrates the tangible wonders of nature as well as the role winegrowing plays in Napa Valley’s varied landscape, culture, and history.

## The Wine Center

Enjoy fine wine and delve into the distinctive details at The Wine Center at Meadowood Napa Valley. Learn about all aspects of wine appreciation and the world’s various regions of production. Examine the technical, aesthetic, and social underpinnings of fine wine as well as wine’s unique role in the culinary arts.

### The Wine Center is exclusive to Meadowood guests, Meadowood Club Members, and The Napa Valley Reserve Members.

With the Fitness Pool for lap swimming and aquatic classes, Family Activities Pool for our youngest guests, and Cabana Pool for adults-only lounging, our three-pool complex encompasses distinct, inviting environments that welcome everyone in the family to while away an hour or two or even the entire day.

## Tennis Courts

Wooded hillsides frame our five immaculately maintained outdoor courts. With our dedicated pros on call, tennis enthusiasts of all ages, interests, and levels can savor learning, playing, and competing in an idyllic environment.

### HOURS

Courts available daily, sunrise to sunset

## Hiking

Canvasing the Meadowood estate, our hiking trails provide a robust workout on our rugged hillside terrain. Take in panoramic views of St. Helena and the ridges that form Napa Valley. A morning hike on the southern trail often provides magical moments with hot air balloons in the distance over glistening vineyards.

### HOURS

Trails are available daily, from sunrise to sunset.

The hiking trails are only available for Meadowood guests and Meadowood members.
