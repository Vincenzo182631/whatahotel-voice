# Meadowood Lodging

## Inviting, understated, and elegant,  our 36 suites and rooms offer guests an immersion in the Napa Valley lifestyle.

Set amid the trees of the Meadowood estate, our Napa Valley accommodations are elegantly furnished for privacy, relaxation, and restoration. Each lodging uniquely combines the wine country’s distinctive blend of rustic and refined materials with an acute sensitivity to the nuances of comfort and the sophisticated ease of visual simplicity.

During your stay, you may notice some work taking place in a small portion of the estate as we prepare for the construction on a new infrastructure project. While the work will be visible as you move through the property, we expect minimal disruption to the overall guest experience, with no noise inside guest rooms. Please note that this work will mostly be confined to the hours of 8 a.m. – 3:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.

## Hill House

Nestled into the wooded hillside next to the Meadowood Spa, this expansive, one-bedroom retreat embraces Meadowood’s tradition of the indoor/outdoor lifestyle. In the separate living area, a sectional sofa angles around a fireplace. Two furnished terraces, each with a fire pit, accommodate under-the-sky entertaining or a private soak and shower.

RESERVE

The layout maximizes comfort and privacy.

The spacious bedroom encompasses a sitting area to the side with a large window overlooking the forested slopes, offering comfort, privacy, and ease. Opposite the bed, floor-to-ceiling pocket doors open to an expansive living room organized around the hearth. Beyond, a second set of pocket doors reveals a trellised terrace with its own sectional sofa. Sheltered off the bedroom, another terrace luxuriates in a deep soaking tub and outdoor shower. Together, the spaces total 1,745 square feet.

Indoor-outdoor living areas flow seamlessly.
