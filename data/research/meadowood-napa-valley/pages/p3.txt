## Frequently Asked Questions

Is Meadowood Spa open to the public?

Yes, we are happy to reserve a treatment for guests who are not members of Meadowood Club or staying at the hotel; however, we do reserve the right to block out certain days due to high occupancy.

Can a gift certificate be purchased for future spa services?

Yes, kindly call (707) 967-1275 or email spaconcierge@meadowood.com to inquire. Please note that a purchased gift certificate may be used at Meadowood Spa, at any one of the three Meadowood boutiques, or at our restaurant, Forum. Gift certificates may also be used toward the purchase of room nights.

Is gratuity included in listed price?

Spa menu prices are inclusive of a 20% service charge, which is distributed in its entirety to your practitioner.

Is there a couples suite?

Yes, there are two couples suites at Meadowood Spa.

Is there an age requirement to receive a spa treatment?

Yes, you must be 16 years of age or older to receive a spa treatment at Meadowood Spa.

Do you have a co-ed relaxation lounge?

No, our relaxation lounges are gender-specific; however, couples are welcome to relax in the reception living room, reserve additional suite time in their couples treatment suite, or reserve the Tree House Retreat suite.

What additional amenities can be found at Meadowood Spa?

In the outdoor relaxation lounges, we have a dry cedar sauna, steam room, plunge pool (cool or hot depending on the season), and an outdoor rain shower.
