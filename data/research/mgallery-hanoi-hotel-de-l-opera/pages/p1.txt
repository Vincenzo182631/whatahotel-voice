## Hotel de l'Opera Hanoi - MGallery

### Hotel Overview

* * *

**‘Xin Chào!’**

From all of us at Hotel de l’Opera – MGallery Hanoi, we look forward to having the opportunity to welcome you to this fascinating and historic city.

Hanoi is an enchanting city with hundreds of serene lakes, scenic boulevards, a colonial heritage and communities with histories that go back over a thousand years.

Located in the heart of Hanoi, the hotel is within walking distance of several city attractions, such as the Hanoi Opera House, the city’s Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake and the Thang Long Water Puppet Theatre.

**‘A NIGHT AT THE OPERA’**

A link to the Hotel’s location adjacent to the  Hanoi Opera House, Hotel de l’Opera – MGallery Hanoi aims to allow guests to enjoy a truly unique experience.

The theme of the hotel is interpreted in every corner, bringing operatic and theatrical flair to the interior design.

**‘AWARD WINNING LUXURY BOUTIQUE HOTEL’**

With its exemplary experiences, strategic location, bespoke design and luxury offerings,  Hotel de l’Opera – MGallery Hanoi is honored to attain the prestigious titles “ **Best Boutique Hotel for Golfers  – Vietnam 2022**” by Golf & Leisure Magazine. “ **Best Hotel Service – Asia 2023”, “Best Design Hotel – Vietnam 2023”,** **“Best Destination Hotel – Vietnam 2023”, “Best Luxury Boutique Hotel – Vietnam 2023**”, **“Best Culinary Experience – Asia 2026**” and **“Most Unique Guest Experience – Asia 2026**” by Haute Grandeur Global Awards, “ **TripAdvisor Travelers’ Choice Awards 2024**“, “ **Top 10 Best City Hotels in Vietnam  2023**” by DestinAsian Reader’s Choice Awards, “ **Best Business Hotel Vietnam 2023**” and “ **Best Design Hotel Vietnam 2023**” by Vietnam Tourism Association.

We look forward to welcoming you soon.
