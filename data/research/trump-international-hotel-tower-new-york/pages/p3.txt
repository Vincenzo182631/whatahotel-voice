# **NOUGATINE**

## CENTRAL PARK DINING

INDOOR / OUTDOOR

DINING _AND_ BAR

+1 212 299 3900

RESERVE A TABLE

A more casual expression of Jean-Georges, Nougatine by Jean-Georges is nestled between sweeping Central Park views and the restaurant’s exhibition kitchen. The menu features signature Jean-Georges dishes alongside seasonal daily specials, offering an approachable take on fine dining.

During the warmer months, the park-facing Mistral Terrace features Dedon furniture designed by Philippe Starck and offers the full Nougatine menu alongside signature cocktails. This open-air setting pairs exceptional cuisine with striking city views, while plush lounge seating creates a relaxed and inviting atmosphere just steps from Columbus Circle.

Open daily for breakfast, lunch, and dinner, Nougatine highlights fresh, high-quality ingredients in every dish.

Business casual attire is required. Outdoor seating is weather dependent.
