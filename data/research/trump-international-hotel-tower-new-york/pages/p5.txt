Trump International Hotel & Tower New York
Across from Central Park on Columbus Circle, this 52-story hotel is a great home base for sight-seeing.

Across from Central Park on Columbus Circle, this 52-story hotel is "great for shopping, dining, Broadway shows, and exploring the city." Each apartment-style room has a sophisticated color palette of gray, aubergine, ivory, and caramel, with Schonbek crystal chandeliers over the dining table and rosewood, mahogany, maple, and walnut finishes. "The kitchens have top-of-the-line appliances, and at evening turndown, water is served in Rogaska glasses." Both Jean Georges and Nougatine are run by Jean-Georges Vongerichten.
