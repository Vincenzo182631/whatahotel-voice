LUXURIOUSLY APPOINTED.

_EXQUISITELY_ DECORATED.

Experience the ultimate indulgence with an extended stay at Trump International Hotel & Tower New York. Our 176 luxury accommodations include superior and deluxe guest rooms, as well as some of the best suites in NYC, with one, two, three, and four bedrooms.. Each room and suite boasts stunning floor-to-ceiling windows that showcase captivating panoramas of Central Park and the Manhattan skyline.

Come discover unparalleled luxury and comfort at Trump International Hotel & Tower New York, where every moment is nothing short of extraordinary.
