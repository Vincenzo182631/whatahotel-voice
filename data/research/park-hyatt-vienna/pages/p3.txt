## The Bank Brasserie & Bar

Located in the centerpiece of the building, with its own entrance from Bognergasse, The Bank Brasserie presents an exceptional dining experience in a warm sophisticated atmosphere.
Focusing on newly interpreted brasserie classics, using sustainable locally sourced products, we serve a daily buffet breakfast, a Plats du Jour menu and A la carte lunch as well as dinner.
The open show kitchen allows diners to watch and follow the chefs preparing delicious menus using high-quality products.
The Bank Bar presents specially created international premium cocktails based on sensorial and optical associations with the former cashier hall and materials like wood, leather, metal and copper - reminiscent of a time when the building used to be a bank.

## Café Am Hof

The display is filled with delicious cakes, chocolates and tartes lovingly prepared by the team around Chef Patissier Oliver Ivanschits. In addition to classic Viennese pastries, you can also enjoy modern patisserie art and much more.
At lunchtime, we offer a variety of delicious dishes. A special highlight: All delicacies can also be packed to go. Upon request and advance order, we also create individual cakes for you - ideal for any occasion.

## The Bollicafé

Open from May to October.

From May to October, the elegant outdoor terrace of Park Hyatt Vienna transforms into the Bollicafé in collaboration with Champagne Bollinger and KATE & KON, an exclusive summer spot for lovers of fine wines and Champagne. In chic black-and-white design with a touch of French flair, guests can enjoy a refined selection of Bollinger Champagne, accompanied by exquisite seafood or small bites such as filled brioche buns, the popular Bollibites, or discover our latest addition: Pinsa, perfect for sharing. The Bollicafé is the perfect meeting place for connoisseurs, right in the heart of Vienna.
