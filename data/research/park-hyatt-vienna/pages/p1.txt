# Luxury Hotel In Vienna Near Cathedral \| Park Hyatt Vienna

## Elegant Luxury Hotel in the Heart of Vienna

At Park Hyatt Vienna an enriching experience for sophisticated travelers is guaranteed. Enjoy exquisite interiors and high-quality products at our five-star hotel in Vienna. Indulge in first-class service, fine dining and luxurious spa moments at the former vault of the historic bank building.

## Discover Park Hyatt Vienna

Private Celebrations

From a birthday party to a baptism or a wedding celebration on a large scale, we offer the ideal setting for your next private celebration with all the luxurious finishes and attention to detail you desire.

Festive Moments at Park Hyatt Vienna

Experience the festive season in Vienna with thoughtfully curated culinary experiences. From seasonal menus and brunches to memorable moments throughout Christmas and New Year.

Your Home Away from Home

Indulge in the luxurious Royal Penthouse Suite, a two-level haven that can host up to seven guests. This sophisticated escape is complete with a cutting-edge gym, private elevator and four expansive terraces showcasing breathtaking panoramic views of Vienna’s charming city centre.

Wellbeing & Fitness

You will discover the ultimate luxury wellbeing experience at Arany Spa. Indulge in the 15-metre (49-foot) indoor swimming pool, relax in the wet area, or unwind with various massages or cosmetic and beauty treatments. The extensive fitness centre, which features modern TechnoGym equipment and a Peloton bike, is open 24/7 for hotel guests and is located on Level -1 of the hotel. Children under the age of 16 are welcome when accompanied by an adult from 11 a.m.–6 p.m. daily.

- Spa hours: Monday–Sunday, 7 a.m.–9 p.m.
- More information: +43 122740 1130 or spa-arany.vienna@hyatt.com.

Grand Salon

Located in a 100-year-old building, this ballroom is bursting with history. Where once bank directors of the Länderbank conducted negotiations, today all types of events can be held within this historic space.

Shopping at the Golden Quarter

Shop in the most luxurious boutiques at the exclusive Golden Quarter (Goldenes Quartier), only steps away from the hotel, or book a private shopping guide and discover small local designers and traditional manufacturers. Our concierge will be glad to advise you.

Travelling with Your Family

Discover our suites that can host your family, allowing everyone to indulge in privacy individually and enjoy one another’s company at the same time. We offer you and your family a place of well-being, so you can truly feel at home away from home.

Hairspa

The Rossano Ferretti Hairspa is located at the former Living Room of Park Hyatt Vienna and welcomes its guests with complete hair pampering. Make an appointment to discover the wide range of personalised services at this prestigious salon.

- Salon hours: Monday, noon–6 p.m., Tuesday–Friday, 9 a.m.–7 p.m., Saturday, 9 a.m.–3 p.m.
- More information: +43 122740 1260 or wien@rossanoferretti.com.

Gift Voucher

Order the perfect gift for your loved ones online. Vouchers can be used for a luxurious experience with culinary highlights, deluxe spa treatments and much more, making your stay at Park Hyatt Vienna even more unforgettable.

### The Bank Brasserie & Bar

Located in the centerpiece of the building, with its own entrance from Bognergasse, The Bank Brasserie presents an exceptional dining experience in a warm sophisticated atmosphere.
Focusing on newly interpreted brasserie classics, using sustainable locally sourced products, we serve a daily buffet breakfast, a Plats du Jour menu and A la carte lunch as well as dinner.
The open show kitchen allows diners to watch and follow the chefs preparing delicious menus using high-quality products.
The Bank Bar presents specially created international premium cocktails based on sensorial and optical associations with the former cashier hall and materials like wood, leather, metal and copper - reminiscent of a time when the building used to be a bank.
