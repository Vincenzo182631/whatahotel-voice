# Parking & Transportation

## Parking

### Valet Parking

Valet parking is available for guests of Park Hyatt Vienna from 6:30 a.m.–11 p.m

Overnight

### Self Parking

Guests can use the public car park (Am Hof) in front of the hotel for self-parking. There’s direct access from the garage through a side entrance into the hotel lobby. The car park has a height restriction of 2.05 metres.

Hourly

### EV Charging Station (near the hotel)

In the public carpark (Am Hof) in front of the hotel there are charging stations available with 22 kW and 11 kW. Fees: Subject to change by provider.
