## Baglioni Hotel Luna - The Leading Hotels Of The World

ex. Luna Hotel Baglioni Venezia

The 5-star Baglioni Hotel Luna - The Leading Hotels Of The World Venice is located nearly a 5-minute stroll from Royal Gardens of Venice, offering 86 rooms, Wi-Fi, and a car park. This Venice hotel is in San Marco with a gorgeous view that stretches to the Lagoon, only 1.9 km from such natural sights as the Giardini della Biennale.

The ornate covered 16th-century stone footbridge "Rialto Bridge" is 850 metres away, and culture-oriented attractions such as the historic Bridge of Sighs lie in the immediate vicinity of the hotel. Baglioni Hotel Luna - The Leading Hotels Of The World Venice is just 5 minutes on foot from the grand showpiece square "Piazza San Marco" and 5 km from Marco Polo airport. Nature lovers will appreciate the proximity to bacino Orseolo, which is only 150 metres away. Also, the accommodation is approximately a 10-minute ride from the satellite Peggy Guggenheim Collection Gallery, and Venezia Santa Lucia train station is located merely 1.9 km away.

In-room facilities include a flat-screen TV with satellite channels as well as a mini fridge bar, coffee and tea making equipment. Climate control are featured for guests' comfort. Offering a bidet, a spa bathtub, and a separate toilet, the bathrooms are fitted with hairdryers and towels.

Baglioni Hotel Luna - The Leading Hotels Of The World is right around the corner from Cafe Florian.
