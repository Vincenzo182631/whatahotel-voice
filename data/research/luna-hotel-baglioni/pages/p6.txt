## A Day in the Life of Our Guests

Discover the canals, secret alleys & intriguing past of this magical, seafaring city. Learn about the legends of Marco Polo, Verdi & the infamous Bridge of Sighs.

## Baglioni Hotel Luna

This historic property near Saint Mark’s Square is one of the top-rated 5-star hotels in the city. Every inch reflects traditional Venetian style, from authentic period furnishings & glorious frescoed ceilings to beautiful Murano glass & bronze accents.

## Nearby Attractions

Explore Venice on foot, by canal, or boat. Start at Saint Mark’s Square, which is literally steps away, then make a plan to get lost and see what you can discover. Here are some highlights you won’t want to miss.

**NEARBY ATTRACTIONS:**

• Bridge of Sighs

• Doge’s Palace

• Harry’s Bar

• Rialto Bridge

• Saint Mark’s Campanile (cathedral tower)

• Saint Mark’s Basilica

• Santa Maria della Salute Basílica

• Venice Opera House
