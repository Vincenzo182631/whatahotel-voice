Corporate Retreats at Zadun, Ritz Carlton Reserve
Meetings
At Zadún, traditional office views are replaced by the endless blue of the Sea of Cortés. Lunch moves from your desk to the edge of a pool, and after-work drinks become a journey through the rich history of mezcal and tequila. Business here is anything but ordinary. Indoor–outdoor meeting venues inspire fresh thinking, cuisine tells the story of the region, and curated activities create meaningful connections among colleagues and the land around them.

Meeting Venues
Set at the very top of the resort, El Nido, or “nest”, offers two indoor spaces for meetings and a terrace ideal for breaks. With their open-air spaces, the event lawn, the beach and El Huerto “the farm”, can host creative setups for banquets and receptions.

Outdoor Venues
La Pergola
Featuring an “Organic Farm” concept, this venue offers beautiful views of the Sea of Cortés and spectacular sunsets. It is ideal for intimate events of up to 20 guests, providing a private and exclusive atmosphere.
Porte-Cochère
Available only with a full buyout, accommodates up to 250 guests and is ideal for hosting an impressive farewell dinner.
Residence Lawn
With breathtaking views of the Sea of Cortés and unforgettable sunsets, this private and exclusive setting is perfect for intimate gatherings of up to 40 guests.
Nido Ballroom
An elegant indoor space designed for privacy and exclusivity, it accommodates up to 100 guests and is ideal for group events, small meetings, or incentive gatherings.
Cultural Lounge
Perfect for private groups, small meetings, or incentive events, this breathtaking indoor venue offers focus and tranquility for up to 100 guests.
The Garden
The Garden, in the heart of the Reserve, is the perfect setting to enjoy the surrounding environment. It allows for exceptional setups and the convenience of having everything in one place. It accommodates up to 140 guests.
Grand Reserve Villa
Grand Reserve is one of the most exclusive and private spaces within the Reserve, ideal for intimate gatherings. It features a dedicated hospitality suite and accommodates up to 180 guests.
