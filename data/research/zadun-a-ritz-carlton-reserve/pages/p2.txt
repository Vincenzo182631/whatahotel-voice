Zadún, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve
At Zadún, expect elite service and pared-down decor complemented by views of the Sea of Cortez.

Why book?
Nestled within the artistic enclave of San José del Cabo and set behind the gates of Puerto Los Cabos, Zadún blends privacy with a sense of place, offering spacious suites and full residences, all with plunge pools and highly personalized butler service.
Set the scene
Every view from Zadún feels cinematic, from the sweeping Sea of Cortez vistas to the desert landscape. The guests here aren’t chasing Cabo’s nightlife; It’s a place for those who want to feel at home, with every need taken care of in an atmosphere designed for true rest and rejuvenation.
The backstory
The fourth in Ritz-Carlton’s ultra-luxury Reserve collection, Zadún technically opened in 2019, but with Covid’s subsequent arrival, it considers 2021 its birthdate. Reserve properties are meant to feel more like private estates than hotels, with guests receiving personal tosoani (“dream keepers”) that orchestrate everything from in-room check-in to bespoke experiences.
The rooms
The majority of Zadún’s 87 guest rooms, 27 suites, villa, and residences face the Sea of Cortez, with terraces that frame the view and private plunge pools. Oftentimes, guests will even have firepits to congregate around after a long day of relaxation. Even my resort view room felt expansive with an oversized bathroom complete with a soaking tub and both an indoor and outdoor shower. While the rooms above me in the two-story building configuration had better sea views, my terrace with plunge pool felt like the perfect escape, with multiple day beds hidden by landscaping—and I could still make out the sea in the distance.
Food and drink
The real buzz at Zadún centers on Humo, the dramatic open-flame grill that will reopen with a new look in mid-October 2025. Here, the ribeye and whole grilled snapper are must-orders. Next door, the Candil bar, located in the resort’s “lighthouse,” steals evenings with its agave program, one of the best in Los Cabos, with the signature Desert Moon cocktail featuring mezcal, prickly pear, and a hint of chili. By day, head to El Barrio, which bursts with color, designed to resemble lively street vendors as it serves up bold Mexican breakfasts and street-food-inspired lunches, and for something lighter, visit the omakase-style raw bar at Equis.
The spa
The beating heart of Zadún’s wellness program is Sensei, a 29,000-square-foot spa devoted to multisensory journeys. In addition to traditional treatments (which Zadún has plenty of for those interested), Sensei's main draw is its holistic approach to wellness: sound baths, sensory eating, and guided experiences that are both grounding and transportive. Some of these are incorporated naturally into à la carte spa offerings, while those who want the total experience can book a wellness-focused package.
The neighborhood/area
Zadún sits in Puerto Los Cabos, a quiet enclave just minutes from the artistic community of San José del Cabo and far from Cabo San Lucas’ more party-centric scene. With golf courses by Greg Norman and Jack Nicklaus drawing a well-heeled crowd, the area feels serene and polished. Its perch also makes it a prime spot for whale watching during migration season, with whales easily visible in the distance from guest rooms.
The service
Service at Zadún is defined by The Ritz-Carlton's overall “Ladies and Gentlemen” philosophy, where staff are held to a high degree of professionalism and expected to provide impeccable service. Tosoani (“dream keepers”) operate with such a quiet intuition that you may not always see the magic being performed behind the scenes, anticipating your every need before you even ask.
For families
Though Zadún feels like it was originally designed for couples seeking quiet, it adapts well for families too, as evidenced by the number of children who can be found splashing in the resort’s pools. The multi-bedroom residences provide space for kids to spread out, and the resort offers fun and educational activities through the Ritz-Carlton’s renowned Ambassadors of the Environment program.
Eco effort
The property has a Green Key certification, a mark of rigorous environmental management. It uses renewable energy sources and electric vehicle charging stations for resident cars and the electric golf carts that transport guests around the property. There's also a strong recycling program.
Accessibility
Although there are areas where design overrules function, the majority of the resort meets ADA standards with ramps and wheelchair-friendly pathways.
Anything left to mention?
The beach at Zadún isn’t swimmable—the drop-off is steep and currents are strong. It’s a trade-off worth knowing in advance, but the upside is front-row views of migrating whales in season.
