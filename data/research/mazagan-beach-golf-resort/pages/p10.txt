# Sel de Mer at Mazagan

Indulge in the finest seafood, sublime service and a truly elegant setting.

## Elegance At Its Best

Inspired by the stunning Atlantic Ocean just outside its doors, this is where unforgettable memories are made over seafood delicacies.

Tables are bathed in a soft blue glow cast by intricate floor-to-ceiling lights, and subtle odes to the ocean and aquatic touches can be found throughout. Intimate, elegant and refined – we invite you to set sail on a culinary journey at Sel de Mer to discover the magic of the sea.
