# Bushra by Buddha Bar at Mazagan

Magnificent, mesmerizing and eclectically mythical. Welcome to Bushra by Buddha-Bar.

## Where curiosity is piqued, and stories become legends

Travel to an exotic, colourful and wholesome world of Middle Eastern flavors, with an abundance of vegetables, aromatic herbs and spices that engage all your senses.

Bushra is a journey. But it is also a destination. It is an escape, but a connection. A story that began with intrepid ancestors across North Africa and Europe, and lives on in the modern Middle Eastern spirit of today.

Imagined by Buddha-Bar, it is a beacon of provocative sophistication in an environment that will always keep you coming back for more.

Magnificent, mesmerizing and eclectically mythical. An oasis of good news and good omens.

Welcome to Bushra by Buddha-Bar.
