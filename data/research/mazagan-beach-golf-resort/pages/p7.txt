Inspired by a classic French bistro, this candlelit restaurant has charm in abundance.

## La Cave French Bistro

La Cave French Bistro is just as its name suggests – warm, inviting and cosy. A traditional bistro serving classic French dishes, La Cave offers everything from rich casseroles and buttery snails to fresh fish and hearty soups.

Under the warm, gentle glow of flickering candles, this is the perfect place to indulge in gourmet cuisine and a fantastic drinks selection while you spend a romantic evening with a loved one, or catch up with family and friends.
