# Accommodation at Mazagan

Uncover your exclusive Moroccan haven within the lavish guest rooms and opulent suites at Mazagan Beach & Golf Resort.

## Rooms And Suites

Where authentic Moroccan tradition and charm meets stylish contemporary design, each of the hotel’s rooms and suites offers you the complete Mazagan experience. Overlooking the Grand Riad pool, lush gardens and forest, or with sweeping vistas of the deep blue Atlantic Ocean, outside each of the 492 rooms is an enchanting world waiting to be discovered.

Whether you’re a couple in search of a romantic retreat, a family seeking extra space for your mini-adventurers, or a sun-lover chasing Morocco’s famous sunsets, here you can enjoy wonderful year-round weather and find your piece of paradise in the unique Moroccan destination.
