## Contemporary Glam

Journey back in time to discover the 1940’s glitz & glam at Studio 42. Luxurious & sophisticated with its royal green velvet shroud, the decor is inspired by the studios where the most emblematic scenes of the legendary film “Casablanca” were filmed.
