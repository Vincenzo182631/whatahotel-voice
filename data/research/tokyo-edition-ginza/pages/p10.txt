# Getting Here

- From Haneda
- From Narita

## From Haneda

to 2-8-13 Ginza, Chuo-ku, Tokyo, Japan 104-0061

## CAR HIRE

Transportation with a sophisticated touch, a private car to/from Tokyo International Airport - Haneda (HND) is available from JPY 23,000 each way, inclusive of tax and gratuity, depending on the vehicle type. A private car can be arranged by the hotel guest service prior to your arrival/departure.

## DRIVING

Proceed along Shutokosoku Wangansen, and take the Ginza exit towards Ginza. Turn left onto Route 304, then turn right onto Route 316. Turn left at Ginza Higashi 2-chome and proceed along Ginza Marronnier Street, then turn right onto Azuma Street. The hotel is on the right.

## From Narita

to 2-8-13 Ginza, Chuo-ku, Tokyo, Japan 104-0061

## CAR HIRE

Transportation with a sophisticated touch, a private car to/from Tokyo Narita International Airport (NRT) is available from JPY 40,000 each way, inclusive of tax and gratuity, depending on the vehicle type. A private car can be arranged by the hotel guest service prior to your arrival/departure.

## DRIVING

Head northeast towards Shinkuko Jidoshado. Join onto Shutokosoku Wangansen and proceed along Shutokosoku No. 9 Fukagawa Line. Use the right lane to enter the Shutokosoku Toshin Kanjosen. Turn left and head towards Chukyo 430, then turn right and head towards Ginza Marronnier Street. Turn right onto Azuma Street and the hotel is on the right.
