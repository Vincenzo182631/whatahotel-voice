# The Roof

## The Roof

RESERVE

Perched above the streets of Ginza, The ROOF is a seasonal rooftop destination designed for those who want to gather, linger, and enjoy.

Whether settling into intimate, plant-fringed seating nooks for a quiet evening or coming together around long communal tables for a more social occasion, the space effortlessly adapts to every mood. Fueled by a curated selection of champagnes, wines, and seasonal cocktails crafted with Japanese spirits and ingredients, each visit is complemented by a refined selection of small plates, including “stuzzichini” authentic Italian light bites from Sophie at EDITION.

For general inquiries, please contact us at +81(3)6228-7400 or at diningreservation.ginza@editionhotels.com.
