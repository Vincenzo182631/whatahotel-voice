# TOKYO’S ULTIMATE EPICENTER OF THE NEW GENERATION OF LUXURY

The Tokyo EDITION, Ginza offers a low-slung blend of old and new unfurling in Ginza, where the cutting edge of the world and traditional Japanese culture are interwoven. The hotel is situated just off Chuo Street, one of the city’s largest upscale entertainment and shopping destinations, and home to luxury brand boutiques and Michelin-starred restaurants. Featuring 86 guest rooms, including 11 lavishly appointed suites, guests can indulge in an understated luxury carefully crafted by Ian Schrager and Kengo Kuma. The hotel offers four dining venues, including Lobby Bar, Japan’s first punch-focused cocktail bar, a modern brasserie, and a roof-top bar, and also features a gym.

View Rooms & Suites

## HOME AWAY FROM HOME

Located on floors 3 through 13, all 86 guest rooms including 11 lavish suites feature understated luxury and a sophisticated design by Kengo Kuma, incorporating the vision of Ian Schrager. The clean-lined interiors weave EDITION’s signature style with Kengo Kuma’s distinct Japanese finesse, infusing every corner with a touch of Japanese influence.
