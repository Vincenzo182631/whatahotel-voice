# Sophie at EDITION

**Sophie at EDITION**

RESERVE WEBSITE

Located on the 14th floor of The Tokyo EDITION, Ginza, Sophie at EDITION is an authentic Italian restaurant that celebrates the purity of ingredients and traditional culinary techniques.

The kitchen brings the true essence of Italian gastronomy to life, seamlessly blending time-honored traditions with Japan's rich culinary bounty. The menu features refined Italian dishes crafted with carefully selected produce from Japan and Italy, with its signature handmade pasta program at the heart of the offering, ranging from beloved classics to seasonal creations that highlight the finest ingredients of the moment. Each dish is prepared with a steadfast commitment to authenticity, allowing the natural character of every ingredient to shine and delivering a genuine expression of Italian cuisine. Sophie at EDITION offers an all-day dining experience, complemented by a seasonally evolving cocktail selection and a diverse, thoughtfully curated wine program.

For general inquiries or reservations, please contact us at +81(3)6228-74006228-7400) or at diningreservation.ginza@editionhotels.com

**Punch Room**

RESERVE

Punch Room is EDITION’s globally celebrated cocktail bar concept, inspired by the refined elegance of 19th-century London private clubs and centered around the timeless art of punch. An intimate and sophisticated retreat, Punch Room invites guests to explore the deep-rooted heritage of punch-making, thoughtfully reimagined through the lens of Japanese culture. The menu is curated around five distinct categories, each reflecting one of the “five flavor pillars” of punch, harmonized with a unique facet of Japanese tradition. Every creation showcases exceptional Japanese ingredients, ranging from artisanal local spirits and sake to ryokucha tea and rare regional spices. Punches are served either by the glass or presented in exquisite silver bowls designed for sharing, accompanied by elegantly crafted ladles.

Punch Room Tokyo is proud to be ranked No. 23 on Asia’s 50 Best Bars 2026, a prestigious award celebrating the most exceptional bars across the region.

For general inquiries or reservations, please contact us at +81(3)6228-74006228-7400) or at diningreservation.ginza@editionhotels.com

**Lobby Bar**

Lobby Bar sits at the nexus of the hotel and serves as a sophisticated retreat for both in-house guests and clued-in locals from early mornings to late nights. The gold-colored bar, surrounded by ivory fabric armchairs and a sweeping statement sofa, serves up coffee with beans from a local roastery alongside pastries for breakfasts on the go.

For general inquiries, please contact us at +81(3)6228-74006228-7400) or at diningreservation.ginza@editionhotels.com.

**The Roof**

RESERVE

Perched above the streets of Ginza, The ROOF is a seasonal rooftop destination designed for those who want to gather, linger, and enjoy.

Whether settling into intimate, plant-fringed seating nooks for a quiet evening or coming together around long communal tables for a more social occasion, the space effortlessly adapts to every mood. Fueled by a curated selection of champagnes, wines, and seasonal cocktails crafted with Japanese spirits and ingredients, each visit is complemented by a refined selection of small plates, including "stuzzichini" authentic Italian light bites from Sophie at EDITION.

For general inquiries, please contact us at +81(3)6228-7400 or at diningreservation.ginza@editionhotels.com.
