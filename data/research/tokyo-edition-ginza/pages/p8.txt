# Services & Amenities

The Tokyo EDITION Ginza creates a unique, one-of-a-kind experience together with the best and most personalized modern service available.

### For further information

Please contact us at info.ginza@editionhotels.com

Share

HOTEL INFORMATION

- Check-in 3:00 pm
- Check-out 12:00 pm
- Smoke free property
- Pets not allowed
- On-site parking daily 4,000 JPY

GUEST SERVICES

- 86 lavishly appointed guest rooms including 11 suites, adorned with Kengo Kuma’s distinct Japanese finesse
- One-of-a-kind 1,237sqft (115sqm) Penthouse, featuring stylishly spacious living and dining areas and pantry
- A signature restaurant seating 64 guests, a modern Tokyo brasserie serving breakfast, lunch and dinner in an inviting space
- The first bar in Japan to offer an authentic punch bowl ritual, inspired by 19th-century London private clubs
- A sophisticated gold-colored lobby bar with 24 exclusive seats
- Ginza’s first natural wine roof-top bar offering natural wines, cocktails, and eaux de vie from local wineries and distilleries, surrounded by lush greenery
- Mid to small scale event plans in dining venues and the penthouse
- Customized menus for private events, luncheons and dinner parties
- 24-hour state-of-the-art gym powered by Technogym
- Sustainable room amenities
- EDITION’s exclusive Black Tea scent enjoyed in public space and bathroom amenities, created in collaboration with Le Labo
- Concierge services
- 24-hour room service
- Multi-lingual staff

EXECUTIVE SERVICES

- Private event space
- Executive car services available
- State-of-the-art audio-visual equipment
- Complimentary high-speed wireless Internet access throughout the hotel

GYM

- State-of-the art fitness equipment including cardiovascular machines with personal flat screen TVs
- Accessible 24 hours a day
- Complimentary towels
- Headphones and refreshments

ACCESSIBILITY

- Mobility accessible rooms available for certain room types
