## Summit Chinese Restaurant

This restaurant, located on a mountaintop, makes it the highest Chinese Restaurant in the hotel. The view of Nanjing, its lakes and mountains, are magnificent. The chosen colors red, gold and black, together with the very high ceilings, make this the ultimate first class Chinese restaurant of the region. Our chefs skillfully prepare classic Huai Yang dishes. The restaurant offers nine private dining rooms that can accommodate groups of different sizes.

## Prime Bar

Prime Bar located on the 78th floor. It offers a 360° panoramic view, overlooking the beautiful scenery of Nanjing, such as Xuanwu Lake and Zijin Mountain. The bar features a rich selection of signature cocktails, such as "Prime Sunset", which are both visually appealing and delicious. With its curved glass window design, it is an excellent place to enjoy the sunset and night view.
