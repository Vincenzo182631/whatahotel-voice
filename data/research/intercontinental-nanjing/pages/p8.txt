# InterContinental NanjingDestination

High-rise giant InterContinental Nanjing is situated at city center Gulou Square, and is walking distance to Dazhongting, Gulou Park, Xuanwu Lake, Nanjing University, Southeast University, and Jiming Temple.

## Things to do

### Xuanwu Lake

NO.1 Xuanwu Xiang, Gulou District, Nanjing, Jiangsu 210009

Xuanwu Lake park is the largest imperial garden of China, and the largest remaining Chinese royal garden and city park of the south of Yangzi river. Culture of Xuanwu Lake could date back to two thousand and three hundred years ago，while known as the “Jinling pearl”, it is now a significant national park.

Area Attractions Confucius Temple Qinhuai River scenery belt
