### Exceptional City Landmark Location

The hotel enjoys a prime location at Gulou Square in Gulou District, surrounded by bustling attractions including Xuanwu Lake, Jimi Temple, 1912 Bar Street, the Ming Dynasty City Wall, Hunan Road Shiziqiao Food Street, and Xinjiekou Commercial Street. Perched at the city's highest vantage point, the hotel offers panoramic views.

### 450-Meter Breathing Architecture

Designed by SOM—the world-renowned architectural firm led by Adrian Smith, the “pioneer of skyscraper design”—the InterContinental Nanjing occupies the iconic Zifeng Tower, soaring nearly 450 meters high. Its exterior features a groundbreaking triangular glass curtain wall that spirals upward like dragon scales.

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Nanjing?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Nanjing.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Nanjing for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Nanjing have a restaurant on site?

When is breakfast at InterContinental Nanjing?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Nanjing?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Nanjing.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Nanjing?

Check-in at InterContinental Nanjing is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Nanjing offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Nanjing offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Nanjing from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Nanjing is conveniently located near Nanjing Lukou International Airport (NKG), approximately 26.1 miles away (42 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.
