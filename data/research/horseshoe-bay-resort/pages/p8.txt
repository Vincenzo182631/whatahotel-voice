## FAQ

**Are dogs allowed?**

Must be on a short leash, non-retractable style, and must be well-behaved. Owners are responsible for picking up after their pet and maintaining safe distances from other dogs and children.

**Are strollers allowed?**

Yes. Wheelchairs, wagons, and running strollers are welcome on the 5K Walk route. We ask that all walkers and strollers start in the back of the pack. Please no bicycles, rollerblades, skateboards, or scooters.

**Are refunds allowed?**

No. We are a rain or shine event. Please note this event is located in Horseshoe Bay, Texas.

**Can I pick up my packet and shirt on race day?**

Yes, but advance pickup is requested. Pickup location: Whitewater Putting Hut, 200 Hi Circle North. Monday, Nov 24, 5-10 PM, Tuesday, Nov 25, 8 AM-2 PM, Wednesday, Nov 26, 5-10 PM.

**Can somebody else pick up my packet for me?**

Yes. The person picking up your packet will need to show an email or text confirming permission to pick up.
