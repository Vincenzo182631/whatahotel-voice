# 1st ANNUAL HOLIDAY PICKLEBALL CLASSIC

DECEMBER 4-6, 2026

A weekend of world-class instruction with top-ranked professionals **Lina Padegimaite** and **Zane Navratil,** two of the sport’s most decorated players. Whether you’re chasing a personal best or just want to swing a paddle alongside the pros, this marquee pickleball weekend delivers competitive play and hotel accomodations on the shores of Lake LBJ.

While you’re here, celebrate the season by joining us for the festivities surrounding our magical event:

**Big and Bright: A Hill Country Holiday**

## Lina Padegimaite

## Lina Padegimaite

Lithuania Fed Cup tennis, Big XII Freshman of the Year at UT, and a Doctorate of Physical Therapy, Lina picked up a paddle two months after her son was born and won a regional title within a year. She’s a certified pickleball instructor with 8 pro medals and a 2x Major League Pickleball semifinalist.

-

## Zane Navratil

## Zane Navratil

The winningest player in Major League Pickleball history, Zane is a former world #1 with over 100 medals and 4 Major League titles. He quit his job as a CPA at Deloitte to go pro and then invented a spin serve the sport had to ban.

A two-night hotel accomodation

6 hours of pro instruction with Lina Padegimaite and Zane Navratil

Round robin competition

Doubles and Mixed Doubles divisions

Cocktail reception — mingle with fellow players and the pros

Swag bag full of event gear and goodies
