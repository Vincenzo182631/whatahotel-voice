# Celebrate Thanksgiving at Horseshoe Bay Resort

Celebrate a festive week of food, fun, and family at the resort! Enjoy a Thanksgiving Day Brunch and seasonal dinner specials at J’s Restaurant & Bar, plus holiday-themed activities throughout the week for guests of all ages. Then, keep the magic going—the holiday festivities kick off the Friday after Thanksgiving, transforming the resort into a sparkling holiday escape!

## Winter Wonderland + Wine Expo

**Saturday, November 28, 2026**

**Live Oak Lawn & Palm Pavilion**

**5:30 PM- 8:30 PM**

Sip and shop with tastings from top wineries and unique retailers offering the perfect holiday gifts! Experience the magic of the season with Santa’s arrival, the lighting of our 50-foot Christmas tree, holiday harmonies by VocaLibre, festive treats, pop-up bars, and endless photo opportunities. Guests can also delight in horse-drawn carriage and train rides throughout the evening.

Wine Expo tickets available at the door for resort guests and members of The Club at Horseshoe Bay.

_Winter Wonderland is complimentary for resort guests and members of The Club at Horseshoe Bay._
