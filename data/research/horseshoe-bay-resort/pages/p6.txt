## Celebrate the 2026 Season at Horseshoe Bay Resort!

Revel in a big and bright holiday season in the heart of the Texas Hill Country, November 27-December 6, 2026. Enjoy the dazzling Winter Wonderland tree lighting + Wine Expo, a journey to the North Pole at the Jingle Bell Express Breakfast, Victorian Carolers and Kid’s Karaoke at Christmas on the Lawn, wake up for the whimsical Charlie Brown Christmas Breakfast, and plan to play in the first-ever Horseshoe Bay Resort Holiday Pickleball Classic. The magic of the season shines at Horseshoe Bay Resort.

**Saturday, November 28, 2026**

**Live Oak Lawn & Palm Pavilion**

## Holiday Classic Pickleball Tournament

## Holiday Classic Pickleball Tournament

December 4-6, 2026

‘Tis the season to dink, drill the third shot drop, and enjoy holiday cheer.

Horseshoe Bay Resort’s 1st Annual Holiday Pickleball Classic offers a weekend of world-class instruction, competitive play, and holiday cheer on the shores of Lake LBJ. Whether you’re chasing a personal best or just want to swing a paddle alongside the pros, this is the marquee pickleball weekend of the holiday season. Make it a full holiday getaway. Revel in Big and Bright, A Hill Country Holiday. Train with two of the sport’s most decorated players: **Lina Padegimaite**, member of a top-ranked pro pickleball team and **Zane Navratil**, ranked in the top 4 in the world.
