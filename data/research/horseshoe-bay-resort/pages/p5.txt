# 21st Annual Wine, Dine & Jazz Festival   Friday, November 6 – Saturday, November 7, 2026

The 2026 Wine, Dine & Jazz Festival features celebrity chefs, jazz performances, and creative culinary experiences throughout the weekend. A seated five-course dinner kicks off the festivities, prepared and presented by three celebrity chefs along with Horseshoe Bay Resort’s culinary team led by Culinary Director, Chef Gilbert Moore, and Resort Executive Chef, Tommy Suddeth.

Saturday features celebrity chef cooking demonstrations, followed by the Wine, Dine and Jazz Stroll – an afternoon of wine tastings, exquisite food samples from top regional chefs and restaurants, and live jazz performances.

**FEATURED ARTISTS 2026**

Saxophonist Paul Taylor, guitarist/vocalist Steve Oliver, saxophonist Michael Paulo, keyboardist Gregg Karukas, vocalist B. Valentine, drummer Land Richards, bassist Darryl Williams, drummer Eric Valentine

## Amanda Rockman

## Amanda Rockman

Rockman Coffee + Bakeshop

Amanda is known for her flagship bakery, Rockman Coffee + Bakeshop, in East Austin, which embodies all her favorite things: Caffeine, Gluten, Butter, Sugar, Booze. She won TV Food Network’s “Top Chef: Just Desserts Season 2, Beat Bobby Flay” (yes, he was beaten). Her award-winning career includes: James Beard Semi-Finalist for Outstanding Pastry Chef 2015, Cacao Barry Chocolate Ambassador 2015-present, Jean Banchet Award Best Upcoming Pastry Chef 2013, and Jean Banchet Best Pastry Chef Award 2014. Amanda has had the pleasure of working for incredible chefs from a three-star Michelin restaurant to a five-star, five-diamond hotel. After the Culinary Institute of America in New York and work in San Francisco and Chicago, she returned to Austin to reestablish her Texas roots and open the South Congress Hotel with New Waterloo, which she called her work home for over nine years. In late 2024, Amanda Rockman opened her flagship bakery, Rockman Coffee + Bakeshop.

## Maribel Rivero

## Maribel Rivero

CookUnity Partner Chef

Chef Maribel Rivero is a James Beard Award–nominated (2019) Best Chef, Executive Chef, Chef-Owner, and Latin-focused culinary entrepreneur known for bold flavors, fire-driven technique, and disciplined execution. She made her mark in Austin as the first female chef to establish a contemporary Peruvian restaurant with Yuyo Peruano Restaurant, introducing modern Peruvian cuisine to the city and earning national recognition for her work. She is now a top-performing CookUnity Partner Chef, ranked #1 in Texas and Florida. Her cuisine draws from Mexico, Peru, Argentina, the Latin Caribbean, Spain, and the Southern United States, blending traditional techniques with modern recipe development and operational excellence.

## Kevin Taylor

## Kevin Taylor

Executive Chef, Bulevar Mexican Kitchen

Raised in family-owned Los Amigos restaurant, Chef Kevin Taylor developed his culinary foundation at a young age. In 1999, he attended the Culinary Institute of America and interned with renowned Chef Dean Fearing at The Mansion on Turtle Creek. In 2005, he joined the acclaimed Inn at Little Washington under Chef Patrick O’Connell. He later returned to his family’s restaurant, Los Amigos, serving six years as Executive Chef. In 2012, he joined the Starr Restaurant Organization, working at Barclay Prime before becoming Executive Chef of El Vez. His career eventually led him to Austin, where he partnered with Guy and Larry Restaurants to open ATX Cocina, Bulevar Mexican Kitchen, and Sugarwolf, continuing his passion for innovative hospitality and culinary excellence.
