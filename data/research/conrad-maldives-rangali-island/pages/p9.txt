## Taste authentic heritage infused with warm island charm

## Overview

On Rangali Finolhu’s tranquil shore, Ufaa, meaning “happiness” in Dhivehi, blends Chinese culinary artistry with Maldivian serenity. Sizzling woks harmonize with ocean breezes while fragrant teas steep as the sun melts into watercolor horizons. Bold ancestral flavors find new expression against a canvas of salt air and fading light.

A Teahouse in Paradise: Ambiance & Inspiration

Step into Ufaa and you’re transported—not to a formal dining hall, but to a vibrant, living teahouse. Laughter rings from a private room where friends play mahjong, while nearby, a family breaks into karaoke—urban China reimagined on a remote atoll. Every corner is crafted for connection and joy. As night falls, the experience drifts to the beach or treehouse, where you dine barefoot beneath the stars. A bubbling “reunion” hot pot simmers with reef fish and greens, the ocean whispering close by. Part Chinese courtyard, part Maldivian lounge, Ufaa invites you to slow down and savor—a celebration of its namesake: happiness.

Flavors of China, Bounty of Maldives

Each dish tells a regional story creating a perfect marriage of tradition and contemporary island flair. Highlights include handmade Cantonese dim sum and hand pulled noodles. For dinner the show stopper is Imperial Peking Duck roasted Beijing style and carved tableside alongside fresh local lobster. Do not miss the signature beachfront hot pot a communal feast under the stars. Here exotic flavors meet nostalgic comfort blending heritage with the island's serene spirit. Every bite evokes memory and belonging. As you step into the tropical night you carry the essence of ufaa happiness in a journey worth returning to.

An Unforgettable Culinary Journey

In a land famed for luxury and beauty, Ufaa shines as the Maldives’ first true Chinese restaurant—and a global dining destination. But its greatest rewards aren’t accolades—they’re moments: a guest’s wonder at tasting handcrafted dim sum on a distant atoll, a Chinese traveler comforted by familiar flavors with a Maldivian twist, or friends toasting across cultures, joyfully connected by food. Here, fine dining becomes a soulful experience. China’s rich culinary heritage meets the Maldives’ serene spirit, blending the exotic with the nostalgic. Every bite evokes memory, emotion, and belonging—whether it’s the joy of discovery or the warmth of home. As you step into the tropical night, you carry more than just flavor—you carry the essence of tradition, innovation, and true ufaa—happiness. This is Chinese fine dining at its most inspired, a journey worth returning to again and again.

### Michelin Master Series: Theodor Falser

With over a decade of Michelin-starred experience, **Theodor Falser** has led acclaimed kitchens across Europe, earning recognition for his refined approach to alpine cuisine and deep respect for nature. ### Michelin Master Series: Simon Hulstone

With a career spanning more than three decades, **Simon Hulstone** is one of the UK’s most celebrated Michelin-starred chefs. A Roux Scholar and winner of the Craft Guild of Chefs’ National Chef of the Year, he has represented Great Britain twice at the prestigious Bocuse d’Or and served as captain of both the British Culinary Olympics and English Culinary World Cup teams.

Since 2005, Simon has led **The Elephant Restaurant**, earning and maintaining its Michelin star for over 20 consecutive years while helping establish the English Riviera as a respected culinary destination.

His expertise extends beyond the restaurant, with appearances on _Saturday Kitchen_, _James Martin_, and _Great British Menu_. Today, he serves on the culinary council for Delta Air Lines, shaping premium inflight dining worldwide, while continuing to champion sustainable, locally sourced ingredients through his restaurant’s 90-acre farm and partnerships with regional producers.
