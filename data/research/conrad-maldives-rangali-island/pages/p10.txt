## Explore fine vintages in an intimate underground setting

## Overview

Descend two meters underground, a first for the Maldives, where 18,000 bottles and up to 800 labels slumber in perfect stillness. Here, a six course degustation unfolds, paired with seven hand selected wines and one champagne, each match orchestrated by our Chef de Cuisine and Head Sommelier.

Dinner Menu

Subterranean Sensory Journey

Leave the island warmth behind for the cool embrace of our underground vault. In this intimate atmosphere, dinner transforms into a shared theater of the senses. Surrounded by a library of vintages, the hushed acoustics and soft lighting amplify every nuance of flavor and conversation for a truly immersive evening.

Put your Taste to the Test

One of the evening’s highlights includes a blind tasting of two wines during the main course. Here you have the chance to present your opinions and showcase the wine knowledge acquired throughout the evening.

The Wine Cellar Collection

Conrad Maldives’ wine collection celebrates the old world wines and traditional growers, but also champions wines that are slowly making waves among wine enthusiasts. Each year, 30% of the wine list is changed, to reflect the latest fashions.

Top Wine Picks

- The first vintage of Krug Clos d’Ambonnay 1995, is the rarest wine stocked at the resort.
- The Methuselah of Cristal 1990 special edition, bottled in 2000 to celebrate the millennium, is the most expensive at USD $52,000.

### The Winemaker: François Labet

For more than five generations, **François Labet** has dedicated his life to the art of winemaking, leading **Château de La Tour Clos Vougeot**, one of Burgundy’s most celebrated Grand Cru estates. ### Michelin Master Series: Theodor Falser

With over a decade of Michelin-starred experience, **Theodor Falser** has led acclaimed kitchens across Europe, earning recognition for his refined approach to alpine cuisine and deep respect for nature. His philosophy celebrates seasonal ingredients, thoughtful craftsmanship, and the connection between food and place.

His work has been recognized by leading culinary guides and celebrated for its commitment to excellence and sustainability.

### Michelin Master Series: Simon Hulstone

With a career spanning more than three decades, **Simon Hulstone** is one of the UK’s most celebrated Michelin-starred chefs. A Roux Scholar and winner of the Craft Guild of Chefs’ National Chef of the Year, he has represented Great Britain twice at the prestigious Bocuse d’Or and served as captain of both the British Culinary Olympics and English Culinary World Cup teams.

Since 2005, Simon has led **The Elephant Restaurant**, earning and maintaining its Michelin star for over 20 consecutive years while helping establish the English Riviera as a respected culinary destination.

His expertise extends beyond the restaurant, with appearances on _Saturday Kitchen_, _James Martin_, and _Great British Menu_. Today, he serves on the culinary council for Delta Air Lines, shaping premium inflight dining worldwide, while continuing to champion sustainable, locally sourced ingredients through his restaurant’s 90-acre farm and partnerships with regional producers.
