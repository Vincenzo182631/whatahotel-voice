# Art of the seaside

## N La Plage

A garden by the sea.  A pause.  A journey. A mesmerising voyage across the azure, infused with the elegance and grandeur of Versailles. At the prow of the flagship opposite, luminous and sun-drenched, N La Plage offers five-star service on Nice’s legendary beach.

The horizon opens to a new perspective on the Mediterranean, and tranquillity triumphs. Here, every sense is awakened.

Beach open daily from May to September

### First, the view

In a serene, nearly monochrome ambience, at once minimal and impeccably modern, you can unwind amid a refined verdigris-and-white décor. The pergolas and latticework echo the French Grand Siècle gardens. From the comfort of a deckchair to the romantic solace of a wooden double bed, guests may experience the French Riviera in all her glamorous sophistication.

**Deckchairs available from 9am to 6pm**

### Then, the scents

Beginning in the lounge area, the luxuriant greenery forms a veritable botanical garden overlooking the sea. Floral and landscape designer Hervé Frezal, a Meilleur Ouvrier de France (Best Craftsman of France), has successfully created a setting of exquisite harmony. All around, native Mediterranean species, such as jasmine, fig, and palm, exude an exotic, elegant perfume.

**Lounge open every day from 9am to 10pm**

### Finally

Τhe flavours of Mediterranean cuisine reach their peak under the supervision of Kévin Pasquet and the eagle eye of chef Virginie Basselot. From the comfort of a deckchair, guests may indulge in a wide array of “Plage & Lounge” dishes, Rolls by Homer lobster rolls (voted best lobster roll in the world), and a selection of “Kids Boxes” beloved by young connoisseurs.

**Restaurant open every day**

**Lunch from 12pm to 3pm**

**Dinner from 7pm to 10pm**

## The Beach Restaurant

### A table overlooking the sea, inspired by Nice’s terroir.

Cast anchor. Surrender to a timeless escapade, and savour the irresistible experience of Le Negresco. Aesthetes will never tire of the spectacle. This represents a singular vision of freedom and pleasure, a true institution within the institution. For lovers of authentic cuisine and sun-drenched coastlines, the N La Plage Restaurant offers a vibrant culinary journey between the flavours of the Niçois terroir and the savoury breath of the sea.
