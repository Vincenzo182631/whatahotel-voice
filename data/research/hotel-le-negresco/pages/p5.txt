# The art of celebrating

## With its legendary history, illustrious guests, opulent interiors, and unmistakable silhouette gracing the coastline, Le Negresco stands as a landmark of Nice’s cultural heritage.

A witness to the past and a stage for the present, it welcomes the ceremonies that infuse life into its walls, steeped in history. Life and celebration form the very essence of the experience, transforming each event into a grand spectacle.

## Excellence in the service of ceremonies

Le Negresco stands as a custodian of the art of hospitality, continuing to uphold it with elegance and refinement. Beneath its gilded splendour, time seems to stand still. In this setting, where centuries of stories linger and works of art seek the gaze, sparkling chandeliers invite a quiet vertigo of contemplation. Here, exceptional events take place — private, cultural, or corporate.
