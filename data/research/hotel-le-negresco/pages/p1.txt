# Le Negresco: 5-star luxury hotel on the French Riviera

Since 1913, Le Negresco has graced the legendary Promenade des Anglais, embodying the very essence of refined French art of living, where history, heritage, and creativity converse.

More than a five-star hotel, Le Negresco is a destination with purpose, where art, tradition, and emotion are nurtured to craft singular experiences.

A living landmark, open to the future, where each stay becomes a rare and timeless moment on the French Riviera.

## The Iconic Elegance of   a 5-Star Hotel in Nice

### A Living Heritage on the Promenade des Anglais

In the heart of the Riviera, there are places one experiences more than describes. Le Negresco is one such place — a dazzling icon, free-spirited and independent, overlooking the endless blue of the Mediterranean. For more than a century, Le Negresco has celebrated art, heritage, and French excellence through a subtle harmony of styles and eras, from the splendour of Versailles to contemporary audacities. Throughout its rooms and suites, salons and dining venues, every space pays a refined homage to craftsmanship and history. At Le Negresco, guests experience the legend — and become part of it.

### Le Chantecler: MICHELIN-starred gastronomy in Nice

Within our 5 stars hotel, our MICHELIN-starred restaurant, Le Chantecler is the perfect expression of historical grandeur and modern creativity. Under the guidance of Chef Virginie Basselot, Meilleur Ouvrier de France, refined Mediterranean haute cuisine is served with sincerity and audacity.

### La Rotonde: Refinement and Conviviality

Bathed in southern light, La Rotonde captures the essence of the Riviera with effortless charm and relaxed sophistication. A contemporary brasserie and a beloved institution in Nice, it is a place for meeting, sharing, and celebrating – for the young at heart.

### Le 1913, Le Negresco’s historic Bar

Discreet and intimate, Le 1913, Le Negresco’s historic bar evokes the atmosphere of English clubs once frequented by dandies and elegant ladies. With its amber-toned panelling, artworks, and bespoke cocktails, every detail conjures an ambience that is enigmatic by day, and festive by night.

DISCOVER

### Le Versailles, Le Negresco's terrace Bar

Echoing the splendour and spirit of the Grand Siècle the champagne bar Le Versailles basks in sunlight that floods its terrace, while the lounge presides with quiet majesty over the Promenade des Anglais. On summer evenings, Le Versailles becomes a celebration of life’s pleasures: cocktails, sharing plates, and live music.

### The Beach Restaurant

From May to September, Restaurant N La Plage celebrates the sun-drenched flavours of the Riviera. Honest, radiant cuisine highlights the treasures of the sea and the terroir of Nice, served with panoramic views over the Mediterranean. A setting where elegance meets simplicity, and every detail enchants.
