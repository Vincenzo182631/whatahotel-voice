## Frequently Asked Questions

##### Where is Stein Eriksen Residences located within Deer Valley Resort?

Stein Eriksen Residences are located slopeside at Deer Valley Resort in Park City, Utah, offering direct mountain access in a private residential setting. The location combines alpine seclusion with convenient access to Deer Valley skiing and Park City amenities.

##### Do Stein Eriksen Residences offer ski-in / ski-out access?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Residences offer ski-in / ski-out access at Deer Valley Resort. Owners and guests enjoy seamless slope access directly from the residences during the winter season.

##### What type of experiences does Stein Eriksen Residences offer?

Stein Eriksen Residences offer a luxury residential experience designed for privacy, space, and refined mountain living. The experience blends the comforts of a private residence with the services and amenities associated with the Stein Eriksen brand.

##### How are Stein Eriksen Residences different from Stein Eriksen Lodge or Chateaux Deer Valley?

Aesthetically, Stein Eriksen Residences are the most modern property of the three. While each property remains focused on world-class service, and luxury amenities, they are all unique. Stein Residences feels modern and homey, a slight departure from the feel of a traditional ski lodge.

##### What types of residences are available at Stein Eriksen Residences?

Stein Eriksen Residences feature luxury condominiums and penthouse-style residences with spacious layouts, as well as private homes, ideal for large groups or extra privacy. Each residence is thoughtfully designed to reflect refined mountain living and modern comfort.
