## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What dining options are available for at Stein Eriksen Residences?

On-site, guests at Stein Eriksen Residences can enjoy 7880 Club, a picturesque new American restaurant serving seasonal, chef-driven menus. Guests also enjoy exclusive access to the restaurants at Stein Eriksen Lodge and Chateaux Deer Valley. This allows for a variety of elevated dining experiences.

##### Are private chef services offered at Stein Eriksen Residences?

Yes, private chef services are available by request for residents. This service allows for fully customized meals prepared in the residence, ideal for gatherings, celebrations, or extended stays.

##### Are there family-friendly dining options?

Yes, dining options available to residents accommodate families of all ages. Residents can choose between preparing meals in their residence, in-residence dining services, or dining at 7880 Club.

##### Can dietary preferences or restrictions be accommodated?

Yes, 7880 Club can accommodate a wide range of dietary preferences and restrictions. Residents are encouraged to communicate specific needs in advance to ensure a tailored experience.

##### Are dining reservations recommended?

Yes, reservations are recommended at Stein Eriksen Lodge restaurants, especially during high season, holidays, or special events. In-residence dining can usually be arranged with advance notice.
