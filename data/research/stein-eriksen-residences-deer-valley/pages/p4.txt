## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What types of specials are available at Stein Eriksen Residences?

Stein Eriksen Residences offers seasonal specials designed to enhance extended stays and residential mountain living. These may include access to select amenities, seasonal packages, or curated experiences as well as resort credit offers.

##### Are there seasonal specials for winter and summer stays?

Yes, Stein Eriksen Residences offers seasonal specials that align with winter skiing and summer alpine activities. These experiences are designed to maximize the value of the mountain lifestyle for owners and guests.

##### Are there exclusive specials for returning guests?

Stein Eriksen Residences values long-term relationships and may offer select opportunities to returning guests or owners. Availability and eligibility vary and are communicated directly by the residences team.

##### How can guests learn about current specials at Stein Eriksen Residences?

Current specials are best explored by contacting Stein Eriksen Residences directly or visiting the specials page of the website. Availability may be limited, and early inquiry is encouraged.

##### Do specials change throughout the year?

Yes, seasonal specials change based on peak periods, availability, and mountain conditions. Guests are encouraged to inquire ahead of time to plan their preferred stay.
