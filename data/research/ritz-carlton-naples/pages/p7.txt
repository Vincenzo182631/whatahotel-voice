Holiday Events at The Ritz-Carlton, Naples
Where Festive Moments Become Cherished Memories.
This holiday season, The Ritz-Carlton, Naples invites families and friends to indulge in an enchanting celebration of holiday splendor, where timeless traditions meet thoughtfully curated festive experiences. From the whimsical Teddy Bear Tea to the Rare New Years Eve Affair, and the favorite Gingerbread House Viewing, each moment is thoughtfully designed to inspire joy, connection, and timeless holiday memories for generations to come.
Featured Celebrations
Step into the joy of the season at The Ritz-Carlton, Naples, where holiday magic sparkles as brightly as the Gulf at sunset. From elegantly adorned suites to festive décor and twinkling lights, every detail is designed to capture the spirit of the holidays and create cherished memories for years to come.

Festivities
A Season of Wonders
Thanksgiving Grand Buffet
Gather family and friends for a bountiful Thanksgiving feast under the direction of Executive Chef Efe Salar. Enjoy traditional cuisine, a uniquely curated children's buffet, festive drinks, and a selection of sweet treats.
Reserve Now
Teddy Bear Tea
Children of all ages are invited to this whimsical afternoon tradition of delicious treats. The little ones will delight in the joy of departing with their new keepsake bearfriend from our magical winter escape.
Reserve Now
Breakfast with Santa
Celebrate the magic of the season with a festive morning filled with laughter, music, and a beautifully curated holiday buffet. Santa welcomes families for a memorable visit, creating treasured moments to cherish for years to come.
Reserve Now
Life-Size Gingerbread House Display
Step into a sugary wonderland and visit our adored life-size Gingerbread House. This photo-ready display looks good enough to eat, thanks to our talented pastry team under the mentorship of Executive Pastry Chef, Lerome Campbell.
Reserve Now
New Year’s Eve: Signature Soirée
Ring in the New Year at The Ritz-Carlton, Naples with a Signature Evening celebrating togetherness and new beginnings. Indulge in an exquisite five-course menu, enjoy unlimited premium beverages, and dance the night away to the lively sounds of the 11-piece Manhattan Band. As midnight arrives, raise a glass of Rare Champagne and toast to an unforgettable year ahead. This event is 18+*
