Activities and experiences

### Signature experiences

Hang ten with pro surfers, explore the rich cultural history of Oahu, or just relax on the beach. A stay at OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort connects you with everything that makes Waikiki epic.

Learn to surf

No trip to Hawaii is complete without a surf lesson from the pros! The famed Faith Surf School is located directly in front of the hotel, on Waikiki Beach. Learn how to surf, stand-up paddleboard and ride a wave in an outrigger canoe on Oahu’s most scenic beach.

Learn more

Duke's on Sundays

Experience the local vibe of Duke's Waikiki. This iconic restaurant and beach bar is the place to eat, drink, dance and sing on Waikiki Beach. On Sundays, enjoy the sounds of local music legend Henry Kapono from 4 - 6 pm right on the beachfront at the Barefoot Bar.

Learn more

Discover the home of surf culture

Experience Waikiki’s surf culture at this fun ongoing program called **Surfers in Residences** only at the OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort. Join us as legendary surfers stay with us and share their stories and craft with guests. Meet Hawaii's surf icons and hang ten for a "sesh" on the waves.

Learn more

Cultural Activities

Here at OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort, we take pride in sharing the culture of the islands – and our unique Waikiki Beach spirit. Take part in everything from hula lessons to lei making for a glimpse into the Hawaiian Islands’ rich history right in the resort.

How to get to the OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort

## How do I get to OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort from the airport?

From airport arrival terminal exit toward WAIKIKI/HONOLULU.

Go up the ramp and stay to RIGHT going east toward WAIKIKI/HONOLULU for 1 mile/1.61 km to H1 Freeway.

Merge onto H1 east. Get into LEFT lane and continue on H1 east for 0.8 miles/1.29 km going toward HONOLULU.

Freeway will begin to veer to the LEFT. Take exit 23 to PUNAHOU ST and get into RIGHT lane, turning RIGHT onto PUNAHOU ST.

NOTE: Kapiolani Women and Children's Hospital STRAIGHT ahead.

Go south on PUNAHOU ST for one block (0.07 miles/0.11 km).

Turn RIGHT onto SOUTH BERETANIA ST and travel for one block (0.06 miles/0.09 km).

Get into LEFT lane and make LEFT at first traffic signal onto KALAKAUA AVE. Continue on KALAKAUA AVE.

Outrigger Waikiki Beach Resort is on the RIGHT.

## How close is the beach to the OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort?

OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort is a beachfront property. You are just steps away from the beach and if you go to the pool area, you will see the beach from there.

If guests stop by the beachfront outside OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort, they can stop by the Aqualani Beach and Ocean Recreation desk to book surfing, paddle boarding and more.

## How far in advance should I leave for the airport from the property to safely make it through security?

Two hours if it's a domestic flight, Three hours if it’s international. The drive to the airport can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 1-hour as it depends on the time of day. If you need additional guidance, please feel free to stop by the front desk or bell desk when you are here.

## Is there an airport shuttle?

There are various shuttle services that offer door-to-door services between the airport terminals and the resort

## How far is Honolulu Airport from OUTRIGGER Waikiki Beach Resort?

If you would like to book a shuttle from the airport, you can make advanced reservations through Speedishuttle. You can book a one-way or round-trip ticket for a fee.

https://www.speedishuttle.com/group-reservations?id=OUT70043

If you request an Uber/Lyft from the airport, it can take you 25 minutes to an hour depending on the time of your arrival/departure.
