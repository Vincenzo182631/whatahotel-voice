## A HISTORIC _palace_ at the HEART of TANGIER

Step into a world where opulence meets history at the Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier, a stunning embodiment of Moroccan grandeur and architectural splendor. Nestled amidst the fragrant eucalyptus forest, this palace transports you to the golden era of 1926, when it was first conceived as a regal sanctuary for the esteemed advisor Ahmad Tazi.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier located?

Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier is located in the heart of Tangier, Morocco, in the luxurious area of Jbel Lekbir, surrounded by lush eucalyptus forests and offering panoramic views of the city.

Does Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier have a spa?

Yes, Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier features a 2,500 sqm Fairmont Spa, offering a range of luxurious treatments and wellness services in a serene and elegant setting.

Does Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier have a swimming pool?

Yes, Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier features a main swimming pool as well as a vitality pool within the Fairmont Spa, providing a perfect setting for relaxation and rejuvenation.

Does Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier offer any business services?

Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier offers comprehensive business services, including a 300 sqm ballroom, a boardroom, a library, and a versatile U-style meeting room. Each space can be customized to suit various business needs and events in the Moroccan city.

Does Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier have a gym/fitness center?

Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier has a well-equipped gym and fitness center, offering state-of-the-art exercise equipment and facilities to help you maintain your fitness routine during your stay.

How can I get to Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier from the nearest airport?

To reach Fairmont Tazi Palace Tangier from the nearest airport, Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport, it’s a convenient 20-minute drive. The hotel can assist with transportation arrangements to ensure a smooth and comfortable journey.
