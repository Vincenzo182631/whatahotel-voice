Elevate Your Getaway With Club Access
Discover our exclusive Lake Tahoe Club Lounge
The Ritz-Carlton Club redefines the luxury hotel experience, combining exclusivity and comfort with signature personalized service and attention to detail The Ritz-Carlton is known for. Often referred to as a “hotel within a hotel,” Club enhances your entire stay by providing a dedicated Concierge, unique in-room amenities, and exclusive experiences in a private, luxury Lake Tahoe hotel Club Lounge that immerses you in the best of the destination.
Upgrade Your Stay to Club
Treat yourself to a memorable stay by booking a room or suite with Lake Tahoe Club Lounge access*, which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. *Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to Club.
Explore Our Club Level Guest Rooms and Suites
Exclusive Check-In for Club Guests
Lake Tahoe Club Lounge Access
Begin your stay with us in our exclusive, luxury hotel in Lake Tahoe with Club Lounge access, where you’ll be greeted by our Club ambassador and enjoy a dedicated check-in, exclusively for our Club guests. For the rest of your stay, consider the Club Lounge your private sanctuary.

In Good Taste
Daily Culinary Presentations
Five times each day, our luxury Lake Tahoe hotel Club Lounge offers complimentary food and beverage presentations. The daily offerings - from the ingredients used in the continental breakfasts to evening hors d’oeuvres - tell the destination’s story through locally sourced ingredients, iconic dishes and signature drinks. Throughout the day and evening enjoy handcrafted cocktails, artisanal cheeses, and gourmet bites while mingling with fellow discerning travelers.
