Make Room for Holiday Magic
This season, experience a Lake Tahoe winter wonderland during the ultimate holiday immersion of festive season. Indulge in unique celebratory experiences, ski the Sierra Nevada slopes, and gather with friends and family.
A Luxury Winter Getaway in Lake Tahoe
Discover the ultimate holiday escape at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe. Enjoy ski-in, ski-out access, après-ski cocktails by the fire, fine mountain dining, scenic ski hikes, and indulgent winter pampering amid the Sierra Nevada’s alpine beauty.

Festive Dining Experiences
Thanksgiving at Manzanita
Gather with family and friends for a festive prix fixe dinner celebrating the flavors of the season. Enjoy thoughtfully curated selections showcasing the finest regional ingredients, complemented by sommelier-curated wine pairings by Lesley Nevis, seasonal mulled cider, and signature cocktails, all set against the warmth and beauty of Tahoe in autumn. Thursday, November 26 | 04:00PM–09:00PM
Dine Amid the Pines: Thanksgiving Celebration
Celebrate Thanksgiving with a festive buffet featuring fresh oysters, ceviche, roasted turkey, prime rib, honey and sage-glazed salmon, and seasonal accompaniments. Live music, holiday desserts, and a curated selection of cocktails, wines, and spirits complete the evening. Thursday, November 26 | 3:30PM–8:45PM
A Winter's Feast: Christmas Eve at Manzanita
Celebrate the season with a festive prix fixe dinner inspired by winter's finest ingredients. Gather with family and friends for an evening of exceptional dining, thoughtfully crafted seasonal cocktails, elegant holiday music, and the warmth of a memorable Christmas Eve celebration. Thursday, December 24 | 4:00PM–9:00PM
A Grand Christmas Dinner
Revel in the spirit of Christmas as the Grand Ballroom transforms into a candlelit winter retreat. Enjoy a festive buffet featuring live seared scallops, artisan cheese fondue, herb-crusted leg of lamb, Certified Angus Prime Rib, glazed Niman Ranch ham, salt-crusted salmon, a live-action ice cream station, and decadent holiday desserts. Friday, December 25 | 4:00PM–9:00PM
Midnight Reverie: New Year's Eve at Manzanita
Ring in the New Year with an elegant alpine celebration featuring a festive prix fixe menu inspired by the season's finest ingredients. Gather with family and friends for an evening of exceptional dining, celebratory cocktails, and a vibrant atmosphere, while younger guests enjoy a dedicated kids' buffet. Thursday, December 31 | 4:30PM–9:30PM
The New Year's Gala Buffet
Indulge in an evening of festive flavors and refined alpine dining as you welcome the New Year. This celebratory buffet features seasonal favorites and elevated culinary experiences, including an oyster and caviar station, prime tomahawk carving station, heritage porchetta, raclette cheese station, and an exquisite pastry display. Thursday, December 31 | 6:15PM-10:30PM
New Year's Eve Countdown Party
Ring in 2027 in one of Tahoe's most striking settings. Set beneath The Living Room's soaring three-story windows with mountain views over the Sierra Nevada. DJ EyeQue will keep the energy high as the countdown builds, with handcrafted cocktails, wine, champagne, and seasonal light bites available for purchase throughout the evening. Thursday, December 31 | 9:30PM–1:00AM | The Living Room
Indulge in the Spirit of The Season
The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Lake Tahoe
Enveloped by towering pines and adorned in rich wood, The Ritz-Carlton Spa, Lake Tahoe offers a place of renewal and connection amid the resort’s awe-inspiring mountain grounds. Signature experiences reflect the surroundings from aromatherapy to decompressing rituals after a day of skiing. The sprawling resort spa features 17 treatment rooms, relaxation areas warmed by fireplaces, a dry-heat lodge, and a eucalyptus steam room. The adult only heated outdoor lap pool looks out over the slopes; a beautiful winter scene. All spa access and use of the facilities including hot tub, steam room, sauna, cold rain-shower plunge, and lap pool are exclusively for spa guests receiving treatment.

Fireside Bliss
Indulge in the perfect blend of luxury and nostalgia as you connect and gather fireside, featuring dedicated service, culinary led learnings, and mountain views. Roast marshmallows, craft gourmet s’mores, savor fondue, and enjoy the crisp mountain air as you learn what it takes to craft culinary après classics. Take the recipe with you to bring the spirit of après to your home. Whether for family fun or a romantic escape, this exclusive experience offers a warm, intimate setting amidst the stunning Sierra Nevada. Full and half-day rentals are available.
