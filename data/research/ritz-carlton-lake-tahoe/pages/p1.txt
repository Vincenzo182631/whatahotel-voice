Welcome to The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe
Find unparalleled slopeside luxury at our Lake Tahoe resort
Experience The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe, and discover the pristine beauty of the Sierra Nevada mountain range. From its crystal-clear lake to its legendary skiing, hiking, and golfing near Lake Tahoe, this luxury resort provides endless possibilities for travelers of all ages and interests. Featuring the only luxury ski-in, ski-out access in Lake Tahoe with mountain concierge services, a stunning slopeside spa, exceptional dining, and impeccable service, The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe is the region’s premier mountain retreat with exclusive access to a private Lake Club on the shores of Lake Tahoe.

Frequently Asked Questions
What are the check-in and check-out times at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe?
The check-in time at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe is 5:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe allow pets?
Yes, pets are welcome at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 80.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $200.00 per stay applies.
What are the parking options at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe?
On-site valet parking is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe. The daily valet fee is $65.00 per day.
View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe have in-room Wi-Fi?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe booking.
What is the closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe?
The closest airport near The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe is Reno-Tahoe International Airport (RNO). RNO is located approximately 44.0 Miles from the hotel.
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe have electric vehicle charging stations?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
Does The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe have a Club Lounge?
Yes, The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe offers an exclusive Ritz-Carlton Club Lounge experience for guests staying in Club-level rooms and suites. Enhance your stay with personalized service, curated culinary presentations, and a tranquil space designed for relaxation and refinement. Reserve a Club Access accommodation to enjoy exclusive amenities and services*.
*Club access requires an upgraded booking at the Club. Marriott Bonvoy Elite Status does not provide complimentary access to the Club.
