Fine Dining & Comfort Cuisine
Dining
Discover the diverse dining experiences at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe, where each dining venue offers a unique taste sensation. Step into the sophisticated ambiance of Manzanita, our signature restaurant in Truckee, where contemporary mountain cuisine takes center stage. Experience the laid-back atmosphere of Backyard Bar & BBQ which is a perfect setting to enjoy traditional barbecue or relax and unwind in the cozy elegance of Living Room Lodge, the ideal spot to enjoy a handcrafted cocktail or a light bite. Indulge in diverse flavors at our restaurants near Lake Tahoe.
Mother’s Day Brunch at Manzanita
Celebrate Mother’s Day with a refined brunch at Manzanita, featuring spring‑inspired cuisine, a fresh raw bar, carving stations, signature cocktails, and live music set against the beauty of Lake Tahoe.

Dining Experiences
Private Dining Room
For an intimate dining experience, reserve the Private Dining Room for up to 25 guests at our Lake Tahoe restaurant. Enjoy this experience for a rehearsal dinner, a corporate dinner with your team, or special time with your loved ones. Prices vary with a room rental fee included and food & beverage minimum required. Contact our Restaurant Group Coordinator at 530.562.3051.
Guest Amenities
Plan your stay or celebrate a friend from afar with a special amenity set up in the guest room upon arrival. Commemorate an anniversary, birthday or special moment with one our selection of hand-crafted desserts, bottled wines and more. Reserve amenities up to 3 months in advance.

Manzanita
Living Room Lodge
Montesa, Cantina Moderna

Does The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe offer room service?
Yes, room service is available at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe.
What cuisine is offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe?
The cuisines offered at The Ritz-Carlton, Lake Tahoe are:
Mexican at Montesa, Cantina Moderna
American at Topgolf Swing Suites, Alpine Exchange
California at The Backyard
International at Manzanita, Living Room Lodge
