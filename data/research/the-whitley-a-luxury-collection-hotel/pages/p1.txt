# Welcome to The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead

## Chic Southern style, timeless Buckhead Atlanta hotel charm

Blending cosmopolitan style with timeless grace, The Whitley, a Marriott Luxury Collection Hotel, is a luxury hotel in Buckhead, Atlanta, ideally located in the vibrant Buckhead community. Steps from world-class...See More shopping, dining, and nightlife, our Buckhead, GA, hotel offers a destination experience that intertwines heritage and discovery. With 31 versatile event spaces totaling over 40,000 square feet, including the 5,000-square-foot Legacy Ballroom with an outdoor terrace, we provide an exceptional setting for meetings and celebrations. Our 507 rooms, including 56 suites, feature skyline views and refined comforts. As a pet-friendly hotel in Buckhead, GA, we welcome every guest with authentic Southern hospitality and timeless sophistication.See Less

We are pleased to be a pet-friendly hotel. Pets weighing less than 50 pounds are allowed in the hotel and in guest rooms and suites. There is a $150 cleaning fee for up to two pets. We hope you and your furry companion enjoy your stay at The Whitley.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead?

The check-in time at The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead allow pets?

Yes, pets are welcome at The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead. Up to 2 pets are allowed per room. Each pet may weigh up to 50.0 lbs. A non-refundable pet fee of $150.00 per stay applies.

What are the parking options at The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead?

Off-site valet parking is available at The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead. The daily valet fee is $62.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead booking.

What is the closest airport near The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead?

The closest airport near The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead is Dekalb-Peachtree Airport (PDK). PDK is located approximately 3.0 Miles from the hotel.

Does The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead have an airport shuttle to Dekalb-Peachtree Airport (PDK)?

No, The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead does not have an airport shuttle to Dekalb-Peachtree Airport.

Does The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead have a The Whitley Club lounge?

Yes, The Whitley, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Atlanta Buckhead has The Whitley Club lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with The Whitley Club lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
