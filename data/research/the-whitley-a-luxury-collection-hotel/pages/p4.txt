### Signature Treatments & Local Inspiration

At The Whitley, your health and wellness are essential to us. So essential, we’ve devoted an entire floor to their pursuit.

The Spa at Whitley unfolds over a lounge, boutique, salt room and exclusive treatment rooms – the beating heart of our spa, where indulgences include facials and body scrubs, salon services, manicures and pedicures, among others.

Restore your inner balance. Reveal your smoothest skin. Nurture your mind. Indulge in deep-tissue therapies. And recharge your soul. Treat yourself to The Spa at Whitley.

Guidelines & Policies

PRE-TREATMENT ARRIVAL: Please arrive 30 minutes prior to your appointment so that your spa treatment may start on time. Late arrivals may not receive full-service time, thus decreasing its effectiveness and your enjoyment. Your treatment will end on time, so the next guest is not delayed, and the full value of your treatment will be charged. As part of your spa experience, we encourage you to plan extra time to relax in our lounge, indoor saline pool, and whirlpool or exercise in our state-of-the-art Fitness Center.

GRATUITY: For your convenience, a 20% service charge will be added to all spa treatment prices upon check-out. Additional gratuity may be added at your discretion.

CANCELLATION POLICY: Please provide at least a 24-hour notice if you need to cancel or shorten your scheduled spa service to avoid being charged. A credit card will be required to reserve appointments. We regret that a lack of notification will result in the full billing of your scheduled services. Any appointments rescheduled within the twenty-four hour window will incur a $25 fee

### Local Attractions

Admire the variety of species at the expansive Georgia Aquarium and the Atlanta Botanical Garden. Tour the High Museum of Art, the Museum of Design Atlanta, and the Center for Civil and Human Rights.

### Music Is In The Air

Attend a performance at the Alliance Theater or the Atlanta Symphony Orchestra.

### History Surrounds

The World of Coca-Cola, the Margaret Mitchell House, and the Jimmy Carter Presidential Library and Museum offer unique perspectives on the history of Georgia and its people.
