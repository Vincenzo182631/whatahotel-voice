### The Park Tower Knightsbridge History

Designed by renowned British architect Richard Seifert and unveiled in 1973, The Park Tower Knightsbridge introduced a distinctive architectural presence to one of London's most prestigious neighbourhoods. Its iconic circular form and expansive windows were designed to showcase the city offering views across Hyde Park, Knightsbridge and London's celebrated landmarks. Perfectly positioned in the heart of Knightsbridge, surrounded by world-class shopping, dining and cultural attractions, the hotel combines a unique perspective on the capital with one of its most sought-after addresses.
