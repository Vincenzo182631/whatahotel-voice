### Local Area & Activity Highlights

## Knightsbridge Attractions

Take a stroll through the breath-taking Hyde Park; covering 350 acres and home to a number of famous landmarks including the Serpentine Lake, and the Diana, Princess of Wales Memorial Fountain. Discover Kensington Palace, one of the captivating residences of the British Royal Family which is steeped in history and intrigue. Complete the visit with refreshments at The Orangery located within the picturesque Kensington Gardens. ptk.concierge@luxurycollection.com

## Shopping

Shop at upscale boutiques or the famed Harvey Nichols department store for innovative European designers or explore in the halls of Harrods with its panoply of luxury goods; both within a few minutes walking distance from The Park Tower Knightsbridge.

Details

Go for a stroll towards Sloane Square and discover luxury in the truest sense. From designer handbags, couture fashion and accessories Sloane Street offers an intimate shopping atmosphere featuring some of the world's most revered brands

Stores are generally open from 10am – 7pm.

## Local Dining

Within Knightsbridge, you will find an Italian fine dining restaurant, Zafferano, and Amaya, for contemporary Indian cuisine with a hint of Orient featuring an open grill kitchen. For a quintessentially-British pub experience with oak floors and an outdoor terrace, visit The Thomas Cubitt on Elizabeth Street. For impressive collection of cupcakes created by a team of expert bakers, our concierge proposes a trip to The Hummingbird Bakery in South Kensington.

## Local Attractions

Discover a range of fascinating exhibitions at the Victoria & Albert Museum or explore contemporary art at the Saatchi Gallery on King's Road; both within walking distance of our hotel in Knightsbridge. Notable for its distinguished address, Number One London, Apsley House was once home to the Duke of Wellington. Today it boasts a collection of exceptional artworks by Old Masters, alongside exquisite silver and porcelain pieces. Slightly further afield, explore the British Museum and visit the renowned mummy exhibit and the legendary Rosetta Stone. A ride on the London Eye – the largest Ferris wheel in the world – boasts spectacular views of the London skyline, while the Tower of London dazzles with centuries of history and its cherished display of the Crown Jewels.
