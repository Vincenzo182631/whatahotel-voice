Housed in a soaring round tower overlooking Harrods department store, stay here for the panoramic views and attentive service. Light-filled rooms are decked out with sleek marble bathrooms—look out for the Molton Brown products—and decorated in warm tones of mahogany, red, yellow, and cream. For dinner, eat at the award winning seafood restaurant One-O-One and order rich, decadent dishes like yellowfin tuna steak with foie gras, or dover sole topped with oyster Béarnaise sauce. But the best part is the location—Hyde Park, the Royal Albert Hall, and some of the city’s grandest museums are just a brisk walk away.
