### The Knightsbridge Lounge

Located in the heart of the hotel, the Knightsbridge Lounge is an idyllic tea parlour that entertains its guests in resplendent style. Graced by a spectacular 18-foot teardrop glass chandelier and a canopy of magnolia blossoms, the enchanting circular room is decorated with a trompe l’oeil wall motif of draped silk fabric and a stellar collection of original artwork. Gilded chairs grouped around elegant...See More marble-topped tables invite guests to chat and linger over our flavoursome English Afternoon Tea.See Less

### Experience Afternoon Tea

Step into a world of pure luxury and indulgence at the Knightsbridge Lounge. Inspired by haute couture salons, our stylish lounge will leave you feeling pampered and enchanted. Make a date to catch up with friends or explore the city, our lounge is the perfect spot to meet. With our proximity to Knightsbridge and Harrods, as well as the world-renowned Royal palaces, our lounge offers both convenience and charm. Whether you’re taking a break from shopping or sightseeing, indulge in our delectable selection of treats. This quintessentially British experience with impeccable royal connections.

## The Terrace

The intimate terrace area, set in the heart of Knightsbridge, provides the perfect sanctuary for al fresco social gatherings, where friends, family, and business professionals can share a drink, converse, and indulge in London’s ‘high-life’ scene.

## Enjoy our Golden Scottish Highland Wagyu Beef Burger

There is nothing better than the elegant simplicity of a well made burger which is currently being served in both the Hyde Bar and In Room Dining. Using grass fed Scottish Wagyu beef, this provides a unique flavoursome taste to our patty, and is balanced with a perfectly placed combination of lettuce, tomato, onion and relish giving you that delightful combination of textures and sweet, salty, crunchy mix. The finest flavours, using the highest quality ingredients.
