## Dining

Nestled in the heart of Kuala Lumpur, our seven restaurants and bars invite guests to embark on a culinary journey. Experience the intimate sushi counter at Taka, indulge in the exclusive Ozaki Beef at USHI, and savor tempura at Tenshin. The Brasserie, our Michelin Guide-selected fine dining restaurant in Kuala Lumpur city centre, seamlessly fuses Western techniques with local treasures. Astor Bar and Decanter boasts a vast selection of over 650 wines, while Crystal offers panoramic city views and unforgettable cocktails. Immerse yourself in the elegance of The Drawing Room, featuring Botero's iconic horse sculpture, and enjoy the refined rituals of St. Regis Afternoon Tea.

### Finest Dining Experiences

Celebrating the diverse culinary heritage of Malaysia and beyond, The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur continually pushes boundaries to create unique dining experiences for every festivity.

### Dining Offers

From our delectable Sunday brunch to our thoughtfully crafted cocktails program and signature afternoon tea in Kuala Lumpur, the hotel presents a captivating array of culinary experiences tailored to meet every palate and occasion.

### Afternoon Tea

Immerse yourself in the timeless tradition of St. Regis as you treat yourself to the signature St. Regis Afternoon Tea in Kuala Lumpur. Delight in a selection of exquisite pastries, accompanied by the remarkable horse sculpture by notable Colombian artist, Fernando Botero.

### The Brasserie Set Lunch

Delight in a remarkable dining affair at our Michelin-selected fine dining restaurant, The Brasserie, perfect for an exquisite lunch experience in Kuala Lumpur​ city centre. With menus refreshed every month, embark on a culinary journey filled with dynamic flavors and tantalizing surprises.

### The M Room

Experience The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur's Macallan M Room, Malaysia's premier whisky bar. Immerse yourself in a sensory journey celebrating St. Regis's legacy alongside The Macallan. Explore Asia's sole complete and unrivaled set, featuring the rare and oldest Macallan Red Collection.

### The Art of Drinking

Embark on a captivating cocktail journey through New York's five historical boroughs—Lower Manhattan, Midtown, Upper Manhattan, the East Side, and the West Side. Experience the art of drinking as you discover the distinct flavors and stories that define each locale.

### Crystal Rooftop Bar

Indulge in the ultimate urban escape at Crystal, our rooftop bar boasting stunning views of the city skyline. Enjoy Malaysian reimagined cuisine and happy hour drinks in the vibrant atmosphere of this downtown Kuala Lumpur bar, perfect for family and friends, all while lounging by the poolside.

### Asam Boi Mary

Experience our rendition of the classic Bloody Mary, uniquely featuring a Malaysian twist with Calamansi Juice, Paprika, Asam Boi, Chilli Padi, and a hint of Sarawak pepper. This blend offers a harmonious and flavorful experience that captures the essence of Malaysia's culinary diversity.
