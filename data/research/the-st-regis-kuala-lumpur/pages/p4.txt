## St. Regis Kids Club

Welcome to the latest enchantment at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur. Crafted with meticulous care and design, it unveils a world of engaging activities and personalized experiences for our younger guests and their families, ensuring indelible moments for both adults and little ones. Whether it's a family vacation, a special celebration, or simply a weekend getaway, The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur promises to elevate your experience to new heights, leaving you with cherished memories that will last a lifetime.

## St. Regis Rituals

Experience the timeless traditions of The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur, where every moment is a celebration of luxury. Indulge in our renowned Afternoon Tea, savoring exquisite flavors amidst refined elegance. As the day fades into evening, immerse yourself in the enchanting Violet Hour, as the sky turns a mesmerizing shade of violet, mirroring the elegance of our champagne sabering ceremony. At our luxury hotel near Chinatown in Kuala Lumpur, every ritual is an unforgettable experience.

## Picnic at Perdana Botanical Garden

Experience the tranquility of Perdana Botanical Garden with a bespoke picnic brought to you by our attentive butlers. Surround yourself with nature's embrace as you enjoy a thoughtfully crafted feast prepared by our chefs. Embrace precious moments with your loved ones, savoring both the food and the company in this serene setting.

## The Art Walk

At The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur, we cherish our exceptional art collection, a testament to our unwavering commitment to elegance and sophistication. From the grandeur of Fernando Botero's monumental horse sculpture to Mark Evans' dynamic leather murals portraying a powerful polo charge, each piece exudes timeless beauty. Our collection also includes Barnaby Hosking's intricate Birds and Butterflies installation, inspired by the historic Perdana Botanical Gardens.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio and Weight-Training Equipment, Cardiovascular Equipment, Free Weights, Strength Equipment, and Treadmill(s), making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur have a pool on-site?

Yes, The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur have an on-site spa?

Yes, Iridium Spa is located on-site at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur?

Perdana Botanical Garden, Taman Eko-Rimba Kuala Lumpur \| KL Forest Eco Park, The National Museum of Malaysia, Central Market, Merdeka Square and more are nearby The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur.
