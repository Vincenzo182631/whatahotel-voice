### Explore The Best Address

Strategically positioned amidst Kuala Lumpur's iconic landmarks, The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur offers convenient access to nearby attractions, providing a plethora of experiences for every discerning guest. Immerse yourself in the beauty and rich culture of Malaysia during your stay.

Perdana Botanical Gardens

Explore the expansive lake garden, one of the city's largest parks, and immerse yourself in the vibrant collection of tropical plants. Experience the ambiance of a lush tropical rainforest oasis, offering a nature-filled escape in the heart of the bustling city.

The National Museum of Malaysia

The National Museum of Malaysia stands as a beacon of the nation's rich cultural legacy. With its impressive collection of historical artifacts and interactive exhibits, the museum offers a captivating journey through Malaysia's past.

Petronas Twin Tower

Standing tall as the world's tallest twin structures, experience interactive science exhibits, visit the world-class aquarium, and unwind in the lush greenery of the 50-acre KLCC Park—all within the embrace of these iconic towers.

Chinatown Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur’s Chinatown is a vibrant district rich in Chinese-Malaysian culture. Explore Kasturi Walk, with its colorful shops, temples, and markets like Petaling Street and Central Market. Savor local street food like nasi lemak, pisang goreng, and teh tarik, and immerse yourself in the area’s rich history.

Little India Brickfields

Explore Malaysia’s rich Indian heritage in the vibrant Little India of Brickfields. Wander through its colorful temples, bustling markets, and aromatic spice stalls, immersing yourself in the lively culture and traditions of this historic neighborhood.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur?

The check-in time at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur.

What are the parking options at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur?

On-site parking is available at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur. Self parking is available at The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur, with rates of RM8.00 per hour, RM80.00 per day, and RM0.00 perstay. Valet parking is available for RM60.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur booking.

What is the closest airport near The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur?

The closest airport near The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur is Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL). KUL is located approximately 58.2 KM from the hotel.

Does The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, The St. Regis Kuala Lumpur has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
