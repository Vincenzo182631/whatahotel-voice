Santuario Wellness & SPA in Valladolid

## A Sanctuary for your well-being

Let yourself be pampered by protocols that do not exist in any other space dedicated to wellness. Santuario Wellness & Spa was inaugurated in 2015 and is located in the old stables area underground, but with several entrances that guarantee 80% of natural light. It occupies more than one thousand square meters and the water is extracted from a well 120 meters deep, it is decalcified and after passing it to a reverse osmosis, it is stored in a tank of 120,000 liters, achieving a quality of drinking water similar to natural mineral water.

**Opening hours: 11:00 a.m. – 9:00 p.m.**

Multiple awards endorse this wellness concept. The most recent are the award for ‘Best Europe’s ‘Historic Luxury Spa’ at the international World Luxury Awards 2022 which it received in October 2022 and Best New Spa & Wellness Property and Best for Wow Factor at the Wellness Travel Awards 2016.

In addition, the wellness offer is completed with other services such as the gym and the yoga room.

## Frequently Asked  Questions

We answer the most common questions before you take the next step. If you can’t find what you’re looking for, our team is just a message away.

FAQ

### 01 What spa and wellness experiences does the Santuario de Abadía Retuerta offer?

The Abadía Retuerta Santuario offers an exclusive wellness experience designed to promote relaxation, balance, and reconnection in an environment of absolute serenity. Among its most distinctive experiences are the Spa Sommelier—a unique concept that blends wellness with wine culture through specialized treatments—and the Spa Suite, a private space designed for couples to enjoy treatments and experiences together. The experience is rounded out by a carefully curated selection of treatments, personalized wellness experiences, and programs designed to promote deep relaxation and holistic balance for each guest.

### 02 What wellness programs or multi-day retreats does the Santuario Abadía Retuerta offer?

The Abadía Retuerta Santuario offers wellness programs and multi-day retreats designed to promote relaxation, balance, and self-care. These programs combine exclusive treatments, personalized rituals, spa experiences, physical and mental wellness, and moments of calm in a unique natural and historic setting. The programs are tailored to each guest’s needs and may include private experiences, customized sessions, and personalized attention that transform every stay into a true wellness retreat.

### 03 Can you gift experiences from the Santuario Abadía Retuerta?

Yes, at Abadía Retuerta you can gift experiences through the Experience Box, gift boxes that bring together a selection of experiences to help you discover and enjoy the best the destination has to offer. They allow you to gift stays, culinary experiences, winery tours, or wellness activities, offering the option to choose between packages with or without accommodations. They’re designed to be a delightful surprise for any occasion and can be enjoyed on the date of your choice, always with advance reservation and subject to availability.

### 04 Is the Santuario open to non-hotel guests? What is the minimum age to access?

Yes, the Abadía Retuerta Santuario is open to non-guests through its wellness day program, which allows visitors to enjoy wellness experiences without having to stay overnight. Access to the spa is subject to specific conditions and a minimum age requirement, which are detailed in each available experience or program.
