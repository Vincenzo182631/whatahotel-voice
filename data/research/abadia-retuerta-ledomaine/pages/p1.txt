## Sensory luxury

It is the moment of sensory luxury and unique experiences. Its environment and surroundings; the scent of the vineyards, of the pure air, of a signature cuisine; the absence of noise, that is, silence or simply a delicate melody; pure flavors in the mouth and wines that thrill; the touch of dreamy sheets, the touch of the skin, smooth and soft, after a visit to the spa. The luxury of a sunset. All this, and much more, happens at Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine.

## Rooms for your rest

A former 12th century Romanesque abbey converted into a unique experiential destination, where architecture, design and nature merge equally. Tranquility and peace make sense in every sunrise that can be seen from each of the rooms of this destination. There are 27 rooms and 3 suites carefully restored, endowed with a unique style and overlooking a sea of vineyards that will make the guest a memorable memory in a dream place in the heart of the Douro Valley.

## Frequently asked  questions

We answer the most common questions before you take the next step. If you can’t find what you’re looking for, our team is just a chat away.

FAQ

### 01 Can I enjoy the cuisine at Abadía Retuerta without staying at the hotel? What dining options does the hotel offer?

Yes, it is possible to enjoy the culinary offerings at Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine without staying overnight. Non-residents can make reservations at Refectorio, our Michelin-starred fine-dining restaurant; at Vinoteca, our contemporary Castilian tavern, where tradition and local ingredients are reinterpreted with a modern twist; and at Calicata Terroir Bar, our wine bar located in the winery, which is open during the summer season. For non-residents, the offerings are limited to lunch and dinner, while breakfast, coffee, and the rest of the facilities and services are reserved exclusively for guests.

### 02 What services does Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine offer?

Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine offers its guests a comprehensive selection of services designed to ensure a unique stay. These include an à la carte breakfast included in the room rate, a shuttle service upon request, bicycles for guests’ exclusive use, access to our Santuario Wellness & Spa, a pillow menu for a personalized night’s rest, an in-room minibar, and an exclusive butler service available 24 hours a day. All of this contributes to a unique and personalized hospitality experience.

### 03 Can private celebrations or events be held at Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine?

Yes. Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine is an exceptional destination for a variety of celebrations (intimate weddings, engagement parties, anniversaries, family gatherings, and corporate events). Its exclusive venues, world-class cuisine, and personalized service make it possible to create unique experiences in an incomparable setting in the heart of the Ribera del Duero. Please contact the Groups team, who will evaluate each request individually to ensure an experience that reflects the essence and exclusivity of the destination.

### 04 What is the hotel's cancellation policy?

Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine’s cancellation policy may vary depending on the selected rate, the type of reservation, and the season. Therefore, we recommend reviewing the specific terms and conditions provided during the reservation process. For detailed and up-to-date information regarding changes, cancellations, or any associated fees, please contact the Reservations team directly, and they will provide you with the information relevant to your stay.
