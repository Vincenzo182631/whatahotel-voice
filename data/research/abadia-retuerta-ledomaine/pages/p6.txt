## Frequently Asked   Questions

We answer the most common questions before you take the next step. If you can’t find what you’re looking for, our team is just a message away.

FAQ

### 01 Can corporate events be held at Abadía Retuerta?

Yes, Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine is an ideal destination for hosting corporate events, meetings, and conventions. It features unique and versatile spaces in a historic setting, combined with a high-end food and wine experience. In addition, it offers corporate incentive experiences, including winery tours, tastings, outdoor activities, and wellness programs—all of which can be customized to suit each project.

### 02 What services are included in corporate event planning?

Organizing corporate events at Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine includes comprehensive support from a specialized team, featuring meeting and convention spaces, customized food and wine experiences, audiovisual support, incentive programs, activities at the winery, and wellness options. All services are tailored to each company’s needs to create bespoke events in a unique setting.

### 03 Can weddings or private events be held here?

Yes, Abadía Retuerta LeDomaine offers an exceptional setting for weddings and private events, combining historical heritage, scenic beauty, and a world-class food and wine experience. These types of celebrations are organized on a personalized basis and are subject to prior consultation with the events team regarding the terms and conditions; the team evaluates each project to ensure an experience that reflects the unique character of the destination.
