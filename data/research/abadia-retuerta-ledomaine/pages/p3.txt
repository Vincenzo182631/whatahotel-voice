## A leading winery in the Duero valley

Since the beginning of 2022, Abadía Retuerta has its own **Protected Designation of Origin**. A seal of quality recognized by the European Union that endorses Abadía Retuerta wine as a product with a guarantee of origin, quality and tradition. It accredits the uniqueness of Abadía Retuerta’s soils and the maximum expression of its terroir, understood as the climatic and geographical characteristics that give unique qualities to its vineyards and grapes, and the personality of its wines. The quality of its wines shows its own identity and is governed by a single criterion: to work with its own grapes, which come directly from its own vineyards and are produced in its own winery.

## Frequently asked  questions

We answer the most common questions before you take the next step. If you can’t find what you’re looking for, our team is just a message away.

FAQ

### 01 Why buy directly from the Abadía Retuerta Winery?

Buying directly from the Abadía Retuerta winery guarantees access to the full range of wines, exclusive editions, and optimal storage conditions right from the source. It also offers a more personal and authentic shopping experience, with a guarantee of quality and provenance directly from the producer.

### 02 What designation of origin does Abadía Retuerta have?

Since May 2022, Abadía Retuerta has had its own Protected Designation of Origin: the Abadía Retuerta PDO. This distinction was granted by the European Commission. It is a seal of quality recognized by the European Union that certifies Abadía Retuerta wine as a product with a guaranteed origin, quality, and tradition, and that is protected against misappropriation and imitations arising from its reputation. This seal serves as a clear guarantee for consumers.

### 03 What kinds of wines does Abadía Retuerta produce?

Abadía Retuerta produces wines that reflect the uniqueness of its estate through various collections. Notable among these are the Pago wines, sourced from specific plots and covered by the estate’s own Protected Designation of Origin (PDO), Abadía Retuerta; the Winemaker’s Collection, limited-production wines that express the winemaker’s vision; and Le Domaine, its white wine. The main grape varieties used are Tempranillo, Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah, Merlot, and Sauvignon Blanc.

### 04 What are the shipping terms?

We ship to all of mainland Spain and the Balearic Islands. Shipping costs are €9 per order, although shipping is **free for purchases over €100.** Delivery takes **48 to 72 business hours on the mainland and 3 to 4 business days in the Balearic Islands.** For shipments to the Canary Islands, please contact us in advance at pedidosonline@abadia-retuerta.es.
