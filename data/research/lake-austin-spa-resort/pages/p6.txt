## What activities are included at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

Guests can enjoy a variety of included wellness experiences, including fitness classes, yoga, meditation, paddleboarding, kayaking, water fitness, culinary demonstrations, and seasonal events throughout their stay.

## Does the resort offer water activities on Lake Austin?

Yes, guests can take part in paddleboarding, kayaking, hydrobiking, water e-bikes, wakesurfing, waterskiing, wakeboarding, group paddle outings, floating meditation, and yoga, aqua fitness classes, and other lake-inspired wellness experiences designed around the natural setting of Lake Austin.

## Are activities available every day?

Yes, the resort offers a rotating schedule of daily wellness activities, fitness classes, culinary experiences, and seasonal events throughout the week.

## Can guests book private wellness sessions?

Private wellness sessions and individual instruction are available for select fitness, wellness, and outdoor experiences.

## What types of events are offered at the resort?

Seasonal programming may include live music, culinary demonstrations, wellness workshops, tastings, guest experts, holiday events, and creative experiences inspired by Austin and the surrounding lake environment.

## Are activities suitable for beginners?

Yes, activities and wellness experiences are designed for a range of fitness levels and experience, with options focused on relaxation, mindfulness, movement, and recovery.
