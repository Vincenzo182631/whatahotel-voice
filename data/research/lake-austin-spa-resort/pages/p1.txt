## Timeless tranquility on the shores of Lake Austin.

Experience the rich legacy of this historic lakeside lodge, now a globally renowned spa destination with decades of excellence. Where rustic charm meets world-class wellness, our award-winning resort offers an unmatched retreat, blending luxury, serenity, and rejuvenation in a way no other destination can.

Nestled in a secluded river valley with historic healing properties, our all-inclusive, adult-only resort offers the perfect escape—close enough to the city for convenience, yet far enough to fully disconnect. Picture the warmth of your favorite lakeside retreat, elevated by a world-class spa, gourmet cuisine, and a team of wellness experts dedicated to your well-being. From the moment you arrive, you’ll feel the unique magic that sets us apart as the #1 destination spa in the U.S.

## What All-Inclusive Means at Lake Austin Spa Resort

At Lake Austin Spa Resort, all-inclusive is designed to remove the distractions of planning so you can focus on rest, movement, and renewal. Each overnight stay includes thoughtfully prepared meals, an extensive daily schedule of wellness classes and lake activities, and access to the award-winning LakeHouse Spa facilities.

From sunrise yoga and guided meditation to paddle boarding on the lake and nourishing seasonal dining, every experience is intentionally curated to support a restorative stay in Texas Hill Country.

## A Wellness Retreat in Texas Hill Country

Set on the peaceful shores of Lake Austin, the resort offers a rare sense of seclusion just outside the city. Here, water, nature, and thoughtful programming come together to create a setting where guests can slow down and reconnect.

With its quiet lakeside location, intimate cottages, and ever-evolving menu of wellness experiences, Lake Austin Spa Resort invites guests to experience Texas Hill Country in a way that is both restorative and deeply memorable.

## What is included in a stay at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

Each overnight stay includes three thoughtfully prepared meals daily, access to the LakeHouse Spa facilities, and a wide variety of wellness classes, lake activities, and guided experiences designed to support a restorative retreat.

## Where is Lake Austin Spa Resort located?

Lake Austin Spa Resort is located on the peaceful shores of Lake Austin in Texas Hill Country, approximately 30 minutes from downtown Austin.

## Can guests visit the LakeHouse Spa without staying overnight?

A limited number of day spa reservations are available, allowing guests to enjoy treatments and access to the LakeHouse Spa amenities without an overnight stay.

## What types of activities are included at the resort?

Guests can choose from a full schedule of daily experiences including yoga, fitness classes, paddleboarding, kayaking, meditation, wellness workshops, and seasonal outdoor activities on Lake Austin.

## Is Lake Austin Spa Resort adults only?

Lake Austin Spa Resort is designed primarily as an adults-focused wellness retreat, creating a calm and restorative environment for relaxation and personal renewal.
