## And every detail flows into a seamless all-inclusive lakeside spa escape.

The only way you’ll know you’re just 30 minutes outside of Austin is if we tell you. Here you’ll find 40 quaint, cottage-style accommodations, reminiscent of your favorite aunt’s lake house, a sprawling 25,000 square foot spa specializing in aquatic treatments, organic gardens, and renowned cuisine, all set amongst the rolling landscapes of the Texas Hill Country and the tranquil waters of Lake Austin.
