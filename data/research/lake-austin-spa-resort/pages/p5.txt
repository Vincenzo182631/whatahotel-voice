# Wellness Experiences on Lake Austin

## Experiences Designed Around Wellness

Each experience at Lake Austin Spa Resort is designed to help you discover the best version of yourself. Whether you’re attending a yoga practice on the water, a chef-led cooking class, or a personalized session with one of our renowned therapists, our goal is to transform your well-being. Our comprehensive experience puts you in control, empowering you to forge your own personal wellness path with our team of experts as your guide. Because, after all, you know yourself better than anyone else.

### Personalized Wellness Experiences

Explore a variety of wellness activities and inspirational events designed to resonate with you each day. With no set schedule, we invite you to go with the flow and discover what empowers you at your own pace.

### Mindful Wellness & Meditation Experiences

Immerse yourself in the art of slow living at Lake Austin Spa Resort, where every moment invites you to savor still waters, and the beauty of simplicity and presence. Nestled along the serene shores of Lake Austin, our resort offers a sanctuary to pause, breathe, and embrace life at a more mindful pace.

From tranquil mornings on the water to unhurried strolls through our lush gardens, the experience is designed to nurture balance, connection, and well-being. We invite you to pause, recharge, and intentionally explore mindful practices during your stay that can then have a ripple effect as you weave them into your daily life.

At Lake Austin Spa Resort, slow living isn’t just a philosophy—it’s a way of life.

### Fitness, Movement & Daily Activities

Make your visit uniquely yours with our exclusive private experiences, tailored to individuals, couples, or small groups. Whether it’s a private nutrition consultation, a serene lake excursion, mindfulness and breathwork, or guidance on life’s next chapter, our dedicated wellness team is here to craft a journey just for you. Backed by years of expertise, accolades, and a deep commitment to your well-being, we’re passionate about creating meaningful moments that meet your unique needs and goals.

### Nourishing Dining

Dining at Lake Austin Spa Resort is a vibrant experience that goes far beyond the plate. Led by our Executive Chef James Limon and Director of Culinary Experiences, Stéphane Beaucamp, our immersive program offers curated cooking classes, interactive demonstrations, and exclusive tastings. Paired with the exquisite menus at our lakefront dining room and spa café, every moment is designed to nourish both body and soul.

## What wellness activities are available at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

Guests can enjoy a wide range of wellness experiences, including fitness classes, yoga, meditation sessions, spa treatments, lake activities, culinary experiences, and seasonal wellness programming throughout their stay.

## Is Lake Austin Spa Resort an all-inclusive wellness retreat?

Lake Austin Spa Resort offers an immersive wellness experience that includes access to daily activities, watersports equipment, wellness programming, lakeside experiences, and curated amenities designed to support relaxation and restoration.

## Are wellness experiences included with every stay?

Many wellness activities and experiences are included as part of your stay, with a rotating schedule of fitness classes, mindfulness sessions, lake activities, and wellness-focused programming available daily.

## Is Lake Austin Spa Resort suitable for couples?

Yes, the resort offers a range of experiences designed for couples, including spa treatments, wellness activities, lakeside dining, and relaxing retreats focused on rest and reconnection.

## When is the activities calendar updated?

The activities calendar is updated regularly with seasonal wellness programming, fitness classes, culinary experiences, and special events taking place throughout the year.
