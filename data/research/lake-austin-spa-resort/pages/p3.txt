## Where Wellness and Culinary Excellence Meet

At Lake Austin Spa Resort, dining is an essential part of the wellness experience. From lakeside breakfasts and nourishing lunches to chef-crafted dinners and immersive culinary events, every meal is designed to support both enjoyment and wellbeing. Fresh, seasonal ingredients take center stage, thoughtfully prepared to balance flavor, nutrition, and individual preferences. Whether you’re dining by the water, attending a culinary demonstration, or exploring new flavors through a chef-led tasting, each experience reflects our commitment to exceptional cuisine and mindful living.

## Crafted by Award-Winning Culinary Experts

Under the direction of Director of Culinary Experiences Stéphane Beaucamp and Chef James Limon, every menu is thoughtfully crafted to celebrate seasonal ingredients, local producers, and global culinary influences. Their approach combines the flavors of Texas Hill Country with innovative techniques and wellness-focused principles, creating dishes that are both nourishing and memorable.

Beyond daily dining, guests can explore new flavors and techniques through interactive Culinary Demonstrations and special guest chef experiences that bring fresh perspectives and creative inspiration to the table.

## Culinary Demonstrations

Discover the art and joy of cooking through immersive Culinary Demonstrations led by acclaimed guest chefs, local artisans, and our award-winning culinary team. These intimate experiences invite guests to gather, learn, and explore the stories behind seasonal ingredients, regional flavors, and innovative cooking techniques.

Whether you’re interested in expanding your culinary skills, discovering new ingredients, or simply enjoying exceptional food in a welcoming setting, each demonstration offers a unique opportunity to connect with the people, flavors, and traditions that inspire our menus.

## Lakeside Dining at Lake Austin Spa Resort

Set along the shores of Lake Austin, our dining experiences combine exceptional cuisine with a tranquil waterfront setting. Guests can enjoy thoughtfully prepared meals throughout the day, with menus inspired by seasonal ingredients, wellness principles, and the flavors of Texas Hill Country. Whether dining indoors or taking in the lake views outside, every meal is designed to be both restorative and memorable.

## Inspired by Seasonal & Local Ingredients

Seasonality plays an important role in our culinary philosophy. Menus evolve throughout the year to showcase ingredients at their peak, while partnerships with local growers, producers, and artisans help bring the flavors of Texas to every table.

Combined with global culinary influences and the creativity of our culinary team, these ingredients help create dining experiences that feel both rooted in place and uniquely memorable.

## Lake Austin Resort Dining Experiences FAQs

## Does Lake Austin Spa Resort accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes. Our culinary team can accommodate a wide range of dietary preferences and restrictions, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and other wellness-focused requirements. Guests are encouraged to share dietary needs in advance so we can create the best possible dining experience.

## Are meals included with a stay at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

Dining is an integral part of the resort experience, with chef-prepared meals designed to complement the property’s wellness-focused approach. Specific inclusions may vary by package or stay type.

## What types of dining experiences are available at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

Guests can enjoy lakeside dining at StillWater, healthy spa cuisine at The Venue Café, culinary demonstrations, guest chef experiences, wine events, and other food-focused programming offered throughout the year.

## What makes dining at Lake Austin Spa Resort unique?

Dining is thoughtfully woven into the resort’s wellness philosophy. Seasonal ingredients, personalized dietary accommodations, culinary education, and lakeside surroundings combine to create an experience that goes beyond traditional resort dining.

## Are culinary demonstrations included in the resort experience?

Culinary demonstrations are a popular part of the resort’s programming and provide guests with opportunities to learn from chefs, explore new ingredients, and discover techniques that support healthy, flavorful cooking.

## Who leads the culinary program at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

The culinary program is led by Director of Culinary Experiences Stéphane Beaucamp and Chef James Limon, whose approach blends wellness-focused principles, seasonal ingredients, and global inspiration.

## Are local ingredients used in the menus?

Whenever possible, menus incorporate seasonal ingredients sourced from local growers, producers, and artisans, reflecting the flavors and agricultural traditions of Texas.

## Can guests attend special culinary events during their stay?

Throughout the year, guests may have opportunities to participate in guest chef events, wine tastings, culinary demonstrations, and other specialty experiences that celebrate food, wellness, and connection.
