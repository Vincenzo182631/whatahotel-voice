## Sophisticated Serenity on the Shores of Lake Austin

Experience the timeless allure of Lake Austin, where the legacy of this historic lodge resonates through the magic of its waters and the natural splendor of the Texas Hill Country.

Our intimate retreat, featuring just 40 exclusive cottages and bungalows, embodies casual elegance and unparalleled comfort. Each space is meticulously designed with bespoke furnishings, handpicked decor, and thoughtful amenities, offering a perfect blend of relaxation and sophistication cradled by nature and seclusion.

At Lake Austin, every detail has been crafted to create a sanctuary within a sanctuary, inviting you to unwind and reconnect in an environment that feels both luxurious and profoundly serene.

### What’s Included in Your All-Inclusive Stay

- Locally sourced gourmet meals, light bites and afternoon appetizers
- Non-alcoholic refreshments, coffee bar, and juices
- Access to Lakehouse Spa amenities, pools, sauna, steam and hot tub
- Access to Lakeside Fitness Center and classes
- Daily activities and wellness programs
- Access to Lakeside & Palms Pools with food and beverage service
- Direct lake access, including complimentary paddleboards, kayaks and hydrobikes
- Amenities including Vitamin C-infused showers, signature lavender bath amenities and in-room filtered water
- Complimentary valet & self-parking
- Complimentary welcome gift
- All tips and gratuities included

## A Thoughtfully Inclusive Stay on Lake Austin

Each cottage and bungalow at Lake Austin Spa Resort is part of a fully inclusive experience. Alongside your accommodation, your stay includes chef-prepared dining, wellness classes, lakeside activities, and access to spa amenities, allowing you to fully unwind from the moment you arrive.

## Moments from the LakeHouse Spa

Your cottage or bungalow places you just steps from the LakeHouse Spa, making it easy to move between your private space and the resort’s wellness facilities. Enjoy access to pools, saunas, and lakeside relaxation areas, with treatments available to enhance your stay.

## Private Cottages & Bungalows in the Texas Hill Country

Whether you choose a lakefront bungalow or a scenic garden cottage, each space is designed to offer privacy and calm. Natural surroundings, outdoor spaces, and thoughtful interiors create an environment that supports rest and reconnection throughout your stay near Austin.

## A Peaceful Retreat Near Austin for Couples & Small Groups

With a limited collection of cottages and bungalows, the resort is well suited to couples, solo travelers, and small groups seeking a more private stay. Select accommodations can support group bookings or room blocks, making them a considered option for wellness retreats, celebrations, business meetings, or small gatherings.

## What is included when booking a cottage or bungalow at Lake Austin Spa Resort?

Each stay is all-inclusive, covering your cottage or bungalow, daily meals, wellness classes, lakeside activities, and access to the LakeHouse Spa amenities.

## Are the cottages and bungalows private?

Yes, each accommodation is designed for privacy, with many offering secluded outdoor spaces, hot tubs, garden settings, or views of Lake Austin.

## Can I book multiple cottages or bungalows for a group stay?

Yes, the resort can accommodate small group stays and room blocks, depending on availability, making it ideal for retreats or special occasions.

## How close are the accommodations to the spa and resort amenities?

All cottages and bungalows are located within a short walk of the LakeHouse Spa, dining spaces, and lakeside activities.
