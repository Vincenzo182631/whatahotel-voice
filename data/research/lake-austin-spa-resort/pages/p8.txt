# Private Sessions & Excursions

## Take Your Transformation Deeper

Our goal is to inspire you to higher levels of wellness and well-being during your stay. For guests that would like to further their practice, flow with one of our Wellness Current paths, or simply consult an expert in the comfort of a private session, we can customize a program, experience, or outing just for you. From traditional fitness and mind/body experiences, to ritual practices, fun and flavors, and time on the lake, our private sessions allow you to deepen your wellness journey.

_Enjoying a package stay?_ Your Spa & Personal Experience Allowance can be applied toward most private fitness, wellness, and lake activities, elevating every moment of your retreat.
