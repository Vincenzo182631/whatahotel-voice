# Welcome to JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo

## Escape to our luxury Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic hotel

JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo is an award-winning luxury hotel in the Piantini District of downtown Santo Domingo. Our luxury hotel in Piantini, Santo Domingo, is the ideal location near the Colonial Zone, Catedral Primada de América and Alcázar de Colón. Retreat to our upscale hotel rooms and suites featuring Wi-Fi access and views of Santo Domingo. Savor delicious international fare at Winston's...See More Grill & Patio and unwind with a specialty cocktail at Vertygo 101 Lounge & Bar in our hotel in Santo Domingo. When you're not conducting business or exploring Santo Domingo, fit in a workout at our 24-hour fitness center and take a swim in our outdoor infinity pool. If you're hosting a business or social event at the hotel, reserve one of our nine elegant venues with catering services and AV equipment. Reconnect with yourself and find your center at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo. Enjoy a rewarding stay filled with exceptional amenities and services.See Less

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 12:00 pm.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo. The daily self parking fee is $15.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo is Las Americas International Airport (SDQ). SDQ is located approximately 33.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo have a Executive lounge?

Yes, JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo has Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
