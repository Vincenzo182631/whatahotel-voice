### Frequently Asked Questions

What restaurants does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo have on property?

The on-property restaurants at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo are:

Winston's Grill & PatioVertygo 101 - Lounge & BarIn-Room Dining

Does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo offer room service?

Yes, room service is available at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo.

What cuisine is offered at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo?

The cuisines offered at JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo are:

International at Winston's Grill & Patio, Vertygo 101 - Lounge & Bar, In-Room Dining

Does JW Marriott Hotel Santo Domingo offer breakfast?

Yes, breakfast is available onsite at Winston's Grill & Patio for a fee. Breakfast is also available through in-room dining for a fee.
