# Dining Journey at the JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa

## Dining

At JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa, dining journey is shaped by Jeju’s seasons, local ingredients, and meaningful storytelling. From vibrant seasonal dishes at Island Kitchen to wood fire-grilled specialties at The Flying Hog and a chef-led journey inspired by Jeju’s haenyeo culture at Yeoumul, each experience reflects the island’s nature and warmth. Island Kitchen

## Jeju Brunch Royale

Discover JW Marriott Jeju’s signature brunch experience inspired by the island’s vibrant flavors and seasonal ingredients. Featuring table-served main dishes, refined local delicacies, premium caviar, and free-flow champagne, Jeju Brunch Royale offers a leisurely dining journey in the heart of Seogwipo.

## Yeoumul Seasonal Course

Inspired by the changing seasons of Jeju, Chef Jin translates the island’s nature, memories, and quiet beauty into a contemporary dining journey. Drawing from what is seen, felt, and discovered while walking through Jeju, each course reflects the island’s ingredients, stories, and sense of place. More than a meal, Yeoumul offers an immersive culinary experience where thoughtful storytelling and refined flavors unfold throughout the journey.

The Flying Hog

## Private Dining Room

Immerse yourself in a refined culinary journey at The Flying Hog, where our exclusive private dining room ensures an intimate setting, allowing you to savor the perfect blend of wood fire grill dining, warm hospitality, and breathtaking ocean views.
