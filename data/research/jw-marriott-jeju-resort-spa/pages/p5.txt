## LET THE MOMENT LAST

As autumn unfolds across Jeju, each hour brings a different light, breeze and temperature. From crisp morning rituals and slow dining moments to golden sunsets and quiet evenings, follow a day shaped by the season at JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa—and let every meaningful moment linger a little longer.

## Autumn Dining Journey
