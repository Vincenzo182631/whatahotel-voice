### Where Nature Meets Luxury

Renowned designer Bill Bensley drew inspiration from the local vibrant canola fields and iconic basalt walls to create JW Marriott Jeju's luxurious interiors. Warm yellow tones, fine wood, and natural stone blend seamlessly, evoking the island's natural beauty. Yellow symbolizes luck, while gray, black, and white capture Jeju's volcanic essence. Discover the perfect balance of nature and luxury in our hotel rooms in Jeju, which have been meticulously designed by Bill Bensley.
