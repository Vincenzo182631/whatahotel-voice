### Frequently Asked Questions

Does JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardiovascular Equipment, Cross fit, In Body, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.
