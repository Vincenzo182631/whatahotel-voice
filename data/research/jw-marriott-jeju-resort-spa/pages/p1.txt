# Welcome to JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa

## Connect with nature in our Jeju Island luxury resort

Experience the breathtaking vistas of South Korea’s Jeju Island at JW Marriott. This first JW Marriott Resort and Spa in South Korea was designed by award‑winning interior designer Bill Bensley. From a clifftop setting in Seogwipo City, our Jeju Island All‑Inclusive Resort places you near Seogwipo Maeil Olle Market and natural wonders like Sanbangsan Mountain and Seongsan Sunrise. Stroll through the...See More JW Garden or ask about nature‑themed recreation. Begin your day with brunch at Island Kitchen, unwind at The Lounge, and enjoy BBQ at The Flying Hog. Focus on wellness at SPA by JW with facials, hot stones, and deep tissue massages, or relax in indoor and outdoor pools, including an infinity pool. Our indoor and outdoor venues offer custom catering for retreats and weddings in Seogwipo. Recharge in hotel lodging with LED TVs, minibars, marble bathtubs, and free WiFi. ### Harmonizing Luxury With Nature

Renowned luxury hotel and resort designer Bill Bensley has beautifully transformed JW Marriott Jeju, drawing inspiration from the island's stunning natural surroundings. Reflecting the lush landscapes and vibrant industries, our Jeju Island luxury resort's interior design features warm yellow tones, luxurious wood, and natural stones, creating a cozy and inviting atmosphere. Inspired by Jeju's colorful fields of flowers, Bensley chose yellow as the main color for its symbolic representation of luck in Korean culture. Secondary colors of gray, black, and white echo Jeju's volcanic terrain.

## Architectural Splendor

Renowned architectural firm, WATG, drew inspiration from Korea's indigenous Choga architecture and Jeju's unique terrain to craft our internationally acclaimed resort. Reflecting Jeju's UNESCO World Natural Heritage sites and volcanic landscape, every detail at our Jeju Island resort embodies the island's rich nature and sustainable lifestyle. The modern reinterpretation of Choga-style roofs, complemented by grid-patterned ceilings, ensures a seamless flow of sea breeze and sunlight, creating a harmonious indoor-outdoor experience. Strategically positioned to blend with the terrain, our pavilion-like structures capture nature's beauty, offering guests breathtaking views.

## JW Garden

The JW Garden is a serene sanctuary to welcome you with fresh herbs and ingredients, nestled alongside the Jeju Olle Trail route 7, offering picturesque views of the beautiful sea of Jeju. As part of your culinary journey, we encourage you to connect with the breathtaking nature of Jeju. Nourish your body and soul with a fresh dish that includes ingredients sourced directly from JW Garden, ensuring a truly enriching and authentic experience.

### Culinary Dining Experience

Embark on a culinary journey like no other at our Jeju Island luxury resort. Our dedicated team strives to provide the ultimate gourmet experience, inviting you to savor the rich and authentic flavors of Jeju using local ingredients, including fresh vegetables and herbs from JW Garden. Explore our diverse range of restaurants and bars, each offering a taste of genuine Jeju cuisine. From traditional favorites to contemporary delights, every dish is crafted to perfection, ensuring an unforgettable dining experience that tantalizes your taste buds and leaves you craving for more.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa?

The check-in time at JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa.

What are the parking options at JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa?

On-site self parking is available at JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa. Self parking is complimentary for guests.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa booking.

What is the closest airport near JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa?

The closest airport near JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa is Jeju International Airport (CJU). CJU is located approximately 40.0 KM from the hotel.

Does JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa have electric vehicle charging stations?

Yes, JW Marriott Jeju Resort & Spa has electric vehicle charging stations (subject to availability).
