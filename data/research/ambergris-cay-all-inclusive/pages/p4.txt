# _Ambergris Cay International Airport_

## _Arrive_ in style

Commercial flights to Turks and Caicos Islands now arrive at either the Howard Hamilton International Airport (PLS) on the main island of Providenciales (Provo), or at South Caicos International Airport (XSC) in South Caicos.

**In Provo**, airport staff are available just outside the arrivals hall and are ready to assist with your 10-minute transfer to Atlantic Aviation FBO, a private jet facility with a modern, comfortable lounge. From Atlantic Aviation, your onward flight to Ambergris Cay International Airport takes about 20 minutes.

**In South Caicos**, as you exit the arrival hall, our concierge team will be there with a sign to welcome you and assist you to your short 10-minute flight directly to Ambergris Cay. Guests of the resort enjoy complimentary round-trip air transfers to Ambergris Cay. Please note that we do require guests to arrive by 4:00 p.m. to make the daytime flight connection. Please contact our flight support team to coordinate your South Caicos flight arrangements.

_On occasion, our private planes may be out of operation for routine maintenance, and we may have to substitute a chartered flight on a domestic airline. In this case, transfers will depart from the Provo (PLS) main terminal and not the Atlantic Aviation Private Airport. If this change is necessary, we will make every attempt to notify guests in advance._

## _Private_ plane FAQs

**Can an aircraft land on Ambergris Cay directly from another country?** _Yes, please provide us with 72 hours notice so we can arrange customs and immigration._

**What are the fees?** _Request fee schedule_ _._

**Is there fuel available on Ambergris Cay?**

‍ _No, there is presently no fuel on Ambergris Cay. Fuel is available at Atlantic Aviation (FBO) Private Airport._

**What is the largest aircraft able to land on Ambergris Cay?**

‍ _An aircraft up to 28 meters in length is able to land on Ambergris Cay, anything larger will require special permission from the Civil Aviation Authority._

**Are there any aircraft restrictions?** _Commercial aircraft are restricted to a maximum take-off weight of 10,000 kgs for operation to Ambergris Cay; if an aircraft is a direct charter it is considered commercial._
