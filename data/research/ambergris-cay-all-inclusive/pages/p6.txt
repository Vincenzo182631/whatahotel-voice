# Where the Island Meets the Water

Introducing the Waterside Bungalows

Explore Founder’s Rates

Some places are discovered. Others are designed to feel as though they always belonged. The new Waterside Bungalows at Ambergris Cay sit along the calm, sheltered waterside of the island, where the light is softer in the morning and the water stays glass-still at sunset. Twelve bungalows, six one-bedroom and six two-bedroom, each with a private heated plunge pool, a shaded terrace that opens directly onto the water, and a kayak and paddleboard waiting at the shore. This is not a reimagining of the beach. It’s a different relationship with the water altogether.

### Your Morning

Wake to the sound of water lapping against the shore. Step onto your terrace. Coffee in hand, watch the light change across the sound. When you’re ready, the kayak is three steps away.

### Your Afternoon

Hop on a boat to the Hangover Tiki Bar, the island's floating bar anchored in the turquoise shallows. Back on land, the Club House is a short ride away, or paddle there by kayak. A game of tennis or pickleball. An hour at Elevate Spa. Or stay on your terrace and let the plunge pool do the work.

### Your Evening

Sundowners from the terrace as the water turns gold. A drink at Mahoney's Bar, then dinner at Calico — or make it a private affair with Chef's Table dining, a starlit dinner on the dock, or one of the island's themed dinner nights at the Club House. End the evening with a beach bonfire under a sky full of stars.

### Your Bungalow

1,600 square feet of luminous interiors and modern Caribbean design. Floor-to-ceiling glass that erases the line between inside and out. A king bed, soaker tub, outdoor rain shower, and every detail considered. The two-bedroom Waterside Bungalows offer 2,257 square feet, ideal for families or couples traveling together.

## All-Inclusive, _All Taken Care Of_

Every stay at Ambergris Cay is all-inclusive. That means gourmet dining, premium spirits and cocktails, non-motorized water sports, yoga, electric bikes, tennis with a pro, the Hangover Tiki Bar, on-shore bone fishing, the Fitness Pavilion, and your island host, all included. The only decisions you need to make are which direction to paddle and what to have for dinner.

## Two Ways to _Experience the Island_

Ambergris Cay’s Beachfront Bungalows line the island’s sandy shore, ideal for ocean swimmers, snorkelers, and those who want the beach at their feet. The new Waterside Bungalows face the calm sound—perfect for kayakers, paddleboarders, and guests who want the Club House and island amenities within a short ride. Both feature private heated plunge pools, all-inclusive dining, and 1,100 acres of private island to explore. The only question is: sand or water?
