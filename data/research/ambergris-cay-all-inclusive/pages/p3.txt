# _Explore the island_

## Time to _revitalize_

Your private island vacation paradise awaits on Ambergris Cay, an all inclusive resort ideal for those seeking laidback luxury and unparalleled natural beauty. Wanderlust and wellbeing converge with activities that promote synergy with nature and self, and are designed around the informal, relaxed pace of island life. It's an invitation to slow down and enjoy each moment, even when you're moving fast.

Active travelers will welcome the opportunity to embark on numerous adventures amidst the island's lush flora, vast terrain, and diverse waters. Navigate the winding roads, hiking trails, and coastlines by golf cart, foot or bicycle. Master a new skill with complimentary access to onshore fishing, Hobie cats, and kayaks; and get up close and personal with marine life while snorkelling. Then venture further out on a sunset cruise, deep sea fishing excursion or a private boat tour to Little Ambergris Cay. Those seeking a wellness holiday will feel it almost immediately; there is a powerful energy on Ambergris Cay, one that can still the mind, rebalance the body, and revitalize the soul. Are you ready to experience it?

## Time to _play_

Breathe in the rejuvenating energy and unhurried pace of Ambergris Cay, a nature-based wellness travel experience in Turks and Caicos. As you exhale, letting go, if even for a moment of any weight you carry, take in the stunning scenery, rustic terrain, and captivating wildlife of this private island community. Ideal for active travellers seeking unique travel destinations, Ambergris Cay exemplifies a synergistic approach to life. Here, the power of nature is never overlooked, windswept hair is always in style, and resort casual is encouraged.

Feel life’s stresses fade into the background, inspiring you to plunge into the playful spirit of island life. With countless marine recreation possibilities – from kayaking through mangroves to whale watching trips, free diving for conch, or bone fishing – a memorable seafaring voyage is always on the horizon. When you’ve had enough sand and surf for the day, take advantage of the land-based activities including spa treatments, beach bonfires, or an exclusive dinner at the Chef’s Table. For a full list of the island’s activities, contact our Concierge team.

## Take in the _moment_

Ambergris Cay’s holistic approach to luxury wellness travel blends modern accommodations, luxury amenities, and restorative activities with immersion in nature; yielding a signature experience that sets it apart from other all-inclusive private island resorts. Designed to shift focus from what’s on your ever-expanding plate to what matters most, this private island resort serves as an inviting haven, promoting rest, mindfulness, and appreciation of each moment.

What's more, Ambergris Cay’s variety of outdoor activities, ocean excursions, and immersive wildlife experiences is enhanced by its refreshingly distinct _“come as you are'' culture_. Here, it doesn't matter who you are, what you do back home or what you wear (though we do recommend resort casual for dinner). So, pack your active traveler gear and prepare to play; your island host is standing by, waiting to help you make the most of your private island vacation, from hiking, biking or paddle boarding to tennis and a post match massage in one of our air-conditioned oceanfront cabanas.
