## All inclusive _à la carte_

Luxury and 'all inclusive' are not typical associations, and Ambergris Cay is not your typical all inclusive resort. Our decision to offer all inclusive dining is based on our promise to deliver a relaxing holiday and the ability to indulge without the hassle of signing a bill after every order. Enjoy à la carte meals featuring the finest local and seasonal ingredients, tailored to your taste and dietary preferences, and cocktails crafted with premium spirits. Menus are dynamic, changing often and always with the option to design any offering " _your way"_. As well, our team of Chefs welcome the opportunity to prepare off-menu requests, provided we have the ingredients readily available or a note about your preferences, prior to arrival.

## Inclusive _activities_

Prioritize balance, relaxation, and leisure with Ambergris Cay’s all inclusive resort activities, including yoga classes, guided nature walks, tennis, non-motorized water sports, and onshore fishing. Visit the Club House, a sunny beach club where comfortable beach chairs and umbrellas are always available for your use. It features a pool table, ping-pong tables, video games, and board games, plus an Explorer’s Hut for kids (from 5 years old) to enjoy interactive games and treasure hunts. Choose from an extensive selection of immersive outdoor activities, including guided nature tours, paddle boarding, onshore fishing, and more; our all inclusive resort experiences are designed to help you get the most from your stay.

## _Complimentary_ transfers

From the moment you arrive at Ambergris Cay International Airport, ease into island life and prepare to be pampered.

Round-trip transfers from either Howard Hamilton International Airport (PLS) in Providenciales or South Caicos International Airport (XCS) in South Caicos are provided for Ambergris Cay guests arriving in Turks and Caicos on commercial flights.

Guests traveling by private plane can clear customs and immigration and be in their villa or beachfront bungalow within minutes of arriving.

With over 1,100 acres to explore, unique experiences to share, and world-class amenities to enjoy, a private island vacation on Ambergris Cay is an opportunity to recharge your body, mind, and soul. Explore the hidden beach coves, winding dirt roads, and wild landscapes of this remote island resort — a refreshingly unique approach to relaxed luxury and nature-based wellness travel awaits you.

_On occasion, our private planes may be out of operation for routine maintenance, and we may have to substitute a chartered flight on a domestic airline. In this case, transfers will depart from the Provo (PLS) main terminal and not the Atlantic Aviation Private Airport. If this change is necessary, we will make every attempt to notify guests in advance._
