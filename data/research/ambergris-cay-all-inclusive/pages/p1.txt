## Private island _paradise_

Ambergris Cay, an exclusive private island in the Turks and Caicos, sits 600 miles south of Miami, on the southeast corner of the string of Turks and Caicos Islands that surround the world-famous Caicos Banks. Originally established as a community for private homeowners, Ambergris Cay opened as a private island resort in 2019. Now, travelers seeking a secluded luxury escape can enjoy access to over 1,100 acres of mostly untouched land, ready to be explored by a select few. With 31 bungalows—including our new Waterside Bungalows, now open—and 12 villas for rent on an all-inclusive basis, the result is a luxury boutique hotel feel in a vast and diverse setting. Guests can choose from beachfront bungalows along the shore, waterside bungalows with direct calm-water access and proximity to the Club House, or a collection of privately owned homes and luxury villas ranging from 3 to 11 bedrooms. Each with a private heated plunge pool. The experience is bespoke, tailored to the individual, and ever-evolving.

## Premium _all inclusive_

Savor delicious made-to-order dishes and refreshing cocktails on Ambergris Cay, a Turks and Caicos private island offering all inclusive villas and bungalows and 5-star resort amenities. A la carte menu selections vary by the day, based on seasonality, and are comprised of the highest quality ingredients. From grilled lobster to organic chicken breast, our international chefs will delight you with local island cuisine and classic favorites, prepared to your taste. Vegan and vegetarian menus are available, and we cater to special dietary needs wherever possible and with advance notice. Our all inclusive service affords guests the convenience of enjoying gourmet meals and premium cocktails in any one of the island’s culinary settings, including poolside bites, deserted beach picnics, and all day dining at Calico restaurant and bar.

## Ambergris Cay _International_ Airport

**Commercial:**

For guests arriving at Howard Hamilton International Airport (PLS) in Providenciales (Provo), your onward flight to Ambergris Cay International Airport is complimentary. In Provo, airport staff are available just outside the arrivals hall and are ready to assist with your 10-minute transfer to Atlantic Aviation FBO, a private jet facility with a comfortable, modern lounge. From Atlantic Aviation FBO, your onward flight to Ambergris Cay International Airport takes about 20 minutes.

**NEW: American Airlines announces new flight from Miami International Airport to South Caicos (XSC).**

For guests arriving in South Caicos International Airport (XSC), your onward flight to Ambergris Cay is complimentary. As you exit the arrival hall, our concierge team will be there with a sign to welcome you and assist you to your short 10-minute flight directly to Ambergris Cay.

**Private or commercial:**

For guests flying directly to the island by private plane, Ambergris Cay offers one of the longest runways in the Caribbean. Our 5,700-foot runway can accommodate aircraft up to 28 meters/92 feet in length regardless if commerical or private. With this seamless arrival, private jet guests can go from landing to their luxurious beachfront bungalow or villa in minutes. Please contact flight support for details.
