## _Luxuriously all-inclusive_

Turks and Caicos all-inclusive villas are a very rare resort offering. In fact, Ambergris Cay is one of just a few private island resorts around the world that offers an all-inclusive luxury villa experience. Though all-inclusive resorts are not typically associated with luxury, Ambergris Cay defies that presumption, combining premium accommodations and dining, with the convenience of a one-time, package rate.

## _About our resort villas_

Ambergris Cay offers 12 Turks and Caicos all-inclusive villas for rent. Given the unique characteristics, location, and amenities of our resort villas, we recommend discussing your preferences and holiday plans with our team, prior to booking. Together, we'll help you select the best option for your family or group. Poseidon, for example, is an all-inclusive three-bedroom villa, with a nearly identical twin, Neptune Villa, just a few meters away. Dream Estate, is the most exclusive and private luxury villa in Turks and Caicos; it is the largest villa on Ambergris Cay and includes two private chef and butlers, four heated swimming pools, and its own private beach. Other unique attributes of our resort villas include:

- 3 bedroom villas near tennis courts and Columbus Beach: Poseidon & Neptune
- 4 bedroom villa with unobstructed view and direct access to Columbus Beach: Osprey & Waypoint
- 4 bedroom villa, all king beds, ideal for couples traveling together: Carpe Diem
- 4 bedroom villas with 2 twin beds, ideal for families : Aurora, Morning Star
- 4 bedroom villa offering max privacy on the southernmost point: Land's End & Puesta del Sol
- 6 bedroom villa with private chef and butler and 2 pools: Dream Pavilion
- 11 bedroom villa with private chef and butler and 4 pools: Dream Estate

## _The luxury of convenience_

Ambergris Cay is a short 20 minute flight from Providenciales in Turks and Caicos (PLS), and with nonstop flights to PLS from major airports including JFK, Boston Logan, and Miami, it's easy to bring everyone together for an unforgettable private island getaway. For guests arriving by private plane, it's even easier. Ambergris Cay International Airport (MBAC) offers a 5,700 foot runway capable of landing a G6 aircraft. Enjoy the ease of landing, clearing customs and immigration, and settling into your villa in under 10 minutes. And unlike other Turks and Caicos villas that often share a beach and sit side-by-side, Ambergris Cay's all-inclusive villas offer seclusion, space to explore, and peace of mind.

## _Villas to suit your style_

Ambergris Cay offers a collection of eight Turks and Caicos all-inclusive villas that range from three to six bedrooms and can accommodate up to 12 guests.
