## Your waterside _escape_

Discover effortless island living in the Waterside Bungalows at Ambergris Cay, a limited collection of six intimate retreats designed for relaxed escapes by the water. Located near the Club House, these luxury waterside bungalows offer convenient access to island amenities while preserving a tranquil, private island atmosphere.

Bright interiors and modern Caribbean design enhance seamless indoor-outdoor living, with a private heated plunge pool, shaded terrace, and sun loungers. Direct water access allows guests to kayak or paddleboard just steps from their bungalow, shaping an experience defined by ease and choice.

## Life _by the water_

Ease into island life in the Waterside Bungalows, designed for privacy, comfort, and connection to nature. Surrounded by lush landscaping and calming water views, these luxury bungalows at Ambergris Cay are ideal for couples or small families seeking a peaceful escape.

Each bungalow features airy interiors, floor-to-ceiling glass doors, and inviting outdoor spaces that blur the line between inside and out—delivering a refined yet laid-back, all-inclusive private island experience.

## Resort amenities

- Island Host for each bungalow and villa
- A la carte breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks throughout the day
- Premium spirits, e.g. Grey Goose, Johnny Walker Black, Tanqueray, Patron
- Beer, house wine, soft drinks, fresh pressed juice, and espresso coffee drinks
- Non-motorized water sports, e.g. paddle boarding, onshore fishing and kayaking
- Hiking trails, electric bikes, pickle ball, and tennis, plus lessons with a pro
- Fitness Pavilion and Yoga Deck
- The Club House activity centre
- Hangover Bar at Little Ambergris Cay
- Resort pool with music and bar / restaurant service
- Boating excursions, including deep sea fishing\*
- Elevate Spa including massages & facials\*
- In-room and private dining experiences\*
