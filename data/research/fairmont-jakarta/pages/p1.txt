## Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Fairmont Jakarta located?

Fairmont Jakarta is located at Jl. Asia Afrika No. 8, Gelora Bung Karno Senayan.

Does Fairmont Jakarta have a spa?

The Fairmont Spa at Fairmont Jakarta is located on the 4th floor with 9 fully equipped treatment rooms, a steam room, a sauna, and an indoor jacuzzi.

Is parking available at Fairmont Jakarta?

Fairmont Jakarta has a parking space building that can accommodate up to 745 vehicles.

Is there a concierge service at Fairmont Jakarta?

Yes, Fairmont Jakarta offers concierge services to assist with your needs, including arranging transportation, and providing information about local attractions.

Is Fairmont Jakarta connected or close to any shopping centers?

Yes, Fairmont Jakarta has the direct access tunnel to Plaza Senayan and Sentral Senayan Office Towers, open from 7 AM - 11.30 PM.

Does Fairmont Jakarta provide halal dining?

Yes, the hotel features a halal-certified restaurant, 1945 Indonesian Restaurant, located on the 3rd floor.

Does Fairmont Jakarta provide accessible hotel rooms?

Yes, accessible rooms are available on the 6th floor and may be requested at the time of reservation. It also provides a selection of accessible washroom amenities.
