# Rooms at The Westin Perth

## Accommodations

Leave the world behind in one of the 368 well-appointed, spacious hotel rooms and suites at The Westin Perth. From the first entrance, you are greeted with pure tranquility, subtle white tea scent, spacious...See More designs and grand interiors. Ceiling to floor windows enhance natural light. Our spacious hotel rooms in Perth CBD feature high-speed Wi-Fi, a 55-inch flat-screen LCD TV, a work desk, dining area and in-room safe. Westin signature amenities and services provide everything you need for a revitalizing stay in Perth, including the Westin Heavenly® Bed, Sleep Well Lavender balm, rainfall showerhead and ensuite. To further enrich your stay, reserve one of our suites with access to the Westin Executive Club Lounge and daily benefits including breakfast, evening drinks and canapés and use of the club lounge throughout the day with complimentary tea and coffee.See Less
