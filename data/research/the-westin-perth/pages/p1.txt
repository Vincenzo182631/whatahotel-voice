# Welcome to The Westin Perth

## Empower a better you at our luxury hotel in Perth

The Westin Perth is a rejuvenating haven with luxury accommodations, world-class food and beverage outlets, and elegant meeting and event venues. Located in Hibernian Place, enjoy the three pillars of well-being under the renowned Westin® brand at our Perth city centre hotel. Sophisticated rooms and suites are impeccably designed with the Westin Heavenly® Bed, sleek bathrooms, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi. Relax with a spa treatment at Bodhi Spa or maintain your wellness at our 24-hour Westin WORKOUT® Fitness Studio. Our Run Concierge can assist with fitness suggestions throughout the city. We also offer an outdoor pool on level 5. The meeting and event venues at our 5-star hotel in Perth are enhanced by our renowned Westin service and elegant décor combined with state-of-the-art technology. Enjoy a nutritious meal or High Tea in the Haven Lounge, or a Roman feast at our acclaimed Italian restaurant, Garum by Guy Grossi. A 5-star experience awaits you at The Westin Perth, Let's Rise.

### Stay in the Vibrant City of Perth

Immerse yourself in the heart of Perth. Positioned in the city's east end and towering over idyllic Hibernian Place, The Westin Perth is a short stroll from Perth's shopping, business, dining and cultural hub. Elizabeth Quay, Kings Park, The Art Gallery of WA and the bustling Perth city nightlife in Northbridge and the Perth CBD, are only a short walk away from our Perth city centre hotel. Iconic sporting venues such as Optus Stadium and The Western Australian Cricket Ground are a short drive away, and breathtaking beaches like Scarborough Beach and Cottesloe Beach are a mere 25 minute drive away.

### Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times at The Westin Perth?

The check-in time at The Westin Perth is 3:00 pm and the check-out time is 11:00 am.

Does The Westin Perth allow pets?

No, pets are not allowed at The Westin Perth.

What are the parking options at The Westin Perth?

On-site self parking is available at The Westin Perth. Self parking is available for $50.00 per day.

Off-site parking is also available near The Westin Perth. The daily valet fee is $70.00 per day.

View additional parking information in the hotel information section on this page.

Does The Westin Perth have in-room Wi-Fi?

Yes, The Westin Perth has in-room Wi-Fi available to hotel guests. Marriott Bonvoy members receive complimentary in-room Internet access when they book direct. Sign up for Marriott Bonvoy for free as you complete your The Westin Perth booking.

What is the closest airport near The Westin Perth?

The closest airport near The Westin Perth is Perth Airport (PER). PER is located approximately 11.3 KM from the hotel.

Does The Westin Perth have electric vehicle charging stations?

No, The Westin Perth does not have electric vehicle charging stations.

Does The Westin Perth have an Executive lounge?

Yes, The Westin Perth has an Executive lounge available to qualifying guests during their stay. Upgrade your stay by booking a room with an Executive lounge access (subject to availability), which opens the door to exclusive amenities and services. Marriott Bonvoy Platinum, Titanium, and Ambassador Elite members receive complimentary lounge access during their stay.
