# Discover Perth

## Experiences

Whether you're an adventurous spirit on your travels desiring to explore the greater Perth landscape or prefer an inner city staycation oasis, immerse yourself in all that is on offer at The Westin Perth. Create unforgettable memories and leave our hotel near Elizabeth Quay feeling empowered and rejuvenated. Perth is renowned for its stunning beaches and nature trails, exciting sporting games, a diverse cultural landscape and bustling city life with a plethora of bars, restaurants and nightlife. Rottnest Island is a 45 minute ferry away with a picturesque landscape, private beaches, adventure tours and bars and restaurants.

### Eventide Yachts

Meet Eventide, our 38’ Lagoon sailing catamaran! The Lagoon 380 is fast, stable, safe and also easy to maneuver. Eventide has all the creature comforts. Inside you’ll find a spacious saloon seating for 8 with uninterrupted 360 degree views, with plenty of headroom and bathed in natural light. ### Art Gallery of WA (AGWA)

Situated in the heart of Perth’s Cultural Centre, the Gallery houses the State Art Collection with works by renowned local and international artists from the 1800s to today. Immerse yourself in the Balancing Act gallery space displaying Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander art. Take a free guided tour or find something unique in the AGWA Design Store to take home.

### Frequently Asked Questions

Does The Westin Perth have a fitness center on-site?

Yes. The Westin Perth offers a complimentary fitness center to hotel guests during their stay.

The fitness center includes equipment such as Cardio equipment and strength machines, Cardiovascular Equipment, Elliptical Machine(s), Exercise Bike(s), Free Weights, Rowing Machine(s), Strength Equipment, Treadmill(s), and Weight Machines, making it suitable for both quick workouts and full training sessions.

Does The Westin Perth have a pool on-site?

Yes, The Westin Perth has one Outdoor pool on-site for hotel guests.

Does The Westin Perth have an on-site spa?

Yes, BODHI Day Spa is located on-site at The Westin Perth. Click here to book a service now.

What are some local attractions and things to do near The Westin Perth?

Perth Concert Hall, Supreme Court Gardens, St. George's Cathedral, Fire Safety Education Centre and Museum, West Australian Symphony Orchestra and more are nearby The Westin Perth.
