### Breakfast

Start your day right with a hearty breakfast at the Garum Breakfast Table to kick-start your mornings. The Tavola Fredda & Tavola Caldo include a variety of Roman Italian sweet and savoury delicacies and traditional breakfast items.

### Aperitivo

With 50% off selected bar snacks, pasta including traditional Roman Cacio e Pepe and beverages, Aperitivo in Garum is an enjoyable Happy Hour at our Italian restaurant in Perth. Open every Monday - Saturday from 4.30pm - 6.00pm.

### Dinner

Unique to Perth, Garum's dinner takes inspiration from traditional and modern Roman cooking. Start with Stagionato and Capesante from the sea, Salumi from the land, then venture into mains including Bistecca, Porchetta, Pesce & Papa's Agnello (Arkady Lamb shoulder). Inspired by the beauty of historical Hibernian Hall, Garum is an Italian restaurant in Perth that sets a chic scene for an outstanding Roman meal.

### Special Events

Garum holds a series of special events throughout the year including Pasta Masterclasses, collaborations with premium wineries and lunch time menu live cooking demonstrations. Alternatively, we welcome guests at our Italian restaurant in Perth to host their own private social and corporate events.

### Guy Grossi Visits

Garum is a collaboration between The Westin Perth and acclaimed chef Guy Grossi. Genuine hospitality and sustainable quality produce is at the forefront of the core values of the venue that Guy has instilled in the Garum Famiglia. Guy visits Perth several times a year. See him during a meet and greet at an event, or see Guy in action behind the kitchen.

### Private & Semi-Private Dining

Private dining in Perth in the Polpo private dining room and alcove area is available to guests. Host a special private social event or a corporate evening for clients and colleagues. Unique set menus and wine pairings are available.
