# Staying Safe: Should Tourists Be Worried About Mpox in Kenya?

### Staying Safe: Should Tourists Be Worried About Mpox in Kenya?

**Staying Safe: Should Tourists Be Worried About Mpox in Kenya?**

The safety and well-being of our guests are always our top priority. We understand that recent reports about Mpox (formerly known as monkeypox) may have raised some concerns for travelers planning their visit to Kenya. We want to assure you that Kenya remains a safe and welcoming destination, and there is no cause for alarm.

The Kenyan health authorities, in collaboration with the World Health Organization (WHO), are closely monitoring the situation and have put stringent measures in place to manage and prevent any potential spread. The reported cases in Kenya remain isolated and under control, with no significant risk to tourists visiting the country.

Our dedicated team is trained to maintain the highest standards of cleanliness and hygiene, ensuring a safe and comfortable environment for all our guests.

We encourage travelers to take the usual precautions, such as practicing good hand hygiene, staying informed, and consulting with their healthcare providers if they have specific concerns. We are committed to providing a safe, relaxing, and memorable experience as you explore Kenya’s incredible landscapes, wildlife, and culture.

If you have any questions or need more information, please do not hesitate to reach out to us. Your peace of mind is our priority!

**We look forward to welcoming you to Kenya!**
