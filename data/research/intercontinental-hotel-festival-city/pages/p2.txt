# InterContinental Dubai Festival CityRooms & suites

Bask in the splendour of this five-star Dubai Creek hotel, where breathtaking views take centre stage. Expansive and luminous rooms cater to every need, from intimate deluxe rooms to opulent suites perfect for families. Complimentary Wi-Fi adds convenience, while Club guests revel in exclusive privileges.

Marvel at the picturesque vistas enhanced by marble floors and vibrant jewel tones. Luxurious touches abound, from indulgent toiletries to a standalone bathtub, ensuring relaxation after a bustling day. Sink into premium bedding for a tranquil night's rest, completing your unforgettable stay.
