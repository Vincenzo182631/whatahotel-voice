## FAQs for InterContinental Dubai Festival City

Does InterContinental Dubai Festival City have a pool?

InterContinental Dubai Festival City has 1 outdoor pool.

Outdoor Pool

Discover an oasis of peace and tranquility featuring a selection of pools including the iconic 'Infinity Pool', a glass-bottom pool suspended over the hotel's fourth floor, is a one-of-a-kind gravity-defying experience.

Pool information

How much is parking at InterContinental Dubai Festival City?

Complimentary parking is available at InterContinental Dubai Festival City for hotel guests.

Complimentary valet and self-service parking make life easy for those travelling to InterContinental Dubai Festival City hotel by car.

Parking for registered guests only

Vehicles allowed: Bus

Parking & transportation details

Can I bring my pet to InterContinental Dubai Festival City?

No, pets are not allowed at InterContinental Dubai Festival City.

Can I cancel my booking at InterContinental Dubai Festival City for a full refund?

Always check the hotel's cancellation policy before booking a rate. Room cancellation is subject to rate booked at time of reservation and cancellation policy varies by rate.

Does InterContinental Dubai Festival City have a restaurant on site?

InterContinental Dubai Festival City has 5 restaurants.

When is breakfast at InterContinental Dubai Festival City?

On weekends, breakfast is available from 6:00 AM to 11:00 AM.

Is there a complimentary shuttle available at InterContinental Dubai Festival City?

No, there is not a complimentary local area shuttle available at InterContinental Dubai Festival City.

What are the check-in and check-out times for InterContinental Dubai Festival City?

Check-in at InterContinental Dubai Festival City is from 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 12:00 PM. Contact the hotel directly for options available for early check-in or late check-out.

Does InterContinental Dubai Festival City offer free Wi-Fi?

Yes, the InterContinental Dubai Festival City offers free WiFi!

How far is InterContinental Dubai Festival City from the nearest airport?

InterContinental Dubai Festival City is conveniently located near Dubai International Airport (DXB), approximately 2.49 miles away (4 kilometers) from the hotel. Whether you are arriving for a family vacation, a business trip, or a weekend getaway, our location makes getting to and from the airport straightforward. We recommend checking with the front desk for the latest transportation options, including ride-share services and local shuttle providers, to help you plan your arrival and departure with ease.

What attractions are near InterContinental Dubai Festival City?

InterContinental Dubai Festival City is ideally situated close to some of the area's most popular attractions and points of interest. Guests staying with us enjoy easy access to the following:

- Dubai's crown jewel: Burj Khalifa - 4.78 miles away (7.7 kilometers)
- Ski Dubai: Dubai’s exotic winter wonderland - 11.81 miles away (19 kilometers)
- Explore Dubai’s rich heritage at Dubai Museum - 11.81 miles away (19 kilometers)
- Sail through history on Dubai Creek - 4.35 miles away (7 kilometers)
- Jumeirah Mosque: Elegance in every arch - 6.21 miles away (10 kilometers)
- Dubai World Trade Centre: A landmark for progress - 5.78 miles away (9.3 kilometers)
- IMAGINE Dubai - 0.62 miles away (1 kilometers)

Whether you are planning a day exploring local landmarks, or simply looking to enjoy what the area has to offer, InterContinental Dubai Festival City puts you within easy reach. Contact our front desk for directions, local tips, and any transportation assistance you may need during your stay.
