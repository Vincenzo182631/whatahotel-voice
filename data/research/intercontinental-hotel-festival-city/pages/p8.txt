## Things to do

### Dubai's crown jewel: Burj Khalifa

Downtown Dubai, Dubai,

Marvel at Dubai's shining symbol of progress, the towering 828-meter Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest structure. Ascend to the observation deck on level 124 for a record-breaking experience. Contact our Concierge team early to secure your booking and make the most of your visit.

### Ski Dubai: Dubai’s exotic winter wonderland

Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai,

Experience the thrill of desert skiing at Ski Dubai in the Mall of the Emirates, boasting five snow runs, including a 400-meter slope. Gear and lessons are provided, followed by a unique coffee experience at Avalanche Café. Enjoy coffee in the snow, amidst the desert’s warmth.

### Explore Dubai’s rich heritage at Dubai Museum

Al Fahidi Street, Bur Dubai, Dubai,

Step back in time at the Dubai Museum, originally constructed as a fort in the 1780s, which was officially inaugurated in 1971. Its galleries vividly depict scenes from Dubai’s history, including the Creek, traditional Arab houses, mosques, souks, date farms, and the varied desert and marine life.

### Sail through history on Dubai Creek

Al Khor Street, Deira, Dubai,

Hailed as the heartbeat of Dubai, Dubai Creek bustles with activities. Experience its charm by hopping on an abra ride between Bur Dubai and Deira, a timeless journey that captures Dubai’s rich maritime heritage. It is a magical ride where history and everyday hustle gently sail side by side.

### Jumeirah Mosque: Elegance in every arch

Jumeirah Beach Road, Jumeirah, Dubai,

A masterpiece of medieval Fatimid architecture, this splendid mosque is a rare gem and one of the best places to visit in Dubai. Open to all visitors, it offers a captivating glimpse into Islamic culture and heritage, inviting exploration and enlightenment for all.

### Dubai World Trade Centre: A landmark for progress

Dubai World Trade Centre, Dubai,

Known for its global excellence, the Dubai World Trade Centre stands as one of the city’s largest and most versatile venues, renowned for hosting some of the world’s most prestigious events. A must-visit among Dubai tourist attractions, it stands as a symbol of excellence and innovation.

### IMAGINE Dubai

Dubai Festival City Mall, Dubai,

Experience the awe-inspiring IMAGINE at Dubai Festival City—an extraordinary sensory journey intertwining light, water, and fire. With massive aqua screens, 30 fountains, and enveloping surround sound, this spectacle captivates like no other, setting Guinness World Records for the largest water screen projection and permanent projection mapping. Prepare to be spellbound by a show that redefines entertainment on a grand scale.
