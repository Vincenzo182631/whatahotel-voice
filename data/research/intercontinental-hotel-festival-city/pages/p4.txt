### SPA InterContinental: Indulge in Pure Rejuvenation

Step into indulgence at the award-winning SPA InterContinental, one of the best spas in Dubai. Enjoy bespoke treatments, a selection of pools, and a 24-hour fitness centre. Don’t miss the glass-bottom Infinity Pool, suspended above the hotel’s fourth floor - a truly unique experience.

### Sustainability

As part of IHG Hotels & Resorts, InterContinental Dubai Festival City is dedicated to doing our part for the environment. We strive to be responsible and ethical in everything we do, making a positive impact on sustainability and reducing our carbon footprint.

### Iconic Infinity Glass Pool

Bask in the sun while taking in the breathtaking panoramic views of the Dubai skyline from our world-renowned glass infinity pool. Indulge in refreshing drinks and gourmet snacks from our poolside bar. With temperature-controlled waters, our pool offers a delightful swimming experience year-round, ensuring comfort and enjoyment in every season.
